Outcome and exact scope
=======================

CA1–CA10 reconstruct the analytic and finite Fourier identities used by the
actual normalized theta family and its coset indicators. CA2 gives the exact
finite presentation Pres after cancellation of chiF; it is independent of the
vacuum theorem. CA3–CA4 prove the signed finite Heisenberg intertwiner calculus,
including the negative inert norm Gauss sum. CA5 identifies those intertwiners
with the source's positive normalization on the one-factor moves occurring in
thMoved. CA6–CA8 calculate the exact rational-unitary scalar. The calculation
uses a Gaussian trace and a real-square finite Gauss sum, rather than treating
theta-line uniqueness or unit modulus as a determination of that scalar.
CA9 proves all three EqP laws for the actual given Base.eNat, and CA10 proves
the chosen-lift indicator's actual ThIndEq and ChiLaw.

CA11 now proves the actual FinAlg step result with its source antecedents.
Its human proof acts directly on the given host-level AmbIn.VacIn, avoiding
an unused full-Stages existence detour in the Lean proof. CA12 retains the
FinAlg restriction in the source's allSet definition and proves that the actual
canonical selected step satisfies it. CA13 proves the literal evLg_canonical
implication: the finite coefficient-field requirements precede the base, the
given frame-inclusion and Nonempty PreX antecedents remain, the selected prime,
frame, excluded set, twist range and ambient datum are preserved, and the final
ValLL0 assertion is universal over later assemblies. Later existence is not
inferred from that universal assertion.

The new VI component supplies the actual same-data vacuum, theta-line and
positive-Iop norm results, and algebraicity of all rational translates. VI1–VI10
are independent of CA; only VI11 uses the independent CA2 finite presentation.
CA5–CA9 use VI2/VI5/VI10, and CA11 uses VI11. This claim-level direction is
acyclic. The report and ledger do not turn a component-wide reference to VI
into a false CA↔VI dependency cycle.

These are human proofs under the stated source data, not a Lean build, kernel
replay, or formalization of the alternative proof. No predecessor artifact or
Lean source was edited, and no PDF was built or rendered. This component does
not claim a completed proof of RH or GRH.

Conventions and nonlocal inputs
===============================

E is the host CM field, K=Q(sqrt(-d)) its actual imaginary quadratic subfield,
Phi the induced CM type, c the curve ideal and delta the prescribed discriminant
normalization. H is linear in its first argument. Its scale is exactly
area(delta*c)^(-1), and Q(x)=sqrt(d) H(x,x)/2. Rational points are delta times
the Phi-embedding of an E-matrix. They are not arbitrary algebraic or complex
points. The period ideal is the actual AA0, multiplied by c where appropriate.

For lattices A and B the relative index n(A,B) is [B:A intersect B], matching
the source direction. The unnormalized sum S(A,B) is over B/(A intersect B).
The signed normalization J(A,B) uses the multiplicative GoodNorm lambda;
the actual Iop uses the positive square root of n(A,B). Their equality is
proved only for the specified one-factor moves in even rectangular ranks.

The source's Base/VacIn is supplied as data: its period pair, unkilled half-point,
rational four-copy isometry, complex extension reading and correction roots are
the actual ones. VI proves the missing vacuum properties of these data; PA9
constructs AmbIn.Ok when needed for the final canonical join. Host existence
itself remains subject to the arithmetic context and the exact AH hypotheses.
No argument chooses a different vacuum and silently substitutes it for thMM.

CA1 — Exact Heisenberg phases and rationality
============================================

The operators are U_x f(z)=exp(-pi H(z,x)-pi H(x,x)/2) f(z+x), and
W_x=exp(pi i Q(x)) U_x. Expanding the exponent gives U_x U_y equal to
exp(-pi i Im H(x,y)) U_(x+y). Polarizing Q then gives the exact W cocycle
exp(-2 pi i c(x,y)), where c=(sqrt(d) Re H+Im H)/2. The commutator is
gamma(x,y)=exp(-2 pi i Im H(x,y)), with no missing factor two or reversed sign.

The source integral element w=(1+sqrt(-d))/2 satisfies w+bar(w)=1 and embeds
as (1+i sqrt(d))/2 at every Phi place. Hence c(x,y)=Im H(wx,y), giving the
literal IsHeis cocycle gamma(wx,y), and multiplication by r has adjoint bar(r).
The w statement is a characteristic-zero integral-order fact; no inverse of
2 in a residue field is used.

For rational embedded matrices the area formula turns the sum over Phi into
a relative trace to K. Thus Im H(x,y) and sqrt(d) Re H(x,y) are rational. This
proves algebraicity and unit modulus under every algebraic embedding of all
phases used in CA2. It does not claim that the full analytic exponential in
U_x is algebraic as a function of arbitrary z.

CA2 — The literal finite presentation Pres
==========================================

The finite Fourier definition has two separate inverse sqrt(volC) factors.
After expanding it, the coefficient of U_(y-s) thMoved is the average over
the inner quotient of 0/1 indicator values times rational roots of unity,
multiplied by alpha(s), alpha(y), the exact Weyl phase, and the factors
chiF^(-1) chiF and volC/(sqrt(volC))^2. The first is 0 or 1 without an
algebraicity hypothesis on chiF; the second is 1 for the actual full lattices
(or 0 in the formal degenerate branch). This is why it is legitimate to call
the resulting coefficients algebraic. Dividing by chiF after calling its
value algebraic would have been an unjustified shortcut.

Every coefficient has absolute value at most one after every algebraic
embedding. The number of outer representatives is at most c^[E:Q]ab. Indeed
c*Lambda is contained in B_c and c*B_c in Lambda. Multiplication by c injects
B_c/(B_c intersect Lambda) into Lambda/c*Lambda, whose size is the stated
power because Lambda is a free abelian group of that rank. Every translate
y-s lies in the rational span of the same Lambda. These are all fields of
the actual Pres predicate, including its conjugate coefficient sum bound.
The proof works for any finite S; no no-conjugate-pair condition is smuggled
into this stronger finite-presentation assertion. Empty matrix dimensions
give a singleton quotient and satisfy the same bound.

CA3 — The finite norm Gauss sum, including the dyadic case
========================================================

For k/k0 with nontrivial involution and |k0|=q, norm zero has one preimage and
every nonzero norm has q+1 preimages, since k^times is cyclic. A nontrivial
additive character eta of k0 has total sum zero, so summing eta(norm t)
gives 1+(q+1)(-1)=-q. This is the negative square root of |k|.

A sigma-invariant nontrivial additive character chi of k factors through
trace: its kernel contains the image of sigma-1, which equals the trace
kernel, and trace is onto. The latter remains valid in characteristic two:
X+X^q is a nonzero polynomial of degree q<q^2. Since trace(w)=1, the exact
sum chi(w t sigma(t)) is the same negative norm Gauss sum. No assertion
about a real additive character or division by two in k is needed.

CA4 — Signed intertwiners compose
=================================

The perfect pairing between the finite quotients of two self-dual modules
proves that the reverse sum followed by the forward sum is n(A,B) times the
identity on A-fixed vectors. This is finite character orthogonality, with
all representatives and cocycle terms retained.

The adjacent-lattice composition has three cases. Two cases are direct
intermediate-lattice quotient decompositions, with raw scalar 1 or |R/P|.
In the third the intersections with the target lattice coincide. A generator
of the simple quotient decomposes as a+c. Its residual character chi(t)
=gamma(a,t c) is nontrivial and invariant under involution. It forces P
to be conjugation-fixed, and the raw composition scalar is the CA3 sum
-sqrt(|R/P|). This is the substantive sign in GoodNorm.

For the actual abelian host a single multiplicative lambda on rational
positive integers can realize all the required signs. All primes over p
have the same residue degree f_p, and abelian Galois symmetry makes
conjugation fix either all or none. At fixed primes choose
lambda(p)=sqrt(p) exp(pi i/f_p); otherwise choose sqrt(p). Multiplicativity
then gives the correct negative value at |R/P|=p^f_p. CM unramifiedness
ensures that a fixed residue involution is nontrivial, including at 2.

For general lattices choose a simple nonzero submodule of B/(A intersect B)
and replace A by (A intersect (Rx)^perp)+Rx. Perfect pairing proves this
new module self-dual and adjacent, and the positive index strictly decreases.
This finite induction proves exact J composition. It never assumes a
globally free O_E lattice or invokes the adelic Weil representation.

CA5 — Why the actual positive normalization has no remaining sign
================================================================

The source Iop equals n(A,B)^(-1/2) times the finite W sum because VI2
identifies the source rounded semicharacter with exp(pi i Q) on the actual
period lattices. To identify it with J, localize at each P and choose DVR
bases. If only the source lattice L changes, Hom(L,L') is a direct sum of
b copies of the source dual lattice. Every local quotient length is thus
divisible by b. If only the target changes, it is divisible by a. Tensoring
by the same locally principal period ideal does not change a relative index.

Both ranks are even, so all inert signs in lambda(n) disappear. The positive
Iop and signed J agree on each of the two actual moves defining thMoved.
CA4 therefore turns that precise two-step definition into J(A0,A) thZero.
This is not an assertion that arbitrary positive-normalized intertwiners
compose without signs. Rational unitary changes preserve the pairing,
indices and representatives and give the exact J naturality formula.

CA6 — Gaussian trace calculation of the scalar
==============================================

Theta-line uniqueness and positive-Iop isometry give a unit scalar c_u,
but not its phase. The reproducing-kernel argument computes it. The kernel
sum converges normally on compact sets, with all derivatives, by a Gaussian
majorant and polynomial lattice-point count. Shifting its summation variable
shows it is in the actual theta line, so the same scalar relation applies.

The expanded manuscript identifies the two integrations explicitly. Set the
theta variable t equal to the kernel parameter z. In the finite-Iop side,
the summand indexed by y and x has z-character exp(2 pi i E_H(x+y,z)).
It is periodic on A_m=(1-u^(-1))^(-1)(mA); its integral is zero unless
x+y=0. Such an x in A exists exactly when y is the zero quotient class.
Its coefficient alpha(y) alpha(-y) is one. The result is c_u times the
positive normalized index factor times vol(F).

The other side is K_A(z,u^(-1)z) times the Gaussian weight. Decomposing
x=s-(1-u^(-1))y unfolds the integral into a finite sum over A/mA and an
integral over the full vector space. Diagonalizing the unitary action and
evaluating two real Gaussian integrals per coordinate gives the reciprocal
complex determinant, with a positive measure scalar. The remaining exponent
is -pi H(C s,s), C=(1+u^(-1))(1-u^(-1))^(-1)/2. Thus the phase is reduced
to the displayed finite Gauss sum. Normal convergence and absolute Gaussian
integrability justify every exchange; no formal oscillatory integral is used.

CA7 — Positivity fixes the Gaussian phase
========================================

The opposite even matrix rank supplies an orthogonal half projection and
the swap of the halves. These preserve the standard period lattice and
commute with the action. The finite Gauss function factors over the halves,
and the two half sums agree. Norm surjectivity in the unramified CM local
algebras, together with CRT, supplies an integral ell with ell*bar(ell)
=-1 modulo 4m. This holds at dyadic primes by DR1's trace lifting.

Multiplication by ell is a bijection modulo mA and conjugates the Gauss
function. The exponent check uses 2m Cx in A and the integral quadratic
polarization; its difference is an even integer. Thus each half sum is
real and the full sum is a real square. It is nonzero because c_u has
modulus one. This proves c_u=|det(1-u^(-1))|/det(1-u^(-1)); it is the
essential extra step beyond the theta-line argument.

CA8 — Exact determinant powers and the eigenvalue-one case
=========================================================

The actual action is T(z)=gamma' z gamma^(-1); the source pull is
f(T^(-1)z). For the source-factor action the determinant in CA7 is
the product of det(1-gamma)^b. Even a gives
det(gamma) conjugate(det(1-gamma))=det(1-gamma), and even b gives
the scalar product_tau tau(det gamma)^(-b/2). The target factor
uses gamma'^(-1) and gives the opposite exponent +a/2. This checks
the source action orientation, not just its absolute value.

Matrices with eigenvalue one are not excluded from the result. The
rational norm-one elements (1+t sqrt(-d))/(1-t sqrt(-d)), t rational,
are infinite. Choose a scalar avoiding 1 and the finitely many inverse
eigenvalues, and factor gamma=(z gamma)(z^(-1) I). Both factors are
covered by the Gaussian result. Exact J composition and naturality
extend the formula, while their determinant characters multiply.
Rank zero is dealt with directly by constant functions and empty products.

CA9 — The actual eNat and all three EqP clauses
==============================================

The determinant ideal of a unitary image is multiplied by the principal
determinant ideal. The actual IsChi law sends that principal norm-one
ideal to the inverse product of its CM embeddings. Hence chiFac transforms
by exactly the CA8 scalar, with source exponent -b/2 and target exponent
+a/2. The naturality calculation now transports the actual given thZero
through J(A0,A), giving eNat(gamma L,gamma' L')=pull eNat(L,L').
The two one-factor transport laws follow from J composition and the
multiplicativity of determinant ideals. These are all three source EqP laws.

This is a deliberate alternative proof of the normalized-family theorem.
The Lean proof builds an auxiliary theta family through an adelic Weil
representation, compares the standard theta lines, and cancels a quotient
of two Hecke characters. Our proof does not assert that entire auxiliary
construction has been reconstructed. It proves the desired actual-family
statement directly from the finite intertwiners and their explicit scalar.
The lemma source counterparts and genuine lower-level suppliers are recorded
separately in the provenance.

CA10 — Exact lift independence, finite normalization and indicator covariance
============================================================================

At P in S, A_c is c^(-1) times the period lattice and B_c is c times it;
at the conjugate prime the exponents reverse. Elsewhere both equal the
period lattice. This table is sufficient for the full argument, with LP1
recovering the global modules. In particular each half lattice has integral
cocycle because the conjugate exponents cancel.

A valid rational lift t is obtained by CRT in a common denominator module.
Its difference from x lies in c Hom_S, and it belongs to A_(Mc). Comparing
with the original chosen lift s shows t lies in A_c and t-s in B_c.
The finite B_c sum is B_c-fixed; the integral cocycle makes W_-t W_(t-s)
=W_-s. Thus the actual chosen-lift definition has the displayed formula.

The only surviving inner Fourier class is zero, and its coefficient is
vol/(n sqrt(vol)). The second inverse sqrt(vol) gives exactly 1/n,
where n=[period intersect A_c:B_c intersect period intersect A_c].
This exact cancellation makes covariance transparent: rational conjugation
carries every module, localization, lift, quotient and representative set
to the transformed one, preserves n and the Heisenberg form, and CA9
transports the theta vector. There is no missing root of unity from a
noncanonical lift or change of representatives.

a unit; b*bar(b)=different and b integral force both exponents zero.

multi-indices of degree at most n. Adjoining their normalized derivative
values, cD entries and an embedded Q-basis of E gives a finite algebraic
generating set. Linear substitution and ChiLaw put every arbitrary-class

The degree statement concerns a fixed Taylor component and repeated

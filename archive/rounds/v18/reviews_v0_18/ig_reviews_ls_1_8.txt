MATHEMATICAL FINDINGS

LS1. The Hasse derivative has the claimed degree and leading binomial
coefficient. Legendre's carry bound gives p^v_p(binomial)<=n, including
p=2. Its value at the original point is bounded by r^(2^k). The nearby
root has at most 2^k conjugates, the recurrence C_(k+1)=C_k*d_k is exact,
and the loss is uniformly bounded by 16. The completed fixed-field
conclusion uses uniform approximation, not a trace with an uncontrolled
ramified denominator. It applies to arbitrary subgroups; a finite
extension fixed field is closed by completeness.

LS2. The primitive-root distance and distinct valuation classes prove
both the exact cyclotomic degree and the coefficient bound, without
assuming that bound from trace integrality. Translation by one preserves
the integral Gauss coefficient bounds. Interleaving P_i(X^N)X^i correctly
isolates coefficients divisible by N. Their constant component is the
normalized trace and has operator norm <=p independently of n,m. Uniform
boundedness and eventual identity on the dense union justify the limit
projections. The case p=2,n=1 is covered: e_1=1 and K_1=Q_2.

LS3. The kernel of the cyclotomic character has the cyclotomic union as
its fixed algebraic field, so LS1 applies. Unbounded cyclotomic degrees
produce a character value with nontrivial i-th power for every nonzero
integer i. Equivariant projections consequently vanish. For general K,
the product over gG_K is well defined and has twist exponent
+[G:G_K]i in the invariance equation, equivalently action exponent
-[G:G_K]i. This matches the main proof and report, including negative i.

LS4. Compatible p-th roots in C follow by successive algebraic root
approximation and the ultrametric inequality. The actual sharp/tilt
construction gives the valuation-ring divisibility used in the one-step
theta-kernel division. Iterating produces p-adically convergent Witt
coefficients. The actual cyclotomic xi is a unit multiple of the
p-minus-Teichmuller generator. For p=2 the proof uses one additional
inverse Frobenius root, with |zeta_2-1|>|2| before squaring. The reduction
comparison does not use a false strict inequality at zeta_1=-1.
Nonzero xi mod p proves p-saturation. Cauchy quotient division and
adjusted lifts justify completeness of all xi-power quotients.

LS5. Infinite xi-divisibility annihilates the zeroth Witt coefficient
and then every subsequent coefficient, using p-saturation at each
division. This proves injection of Ainf[1/p] into its completion.
The residue field, regular uniformizer and separatedness give the
complete DVR and fraction field with the stated filtration. Hensel
uniqueness gives the compatible Q_p-linear, additive, multiplicative,
Galois-equivariant algebraic section. No section on all of C is asserted.

LS6. This review checked completion existence and completion injectivity
separately. The image modulo xi^n lies between A/(xi^n) and
p^(-n)A/(xi^n). The bounded shift proves completeness and the exact
IsCrisP.bdd quantifier with N=n uniformly in x. Dense approximation is
used only in these p-adically separated quotient modules. No continuity
from the p-adic topology to the whole xi-adic topology is assumed.

The finite intersection L_N intersect xi^N A[1/p] is contained in
A*(N gamma_N). In its induction, multiplying by N! and cancelling xi^N
reduce to xi|(a+Nc), which saturation lifts from A[1/p] to A. For j(x)=0,
write x=s'+p^(n+1)v and use p^n j(v)=a mod xi^n. Then u=s'+pa is an
envelope element in the kernel modulo xi^n, and completion divisibility
gives s=u+pw within the original envelope. Truncate w at n=pM. The
intersection formula forces the remaining coefficient to be divisible
by p. Iteration puts x in every p^rD and proves it zero. This establishes
injection on the completed ring, not only on its dense subring.
Frobenius preservation uses phi(a)=a^p+pb and p^n/n! integral. Its
injectivity is correctly left for LS9, outside this scope.

LS7. The polynomial congruence for m^(p-1) and the cyclotomic sum gives
p*w=m^(p-1). The two kernel terms have divided powers. The valuation
v_p((p^2)!)=p+1 proves w^p in pD_0, including p=2. Delaying the first
nontrivial compatible root multiplies w by an integral cyclotomic
factor and preserves this divisibility. With q=floor(k/(p-1)) and
v=v_p(k+1)<=q, the coefficient formula proves integral convergence at
the stated rate. Nonconstant coefficients are nilpotent modulo p;
only finitely many survive there, so the limiting quotient is a unit.
The factorial-normalized IsLogA condition follows exactly.

The bounded quotient map identifies this integral limit with the
formal logarithm. Approximating chi(g) modulo p^n and taking a compatible
p^n-th root of the difference proves exact Galois covariance. Finally
mu=xi*nu and theta(nu)=zeta_1-1!=0 give filtration degree exactly one,
also at p=2. This avoids an unsupported odd-prime convergence shortcut.

LS8. From t=mu times a unit and p*w=mu^(p-1), the candidate localization
Acris[1/t]=Acris[1/mu] contains p^-1 and embeds into BdR. Prime-to-p
roots have compatible roots inside their finite cyclic group. Hensel
uniqueness identifies their Teichmuller lifts with the algebraic

actual unramified embedding. Root generators determine the Frobenius
and Galois compatibilities and the restriction to every finite K_0.
The paragraph explicitly defers identification with every source

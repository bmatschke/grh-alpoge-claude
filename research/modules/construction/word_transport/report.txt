0. RESULT, SOURCE MAP, AND SCOPE

This note proves the operator-word half of incoming MemAt. The actual
endpoint is WordConditionAt, not the distinct outgoing assertion MembAt.
For C>=16 and the source DataII, it proves

  MemAtG(0,1/8;F) => MemAtG(1/8,3/8;opWord_l F)

for every allowed list l: its positions are in TT, and at most c0 entries
have any one position s. There is no assumption that the TOTAL word length
is at most c0. The argument is uniform in all coordinate indices, residues,
plane/normal degree blocks, and normal joint projections quantified in
MemAtG. No integral-projector denominator is spent in this membership
argument; its linear algebra is over the characteristic-zero fraction
field, and the conclusions concern algebraic prefix orders.

The substantive source route is:

  TwistedSubstitution/MemRed.WordConditionAt
    <- WordLossNonneg.restWordStep_of
    <- PlaneCoefficients/RestOneOpOf.oneOpStep_of and WBound.wordBoundStep.

The one-operator proof uses four ingredients, all expanded below:

  DoubleMonomialApply: exact first-digit derivative/multiplication formulas;
  TouchCOfMem + XXgZeroP: second digits and plane cutoff transport;
  MonomialSupport/Lam1SPderivAndXMulLeAddIndicator: normal type transport;
  PlaneCoeffMonomial + GeoTouch: threshold and physical-prefix comparison.

The input from the initial-symbol companion IN9 is EXACTLY Lemma5aAt, whose
output is MemAtG(0,1/8) for each symP(B_b). The actual initial source route
uses rest5aMono_of_cut and retains its block-degree conditions internally.
We do not replace it by the stronger Lemma5aGeneratorProductsAt, which is
not the instantiated initial theorem on this call path.

All local steps from that input are proved here. Initial-symbol membership
is proved in the v0.6 companion IN9; together the two sections close the
incoming membership premise. The existence of a frame satisfying FramesOK
and the global parameter supply satisfying Hyps remain separate inputs. The
finite-dimensional rational representation theory of split GL in
characteristic zero (complete reducibility and highest-weight dominance)
is background, as in IW/PG; no new integral semisimplicity assertion is
introduced. No Lean source was changed and no compilation or kernel replay
is claimed. Exact declaration ranges, hashes and URLs are in provenance.json.

1. THE ACTUAL LETTERS, PREFIXES, AND UNIVERSAL MEMBERSHIP [WT1]

Use n=n' for the physical period and k=r+K for the coordinate dimension,
with r,K>=1. Work over a field F0 of characteristic zero; the actual F0 is
KK O. A valid frame has positions f_(j,i), 0<=i<r, satisfying

  f_(j,0)<...<f_(j,r-1)=z_j<f_(j+1,0),
  f_(j+J,i)=f_(j,i)+n,               J>=1.

Every frame position is even and has selected residue in TT. There are
no selected positions strictly between consecutive entries of one frame.
Define

  W_j={x in [f_(j-1,0),z_j]: x mod n in TT},
  L_j={x in (z_(j-1),f_(j,0)): x mod n in TT}.

These are Frames.letters and Frames.LLset. The true letters in segment j
are L_j and the current frame. Let x_(j,0)<...<x_(j,nL_j+r-1) list them.
The source calls the internal letter with index l Sum.inl(l), and calls
the frame entry f_(j,r-1-m) Sum.inr(m): frame indices are REVERSED, although
the prefix position index u increases physically. Its NSet(j,u) consists
of the first u true letters, so u ranges from 0 to nL_j+r inclusive.

1.1. Why the true physical residue is unique.

The selected positions in (z_(j-1),z_j] are precisely L_j and the current
frame: the interval before f_(j,0) gives L_j, and the no-selected-gap
condition eliminates every other point between frame entries. These
half-open intervals partition (z_-1,z_(J-1)], an interval of length n by
periodicity. Each residue modulo n has exactly one integer representative
there. Restricting to selected residues proves that

  true letters x -> posTL(x) mod n

is a bijection onto TT. This proves the actual GeoTop property, including
the uniqueness needed to change only one coordinate under digit extraction.
It does not require assuming that a whole overlapping W_j is injective
modulo n.

1.2. Exactly which physical prefixes can be affected.

For s in TT define

  delta_(j,u)(s)=1 if some x in W_j has x mod n=s and x<=x_(j,u),
                 0 otherwise.                              (1.1)

This is cost1, while opCost is 2 delta. For an internal true letter x0,
membership in NSet(j,u+1) means its position index is <=u, hence its
physical lift is <=x_(j,u). The same statement for a current frame follows
from its reversed m-index: nL_j+(r-1-m)<u+1 is exactly the corresponding
position inequality. Every previous-frame entry is less than every
x_(j,u) and belongs to W_j. At j=0 the source representative of j-1 may
lie one period later; subtract n from its physical position. The residue
is unchanged, and the preceding-frame inequality then applies. Thus

  x0 in NSet(j,u+1), or x0 a previous-frame letter,
       implies delta_(j,u)(posTL(x0) mod n)=1.                (1.2)

These are the full GeoTouch cases. The chain set in the second-digit model
contains the internal letters of index <i and all carries from the previous
frame. Therefore an operation can affect that chain only in one of the
cases (1.2), with u=i. The link TSet(j,m) consists of all internal letters
and frame indices m'>m, hence of the true positions strictly before
x_(j,nL_j+r-1-m). It too is covered by (1.2). The use of <= in (1.1) is
deliberately generous: it covers the adjacent normal prefix as well.

1.3. The threshold and the precise universal condition.

Write s_(j,u)=slU(j,u), B_(j,u)=Bo(j,x_(j,u)), and let a_(j,u)=kappa_(j,u),1
be the first coordinate of a normal highest-weight tuple. The source's
thStar is the following function T_(j,u)(kappa): take the maximum of
B_(j,u)/2 and the active expressions

  a_(j,u+1)+mStar+s_(j,u), if x_(j,u) is even;
  a_(j,u-1)+mStar+s_(j,u), if u>=1 and x_(j,u-1) is odd.       (1.3)

An inactive expression is replaced by B/2. The threshold is independent
of the particular plane and normal multidegrees being extracted.

The first extraction levImg retains, in every selected physical coordinate,
exponents rho_v+q_v b_v, where q_v=p^(z_j-posTL(v)), and replaces them by
b_v. It then selects the prescribed total quotient degree dPl(x) in the
first r coordinates of each true letter and dNo(x) in the last K. It
views the result as a plane polynomial with normal polynomial coefficients.
Variables at unselected physical positions are effectively sent to 1;
all the operators here act at selected positions, so they commute with
that harmless specialization. Thus the definition applies to arbitrary F,
without an unstated support hypothesis.

For every residue array, both quotient degree arrays, and every normal
joint projector e_kappa for the NSet prefixes, extract each normal monomial
coefficient of (1 tensor e_kappa)levImg(F). MemAtG(alpha,beta;F) requires
these plane polynomials to have the prescribed plane multidegree, all
second-digit chain orders >=T-alpha*s, and all links >=T-beta*s.
The chain cuts are 0<=u<=nL_j; the imposed links correspond to
nL_j<u<nL_j+r, equivalently m+2<=r. The link thresholds must be positive,
as required by SignedChainData.

It is useful to allow an extra arbitrary real loss L_(j,u), and arbitrary
positive link thresholds BELOW those displayed bounds. Call that property
M_(alpha,beta)(L;F). It is precisely MemAtLe, or equivalently MemLev after
fixing the geometric datum. MemAtG implies M(0;F): given smaller thresholds,
replace them by their exact upper bounds, apply MemAtG, and weaken back.
The new link thresholds are positive because they dominate the given
positive ones. Changing dPl and thresholds leaves the signs, digit maps,
normal actions, and unit hypotheses unchanged. This proves restLeOfG and
memAtLe_iff; these are harmless transports, not extra mathematical inputs.

2. A DIRECT PROOF OF THE SIGNED TWIST BOUNDS [WT2]

For a set S of signed letters, choose a generic invertible matrix g and
put A(t)=g diag(1,...,t,...,1)g^(-1). Its entries have Laurent exponents
in {0,1}, and those of its inverse have exponents in {0,-1}. For a vector
letter the source substitution sends x_i to sum_b A_(b,i)x_b; for a
covector it sends x_i to sum_b A^(-1)_(i,b)x_b. Letters outside S are fixed.
Let v_S be the minimum Laurent exponent using the last diagonal coordinate,
and M_S the maximum using the first. These are literally valS and lam1S.

Multiplication transforms by the indicated matrix. The chain rule gives,
for vector coordinates,

  twist(partial_i H)=sum_b A^(-1)_(i,b) partial_b(twist H),

and for covectors it gives the same expression with A_(b,i). Coefficients
of A are independent of the polynomial variables. Differentiating a
coefficient polynomial cannot introduce any new Laurent exponent. Hence,
when the letter belongs to S, the possible minimum-order losses are

                    multiply     differentiate     coordinate Euler
  vector                0              1                  1
  covector              1              0                  1,

and the possible maximum-order increases are

                    multiply     differentiate     coordinate Euler
  vector                1              0                  1
  covector              0              1                  1.             (2.1)

For a letter outside S all costs are zero. Euler is multiplication after
differentiation, and one of those operations has zero cost in each row;
therefore its cost is ONE, not two. Scalar multiplication and finite sums
preserve lower cutoffs and upper cutoffs, since they cannot add exponents
outside the existing support. Zero polynomials cause no exception: their
orders are +infinity and -infinity respectively.

This proves the first-order instances of the source twisted-filtration
lemmas directly, including both vector/covector signs and the unchanged
prefix case. It is a simplification of the source's more general divided-
Taylor proof, specialized to exactly the operators needed by WordConditionAt.

3. THE TWO EXACT DIGIT TRANSPORTS [WT3]

Fix one coordinate X and integer q>=1. Let L_rho send X^(rho+qb) to y^b
and discard other residue classes, with 0<=rho<q. Other variables are
carried along unchanged. Then

  L_rho(partial_X H)
    = ((rho+1)Id+q y partial_y)L_(rho+1)H,   rho<q-1;
    = q partial_y L_0 H,                   rho=q-1;          (3.1)

  L_rho(XH)
    = L_(rho-1)H,                          rho>0;
    = y L_(q-1)H,                          rho=0.            (3.2)

Proof: for the first case the incoming exponent is rho+1+qb, whose
derivative coefficient is rho+1+qb and whose quotient remains b. At the
carry boundary the incoming exponent is q(b+1), so the coefficient is
q(b+1) and its quotient drops from b+1 to b. Multiplication either raises
the residue without a carry or changes residue q-1 to zero and quotient
b to b+1. These are identities on every monomial, hence on every polynomial.
They also cover q=1, when only the carry cases occur. Invalid residues
give zero. There is no division by q and no analytic convergence argument.

In the first extraction q=lowP(x0). The true-residue bijection in Section 1
ensures that no other true letter changes. After selecting quotient
degrees, the noncarry derivative terms use the same dPl,dNo; its carry
term uses the input degree dPl(x0)+1, or dNo(x0)+1 for a normal coordinate.
The multiplication carry term uses input degree dPl(x0)-1 or dNo(x0)-1.
If the requested output degree is zero this term is zero, since a quotient
exponent increased by one cannot have total degree zero. These are exactly
TrDerPl, TrDerNo, TrMulPl and TrMulNo, with the source's three/two-term
formulas obtained by setting unused scalar coefficients to zero.

The second extraction Fdig leaves internal letters untouched. At a frame
coordinate in segment j it uses q=P_j=p^(z_(j+1)-z_j), retains a residue,
divides the exponent by P_j, and renames the quotient coordinate to the
carry letter for segment j+1. Formulae (3.1)--(3.2) apply again. At an
internal coordinate differentiation/multiplication simply commutes with
Fdig and the injective renaming. The extra Euler term in (3.1) appears
only at a frame letter, which is a VECTOR. This sign fact is essential
to the sharper derivative argument below. The named source statements
are Fdig_der_ex, Fdig_mul_ex and modelLet_vec.

These exact identities explain how Frobenius powers and differentiation
interact. Differentiation does not simply commute with digit extraction;
the residue, carry and q factors have all been retained. Their scalar q
factors do not change an algebraic Laurent-order cutoff.

4. TRANSPORT OF THE PLANE COEFFICIENT CONDITIONS [WT4]

Fix geometric SignedChainData and a true letter x0. For every chain and
link impose a target threshold theta, and an input threshold theta'. Assume
theta' >= theta always, and theta' >= theta+1 on each cutoff whose variable
set can be affected by x0. This is the precise ThDrop condition.

For links, the estimates are immediate from (2.1), using the real threshold
inequality: order>=theta' and a loss of at most one imply order>=theta.
For chains apply the second-digit identities of Section 3. Every resulting
term is a scalar times another digit component, its derivative, or its
coordinate Euler operator. The internal derivative has loss zero for a
covector and at most one for a vector in the chain. A frame derivative
has the scalar/Euler/derivative expansion, but its renamed carry is a
vector, so both nonscalar terms cost at most one. A frame multiplication
has either no operation or multiplication by a vector carry, both of
which cost zero. An internal multiplication has loss only for a covector
in the chain. All other chains are unchanged.

The letter membership checks in Section 1 identify the affected chain:
an internal letter belongs to its indicated initial subset, or a frame
is carried to the next segment. These are exactly touchC. Multiplication
and differentiation change one letter degree by +1 and -1; monomial sums
show the stated homogeneous degree spaces map to the required ones.

Consequently PlDer and PlMul hold. More precisely, derivative spends one
only for a vector, and multiplication spends one only for a covector.
For Euler, if the letter has degree zero the result is zero. Otherwise
differentiate and multiply back. For a vector the derivative spends the
one unit and multiplication spends zero; for a covector differentiation
spends zero and multiplication spends one. This proves PlEul with the
same one-unit ThDrop, not with a mistakenly doubled loss. It also proves
the stronger sign-sensitive plDer'/plMul' used in the source.

5. NORMAL JOINT TYPES AND WHY THE NORMAL STEP IS NOT ASSUMED [WT5]

Normal polynomials carry the signed GL_K action on every prefix NSet(j,u).
For any fixed finite collection of polynomial degrees this is a finite-
dimensional rational representation of split GL_K in characteristic zero.
It decomposes into its canonical isotypic components. An isotypic
projection is equivariant for that prefix group and commutes with actions
on disjoint letters. For S subset T it therefore commutes with the
diagonal action on T, the product of the actions on S and T\S. It preserves
the T-isotypic components and so commutes with the T-isotypic projections.
Projections for disjoint prefixes also commute. The NSet prefixes are
nested inside each segment and disjoint across segments. Thus their
products e_kappa give a finite joint decomposition of every polynomial.
An operator satisfying the source IsJProj for one specified joint type
is unique, since its values on this decomposition are prescribed.

The generic maximum M_S(H) equals the maximum first highest-weight
coordinate among the types present in H. To see this, undo the outer g
in the conjugated diagonal substitution. Its exponents are the coordinate
weights of the generic translate g^(-1)H. Those translates span the cyclic
GL-module generated by H. In an irreducible highest-weight module of type
lambda, every coordinate weight is at most lambda_1 by dominance and
permutation symmetry, and lambda_1 occurs in the highest vector. Complete
reducibility gives the asserted maximum for the cyclic module. This also
shows that an M_S cutoff kills every projected type with larger first
coordinate. No lattice comparison is involved.

Let D be a derivative, coordinate multiplication, or coordinate Euler
on the normal letter x0. By (2.1), for every normal prefix,

  M_(j,u)(DH) <= M_(j,u)(H) + 1_(x0 in NSet(j,u)).

Since e_kappa H lies in type kappa at every prefix, it follows that

  e_(kappa') D e_kappa = 0 unless
  kappa'_(j,u),1 <= kappa_(j,u),1 + 1_(x0 in NSet(j,u))
       for EVERY j,u.                                      (5.1)

Indeed if one inequality fails, the corresponding individual output
projection kills D e_kappa H. The joint projection contains that factor;
all its factors commute, so the entire joint output is zero. Notice that
D need not commute with any of these projections. Formula (5.1), rather
than an incorrect commutation assertion, is the transport mechanism.

Now let G be a plane polynomial with normal coefficients. Decompose the
finitely many normal coefficient polynomials into joint types, using a
finite union of their finite supports. In (1 tensor e_(kappa')D)G only
the compatible types in (5.1) survive. For each compatible kappa, every
new plane coefficient is a finite F0-linear combination of the plane
coefficients of (1 tensor e_kappa)G. The plane space XXg with FIXED degree,
chain thresholds and link thresholds is an F0-subspace: digit extraction
is linear and Laurent-order lower bounds are closed under addition and
scalar multiplication. Therefore the desired XXg property passes to
the new coefficients. This is the full substantive NoOpStep proof.

The source writes kappa(j,u) for all natural u, although actual joint
indices have 0<=u<=nL_j+r. Extend an input kappa outside this finite set
by the output kappa'. The comparisons outside the set then hold trivially
because the indicator is nonnegative. This covers its exact universal
quantifiers without adding an infinite decomposition.

6. THRESHOLD COMPARISON AND THE ONE-OPERATOR THEOREM [WT6]

Let x0 have selected physical residue s. Put delta=delta_(j,u)(s).
If kappa,kappa' satisfy (5.1), then both active adjacent first weights
in (1.3) increase by at most delta. Indeed NSet(j,u-1) is contained in
NSet(j,u+1), and (1.2) applies whenever x0 lies in the latter. The baseline
B/2 is unchanged and delta>=0. Taking the maximum yields

  T_(j,u)(kappa') <= T_(j,u)(kappa)+delta_(j,u)(s).             (6.1)

This proves thStar_le and its actual thLe_ka specialization. Its use of
the neighboring cuts u+1 and u-1 is explicit. At a chain or link cut
u<nL_j+r, the needed u+1 is still among the actual normal joint indices.

Suppose M_(alpha,beta)(L;F). We prove it for partial_(s,b)F and X_(s,b)F
with L increased to L+delta(s), considering arbitrary output residues,
blocks, normal type and positive link thresholds below the new budget.

If b is a plane coordinate, use (3.1) or (3.2). Normal projection and
normal coefficient extraction commute with all plane operations, by
linearity on monomials. For an identity term the original hypothesis
applies at the same thresholds. For a derivative, multiplication or Euler
term, raise the input chain and link thresholds by delta at their cuts.
They remain within the old budget because

  theta <= T-alpha*s-L-delta  =>  theta+delta <= T-alpha*s-L,

and likewise for links. Raised link thresholds remain positive. Section 1
shows that every affected cutoff receives a unit; Section 4 therefore
maps back to the requested thresholds. All degree changes in (3.1)--(3.2)
are allowed because the membership hypothesis quantifies universally over
both degree arrays. The degree-zero multiplication case contributes zero.

If b is a normal coordinate, apply (5.1) to each derivative/multiplication/
Euler term. For every compatible input type kappa, (6.1) gives

  theta <= T(kappa')-alpha*s-L-delta <= T(kappa)-alpha*s-L.

The same statement holds for links. Thus the hypothesis supplies the
required plane coefficients at the UNCHANGED thresholds for every
compatible input type. Section 5 combines them, proving the normal case.

Finite sums and scalar factors in the digit formulas preserve all these
conclusions. We have proved one unit of touched-prefix cost for each
physical derivative and each physical multiplication. Apply first the
derivative and then multiplication, possibly in different plane/normal
blocks but at the same selected physical position s. This gives

  M_(0,1/8)(L;F) =>
  M_(0,1/8)(L+2 delta(s); X_(s,a) partial_(s,b)F).             (6.2)

This is the actual OneOpStep. Neither a,b nor the intermediate degree
block was restricted. The complete operator preserves the physical total
degree at s, as each monomial loses and gains one coordinate there.

7. THE EXACT WORD-LOSS AND NUMERICAL ABSORPTION [WT7]

For a list l put L_l(j,u)=2 sum_(s,a,b in l) delta_(j,u)(s). Let m_l(s)
count entries of l at position s. If m_l(s)<=c0 for all s, then

  0 <= L_l(j,u)
     <= 2 sum_(s in image(W_j mod n)) m_l(s)
     <= 2 #W_j c0 <= 2 N1 c0.                              (7.1)

The image may have fewer elements than W_j; injectivity on an overlapping
window is unnecessary. This is the exact wordLoss_le proof. An individual
position contributes its count, regardless of the many different pairs
of coordinate indices used there.

We now derive the actual slack bound, not a renamed WordBoundStep input.
By FramesOK, z_j-f_(j-1,0)<G'. Put Q=p^G'. For a nonprevious-frame position
x=x_(j,u), the preceding frame supplies r even selected positions before x.
The source oldest(j,x), the r-th most recent even selected position before
x, therefore belongs to W_j. Its distance from z_j is at most G'. Selected
flat degrees satisfy deg(s)>=a; hence

  B_(j,u)=deg(oldest mod n)/p^(z_j-oldest) >= a/Q.             (7.2)

For the actual degree, deg(s)=flatDeg(lambda0,s). WeightHyp gives
lambda0_s,1=aT_s, |lambda0_s,2|<=cpp and a+cpp<=aT_s;
therefore lambda0_s,1-lambda0_s,2>=a. Its nonnegative integer value is
exactly flatDeg, proving the lower bound used in (7.2).

Write N=#TT, k=m+1=r+K and

  cP=cStar+r N c0+2 r n cpp.

The actual Hyps include N1^2<=rN and

  C k^3 r^2 N Q^5 cP Y <= a.                               (7.3)

All quantities are nonnegative; k,r,N,Q>=1 and Y>0. Since N1 is a
nonnegative integer, N1<=N1^2<=rN, so N1 c0<=cP. Also, for C>=16,

  16 k^2 Q <= C k^3 r^2 N Q^5.

Multiply these inequalities and then by Y. Equation (7.3) yields

  16 N1 c0 Y k^2 Q <= a.

Together with (7.2), this gives B_(j,u)/(Y k^2)>=16 N1 c0. The right side
is an INTEGER, so taking the floor introduces no additional loss:

  s_(j,u)=floor(B_(j,u)/(Y k^2)) >=16 N1 c0.                 (7.4)

Thus L_l<=s/8 at every chain and link cut. This proves the actual
WordBoundStep for C>=16. It is conditional on the original quantitative
frame/weight data; it does not assert those data exist automatically.

8. ITERATION, THE EXACT EXPORT, AND THE WORD SPAN [WT8]

The source opWord uses a right fold: the head operator acts after the
tail word. The empty word has loss zero; by (6.2), adjoining its head
adds exactly 2 delta at every cut. Induction gives

  MemAtG(0,1/8;F) => M_(0,1/8)(L_l;opWord_l F).              (8.1)

By L_l<=s/8, a chain threshold T-s/8 lies within the budget T-L_l.
A link threshold T-3s/8 lies within T-s/8-L_l, since s>=0. Therefore
we can take precisely the target thresholds and obtain

  MemAtG(1/8,3/8;opWord_l F).                              (8.2)

In fact (8.1) gives link threshold T-s/4, a little stronger than the
exported T-3s/8. We record that elementary consequence but keep the
source's 3/8 in the assembled argument; no later constants are changed.

For fixed extraction data, every condition involved is linear in F.
Explicitly, levImg, the normal projector, coefficient extraction, and
Fdig are linear; prescribed multidegree is a linear subspace; and
Laurent-order lower bounds survive finite linear combinations. Thus if
each initial symP(B_b) satisfies MemAtG(0,1/8), so does their F0-span.
Apply (8.2) to each allowed word on each member of that span. The same
linearity then gives MemAtG(1/8,3/8) on the literal OpCl span. The equality
of its threshold formula with Tied proves that this is exactly incoming
MemAt, by memAt_iff. This is the mathematical content of
memAt_of_wordConditionAt, with both span extensions made explicit.

9. EXACT APPLICATION INTERFACES AND WHAT HAS BEEN CLOSED [WT9]

Closed here: actual first and second residue/carry formulas; signed-twist
prefix estimates for all relevant first-order operations; plane chains
and links; normal joint-type transition and coefficient recombination;
adjacent-prefix threshold comparison; the selected-position geometry;
the per-position word counting; and the numerical C>=16 absorption.
Consequently RestLeOfG, OneOpStep and WordBoundStep are not retained
black boxes, and their composition proves WordConditionAt.

External inputs are precisely:

  (i) Initial Lemma5aAt, with output MemAtG(0,1/8) for symP(B_b),
      proved by companion IN9 through its actual block-aware source
      route. This is an external theorem for WT, but it is no longer
      an unresolved membership input in the combined v0.6 reconstruction.
  (ii) FramesOK and Hyps for the actual application. We proved every
      consequence of them used in this word branch, but not their
      global existence or parameter construction.
  (iii) Characteristic-zero rational GL highest-weight theory, the
      same field-level background already used in IP/IW/PG.

No additional operator-word, twisted-substitution, integral spectral,
or normal-membership theorem is being left behind under a new name.
Outgoing MembAt was treated in DT10 and is not re-proved or conflated
with the incoming assertion here. Closing the operator branch alone
does not establish the whole Type-II theorem or RH/GRH.

CHARACTER-TO-WINDOW CLOSURE — VERSION 0.23

Endpoint
--------
WB1–WB8 give a complete proof of the implication from the precise
same-datum arithmetic contract in WB7 to the fixed positive character
window and thence to RH and Dirichlet GRH. They do not construct the
arithmetic gap or the common family required by that contract.

The entire chain
----------------
1. Exact finite-character orthogonality includes every ramified prime.
   Inertia averaging gives the primitive inducer at all positive integers.
   The onto reciprocity map makes primitive induction injective on host
   characters, so no varying multiplicity enters the fixed target family.
2. The asked arithmetic prime sum minus its exact main term equals the
   centered character expression. The exceptional d-prime contributes
   with coefficient one; the ordinary inert primes contribute two.
   The logarithmic derivative of chi_d has the required positive sign.
3. Elementary Chebyshev estimates give all needed regularity uniformly
   in the host, including the exact weighted Lipschitz constant three.
4. Thick/thin replacement and subgroup averaging give coefficient d2/2.
   The total replacement error is at most 2Q/x.
5. The actual 3:4 biased coset counts select weights 1 and 1/(3o+1).
   Moving cutoffs lose at most 4 x^(1/2+epsilon), uniformly in the growing
   group: only the bounded quotient of order 4o is involved.
6. SC1 and the gap E <= x^(C epsilon) B give V <= x^(1/2+(C+3)epsilon).
   Restoring the omitted character terms gives
       H_psi <= x^(1/2+(C+4)epsilon).
   The source C=eD+eL+3 gives exactly 1/2+(eD+eL+7)epsilon.
7. All inputs are collected into one explicit contract, including the
   exact IdentU reindexing and main-term equalities. The target and C are
   fixed before epsilon; thresholds are uniform as windows go to infinity.
8. AN2/AN3/GH6 finish the conditional proof. The positive family includes
   real-pole positivity; primitive induction and reflection give the full
   Dirichlet scope. No duplicate analytic endpoint is counted.

What remains
------------
The actual same-family arithmetic gap, SizeData2/ErrWindows/BNBounds,
compatible host/local readings, cohomological suppliers and all original
geometric, theta, integral-metric and spectral data remain obligations.
The audit records their source interfaces. Closing a Fourier identity
does not construct a geometric family.

Independent review
------------------
Two independent component reviews examined WB1–WB8. They checked the
ramified contribution, signs, constants, uniformity and final analytic
application. Three clarity corrections were incorporated: the principal
ramp is defined for every positive argument, the factorial auxiliary k
is a positive integer, and epsilon/delta have their explicit positive
ranges. Review records and frozen proof hashes are in reviews_v0_23/.

Version 0.29: the remaining ordinary support producer

This is a dependency audit, not a new proof or an assertion of a gap in the supplied Lean development. It records the actual arithmetic producer that remains after the completed universal-ring and adjoint chains.

The exact source endpoint HidaUAlt chooses one ideal J for each original datum, coefficient ring, universal determinant ring, weight object and fixed embedding. The same J must satisfy all six HidaPropsAlt fields and carry the Taylor-Wiles module package HasAut. The KX construction supplies the actual universal objects and their maps, but none of these automorphic-support assertions follows just from their universality.

What the selected consumer needs

QX traces the two actual Link objects and their common coefficient threshold. Its PMEx construction can use rank-four HidaPropsAlt plus P1core on those exact objects; the broader P3core and the all-rank ModUAlt quantifier are not required by that selected construction. The final QX chapter gives the precise quantifier order and any separate descent inputs.

Why a nearby published theorem has not been substituted

Allen–Newton–Thorne, arXiv:1912.11269v2, Theorem 4.1, proves a closely related characteristic-ell support result under a residually Schur polarized problem, specified local/genericity/primitivity conditions, and ell>3 with ell not dividing n. The literal source interfaces do not state this whole hypothesis list. In particular, polarization of the sum permits its distinct constituents to be paired rather than individually polarized. No proof of the missing implications has been supplied here. The theorem is therefore recorded as a candidate for a narrower matched subcase, not as a proof of P1core.

Next complete producer to reconstruct

Construct the actual localized ordinary Hecke module, its commuting operators and exact determinant/character map, then prove all six properties for its kernel. For characteristic ell, reconstruct the original finite Taylor-Wiles module support argument, or supply a published theorem whose entire hypothesis list is proved on the two actual rank-four Link objects. A zero localized module gives J=top and is allowed in the source construction, but its automorphic-point membership assertion still has to be verified; it is not a shortcut to a useful support witness.

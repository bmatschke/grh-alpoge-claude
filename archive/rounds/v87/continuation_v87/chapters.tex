{\Large\bfseries The global denominator bound}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.87 \quad 25 November 2026
\end{center}

The last two member conditions in the denominator proof ask whether
the upward theta image contains the entire good upper eigenspace.
We fix an arbitrary nonzero vector in that space and prove that its
prescribed downward theta lift is nonzero. This proves the two
conditions simultaneously, even when a Hecke character occurs with
multiplicity. We then trace the norm, the bound and its quantifiers
back to the original family. The final sections start the separate
period and height estimate.

CW1 — The actual finite lattice and Heisenberg law
================================================

coefficient host.AA. The trace pairing contains the source xi0 and the half

self-duality. This is not the statement for an unscaled plain Hom lattice.
Local bases are sufficient even when the integral lattices are projective.

An integral w with w+bar(w)=1 is obtained from the trace ideal. At odd places
2 is invertible; at dyadic places the host's unramified quadratic algebra has

On a self-dual integral module the cocycle is trivial. This remains valid at
residue characteristic two, and makes w an actual integral half rather than

CW8 — Gaussian projectors and the smooth domain
==============================================

STATUS

This report proves elementary character, field-degree, window-quantifier,
and imprimitive-endpoint deductions. It does not prove the full PairEx or
VWinAt arithmetic inputs, rebuild the repository, or audit all axioms.
The fixed-positive-family analytic argument AN3 was proved in v0.3 and is
used here. The remaining source interfaces are stated explicitly below.

The most useful new simplification is the exact eventual constant

 c = lcm(ord(psi_odd), ord(psi_2(-1))).

It replaces an abstract finite stabilization argument and identifies the
source order as n=c*2^e. A second useful deduction is that the source's
apparently variable auxiliary modulus is exactly lcm(2^(e+2),M) for a
primitive target of conductor M. A third is that the prime pair can be
fixed for an entire rescaled analytic window.


GH1. WHAT THE EXPORTED THEOREMS ACTUALLY SAY

Statements.lean defines, for EVERY N>0 and every complex Dirichlet
character chi modulo N:

 (a) Re(s)>1/2 and (chi nonprincipal or s!=1) imply L(s,chi)!=0;
 (b) 0<Re(s)<1 and L(s,chi)=0 imply Re(s)=1/2.

Main.lean exports these as LFunction_ne_zero_of_half_lt_re and
LFunction_eq_zero_re; it also exports the primitive version and ordinary
RH. Its imports go through Supplements/Kernels/MainTheoremsAll,
MainTheoremsCritical, and MainTheorems. The latter takes allChi from
UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter, then calls the elementary
critical-line implications. No unrestricted number field, Hecke character,
Artin representation, or automorphic representation is quantified in the
headline GRH definitions. The use of automorphic inputs in the proof does
not change this scope.

Dirichlet GRH would, with the usual abelian Dedekind-zeta factorization,
also imply RH for zeta functions of abelian extensions of Q. That standard
corollary is different from an exported theorem for every number field;
we make no claim here of unrestricted Dedekind or automorphic GRH.

GH2. THE EXACT FIXED POSITIVE FAMILY

Fix a primitive psi of conductor M, with finite order o. For j modulo o,
let psi_j denote the unique primitive character inducing psi^j modulo M.
The map psi^j -> psi_j is injective: if psi_j=psi_k, inducing that common
primitive character back to the fixed modulus M gives psi^j=psi^k.
Thus the family has exactly o members, including psi_0=1 and psi itself.
It is closed under inversion since the primitive inducer of chi^{-1} is
the inverse of the primitive inducer of chi. The principal primitive
character has conductor one.

Level.ofSub has KEPT equal to the image under PChar.of of its character
subgroup, psK=none, and o equal to its subgroup cardinality. Its nontrivial
weights wt are therefore all (3o+1)^{-1}. Level.cyc takes the subgroup
generated by psi. Consequently, exactly, not only up to an error:

 H_psi(x)=G(x)+(3o+1)^{-1} sum_{j=1}^{o-1} Re G_{psi_j}(x).

The principal term is the ordinary centered zeta sum G; for a nonprincipal
primitive xi the centering in G_xi is x*(L'/L)(1,xi). The family is fixed
BEFORE epsilon, scale, auxiliary primes, or the cyclotomic tower varies.
All coefficients are strictly positive. Positivity at the analytic
endpoint is positivity of the multiplicities in a pole residue; it does
not mean each prime coefficient or each G_xi is positive.

AN3 from v0.3 applies to this finite family. A constituent zero rho in the
open strip has residue b_rho/[rho(1-rho)] in the Mellin transform, with
b_rho a strictly positive weighted sum of zero multiplicities. Thus
coincident zeros cannot cancel. The inverse-closed family also handles
nonreal targets, while positive real residues handle real target zeros.

The arithmetic estimate must have the quantifier order

 for each fixed psi, there exist A_psi>0 and epsilon_0>0;
 for every 0<epsilon<epsilon_0 and every lower threshold Y0,
 there exists Y>=max(2,Y0) such that
 H_psi(x)<=x^(1/2+A_psi*epsilon) for all x in [Y,Y^(3/2)].

In the current source comparison A_psi is eD+eL+7, with eD,eL fixed before
epsilon. For a proposed zero with beta>1/2, choose epsilon with
A_psi*epsilon<beta-1/2; then AN3 excludes it. No uniform A over all psi
is needed. The constants J, scale thresholds, and auxiliary-prime choices
may depend on psi and epsilon.


GH3. EXPLICIT HOST, AUXILIARY MODULUS, AND STABLE DEGREE CONSTANT

3.1 Field identification

Let T_e=Q(zeta_{2^(e+2)})^+ and K_psi=Q(zeta_M)^{ker psi}.
Let q and d be the source primes, q=3 mod8 and d=7 mod16,
with gcd(M,qd)=1. They are distinct, and both are 3 mod4.
In the common cyclotomic field of modulus Q*qd, Q=lcm(2^(e+2),M),
an exponent u fixes the compositum

 E_e=T_e K_psi(sqrt(-q),sqrt(-d))

if and only if all of the following hold:

 u=+1 or -1 mod2^(e+2), psi(u)=1, J_q(u)=1, J_d(u)=1.

The first condition fixes the real cyclotomic field, the second is the
definition of K_psi, and the last two follow from the quadratic subfields
of the qth and dth cyclotomic fields. Galois correspondence identifies E_e
with every field satisfying IsChiField, up to Q-isomorphism. This is also
the actual source host: KerChiAny proves the Artin kernel description,
and IsChiField.isChiField translates it to that fixed-field condition.

It is not necessary that K_psi be real. The source AnyGrp construction
allows odd as well as even characters. One must not use the ordinary
formula for the maximal real subfield unchanged when K_psi is complex.

3.2 Exact Q_S

The source first defines Q_S as the least divisor Q of
L=lcm(2^(e+2),M) such that 2^(e+2)|Q and the quotient character map
factors through units modulo Q. For the cyclic subgroup generated by
primitive psi, its annihilator is exactly ker psi.

Here is the short proof of the less immediate divisibility M|Q_S.
Put g=gcd(Q_S,M). If a unit u modulo M is 1 modulo g, generalized CRT
gives a unit z modulo L with z=u modM and z=1 modQ_S. The defining
property of Q_S then gives psi(u)=psi(z)=1. Thus psi factors through
units modulo g. Primitivity forces g=M. Hence M|Q_S. Combining the two
divisibilities into Q_S with Q_S|L gives Q_S=L.

This argument works for every e, not merely for eventual e. The source
records M|Q_S as KerChiAny.dvd_QS (and duplicates it as TwistAny.dvd_lemma_3).
The equality with the lcm is the elementary consequence recorded here.

3.3 Constant c: character-group proof

Write M=2^a M0 with M0 odd. CRT decomposes psi uniquely as
psi_2*psi_odd, where psi_2 has modulus 2^a and psi_odd has modulus M0.
Let

 u2=ord(psi_2), r=ord(psi_odd), eps2=ord(psi_2(-1)) in {1,2}.
 Then o=lcm(u2,r), and c=lcm(r,eps2) divides o.

Assume e>=1 and e+2>=a. Embed all characters in the character group of
units modulo L. The character group of K_psi is <psi>, of order o.
The character group of T_e consists of the even characters supported at
the 2-primary factor. Its intersection with <psi> contains psi^j iff

  (i) psi_odd^j=1, and (ii) psi_2(-1)^j=1.

Condition (i) is r|j, and condition (ii) is eps2|j. Thus the intersection
is <psi^c>, of order o/c. Finite abelian Galois duality now gives

 [K_psi intersect T_e:Q]=o/c,
 [T_e K_psi:T_e]=[K_psi:K_psi intersect T_e]=c,
 [T_e K_psi:Q]=c*2^e.

CRT at q and d shows that J_q and J_d are independent over the base
character group. Each new sign can be varied while all base coordinates
are fixed. Therefore [E_e:Q]=4c*2^e. Source frame.ord is the degree
before the two signs: n=c*2^e, whereas the full field degree is 4n.

This proof accounts for parity compensation. Evenness of psi means
psi_2(-1)*psi_odd(-1)=1, but a power supported only at 2 must FIRST have
trivial odd component. For such a power psi_odd(-1)^j=1, and the remaining
condition is exactly psi_2(-1)^j=1. Testing total parity while leaving an
odd-conductor component would give the wrong intersection.

3.4 Equivalent direct proof of the source order

OrdAny.ord_eq_mul identifies the source order with

 2^e * |image of xi on ker(cl)|,

where cl is the quotient of units modulo 2^(e+2) by {+1,-1}, and xi is
the quotient by ker psi. Once Q_S=L and e+2>=a, CRT identifies ker(cl)
with the independent choices of a 2-coordinate +1 or -1 and an arbitrary
odd coordinate. Its image under psi is generated by psi_2(-1) and the
image of psi_odd. Finite subgroups of C^times are cyclic, so that image
has order lcm(eps2,r)=c. This matches the exact source order, including
the case where the two generators have overlapping images.

The source proves merely that these image subgroups decrease and then
stabilize. The explicit proof supplies a usable threshold: e+2>=a.
For all the numerical size conditions appearing in FieldChi, one may
conservatively take e0=max(2,a,4M,64o). The latter two terms ensure
4M<=2^e and o^64<=2^e by the same elementary estimates in FieldChi.
This replaces the source's unnamed stabilization threshold; the complete
HostAx/FieldRows package is still supplied by its pinned constructor.

3.5 Small 2-adic exponents and examples

If a=0, the 2-factor has modulus one and is trivial: eps2=1, c=r=o.
If a=1, units modulo2 are trivial and again eps2=1. A primitive conductor
cannot have 2-adic valuation exactly1: its character would already factor
through M0. The formula remains correct for an imprimitive presentation
of such modulus, though our primary setup uses the conductor.
If a=2 and the character is primitive at2, the 2-factor is chi_{-4},
so eps2=2 and c=lcm(r,2). For a>=3 both even and odd primitive 2-factors
occur. The eventual threshold e+2>=a covers every case.

Examples (chi_D is the primitive quadratic Kronecker character):

 Target                                      o   c   [K_psi intersect T_e:Q]
 principal, M=1                              1   1              1
 chi_8, even primitive modulo8               2   1              2
 chi_{-4}, odd primitive modulo4             2   2              1
 chi_{-4} chi_{-3}, even modulo12             2   2              1
 quartic primitive modulo5                   4   4              1
 even quartic modulo16 times cubic mod7     12   3              4
 odd quartic modulo16 times cubic mod7      12   6              2

For the last two rows, units modulo16 are generated by -1 and3;
send 3 to i and -1 to +1 or -1. Both characters are primitive because
their value at9 is -1. Choose a primitive cubic character modulo7.
These are mixed examples where an odd-order part and a 2-power part of
the character order interact, rather than just quadratic special cases.

Because ord(psi_2) is a power of2 and eps2 is 1 or2, c has the same odd
part as r and hence as o. Thus the eventual frame order n is a power of2
exactly when o is a power of2. This does not by itself remove the other
cohomological inputs or make a general conductor behave like M=1.


GH4. TWIST CLASSIFICATION AND A PRECISE FOURIER IDENTITY

On the subgroup generated by psi,J_q,J_d there are exactly 4o characters:
psi^j J_q^i J_d^k with 0<=j<o and i,k in {0,1}. Distinctness follows by
varying the q and d coordinates independently using CRT, and then testing
powers of psi. In the actual source, TwistAny first restricts a frame
character trivial on its first factor to the A-coordinate. That
restriction is trivial on ker(xi), so descends to
B=(Z/MZ)^times/ker psi. The character group of B is precisely <psi>.
Its restrictions to the two remaining sign factors are each either the
trivial character or the sign. The source's q-factor is embedded with a
compensating A-coordinate; TwistAny's explicit decomposition shows that
the Artin values nevertheless become exactly psi^j J_q^i J_d^k.
Thus one should use that decomposition, not assume the displayed source
coordinates equal the three obvious Artin coordinates term by term.

For (n,2Mqd)=1, finite geometric sums give

 sum_j sum_i sum_k (-1)^k psi(n)^j J_q(n)^i J_d(n)^k
  = o*1_{psi(n)=1}*(1+J_q(n))*(1-J_d(n)).

It is supported exactly where psi(n)=1, J_q(n)=1, J_d(n)=-1, with
value 4o there. At n=p^m the same identity uses psi(p)^m and the mth
powers of the two signs. A support identity alone supplies no estimate
for the number or size of contributing primes.

This orthogonality is stated on units deliberately. At p|M the character
psi^0 modulo M is zero, whereas its primitive inducer, the conductor-one
principal character, equals1. Other powers may also lose ramification
when made primitive. Consequently the formula cannot simply be inserted
at every prime in the sum of the primitive G_{psi_j} without separately
tracking the finitely many local Euler terms. The source uses primitive
inducers and its explicit-formula bookkeeping, not an unqualified
all-integer identity of primitive character values.

BoundedTw supplies the exact separation needed for the analytic errors.
A source character in the bounded/omitted set cannot have (i,k)=(0,0):
in the plus part it would be the primitive inducer of a power of psi and
thus kept; in the minus part its value on iod would be simultaneously
-1 and +1. Therefore every omitted character is one of the genuinely
quadratically twisted characters covered by PairGood. This is a finite
classification proof; the asserted small G and logarithmic-derivative
bounds for those twists are not derived by the classification.


GH5. WHOLE-WINDOW QUANTIFIERS WITH ONE PRIME PAIR

Fix psi, c>=1, 0<epsilon<1/52, alpha=2+8epsilon, kappa=c^(1/alpha),
and e(t)=ceil(alpha log_2 t) for t>=1. Then
t^alpha<=2^e(t)<=2t^alpha. Assume PairEx and VWinAt(psi,401).

VWinAt supplies J>=1 and X0 after e(t) is chosen. PairEx at the fixed
modulus M and exponent J supplies a selection threshold Y1. Choose
X>=X0 with Y=X^(1/J) sufficiently large: at least Y1, the desired
initial window threshold, 1, kappa^3, and kappa^400. It may also exceed
any finite bounds needed for the host or the exponent e.

Choose q,d satisfying PairGood(M,epsilon,Y,Y^J)=PairGood(...,Y,X).
Now VWinAt gives W>=Y with W^2<=X, and the cohomological bound

 V(E_{e(t)},floor(t^401))<=exp(t^epsilon/2), W<=t<=W^2.

Set Z=kappa*W. For every x in [Z,Z^(3/2)], one has

 x<=Z^(3/2)<=W^2<=X, since W>=kappa^3;
 t=x/kappa belongs to [W,W^2];
 x^(1/J)<=X^(1/J)=Y<=W<=x;
 x^alpha=c*t^alpha;
 x^400=kappa^400*t^400<=t^401, since t>=W>=kappa^400;
 exp(t^epsilon/2)<=exp(x^epsilon/2).

Monotonicity of PairGood in its upper endpoint and of VVle in depth and
size gives exactly GH5 in the main fragment. The values Y,q,d were all
chosen before x. This is stronger in quantifier placement than the
displayed conclusion of WinPairs, but is exactly what its proof builds.

It is harmless to impose e(t)>=e0 for a fixed e0: require additionally
W>=2^(e0/alpha). Since 2^e(t)>=t^alpha>=2^e0, e(t)>=e0 follows.

Optional further interface deduction: for any fixed 1<lambda<2 the same
argument works on [Z,Z^lambda], replacing W>=kappa^3 by
W>=kappa^(lambda/(2-lambda)). The condition guarantees
(kappa W)^lambda<=W^2; the depth comparison is unchanged. This does not
claim that all downstream arithmetic interfaces have been generalized
to a different lambda. The source uses 3/2, and the main fragment keeps
that value. When c=1, kappa=1, OH's full square window is available
without any rescaling.


GH6. IMPRIMITIVE CHARACTERS, s=1, AND STRIP BOUNDARIES

Let chi modN be induced by primitive chi* of conductor f|N. The Euler
identity is

 L(s,chi)=L(s,chi*) product_{p|N, p not dividing f}
                         (1-chi*(p)*p^{-s}).

It holds as an identity of meromorphic functions, and as a value identity
where neither side is a principal pole. For Re(s)>0,
|chi*(p)*p^{-s}|=p^{-Re(s)}<1 at every displayed prime. Thus all extra
factors are nonzero in the open right half-plane. The source often
multiplies over every prime dividing N; primes already dividing f give
the factor1 because chi*(p)=0.

If chi is nonprincipal, chi* is nonprincipal: otherwise inducing the
principal character back to N would make chi principal. Therefore the
value identity remains valid at s=1, and classical nonvanishing of a
nonprincipal primitive L-function there gives the required nonvanishing.
The precise baseline invoked in the pinned repository is Mathlib's
DirichletCharacter.LFunction_ne_zero_of_one_le_re, with the nonprincipal
alternative supplied. DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/
CharFamilyLFunctionNeZeroOfOneLeRe.lean, declaration
Deduction.charFamily_LFunction_ne_zero_of_one_le_re, calls it in its last
line; its principal modulus-one branch calls
riemannZeta_ne_zero_of_one_le_re. We use the usual classical analytic
interpretation away from the principal pole, not the totalized value at1.
If chi is principal, it has a pole at s=1 in the usual analytic meaning,
and the exported right-half-plane statement explicitly excludes that
case. The source uses totalized complex-valued definitions and includes
riemannZeta_one_ne_zero internally; the human argument need not assign
an artificial finite value at the pole. No conclusion about a principal
L-value at its pole is being drawn here.

For 0<Re(s)<1, a zero of L(s,chi) is a zero of L(s,chi*) because the
Euler factors do not vanish. The completed primitive functional equation
is Lambda(s,chi*)=w(chi*) Lambda(1-s,(chi*)^{-1}), with a nonzero root
number and nonzero conductor powers. In this strip, the gamma factor
Gamma_R(s+a), a=0 or1 according to parity, has neither zeros nor poles.
Hence a zero reflects to one at 1-s for the inverse character. If the
first real part is below1/2, the reflected one is above1/2, which has
already been excluded for every primitive target. This proves the
all-character open-strip statement.

If an added Euler factor vanishes, taking absolute values forces Re(s)=0
(provided chi*(p)!=0, as at displayed primes). Such boundary zeros really
can occur, e.g. the principal character modulo2 has the extra factor
1-2^{-s}. Thus an unrestricted assertion that every zero of every
imprimitive L-function lies on Re(s)=1/2 would be false. Statements.lean
uses the open strip and thereby states the correct result.

The PairGood definition is slightly stronger than the minimum cyclic
classification needs: it quantifies over every Dirichlet character xi
modM, not just powers of psi. It asserts, for every nonzero quadratic
twist and each primitive inducing character chi, simultaneously on
Y<=y<=X,

 |G_chi(y)|<=y^(1/2+epsilon/2),
 |(L'/L)(1,chi)|<=4 log y.

Its real-character boxes range over primitive real rho of conductor
dividing8M and require nonvanishing for Re(s)>399/400 and

 |Im(s)|<=8M exp(Y^(epsilon/5))
              exp(Y^(epsilon/5)*(log Y)^2)^j.

The congruences and sizes of q,d are the same as OH, plus gcd(M,qd)=1.
PairEx makes these conditions available for every sufficiently large Y
and any fixed exponent A>=1 with X=Y^A. The finite Fourier calculation
does not prove this analytic selection theorem.

The full VWinAt input also remains. It quantifies over every PageN
exponent function, obtains J and X0, then for every X>=X0 and EVERY
WinPair at X^(1/J) supplies an intermediate square window. At each scale
it bounds all the finite cohomological lengths and every finite prime
subsum in VVle, not merely a selected cohomology group or a two-primary
part. VwinOfRow supplies this via automorphic_window_of_inputs, taking
the Galois row, BaseChangeIsobaricN, and the assembled local, automorphic,
Galois-cohomological, and CM inputs. This bridge has not been replaced by
the elementary host theorem.

The source's StmtFields makes some fixed-character dependence explicit:

 ordC(psi)=o;
 pK is the least prime >=7, 3 mod4, coprime to M;
 hStar is the class number of Q(sqrt(-pK));
 pOne is the least prime congruent to1 modulo the odd part of o,
       coprime to 2*M*pK;
 Bchi={3} union {odd prime divisors of o*M*pK*hStar*pOne}.

Its minusBound is

 3*n*t^2*log(tN)
 + C sum_{ell|o, ell odd prime} ell*n^2*t^2*log(t*ell*N)^2.

For ordinary RH the sum over odd divisors of o is empty and the previous
OH report identified the small reference data. For an arbitrary fixed
psi those constants are still fixed while e,q,d,scale vary, but they
must be carried through the arithmetic theorem. Even a power-of-two
character order only deletes this displayed odd-order sum; it does not
remove Bchi or all odd-primary cohomology obligations.

SAFE COMPRESSION BOUNDARY

The results above close the finite field/character identification, the
exact degree rescaling, the fixed-pair quantifiers, and the final
imprimitive/functional-equation transition. They leave precisely the
same deep local and global arithmetic mechanism as the ordinary
reconstruction, now with its fixed-character parameters. They supply no
new shortcut from a support identity or a two-primary host to the full
square-root estimate. A readable proof of the whole result still needs
the remaining polynomial and cohomological existence/quantitative inputs.

\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Patching at a point with two residual blocks}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.71 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

At an exceptional point the residual representation has two blocks,
and the off-diagonal deformations affect which primes can pass through
that point. We construct the universal matrices on its completed ring,
derive their relations from the limiting Selmer conditions, and apply
the resulting prime comparison to arbitrary chains. The final chapter
locates the remaining premise of the marked theorem in the construction
of the Hida ideal with its automorphy system.

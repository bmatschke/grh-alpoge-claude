1. The completed local entry

For the same original frame F.Ok(C0), coefficient DVR O and supplied
rank-n Space, n=2 or 3, ALC supplies full loc_cpl, loc_flat, types,
loc_rho and loc_ex. Its completion algebra also supplies the original
cpl_of_univ, cpl_is, cpl_uniq and c3a. The general coefficient-completion
statements allow arbitrary local O; the arithmetic applications retain
the original DVR, finite residue field and exact scalar towers.

At every original maximal x over mB, ALC21 first chooses the actual
finite residue field A/x, constructs a finite unramified O', and fixes
the residue map O' tensor_O A -> Res(O'). Its completion gives one Loc
before every later test algebra and ideal. All local/global comparisons
are through its actual iota:A->L.R. ALC22 installs that map in the
original ChartOfFlat consumer. No fresh local-existence assumption is
needed in ComponentsGoodRankTwo.lean:149–158 or
PropagationTheoremGL3.lean:150–161. Generic gr_A and cont_u were already
proved in RTC7/RTC9; UCE13's FinA-dependent analogue is not used.

The arbitrary-field classification in IrrOfConn.lean:103–190 is also
fully supplied. It does not require the target field to be finite or
presented as a coefficient residue field. Rank three uses its own
reflection/odd-entry pattern; no rank-two-only RTC17 argument is used.
The proof needs only division by 2, so the permitted Host ell=3 case
remains available.

2. Preferred next substantial target: actual local presentations

FrobeniusSmoothVectors/HasObs.lean:26–124 proves pres at the original
Ok premise, for both ranks and every supplied Loc. Its output is the
literal Pres record in Integral.lean:64–67: a surjective O'-algebra map

reconstruct a complete tangent/obstruction supplier or pres itself,
including the numerical difference; it should not rename all of
these bodies as a new presentation hypothesis.

3. Independent component-chain and dimension work

FrobeniusSmoothVectors/EnnrealVolumeRatioStepCancel.lean:24–51 gives
c1ch. For a complete Noetherian local ring with prime nilradical, an
original complete-DVR coefficient presentation, h elements in the
maximal ideal, and dimension at least D+1+h, it returns Chained of
their generated ideal at D. Its real inputs are the component and
connected-chain theorems in AlgebraTools/ConnectedPrimeFields/ClComp
and ClChain. This algebraic branch can proceed independently of the
arithmetic deformation presentation.

FrobeniusSmoothVectors/ChainedBot.lean:51–86 then consumes pres,
c1ch, the formal-power-series dimension and the triangular bound to
give full sharp2: Chained(0,2*d+1) and TI at every minimal prime of
the actual local ring, under F.Big and triP not contained in x.
The dimension lower bound for minimal primes is D+1, while the
intersection-chain bound is D; preserve this one-unit difference.

The numerical triangular supplier is CharpolyCoeffsInSubalgebra.lean:
50–84, l51. It bounds dim(L.R/triM) by
(n-1)*d+2+(3*C0+5), and returns triP=top. The latter is now ALC17;
the former still uses abelian generators, residual traces and the
actual fiber-dimension argument. TameInertiaCondition.lean:257–280,
l52, separately bounds a coincident-character prime over a supplied
FinLam ideal. KrullDimLtOfNotLeTriangular.lean:36–58 combines these
with the exact Big inequalities to obtain both strict bounds below
n*d. Both clauses are consumed by the original nice-prime argument.
Proving only the first triangular clause will not complete ld_ok.

4. A useful parallel incidence branch

FrobeniusSmoothVectors/LocDim.lean:110–178 gives the full local
dimension comparison. For every local prime it has an upper bound
from the transcendence degree of its contraction; for every prime
eta contained in x with prescribed finite transcendence degree t,
it produces a prime P with contraction eta and exact dimension
dim(B/pi(eta))+t. The completed loc_cpl/loc_flat inputs are now
available, but the dimension-theoretic bodies in its three imported
CommutativeAlgebraHeight files remain to be reconstructed.

FibreClosed.lean:45–58, MinHom.lean:26–30 and MonMemPow.lean:81–112
are concrete graded incidence suppliers used in the global argument.
Seedpt.lean:9–30 joins the last of these with loc_dim and
FrobeniusCornerLiftTwoToThree.lean:133–180. Despite that filename,
the latter theorem constructs a homogeneous prime eta in the SAME
rank-n A, over the prescribed p in B, with TI and transcendence degree
one. It is not the later map between the rank-two and rank-three
spaces. The seed endpoint chooses x first and then works for EVERY
Loc at x; retain that order instead of selecting one favorable Loc.

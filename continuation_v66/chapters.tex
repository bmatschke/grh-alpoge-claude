{\Large\bfseries The Case III seed}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.66 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The seed argument needs one residual representation that can be chosen
before the tower is enlarged. Two representations of dimension two
supply its four-dimensional lift. One comes from a cuspidal eigenform
congruent to an Eisenstein series. The other is induced from a character
of an imaginary quadratic field. We construct the factors, explain
why one character power works over all the required extensions,
and return to the residual and integral assertions of the original theorem.

\paragraph{W85M1. Both infinitesimal characters of an original selected member.}
Let $D'$ be a selected member, with its given realization as the local
layer of $D$. Write $M,A,\mathrm{Au}$ for the level family, automorphic
side and specialization in that realization. For every location $i$ and
every CM-type label $\rho$, put $j=j(i)-1$, $n=n_V$, and
$P=\mathrm{PP}(D',i,\rho)$. Then the infinitesimal weight at the two
embeddings above $\rho$, in the source order, is
\[
       \{x-\tfrac12:x\in P\},\qquad
       \{-(x-\tfrac12):x\in P\},
\]
as multisets in $\mathbb C$. This supplies the original
\texttt{GaloisPreconditions.weight}.

Indeed $1\le j(i)\le m$, so $j$ is a legal zero-based row.
Choose any Hecke point at $i$ and a representative $\pi$ above it by
point surjectivity. As in M84T7, the member's own specialization
identifies the class of this representative with the representation on
$\mathrm{Au}.S(i,\pi)$ with coefficient $M_i.\mathrm{wt}$. Take the
ordinary algebraic contragredient $a$ of that coefficient. The evaluation
pairing is nonzero and invariant. Its matrix entries are inverse-transpose
entries in algebraic coordinates, so this dual is again algebraic.
For every embedding $\tau$ and highest weight $\lambda$ of $a$ there,
the defining infinitesimal weight of the representative, followed by the
two comparison identities $A.\mathrm{au}.\mathrm{wt}$ and
$A.\mathrm{au}.\mathrm{infW}$, therefore gives
\[
 \mathrm{infWt}(i,\tau)=
 \{\lambda_s+(n-1)/2-s:0\le s<n\}.
\]
A highest weight exists at each embedding: over $\mathbb C$ the
unitary group has one $\mathrm{GL}_n$ factor per conjugate pair.
The algebraic representation extends from the Zariski-dense rational
points to this product. Its irreducibility there follows from
irreducibility on the rational points. An irreducible representation
of the product is an external tensor product, so its restriction to
each factor is isotypic and has a single highest weight.

At the chosen CM embedding $\tau=\mathrm{embT}(D',\rho)$ the member's
level input $\mathrm{mt}.\mathrm{wt}$ applies to $\mu(i),j,\rho,a,\lambda$
with shift $1/2$. The three resulting lists are $v_h-1/2$ for
$0\le h<k-1$, $-b_h-1/2$ for $0\le h<m$, and
$-e_j-1/2+(t-1)/2-c$ for $0\le c<t$, with parameters evaluated at
$\rho$. The last unshifted entry is $-e_j+(t+1)/2-(c+1)$.
Thus their multiset sum is precisely the first asserted list.

The other embedding is $\bar\tau$. In the unitary coordinates its
$\mathrm{GL}_n$ action is obtained from that at $\tau$ by inverse
transpose. This takes the triangular Borel to the opposite one.
Conjugating by the reversal permutation matrix brings it back and sends
the diagonal entries to their reversed inverses. Conjugation is inner,
so it identifies the highest-weight lines in the corresponding
irreducible factors. The highest weight at $\bar\tau$ is consequently
$\lambda'_s=-\lambda_{n-1-s}$. Its shifted entry at $s$ is the negative
of the shifted entry at $n-1-s$ for $\tau$. Reversal preserves
multiplicities and supplies the second list for the same $i,\rho$.

# The common set of primes and the grid of twist characters

Start with the [first-pass intermediate level](FIRST_PASS_CUSPIDAL_ROW.md#the-intermediate-level-on-the-first-pass). The next state needs a system of forms over the twist coefficient rings at **every** finite coefficient place. It also needs one grid of Hecke characters whose complex values agree with the local character data at the specified embeddings. This construction takes place on the eventual family selected by the advance field requests. In particular, inclusion in the prescribed subfield $L_0$ must be arranged before the fitted state is chosen. [Exact quantifiers][goal] · [Four conditions on the new data][settings]

## One field request for all the grids that can occur

Write $E/F$ for the CM extension of the host. At fixed dependence constant $c$ and size parameter $b$, use the scale $(1,c,b)$. The [grid construction](../construction/ROW_GRID_CHOICE.md) supplies the finite places and split halves, its characters, and the Hermitian spaces and lattices of its vertices. The rational prime $q_0$ is the prescribed least auxiliary prime of the preliminary data. Write $v_0$ for the selected place of $F$ above it, and $S_1$ for the places of $F$ above the two type primes and the two primes of the frame characters $\delta,\delta'$. The grid support is $S_1\cup\{v_0\}$. At the places over $S_1$ the auxiliary characters are trivial on Teichmüller units, with the specified conductor on principal units. At the chosen half over $v_0$ the conductor exponent is one. [Characters and the prescribed base place][grid] · [Characters and levels at the original primes](../construction/ROW_GRID_CHOICE.md)

A given grid requires finitely many roots of unity, one of exact order $(Nv)^{d_v}$ for each supported $v$ of $F$, where $d_v$ is its prescribed depth. It also requires the **whole** set of unramified uniformizer values of each grid character $\chi_a$, $1\leq a\leq m$, to lie in the chosen field. These infinitely many values still require only a finite extension. At the chosen CM embeddings the angular exponents of $\chi_a$ are $2e_{a,\tau}$ with $e_{a,\tau}\in\mathbf Z$. Thus the infinity character on a principal idele is a product of integer powers of $\tau(x)/\bar\tau(x)$. These values lie in a fixed normal closure of $E$. Pass to the ideal character modulo its conductor. On principal ray ideals it is determined by that infinity character. The ray class group is finite. Choose an ideal in each class and raise it to the order of the class. Its character value then has a positive power in the fixed number field. Adjoining the finitely many chosen values controls the character on every ideal prime to the conductor, hence at every unramified prime. Take the compositum over $a$ and adjoin the required roots of unity. This is the classical field-of-values argument for an algebraic Hecke character, applied here at twist exponent zero. [Uniformizer-value convention][values] · [Field and root conditions for one grid][field]

There is a second finiteness issue. The later state and its levels have yet to be specified when the first field request is made. Fix the preliminary base and group together the possible grids by their base place $v_0$. There are only finitely many such places because the rational prime $q_0$ depends only on those preliminary data. For each base place that occurs, choose one grid and one number field as above. On a new stage with that base place, keep its places, chosen halves and vertex lattices. Use the auxiliary characters of the representative. Its infinity, unramifiedness, conductor and Teichmüller conditions continue to hold: the support and depths are determined by $v_0$ and the preliminary data, and the conductor conditions at conjugate halves agree. The stored frame characters of the new level still enter the eventual twists. Taking the compositum of these finitely many number fields therefore works on every such stage. Add generators for the compositum to the first field request. On a base meeting it, the resulting embedding into $L_0$ induces exactly the original embedding into $\mathbf C$. This is the point that gives the required field for the whole range of later choices. [Replacement at the same base place][replace] · [Finite compositum over all possible places][compositum] · [Inclusion in the actual first field request][advance]

For coefficient characteristic $\ell$, a zero-based row $j$, and character index $a$, the resulting complex character is
$$
 \lambda_{\ell,j,a}=\mu_{0,j}\,\delta_{\ell}/\chi_{a+1}.
$$
Here $\delta_\ell$ is the frame's prescribed choice at $\ell$. The numerator uses the given level's frame. The ordered infinity weights give the required gap inequality for the full prescribed range of critical integers. At either $\delta$ prime the denominator is trivial on Teichmüller units, so the restriction of $\lambda_{\ell,j,a}$ there is the restriction of the numerator. The chosen prime over $q_0$ and the four marked rational primes satisfy the original splitting and distinctness conditions. [Character formula and Teichmüller law][lambda] · [Gap and place conditions][gap]

## One exceptional set for both constructions

Let $\mathfrak c_K$ denote the ideal called `ccK` in the host. Put
$$
 P_{\mathrm{low}}=\{2,q_0,g_0,g_1,p_\delta,p_{\delta'}\}
      \cup\{p:p\mid N\mathfrak c_K\}
      \cup\{p:p\mid |\operatorname{disc} E|\},\qquad
 S_x=\{w\text{ of } E:w\mid p\text{ for some }p\in P_{\mathrm{low}}\}.
$$
Each fibre is finite, and complex conjugation preserves the rational prime below a place. Thus $S_x$ is finite, contains **every** place in the indicated fibres and is stable under conjugation. It includes the old Hecke exceptional set by the fitted rationality input. Every prime of the auxiliary character conductor belongs to it, including the fibres of the chosen base place. [Rational prime lists][low] · [Full fibres and conjugation][sx] · [Use for the conductor and Hecke data][use]

The forms and the twist rings each ask for a finite local coefficient extension for each pair $(\ell,u)$, where $u$ is the prescribed coefficient embedding. In a common algebraic closure of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ take their compositum at that pair. It is still finite and meets both inclusion requirements. The source uses this same extension in the two slots of its ring construction. This choice is made **pointwise** in $(\ell,u)$ and works for all primes of $L_0$ with the relevant embeddings. Use the common $S_x$ in both constructions. Then the exceptional set $S_0(i)$ for a local twist index $i$ contains the old Hecke exceptions, and every place in $S_0(i)$ lies above $P_{\mathrm{low}}\cup\{\ell_i\}$. In particular the extra prime in this last inclusion follows the index. [Pointwise compositum, rings and forms][join] · [Proof of both support inclusions][support]

## Installing the forms and the character grid together

Use the [coefficient rings and class groups](FIRST_PASS_TWIST_RINGS.md) and the [integral forms](FIRST_PASS_TWIST_FORMS.md) at these same indices. Each full lattice is free over its local discrete valuation ring. Use an integral basis to test membership in the lattice by boundedness of all the coordinates, for both choices of side. This supplies the auxiliary coordinate data first for the empty character list and then for the list built from the grid. The forms, their central idempotent, Hecke readings and integral row representations enter both times with the same coefficients. The [toric coefficient argument](FIRST_PASS_TORIC_COEFFICIENTS.md) gives the two remaining comparisons. The Galois characters take values in the unit group over the finite local coefficient extension. The normalized sums over the fibres of the anticyclotomic quotient belong to the fraction field of the larger complete ring. Finite Fourier inversion in the latter argument involves the order of an $\ell$-group, so this descent gives precisely a field assertion. [Use of the two coordinate models][support]

For each required nonzero index, the list identifies one embedding over the prescribed $L_0$ reading and its conjugate-side reading. It contains a matching character for each row pair, each integer in the required critical range and each side. Conversely each entry comes with such indices, the conductor condition and the corresponding normalized toric values. This is the comparison called `IsLamTieFull`. It is recorded for the very grid whose field of values was requested above. [Both directions of the character correspondence][tie]

Finally install this twist datum on the existing pre-stage. Transport the fitted finite group identifications and its geometric and normalized row readings. The resulting intermediate state identifies the selector $P_q$, coefficient form, Hecke operators, eigenvalues and unipotent character with the transported originals. Consequently the comparison of forms, the two support inclusions and the complete character correspondence hold for those data. The level record also transports the grid: its old character values and roots of unity still have the same $L_0$ readings. This constructs the first subsequent level (`FirstIntermediateLevel`) together with its prescribed list (`ListSt`) on the first pass, under the inputs of the component proofs. The auxiliary coordinate models used here acquire their theta-series comparisons at the following stage.

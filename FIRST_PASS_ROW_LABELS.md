# Installing row labels at the first empty-localization stage

Let $x$ be the initial unlocalized state on the pinned weight-supported column. The [starting state](FIRST_PASS_STARTING_STATE.md) has empty localization index set $\mathrm{Loc}$, which permits new parameter and type data to be installed. The comparison required of the new state keeps the rank record, host, local period data $D.\mathrm{loc}$ at every coefficient characteristic, global algebraic closure with its field and algebra structures, all its chosen valuation rings, and both marked-place index types. The comparison permits the row-label polynomials and chosen tame generators to change. The lift of a real prime used in the final record can be taken to be the original column selection $P_q(v)=w.I.\mathrm{sel}(v)$. ## Tame labels for the actual row representations

Fix either marked prime $g=g_k$ and a selected place $w$ of the CM field above it. Its completion is $\mathbb Q_g$. Use the prescribed valuation ring $A_w$ in the stored algebraic closure. There is a residue identification with $\overline{\mathbb F}_g$ and a generator of tame inertia coupled to the frame's entire compatible system $\zeta_d\in\mathbb F_{g^d}^{\times}$ of generators. To see the simultaneity, put $D_n=(n+1)!$, choose $u_n^{g^{D_n}-1}=g$, and require
$$
 \overline{\gamma u_n/u_n}=\zeta_{D_n}
$$
under the residue identification. Finite tame Kummer theory makes each condition a nonempty closed subset of inertia. Indeed over the maximal unramified extension the roots of unity of order $g^{D_n}-1$ are available, and adjoining this root of the uniformizer is totally tamely ramified of precisely that degree. Inertia fixes the prime-to-$g$ roots of unity, so changing the root $u_n$ does not change the ratio. The divisibility $D_n\mid D_{n+1}$ and norm compatibility of the $\zeta_d$ make these conditions nested. Compactness gives one $\gamma$ satisfying all of them. Every integer coprime to $g$ divides some $g^{D_n}-1$. Hence the class of $\gamma$ generates every finite tame quotient. This is the generator chosen when the empty-localization stage receives its type frame. The frame's finite cuspidal character polynomial is supplied by the stated Green parametrization. [Coupled generator and fixed valuation][lab-gen] · [Its insertion into the Green frame][lab-pre]

For each actual row $\sigma$ there is a local factor of its finite automorphic row representation at $w$. The row depth calculation supplies a nonzero vector fixed by a conjugate of the principal level-one subgroup. Its local Langlands parameter $V_{\sigma,w}$ therefore has rank $r$ and trivial wild inertia, by the depth-zero local correspondence used in the pin. In the version used by the third-stage induction this applies the depth clause of the local correspondence on the actual completion identified with $\mathbb Q_g$. The choice of a representative of the local factor does not affect the parameter because the row determines its local isomorphism class. At unconstrained primes the source completes its parameter family by the rank-$r$ unramified parameter if there is no split realization. The marked places all have actual local realizations. [Depth of the actual primitive rows][lab-depth] · [Installing the split parameters][lab-row] · [Depth clause on the marked rational completion][lab-true]

Let $\tau:\overline{\mathbb F}_g^\times\to\mathbb C^\times$ be the specified Teichmüller map and $V$ any Weil–Deligne parameter on the chosen valuation. On its wild-inertia invariants the eigenvalues of $\gamma$ are roots of unity of order prime to $g$. Write their multiset as $\{\tau(a_i)\}_i$. Frobenius conjugacy permutes the multiset by raising it to the $g$th power, since the residue norm here is $g$. Thus
$$
 L_{V,w}(X)=\prod_i(X-a_i)\in\mathbb F_g[X].
$$
This is the unique monic polynomial whose multiset of roots lifts by $\tau$ to the given eigenvalues. Its degree is the dimension of the wild-inertia invariants and its constant term is nonzero. On every actual row it therefore has degree $r$ and the characteristic polynomial of $\gamma$ is exactly $\prod_i(X-\tau(a_i))$. This is an equality of polynomials with multiplicities. The roots can lie in extensions of $\mathbb F_g$, and this calculation works with an arbitrary monodromy operator. [Stability and the unique residue polynomial][lab-poly] · [Degree criterion and recovery of the inertia polynomial][lab-degree]

For the conjugate CM place $\bar w$, extend CM conjugation to an automorphism $c_0$ of the stored algebraic closure. Its image of $A_w$ and the already chosen $A_{\bar w}$ both prolong $\bar w$. Conjugacy of prolongations gives $s\in G_E$ so $c=s c_0$ carries the first valuation ring to the second. Store $\gamma_{\bar w}=c\gamma_w c^{-1}$. For fixed $k$ the marked places are distinct and no two are CM conjugate, so these assignments preserve all marked choices. Away from them and their conjugates extend by the identity. A label at the conjugate place is then the evaluation of its *own* local parameter on this transported generator, exactly the comparison required for every parameter at that place. The comparison uses the conjugate valuation and transported generator on that same row. [Transport of valuation rings and generators][lab-conj] · [Insertion of labels and original row parameters][lab-install]

## The ambient representations on the empty-localization stage

Fix the installed dictionary between the stored local parameters and Weil–Deligne isomorphism classes. The ambient automorphic data and their spherical comparisons can be constructed on this stage for the prescribed local Langlands correspondence. The construction uses coordinate models of algebraic weights and the actual automorphic representations. It concerns the label of every such representation. It imposes the spherical formula at the places and on the vectors described below. [Coordinate models and isomorphism classes][lab-model] · [Statement on the empty stage][lab-au]

For the rank $n=n_V$, assign to an automorphic representation the set of its isomorphic coordinate models. Changing a basis of its finite-dimensional algebraic weight gives a model in this set, transporting both the coefficient action and its automorphic subspace. Two such sets agree exactly when the original representations are isomorphic, and choosing one representative in each set recovers every label. Construct the weight labels by the same procedure. On the representatives record multiplicity, sphericity and the class of the contragredient algebraic weight. An intertwiner transports equivariant maps into the automorphic functions bijectively, by conjugation on the domain and on the coefficient values. It therefore preserves the multiplicity. It also transports vectors fixed by the local maximal compact. The local chart can vary at an inert place. Transporting the spherical Hecke algebra with it preserves its eigencharacter and the Satake description. When an inert Satake tuple is given, choose a witness on the representative and carry it back to the original inert datum. The same finite multiset then serves both. At a real embedding take the decreasing highest weight $\lambda_0\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_{n-1}$ of the labelled algebraic weight. The $n$ numbers $\lambda_i-i+(n-1)/2$ are distinct and lie in the required translate of $\mathbf Z$. Uniqueness of the dominant highest weight makes this reading independent of the coordinate model. [Isomorphism classes and sphericity][lab-quot] · [Multiplicity by transport][lab-mult] · [Change of the inert chart][lab-inert] · [Satake and weight comparisons][lab-sat] · [Highest-weight shift][lab-inf]

At a split chart use the local representation class of the representative and take its local Langlands parameter, transported back by the fixed dictionary. Uniqueness of the local component under change of chart and isomorphism makes this agree with any actual realization there. On unconstrained entries the record uses its prescribed default. The embeddings indexed by the auxiliary localization set have empty domain. This defines the ambient functions on the same state as the row labels. [Definition on every place][lab-total] · [Recovery of an actual local component][lab-recover]

The spherical comparison has a specific choice of half and twist. Take a split chart over $v$, the CM place $w$ conjugate to its selected half, an allowed uniformizer and a level whose subgroup supported at $v$ is hyperspecial. Let a nonzero automorphic vector $f$ fixed by that subgroup have normalized Hecke eigenvalues $c_k$ for $1\le k\le n$. Write $q=\mathrm Nw$, and $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n$ for the Frobenius eigenvalues of the local parameter **at $w$** after twisting by $(1-n)/2$. Then this parameter is unramified and
$$
 \prod_{i=1}^n(X-\alpha_i)=X^n+
 \sum_{k=1}^n(-1)^k q^{k(k-1)/2}c_k\, X^{n-k}.
$$
Indeed the conjugate chart realizes the unique local component at $w$. Express $f$ in its isotypic part as a finite sum of images of spherical vectors, by averaging their preimages over the hyperspecial. At least one is nonzero. Local Langlands takes this spherical component to its unramified parameter. On each such vector the normalized spherical Hecke computation gives $q^{k(k-1)/2}T_k=e_k(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n)$ as a scalar operator. The defining double coset has finitely many right cosets, so the same identity holds on the sum. Since $f\ne0$, its two eigenvalues agree. Viète's formula gives the polynomial. The conjugate half and the twist are those used in the actual automorphic record. Together with the multiplicity, weight, spherical and local-component comparisons, this proves its ambient assertion $\mathrm{AUTrue}$ on the empty-localization stage under the indicated correspondence and spherical inputs. [Action on the fixed vectors and Viète's identity][lab-frob] · [Assembly of the ambient data][lab-au]

## The cuspidal character family and the two frame characters

For the third-place-series construction, use its specified local reciprocity theorem and its stated local laws on the eligible representations. In each positive rank take the actual unitary cuspidal general-linear representations all of whose local components meet the prescribed condition. The inclusion into the host cuspidal classes is injective, and a positive-rank class satisfying that condition provides its own preimage. Rank zero has no classes. A twist by a unitary Hecke character preserves the condition at every place by the toral twist law. Define the local parameter by applying the given reciprocity map to the local component at the stored valuation and using the dictionary to return it to the state. The resulting dimension, almost-everywhere unramifiedness and twist law follow from the local laws on precisely these eligible classes. Infinity parameters and conjugate self-duality are those of the actual automorphic representation. This constructs the cuspidal record on the stage with empty auxiliary localization set. [Admissible local classes, character twists and unramified parameters][lab-twistlaws] · [Construction from the actual cuspidal classes][lab-cuspclasses]

In rank one every unitary Hecke character gives an eligible class. The local component acts through the character on $\mathrm{GL}_1(E_w)$, and the rank-one case of local reciprocity identifies its parameter there. Its inertia is trivial exactly when the character is trivial on $\mathcal O_w^\times$. At such a place the sole Frobenius eigenvalue in the pin's convention is $\chi_w(\varpi_w)^{-1}$. It is independent of the uniformizer because $\chi_w$ is trivial on units. Character multiplication gives the twisting operation. For the specified angular infinity type the two conjugate embeddings have singleton weights $a_\tau$ and $-a_\tau$, and conjugate self-duality is the ordinary character relation $\chi^c=\chi^{-1}$. This identifies the arithmetic data as well as the isomorphism classes in rank one. [Rank-one correspondence and inverse Frobenius value][lab-rankone] · [The character realization on the same data][lab-chars]

Take the stored frame character $\eta$ and its inverse. Both are unitary, and each local component of each is unramified. Their uniformizer values are reciprocal, so the formula just proved gives reciprocal Frobenius eigenvalues. At a nonsplit prime the frame law gives $\eta_w(\varpi_w)=-1$, hence Frobenius also has value $-1$. At the conjugate halves of a split prime the two frame values multiply to one, which gives the same product for the two Frobenius eigenvalues. The character identification sends the two selected automorphic classes to precisely $\eta$ and $\eta^{-1}$. It also retains the original divisibility at the marked places since their frame is unchanged. [Two frame characters in the specified normalization][lab-eta] · [Installation after the row labels][lab-cgstep]

- **CVI1 and CVI2.** The weak Case V guard gives the strict conductor bounds required by HostData and the non-strict bound in XData, with the original exponent, conductor and order. The frame uses the actual quotient, characters, stored places and Frobenius at every selected decomposition group. The local separating element from AFE10 covers every marked prime, including the coefficient prime. Class one uses the full conjugate-inertia condition on the Gaussian kernel and the trivial ideal class group of the Gaussian integers.
- **CVI3.** At a marked place, the scalar witness with trivial mod-ℓ cyclotomic character rules out invariants for each nonzero weight and its Tate dual. At weight zero the independent cyclotomic automorphism fixes the quadratic field. The obstruction to surjectivity of the global degree-two restriction in the Poitou–Tate sequence thus vanishes at every weight. The trace pairing identifies the finite-field linear dual with the prime-field linear dual after restriction of scalars. These are the native local and global groups of HostLocVan and HostCount.
- **CVI4.** The local kernel condition for all non-distinguished places agrees with unramified cohomology after averaging over the prime-to-ℓ inertia quotient. At the distinguished place the negative, zero and positive weights impose coboundary, unramified and unrestricted conditions. The exact sequence obtained from the defining local primitives of the degree-two obstruction gives the claimed Euler difference. Substitution of the local dimensions and complex-conjugation invariants gives −1 at weight −1, 1 at weights 1 and 2, and zero elsewhere, including weights outside the matrix grading.
- **CVI5 and CVI6.** The adapted variables, including diagonal entries, use differences from the marked base representation. The common trace test has weight zero and vanishes at that base point. The presentation hypotheses, algebraic tests and quotient-derivation injection are those used in AbstractCount and AbsCoord. The empty list when there are no weight-minus-one variables is allowed. The valuation normalization introduces one ramification factor and VLT3 cancels it. The exponent is selected before the varying host parameters and the separating element before the finite list.

The conclusion of CVI5–6 is conditional on the specified comparisons between the marked universal deformation and the weighted-coordinate model. Earlier results on general local presentations, the one-character model and the abstract algebraic intersection bound supply pieces of this argument. They do not identify all the native tangent, point and orbit comparisons and the integral coordinate injection on this single marked representation. The whole obligations `TASK_COUNT_V_INSTALL` and `HOST_FRAME_LENGTH` therefore stay open.

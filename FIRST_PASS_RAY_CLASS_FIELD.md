# The ray class field on the first-pass state

On the prescribed empty-localization state, both type primes $g_0,g_1$ split completely in the same finite field
$$
 H=K_{\mathfrak m}\subset\overline{\mathbf Q},\qquad
 K=j_E(\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-d})),\qquad
 \mathfrak m=M\mathcal O_K,\qquad M=Q_S qd .
$$
Here $j_E$ is the frame's given embedding of the CM host $E$. The ray extension is over its distinguished **imaginary quadratic subfield**. It occupies the parameter-field slot of the state. The [first-pass coefficient-field construction](FIRST_PASS_FIELDS.md) uses a separate ray extension over $E$. The absolute discriminant of $H$ is supported on the rational primes dividing $M$. The field, the two residue comparisons and the labelled local parameters below supply the ray and parameter records on the original character frame. [Host, modulus and discriminant][ray-host] · [Field and parameter requirements][ray-record]

## The two prescribed primes

For each $k=0,1$, the arithmetic context supplies an algebraic integer $\pi_k\in\mathcal O_E\cap K_{\rm host}$ with $\pi_k\overline{\pi_k}=g_k$ and $\pi_k-1\in M\mathcal O_E$. Identifying the host with its image under $j_E$, one has $\mathcal O_E\cap K=\mathcal O_K$. Both $\pi_k$ and $(\pi_k-1)/M$ therefore lie in $\mathcal O_K$. Complex conjugation on the embedded host extends to an automorphism of $\overline{\mathbf Q}$, so these very elements satisfy the source's norm-ray condition. In particular $g_k\equiv1\bmod M$. The state obtains the witnesses by restriction from its given arithmetic context. The first-pass condition called `Forced` places the two primes on a list. The stronger norm and congruence conditions come from the context's `gnorm` clause. This distinction preserves the actual type primes, together with the already chosen places of $E$ above them. ## Identifying the field defined by splitting

The pinned definition takes the compositum of **every** finite field $L\subset\overline{\mathbf Q}$ containing $K$ in which every norm-ray rational prime splits completely. There is no a priori normality condition on $L$. We prove that this compositum is precisely the embedded ray class field $H$. Existence and ideal reciprocity give $\operatorname{Gal}(H/K)\simeq\operatorname{Cl}_{\mathfrak m}(K)$, sending an unramified prime ideal to arithmetic Frobenius. Its kernel consists of principal ray ideals. There are no real places of $K$. We have $\operatorname{disc}(K)=-d$, and $H/K$ is unramified outside $\mathfrak m$. The discriminant formula in the tower $H/K/\mathbf Q$ gives the absolute ramification assertion. Since $\mathfrak m$ is stable under conjugation, conjugation preserves the principal ray subgroup. Uniqueness in class field theory now shows $H/\mathbf Q$ is Galois. Its degree is $2\,|\operatorname{Cl}_{\mathfrak m}(K)|$. [Pinned splitting definition][ray-sup] · [Artin, *Beweis des allgemeinen Reziprozitätsgesetzes*, pp. 353–363][ray-artin] · [Milne, *Class Field Theory*, V.4.6, V.5.3 and V.5.5][ray-cft]

For a norm-ray prime $p$ the defining condition reads $\pi\sigma(\pi)=p$, with $\pi\in\mathcal O_K$, $\pi\equiv1\bmod\mathfrak m$, and $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbf Q}/\mathbf Q)$. The identity restriction of $\sigma$ to $K$ would give $\pi^2=p$, which an imaginary quadratic field cannot satisfy. Thus $N_{K/\mathbf Q}\pi=p$, and $p\equiv1\bmod M$. In particular $p\nmid M$, so $p$ is unramified in $K$. The two ideals $(\pi)$ and $(\bar\pi)$ have norm $p$ and are distinct. Both generators are $1\bmod\mathfrak m$, so both Frobenius elements in $H/K$ are trivial. It follows that $p$ splits completely in $H/\mathbf Q$. Conversely, for $p\nmid M$ splitting completely in $H$, each prime $\mathfrak p$ above $p$ in $K$ has norm $p$ and has trivial ray class. Write $\mathfrak p=(\pi)$ with $\pi\equiv1\bmod\mathfrak m$. Its generator is integral because $\mathfrak p$ is integral, and $N_{K/\mathbf Q}(\pi)=p$ because the imaginary quadratic norm is positive. This gives the converse norm-ray witness. In particular every completion of $H$ over either prescribed $g_k$ is $\mathbf Q_{g_k}$. [Forward splitting criterion][ray-split] · [Reverse ray generator][ray-reverse]

Suppose an admissible $L$ is not contained in $H$. In a finite normal extension $N/\mathbf Q$ containing $H$ and $L$, choose $s\in\operatorname{Gal}(N/H)$ that moves an element of $L$. Chebotarev supplies a rational prime $p\nmid M$ unramified in $N$ and a prime of $N$ at which the Frobenius is $s$. It splits completely in $H$, hence is norm-ray. Complete splitting in $L$ would make every orbit of $s$ on the embeddings $L\hookrightarrow N$ a singleton, contradicting its action on the inclusion. Thus every admissible $L$ lies in $H$. The forward criterion includes $H$ among the admissible fields, proving the desired equality inside the fixed algebraic closure. Chebotarev here distinguishes fields. The witnesses for $g_0,g_1$ were already available before this argument. [Pinned maximality and field identification][ray-max] · [Tschebotareff, *Mathematische Annalen* 95 (1926), pp. 191–228][ray-cheb]

## The local comparison and the existing frame

For a coefficient place $\lambda$ of residue characteristic $\ell$, choose an embedding of the actual coefficient field into $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ inducing $\lambda$. An abstract isomorphism $\iota_\lambda:\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbf C$ can carry this embedded coefficient field to its specified complex embedding. Indeed, over the identified number field the two algebraically closed fields have the same transcendence degree, so a bijection of transcendence bases extends to an isomorphism. Transport the unit ball through $\iota_\lambda$ and identify its residue field with $\overline{\mathbf F}_\ell$. Its intersection with the coefficient field consists exactly of the elements of nonnegative $\lambda$-valuation. Using the **existing** lift $\tau_k:\overline{\mathbf F}_{g_k}\to\mathbf C$, define $\iota_{k,v,\lambda}(x)=\operatorname{red}_\lambda(\tau_k(x))$ at every marked $v$. Each nonzero $x$ has finite order, so $\tau_k(x)$ is a unit. At zero its value is in the unit ball as well, since its image is idempotent. This gives the required multiplicative map. If nonzero $x,y$ have orders prime to $\ell$ and the same reduction, $z=\tau_k(x)/\tau_k(y)$ lies in $1+\mathfrak m_\lambda$ and has order dividing an $n$ prime to $\ell$. The sum $1+z+\cdots+z^{n-1}$ reduces to $n\ne0$, so $z^n=1$ forces $z=1$. Injectivity of the prescribed lift on nonzero elements gives $x=y$. This also covers $\ell=g_k$. [Places and their unit balls][ray-place] · [The prescribed prime-to-$\ell$ injectivity][ray-red]

Take the parameter labels to be ordered lists of distinct blocks $(a,\pi,b)$ with $a,b\ge1$, $\pi$ a conjugate-self-dual cuspidal label of rank $a$, $\sum ab=r$, and the prescribed monic degree-$r$ inertia polynomial at each marked place. Here distinctness concerns the pair $((a,\pi),b)$. At $w$ the stored local parameter is the direct sum of the parameters of $\pi_w$ with twists $(b-1)/2-u$, $0\le u<b$, over the blocks. It has rank $r$ and its marked polynomial is obtained by applying the existing label to that very parameter. Thus the parameter identification and the field identification over $j_E(K_{\rm host})$ can both be taken to be identities. The construction copies the tame generators, Teichmüller lifts, finite series idempotents, both central actions, and the character frame $(P,W,\mu_0,\eta,\chi_\Psi,\delta,\delta')$. The old inertia labels, conjugate labels and character readings consequently apply to the same inputs. On the empty-localization state the construction sets the parameter-indexed ambient and row subspaces to zero at this step. Their later occurrences and the filtered-dimension comparison require separate arguments.

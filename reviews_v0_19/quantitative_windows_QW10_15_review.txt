QW10: WEIGHTED SUM TO THE SAME NORM-PRIME SET
The prime contribution uses the actual LFam support implication for lambda_0:
a prime with nonzero lambda_0 must satisfy that family's NormOne(d,M,p).
Nonnegativity and the summed von Mangoldt bound imply lambda_0(p)<=2 log p.
The nonprime remainder consists only of prime powers of exponent >=2 and is
bounded by 2(psi(Y)-theta(Y)) after extending the interval to (0,Y]. The stated
Chebyshev O(sqrt(Y)) bound follows from theta(t)<=C t and the displayed split
of the j=2 term and j>=3 terms; it does not import a prime-number-theorem error
estimate. The original floor convention, upper coefficient 2 log Y, and
same-family arithmetic support are preserved. PASS.

QW11: EXACT CONDITIONAL SHORT-NORM REDUCTION
The arithmetic LFam supplier and its uniform weighted lower bound remain
explicit inputs. With B=4dM^2 and K=8(|C|+1)/e, the chosen c forces
s=sqrt(log X)>=2(1+A)+2+K. It yields B^A<=exp(A s), h>=exp(-s),
and K sqrt(X)<=hX/B^A. The inequality exp(t)>=1+t makes the first exponential
comparison quantitative with no unstated asymptotic threshold. Since
Y=X(1+h)<=2X, log Y<=2 log X and sqrt Y<=2 sqrt X, the prime-power error
is at most half of the weighted main term. Dividing by 4 log X gives exactly
c'=e/8, matching the literal source. All divisions are legitimate because
log X>=1. The proof is a conditional construction of ShortNorm, not a proof
of its retained analytic or arithmetic input. PASS.

QW12: UNIFORMITY IN THE ORIGINAL LEVEL WINDOW

QW14: NONVANISHING AT ONE WITHOUT A HIDDEN ABSCISSA ASSUMPTION
The argument explicitly retains classical analytic continuation: all
nonprincipal Dirichlet L-functions are entire; the principal factor has only
its simple pole at 1 and vanishes at -2. If one nonprincipal L(1,chi) vanished,
the finite character product would be entire and zero at -2. Its Euler factor
at a prime with residue-class order d is (1-T^d)^(-|G|/d), so its Dirichlet
coefficients are nonnegative and its first coefficient is 1. For any real
sigma>1, absolute convergence in a smaller right half-plane justifies every
derivative. The entire Taylor series evaluated at sigma-(sigma+2) has only
nonnegative terms, including constant term F(sigma)>=1, contradicting its
value zero at -2. This is a complete entire-function/positivity contradiction;
it does not invoke an unproved lower bound on an abscissa or a zero-free
interval to prove the nonvanishing. Small moduli with no nonprincipal
characters cause no exception. The conclusion is solely L(1,chi)!=0. PASS.

QW15: THE LITERAL FINITE-CONDUCTOR, REAL-VARIABLE NoExc
For each nonprincipal character, continuity at the nonzero value at 1 supplies
a positive interval with no real zero. Taking a minimum over finitely many
characters and finitely many positive conductors <=N0 gives the literal
quantifier order forall N0 exists c0>0. The principal character is excluded;
its assigned harmless constant is not used to assert a nonexistent value at
its pole. N0=0 is vacuous and can use c0=1. The statement concerns real beta
only, with a constant depending on N0. It gives neither an effective rate nor
a single all-conductor margin, and it cannot replace the weighted short-
interval input. These exact limits are stated. PASS.

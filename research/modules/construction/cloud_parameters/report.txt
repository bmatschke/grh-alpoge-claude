supplies FramesOK from the stated OrbitHyp. The actual compatible

The inequalities needed here are

  p>=13, r>=2, Y>=r, N1+3r<=k,
  256 k² N Y² <= p^(L1-1),
  C L <= Dp, 8(Dp+1)<=c_*,
  C k³ r² N Q⁵ cP Y <= a.                              (1)

The orbit/frame conditions imply N>=1, N1>=1, G'>=1 and L1>=1.
Thus k>=7, Q>=p and cP>=1. For C>=100, the last inequality gives
a>=max(2,p,k,4N,r), and consequently h>=1 and L>=1. For example,
all factors other than Q⁵ in its product are at least one, so a>=Q⁵>=p;
similarly k³N>=4N and k³>=k. Also

  cP >= c_* >= 8(Dp+1) > 8 C L.                        (2)

The slack is the INTEGER floor

  s(B)=floor(B/(Yk²)),

not the real quotient. If B>=a/Q² and u=B/(Yk²), (1) gives

  u>=C k r² N Q³ cP>=2, hence floor(u)>=u-1>=u/2.

Moreover
p^(L1+1)>=256p²k²NY²>=48rk²Y, since p,N>=1 and Y>=r.
It follows that

  3r p^(-L1-1) B <= u/16 <= s(B)/8.                   (3)

If B>=a/Q, then u>=C k r² N Q⁴ cP>=100r²cP. The latter
number is an integer, so floor(u)>=100r²cP with no rounding loss:

  s(B)>=100r²cP.                                      (4)

These prove precisely the two scalar implications used in
LeOfOneLeMul.numAt_of and MembTop.mfh_of. More generally, whenever
p^D<=Q, 1<=Qj<=Q, dj<=3a p^(-D-L1-1) and B>=a/(p^D Qj),
the latter B is at least a/Q², and

  r dj/Qj <= 3r p^(-L1-1) B <= s(B)/8.

This is the exact universal hII5 premise. Equation (4) is hII0.
The source derives them from broader CountingSumsFrames parameter

2. GEOMETRY SUPPLIES HYP26 AND THE OUTGOING MARGINS [CP2]

Write f_{j,i} for the actual frame coordinates, z_j=f_{j,r-1},
D_j=z_j-f_{j,0}, g_j=z_{j+1}-z_j, P_j=p^{g_j}. For the cut used

  B >= a/p^E, E=z_{j+1}-f_{j,0}=D_j+g_j <= G'.

For cyclic indices, use the representative of j+1 shifted by n' when
needed. Both its last frame coordinate and the preceding first frame
coordinate receive the same shift. Thus E=D_j+g_j also at the wrap;
one must not compare unshifted representatives from opposite ends.
This is the content of seg_shift and Bo_ge.

Every intermediate letter x of segment j has x+L2<f_{j,0}. This
is stronger than the old-frame-distance condition alone and is why
the full FramesOK construction was necessary. If d_x is its plane
digit-block degree, BlockOK gives

  p^(z_j-x) d_x <= d_(x mod n') <= 2a.

The integers z_j-x are distinct and at least D_j+L1+1. Therefore

  dLL_j=sum_intermediate d_x
      <= 2a sum_{e>=D_j+L1+1}p^(-e)
      <= 3a p^(-D_j-L1-1),                            (5)

using p>=3. With B>=a/(p^{D_j}P_j), (3) proves
r dLL_j/P_j<=s(B)/8. From (4), r>=2 and
26r<=25r², one also has 26r cP<=s(B)/4. Hence

  r dLL_j/P_j+26r cP <= 3s(B)/8.                       (6)

The actual signed-chain slack at this next cut is 3s(B)/8, and its
c is cP. This is every clause of SignedChainData.Hyp 26, for each
segment, each carry index and each chain cut. The carry exponent is
the same P_j for all the r frame indices; it is not a new independent
choice of powers.

For any non-old-frame letter, Bo_ge and the full segment bound again
give B>=a/Q. Equation (4) gives s(B)>=100r²cP>=400cP>=128cP.
MFH has only the following other fields: p is prime, r>=2, FramesOK,
the selected degree equals the flat degree, and the degree is zero

3. THE 20 AND 9 BUDGETS, WITH LITERAL INDEX COUNTS [CP3]

The true letters are the intermediate letters and current-frame
letters, once each in a single period; the late-position map is
injective modulo n'. Hence their number is at most N. This is the
same periodic geometry established in LA/FG. Put nL_j for the number
of intermediate letters. Then

  |TL|=sum_j(nL_j+r)<=N,
  sum_j(nL_j+1)<=N, J(r-1)<=N,
  #{normal cuts}=|TL|+J<=2N.                           (7)

The plane and normal digit-block degrees are nonnegative and each
at most 2a, by the same BlockOK equality. For
lg(p,r,b)=1+log_p(b+r), we have b+r<=3a<=a³ for b<=2a.
Also 2 sum_x d_x+r<=4Na+r<=a²+a<=a³. Since h>=1,

  lg(p,r,d_x)<=4h,
  lg(p,r,2sum_xd_x)<=4h.

In the literal source definitions,
eP=r²lg(p,r,2sum d), Dm=(sum_j(nL_j+1))eP,
Dl=J(r-1)eP and kp=sum_x r lg(p,r,d_x). From (7),

  Dm<=4L, Dl<=4L, 0<=kp<=4L,
  MD=Dm+Dl+3kp<=20L.                                  (8)

We could use the slightly smaller kp<=4Nrh, but retain the source's
coarser common witness. Since d_t<=2a<=a² on TT and is zero off TT,

  kappa_fl=sum_t k ilog_p(d_t)<=2Nkh<=20L.              (9)

The integer logarithm of zero is set to zero here, as in Nat.log.

For the normal joint projector, the literal exponent is

  Dno=sum_cuts binom(K,2)
            ilog_p(2sum_{x in cut}dNo_x+K-1).

The logarithm argument is <=4Na+k<=a²+a<=a³, and binom(K,2)<=k².
There are at most 2N cuts. Thus Dno<=6L. For each plane or normal
multinomial factor, Legendre's formula bounds the factorial deficit
by n ilog_p(d_x), where n=r or K. Summing gives deficits at most
3Nrh and 3NKh; taking half of each plus Dno gives at most 9L.
This is exactly SizeAt 9. It bounds the coefficient-selection cost,
not the dimension of a new family.

Finally s(B)<=B/(Yk²)<=B/2 and B>0, so 3s(B)/8<B/2.
Together with cP>=1, J>=1, (8), (9) and (2), all of SizeStep,

6. NORMAL-DEGREE TRUNCATION, Ct0=10 [CP6]

The selected-symbol theorem RK, after EA's explicit parameter matching,
applies to every chosen B_b and to its full source span. It bounds its
normal degree by

  X=10 N [1088 min(N1,2N)² log(a) a/(pk)+a p^(-L1)+kQ].

In the chosen chart the fixed SPtrue multiplier is tangential; its
normal degree is zero. Thus the same bound holds for symP(B_b).
Chart substitution conjugates each X_a partial_b to a linear combination
of elementary X_c partial_d at the same physical position. Applied
to a monomial, each such operator replaces one variable by one variable,
so increases normal degree by at most one. The permitted word length
is at most c0 PER selected position, and hence at most Nc0 in total.
It follows that all relevant chart images in the word span have degree
at most tF=floor(X)+Nc0.

For C>=1088, min(N1,2N)<=N1 implies X<=t0(C,10), with the exact
t0 definition in ManyEdges. Thus tF<=t0(C,10)+Nc0. Each one-sided
test summand is homogeneous in normal degree o. If o>tF its monomial
support is disjoint from the input's support, so its Fock pairing is
zero. Discard all such summands. The bound is uniform in the input;
the truncation does not change y or DX. This proves the literal XiAt
height requirement, rather than assigning a fresh height to each input.

7. THE REALIZATION-TO-DUAL SEAM AND ITS ERROR [CP7]

The OR component proves that the particular one-sided symbols obtained
above have integral separated realizations under the same sufficiently
large actual Type-II hypotheses. This is a stronger application than
SY's regular source-symbol realization: OR extracts the required
determinant factor from each test before invoking the finite cleaning.

Here is the precise passage from that realization to a dual test.
If b=hm (g^{-1})^t is the edge basis, the basis of the dual edge is
b^{-t}dE. Choose the corresponding chart gv; the target lattice is
standard, so its relevant basis matrix is integral invertible and lifts
through the Witt identification. The contragredient Fock pairing from
IW satisfies the exact PairFormula for g and gv. Its scalar normalization
is p^{kappa_fl}/f. By the factorial divisibility in Section 5 it maps
the integral coefficient lattice of the dual edge into the integral
dual of the original edge, and the standard target into its dual.

For an order-o symbol sigma, OR supplies an integral polynomial F_o
of the same multidegree with

  Phi_gv(F_o)=w^o sigma+w^{o+cP}E, E integral,

and order at least o+cP on every other plane. Apply the normalized
Fock identification to w^{-o}Theta_v(F_o). This is the desired dual
functional T_o. The own-chart congruence makes it integral on the
chosen edge; the other-chart orders make it belong to w^{cP} times
every other incoming edge dual. Its target height is at most o.
Distinct incoming edges give distinct dual planes; the dual-edge
involution and EL's graph-plane description prove this, without
assuming that distinct representatives necessarily define distinct planes.

For an integral input on the chosen edge, the exact pairing formula
gives

  beta_fl(Theta F1,T_o)
    = p^{kappa_fl}<Phi_g(F1),sigma>/f + w^{cP}z_o,
  z_o in O.

Integrality of z_o follows coefficient by coefficient from
f|p^{kappa_fl}alpha!, so no denominator has been silently absorbed
into the error. Summing the surviving components gives y+w^{cP}z,
with y from (11), target height at most tF and all other-edge orders
at least cP. For C>=100, DX<=50L<=Dp, and
cP>=8(Dp+1)>2DX. Since yb=p^{DX},

  (y+w^{cP}z)b=p^{DX}(1+w^{cP-2DX}zb).

The parenthesis is a unit of O. Thus the new value still divides p^{DX}
and the actual FlatAt assertion is proved. This explains why the
normalization v(w)=1/2 produces the factor 2 in the strict inequality.

8. THE FULL CLOUD AND THE ORDER OF QUANTIFIERS [CP8]

The full cloud multiplies the flat symbols by the exact complementary
family of CB/CL. Their leading monomials recover both factors. The
selected B span is an ambient direct summand and SPtrue has nonzero
reduction. Consequently every coefficient array having a unit entry
gives a nonzero class modulo w in the full edge lattice (CL3).
This is the uniform primitive-array conclusion; testing the displayed
basis elements separately would not suffice.

The integral Weyl comparison, contraction and characteristic-zero
operator-word calculation of IW/CL supply a primitive flat F1, an
integral scalar c, and a dual map Lambda with

  c beta(U,Lambda T)=p^kappa beta_fl(Theta F1,T),
  kappa<=4L.

Lambda preserves own-edge integrality, subtracts ell from the other-edge
order, and increases target height by ell. The flat test from Section 7
therefore yields full other-edge order cP-ell=c_* and height
at most t0(C,10)+Nc0+ell. If its flat value times b' equals p^{DX},
the full value x obeys

  x(cb')=p^{kappa+DX}, D1=kappa+DX<=54L.                (12)

No division by c is made, and c is not assumed a unit. The normalized
edge pairing is integral by EL, so (12) is the literal ValLe condition.
The same degree, height and exponent bounds work for every primitive
array, although F1,Lambda,T and c may depend on that array.

Thus the cloud witnesses are Ct0=10 and CD1=54. The fixed threshold is
4129 (EA's RK compatibility); OR proves its actual-data threshold is 100.
The numeric, membership, selection, primitive
and contraction thresholds are all <=100 or <=16 and hence below 4129.
Constants are fixed before choosing the local field, orbit, frame, edge,
basis and coefficient array. Taking the common threshold is legitimate only
because every component proved a uniform statement in that order.

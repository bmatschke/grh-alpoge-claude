MAIN COMPLETION

1. Literal CountF, GVC7.
The proof establishes all three original Count_at clauses on the same
supplied family, including the previously missing comparison of different
words. It does not substitute TuSize for CountF.

The original first and last Harish–Chandra coordinates are independent of
the word. Each interior coordinate is in its original interval of length
Delta. First-row gaps are at least 4k Delta; other gaps at distance d are
at least 3d Delta. Logarithmic perturbation and the harmonic sum give
  |log V(u)-log V(u')| <= 1/4 + (2/3)k(1+log k).
The proof includes both top and non-top last-coordinate cases. The actual
DataA subconditions are installed directly on D.s.Del from the original
params/sizes/endpoint fields; no new Delta or family is selected.

The common mass/index/row factors cancel in the original logXu expression.
Averaging over the same f-place set and dividing by fk gives
  |X_u-X_u'| <= 1/(4k) + (2/3)(1+log k).
The source GamP_eq makes the center correction identical for both words.
The prime-window logarithmic offsets lie in [0,1], so
  |log T_u-log T_u'| <= 4 log k.
QW6 and DSZ6–DSZ9 supply the two neighboring same-object bounds. With
  CY=max(5,CYL+1),
  Ceps=30+(2+2CY)/eps,
  Cc=max(Ceps+2,8+3CY,4),
every Count_at clause has this one Cc. Cc precedes the common threshold,
and the threshold precedes every member and both words.

This is a direct proof of the original CountF consumer. The stronger
FamilyVandermondeCountExplicit/PartFiveFamilyBounds interfaces have unused
assertions and are not claimed closed.

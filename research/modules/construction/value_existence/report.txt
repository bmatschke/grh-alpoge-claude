Purpose and current status
==========================
This component reconstructs the value-existence portion of the actual
global/local comparison. Version 0.13 proved exact integral span conditional
on supplied ValEx. We now prove ValEx from the supplied global form, common
frame, level and coefficient-character data. This is a mathematical
construction of the rational polynomials and their independence of all
representatives. It is not an unconditional construction of the global
forms or the geometric true-column input.
FC19 supplies the necessary Forms0/level joins from the actual true-column
record; DR1–DR3 supply its initial decorated-point realization. VE11 therefore
constructs the concrete integral comparison from the supplied arithmetic
host and TC.Ok/Pins/Laws/Later without an extra realizability hypothesis.

Notation and exact interfaces
=============================
E/F is the CM extension and sigma is its involution. The coefficient field
L has a fixed embedding j into C and embeddings t_tau:E->L such that
j*t_tau is the original complex embedding. The coefficient DVR O lies in K,
and e:L->K is the specified local coefficient embedding. The frame B is
the same global matrix used in SF, with B* J B=I. The LI position map is
retained, including the involution at odd Frobenius positions.

The source's Frames(G) is a tuple of invertible complex matrices. Although
G is a Gram-matrix label, this type does not impose orthonormality or an
equation involving G. This point is important for the elementary polynomial
evaluation-injectivity proof below.

A supplied Forms0 datum gives the coefficient-field vector space V, its
integer lattice, its complex value functions f_(x,c), its rational value
description and its adelic function-space realization. We use these literal
fields; the new CV/FC components reconstruct significant parts of their
actual suppliers. It is not legitimate to infer such a Forms0 datum from
a numerical dimension equality alone.

For an away component i and a full local lattice tuple L_tuple, IsVal(x,i,
L_tuple,Q) means (i) Q lies in the source polynomial tensor space VtupR over
L; and (ii) for every tuple of bases g of L_tuple and every presentation
a=gamma*y_c*kappa having the specified local/away components, its complex
evaluation is chi(kappa) f_(x,c)(z') whenever z=gamma_infinity*z'. ValEx is
existence of such a Q for each full tuple. ValUniq is uniqueness. On tuples
which are not full lattices the actual comparison is extended by zero.

VE1. Rationality of one block, with all signs
==========================================
Take k=m_loc+1 and a decreasing integral weight lambda. Set e=lambda_k,
lambda*=(-lambda_k,...,-lambda_1), e*=-lambda_1, and epsilon=det R_k, where
R_k reverses columns. Let T(Y)=Y^(-transpose) R_k. Then T(T(Y))=Y and
det T(Y)=epsilon/det Y.

The independent integral duality CS2, proved by complementary minors and
divided-power row operators, gives for every f in Delta_nu(lambda)(Z) a
polynomial f* in Delta_nu(lambda*)(Z) with

  f*(X)=epsilon^(lambda_k) det(X)^(lambda_1-lambda_k) f(T(X))

on invertible matrices. This expression is polynomial on the stated Weyl
space: for the highest polynomial the minor denominators cancel, and each
divided-power word transports by the signed transposed row operators.
Applying the two duality maps gives the identity, since the epsilon powers
cancel. Substituting T(Y) into the displayed formula gives

  det(Y)^e f(Y)=det(T(Y))^e* f*(T(Y)).                 (1)

Let M0 be any invertible matrix over L and M=j(M0). There is no integrality
requirement on M0 or det M0. The actual coordinates are Z=MY at an even
position and Z=T(MY) at an odd position. Define

  F_even(X)=det(M0)^(-e) f(M0^(-1) X),
  F_odd(X)=det(M0)^e* f*(M0^transpose X).              (2)

Both have coefficients in L. At an even position, substitution immediately
cancels det(M)^e. At an odd position,

  T(MY)=M^(-transpose) T(Y),  M^transpose Z=T(Y),

so det Z^e* times F_odd(Z) equals the right side of (1). These are exactly
the block evaluation identities required by BlockRational.

Membership in the required block space is also proved, not just an equality
of evaluations. The highest minor product has the prescribed column degrees
and is invariant under adding an earlier column to a later column. Row
operators commute with these column operations, and extracting their formal
parameter coefficients preserves both properties. Thus every integral Weyl
generator lies in the dual-Weyl coefficient space. A row substitution by an
arbitrary matrix again commutes with column operations and preserves column
degrees. Hence (2) belongs to the appropriate dual-Weyl space after extension
to L. This argument uses Delta subset nabla, not equality over the integers.

The weight tie says the position weight is lambda for even s and lambda*
for odd s. The negative reversal of a decreasing weight is decreasing.
Thus the conditional definition of blkMod selects precisely the displayed
spaces, never the zero branch. This proves BlockRationalityField. In the
actual record, M0=t_tau(B^(-1)), so BlockRationalityAtPlace follows with the
original coefficient embeddings. No new field extension is introduced.

VE2. Tensor and additive closure give representative rationality
===============================================================
By the supplied rational-value description, f_(x,c) is represented by a
polynomial in the additive subgroup generated by

  j(r) tensor_product_tau f_tau,

where r is in L and each f_tau is in the integral Weyl lattice. Its frame
value includes the determinant shifts attached to lambda_tau. For one
generator use (2) independently at every position. Rename each block into
its own variables, multiply all blocks, and multiply by r. This is an
explicit VtupR generator times an L scalar. Evaluation is a product, and
the bijection (v,s)<->tau matches every determinant factor and Weyl block.

Every element of the additive subgroup is a finite sum of these generators
and their negatives. Add or negate the constructed polynomials. This gives
a Q_c in VtupR whose frame evaluation is exactly f_(x,c). It proves
evRat_gen, evRat_of_blocks and evRatRep_rec. The passage is finite algebra;
there is no completion, density argument or interchange of infinite sums.

VE3. Transport a rational value by a rational unitary and a level element
========================================================================
Put H=B^(-1) gamma B. From gamma*J gamma=J and B*J B=I, H*H=I. Hence
bar(H)=H^(-transpose). At an even position use h=t_tau(H); at an odd one
use h=t_tau(bar(H)). The latter is exactly the inverse-transpose frame
change. This is the source blkLL formula, including tPos's sigma factor.

For the tuple h of these matrices, the natural action on polynomials is

  rho(h) Q(X)= product_(v,s) det(h_(v,s))^(-e_(v,s))
                       * Q((h_(v,s)^(-1) X_(v,s))_(v,s)).

It preserves VtupR because each row substitution preserves the dual-Weyl
block. To check tensor realization directly, substitution commutes with
renaming one block into the full variable set, and sends a product to the
product of the substituted factors. The determinant scalar is the product
of the individual block scalars. Linearity proves preservation of the span.

If z=gamma_infinity*z', the block coordinates satisfy Z(z)=h Z(z'). For
even positions this is ordinary multiplication. At odd positions it is
T(HY)=H^(-transpose)T(Y), exactly as above. Therefore the determinant twists
and substitutions cancel to give

  ev_C(j rho(h)Q)(z)=ev_C(j Q)(z').                    (3)

The coefficient-character premise LevelZeroCharacterRational lifts each
level-zero local psi0 value to L. The actual level character is a product
of their inverses; the possible higher-block chiB products are empty at
level zero. The image j(L) is a subfield, so all products and inverses lie
in it. Finite-product support is part of the actual level character
construction; no unjustified convergence argument is used. Thus every
kappa in U has a scalar r_kappa in L with j(r_kappa)=chi(kappa).

Given Q_c from VE2, the polynomial

  r_kappa rho(h) Q_c                                  (4)

lies in VtupR and evaluates to chi(kappa) f_(x,c)(z'). This proves EvRatPt.
It needs coefficient-field rationality, not integrality of r_kappa; the
integral unit character is supplied separately by LI8 when used by CS.

VE4. Exact adelic point existence, all classes, and away correction
==================================================================
Above p the CM algebra is split, with sigma interchanging the factors.
The nondegenerate Hermitian equation identifies the unitary group with
GL_k(F_v): the first component is arbitrary and the second is its
Gram-conjugated inverse transpose. LI proves this for the actual places.

Given a tuple g_v of local matrices, insert the unitary elements whose
first components are B_v g_v B_v^(-1) at every place above p. Use identity
at all remaining places. There are only finitely many places above p, so
this is a legitimate finite adele and its inverse is also integral almost
everywhere. The unitary equation is checked componentwise. Denote it j_p(g).
The away representative y_i is normalized to be the identity above p.
Consequently a=y_i j_p(g) has all the required IsPt components.

The supplied y_c enumerate the unitary double quotient by rational points
and U. Therefore a=gamma*y_c*kappa for some c, rational unitary gamma,
and kappa in U. This proves PtEx without requiring rational approximation.

For precision, the further point properties used in the existing comparison
also follow from the same insertion. If b is any unitary adele, let s(b)
be the section of its first components above p. Then u=s(b)^(-1)b is
identity above p: projection to the first factor is injective on the
split unitary group. Away from p, u=b. If b is in the level away from p,
then u belongs to U, since its remaining local components are identities.

For PtAll, take an away decomposition y_c=gamma*y_i*b with b in the level
away from p. Choose g from the first components of b in the common frame.
Set u=s(b)^(-1)b in U and kappa=u^(-1). Then

  gamma^(-1) y_c kappa=y_i s(b)=y_i j_p(g).

This is the required presentation of every class. For AwayEx, start with
b=y_i^(-1) gamma y_i for a gamma in the away stabilizer. The same u has
identity at p and the prescribed conjugate components elsewhere. These
are the exact PtAll and IsAway formulas in EqOneOfPh/TiePtRec.

VE5. Different bases and presentations differ by character-kernel level
======================================================================
Suppose a and a' describe the same away component i and the same full
lattice tuple, using g and g'. The quotient kappa0=a^(-1)a' is identity
away from p. In the common frame its first component at v|p is
g_v^(-1)g'_v. Equality of column spans says that matrix preserves W_v^k,
and the reverse equality says its inverse does too. Equivalently both
matrices have integral entries, so it lies in GL_k(W_v).

The actual good-prime condition excludes T and the auxiliary level primes.
Self-duality and LI's integral splitting identify GL_k(W_v) with the actual
local level subgroup, as established in CR3 and SF. The equality with the
forms' local group is supplied by Joint0.Uloc_eq. Thus kappa0 lies in U.
The character is supported at the bad auxiliary places; p is not below
any of them. Since kappa0 is identity away from p, the StepLevel chi_triv
condition gives chi(kappa0)=1. Hence

  a'=a*kappa0,  kappa0 in U,  chi(kappa0)=1.           (5)

This is PtOne. It is stronger than merely saying a and a' have the same
class, and it is exactly the strength required for representative-independent
polynomial values. Its proof does not assume stabilizer triviality or divide
by a stabilizer order; it uses lattice-basis integrality and character support.

VE6. Gluing the values and proving ValEx
======================================
The supplied Forms0 rationality field first places f_x in the actual
complex form space. The field identifying that form space with restricted
adelic functions supplies F_x with F_x(y_c,z)=f_(x,c)(z) and covariance

  F_x(gamma*y_c*kappa,z)=chi(kappa) f_(x,c)(z')
  whenever z=gamma_infinity*z'.                       (6)

This is adExt_rec. It unpacks the actual global form realization; it is not
obtained merely by prescribing arbitrary values on an unrelated class set.

Fix x,i,L_tuple. Choose bases and one presentation by VE4. Construct Q by
(4). For every z take z'=gamma_infinity^(-1)z; it is again an invertible
frame. Equation (3) and covariance (6) show ev_C(jQ)(z)=F_x(a,z).

Now take any other bases and presentation a'=gamma'*y_c'*kappa'. VE5 gives
(5); adelic covariance with rational element 1 gives
F_x(a',z)=chi(kappa0)F_x(a,z)=F_x(a,z). Applying (6) to a' proves the full
IsVal identity for the same polynomial Q. All universal quantifiers over
bases, c', gamma', kappa' and frames are satisfied. This proves valEx_of,
valEx_of_rep' and ex_C_blk with their exact data.

VE7. Evaluation injectivity, uniqueness, and the semilinear comparison
=====================================================================
Given arbitrary invertible block matrices Z, the even coordinate inverse
is Y=M^(-1)Z. For odd blocks the inverse is Y=M^(-1)(Z R_k)^(-transpose).
Thus every invertible block tuple occurs as a frame's block coordinates.
All determinant factors in ev_C are nonzero.

Suppose a complex polynomial Q has zero ev_C. It vanishes at all invertible
block tuples. Let D be the product of the generic determinants of all the
blocks. For every tuple of matrices, QD evaluates to zero: either every
block is invertible or D itself is zero. Over C, a polynomial that vanishes
at every point is zero, by induction on the finite number of variables and
the univariate root bound. The polynomial D is nonzero because its value
at the identity tuple is 1. Since the polynomial ring is a domain, Q=0.
This also handles an empty block index set, where D=1.

If Q,Q' satisfy IsVal for the same x,i,L_tuple, choose one presentation by
VE4. Their evaluations agree for all frames, so Q=Q' after applying j and
therefore over L, since coefficient mapping by a field embedding is
injective. This is ValUniq. Additivity and L-linearity of f_(x,c) show that
Q_x+Q_y and aQ_x satisfy the value conditions for x+y and ax. Uniqueness
therefore makes the chosen values linear. Mapping coefficients by e:L->K
and assigning zero to non-full tuples gives the source's cmpL, semilinear
for e. The definition by a chosen witness is consequently independent of
that choice on all full tuples.

VE9. Concrete comparison from the stated global record
=======================================================
Here is the precise conditional existence conclusion matching the pinned
source. Supply D7, levels L0, forms T_u, StdTied, Joint0 for every u,
LevelZeroCharacterRational for every u, and an ideal a_u for every u with

  L_(b,u) + a_u L_(b,u)# = Lambda0.

For any supplied scale sc, LI constructs the actual local places, original
coefficient embeddings and their position identities. LI8 gives the integral
inverse away characters. Choose the common standard B using SF, and replace
the local frames using the companion ideal identity. This replacement
retains all the embeddings, positions and normalization data. CR supplies
the literal rank equality; its unused Joint0 rank argument causes no issue
because Joint0 remains needed by VE5 and the comparison.

The resulting recursion is recC with Ce=1, exactly the constant in the
source exists_concrete_of_record. Transport Forms0 to that recursion without
altering its underlying form spaces or characters. VE8 supplies the concrete
comparison record and CS gives O-span(c(global integer lattice))=M at every
good coefficient place. Neither block rationality nor ValEx is an extra
remaining premise of this conditional theorem.

Boundary of VE1–VE9
=============================
The global form and level data must still come from the same arithmetic
geometry. The actual TC.Ok/Pins/Laws/Later, chart and carrier construction,
and higher-level up/down/terminal compatibility have not been manufactured
by the preceding conditional argument. CV/FC address their own exact
portions. Metric/isometric transport, spectral and mass estimates,
cohomological windows and the global prime-sum window remain independent
tasks unless a separate component explicitly proves them.

The source's optional IsRecCEv interface is not imposed: VE9 follows the
actual exists_concrete_of_record route. No analytic approximation, averaging
over finite stabilizers, integral semisimplicity, or re-choice of coefficient
embeddings is used in the value-existence argument.

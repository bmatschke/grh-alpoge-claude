# From a fitted retwisted level to a good arithmetic point

Fix the depth and the enlarged pinned column of the [second pass](SECOND_PASS_RETWIST.md). Suppose its [intermediate state](SECOND_PASS_ROW_STATE.md) has been obtained on the restricted datum. The construction below gives a good arithmetic point with the prime choices and all the coprime-power bounds of the exported seed under the following hypotheses. Use the [normalized series transport and ramified character fitting](SECOND_PASS_RAMIFIED_SERIES.md) on the constructed intermediate level, the third-layer automorphic and geometric Galois inputs of the [localization argument](SECOND_PASS_THETA_REALIZATION.md), and the representation, volume, and kernel inputs of the fourth-stage constructions cited below. In particular the series transport must apply at the **nontrivial** conductors of the retwist. This identifies what the finite-level and fourth-stage steps contribute on this column. The [prescribed-size theorem](SIZE_FACE_REALIZATION.md) then gives a linked bundle over each compatible reciprocity datum at every real size. ## Finiteness at the fitted character level

The transported state $y$ is taken over the actual preliminary package. The comparisons bring the old series level and its grid to it. Assign to a coefficient place the old index at its contraction. The support equality for the retwist gives saturation, and compatibility with conjugation makes this an admissible index assignment. Apply the character-fitting theorem on $y$ to this function. Its conclusion identifies the indices of the fitted character level $l$ pointwise with those just assigned. On the constructed origin, transport of the first-pass normalized rationality witness supplies the finite-character reading on this level. The geometric and representation comparisons in the ramified fitting still apply. [Order of these constructions][carry] · [The fitted index and the retained level][kept]

For this $l$, the congruence-index and large-coefficient requirements can be met on a common [localization](SECOND_PASS_THETA_REALIZATION.md), including at nontrivial twists. There is a short direct construction of the other theta input to finiteness. At a level with its series comparison take as the admissible set of indices the whole index space for each $1\le j\le m$ and the empty set outside those layers, at either orientation and every coefficient index. From the moved datum take its uniform coordinates and the highest-weight model in its pins. The export comparison of the series level now places each prescribed vector in the intersection of the kernels of the differences of the two compared maps. Membership in each individual kernel gives the equality required at each chosen index. The out-of-range condition is vacuous. This constructs the auxiliary theta index data of the finiteness lemma. It does not presuppose the theta object on the subsequent fourth stage. [Native all-index construction][alltheta]

The semisimple specialization puts the updated coefficient level in the large range and gives all its local length equalities. The all-level comparison supplies the local levels on that specialization. The [localized row proof](SECOND_PASS_THETA_REALIZATION.md) gives the full reader on the chosen geometric datum. Apply the grid/list construction with those **same** levels and reader. It retains the grid at the prescribed depth and gives the comparison between $l$'s character and the character in the resulting normalized third level. The uniform finiteness theorem makes its request on every fitted state and every depth in this order. Finally use the carried all-powers inequalities at that character. A change of primitive root raises the old character to a power prime to the residue characteristic. Composing with any further allowed power therefore gives an old allowed power. The strengthened third level is obtained by adjoining just this goodness property to its norm witness. ## Preserving goodness through the two changes of spectral realization

The fourth-stage construction requires two changes of the spectral realization on the same column. It first normalizes formal degrees, volume and the comparison at the split prime. The second change supplies the spectral realization on the actual row. The uniform gap condition depends only on the base. For the extension with origin alone the [two transports](FIRST_PASS_FOURTH_STAGE.md) carry the original depth, row packet and character. The additional condition on the retwisted extension involves the character **and** its associated theta index data. Here is why that condition survives both changes. [Scope of the fourth-stage theorem][four]

For a replacement stage use the transported character choice and theta index data. Their twist-index sets, local character readings, chosen conductors, exponents and raw Galois characters identify with the preceding ones. In the goodness witness write $(E,N_0,S)$ for the theta family and finite choices, with $E$ equal to the family derived from the theta index data. After transport take the derived family of the new index data, the same $N_0$ and the same $S$. The preceding equality identifies each tested row with its transported row. Thus all the estimates transport, and the disjointness assertion concerns exactly the old conductor sets. The transported origin consists of the original preliminary package, original state and original fitted character level, followed by the composite comparison to the new character. The row comparison transports the pinned grid with the same depth. This verifies the strengthened extension at every allowed replacement. Apply it first at the volume-normalized realization and then at the spectral row realization. [Goodness of the transported theta family][move] · [Origin, list and strengthened norm on the replacement][origin]

On the resulting fourth level choose the two ambient lattices and form the theta object using its actual row reading. The used tuples include its new nonreference characters. The [fourth-stage theta proof](FIRST_PASS_FOURTH_STAGE_THETA.md) works at each such tuple, and uses the vanishing supplied by the row for unused tuples. The [divisor construction](continuation_v78/producer.tex#L538-L591) applies to both norm choices. Use the [weight bound](FIRST_PASS_WEIGHT_BOUND.md) before choosing the local maps and the [integral kernel](FIRST_PASS_DIVISOR_KERNEL.md) on the chosen divisor and its actual tuple comparison. With the [normalized measures and split column](FIRST_PASS_UNITARY_MEASURES.md), these are precisely the inputs to the [assembly with certified kernel entries](continuation_v78/producer.tex#L139-L183). The assembled object uses the fourth level's character and its theta index data, so its witness still contains the goodness just transported. [Application to each of the two norm choices][both]

Now project the strengthened norm witness to its ordinary extension with origin. The same core and kernel certificate witness construction for the ordinary slots. Its second projection gives goodness on this core. The original character choice supplies conjugation of the packets and the side switch at distinct conjugate places. Substituting the derived theta family in the retained $(E,N_0,S)$ supplies the common twisting data on this object. This produces a good point from the specified second pass under the hypotheses above. Choosing the reciprocity record on a good point can then precede the size request.

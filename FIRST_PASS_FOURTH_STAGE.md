# The first-pass fourth stage with its origin

Let $w$ be a pinned, untwisted first-pass column, and fix the requested depth $c_{\rm dep}$. Use the [level-three extension](FIRST_PASS_EXTENSION.md) with its saturated package $P_0$, its initial state $x$, and its fitted character level $l$. Take the norm slot to be $\mathrm{extensionWithOrigin}(h_L,c_{\rm dep})$, the extra spectral slot to be empty and the geometry slot to be the native $\mathrm{geoCl}(\mathrm{anyGeometry})$. On the common eventual range there is a **fourth stage on this same column**. It retains $P_0,x,l$, their active set and the grid at $c_{\rm dep}$. It supplies a split-prime column, the actual spectral comparison with its rows, the prescribed $\Delta$, and the compact-dual formal degrees for its real places. The late stage and its displayed automorphic models can change. [Fourth-stage fields][lv4] · [Slot used in the construction][uses] · [Scoped implication][native]

**The range and the origin.** Intersect the range of level three with the eventual node bound. On every base over an admissible context in this intersection, the real rank $r$ is at least $1801$ and all consecutive entries of each $b_\tau$ differ by at least $16r^{19}$. Indeed they are an arithmetic progression with common difference at least this large. This bound applies to all stages over the base, so it applies after each change of stage below. [Uniform node bound][gap]

At either change keep the old division submodule $L_u$ for each word $u$ and the old height function. Transport the row, its geometric data and its normalized choice. The new row package is the transport of the old one, including its linear map $e_E$. The list transport keeps the depth and the row's active set. To check the origin itself, write its witness as $(P_0,x,l)$ together with the list, saturation and the comparison from $l$ to the current choice. Keep the three witnesses, list and saturation, and compose this last comparison with the comparison to the new choice. This composition compares the Galois data, twists, forms, bridge, index, side, $Q,k,\psi$. Apply the same construction twice. There is no further coefficient request. **Normalize volumes and formal degrees.** Column positivity and the bound $D_{\rm rows}\le D_{\rm all}$ belong to the existing spectral stage. On its base, column and row data, choose a replacement spectral stage with
$$
 \Delta=\mathrm{Delv}(K.s,K.c_D),\qquad
 s_{\rm Haar}=\mathrm{trueScale}(cd),\qquad
 d_\tau=\mathrm{trueDsig}(R,\mathrm{hc}_\tau).
$$
It identifies the prescribed local model with the concrete model at every finite prime. Make the first transport just described. The existing group-stage law supplies actual adelic volumes on this assembly and a split-prime column. For the compact-dual Haar measure, the parameter $\lambda_\tau=\mathrm{lamSH}(R,\mathrm{hc}_\tau)$ has degree
$$
 d_\tau=\prod_{a<b}\frac{|\lambda_{\tau,a}-\lambda_{\tau,b}|}{b-a}.
$$
Harish-Chandra's polynomial degree formula supplies the parameter dependence. The computation on one holomorphic representation, with probability measure on the compact center, fixes its constant in this measure. On the large-gap range the model exists at the requested parameter, and every admissible realization gives this value. These facts supply all four fields of the real comparison, including its universal degree clause. For the required row count, the convergent Siegel–Weil formula at a nonzero one-vector value gives the ratio for successive Hermitian spaces with these Haar measures. The definite adelic double-coset decomposition computes the starting mass by reciprocals of stabilizer orders. Unfolding the ratios identifies the volume of $Y_0$ with the standard genus mass of rank $r$, since its relevant signature index is at least two. Substitution in the base's own size identity gives
$$
 D_\star=\operatorname{mass}(\Lambda_r^{\rm std})
       \prod_{i=0}^1\operatorname{idxRowLoc}(g_i,k,m)^f
       \prod_\tau d_\tau
       =\mathrm{DstarD}(D).
$$
The finite factors are the two actual row indices. The scoped wrapper also accepts a CM mass-ratio theorem, used elsewhere in the project. Its proof of this step uses the convergent formula, unfolding and the definite decomposition. **Match the spectral space to the actual row.** Start with the forms of the split-prime column just obtained. The local model identifications supply its full setting. Large-gap decay makes the real projector kernel integrable, and finite dimensionality of the row spaces supplies the finiteness record. At each word the level comparison, finite-class representatives and stabilizer calculation give the prescribed column isomorphism. This constructs a single spectral package for these forms. The archimedean Bessel multiplicity input used in the integrability argument has the [Jiang–Sun–Zhu identification](MATHEMATICAL_GUIDE.md). The level-three reading supplies the concrete group and level identifications, the range of $e_E$ on the reference level, and $\|e_E v\|^2=n_P(F,\mathrm{true})\|v\|^2$. The reference comparison also fixes $D_{\rm all}$. Reversal of the finite Green-series labels fixes the good idempotent's coefficients under complex conjugation. A finite adelic translate therefore carries the reference level and its good projection to the concrete one. Lowest-vector evaluation at infinity identifies the level rows with the conjugate spectral rows with a single positive normalization. Restrict to the good projection and use the stated range and norm identities. This gives the isometry $j:EE\simeq E_{\rm good}^{\rm spectral}$ and the equality $\dim_{\mathbf C}\mathcal H_X=D_{\rm all}$. Here the discrete-series classification gives both the infinitesimal character and uniqueness at the specified signed parameter. Fixed-level finiteness and cuspidal rapid decay give the required inclusion in the finite automorphic space. Use the just constructed dimension and isometry to choose the second spectral stage. Its spectral comparison works for all future division lattices, heights and form models carrying the specified levels and evaluations. Specialize to the current lattices and heights and make the second transport. The split-prime column returned by the group-stage law here may be a different presentation. The carried model and this new column have the same levels, level characters, representatives and evaluation maps in the precise finite-column comparison. Transport the isometry and the normalized evaluations along that comparison. They now give the spectral witness on the new row and its actual column. The true degree and $\Delta$ identities persist through this replacement. The new group-stage law supplies its volume tie, and the large-gap computation gives its real comparison again. The additional spectral slot takes its unique value. This proves the asserted first-pass fourth stage, under the classical inputs identified above and the assumptions of its input extension.

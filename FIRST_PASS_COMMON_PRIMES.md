# One set of auxiliary primes for all first-pass tests

For a built untwisted origin on the fitted first-pass data, fix an eligible index with residue characteristic $\ell$, any valid bases of allowed theta tests, a positive tolerance $\epsilon$ and a finite set of places to avoid. There is **one** conductor $Q$, one coefficient level $k$ and one ray character $\xi$ such that every $\xi^h$, $h\in\mathbb Z$ with $(h,\ell)=1$, satisfies the Selmer, projected-lattice, toric and tame bounds. The signed powers used on individual rows are included. The [order-class exponent](FIRST_PASS_ORDER_CLASS_BOUND.md) and the [basis chosen for each active row](FIRST_PASS_EXPORT_TEST_BASES.md) precede the tolerance and exclusions. This result is pointwise in the built origin and applies throughout its standing eventual range. It is the all-powers assertion used in the first export. ## The order of choices

1. On the original lattice at the index use the [local self-duality, Hodge jump conditions and nonvanishing](FIRST_PASS_EXPORT_LOCAL_CONDITIONS.md). The Galois construction fixes a number $R$ of pairs in each half, a coefficient degree $D$, and a lower level $M_0$. Its [square presentation](FIRST_PASS_SELMER_PRESENTATION.md) uses integral finite cocycles above $\ell$. Its [bounded-coefficient theorem](FIRST_PASS_GALOIS_PRIMES.md) permits any additional open normal congruence to the same designated tame element $z$, the installed admissible ray coordinates at each level, and arbitrary finite exclusions.
2. For each toric label impose the open normal congruence in the [order-class distribution calculation](FIRST_PASS_TORIC_DISTRIBUTION.md). For each row and sign impose that in the [Hecke determinant calculation](FIRST_PASS_AUTOMORPHIC_FACTORS.md). Both calculations hold for **every** prime family meeting the respective congruence and exclusions. Intersect these finitely many subgroups and unite all the exclusions with the caller's finite set and the levels of the basis vectors. The intersection is again open normal. All the conditions ask for the image of $z$ in the respective quotients. Its image in their joint quotient witnesses compatibility even when the quotient fields have common subfields.
3. At each $M\ge M_0$ invoke the Galois construction with this intersection. Its first Frobenius elements are conjugate to $z$ modulo the intersection. At each second prime the first-coordinate characters read its prescribed basis vector. Let $Q_M$ be this prime list together with its conjugates. The Galois polynomials, the toric polynomials, and the row determinants at this level all concern $Q_M$. Their low-degree and residual-order bounds precede $M$. There is no requirement that $Q_M$ be nested.
4. Apply [cyclotomic specialization](FIRST_PASS_COPRIME_SPECIALIZATION.md) to these three lists. For the sum of errors use tolerance $\epsilon/(1+|\mathcal S|+2m+4Rt)$, where $\mathcal S$ is the good-signature set and $m,t$ have their original row and tame meanings. It returns one $M$, one $k\le M$ and an assignment of all the ray coordinates at one primitive $\ell^k$-th root. Replacing this root by any coprime power preserves every valuation bound. Ray reciprocity turns the assignment into the character with the prescribed local conductors. The small weights of its inertia coordinates give the tame bound summed over at most $4R$ places. ## Returning to the original tests

The Galois determinant measures the total length of the specialized kernel by Smith normal form, in the normalization dividing by the valuation of $\ell$. For a toric label both the base and twisted polynomials have been scaled by $\ell^{N_0}$, so undoing that common scaling gives the claimed lower bound and the untwisted upper valuation plus the assigned tolerance. For a Hecke determinant the clearing power occurs once per basis column in both determinants. Its contribution cancels in their difference. The resulting full-rank specialized columns are allowed vectors at the selected level. Their lattice is contained in the projected twisted theta image by the integral class-form trace identity. Enlarging a full-rank sublattice only decreases its signed index against a fixed lattice. Changing this fixed lattice shifts the two indices equally. Finite coefficient extensions multiply the numerator and the normalization of each valuation by the same ramification index. These facts give the lattice comparison in the project's original readings, including rank zero. [Integral trace and full rank](FIRST_PASS_EXPORT_TEST_BASES.md) · [Return from determinants to indices][index]

For the actual fitted presentation, the rational universal reading, the integral Hecke action away from the coefficient characteristic, and its compatibility with the normalized geometric and adelic readings are used above. The geometric Hecke correspondence is an isomorphism on integral Lie lattices off the Hecke residue characteristic, and the normalized trace has only powers of that characteristic and units in its local denominators. The section and cusp comparisons give preservation of the sup lattice there. The local Bloch–Kato input at the level of the row is its finite-condition duality and dimension formula. The fitted base tuple, Galois field and roots-of-unity tests are the same ones used for the prescribed bases. Applying the statement to the real row and universal series of each such origin gives the indexwise part of its export. Combining this with the existing full rank and integral bases at its chosen places gives its three export clauses on these standing inputs.

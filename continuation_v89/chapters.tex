{\Large\bfseries Variation and integrality of theta periods}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.89 \quad 25 November 2026
\end{center}

Changing the word changes both a level prime and an archimedean
weight. After correcting for the prime, only a short interval of
weights can affect the assigned height. This gives the ninth
period-height estimate on the selected families.
At the finite places the remaining integrality question concerns the
ideal generated by the values of one bilinear form.
For the complex comparison we exhibit the kernel at the last pair
of theta lifts, and determine the base-tuple period identities
sufficient for the common isometry.

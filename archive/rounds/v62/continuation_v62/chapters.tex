{\Large\bfseries From residual chains to automorphy}\\[4pt]
{\large Counting on the original rank-three deformation space}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.62 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The first chapter follows a rank-four residual chain from an automorphic seed
to a representation over the original valuation ring. One complex embedding
then suffices for all the points in the image-density conclusion.
The construction uses the separate arithmetic inputs specified in each theorem.

The middle chapters treat the rank-three Case V count on its original marked
deformation space. A weight $-2$ Selmer calculation rules out the last
reducible block. This allows a trace valuation to bound the normalized
length of the arithmetic Selmer group. The final chapter compares two words
in the spectral construction and identifies the two estimates still needed
for the off-diagonal norm bound.

# A denominator for the theta series on every auxiliary cover

Fix a standard interior row with its original reference tuple, marked frame, extremal vector and harmonic coefficient map. The goal is an integral **section** at varying auxiliary level. A single bound on the values of one character specialization would give less information. Here are the quantifiers of the tower assertion. There is a complex scalar $\alpha$ of absolute value one, fixed for the row and its specified maps. For every rational prime $\ell$, finite translate $s$ and finitely generated $\mathbb Z$-lattice $L$ in the polynomial input, there is an integer $C\ge0$. It works on every admissible cover, pinned for every dominant weight of the prescribed rank, of level $M=M_0p_d$, every set $S$ above $\ell$, and every allowed determinant twist $\xi$. Here $M_0=g_0g_1$ is the old level, $p_d$ is squarefree and prime to the prescribed bad integer, $M_0$ and $\ell$. The cover field must contain the prescribed splitting-character values, polynomial coefficients and ray data. Choose a level pair $(\Phi,f)$, with $\Phi$ supported on the specified half-lattice and periodic modulo $M$ there, and $f$ its realization relative to the pinned vacuum. If $\Phi$ takes field values integral at $S$ and $v\in L$, the function
$$
  g\longmapsto \ell^C\alpha\,\xi(\det g_f)\,\Theta_{s,f,c_0(v)}(g)
$$
is the prescribed adelic reading of a cuspidal section integral at all the framed tests above $S$. The comparison uses the given Lie factor, component constants and determinant character. The exponent may depend on $\ell,s,L$ and the fixed row. [Hypotheses and statement][def] · [Reference normalization](FIRST_PASS_REFERENCE_PHASE.md)

## Computing the losses before the auxiliary level is chosen

The finite-vacuum identity and the extremal-vector comparison write the analytic series using a linear harmonic-polynomial map $J$ and two scalars $\kappa,\varphi$ of modulus one. Take $\alpha=(\kappa\varphi)^{-1}$ once, before $\ell,s,L$. On a finite generating set of $L$ take the finite union of the coefficient lists of the polynomials $Jv$. For each algebraic coefficient choose a nonzero integer clearing its denominator, and take $c_1$ to be the largest of the $\ell$-adic orders of those integers, with zero available if the list is empty. The cover's field condition places the coefficients of the input vectors in the embedded field. The comparison for $J$ preserves every such field containing the base field, so its coefficients on those vectors are algebraic. The parts of their clearing integers prime to $\ell$ are invertible in every local ring above $\ell$. Linearity over $\mathbb Z$ proves the same bound for every $v\in L$. [Finite polynomial lists][poly] · [Choice of phase and exponent][phase]

For the standard series with integral polynomial coefficients the loss is
$$
 c_2=v_\ell(M_0)K+c_{\rm flip}+c_{\rm arch}+c_{\rm geom}.
$$
The following four computations explain each term. [Standard-series identity and order of choices][bound]

1. Choose $K$ finite generators that span the adelic vacuum lattice modulo each integer $M$ in the sense that every vector is a generator combination plus $M$ times a lattice vector. Such a list comes from the two fixed Hermitian lattices and integral generators for the coefficient ring. After projection to a half-lattice, the $M$-torsion dual translations are parametrized, with possible repetitions, by $(\mathbb Z/M)^K$. Orthogonality on these coordinates expands $\Phi$ in characters with denominator $M^K$. In its vacuum realization this expresses $f$ as a sum of Heisenberg translates with the same denominator. More precisely the numerators are sums of test values times $M$-th roots of unity. Self-duality of the vacuum lattice makes the sum of characters vanish off the prescribed coset. The roots of unity belong to the cover ring and are integral. Writing $M=\ell^{v_\ell(M_0)}m$ with $m$ an $\ell$-unit now removes $m^K$ from the bound. [Generation modulo all levels][gens] · [Character sum on the half-lattice][fourier]
2. The half chosen for a test may differ from the half used by the marked level. Split its required changes into primes over $M_0$ and primes over $p_d$. Over $M_0$ take the maximum exponent over all subsets of a fixed finite set of places, and keep the corresponding finite set $Z$ of translating elements. This is $c_{\rm flip}$. At the auxiliary primes the flip scalar is integral at $\ell$, and its translating element is the identity at $\ell$. Thus the unbounded number of auxiliary primes contributes no new power here. [Fixed set and maximum][flip] · [The auxiliary scalar and the translate][flipend]
3. For each $z\in Z$ bound the denominators at primes above $\ell$ of the entries of the finite matrix of $sz$ and of its inverse. Moving a class to a split representative supported away from $2\ell M$ leaves the resulting rational change of polynomial variables and its inverse with the same bound up to prime-to-$\ell$ factors. Indeed the right compact factor preserves the prescribed lattice at $\ell$ and the split representative and auxiliary flip contribute no pole there. Take the maximum $a$ of these exponents over $Z$. If the homogeneous polynomial has degree $d$ and the vacuum transforms by determinant powers $k_\tau$, one can take
   $$
   c_{\rm arch}=ad+\sum_\tau a\,\dim_E V\,|k_\tau|.
   $$
   The first term clears a product of $d$ linear entries. The determinant of a matrix of size $\dim_E V$ is a sum of products of that many entries. For negative powers use the inverse matrix. This bounds the archimedean substitution **arithmetically** while retaining the actual transformation law. [Split representative and stabilizers][move] · [Both matrix denominators][den] · [Substitution bound][arch]
4. The translated short theta series is a finite sum of algebraic cuspidal sections with coefficients integral at the chosen $\lambda\mid\ell$. The geometric identity comes from writing the translated test in the marked level-point functions, expanding the harmonic data in integral coordinates, and reading the resulting theta sums on the finer marked cover. The bounds at framed points initially carry an integer factor. To see its $\ell$-part, write $N=Mu$ with $(u,\ell)=1$, $n=\dim_E V$, $r=\dim_E W$, and let $D_w.\mathrm{La}$ be the marking lattice. The factor has absolute value
   $$
   m_{\rm idx}(\Lambda,N,r)\,|\mathrm{LevQ}|^n\,|2\,\mathrm{disc}(E)N|^d .
   $$
   The first index divides $N^{[E:\mathbb Q]nr}$ since the intermediate integral lattice is between $N\mathcal O_E^n$ and $\mathcal O_E^n$ before taking the $r$-th power. The marking factor divides $M^{n\,\mathrm{rank}_{\mathbb Z}(D_w.\mathrm{La})}$. Thus take $c_{\rm geom}$ to be the $\ell$-adic valuation of
   $$
   M_0^{[E:\mathbb Q]nr+n\,\mathrm{rank}_{\mathbb Z}(D_w.\mathrm{La})}
          |2\,\mathrm{disc}(E)M_0|^d .
   $$
   The remaining clearing factor $u'$ may vary, with $(u',\ell)=1$. Multiply each section by $\ell^{c_{\rm geom}}u'$ and replace its coefficient $\beta_k$ by $(-1)^d\,\mathrm{clsScal}\,\beta_k/u'$. The split-supported class scalar is integral at $\lambda$. The cancellation of $u'$ gives precisely the additional factor $\ell^{c_{\rm geom}}$ on the analytic side of the identity. [Construction of the short theta sections][short] · [Index divisibilities][idx] · [Moving the factors in the identity][factors]

Apply the standard bound to $\ell^{c_1}Jv$. The two comparisons of the reference vacuum remove $\kappa\varphi$ on every pinned cover. Taking $C=c_1+c_2$ now gives the required identity at every $\lambda\in S$. To obtain one section, apply the expansion at any place $\lambda_0\mid\ell$ with the entire embedded field as coefficient ring. This gives a rational section, even if $S$ is empty. At each $\lambda\in S$ apply the theorem again with its local coefficient ring to obtain an integral section. The two sections have the same reading. Injectivity of the reading on the pinned cover identifies them, so the rational section is integral at every place of $S$, including when $S$ is empty with the integrality condition vacuous. Multiplication by the admitted determinant ray character preserves the integral-form space on this cover. [Identification of the readings][read] · [Rational and local spans][spans] · [Twist on the actual cover][end]

The [descent proof](FIRST_PASS_UNRAMIFIED_DESCENT.md) treats a section invariant under the original compact level. Together these are the two parts of the integral series input used in export. They control auxiliary levels with $(p_d,\ell)=1$. Bounds on order-class toric periods and the downstream coprime powers involve additional data. [Assembly of both parts][join]

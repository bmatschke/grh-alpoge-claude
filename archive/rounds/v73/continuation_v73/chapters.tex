\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Rational theta tests on the selected data}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.73 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The rationality argument uses a geometric section at each tuple and an
adelic function on the same family. We construct the prescribed higher moduli
space as a finite cover, lift its frames, and construct its determinant
character section on the marked dictionary. We prove uniqueness of the geometric class sum, and relate
a theta kernel at one finite point to lifts of the original rational
vectors. A rational vector in the prescribed part supplies the support
needed in the bundle construction.

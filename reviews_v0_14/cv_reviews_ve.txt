VE1--VE3: signs, domains and rational transport
==============================================
The independent CS2 duality is used only for its integral polynomial
isomorphism, before any comparison/ValEx result. Put d=lambda_1-lambda_k,
e=lambda_k, e*=-lambda_1 and epsilon=det(R_k). With T(Y)=Y^(-t)R_k,
T(T(Y))=Y and det T(Y)=epsilon/det Y. The manuscript's

 f*(X)=epsilon^e det(X)^d f(T(X))

gives det(T(Y))^e* f*(T(Y))=det(Y)^e f(Y) exactly, since
e*+d=-e and epsilon^e epsilon^(-e)=1. Thus the displayed odd
normalization has no lost sign. For Z=T(MY)=M^(-t)T(Y), the product
det(Z)^e* det(M)^e* f*(M^t Z) reduces to the same expression. Both
row substitution directions in VE.3 are correct. The source oddStep
uses an intermediate differently normalized dual polynomial; its
additional epsilon scalar is absorbed in the manuscript's f*.

Membership is addressed separately from evaluation equality. Integral
Delta embeds into the upper-column-unipotent invariant column-degree
space; row substitutions commute with that column action and preserve
the degrees. This proves membership in the required rational
dual-Weyl blocks, without asserting integral Delta=Nabla. The finite
tensor/additive-span argument of VE2 is valid.

The standard frame gives H*H=1, hence bar(H)=H^(-t). The odd tPos
coefficient embedding therefore produces exactly the inverse-transpose
frame change. In VE.7 the inverse row substitution and determinant
power -e cancel the transformed frame determinant. The actual
coefficient-field lift of the level character is a field statement;
integrality is correctly deferred to LI8 and CS.

VE4--VE7: presentations, existence and uniqueness
================================================
The finite adelic insertion does not invoke rational strong
approximation. The common frame converts each chosen local g into
the first split component; unitarity determines the second. The
section has finite support, and the supplied quotient representatives
give the required decomposition. The report spells out the correct
order for PtAll: for y_c=gamma y_i b and u=s(b)^(-1)b, choose
kappa=u^(-1), giving gamma^(-1)y_c kappa=y_i s(b).

For two bases of the same lattice, g^(-1)g' and its inverse are
integral. Therefore a^(-1)a' lies in the actual local level above p
and is identity elsewhere. The manuscript explicitly retains the
Joint0.Uloc_eq equality needed to match that group with the forms'
level. The bad-character support is disjoint from p, so the quotient
has character 1. This is the exact PtOne statement, stronger than
class equality and independent of stabilizer-order division.

The adelic extension in VE6 is unpacked from the supplied Forms0
realization. One presentation gives Q with ev(Q)(z)=F_x(a,z) for every
frame z. PtOne then gives F_x(a',z)=F_x(a,z) for every other basis
and presentation. Applying adelic covariance at a' proves all the
universal quantifiers in IsVal. The reasoning matches valEx_of at
NatActRVtupR.lean 56--77, not only its wrapper theorem.

VE7 uses the full invertible-frame domain, which the source Frames
actually has. Its odd inverse Y=M^(-1)(Z R_k)^(-t) is correct. If Q
vanishes on all invertible block tuples, Q times the product of
generic determinants vanishes on every tuple. Infinite-field
polynomial separation and the domain property imply Q=0, including
the empty block-index case. PtEx then supplies an actual presentation
for uniqueness. The source's additivity, scalar compatibility and
zero value on non-lattice tuples justify the literal semilinear cmpL.

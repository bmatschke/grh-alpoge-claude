# Carrying the first-pass data to the chosen localization

Fix the character level of the [constructed first pass](FIRST_PASS_ACTIVE_CONJUGATION.md), a finite coefficient request, and any of its [automorphic semisimple realizations](FIRST_PASS_CONGRUENCE_LENGTHS.md#realizing-a-request-on-the-constructed-first-pass). Denote the realization by $\mathcal L$. We can equip this very localization with all the intermediate level comparisons needed before asking for the new congruence lengths. In particular the frame over $E$, the definite forms, their character tuple and their coefficient readings can be taken from the original row. This statement makes no size demand on the request. [Inputs retained on the localization][input] · [Exact assertions about the levels][goal]

## The archimedean and local comparisons

The archimedean comparison in this step has three fields. The one with mathematical content counts filtration jumps for the de Rham representation, at the particular embedding defining the place. This is the [first-pass filtered-dimension calculation](FIRST_PASS_FILTERED_DIMENSIONS.md). In the application the labelled state already supplies the count on its frame. The other two conclusions ask for inhabitants of records with no fields. Both have a canonical element. This explains why one need not invoke another archimedean classification theorem here. The local Langlands, automorphic and Hodge identifications used in building the labelled state enter through that state and the specialization. [Definition of the comparison][archdef] · [The three witnesses][arch] · [Jump count in the comparison][jumps] · [Use of the stored jump count][use]

For the local arithmetic at $\ell$, the field and its valuation ring give the original local arithmetic data. The two prescribed kinds of local bridge recover membership in the chosen valuation ring of the global closure by the test $|\jmath(x)|_\ell\leq1$. These witnesses are retained for the new local factors and the old twist factors separately. This is the substantive local condition accompanying the empty records. [Bridge condition][bridge] · [The local arithmetic witnesses][arith] · [Construction on the specialized datum][local]

## The Hecke spaces and the row parameter

Let $i$ lie over $(j,w)$. The specialization already gives its residual maximal ideal, integral and field embeddings, the embedding of the scalar-extended local module into the common row, its projected lattice and its characteristic-zero Hecke points. Put these same maps into the localized row. The embedding into $\mathbf C$ is still the composite of the prescribed embedding of $\operatorname{Frac}(O_i)$ into $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ with the place isomorphism to $\mathbf C$. The equality of both component maps in the automorphic specialization proves the required compatibility on the whole field. Rationality, separation of points and the datum on the stored generators now follow for the same operators. The identification of the source levels transports the hyperspecial condition outside the excluded primes and the parahoric and marked characters on their stated domains. The weight shift in this transport is $1/2$, as follows by comparing the two singleton infinitesimal characters of the rank-one normalization. [Embeddings and the localized Hecke action][hecke] · [Local level and weight checks][level]

Retain the field frame with its original embedding $j_E$ and the transported definite family. If a row frame gives the prescribed old representation, use its original Satake comparison and substitute the automorphic specialization's equality of Satake multisets. This gives the row comparison at every specified local argument of $\mathcal L$. For the marked types retain the original roots, Teichmüller lifts, tame generators and place maps. Reduction of $O_i$ now agrees with the old place reduction by the residual embedding in the specialization. The residual representations are semisimple, and their characteristic polynomials agree with the reduction of every realization, by the [residual comparison](LOCAL_RESIDUAL_REPRESENTATIONS.md). [Row frame and Satake multisets][row] · [Local types and residual data][types]

There is a characteristic-zero Galois comparison in the record as well. On its stated hypotheses the old distinguished row is nonzero and good. For a singleton cuspidal list realizing it, the split local parameters identify the same cuspidal class in the old and new readings. Compose the old twist-ring embedding with the coefficient map of the local factor and pass to fraction fields. The resulting complex reading is the prescribed one on the small coefficient field, including the chosen side. The original row comparison and the local one now give semisimple representations of the same twist by $\eta_T^{-1}$, with the same exponent $(1-n_V)/2$. Their Frobenius polynomials agree on the dense set of allowed split Frobenius elements. Continuity and characteristic-zero character theory give conjugacy. This proves the comparison whenever its antecedent is satisfied. [Regular algebraic row and equality at split places][rho1] · [Comparison of fields and Frobenius data][rho2]

## The twist data and their readings

Keep the original base character $\mu_0$, the distinguished character and $\eta_T$. At each local index take the auxiliary character from its place. On this pass it is trivial. The matching reciprocity statements and the reduction of the distinguished character follow from the original character frame and the specialization. Carry the old coefficient-valued definite test to the new datum by the existing transport of forms. It uses the same models at **every** admissible variable tuple for which the old reading was given. Thus the two finite sets of places assigned to a test coincide, and each old readout remains a readout for the localized model. This assertion preserves precisely the domain of the old expansion laws. [Characters and reciprocity][chars] · [Expansion transport][exp] · [Quantifiers on the places and readings][expdef]

The resulting level has the transported original character frame and tuple, the original $j_E$, and exactly the transported definite family. It supplies the normalized and Galois character comparisons, the expansion comparison, and the row comparison for every frame with the given old representation. The small-prime exclusions needed in the twist frame hold because the initial list contains the primes dividing the conductor norm and the host discriminant, and the marked primes. All these conditions concern the same $\mathcal L$. Neither the equality of congruence lengths nor irreducibility of a residual representation is used to install them.

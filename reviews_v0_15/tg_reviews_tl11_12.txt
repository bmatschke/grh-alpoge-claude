TL11 — EXACT RAW LATTICES AND THE LEVEL-PRIME INTERFACE
For a self-dual N and K={x in N:f_P(x)=0}, the down lattice K+P K# has selected
localization ker(f_P), conjugate localization (K#)_barP and N elsewhere. The
paired localizations are dual, so NB1/LP1 gives global self-duality. A local
basis adapted to f_P has D_P=O_P^(n−1) direct-sum T O_P. If f_P(e)=1, then Te
belongs to D_P but not PD_P, and its membership at every other prime is as
stated. Thus te_mem is global, uses the actual integer T with valuation one,
and proves the required nonmembership as well as membership.

For up, A=N+P^−1 x and U=A#+bar(P)A yield the exact table U_P=N_P+T^−1 O_P x,
U_barP=(A#)_barP and N at all other primes. The new f_P(Ty) has kernel N_P.
The down residue map on D/PD extends by rho_P(s)^−1 g([sy]) on D_P and zero
off D_P, independently of the chosen denominator s. Conversely, the actual
validity clauses for any supplied ambient down functional force that same
quotient descent and localized formula. Thus the proof covers the literal
dn_deco and dn_act quantifiers, not only a specially normalized down map.
For down after the specified up, the lattice is N and the functional is the
original f_P, including its off-lattice convention.

The final paragraph correctly proves unconditional up_congr: if x−x' belongs
to PN, then P^−1(x−x') is contained in N, so N+P^−1 x=N+P^−1 x' globally. Hence
the raw up decorations agree even for invalid inputs and both validity and
fallback branches agree. No f_P(x)=1 or x in N hypothesis has been silently
added to this interface. Other functionals, auxiliary reduction maps, rational
frames and coupling labels are preserved at places off the selected pair.
The raw operations commute with rational unitary transport, with the inverse
matrix in the down functional exactly as required. Dilation rescales the
specified f_P and preserves N and L#. The kernel lattice is full by a finite
integer multiple of the full dual lattice. Preferred-representative changes
alter none of these facts.

The actual prime cardinalities, the nontrivial Weil character from the local
unit xi0, TL4 zero-step freeness, and positive base constants supply all AtIn.Ok
fields. The proof needs only the primitive NB1 paired duality (its dependency
is LP1), not NB7/NB8, FC19, or the terminal chart construction.

TL12 — ACTING MATRICES, POINT FRAMES, AND THE THREE LATTICES
The row step has no type primes and its residue/preimage levels have finite
index and finite-order characters, supplying stepX.Ok through TL2–TL3.
The injective acting homomorphism extends rational, auxiliary, and twist
ACTING matrices by last entry1; its first block recovers the original element.
The point's TWIST FRAME is diag(fr,pi). This distinction was material in the
initial draft and is now corrected in both main.tex and report.txt. The exact
identity is
  diag(gamma,1) diag(fr,pi) diag(q,1)^−1
     = diag(gamma fr q^−1,pi).
The source tilFr/tilF_fr uses unitOf(pi), and pi notin Q proves this is the
actual scalar pi and that the indicated frame is valid. The conditional
constructor therefore selects its intended branch. Top auxiliary levels are
the diagonal images; top twist levels are the extended row levels times the
principal congruence group. Their characters are trivial on that congruence
kernel and restrict to the actual row characters. This proves chr_ext and
full point equivariance at all twist primes, not just the reference empty-Q
case. The rational action fixes the last coordinate and has the old action
on the first coordinates.

The proof keeps the three actual lattices separate:
  N_tilde = N direct-sum O,
  L_tilde = N direct-sum pi O,
  L_tilde# = N direct-sum bar(pi)^−1 O.
The selected prime product is (pi): there are f distinct selected primes of
norm T, pi lies in them, and its ideal norm is T^f. This proves exact equality
and exponent one without invoking FC1 under its older Laws premise. Hermitian
duality is conjugate-linear in the first variable, giving bar(pi)^−1 in the
dual. The functional is rho_P(bar(pi) z), t_z=bar(pi), and star(t_z)*t_z=T.
These are exactly Lsh_til, norm_tz, N_til and f_til, with f_til used on its
correct N_tilde domain.

The preferred-representative predicate at the terminal step is membership in
the extension image. Every extended point satisfies it, so the preferred
representative selected in THAT orbit is again in the image. This supplies
rep_til without asserting that all terminal orbits come from row points.
The subgroup H is the full row acting group, of index one, with the same
rational image. This checks every PerIn.Ok and PerIn.Over field.

actual context. They do not assume Pins, Laws, Later, Forms0, TG, an initial
adelic realizer, or a stronger rational SameGenus assertion. In particular

not create a cycle. This review does not construct TC.Ok itself or prove an
analytic/spectral/RH/GRH endpoint. Byte checking is not Lean elaboration or
a transitive kernel audit; no Lean or PDF compilation was performed.

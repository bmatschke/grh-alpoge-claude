2. What coherence actually means in this source

Read N/ExistsOfLayers.lean before imposing a stronger compatibility
condition. Its Layers record contains, in order,
  Gamma, R, I, ioW, E, Mo, Cl, Cm.
These are dependent records: the series object uses the chosen Gamma;
the embeddings use that R; the etale module uses that I; the Wach
lattice and comparison use that exact E and Mo.

The source's exists_of_layers at line 323 takes a complete Layers
record for every Good(K,s,LL), together with the two fixed tied-period
identities ht and hpi. It chooses a whole record at each allowed layer
and builds the local output. This interface permits such pointwise
choices. Do not invent an additional inter-layer transition-map
requirement absent from the printed record. The nontrivial functoriality
is already internal to WEt/WN/WCmp: it concerns all the original
lattices and their homomorphisms on each common layer. Independently
chosen E, Mo and Cm that do not share their dependent inputs cannot
be combined.

WachEx in N/ConvZero.lean is stronger than existence for one convenient
series or etale model. It quantifies over every Good(K,s,LL), every
Gamma, WSer, IoS satisfying IoW, and every WEt over them, and supplies
Nonempty(NS E). Preserve that full quantified scope.

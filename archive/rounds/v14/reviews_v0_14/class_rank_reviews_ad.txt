Findings and checks
-------------------
1. AD1 conjugate-component orientation is correct. For S=divs(pi),
   a_P=conj(pi)^(-1) on S and a_P=pi on conj(S). Conjugation at P reads
   the opposite component; therefore (conj(a))_P=conj(pi) on S and
   pi^(-1) on conj(S). S and conj(S) are disjoint by coprimality.
   Both a and a^(-1) are integral: the inverse field elements occur
   only where their denominators are local units. The diagonal inverse
   has exactly the required last row, before taking residues. Support
   outside the rational type prime and the dimension-zero case agree
   with the pinned exists_base and exists_kStd conclusions.

2. AD2 transition integrality uses the correct inclusion direction:
   N'_P subset N_P gives a_P^(-1)b_P integral when a carries N and b
   carries N'. Equality supplies the inverse transition, so the result
   is GL_n(O_P), not merely integral entries in one direction. The
   proof extends rational-local membership under finite sums and integral
   scalars; it never assumes adelic basis columns are rational vectors.
   The HasRes cancellation first proves z integral from s*z integral
   and s a local unit, then uses z-tb=t(sz-b)+rz. This matches source
   hasRes_of_mul with its explicit integral lift.

3. AD3 checks all five IsPtOf fields. The splice idempotent is fixed by
   conjugation and has disjoint component supports, proving both inverse
   equations and unitarity. The epsilon orientation h^(-1)g is correct,
   and h*epsilon=g. Both transition directions to the supplied h0 are
   integral. At unchanged type/auxiliary primes a denominator outside
   the prime moves a vector into the old global lattice, after which
   HasRes cancellation gives the new identity. Joint orientation and
   disjointness exclude every decorated prime from the changed conjugate
   pair except the intended P. Frame equality is exact. The initial h0
   realizer and actual neighbor-specific compatibility remain explicit;
   the proof does not infer them from support alone.

4. AD4 frame orientation is correct: the conjugate component is
   conjugate(M_P)^(-transpose). For b=x^(-1)g, the identity
   b_conj(P)=conjugate(b_P^(-1))^transpose transfers integrality of both
   b_P and its inverse to the conjugate prime. The transition is identity
   elsewhere. Thus the carried lattice is unchanged and the selected
   component equals the prescribed rational matrix exactly. Outside the
   selected pairs the inverse pullback coordinate maps are identical,
   preserving type and auxiliary reductions.

5. The inserted Carries(globLat(x)) proof closes the source interface.
   A single nonzero integer clears x and x^(-1) at all finitely many
   exceptional primes, giving two-sided lattice bounds. LP2–LP3 patch
   their rational intersections exactly. Density is used separately in
   each single completion to approximate a basis; the determinant is
   1 modulo the maximal ideal, hence the correction lies in GL_n(O_P).
   Finite CRT applied to dN modulo d^2 identifies its adelic span with
   the product of the completed local lattices. The resulting rational
   intersection is literally globLat(x). No simultaneous rational strong
   approximation is invoked. No genus or reduction-surjectivity result
   is obtained from this recovery step.

6. The initial self-dual decorated-lattice realizer in AD3 and the initial
   genus/reduction realization in AD4 remain explicit. The source's CM
   unramified hypothesis is retained in the genus supplier. The component
   does not assert an arbitrary ramified Hermitian genus equivalence.

2. INTEGRAL TYPES: KEEPING THE ORIGINAL COEFFICIENT RING

ITA1–ITA13 prove the original TypeForAlt existential and supply its witness
to the existing OTW13/CAP17 construction of UnitaryHeckeData and HdEx.
This is the original integral representation, with the original fixed
complex embedding. Its choice precedes every later Teichmuller lift
family, local-correspondence map and test module in the source contract.

The first simplification is uniqueness. The finite residue field k of
the original complete DVR O has one multiplicative Teichmuller lift
whose values satisfy t^(|k|−1)=1. Newton lifting proves existence, and
the geometric-sum factor proves uniqueness. Consequently the universal
quantifier over all source lift families is genuine but harmless: each
such family agrees as an actual map with the one used to construct the
representation.

At a type place, the prescribed inertia characters extend to characters
of the actual decomposition group. A zero-monodromy Frobenius-semisimple
parameter with this inertia therefore splits into Weil characters:
Frobenius preserves each inertia-isotypic space and can be diagonalized
there. Its ordered singleton Langlands quotient has the original Good
property. The rank-one, Langlands and Good-injectivity clauses of every
IsRecT map then identify its parameter on this sector. This proves the
required every-map equivalence without claiming that all weaker source
correspondences agree everywhere. The original RecTExists nonemptiness
foundation is explicitly retained to avoid a vacuous universal condition.

The substantial classical input is the crystalline inertial-type theorem,
used in the precise form of Emerton–Gee, Theorem 8.2.1. It gives a smooth
finite-dimensional representation of GL_n(O_F) detecting the prescribed
inertia and N=0 in every smooth irreducible representation of GL_n(F).
The application has no genericity or prime-to-rank restriction. Its field,
rank, inertia extension and normalization are matched explicitly.

The coefficient descent is the main new construction. Choose a finite
extension E of the original fraction field containing the matrices of
this finite-quotient representation. Sum the translates of O_E^d to make
a stable lattice. Then regard the same lattice as an O-module by
restriction of scalars. It is finite free over the original O.

Complexifying this O-module produces a sum over all E-embeddings into C
fixing the original coefficient field. The proof checks that every
conjugate detects the same type. The source's algebraic normalization
combines with the half-modulus so that its Borel norm factors have
integer exponents n−1−i. Arbitrary algebraic coefficient automorphisms
therefore fix those factors; no unjustified claim about preserving a
positive square root is used. The original inertia values are fixed too.
Thus restriction of scalars preserves precisely the desired detector,
even when the individual irreducible type has no chosen model over O.

Finally, the dual representation gives the source column equation with
the correct inverse transpose. A successive tensor–Hom argument handles
arbitrary nonzero representations of the product group that are
individually isotypic. It adds no finite-length or admissibility assumption
on that product representation. The empty set of type places gives a
rank-one trivial type and is included.

The output retains all original ranks n>=1, coefficient prime 3 and every
allowed local residue prime, repeated inertia characters, the original
ring O and prescribed iota. Classical local Langlands, RecTExists and
the stated original literature foundation remain named inputs; this
chapter is not a reconstruction of all local Langlands theory.

3. TWO DECOMPOSITIONS ON THE ACTUAL FUNCTIONS

ADP1–ADP12 prove formU and decompU on the original definite unitary group.
The existing finite-class argument gives a compact fundamental set in
the original rational adelic quotient. Its actual invariant quotient
measure gives a positive definite form on continuous automorphic
functions. Positivity is checked on functions themselves, rather than
only after identifying functions almost everywhere.

Evaluation on the finite class representatives bounds the multiplicity
of every prescribed infinity type at each finite level. These bounds,
smooth compact orbits and the positive form satisfy all hypotheses of
the previously written algebraic semisimplicity argument. The result is
an actual finite-sum decomposition into irreducible automorphic
subspaces. A Hilbert-space closure would not meet the endpoint, and is
not used.

The coefficient/function comparison is explicit:

    T_F(lambda*)(g) = lambda*(R(g^(-1)) E(F(p(g)))).

Here E, R and p are the original basis, infinity representation and
finite-coordinate projection. The chapter proves the exact translation
rule and reconstructs F by finite-dimensional biduality. Rational
equivariance and the actual kernel of p make the inverse well defined.
Finite equivariant projections of all scalarizations preserve every
matrix-valued coefficient equation. Their inverse scalarizations give
the required decompU decomposition in the literal YOf spaces.

The all-depth smoothness check was made explicit during review: the
principal congruence subgroup modulo m_F^(b+1) lies in I_1(b), by the
same upper–diagonal–lower elimination with unit pivots. Together with
the original away-level and type-kernel conditions, it gives one common
finite open stabilizer before a coordinate functional is chosen.

CFD1–CFD12 address the next step. Given the original family of parts,
they refine a decomposition to preserve every classical level and
finite-part equation. The averaging argument is valid for nonnormal
finite-index stabilizers: it uses left-coset representatives only on
the vectors fixed by the stabilizer. Successive commuting compact
actions preserve their scalar profiles. No enlargement of the original
level or depth is required.

Polynomial projectors then preserve the generalized eigenspaces of the
actual normalized U_z. Summing the projectors with the original
ell-adic norm-one eigenvalue condition gives the ordinary decomposition.
Diagonalizability is not assumed. This proves full clParts_alt and the
same result for any specified predicate on the original parts.

The exact remaining distinction is arithmetic. An automorphic YOf
subspace does not yet include the Galois representation, spherical
polynomial readings, local ordinary comparison, auxiliary local
correspondence and point-property record required by PartAltAlt.
The original partX_alt/partU_alt producer supplies these. ADP does not
assert them, and CFD keeps its actual AutomorphicHeckeSet premise.

4. ARITHMETIC FIBERS AND SEPARATION OF THE SAME IDEAL

AFS1–AFS8 establish the full domain and DenseWt results for every
supplied original universal weight ring and every original weight.
The argument uses the existing exact power-series coordinates of the
given ring, not an unrelated isomorphic parameter space.

A bounded integral one-variable series has at most N zeros in the
algebraic open unit disk, where N is the first index of a coefficient
of maximal norm. Division by a root lowers that index. Iterating the
argument in the variables proves separation by any infinite Cartesian
sets in that disk. This avoids assuming an accumulation point in its
interior, which is unavailable for the torsion points used here.

The constructed torsion boxes use distinct ell-power orders for
different weight indices. The actual local abelianization and Artin
map turn torsion on the chosen inertia generators into finite-image
characters and one common finite-part depth. The distinct orders give
the original good-point distinctions. The exact cyclotomic reciprocity
identity is cited through CIP5, with its sign unchanged. Evaluation
takes values in the algebraic closure Qbar_ell itself: each finite tuple
lies in a finite complete extension, so its convergent series values do
as well. No passage to C_ell is hidden in the construction.

AFS9–AFS19 then handle the original Hecke fibers. If a commutative
operator algebra T acts faithfully on a finite module N, every specified
field character of T has a nonzero fiber of N. Localization, faithfulness
and Nakayama prove this before any flatness question arises. The
corresponding Artinian local character block contains a nonzero common
eigenvector. If the module is a sum of stable subspaces, the eigenvector
can be placed inside one of those subspaces.

In particular, every nonzero original finite-free ordinary block has a
nonzero fiber at every coefficient point. The proof does not assert
nonvanishing of an arbitrary residual block. The split idempotent and
the already constructed specialization map identify these fibers with
the exact original complex spans even for a nonflat point map.

The local scalar calculation is also written out. Compact averaging
identifies the kernel of the actual Jacquet projection with vectors
killed by a positive U_z power. The stable ordinary image injects into
the Jacquet module. Under the source's explicitly retained local
literature clauses, distinct unit characters force the relevant
principal series and a one-dimensional character space. The original
double-coset index and modulus give exactly

    psEig(omega,j) = product_(0<=i<j) omega_i(varpi) q^(-i).

The source lowVal factor is then applied without changing normalization.
A determinant polynomial with constant term 1 makes the integral
ordinary eigenvalues units. This supplies goodScalB_alt and the exact
partAct0_alt action and fiber assertions.

AFS20 proves the required separation theorem. At each good coefficient
point, a Hecke element t vanishing on all original arithmetic points
acts by zero on every part, hence on the whole specialized block.
Write t f in the original finite ordinary basis. Specialization basis
independence kills every evaluated coefficient. DenseWt then kills
the coefficients themselves, so t=0 in the original operator algebra.
Reducedness follows from this argument; reduced fibers were not assumed.

For the original map f:B->T, AFS21–AFS22 apply that separation to the
literal ideal J=ker(f). Composing the separating T-points with f proves:

    if b vanishes at every original good point killing J, then b is in J.

The result is the exact same-ideal density conclusion, not just membership
in the radical of J. It includes the zero-module case J=B and every
increasing original weight.

Its remaining installation input is stated explicitly:

    H.AutomorphicHeckeSet(iota, UnitaryPointProperties(r,iota)).

Until the actual Galois-decorated part supplier is reconstructed, the
bare TJDense endpoint remains open. The full point output, RingProps,
other Hida fields and all-level same-J automorphy are separate tasks.

5. FINITE LOCAL WEIGHTS WITH THE ORIGINAL LABELS

FLC1–FLC5 prove the full remaining root-injectivity collision estimate,
with the original constant 23, for every finite field of cardinality
q>=2^12 and every degree. The weight of a squarefree polynomial F is

    w(F) = product_(P divides F) (q^(deg P)−1)^(-1).

Within a single irreducible factor, a collision after removing the
ell-primary root component shortens its projected Frobenius orbit to
a proper divisor of the original degree. Counting roots, then dividing
by that degree, gives the same-factor bound. Between two factors, a
common Frobenius translation moves the collision to one fixed fiber
over a fixed root of the second factor. This prevents an unnecessary
factor of the second degree. The existing weighted divisor estimate,
two union bounds and explicit geometric-series inequalities give 23.

FLC6–FLC18 complete the two character laws required by the consumer.
Bruhat compatibility gives the exact Gelfand–Graev norm q^n−q^(n−1).
The finite mirabolic orbit argument and a literal sequence of pattern
root exchanges then prove the lower e_J comparison. Each exchange is
checked on the original allowed matrix entries and character edges.

For squarefree series, finite cuspidal Mackey theory shows that distinct
cuspidal factors induce irreducibly. Whittaker multiplicity is computed
from the same finite induction and mirabolic constructions. The one
substantial published finite-group input is Green's cuspidal theorem.
Its simplex/partition(1) symbol, degree and elliptic-value formula are
matched to the original LabelDatum. A separate Fourier-support argument
proves the needed uniqueness of the original prime label.

The normalized-label comparison is an equality of the actual series
projectors. Its prime-label permutation preserves factor degrees and
squarefreeness, so the weight formula transfers. Root-collision events
are always evaluated on the original polynomial; the proof does not
move them through an arbitrary label permutation.

FLC19 assembles the original WeightAllRows conclusion and unchanged Wg
for the selected eventual family. All five local error mechanisms now
have written producers. The long-prime-degree term uses the existing
eventual theorem with its explicit actual-rank threshold: the selected
family has N>=f>=x^2, so one late x threshold suffices uniformly in all
later choices. This is not inferred from abstract SizesAt alone.

The source consumer uses the first three admLq clauses. The fourth,
stronger upper comparison remains unclaimed and is unnecessary here.
The original normalized-type record, actual adelic reduction/inflation
and Haar comparison, row identities, common AllRows object and the
three sufficient arithmetic cylinder counts remain to be constructed.
Thus GLOBAL_RETAINED_DEFECT is not yet closed.

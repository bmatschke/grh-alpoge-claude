# Rational theta tests on the selected bundle

The rationality input used by BTH8 and BTH11 holds on every member of the original common selection. At the base level it follows directly from the witness for that very bundle. At both levels one can also transport the same coordinate tests through the final change of faces and scalars, provided the original weighted point meets the geometric field request. These statements have different quantifiers. ## The endpoint on the original selection

Let $p$ be the construction parameter. Fix a class $\mathcal A$, the selector $s$ and a row condition for which the source gives the selection property with $\mathrm{IsLinked}(p)$. For every $\mathcal F\in\mathcal A$, every $x>\mathcal F.x_1$, and every admissible index $a$ at $x$, take the **specified** bundle $b=s(\mathcal F,x,a)$. There exists a realization of this bundle with complex datum $X$ and coefficient embedding $\sigma:L\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ such that, for each layer $j<m$, a finite family $\psi_k\in X.W_j^\vee$ satisfies
$$
  \bigcap_k\ker\psi_k=0,\qquad
  \psi_k\bigl(X.\theta'_{jt}(X.jL_{jt}v)\bigr)\in\sigma(L),\qquad
  \psi_k\bigl(X.w0C_{\mathrm{false},j}(e_l)\bigr)\in\sigma(L).
$$
The middle assertion holds for every tuple $t$ and every rational source vector $v$. The last holds for every stored base-coordinate vector $e_l$. The same family of functionals gives both assertions. The bound $x_0=\mathcal F.x_1+1$ suffices for all the indices and layers of the family. [Source selection and extraction](LOCAL_SELECTED_FAMILY.md) · [Exact test predicate][tests]

Indeed, eliminate the dependent equality in the selected bundle's construction and then the equality in the definition of linkage. The resulting weighted point $q$ has a witness $w:\mathrm{BWit}(q,b)$, with the transported bundle still equal to the selector output. Take $R=w.x.R$, $F=w.x.F$ and $X=R.\mathrm{toCx3}(F)$. The witness $w.y$ gives $H:\mathrm{Holds}(R,F)$ and identifies the row family, faces, periods, scales and real realization of $b$ with those from this same $R,F$. The field $H.\mathrm{prim}.\mathrm{liftTestB}$ is precisely the displayed test assertion at level $\mathrm{false}$. The dependent bundle equality carries the assertion back with all its bases. This argument uses the original class and selector, with no extra field request at the selection step. [Point and linkage][link] · [Equality with the selected bundle][bundle] · [Primitive test field][prim] · [Memberwise proof and threshold](continuation_v74/bundle.tex#L169-L193)

BTH8 evaluates the first rationality clause at the base tuple. The resulting matrix defines the kernel of the lift over $L$, since the test map is injective. The rational source Hermitian form then gives its orthogonal complement, the adjoint image, over $L$. BTH11 uses the last clause on the stored base frame. The matrix of its test values has full column rank because the frame is independent and the tests separate. On the geometric inclusion of the good image in this frame, solving an invertible minor expresses the theta columns in $\sigma(L)$. Both arguments use $X.w0C_{\mathrm{false},j}$ in this realization. Thus both endpoints' common base-rank argument has its required rational tests on each selected bundle. Existence of design members at the required scales is a separate input. ## Transport at both levels on a weighted point meeting the field request

Now keep a particular $q$ and assume that **its own** base, frame and character set satisfy the Weber condition of U76W2. Write $u=q.x.\mathrm{what}_2$ and $z=u.r.z$. The weighted point contains $u:\mathrm{RowsT}$, whose fields include $z.R.\mathrm{toRealDat}.\mathrm{ExAt}$ and $\mathrm{InGeo}(z.\mathrm{geo})$. Its witness of being over its context gives $n_V\ge 8fr+4$. Thus all the hypotheses of Q76Z1 apply to this $z$. Its all-tuple arithmetic proof gives the good projected lifts in the ambient rational coordinates of $z$, including their prescribed phase. In those coordinates one dual basis per layer reads every rational lift and the stored frame vectors at **both** levels. Algebraically, the coordinate values on a stored level vector are $\sigma(b_L(d,j,l)_k)$, where $d$ is either Boolean level. [Weighted point][point] · [Geometric fields in its row][rows] · [Rank from the context](continuation_v77/stage.tex#L3-L17) · [Coordinate proof](SECOND_PASS_RATIONAL_BASES.md)

For any $w:\mathrm{BWit}(q,b)$, its twist argument gives a dependent equality of the field, family, real datum and face. On the row side the datum and face are
$$
  (z.R.\mathrm{updS}(z.R.\kappa,\lambda)).\mathrm{withErr}(e),
  \qquad \mathrm{reFace}(z.F,D_c,S_p,Z_{\rm sc}).
$$
The four entries $P_0,\psi_0,P_1,\psi_1$ determine the face used by the lift coordinates, denoted $F.\mathrm{lv2}$, and re-facing retains all four. In particular, re-facing gives literal equality of $\mathrm{toCx3}$ for a fixed real datum. The scalar update changes only $\kappa,\lambda$ in that complex datum, and the error update changes its error field. The formula for $\mathrm{toCx3}$ displays the source map $jL$, the target $W$, the phased operator $\theta'$, the dimensions $dW$, the target frames and their inclusions before these three fields. Consequently the full test predicate is preserved for either $d$, even at indices where the twist allows a new $\lambda$. Equivalently, the stored vector $w0C_{d,j}(e_l)$ still reads as the twisted image of the same arithmetic vector $b_L(d,j,l)$. No comparison of independently chosen coordinate bases is needed. Finally $w.ax$ identifies that twist argument's realization with $w.x$, and $w.y.\mathrm{eq}$ identifies the latter's bundled reading with $b$. Transport the preceding two-level assertion along these equalities. It applies to every linked bundle at $q$, at every scale at which such a witness is given. In particular it applies to outputs linked to second-pass points made with the Weber values included in their final coefficient request. ## Scope in the obligation register

U76W2 constructs one finite set of algebraic numbers for each prehost and character set. It proves a field condition on bases meeting that request. Membership in the original selected class yields a weighted point and a link. The row of that point supplies the geometric and rank hypotheses just used. Its explicit prime condition, called `Forced`, concerns inclusion of rational primes in the twist set. To apply the two-level argument on an arbitrary member one would also need containment of the Weber values in **that member's** coefficient field, or a proof of the same arithmetic coordinates there by other means. Adding Weber values when constructing new members does not establish that containment for a member already specified by the selector. [Quantifiers of the Weber request](continuation_v76/weber.tex#L40-L87) · [Prime condition][forced] · [Definition of linkage][link]

Accordingly the endpoint assertion of `BASE_THETA_RATIONAL_TESTS` is supplied on the original selection. The broader historical entry records simultaneous two-level tests and the upstream geometric construction of their common arithmetic data. The preceding proof gives the two-level transport on the stated field range and the base tests on the unrestricted selected class. It leaves the construction and existence of the common family to `DESIGN_CHOICES_COMMON` and preserves the distinction in the historical ledger.

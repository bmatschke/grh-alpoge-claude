Completed original endpoints

FCD8 proves exists_noetherian_subring_homogeneous_matrix_flat_charts.
The input is the original arbitrary commutative R, fixed homogeneous Phi,
and all actual chart data. The output is ONE Noetherian subring R0 and
ONE Phi0 mapping to Phi, with preserved integer homogeneous degrees and
flat dehomogenized cokernel on EVERY chart. The proof displays the
projective splitting, generalized inverse, annihilator matrices, finite
formal-smoothness certificates and explicit retract. The Rees-module
Artin–Rees argument, completion exactness/flatness, and compatible lifting
to the completion are included, so the relevant smoothness-to-flatness
step is explained rather than retained as an opaque theorem.

FCD12 proves exists_graded_presentation_saturating in its original scope.
The original graded G is not assumed finitely generated. Finite chart
quotients give one finite homogeneous generating family up to powers;
the same argument on the actual graded kernel gives finite relations.
The actual map coker(Phi) -> G has elementwise X_i-power torsion kernel
and cokernel for EVERY chart i. The powers may depend on i and the element.

FCD18 proves the full original exists_noetherian_model_grCube, extending
the initial assignment to its actual model join. It constructs one R0,
the degree-zero localization cube C0 of coker(Phi0), and the actual map
theta: R tensor C0 -> grCube G 0. Nonempty C0 vertices are flat; every
Cech cohomology group is finite; theta is bijective at every nonempty
vertex. Its maps commute with restrictions. Scalar extension is handled
by cokernel right exactness and graded localization, without flatness
of R0 -> R. FCD15 supplies the required Cech calculation through an
explicit Laurent-monomial contraction and descending dimension shifting.
Empty index sets and Cech degree zero are explicitly covered.

The formal proof is accepted as checked and correct. The task is to turn
that proof into a complete human-readable argument for RH and the
unrestricted Dirichlet-L-function GRH covered by the source. A simpler
argument is welcome whenever it proves the actual conclusion needed,
with its original objects, hypotheses and order of choices.

This roadmap supersedes the planning recommendations in the v0.49
roadmap, preserved in history/version_0_49. It incorporates all seven
v0.50 proof components. It proposes further work; it proves no new
theorem and closes no additional selected obligation. References in
brackets identify proof components or source families listed in section
13. The companion RH_GRH_parallel_execution_plan_v0_50.txt gives concrete
assignments for three agents and a coordinating fourth agent.


1. WHERE THE RECONSTRUCTION NOW STANDS

The terminal analytic deduction is already written. For a fixed primitive
target character, the remaining inputs must produce the actual centered
smoothed prime-sum identity and its square-root-scale estimate on
arbitrarily late intervals [Y,Y^(3/2)]. A zero with real part greater than
1/2 then contradicts that estimate. The functional equation and the
primitive/imprimitive comparison finish the intended conclusion. Ordinary
RH is the principal-character specialization. [CMP1–CMP5]

The immediate work is therefore to construct compatible arithmetic,
geometric and analytic inputs and prove their quantitative bounds on one
common selected family. The seven new components have advanced several
of the internal theorems substantially:

  • Test-ring descent and AllTest are now complete at the original
    section base-change premise.
  • The actual pointwise normalized theta frames are constructed,
    including the precise Gaussian mean normalization.
  • The common arithmetic field, character seed, marked frame and
    strict cocycles are constructed; 21 of the 28 Ok fields are proved.
  • The Bessel nonvanishing argument has its asymptotic base case,
    weight walk, period calculus and finite-vector realization.
  • The entire ConclF -> AdmF -> LinkN denominator export is proved.

The next work should use these conclusions directly. The principal
remaining interfaces are StepStatement; the last three FramesOK fields
and CoverTri/FibTri; the seven remaining arithmetic Ok fields and the
full original Gen entry theorems; and an actual ConclF supplier on the
common family. None of BesselNVGap, full IsUnf, GdEnd or
GLOBAL_DENOMINATOR has yet been supplied unconditionally at its required
human-proof scope. [BNV23; BSP10; NFR13; MFC14; DNE14]

The v0.50 inventory is:

  3,112 cumulative human claims.
  98 new claims: TDR 13, NFR 13, CAF 11, MFC 14, BNV 23, BSP 10, DNE 14.
  121 selected dependency nodes and 161 edges, unchanged.

The B/C categories describe the inherited assessment of the size of the

2. EXACT OUTPUTS OF THE SEVEN NEW COMPONENTS

2.1. TDR1–TDR13: test rings at the required coefficient-ring scope

The proof represents actual framed moduli points through the Lie
eigenframe bundles and the theta-section frame line, constructs the
literal FrMap under base change, descends finite data, and gives the
radical polynomial presentation needed for TRing. It proves the entire
TestDescent.exists_fg_descent for arbitrary commutative coefficient
algebras, including non-Noetherian, nonreduced and nonflat ones. It then
proves Frames.allTest_of_secBaseChange for every reduced coefficient
algebra and every actual framed point. [TDR3–TDR13]

The original Moduli record and SecBaseChangeSt remain inputs. The latter
requires a locally Noetherian original base but allows an arbitrary
coefficient target; the application uses its n=1 case. This is not a
replacement by field-valued points or a stronger base-change hypothesis.
The existence of the Moduli record and of the original base-change law
has not been inferred from this implication.

2.2. NFR1–NFR13: the actual pointwise normalized family

The proof constructs ThetaData on the given FamilyUnif and ModelsOf,
including the CM column/period dictionary, the adapted mod-eight
symplectic basis and its integral lift. It identifies the actual complex
theta section line by Fourier expansion, constructs IsThetaPt and
IsNormUn, and proves every FrameOK clause. The Hermitian determinant,
quadratic gauge and Gaussian integral give precisely the source's mean
one normalization under its minor-norm hypothesis. The same section,
periods and derivative eigenframes are retained. [NFR1–NFR12]

One family un is selected before all its points; NFR13 supplies pointwise
FrameOK and, using TDR, FromTest for those same points. Its square roots
are chosen pointwise. No holomorphic choice of square root is asserted.
The complex-fiber Fourier argument removes the SecTwistSt premise from
this particular constructor; it does not prove the global SecTwistSt law.
The remaining transformation and reading theorems must still prove or
retain the exact instances of that law they use. [NFR5–NFR6, NFR13]

2.3. CAF1–CAF11: common field and character seeds

The full seed_cl, auxw_ex and field_ex constructions are written. The
core characters and Q are chosen before every later compatible field L
and auxiliary prime w. The actual quadratic fields, prime-to-ell order,
parity, residual norm congruence, square witness, splitting and
discriminant conditions are supplied. The field is the actual fixed
field of U intersect ker(eta), with the stated Galois groups. The seed is
installed on the same marked element g1. [CAF1–CAF11]

The two original entries remain distinct. For Dat,
M=ell*N*|disc K| and Q=|disc K|*N_(ell'); for HostData, M=ell*N and
Q=N_(ell')^2. The host construction preserves ell=3 through its dyadic
witness. These are character seeds. They do not supply the rank-two and
rank-three automorphic seeds or all nineteen fields of Gen.

2.4. MFC1–MFC14: a fixed marked frame and its strict classes

From the full original Gen and GC.Hypotheses2, the proof constructs H0,
hlay, l15, UFacts, the uniform bound b0, the actual linked quotient and
link_ex, ok_basic, and both strict cocycle classes. The H0 argument uses
a direct Chinese remainder construction of a real quadratic field.
The class-rank bound C0 is chosen before the depth m, and the depth is
then m=3*C0+7. The actual field L, quotient Phi, local groups and marked
g1 are fixed together. Both strict classes and Big live on those same
data for the full general local-ring scope of Gen/P. The additional CAF
SeedClause is joined when O=c.O is the original Coef DVR and CAF7's Q
was chosen first; it is not asserted over every local coefficient ring.
[MFC1–MFC14]

The result supplies 21 of the 28 fields of Ok. The seven still open are
phi, gen, cl, ec, b1p, b1 and h1. The full original Gen_gd and Gen_host
suppliers are also open. The already written MFC theorem may be consumed
universally on a fixed linked frame; it does not yet prove GdFrameEx,
HostFrameEx, HasFrameW or the common GdEnd.

2.5. BNV1–BNV23: the base case and the nonvanishing reduction

The proof supplies the full translated integrability statement IntS,
the gap and weight-walk lemmas, explicit cyclic Cayley data, the global
Haar majorant, the Gaussian asymptotic with its uniform coefficient
control, and the exact compact Schur factor. These give the original
StepIBaseStatement for sufficiently large integer deformations. The
passage from BesselNZ to the original endomorphism-valued BesselAll is
also written for every allowed datum, nonzero compact vector and weak
Haar pair. [BNV1–BNV22]

BNV23 proves StepStatement -> BesselNVGap, then invokes BWP to obtain
the analytic setting on supplied Forms0. The StepStatement premise is
still to be constructed. The new base case alone does not give every
weight needed by BesselNVGap.

2.6. BSP1–BSP10: period calculus and the finite-vector step consumer

This component proves GapNeedStatement, PsiDatumStatement, the exact
Capelli annihilation on the whole generated module, MultStatement and
PerStatement for every derivative word and the original Haar data. It
supplies the required integrability, closed-subspace reproduction,
finite projection and Fubini arguments, with a direct product majorant.
Finally it proves the full FinStatement from its original shifted-
quotient hypotheses. [BSP1–BSP10]

The remaining StepStatement suppliers are TMapStatement/TMapDStatement,
EigStatement, EvalP/EvalM, and QuotP/QuotM. Scalar multiplier estimates
are not yet the tensor-map theorem; Capelli annihilation is not yet the
complementary-minor eigenvector theorem; and FinStatement consumes the
shifted quotient rather than constructing it.

2.7. DNE1–DNE14: the complete denominator export

The proof constructs the actual admissible table from Concl, including
all seventeen admissibility clauses, the original place weights and
prime-three equal sharing. It bounds every ordinary, split, inert,
exceptional, small and ramified prime contribution, preserving the row
ranks, exact dZ0, coefficient field and same-host vvTerm. The uniform
exit is the full original ConclF -> AdmF -> LinkN implication. Its
constant is C_out=2000*(|C_a|+1)^3 for a fixed local-bound constant C_a;
the threshold is allowed to depend on the original family as prescribed.
[DNE1–DNE14]

The proof retains the original ShaFinOdd and vvTerm summability guards.
The numerical aggregation and export should now be reused. Constructing
ConclF on the actual common family, including compatible killing data
and all local bounds, remains the GLOBAL_DENOMINATOR task.


3. FIRST PRIORITY: COMPLETE THE UNIT WEIGHT SHIFTS

The source organizes Bessel nonvanishing as a large-weight base case
followed by single-coordinate shifts. BNV supplies the base and the walk;
BSP supplies the period calculus and the final finite-vector consumer.
The following four bounded producers fill the remaining interval between
them. Their exact contracts are in PsiDatumStatement.lean. [Analytic]

A. TMapStatement and TMapDStatement

For every tuple in the whole generated module, prove that the standard
or dual coefficient tensor map is smooth and square-integrable. Prove
the exact E_ab intertwining identity and, for every derivative word,
a bound C*frob(g)^(1-aExp), where aExp=h/(8*sqrt(2*q0)). The constant
may depend on the word and the fixed original data, but not on g.
Retain the free translates, original representation, compact type,
characters and Haar data. Lowest-type vectors alone do not cover this
contract. Entries: SmoothMatrixMaps/TMapSpec.lean, MemLpOfBound.lean,
ReproducingKernels/DecDeriv.lean, DecDerivA.lean and TmETmapSmooth.lean.

B. EigStatement and the complementary Capelli algebra

Starting with an arbitrary stable submodule annihilated by the relevant
Capelli polynomial, prove that nnPlus and nnMinus land in the required
standard and dual Euler eigenspaces, with the exact shifts -omega and
omega-m. This is a universal algebraic theorem and can proceed while
the tensor-map analysis is being written. Entries:
ListProdMemOfForall.lean and CapelliIdentity/{WhittakerZeroStatement,
Tensor,CapelliWhittakerZero}.lean. BSP's annihilation theorem supplies
its actual generated-module input when the two strands meet.

C. EvalP and EvalM

Prove that the corresponding complementary-minor tensor has a nonzero
actual period for some y or beta whenever the original period is
nonzero and the forbidden value is avoided. The exclusions are exactly
u_j != lambda_s+1/2 for the positive shift and
u_j != lambda_s-1/2 for the negative shift. Keep the character, Cayley
point, free translate and Haar pair fixed through the evaluation.
Use BSP's period theorem and the justified tensor-map differentiation.
Entries: CapIdx.lean, SumGammaNnMinus.lean and the EvalP/EvalM supporting
files. A nonzero abstract functional does not replace the displayed
integral required by the source.

D. QuotP and QuotM

Construct the actual Wf and W' for lambda+e_s and lambda-e_s: the exact
image, shifted lowest compact type and Casimir value, compact stability
and injectivity of the period on the quotient. The pure compact-type
and commuting-projection arguments can start independently. The full
QuotP/M theorems are conditional on a nonzero tensor period, so their
proofs can proceed before EvalP/M is finished, provided that premise
remains explicit. Entries: KTypeLemma/K2V.lean and K2Vd.lean,
ThreeAddLeGapBound.lean, CasimirValueSubSingle.lean,
ExistsCommutingProjectionImage.lean, QuotPA/QuotMA and QuotP/QuotM.

Join

The standard tensor map, EvalP and QuotP yield StepUp at lambda+e_s;
the dual tensor map, EvalM and QuotM yield StepDown at lambda-e_s.
BSP supplies the exact datum and Capelli annihilation, Eig supplies
the required eigen-tensor, and BSP10 turns the actual shifted quotient
into new BesselNZ. Both directions, at the original gap and parameter
scope, give StepStatement. Only then apply BNV23 to obtain BesselNVGap
and BWP24 to obtain ConcreteSettingExists on the inherited Forms0 scope.

After that join, the original orbital/fiber identities, Bessel local
ties and convergent Siegel–Weil arguments remain. They must identify
the measures, additive characters, formal degrees and transport maps
on that same setting, including the factor 4*pi/g_D and the distinction
between group conjugacy and an equivariant homogeneous-space map.
AutTie, RowDictTie, LabTie and the actual CountIdempotentsClass theorem
or its three sufficient cylinder counts remain separate applications.
They are not consequences of nonvanishing alone. [ASG/AMD/BWP]

6. FIRST PRIORITY: SUPPLY ACTUAL CONCLF ON THE COMMON FAMILY

DNE has completed the export. The next denominator work should prove
its original local hypotheses on the actual family, then use that
export once. The exact family predicate is:

  For the fixed Fm, there exist C and x0 such that for every x>=x0,
  every original hx: Fm.x1<x and every i in Fm.Ix(x),
  ShaFinOdd(Fm.D(x,i)) implies that there exists actual HubData X
  satisfying Concl(Fm.L(x,i), Fm.params(x,hx,i), X, C).

Thus C and x0 precede all later members. X may depend on the member and
is chosen after its ShaFinOdd premise, as in the source. At each member,
the same X must satisfy all fifteen Concl fields:

  pos, pass, mult, rank, kill, num, mid, bb, large, exc, two, ram,
  typ, par, small.

These include the actual multiplicity and rank, compatible killing
points, RowNum, StarAD and BB bounds, large and exceptional primes,
the prime-two/three and ramified bounds, type primes, polynomial
parameter control and Small. In particular, the arithmetic point used
for a row bound must be the one used by the killing and admissibility
statements. A collection of unrelated favorable points will not yield
Concl. [DNE; Denominator definitions]

Begin with the actual NormChoiceFacts and compatible Galois/row-point
suppliers, then one complete local bound on those same points. Develop
the small-prime two/three and type-prime suppliers alongside the other
row bounds. The source's conclusion_of_family_facts combines its
original Hypotheses2, TwGood, FactsF and BoundAtTwoAndThreeForFamily
into ConclF; retain those premises until their actual family instances
are constructed. RowClosureLinking identifies the common-bundle join.
The point of this split is to prove complete local theorems whose
constants can be combined uniformly, not to add a new assumed FactsF
package and declare the producer finished. [Denominator source map]

Apply DNE14 after this construction. Its fixed-C_a export has the same
C_out for all permitted families at that fixed C_a; ConclF itself
allows its C_a to be chosen for the given Fm. Do not interchange these
two quantifier orders. Finally prove Closed(Des(C,3),LinkN) on the
selected common class, with actual etaSum, exact dZ0, same-host vvTerm
and only the original ShaFinOdd/summability guards. That is the
GLOBAL_DENOMINATOR exit.

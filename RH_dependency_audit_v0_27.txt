1. Outcome and exact scope
=========================

This increment closes the actual arithmetic and deformation-model
construction underlying the paired CountII/CountIII branch.  It also
closes the internal comparison from that model to the original Selmer
length, representation points, trace differences and torus orbits.
These are complete chains on the original objects, not further names
for the same unresolved existence assertions.

The resulting sufficient cut for the paired counts is now:

  (a) literal SymplecticFourImageDense on the actual Good/OfCase data;
  (b) actual irreducible rigidity D.AllIrr D.RigidAt on each same datum;
  (c) the exact uniform finite automorphic catalogue required by SP9.

The three requirements remain substantial arithmetic inputs.  The first
supplies Fin0, NoEnd and one common embedding for all relevant points.
The second concerns every actual dual-number deformation of every
irreducible point.  The third is a uniformly bounded, integral, pure,
conjugation-compatible catalogue with the specified classification and
arithmetic Frobenius formula.  None follows just from the availability
of a universal presentation, a local chart or a finite geometric cover.

CountI and CountV remain separate actual arithmetic obligations.  The
previously completed implication from CountI/II/III/V to PointHV, and
then to the cohomological windows, is unchanged.  The global compatible
family, host, denominator, theta/type/rank, metric, spectral and final
gap requirements also remain.  The map therefore does not mark RH or
Dirichlet GRH, PointHV, or any of the four remaining counts complete.

dependency graph nor a minimality claim.  related_nodes annotations
indicate useful supplied tools or alternative source routes and are
explicitly not implication edges.  A universal theorem's antecedents
are distinguished from its proof dependencies and from the actual
arithmetic installation of those antecedents.

Every v0.26 node identifier and meaning is preserved.  A changed node
contains its complete previous record, and previous_obligation_map.json
is byte-identical to the prior map.  Current node counts are calculated

are checked against the pinned Git blobs; every new human proof range
is checked against its component ledger.  Source anchors carry the
full-file and range hashes and pinned URLs.  These integrity checks
support reproducibility and correct scope bookkeeping; they do not

The input manifest binds the component texts, ledgers, classical and

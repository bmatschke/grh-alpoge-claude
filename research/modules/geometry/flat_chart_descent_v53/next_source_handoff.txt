3. Most useful next bounded proof packet: geometric localization and gluing.

Prove the two literal section statements underlying
C/TwistedSectionsLocaliseToCharts.lean:24–50:

  (a) An actual twisted global section vanishing on the nonzero locus of
      sigma is killed, in its actual coordinates, by one power of sigma.
  (b) Every given section on that nonzero locus extends to a global
      twisted section after multiplication by a power of sigma.

The precise producer of (a) is
C/ExistsNhdsPowMulTwCoordEqZero.lean:48–62,
exists_pow_mul_twCoord_eq_zero. Local power-killing is first obtained
near each point; quasi-compactness chooses a finite subcover; the maximum
of its exponents gives one global power. Keep all line-bundle charts and
the exact coordinate identities.

For (b), the producer is C/ExistsTwExtendPowMul.lean:80–171,
exists_tw_extend_pow_mul. Its actual body first chooses a finite affine
cover subordinate to BOTH line bundles. It clears local denominators
using one common exponent N. On overlaps the resulting discrepancies
vanish on D(sigma); quasi-separatedness makes those overlaps compact,
so another common exponent K kills all discrepancies. The adjusted
coordinates glue to a section of M tensor H^(t+(N+K)d), with the required
restriction sigma^(N+K) times the original section. Track the transition
units through this adjustment; equal values on the open set alone do not
give the global gluing assertion. The original base need not be Noetherian.

Apply these results to the product section over every finite intersection
of the D_i, as the actual localization producer does. Then reconstruct
C/ExistsDehomogenisationTwistedChart.lean:127–189 and
C/ExistsTwistedSectionsCubeBijective.lean:45–63. Their output is an actual
semilinear dehomogenization and a comparison map gamma from the graded
localization cube to the section cube, compatible with all restrictions.
This is a complete supplier target, not another assumption called a
geometric comparison.

# Rationality and the lattice bound for the installed theta reading

Fix the real and series data of a first-pass column with its field conditions, and use global Artin reciprocity and rapid decay of cusp forms. Let $i$ satisfy the nonzero-index-place condition, $j$ be an interior row, and $b$ either side. Write $K_i=\operatorname{Frac}(O_i)$, $\iota:K_i\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ for the specified embedding, $\ell=\ell_i$, and $r_\nu$ for the actual reading at its stored scalar. The three rationality assertions concern the base generator over $K_i$, every admitted finite-character generator over its specified cyclotomic extension, and every universal coefficient over $K_i$. For the last assertion the auxiliary set $Q$ must lie in range and be coprime to the norm product of the support $s$. For each admissible $(s,h,P)$ there is also an integer $N\ge0$ such that
$$
 r_\nu(x)=\xi_{Q,M,a}(h,P)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \ell^N x\in B_{\rm sup}
$$
for **all** such $Q$, exponents $M$, and group-ring indices $a$. [The three tests][tests] · [The two conclusions supplied to export][export]

## Obtaining a single rational normalization

On a standard frame use the phase from the [tower expansion](FIRST_PASS_TOWER_SERIES_BOUND.md). For an individual pinned cover one can choose a rational prime larger than its auxiliary level plus one. Apply the short-series expansion with the entire embedded cover field as coefficient ring and divide the resulting identity by the nonzero power of this prime. This gives the rational section for each finite translate and each harmonic vector defined over that field. The phase comes from the fixed vacuum and harmonic comparison, before the cover is chosen. For an admissible change of splitting characters the source determinant contributes a scalar in the cover field. The target determinant gives a function on components defined over that field by ray reciprocity. Their product preserves rationality for the same phase. This establishes the full framed rationality input on the covers just described from the short-series theorem. [Untwisted comparison][untw] · [Division in the field][divide] · [Determinant twists][twist]

For the finite-character test take a cover containing the required marked and variable character values in the specified cyclotomic reading field. Each additional supported translate $h_Q$ makes $hh_Q$ a finite translate, so the expansion applies to the actual generator there. The right type law gives the invariance needed for descent and the harmonic expansion has coefficients in the reading field. The base test uses its fixed distinguished character. The universal test uses the coefficient formula below and the corresponding untwisted reading field. For a nonzero rational cusp space let $\alpha$ denote the geometric phase, $\beta$ the native phase at this embedding, and $c$ the nonzero harmonic scale. The conjugation law supplies a root of unity $\zeta$. Comparing a nonzero reference evaluation with its small-field expansion shows that every automorphism fixing the small field fixes $\zeta\beta/\alpha$. This quotient belongs to the small field and has modulus one. Multiplying $\alpha c$ by it preserves rationality and gives $\zeta\beta c$, whose power of order $\operatorname{ord}(\zeta)$ is the required power of $\beta c$. If the cusp space is zero the readings vanish and one can use the native normalized scalar directly. This constructs one normalized witness for all three tests. The defining implication for the installed scalar then gives all three tests for $r_\nu$. ## Extracting a coefficient integrally

Let $A$ be the selected half of the admissible conjugate pairs in $Q$, $H=(\mathbb Z/\ell^M)^A$, and $d(g)\in H$ the target determinant residue. In the group ring the coefficient of $[a]$ in $[d(g)]\sum_{t\in H}[t]f_t(g)$ is exactly $f_{a-d(g)}(g)$. Each local test counts subspaces of the residue vector space whose determinant minor is nonzero and has the prescribed class, divided by a power of the residue cardinality, a unit at $\ell$. Its fixed character factors are integral roots of unity. The identity
$$
 \xi_{Q,M,a}(g)=\sum_{t\in H}1_{d(g)=t}\,f_{a-t}(g)
$$
therefore permits termwise use of the same tower bound. The indicators are functions defined over the coefficient field, constant on open-and-closed unions of components and take integral values. Their multiplication preserves integral sections. There is exactly one surviving summand at each argument. Extracting the coefficient contributes no power of $\ell$ from the order of $H$ or of a ray class group. [Group-ring identity][coef] · [Local integrality][local] · [Integral component indicators][indicator] · [Sum over fibres][fiber]

Put $e=v_{O_i}(\ell)$ for the normalized additive valuation. The cover field lies inside $\iota(K_i)$, so the valuation induced by $O_i$ selects a place there. Its absolute ramification index $e_C$ divides $e$, by applying the restricted valuation to $\ell$ and to a local uniformizer. Choose the common nonzero scalar $u$ from the geometric phase and coefficient map. On a small-field harmonic generator the tower bound costs $c_2$ powers of $\ell$, chosen before $Q,M,a$. The coefficient section is invariant at the original compact level by cancellation of the determinant character with the finite test. The positive last weights and $n_V\ge8fr+4$ give the spanning by base cusp sections required for descent. Express the invariant reading in a primitive basis of the base local supremum lattice. Taking traces after multiplication by integral elements isolated at the selected prime puts each coordinate in the inverse relative different. [Choices in order][order] · [Invariance and cusp span][inv] · [Primitive coordinates and traces][trace]

The pinned bound $c_1=e^2$ clears this different in powers of $\ell$. One can sharpen it to $c_1=1+v_\ell(e)$ for $e>1$, and zero for $e=1$. In the completed cover field take a uniformizer with Eisenstein polynomial of degree $e_C$ over the maximal unramified subfield. The nonzero terms of its derivative at the uniformizer have distinct valuations modulo $e_C$. The leading term bounds the different exponent by $e_C-1+e_Cv_\ell(e_C)$. Transitivity bounds the relative different by the absolute one, while $v_C(\ell)=e_C$. This proves the asserted clearing power. [Pinned bound][different] · [Serre, *Local Fields*, chapter III, Remark 1 following Proposition 13, p. 58](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4757-5673-9_4)

Localizing the original integral core at the selected prime costs only units there. An admissible $P$ is a finite $O_i$-linear combination of the small-field harmonic generators as coefficient functions, and harmonic evaluation is injective on their space. For that combination take $C=c_1+\max c_2$, allowing zero for an empty list. The integral core is closed under sums, and a smaller exponent can be raised to the maximum. We have expressed $\ell^Cu\,\xi_{Q,M,a}$ as an integral combination of readings of the original core, uniformly in $Q,M,a$. [Localization][lattice] · [Extension in the polynomial][poly]

Finally set $\lambda=\nu^{-1}\ell^Cu$. For each coefficient with reading $r_\nu(x)$ the integral combination gives $x'\in B_{\rm sup}$ with $r_\nu(x')=\lambda r_\nu(x)$. The reading preserves linear independence after scalar extension to $\mathbb C$. Thus a nonzero $x$ forces $\lambda=\iota(\mu)$ with $\mu\in K_i^\times$ and $x'=\mu x$. Take $N=\max(0,\lceil v_{O_i}(\mu)/e\rceil)$. Then $\ell^N\mu^{-1}\in O_i$. If all coefficients vanish, injectivity gives $N=0$. The bound allows dependence on the row, side, support, translate, harmonic test and stored scale. To return to the supplied column, use rapid decay and the same size bound $8fr+4\le n_V$ to choose a component function and a central level translate on each interior row at the distinguished embedding. Use the old component functions and the identity translate at the other embeddings. These choices give one framed presentation simultaneously for every row and preserve the chosen extremal vectors. The comparison matches the supremum lattices and identifies the readings as maps, so it carries both conclusions to the supplied column. The separate toric-denominator input to export concerns values on the original order classes.

Substantive proofs and simplifications
-------------------------------------

The primitive dyadic character is constructed on the explicit cyclic
factor of (Z/2^n Z)^*.  Multiplication by a lower-conductor twist cannot
remove primitivity.  The quantitative separation argument turns a
vanishing character power into divisibility of an explicitly bounded
integer; this is the arithmetic needed by condD, rather than an appeal
to the output predicate itself.

The primeTo construction uses powers g^(ell^(n!)) in compact finite
quotients.  The limit has the required residue and kills the prime-to-ell
part in every actual continuous finite-dimensional lift.  The argument
does not assume an arbitrary discontinuous residual representation is
continuous: the needed residual continuity comes from the given lift.

For spPtL, the unnormalized polynomial projectors keep all formulas in
the original algebra.  Dividing an eigenvalue by a nonzero O-scalar is
handled in a finite integral algebra over the source complete Henselian
domain.  The given diagonal O-point shows that the divided element lies
in its maximal ideal, and every algebraic specialization therefore has
norm strictly less than one.  This proves the residual character
control without assuming denominators are units.  The source diagonal
point, domain hypothesis and original coefficient embedding are all
used explicitly.

At primes away from ell, the finite inertia image is diagonalized and
its eigencharacters are extended from that finite abelian subgroup to
the global finite character image.  Consequently the resulting global
characters satisfy the required KillsMu condition.  Merely producing
local inertia characters would not establish the source LevelAway
predicate.  At ell the ordinary flag and strict residual estimates give
the noncritical branch directly; no unstated potentially crystalline
comparison is needed for this branch.

# The exact coefficient base for the first pass

The base condition in P78C7 fixes a preliminary object and a character frame **before** the caller supplies two finite field requests. The first request can depend on the small field. The second can depend on both the small field and the fixed coefficient field just chosen. This order matters because the first-pass column will use the two requests for different coefficients. [P78C7](../../../archive/rounds/v78/continuation_v78/producer.tex#L218-L239) · [Definition of the base condition](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/NotBaseCondFalse.lean#L15-L20)

**Proposition.** The original `BaseCond KPx` holds in the stated eventual range. Write $F_s\subseteq F_0\subseteq L\subset\mathbf C$ for the images of the three coefficient fields. One can choose a single preliminary object $P$ over the given context and a frame $f$ on $P$ so that, for every pair of finite algebraic requests $S_1,S_2$, a base over this $P,f$ satisfies
$$
F_s=\operatorname{listField}(D,f),\qquad
S_1(F_s)\subseteq F_0,\qquad S_2(F_s,F_0)\subseteq L .
$$
Here $D$ is the field datum below. Every allowed ambient enlargement with the transported frame again satisfies `KPx`. Together with the [starting-column proposition](FIRST_PASS_COLUMN.md), this supplies the first two hypotheses listed in P78C7. ## The preliminary object and its small field

Choose the coordinate model of the diagonal spaces of the context. Its inclusion into the last space sends each coordinate vector to the coordinate vector of the same index. This is the required coordinate condition. The host-frame construction and the [successive-rank mass estimate](../construction/REMARK_NORMS.md#a-short-literature-proof-of-the-successive-rank-mass-ratio) give a character frame on this model in the eventual range. Fix such a frame $f$. Let $E$ be the embedded CM host, $d$ its distinguished discriminant prime and $K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-d})$. Choose the ray field $R/E$ for the ideal generated by $g_0g_1p_\delta p'_\delta$, and choose ideal-class generators of $E$ with orders and generators of their principal powers. For the remaining part of $D$, put $J=\mathbf Q(j(\mathcal O_K))$. The host has $d\equiv7\pmod {16}$, so $d>3$ and $K$ has prime discriminant $-d$. Gross constructs a CM curve over $J$ with a global minimal Weierstrass model of discriminant $-d^3$. To match the embedding of $J$ one may transport the model to the conjugate singular modulus of the principal lattice, using transitivity of the ideal classes on the CM conjugates. The pair $1,(1+\sqrt{-d})/2$ spans exactly $\mathcal O_K$. Localizing the global equation gives a minimal equation at every finite prime, with good reduction away from $d$. The local unit in the required discriminant equality may everywhere be taken to be $1$. These choices give $D$. Take $F_s$ to be the compositum prescribed in the source. Its factors are $E$ itself, $KJ$ with the coordinates of the curve's $12$-torsion and all twelfth roots of its discriminant, the ray field $R$, the specified cyclotomic fields, the normalized coefficient fields of $f$, and the fields $K_{\Psi,j}$ for $1\le j\le m$. ## The two ideal conditions inside this exact field

The Hilbert class field of $E$ lies in $R$ by divisibility of the moduli. Furtwängler's principal ideal theorem therefore gives a generator over $F_s$ of every extended ideal of $E$. For $p=p_\delta,p'_\delta$, the cyclotomic Artin action on $\zeta_p$ is by the absolute ideal norm modulo $p$. This action is trivial on ray principal ideals because a generator congruent to $1$ modulo $p\mathcal O_E$ has norm congruent to $1$ modulo $p$. Hence $E(\zeta_p)\subseteq R$. The class-number roots of unity also lie in $F_s$ via $K_{\Psi,1}$. [Ray-field and reciprocity argument](../construction/COMMON_DESIGN_EXISTENCE.md#identifying-the-ray-field-by-its-characters) · [Furtwängler, *Beweis des Hauptidealsatzes*, pp. 14–36](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02941157) · [Use of the ray field and principalization](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/RayFieldLeAndExistsBase.lean#L122-L137)

The second ideal condition prescribes the sizes of a single generator at **all** complex embeddings. Fix $j$. Its nonnegative exponents $r_\tau$ on the embeddings of $E$ have $r_\tau+r_{\bar\tau}=n$, where $n$ is the sum of the two entries of the $j$th signature. Put $N_r(\alpha)=\prod_\tau\tau(\alpha)^{r_\tau}$. Write $\mathfrak c_i$ for the chosen class representatives, $h_i$ for their orders and $\mathfrak c_i^{h_i}=(\gamma_i)$. The definition of $K_{\Psi,j}$ gives $\eta_i\in F_s$ with $\eta_i^{h_i}=N_r(\gamma_i)$. For any nonzero integral ideal $\mathfrak a$ write $\mathfrak a=(\alpha)\prod_i\mathfrak c_i^{e_i}$ with $e_i\in\mathbf Z$. Take
$$
\beta=N_r(\alpha)\prod_i\eta_i^{e_i},\qquad
\gamma=\alpha^h\prod_i\gamma_i^{e_i h/h_i},\qquad h=|\mathrm{Cl}(E)|.
$$
Then $\mathfrak a^h=(\gamma)$ and $\beta^h=N_r(\gamma)$. The host is Galois over $\mathbf Q$, so $F_s$ contains the images of all the maps $\tau$. The principal fractional ideal $(\beta)$ and $\prod_\tau(\tau\mathfrak a\,\mathcal O_{F_s})^{r_\tau}$ have the same $h$th power, hence are equal by unique factorization of fractional ideals. Each embedding $\sigma:F_s\hookrightarrow\mathbf C$ permutes the embedding pairs of the CM host on restriction. The balance condition gives
$$
|\sigma\beta|^{2h}=|N_{E/\mathbf Q}\gamma|^n
=(\mathrm N\mathfrak a)^{hn}.
$$
Taking positive $h$th roots yields the simultaneous size identity required by the base. ## Meeting the requests in order

Take a finite Galois extension inside $\mathbf C$ containing $F_s$ and the roots of unity of the ramification order and all the type-prime orders. Complex conjugation stabilizes it. With $F_s$ as the specified subfield, the two ideal properties and the roots verified above give an initial base. Enlarge its ambient field by the finite set $S_1(F_s)$ and take the normal closure. Use this whole field as the fixed coefficient field $F_0$. Now evaluate $S_2(F_s,F_0)$ and take a further finite normal closure $L$ containing this set and $F_0$. Embed the previously selected fields into $L$ by inclusion. Their images in $\mathbf C$ are exactly $F_s$ and $F_0$, and the preliminary object and frame still agree with the original choices. This proves the existence part. Any further allowed enlargement preserves the embedded $F_s$ and the coordinate data on $P$. Consequently the same $D$ witnesses the exact-field condition and the coordinate condition transports, as required by the enlargement part.

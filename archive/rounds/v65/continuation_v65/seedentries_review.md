* E65S1. Reducing the two rank-two factors gives a lower triangular first factor and a diagonal second factor with sign on the quadratic coset. In the specified four-dimensional basis the entries at positions 21 and 30 are respectively minus the sign times the rank-two lower entry and the lower entry, both multiplied by the scalar twist. The four diagonal formulas agree with the original order for both labels. Conjugation by the lower symplectic flag preserves each diagonal value. Its 10 position is a sign at each chosen place. These are precisely the two support conditions, residual character and determinant tests. The assertion does not assume the integral representation is unramified.
* E65S2. The ratio of marked residual characters at indices 0 and 3 equals the reduced determinant character and is distinct from one. The unit guaranteed by the absolute rank-two lattice survives any permitted field specialization. The displayed cocycle recurrence proves the conjugation identity when the initial marked lower entry vanishes. Normality of the later real tower field puts the resulting conjugate in its subgroup. The same element witnesses both entries on that tower; it may depend on the tower.
* E65S3. The ordinary slope comparison takes place between each chosen pair of prolongations using the actual element moving one place to the other. At a decomposition element separating the two ordinary characters, uniqueness of its eigenline yields the slope identity in the specified direction. Transport of the sign gives the other identity. Direct multiplication of the two elementary flag factors cancels both possible lower entries. Together with exact restriction of the representation this gives the source OverT assertion.
* E65S4. Any map killing the maximal ideal factors through the residue ring; hence each tensor matrix entry becomes locally constant even when the target is an arbitrary commutative ring. Before specialization each factor is locally constant modulo every prescribed maximal-ideal power, a property preserved under the integral inclusion and under multiplication. This proves both continuity clauses.

The construction of the arithmetic tensor datum and its automorphy remain separate from these four statements.

MATHEMATICAL FINDINGS

SE9. The construction uses F_v and F_v tensor_F E, with involution
1 tensor conjugation, its module topology and Borel structure, and the
integral closure of O_(F_v). It gives the literal rank, span, embedding,
density, valuation, scalar-field and principal-part additive-character
fields of exists_locAt. The trace is from the full finite algebra E_v
to Q_l, so the split case still has the sum of both factor traces.
The full-splitting hypothesis forces local degree one at every prime
over l; hence F_v=Q_l and E_v=F_v times F_v with swapped factors, and l
is a uniformizer. The chosen optional split datum is present exactly
at g0,g1. The argument does not assert that other primes fail to split.
Ordinary local completion is properly distinguished from LocStage
and its additional Fontaine/comparison data.

SE10. The inner-product convention is conjugate-linear in its first
variable. The displayed map uses <x,b_i>, so it is the required
conjugate-linear isometric embedding into the larger coordinate space.
Drows<=Dall is retained. The Cu metric is the actual positive class
metric, with the identity linear equivalence and exact inner-product
reading. The supplied gap and Boolean letter identities remain intact;
only the permitted auxiliary terms and predicates are chosen trivially.
The singleton Term/Pair types are nonempty, and all nine literal SStage
fields match the source constructor. No nonzero spectral term or estimate
is inferred from this formal stage.

SE11. For J=diag(1,-1), the displayed inverse
  X=(Z-J Z* J)/2, Y=-i(Z+J Z* J)/2
has X,Y in the stated real Lie algebra and X+iY=Z. This proves the
real-form decomposition with its exact orientation. If the pushforward
of lambda0 times lambda0 is a times entrywise complex volume, scaling
lambda0 by a^(-1/2) gives the required product measure, because both
factors are scaled.

The local logarithm/exponential argument is sufficient for the density
step: near identity, log(g*)=(log g)*, log(JgJ)=J(log g)J and
log(g^(-1))=-log g, so g*Jg=J forces the logarithm into the stated Lie
algebra. This supplies genuine local analytic coordinates. Translating
a positive density at identity makes a smooth left-invariant Radon
measure, and ordinary Haar uniqueness identifies each Haar measure with
a positive multiple of it. Its exponential-coordinate density is
continuous, positive and finite at zero. Bounding that density above
and below on the exact sum-of-entry-norm balls proves the required
ratio limit, not merely existence of an arbitrary normalizing scalar.
Local injectivity of exp identifies the measured image with the exact
set in OmegaDensity.

The zero-dimensional case is separately handled by unit point masses.
For compact U(N), closedness and boundedness give compactness and allow
probability normalization. Scaling each noncompact signature by the
ratio of the compact density to its own density gives the same finite
nonzero constant as U(P+Q). Keeping the compact measure itself when Q=0
satisfies the probability clause. All four CDHaar fields follow.
Finite-dimensional change of variables, local matrix power-series
calculus and Haar existence/uniqueness remain explicit background.
This does not establish a global mass formula or external volume
normalization.

SE12. The no-variable-place model has one lattice tuple and trivial
group action. Its HtupW is exactly the O-span of 1. Evaluation at the
constant coefficient is injective, and every allowed vector of functions
is a vector of O-valued constants, proving surjectivity as well. Thus the
actual integral module is O^d, not merely a vector space of equal rank.
Its classes are finite in every component, and d=0 is included.
Using O=Z_l and Kf=Q_l supplies the prescribed residue-characteristic
GAtP model and exact d field.

The general GJ construction retains a positive volume function, CDHaar,
the actual ThetaJet and GlobalExtra, and nonempty adjacent class sets.
Lying over supplies a prime with the prescribed residue characteristic;
the supplied positive CM-type cardinality supplies each Tau label.
All prescribed classes and jet data can therefore be chosen. In the
bare existence construction both situation types are empty and the
volumes/extra scalars are 1; no adjacent class or inert-prime choice is
needed. The source's stronger baseVol=trueVol conclusion is explicitly
excluded unless its positivity and geometric identification are supplied.

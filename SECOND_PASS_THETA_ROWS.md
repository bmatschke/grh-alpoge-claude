# Theta rows and the strengthened third level on the retwisted column

Fix the enlarged pinned column furnished by [the retwisting construction](SECOND_PASS_RETWIST.md), a built untwisted origin, and the seed exported from that origin. The geometric extension gives an actual starting datum on the prescribed column. Given the general row, series and finiteness hypotheses in the [pinned second-pass theorem][assembler], its continuation gives a third level whose own characters and theta data satisfy the good-twist inequalities simultaneously for both sides and all prime-to-residue-characteristic powers.

## The geometry and its support

Write $z$ for the old fine datum, $W=W_1(z,F_0)$ for its active set and $u$ for restriction of coefficient places. The old intersection law gives its weaker $\mathrm{HoldsL}$ comparison. On the new pinned column take the permitted group stage and the required volume on that group. Green's finite-group character input supplies the auxiliary cuspidal type. The [fine extension](THETA_FINE_EXTENSION.md) now extends this particular $z$ to $z'$ on the new stage. Among its identifications are the reference theta readings, integral reference rows and signed realizations. Other levels and the new tuples carry their own theta maps. The extension assertion applies before any new third level has been constructed. [Extended fine datum and its group][geo]

For a place $v'$ above $v$, apply the nonzero-index comparison first to the identically zero forced function. It identifies the union of the Iwasawa and reference-row contributions, since zero has neither nonzero content nor conjugate nonzero content. For the actual $F_0$, extension of the local valuation compares the nonzero forced-content contribution, including its conjugate. Taking the union proves

$$
 W_1(z',F_0')=u^{-1}(W).
$$

Every place upstairs has a restriction and its residue characteristic equals that of its restriction. Thus the new active set lies above the allowed twist primes and the character off it is trivial. These are the support assertions in the geometric alternative of $\mathrm{Start}$. Restrict the adelic datum from all the allowed places to this active set. Conjugation stability gives a legitimate new datum and restriction preserves its exact active set, eight maps, geometric realization, core and standard reading. On the restricted datum apply the general weight-case comparison to obtain its intersection law and construct its row-lift family in the resulting preliminary package $P'$. The extension from the old datum composes with this restriction, so it ends at the very $P'.z$ used below. ## Rational tests on the new tuples

Roadmap B8.17–18 give the bottom rational-test input under their specified theta and period hypotheses. Their finite coefficient request can depend on the prehost, frame and the **final finite twist-character set**. It can therefore be included in $S_2(P,f,\Xi)$ before the final field is chosen. The [application on this retwisted row](SECOND_PASS_BOTTOM_TESTS.md) verifies the place exclusions, pin, coefficient request and transfer back from the auxiliary size stage. It identifies where period separation, the functional equation and the local and global analytic comparisons enter. [Request and precise stage conditions][req] · [All-tuples test statement][roadstmt]

At the fixed real pair and smooth tensor, choose sufficient Bessel columns to separate the bottom row space. Vanishing on all the columns implies vanishing for every real pair by the common-real-factor property. Period detection then gives separation. For each tuple $t$ project a rational source vector $x$ to its good summand $x'$. The theta image is unchanged. If the common real scalar $\kappa\ne0$, normalize every test by $c_{\mu_0}/\kappa$. Its value on the row becomes

$$
 \frac{v_i(t,x')}{\iota_H(z_t)\,(c_t/c_{\mu_0})},
$$

where the numerator, character phase and normalized nonzero ratio lie in the embedded field. If $\kappa=0$ all these theta evaluations vanish and the original separating functionals suffice. Conjugate the evaluations when the real orientation requires it. This proves coefficient-field values on images at every $t$ for a stage meeting B8.18. The pin uses bottom row rationality along with local multiplicity and the integral identity to obtain the weight comparisons. A second use of rationality in the construction asks for an entire Hecke record on the fitted level. Its general producer uses the all-state isotypic assertion, the series package and the third-layer inputs. These are two distinct statements. [Normalized Bessel values][roadproof] · [Row rationality in multiplicity comparisons][weights] · [Hecke rationality at all states][inrat]

The bottom period tests act on the common target $X.EE$. On each individual theta layer $X.W_j$, use the distinguished rational coordinates of the geometric realization. Their dual coordinate functions take values in the given embedded field on all rational theta images and on both levels of prescribed bases on this restricted datum. ## Fitting the characters without changing the row

In the pin the starting choices on $P'$ give a bare state, a compatible Galois level, row multiplicities, the Hecke rationality record and cuspidality. The new stage has the old Galois comparison by composition of the two group identifications. Using the old intermediate level, the extension to $P'.z$ and the preserved column parameters, the transport theorem gives a state $y$ over $P'$, its new intermediate level and a comparison with the exported original data. It preserves every previously pinned grid at its original depth, with its field values, roots and tie to the level characters. [New state and its two transports][carry] · [Transport of the grid][grid] · [Order at the caller][new]

Choose at $v'$ the seed index assigned to $u(v')$. The comparison transports its reading, and restriction commutes with conjugation, so the fitted character level has exactly that index function. The active support equality and original saturation give saturation on this column. The finiteness-with-list theorem can therefore retain both the grid and the fitted character through an extension with origin. Its general proof requires the archimedean comparison, the forced local $\eta$ identities for all row labels and places, and actual theta data meeting their laws on the state. It quantifies over all starting columns, including this $P'$. [Finiteness hypotheses and constructed rows][fin] · [Fit and retain the index][fit]

On the resulting third level each active place lies over an active old place. Its fitted character has the transported conductor and exponent. The old and new embeddings can read the same character through different primitive roots of unity. Their ratio of exponents is a unit modulo $\ell^k$. Checking at Frobenius elements representing every class in the ray quotient identifies the new raw character with a single prime-to-$\ell$ power of the old one at this place. A further signed power $c$ corresponds to an old exponent of absolute value $|a|\,|c|$, again prime to $\ell$. Apply the seed inequality for that exponent and transport across the carried and retained records. This also preserves the disjointness of conductors at distinct indices. The source augments the norm-extension field of **this very third level** by the resulting $\mathrm{GoodO}$ witness. Its group, rows, rational readings, character and theta data are the original fields of that level. [Root and Frobenius comparison][raw] · [Transport of all powers and disjointness][powers] · [Same third level, strengthened norm slot][fit]

The [retwisted row state](SECOND_PASS_ROW_STATE.md) and [localized readings](SECOND_PASS_THETA_REALIZATION.md) match these constructions to the actual data under their stated analytic and Galois hypotheses. From a fitted level with ramified series transport, [the fourth-stage construction](SECOND_PASS_GOOD_CORE.md) preserves goodness on the same theta family. The remaining design problem includes that series comparison at nontrivial conductors and the [division, period and eigensystem comparisons at a prescribed size](PRESCRIBED_SIZE_BUNDLE.md).

\section{The rational base lift and the final analytic record}

% BEGIN V77Q1
\paragraph{V77Q1. A rational base vector before uniform spanning.}
Use the specific $R_{\rm geo}$ constructed in A77G3, the ordered
prime equality, and the original model laws. For every row $i$ and
every rational input $x$ to the base definite lift there is a
coordinate vector $y\in L^{n_i}$ such that
\[
 (R_{\rm geo}.FW_i).j_{\sigma_*}(y)
   =R.\mathrm{thG}_{i,\mu_0}(R.jV_{i,\mu_0}(x)).
\]
Here $\sigma_*=e$ is the distinguished embedding. The finite sum
in the proof is over the original $L$, which can be larger than the
core coefficient field $K$.

Take the projection of the base lift on the right and apply the
model embedding into multilinear automorphic functions. Call the
result $\Theta$. The first part of $\mathrm{Fired}(1)$ contains
the base row's phase-rational reading identity. Applied to $x$
it supplies finitely many $a_l\in L$, $f_l\in C_i$, such that
on the distinguished extremal vector and every adelic argument $q$,
\[
 \Theta(v_0)(q)=
    \sum_l e(a_l)\,\ell_i\,\psi_T(f_l)(q).
\]
Here $\ell_i$ is the prescribed Lie factor and $\psi_T$ is the
core reading with the installed $c_E$. These constants and the
phase are precisely those in $\mathrm{Fired}(1)$. The identity in particular holds on representatives for the rational
quotient, as required in the model evaluation.

The distinguished embedding restricts to $T.\sigma_0$ by the
algebra tie. It is good, and the core space law identifies its
row Hermitian space with $W_c(D,i)$. By G77D2 an evaluation lift
at this embedding exists. Hence the good branch of $j_{\rm Top}$
actually evaluates on $v_0$ as $\phi_C(f)=\ell_i\psi_T(f)$.
Take
\[
                    y=\sum_l a_l\operatorname{coord}(f_l).
\]
A77G3 and semilinearity give exactly the displayed function for
the evaluation of $J_e(y)$ on $v_0$. Evaluation on the prescribed
unit tuple is injective on the row Hilbert space (G77D2), so this
is equality of the two Hilbert vectors. The empty sum gives zero
by the same argument. Thus the rational-vector clause required
of $\mathrm{GeoExV}$ is already available on this constructed
record. The other embeddings and the assertion that these maps
span each target do not enter this argument.

\paragraph{V77Q2. Choosing the weak analytic data with the reading in place.}
Fix the original column, its stages, prime set and small-field
core. Suppose given the source preliminary seed $R_0$ with the
chosen finite character set $X_g$, initial trace $t_0$, residue
group isomorphism $c_G$, the level, inert and tuple pins,
all-place clauses, the conditions $\mathrm{Keep}$ and the
ordinary and lift readings $\mathrm{BrgStd}$. B77R1--B77R4 give
these reading maps and their Hecke comparisons from the indicated
coefficient presentations and action laws. B77R5 constructs the
split neighbor representatives simultaneously for every translate.
The full-level double-coset and inert operator comparisons of B77R4
are still used. Use the original
analytic model and kernel inputs ($\mathrm{Inputs}$,
$\mathrm{IdemClass}$, $\mathrm{LiftsAt}$ and $\mathrm{SeedFacts}$)
in the weak analytic construction. We record how that construction
selects $R_1$ while preserving these readings.

In the existence statement for the model update, carry the following
property of the record being built. With the inert labels, splitting
data and auxiliary scalar labels of $R_0$, it has the inert, tuple
and all-place properties. Its level character record is equal to
that of $R_0$; after identifying prime sets its reduction
isomorphism is the old one. Its additive character, selected
tuple and twist-place fields have their old values. The original
reading implies the new reading, and $\mathrm{Keep}$ holds for
every group identification equal after this transport to $c_G$.
Initially all assertions follow from the seed and the identity maps.

First install the Hilbert models, measures and their comparisons by
the preliminary model constructor. Its bridge hypothesis uses the
seed's actual definite reading maps. In the subsequent tower
kernel update, install the vacua and extremal vectors; then choose
the chain intertwiner, the multiplicity map depending on it, and
the last projected map in that order. Make the analogous updates
for the row kernel and choose the row intertwiner, its theta map
and its projection in order. In the weak clauses an existential
choice is taken when its defining predicate has a witness, with
the specified zero alternative otherwise. At each of the three
types of update the data occurring in the carried property are
preserved by the record formulas and the retained reading
implication. In particular the model update does not reselect the
underlying ordinary and definite coefficient readings. The result
is $R_1$ with $\mathrm{Pre1}$, weak chain, weak row theta, full
$Y$ and full $P$ kernel clauses and the carried property. Equality
of the level records transports $c_G$ to the required group over
$R_1$. The source analytic existence inputs to these constructors
are hypotheses of this selection.

\paragraph{V77Q3. Preserving the same datum through the scalar choices.}
Assume the preceding seed construction. For its admissible weak
records suppose the native existence clauses can be discharged.
Also assume that for the resulting preliminary characterization
one can supply the adapted core and base comparisons of
P77R1--A77G3 with their stated inputs. Apply existence first to
$R_1$. Its weak chain and row laws then give the strong laws needed
by $\mathrm{PreChar}$. The old character value and ordered prime
pins pass to $R_1$ by its level equality. Apply A77G3 and V77Q1
here. This supplies the corrected $n_i,FW_i$, the group character
law, a geometric half with phase one, and the rational base vector
on each corrected row.

Install these two target fields. The seed's coefficient readings,
the level record and its group identification still agree with the
initial ones. With these target fields fixed, choose a trace which
satisfies the source separation and trace semantics if such a trace
exists. Otherwise retain $t_0$: the trace requirement in the pins is
itself conditional. Set the phase to one. For each row and
eigenvalue label take the scalar from its base or off-base eigenvalue
condition if witnessed, otherwise take the stated exponential
of the size parameter. On a nonzero eigenspace an eigenvalue of
the stipulated operator is unique by evaluation at a nonzero
vector. The scalar $\kappa$ in the product formula is similarly
unique when its nonzero test holds: evaluate at a nonzero vector
in the indicated space and cancel the nonzero product of row
scalars. Use the native zero fallback if this test has no witness.
These choices are exactly the guarded scalar laws.

The target-field update and the final scalar update preserve the
preliminary, weak and full kernel clauses. The scalar update carries
the geometric half by copying its tie, level action, component rows
and their proofs; it changes no functions in those proofs. V77Q1
gives the phase condition on the final data because the
embedding-conjugate of one is one. Apply the assumed
map-existence construction \emph{again} to the final weak record.
This produces $\mathrm{ExAt}$ on the record whose pins, geometric
half and scalar laws have just been constructed. Applying existence
only on the preliminary record would leave this last conclusion
without its required witness.

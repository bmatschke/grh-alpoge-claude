Scope and result
---------------

This component contains fifteen human proofs, BTH1–BTH15, for the
original base tuple t0=A3.A.mu0 on a supplied selected Bundle. All are
category B arguments with their actual-data hypotheses explicit. They
reduce the base theta part of SmallLevelRankEq to three coupled category C
producers. RowsHold0_3 remains a fourth, separate C producer on the same
Bundle. No additional finite covariance assumption remains.

The Lean source is assumed fully checked and correct. This component
does not replay Lean and does not identify a formal-source gap. Its
remaining obligations concern the human reconstruction.

Completed mathematical contributions
------------------------------------

BTH1 proves that the nonzero lowest compact isotypic space contains a
nonzero smooth vector, using compact-conjugation-invariant smoothing.
It then proves cyclicity in the exact Sobolev topology used by the
source. Hilbert-space irreducibility alone would not justify applying
a merely Sobolev-continuous functional; the smoothing argument supplies
the missing bounded Hilbert functional and then a smooth approximate
identity.

BTH2 applies that cyclicity one archimedean tensor factor at a time.
The original multiplicity maps are therefore determined by their
lowest-type values. Right translations are restricted to the matched
finite lifts that normalize the prescribed principal flag level and
preserve its character.

BTH3 proves absolute convergence of the actual original lowest-type
theta integral. The finite double-coset representatives already stored
in ClassPres and the positive definite source real group make the
relevant quotient compact. A finite Haar-volume unfolding argument,
the actual coefficient basis, and the original Weil continuity and
rational automorphy laws give the required bounded integrand. This
uses neither a new class-number theorem nor an IsIoW producer.

BTH4 shows that extensions for a finite basis of the original HV suffice
to construct a simultaneous linear thF. The original weak OrZero choice
therefore equals that map. Pulling the good-projected lift through the
injective HW realization also constructs the original thG, so no second
independent lift-existence problem remains.

BTH5 proves the exact inverse finite-level character. It retains the
crossed splitting characters, the same upper and lower determinant
blocks, and the half-datum change G A H^{-1}. The scalar is fixed by
BTH15, not by identifying an arbitrary supplied action with a canonical
one without proof. The source eta character satisfies IsEtaTilde:
the two universal rank restrictions and the odd rank difference give
the required quadratic restriction, while the original character
reading supplies its infinity and finite unramified properties.
Finite/creation-operator commutation is justified through Haar-L2
isometry and difference quotients, rather than continuity on the
ambient space of all functions.

BTH6 proves unitarity and theta equivariance of the actual target finite
action. The multiplicity pairing's scalar is unique after evaluating
on a tensor of nonzero smooth vectors, so its conditional definition is
preserved as well. Missing target lifts are handled by the source's
zero-action branch and invertibility: the relevant HW must then be
zero. Source lifts come from the already supplied ActTie.red_surj and
grp_red_L, using exists_finLift, without a new parahoric existence
theorem. The exact central character idempotent is the sum over all
the stored distinct irreducible characters.

BTH7 proves the original base Hilbert reading T1 by an explicit full
tensor witness, namely the lift of the actual thF after the inverse
source realization. It retains the actual geometric LiftTie.theta
premise in the remaining reading producer.

BTH8 shows that one finite base LiftTestB family simultaneously gives
the rational theta kernel and the rational adjoint image. Invertibility
of the original rational Gram matrix ensures that a K-basis becomes a
complex basis under the original isometric realization.

BTH9 gives the complete finite unitary idempotent orthogonal-kernel
argument. It does not assume that the source idempotent is self-adjoint.

BTH10 supplies the original T2 kernel statement with the exact inverse
source character and metric normalization. The class-form sum equals
|P| times the original level Hermitian form, not its reciprocal.

BTH11 obtains the required target containment from T1 and the basis
already stored in ThDef3. The same rational-test family then identifies
the actual relMat/kMat coordinates. The stronger PLevW, LUnif, and H5
common-image packages are unnecessary for this base consumer.

BTH12 combines these statements with Pi_i E_i=1 and the original
orthogonal-complement identity. It proves the exact base square

  D.thetaP(mu0,i+1,D.jMt(mu0,i+1)(Y.eMt(i,t0)x))
       = J_i(M_i Pi_i x)

for every rational coordinate x and every i<m. With RowsHold0_3 on the
same Bundle, SLR8 gives the base rank equality.

Original finite-action normalization
------------------------------------

The independent review initially found that canonical vacuum fixing
could not simply be applied to an arbitrary supplied IsWeilDatumO.
BTH13–BTH15 reconstruct the necessary comparison and remove that
additional producer.

BTH13 proves a continuous adelic unitary character trivial on rational
points and every split-supported finite element is trivial. The form
may be indefinite.

For a rational Hermitian projector P and norm-one u=alpha/bar(alpha),
the parameters

  u_n = (1+n! alpha)/(1+n! bar(alpha))

approach u at infinity and 1 at every fixed finite place. At all
nonsplit primes they are units. After removing the finitely many split
components where the corresponding reflection or inverse is
nonintegral, the remaining finite elements lie in ONE fixed compact
lattice stabilizer and converge there to 1. Rational and split
triviality therefore imply triviality on the infinite component of
every rational reflection. The elementary two-reflection generation
argument and the Cayley weak-approximation chart give all of the
infinite factor.

The second half generalizes the QD13–QD14 correction to every
nondegenerate Hermitian form. Norm-one parameters are approximated at
the designated primes and made close to 1 at nonsplit projector
denominator primes. This yields a rational/split/congruence
factorization with the last factor in principal n! congruence.
The already established infinite triviality then proves finite
triviality. No claim that a continuous character on the full adelic
group is locally constant is used.

BTH14 compares two full Weil actions with the same characters on the
same original theta functions. The Haar-unitary Heisenberg scalar lemma
CW15 gives a continuous circle-valued ratio character. Evaluation at
zero after a Heisenberg translate proves its rational triviality.
CW3's finite projectors produce a nonzero split-half vacuum, so the
WA5 coinvariant argument is applicable. The positive real scalars
in the two actual om_split clauses force the ratio to be one on
split-supported pairs. BTH13 gives equality everywhere.

BTH15 proves the exact finite stabilizer character for the original
action. At a split place, the difference between its vacuum image
and the positive scalar times the prescribed splitting factor is
fixed by both half-lattices. A nonzero such difference would be detected
by the WA5 half-coinvariant, contradicting om_split. Haar norm then
makes the positive scalar exactly one.

At a nonsplit place, compare the original action with the CW23
canonical action twisted by WA18. BTH14 justifies that comparison.
A norm-one determinant a has a unit Hilbert-90 root x=b+a bar(b):
choose b so its residue is nonzero, using the nontrivial residue
involution in the original cmUnram condition. The unramified
characters kill this unit root. The original IsLatVac guard gives all
local Heisenberg fixing because every dyadic place is split, so 2 is
invertible at the present nonsplit place. Finally finite-support
truncation and the original pointwise continuity extend the result
to all finite lattice stabilizers.

Exact remaining producer contract
----------------------------------

For every family Fm in the selected class A, choose one threshold a_Fm.
For every x>=a_Fm with x>Fm.x1 and every alpha in Fm.Ix x, all of the
following must refer to the SAME selected Bundle
b=sigma(Fm,x,alpha), with the same original maps, fields, kernels,
measures, coefficient models, and weak choices.

I. ORIGINAL BASE READING — C, open.
Supply the actual R,F,Read,ReadL, weak characterization, phase-one Pins,
and Ok block data, identified with the stored A3,Zs,S0 and ThDef3
maps and basis. In particular the geometric LiftTie.theta reading
is required. Equal dimensions of unrelated realizations do not suffice.

II. ORIGINAL SMOOTH CUSPIDAL EXTENSION — C, open.
For each i<m, a basis of the actual HV(i,t0) must have its exact
lowest-type integral extended to the prescribed original Wall_i.
Convergence, finite linear assembly, and the original thG construction
are now proved. Smooth cuspidal multiplicity extension is not.

III. ORIGINAL BASE RATIONAL TESTS — C, open.
For each i<m, one finite separating complex-linear test family must
be rational over the exact sigma(K) simultaneously on every original
rational full-source theta image and every original target-coordinate
basis vector. Finite-dimensionality alone does not imply this
simultaneous rationality.

IV. SAME-BUNDLE BASE ROWS — C, open and separate.
Supply RowsHold0_3 on that same Bundle. The theta square does not
construct its primitive row package.

I–III imply the actual base-tuple thTheta square. Adding IV gives the
SLR8 rank consequence. For the universal family supplier the threshold
is max(a_Fm,Fm.x1+1). If only a source Closed supplier is established,
its own epsilon and K bounds must be retained; this component does not
convert it into a universal supplier.

No fifth finite-covariance producer survives BTH13–BTH15. None of
I–IV has been marked complete merely because these conditional
consequences are complete.

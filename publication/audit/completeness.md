## What is left of the strict Selmer bound

Fix the level $N$, twist $e$, character kernel $H$ and excluded auxiliary prime $p$. Let $F$ be the cyclic field cut out by $H$, let $d=[F:\mathbf Q]$, and let $P_d$ project to the characters of exact order $d$. At each eligible coefficient prime $\ell$, ETS11 proves that the orbit sum is $m_\ell=\operatorname{length}_{\mathbf Z_\ell}P_d(K_{2e}(\mathcal O_F)\otimes\mathbf Z_\ell)$. The least positive integer satisfying the simultaneous factor-two valuation inequalities is $M=\prod_{\ell\ne p}\ell^{\lceil m_\ell/2\rceil}$, over the eligible primes. Only primes dividing the order of the finite K-group can contribute. For the fixed quadratic field $K$ of this argument, the outstanding estimate is $\log M\le c_K\varphi(d)[N(e+1)]^{A_K}\log p$ with constants independent of the remaining data. The original selected-window argument could instead be proved by bounding the weighted sums just on the windows it uses.

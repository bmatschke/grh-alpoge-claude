{\Large\bfseries Finite points on the marked rank-three space}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.67 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The shared marked theorem needs a finiteness statement for all
weight-zero points over a fixed upper field. A rank-two space
controls the exceptional primes of the rank-three space. To reach
the other primes, we connect the triangular points by explicit
polynomial curves and show how a seed reaches a full component.
The final chapter explains how these geometric results apply to
arithmetic ideals and which inputs are still required on the marked
frame.

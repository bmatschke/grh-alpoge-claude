# Review of integral

At places over the level prime the image is taken in the complex realization and then intersected with the rational subspace. No rationality of a global Hecke operator is needed for this finite-generation step. Clearing one local denominator gives a lower lattice. The shift uses the negative integral floor and the row lattice uses the tuple at that place. The resulting finite set of prime ideals is specific to the member.

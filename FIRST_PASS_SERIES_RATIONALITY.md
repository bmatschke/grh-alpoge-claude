# Reading the active class sums over the prescribed field

The [first-pass character comparison](FIRST_PASS_ACTIVE_CHARACTERS.md) uses the actual theta map on definite forms at each active place. The [series construction](FIRST_PASS_THETA_SERIES.md) identifies this map with a class sum on the subspace where that sum admits the given coefficient reading. Here we give a finite sufficient test for that equality at each active row and trace the arithmetic normalization of its generators. The comparison of phases also requires the chosen row normalization. ## A finite test on the original coefficient space

Fix the index, row and side. Write $k_0=\operatorname{Frac}(O_{i,0})$ and take the semilinear reading into complex functions with the embedding used to define the series at cyclotomic level zero. For this argument keep the coefficients in that field until the map has been identified. Equality extends to larger coefficient fields by scalar extension. The extra conductor set is empty and the variable character is trivial. The fixed order-$15$ character $\delta_{\ell_i}$ remains in the Weil action.

Choose a finite spanning family in the integral lattice of definite source forms. For each definite double class choose a representative supported at split places away from the exceptional primes. To do so, first take a principal congruence subgroup inside the definite level, and use the split-representative theorem for this subgroup. Moving from the old representative to the chosen one uses a rational group element on the left and a level element on the right. The level and polynomial transformation factors for the form and its kernel cancel in the series term. On the split representative the value of an integral source form lies in the span of the translated harmonic coefficient lattice over the indicated ring. Expand each of the finitely many values as a finite sum of its generators. This gives a finite set of pairs $(h,P)$ on this row. [Split representatives][reps] · [Cancellation of the level factors and expansion in the small harmonic lattice][terms]

It suffices to read the ordinary base theta function for each of these pairs in the normalized target reading. Indeed the extra set is empty, so the twisted finite generator at the identity translation is the same base function, with the fixed distinguished-prime test still in it. Add the finitely many terms with their coefficients and the rational factors $1/|\Gamma_h|$, where $\Gamma_h$ is the stabilizer. The image of the target reading is closed under addition and scalar multiplication by the image of $k_0$. The analytic class sum for every chosen source form therefore belongs to that image. Since the lattice spans, the same follows for every source vector. The defining equality for the stored class-sum map now holds on this entire row and hence after scalar extension. This proof asks for no bound on the valuations of the stabilizer factors. Both the spanning family and its finite set of tests depend on the row and the place. There is also a shorter dependency in the pinned general argument. It proves rationality of three sets of generators for a single allowable normalization, at base level, at admitted finite twists, and for the universal finite-group coefficients. Separately it proves a bound for the latter coefficients. On moving back from the standard frames the rationality implication uses the normalized generators on the standard frames, global reciprocity and the existing geometric hypotheses. The uniform prime-power integrality theorem enters the proof of the separate bound. The two good-prime Hecke assertions in the combined state construction already have their joint supplier in the [moduli-point proof](FIRST_PASS_MODULI_POINTS.md). For the class-sum equality itself the general argument uses the twisted-generator part of rationality, at the one selected twist. To fix the phase of the actual reading it also uses existence of a normalization with the prescribed power of the vacuum scalar. ## The distinguished conductor and the phase

On the cover carrying the fixed distinguished conductor, let $K$ denote the image in $\mathbf C$ of $\operatorname{Frac}(O_i)$ for this side. Let $H$ be the finite group in the marked level character and choose $n_\delta$ annihilating the values of the fixed character. Starting with the fixed normal small field, adjoin the roots of $X^{|H|n_\delta}-1$ that belong to $K$. This gives a finite field $L$ contained in the actual reading field. All selected character values are in it. It is normal over $\mathbf Q$. Indeed the roots in $K$ form a finite subgroup of the roots of unity and generate a cyclotomic field. The ray-field and splitting conditions on the first-pass state give the required cover with complex coefficient field $L$. Its auxiliary level is $p_{\delta_{\ell_i}}$, the distinguished rational prime selected at this index. The two marked-prime residue characters and the fixed $\delta_{\ell_i}$ test now have values in $L$. This works even with no additional variable primes. [Small field, roots and cover][cover] · [Values of the finite level function][vals] · [Original ray field](FIRST_PASS_RAY_CLASS_FIELD.md)

For the underlying untwisted Weil action, write $\alpha$ for the unit complex scalar from the harmonic coefficient map and $\beta$ for the unit scalar from the vacuum identity. Both are fixed before the cover and its test are chosen. In the geometric comparison, the product $(\alpha\beta)^{-1}$ corrects the raw theta series. For a level function $\Phi$ over the cover field and a harmonic polynomial with coefficients there, the normalized short-series expansion has the shape
$$
 p^c(\alpha\beta)^{-1}\Theta(h,P)=
     \sum_t a_t\,\mathrm{rd}_C(s_t),
 \qquad a_t\in L,\quad s_t\in H^0_{\rm cusp}(C,\mathcal V).
$$
Here $p$ can be an auxiliary prime larger than the auxiliary level plus one. The geometric reading includes the fixed Lie-algebra, component and character factors. The span of rational sections on the right is an $L$-vector space and $p^c\ne0$, so division gives a rational section on that cover with exactly the chosen phase. The ingredients for the short generators include chart independence of harmonic evaluation, algebraic descent and comparison of the finite Fourier sum with the normalized lattice. The earlier manuscript carries these out on the identity cover. For the base generators one can choose the distinguished-conductor cover to match the Fourier translates. This gives the [expansion with its actual frame and component factors](FIRST_PASS_DISTINGUISHED_COVER.md). The remaining fixed twist has an explicit effect. If $h,g$ are the source and target arguments, respectively, changing the two splitting characters multiplies the series by $d_V(h)^{-1}d_W(g)$, with the determinant characters defined through Hilbert 90. The source value at fixed $h$ belongs to $L$. The target multiplier is constant on the finite components. Its value is invariant under rational translation, the real connected action relevant to the reading, and the principal level. Global reciprocity and the ray-field condition identify these componentwise multipliers with functions defined over the cover field. Multiplying the untwisted section by them preserves the phase chosen above. The exact type law implies invariance of the resulting reading under the lifts of the identity in the marked quotient. Under the prescribed size bound $8fr+4\leq n_V$ the descent on the cover then writes it as an $L$-linear combination of readings of the original rational row. The inclusion $L\subseteq K$ gives the result for this geometric normalization in the specified coefficient field. The fixed local reading here requires the smaller $L$, the chosen additive character and the common phase of the harmonic and vacuum identifications.

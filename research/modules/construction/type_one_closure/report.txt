Scope and conventions
---------------------
This component proves the source Conclusion2 / projection_lemma_base_level
for the actual local polynomial representations, with explicit sufficient
constants. It consumes the proved RU, RD, HM and NP components and earlier
CB/IW/EL/SW/RL, not an unproved AdmB or AdmT record. It does not assert that
the simultaneous global arithmetic data satisfying HypI have been supplied.
The full RH and all-Dirichlet-GRH reconstructions remain incomplete.

Let O be the source complete DVR, F its fraction field, w its uniformizer,
and w^2=p. Write v_p(w)=1/2. A weight lambda has normalized edge pairing
  be_lambda(x,y)=p^{A_lambda}y(x), A_lambda=Ar(1,lambda).
The midpoint lattices R_e,R'_e are exactly dual for this pairing (HM8).
CCL_lambda(t,c,e) is the intersection of w^{-t}HL(source), R_e, and
w^c R_f for all other same-source rank-one edges f. Its dual analogue
CCL'_lambda uses w^{-t}HL(target)^*, R'_e and the other same-target edges.
A FactorsLE certificate of n factors at depth D consists of n vectors
and n dual vectors in the named lattices whose pairing matrix becomes
z I_n after an integral column operation, with z!=0 and v_p(z)<=D.
The zero certificate is always allowed. RhoL means such certificates
on every edge with n at least a specified real lower bound.

Put q=p^{n'}, k=m+1, G=(q^k-1)/(q-1), and
  d(lambda)=dim_F V(lambda)/G.
HM5 counts G incoming or outgoing rank-one edges at each lattice and
transports certificates between any two edges. The one-sided upper bound
also proved there is: n factors between CCL_lambda(t,c,e) and R'_e at
depth D>=0 imply n<=d(lambda) if 2D<c. Dual cleaning is not needed for
that upper bound. This distinction is used in TC1.

1. TC1: PRIMITIVE RETRACTION, SMITH SPLITTING, AND CLOUD COUNT
------------------------------------------------------------
Use the actual maps of HM11, for weights H,H+,lambda:
  io:V(H)->V(H+), U_b:V(H)->V(lambda), L_a:V(lambda)->V(H+),
  L_a U_b=delta_ab p^{kappa_b}io, 0<=kappa_b<=K,
with b in a set of cardinality B. They preserve the source cleaned
lattices at height t and conductor c2. The normalized pullback
  g |-> p^{A_H+-A_lambda}(g composed with L_a)
preserves the dual cleaned lattice at height t and conductor c3<=c2.
HM11 also gives io^{-1}(R_H+,e)=R_H,e.

This exact preimage supplies an integral retraction. Indeed io is
injective: if a nonzero v belonged to its kernel, some scalar multiple
of v would not belong to the full lattice R_H,e, but its image 0 would
belong to R_H+,e. Its lattice image is primitive: if w y=io(x), with
y integral, then io(x/w)=y implies x/w integral. Thus the quotient of
R_H+,e by io(R_H,e) is finite free over the DVR. Extend a basis of the
image to a basis of R_H+,e, project onto the image and invert io there.
Extending scalars gives ret with ret io=1 and ret(R_H+,e) subset R_H,e.
This is the actual LatRetract assertion, with no denominator loss.

Suppose H and H+ have respectively n1,n2 factors at depth D>=0,
where 4D<c2. Absorb their integral column matrices to arrange
  be_H(x_i,y_j)=z1 delta_ij, be_H+(x'_i,y'_j)=z2 delta_ij,
where v_p(z1),v_p(z2)<=D. Transfer y_j by
  t_j=p^{A_H-A_H+}(y_j composed with ret).
The exponent is required: then be_H+(v,t_j)=be_H(ret v,y_j).
Perfect normalized duality shows t_j in R'_H+,e. Let
  N_ij=be_H+(io(x_i),y'_j), B_ji=be_H+(x'_j,t_i).
Both matrices are integral.

Smith-reduce N and let r be the number of its nonzero invariant
factors dividing z=z1 z2. The other s=n1-r rows are z times a
matrix E with entries in wO. The same integral row changes, with
inverse changes on the t_i, retain their z1 I_s diagonal. In the
good rows one obtains integral P,Q with P N Q=z I_r. In the bad
rows, the combined pairing against t_i and y'_j has matrix
               M0=[ z1 I_s    z E ]
                  [ C        z2 I_n2 ].
Set U=I-EC and V=I-CE. Both are identity modulo w, so are invertible
over O. Direct multiplication gives
  M0 [ z2 U^{-1}   -z E V^{-1} ] = z I_(s+n2).
     [ -C U^{-1}    z1 V^{-1}  ]
Thus there are s+n2 factors at depth 2D between the source-cleaned
lattice for H+ and its full midpoint dual. HM5 applies because
2(2D)<c2, giving s+n2<=d(H+), hence
                         r>=n1+n2-d(H+).               (TC1.1)

The good rows give F_i in CCL_H and g_j in CCL'_H+ with
be_H+(io(F_i),g_j)=z delta_ij. Apply U_b to F_i and the normalized
pullbacks through L_a to g_j. Multiply the latter by the integral
scalar p^{K-kappa_a}. Their pairing is exactly
                 p^K z delta_ab delta_ij.
Their source membership at c3 follows by weakening c2 to c3.
We have therefore proved
  FactorsLE_lambda(t,c3, B r, 2D+K),
  r>=n1+n2-d(H+).                                     (TC1.2)
This is CloudCount.cloud_count, with the source pivot induction
shortened to Smith normal form and the displayed finite block inverse.
All operations remain over O; the retraction is used only for its
midpoint dual, never falsely asserted to preserve a cleaned dual.

2. TC2: THE GENERAL-WEIGHT DIMENSION COMPARISON
---------------------------------------------
Let the selected set TT have N elements and let c=ccl. At a selected
position the weight is
                 (A+c, beta_1,...,beta_(k-2), -B-c),
where |beta_i|<=c. Put H=(A,0,...,0,-B) at each position,
H+=(A+2c,0,...,0,-B-2c), and let Cl be the product of Weyl dimensions
of the middle (k-2)-coordinate weights. Off TT the current weight is
zero. Odd positions use the reversed dual, which has the same dimension.

Assume A,B>=b'/3>0 and set u=6c/b'. In the Weyl product, middle-middle
factors form Cl; the other 2(k-2)+1 factors are the flat H factors
multiplied by numbers in [1,1+u]. For example their numerator increments
are c-beta_i, c+beta_i and 2c, each between 0 and 2c; their denominators
are at least A, B or A+B. For H+ the increments are 2c,2c,4c,
and the same upper bound holds. Thus, with
                 E=(1+u)^{N(2k-3)},
  Cl dim H <= dim lambda <= E Cl dim H,
  dim H+ <= E dim H.                                  (TC2.1)
If epsilon=12Nkc/b'<=1/9, then E<=exp(epsilon),
(1-epsilon)E<=1 and E<=2. The first uses log(1+u)<=u
and 6(2k-3)<=12k; the second follows from
log(1-epsilon)<=-epsilon. The last follows, for example, by the
power series exp(epsilon)<=1/(1-epsilon)<=9/8.

Suppose H,H+ have fractions at least 1-kappa1 and 1-kappa2 at the
same height, conductor c2 and depth D, where 0<=kappa1<=1,
kappa2>=0, 4D<c2 and 2Nc<=c2. HM11 supplies TC1 with
  B=Cl, K=N ceil(kappaOneExponent(p,a,m)), c3=c2-2Nc,
provided 2c<=8a. The retained mass is at least
             Cl[(1-kappa1)d(H)-kappa2 d(H+)].
Divide (TC2.1) by the same G. Since 1-kappa1>=0,
this mass is at least
             (1-kappa1-2kappa2-epsilon)d(lambda).        (TC2.2)
Indeed Cl d(H)>=(1-epsilon)d(lambda) and
Cl d(H+)<=2d(lambda); the extra kappa1*epsilon is nonnegative.
Thus CloudWeightLowerBound holds with depth 2D+K and conductor
c2-2Nc. A negative lower mass is harmless; there is no multiplication
of a lower bound by a possibly negative retained fraction.

3. TC3: RESTORING THE SMALL WEIGHTS OUTSIDE TT
--------------------------------------------
Decompose lambda=lambda_T+lambda_O, with disjoint support TT and
its complement. Multiplication of polynomials in disjoint letter
variables gives an integral tensor isomorphism for every ordinary
lattice family and for the midpoint lattices. An explicit inverse
sends variables indexed by TT to X tensor 1 and all other variables
to 1 tensor X. Zero-weight factors are the constants by the monic
CB2 basis. This proves RestTensor, both HL/HLe equalities, the dual
evaluation product and
                 dim V(lambda)=dim V(lambda_T)dim V(lambda_O).
The integer A_lambda is additive. Hence normalized be is also the
tensor product of the two normalized pairings.

All coordinates of lambda_O have absolute value <=c. Write
E_last=sum_s lamAt(lambda_O,s,last). Then
        A_lambda_O-E_last
          =sum_s[lamAt(lambda_O,s,0)-lamAt(lambda_O,s,last)]
          <=2n'c=:h.                                  (TC3.1)
In the elementary-divisor frame for an edge, every tableau has
nonnegative row degree. The signed determinant twist therefore gives
  w^{-E_last}H_mid(lambda_O) subset HL_src(lambda_O),
  w^{-E_last}HL_tgt(lambda_O) subset H_mid(lambda_O).
Multiplication by the remaining nonnegative power
w^{h-(A_lambda_O-E_last)} and evaluation duality yield
  R_O,e subset w^{-h}HL_O,src,
  R'_O,e subset w^{-h}HL_O,tgt^*.                      (TC3.2)
This is RestOrdBound, not a postulated small-weight norm estimate.

Take the actual HM8 dual bases r_a in R_O,e and rho_b in R'_O,e,
with be_O(r_a,rho_b)=delta_ab. Tensor them with the cleaned factors
for lambda_T. Equation (TC3.2) increases the height by h.
On a different same-source edge, HL_O,src subset R_O,f (HM8), so
r_a is in w^{-h}R_O,f. On a different same-target edge the dual
inclusion is identical. Consequently the other-edge conductor
decreases by h. The chosen-edge conditions remain exact tensor
conditions. Pairing becomes the old matrix tensor I, so depth is
unchanged and rank multiplies by dim V(lambda_O).

For c3>=h this proves, with the same fractional loss kappa,
  RhoL(lambda_T,t,c3,D,(1-kappa)d(lambda_T))
    => RhoL(lambda,t+h,c3-h,D,(1-kappa)d(lambda)).        (TC3.3)
This is the literal ResidueClassCount. The argument also covers
empty complementary support and zero retained rank.

4. TC4: FROM EVERY EDGE TO LPprime AT EVERY TARGET LATTICE
---------------------------------------------------------
Fix a target lattice L. Its G incoming rank-one edges are the
inverse images of hyperplanes in L/pL; each is an actual edge,
and distinct hyperplanes give distinct sources. Suppose every
edge has n_e>=(1-kappa)d(lambda) factors at height t, depth
D in N and conductor c, with 8D<c and 4(D+1)<=c.
Absorb each certificate's integral matrix to get diagonal z_e I,
where z_e divides p^D. The vectors lie in Nsrc, the duals in Ntgt:
cleaning by w^c on the other edges implies the unscaled membership.
For distinct edges e,f with the same target, the f-dual is
w^c-integral on R_e; hence all cross pairings are in w^cO.

For T=sum_e n_e, the combined T-square pairing matrix B has
diagonal blocks z_e I and off-diagonal entries in w^cO. Let
Q be diagonal with entries p^D/z_e, all integral. Then
                     B Q=p^D(I+E), E in wO,
because c>2D and p^D=w^{2D}. The determinant of I+E is a unit,
so its inverse is integral. This supplies the actual GoodRankK
identity, by using the Q-scaled target dual vectors and multiplying
the testing rows by (I+E)^{-1}. The rank is at least
                     T>=(1-kappa)dim V(lambda)
when kappa<1. If kappa>=1, the required rank is nonpositive and
the zero certificate suffices. The source vectors retain their
individual other-edge cleaning conditions under this construction.
This proves RhoLowerBoundLargeConductor and every clause of LPprime.
The proof only needs c>2D for the inverse, but keeps the source's
stronger 8D<c and 4(D+1)<=c as stated.

5. TC5: INFIMUM ATTAINMENT AND THE ACTUAL EXCEPTIONAL FUNCTION
------------------------------------------------------------
Let K be the fixed constant package and X the literal data, with
the definitions in NP and HM6. For each flat weight set
  kF(A,B)=inf {kappa>=0 : RhoL(flatW(A,B),tStar(A,B),c4,D1,
                                      (1-kappa)d(flatW(A,B)))}.
The set contains 1, so 0<=kF<=1. Its infimum is admissible.
Here is the finite-rank reason. At any fixed edge, if
T=(1-kF)d>0, choose an admissible kappa sufficiently close to kF
that (1-kappa)d>ceil(T)-1. Its integer rank is at least ceil(T),
and hence at least T. For T<=0 use rank zero. Repeat for each
edge; the definition does not require a common choice of vectors.
Thus the infimum causes no missing limiting or compactness argument.

For degree families aw,bw put epsilon=12Nkc/b' and
  kText(aw,bw)=
    [kF(aw-c,bw-c) if aw-c>=a and bw-c>=bl coordinatewise;
     1 otherwise]
       +2 kF(aw+c,bw+c)+epsilon.                       (TC5.1)
All kF arguments mean coordinatewise natural values; in the
nontrivial case the displayed lower bounds make truncation exact.
This is exactly Dat.kText, including its outside-window branch.

Assume the literal FactsLP, proved by NP8. If kText>=1, LPprime
is witnessed by rank zero and 4(Dp+1)<=cStar. Otherwise the inside
condition holds. Set A=aw-c, B=bw-c and enlarge the height of the
first flat certificate to tStar(aw+c,bw+c). This is legitimate:
t0 depends on the degrees only through its monotone minima, and
the ceiling preserves monotonicity. The second flat certificate
already has that height. Apply TC2 with kappa1=kF(A,B),
kappa2=kF(A+2c,B+2c), conductor c4 and depth D1. Its degree,
dimension, small-epsilon and conductor hypotheses are precisely
the fields of FactsLP plus c4=cStar+2Nc+2n'c.

It gives lambda_T a retained fraction 1-kText, depth
  2D1+N ceil(kappaOneExponent)<=Dp
and conductor c4-2Nc. TC3 then restores lambda_O at the exact
conductor cStar, with height
                  tStar(aw+c,bw+c)+2n'c.
NP7 gives this height <=tI(C0,C), and hence <=the requested t.
Increase depth to Dp, height to t, and use TC4; the conductor
field 8(Dp+1)<=cStar supplies both of its strict inequalities.
This proves MidLP directly for the actual source objects.

6. TC6: EXPLICIT CONSTANTS AND THE COMPLETE LOCAL CONCLUSION
-----------------------------------------------------------
Choose
  C0>=880, CE1=CE2=Cm=17, Ckap=3, Ct=30, Cc=4,
  CA=50000, Cs=351, CH_inner=700000.                   (TC6.1)
These constants do not depend on any prime, period, weights or
coefficient ring. NP2 with H=4 has threshold
  1000(CE1+CE2+1)(Cc+1)H=700000.
This supplies Std(H=4), and consequently Std(H=1), for all the
extended local data at the inner threshold. RU8 uses no further
constant, RD9 requires C0>=880, and RL11 requires exactly
CE1,CE2,Cm>=17 and Ct>=30. RU9 needs Ckap>=3.

  max{56,2(31exp(3)+5)exp(3),1}.
Since exp(1)<3, it is <45468<50000. NP9 gives
  Cs=Ckap(Cm+4CE1+CE2+15)=3(17+68+17+15)=351.

There is no hidden GoodShare premise in the final application.
RU10 proves that each actual segment lattice L is finite free.
The ranks of saturated GoodSub sublattices form a nonempty subset
of {0,...,rank L}, so a largest rank g is attained. The actual
definition badShare is 1-g/rank L when rank L>0. When rank L=0
its definition gives 0 and the zero sublattice still supplies the
required GoodShare. Thus each literal kapTI gives GoodShare
pointwise, with no new error. Choose every segment depth DjI.

  sum_j DjI+4kap0I=totalDegreeBudget=D0,

  meanKap(kText)<=Cmean N N1/Y,

To obtain one kappa for all rings, choose one RS setting R0 if one

Conclusion and provenance boundary
-----------------------------------
TC6 closes the complete supplied-data Type-I Conclusion2, and therefore
its ClosedP quantifier statement, with sufficient constants (TC6.4).
Together with EA9, both local Type-I and Type-II constructions are
now reconstructed. The full global hypotheses, theta/spectral identities,
metric transport, cohomological windows and simultaneous prime selection
are not consequences of this local theorem and remain in the frontier.
The direct constants, Smith compression and finite-rank infimum argument
are human proofs and simplifications, not newly compiled Lean claims.
The ledger records exact source definitions and parallel proofs; byte
verification is not a kernel replay or independent certification of RH.

# Source review for the integral flag models at two prime cutoffs

The determinant sign and the wall dimension were checked on the complete flag. The two extensions in the common model contribute the same degree over the reference field. In Newton degree one the correction vanishes for a side-preserving Frobenius and is (s−r)ℓ/(ℓ²−1) times the indicated intersection and log ℓ when the sides are exchanged. On the given semiabelian model, §7.3, Lemma 2(a) of Néron Models and the open immersion in Zariski’s main theorem give the finite part. Closure under addition follows by taking the image of a closed subset of a finite scheme over the local base. Fargues’s printed §5.4, Theorem 3 applies to a finite flat Cartier dual over the completed valuation ring, including at 2. Applying Nakayama to κ times the quotient removes the level-four error. The gluing proof consumes the chart estimates, and the final wall estimate uses constants fixed for the tower. The claim record for neither argument asserts construction of all six good-tower laws.

3. First subtarget: shared holomorphic coefficient uniqueness
------------------------------------------------------------

Exact target:
  DP/HolomorphicTypeEqZeroOfTorusLawAtOne.lean
  GlobalTheory.HolDict.Uniq.local_zero, 133–226;
  GlobalTheory.HolDict.Uniq.eq_zero_of_torus_law, 228–399.

The global contract is finite-dimensional and explicit. For a finite index
set, Hermitian matrices H_tau, integers q_tau, orthogonal frames x0_tau,
and integer weights w_tau,a, assume

  OrthFr(H_tau,q_tau,x0_tau) for every tau;
  HolFn(H,q,x0,w,f);
  f(torEl(x0,s) u) = torCh(w,s) f(u) for all u in UG(H), all s;
  f(1)=0.

Then f vanishes on UG(H). Here HolFn means that f(u)=P(u x0), where P is
holomorphic on the original negative-frame domain and transforms on the
right under every invertible upper-triangular b by
  P(x b) = [product_(tau,a) b_tau,aa ^ w_tau,a] P(x).
The left torus law is a separate premise. Definitions, including the exact
signs and frame domain, are in AV/Definitions/Divisors/IsSmoothVector.lean:
20–39 and 59–62.

The concrete proof to reconstruct first is local_zero. In coordinates
near the identity, scale each strictly lower entry z_ac by zeta^(a-c),
a>c. For |zeta|=1 this is diagonal unitary conjugation, so the torus law
makes the value constant on the boundary circle. The center is the
identity, where the value is zero; holomorphy forces zero on this lower
chart. Multiplication by an upper-triangular factor preserves the zero
set. The map (lower entries, upper entries) -> (1+lower)(1+upper) has
derivative the identity at zero and covers a neighborhood of the identity.
This yields local vanishing, without any representation-theoretic
multiplicity-one theorem.

For the global conclusion, preserve the actual domain and its connectedness.
The immediate source suppliers are:
  DP/SmoothGramSchmidtIndefiniteHermitianFrames.lean:176–219,
    GS.smooth_gramSchmidt;
  DP/NegativeFrameDomainProductOpenPreconnectedStable.lean:148–208,
    isPreconnected_frDom, and 212–225,
    frDoms_open_preconnected_stable.
They transfer the original torus law to P and permit the holomorphic
identity theorem. These geometric arguments must be reconstructed or
matched explicitly; merely calling the domain a familiar flag variety
does not verify this source's open negative-frame domain.

This is a good bounded first deliverable: the local chart lemma and its
actual-domain globalization, followed by two short stated applications.
The local argument looks elementary/moderate. That assessment is a work
plan, not a new B certificate or a closure of the larger C producer.

# v0.28 milestone: the actual valued-point image-density reduction

## What is now a complete chain

The ten claims reconstruct the entire lower image-density argument, including the substantial geometric and cohomological suppliers. The endpoint is a proved implication on the original good datum and a single fixed complex embedding:

`Con(D) + PurityAtPoints(D,iota) + AutmAlt(D,iota)`

implies

`Fin0(D) + NoEnd(D) + AllIrr(D, IsAut4Alt(iota))`.

There is no remaining universal-point, curve, valued-flag, pattern-classification, integral-cocycle, nonsplit-extension or coefficient-identification hypothesis inside this implication. Those are proved in IX1–IX7 using previously reconstructed AX/DX/BR/FP/MX/PX/LD/KB suppliers. The full `CohomologyHyp` record is not needed for this lower chain.

The characteristic-ell side now extends further upstream. IX9 proves, from the exact actual `Link`, universal ordinary determinant ring, weight algebra and finite modular quotient, the entire special-fibre finiteness argument of `TPmFin`. It then proves that an actual `ProModAlt` point in characteristic ell cannot have full matrix span or be the nonsplit two-block type. No purity or automorphy theorem is used in that contradiction.

IX10 proves the exact characteristic-zero implications of `ProModAlt`: weak purity and, for irreducible actual pairs, the literal `IsAut4Alt/IsAutQA`. It includes the missing restriction-irreducibility argument, so the automorphy clause is applied only after all its hypotheses have been verified.

## Simplifications and the points that needed care

The local component is cut out by an actual prime ideal: the local chart kernel is annihilated by an element nonzero at the original origin. Every domain witness through that origin kills the kernel. This gives the full all-target component condition, including nonreduced targets, without assuming integral formal smoothness.

Proper flag closure replaces several long flag-specialization arguments. The residual quotient labels are proved separately: the generic eigenvalue belongs to a finite local algebra over the complete domain, its evaluation at the origin is integral and hence lies in the original DVR, and a monic residual-nilpotence equation forces the same label after specialization. Smallness is restored by capturing the finitely many basis entries in one finite complete DVR extension.

The nonconstant curve is a point of the original universal functor. It is built from a one-dimensional complete prime quotient of the invariant ring, a nonzero finite-type fibre, rational torus rescaling and continuous evaluation of the complete weighted presentation. An arbitrary point of a larger formal fibre would not suffice.

The integral cocycle proof is finite at every stage. Greenberg–Wiles counting gives a lower bound growing with the coefficient length; a division argument preserves the saturated local condition and forces primitive cocycles at every level. A finite-branch inverse-limit argument then chooses compatible cocycles and local primitives without assuming that the reduction maps are surjective.

The block twist uses the exact rank-three lower cross-block symplectic module. Its elements have zero pairwise products, which makes the representation and flag formulas explicit. Complex conjugation acts by minus one, so the normalized cocycle cannot become a cross-block coboundary even after extending to the ambient field. The result is an actual `KPair`, but need not be an original `EPt`; this is why the full `PurityAtPoints` quantifier is necessary.

The source's `PureW` is weaker than usual purity. It asserts a strict bound `|iota(x)| < p^f |iota(y)|` for Frobenius roots after passage to one open subgroup. IX7 uses exactly this inequality. The two block determinant exponents are `e+2` and `e`, so multiplying two such inequalities contradicts their exact determinant ratio. Equal absolute values of the roots are never assumed.

For the special-fibre argument, integrality of characteristic-polynomial coefficients of a square is descended through the original injection `X.B → X.A` before passing to a prime quotient. This avoids an unjustified assumption that the injection remains injective after quotienting. In characteristic ell, algebraic-over-finite-field trace differences in the valuation maximal ideal must vanish. A nondegenerate trace pairing then rules out the full matrix algebra; a squarefree separating element gives the same contradiction on the full two-dimensional block.

For characteristic-zero automorphy, the source weight parameter uses squares of the four local characters. IX10 therefore supplies exactly `2(e+1), 2e, 2, 0`. The abelian tower and its residual root separation imply irreducibility on the CM field: otherwise a nontrivial finite self-twist forces a forbidden linear relation among the four distinct residual characters. The same fixed embedding is retained for all points.

## What remains, and why the source wrappers do not close it

The remaining endpoint is the actual arithmetic realization in IX.35: for the original Good/OfCase datum and one embedding chosen before the point quantifiers, every actual star `KPair` must admit the full `ProModAlt` witness.

The source's `sp4In_alt` obtains this from `PMExAlt`, `SeedIIAlt`, `SeedIVAlt`, cohomological/tower facts and the case-two/case-three component constructions. `sp4In0_alt` obtains the arithmetic packages from `HypothesesH'`. The latter is a collection of exact literal automorphy, cohomological, base-change, descent, semisimplicity and quaternionic assertions. It is not an independently matched single classical theorem.

Three precise construction obligations remain:

1. **Actual modular support.** For every Fit/Deep tower and fixed embedding, choose a coefficient threshold before the later coefficient ring and `Space`, then produce an ideal with all five `PotentialModularityTower` fields. IX9 supplies its finite-fibre step once the finite ordinary module and actual link/map are given. Constructing those objects, local vanishing at characteristic-ell points and seeds, and purity/automorphy of the supported arithmetic points remain outside this component.
2. **Exact seeds.** The second seed needs the stated residual-entry alternative. The fourth seed chooses its auxiliary prime and residual pair before every extension tower satisfying the precise `Ok`/`Dies` conditions, and then supplies the same residual pair's extension with both specified negative-root entries. Generic potential automorphy does not establish these quantifiers or entry constraints.
3. **Reaching the support from the original star point.** The actual lattice, tower, coefficient, `Space`, residual chain and component propagation must produce a map annihilating the same ideal, preserving the original inertia characters and conjugate global representation. Nonempty support by itself is insufficient. The exact source targets are recorded separately as retained frontier anchors.

Completing a general Bloch–Kato/cohomology record will remove that record from the original upstream source hypotheses. It will not by itself construct the modular quotient, seeds or component chains. Conversely, the lower chain in this chapter already closes without the full record.

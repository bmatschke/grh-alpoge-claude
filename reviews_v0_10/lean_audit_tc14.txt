TC1: primitive retraction and Smith cloud count
------------------------------------------------
The exact midpoint-lattice preimage implies injectivity: a nonzero kernel
line cannot be contained in a full DVR lattice. It also makes the image
saturated, by applying the preimage condition to x/w. The finite free
quotient therefore gives the asserted integral retraction.

The transferred dual must have the displayed scalar p^(A_H-A_H+).
Direct evaluation gives be_H+(v,t_j)=be_H(ret(v),y_j), hence midpoint-dual
membership by perfect normalized pairing. No cleaned-dual membership of
this retraction is needed or claimed.

For rectangular N, Smith form separates the nonzero diagonals dividing
z1*z2 from the others; zero rows belong to the bad part. Every bad entry
is zE with E in wO. Integral row changes and inverse dual changes preserve
the z1 identity block; corresponding changes on the second source/dual
family preserve z2. Both Schur complements I-EC and I-CE are identity
modulo w. Direct block multiplication verifies the displayed inverse
identity, including both off-diagonal zero blocks and common scalar z.
The resulting bad certificate has depth 2D and only requires the full
midpoint dual. HM5's now-explicit one-sided nLeD therefore applies using
4D<c2. The good rows and the normalized L pullbacks produce the exact
common scalar p^K z; K is the actual natural-valued uniform exponent bound.

TC3: small-weight tensor and conductor bookkeeping
----------------------------------------------------
Disjoint letter variables give the integral tensor isomorphism; there is
no integral isotypic decomposition being assumed. In an edge basis write
pi for the ordinary target exponent and E_last for the sum of last weights.
The tableau row-degree bound gives pi>=E_last. With h>=A-E_last,
R has exponent pi-A>=-h relative to the source lattice. Its normalized
dual has exponent -pi-A relative to the source-dual basis and therefore
lies in w^(-h) times the target dual, whose exponent is -2pi. These verify
both displayed inclusions, including signed determinant shifts.
Tensoring exact dual midpoint bases increases height by h, decreases
other-edge conductor by h, leaves depth unchanged, and multiplies rank
by the complementary dimension. Same-target dual inclusion follows from
HM8's exact normalized pairing and pi<=A. The zero-support/zero-rank cases
are harmless.

TC4: incoming edges and the literal GoodRankK identity
-------------------------------------------------------
For each target, incoming index-q sublattices are exactly the inverse
images of hyperplanes modulo p, giving G edges. The CCL and CCL' source
and target memberships imply the literal Nsrc and Ntgt conditions because
w^c times an integral lattice is contained in that lattice. Different
incoming-edge blocks pair into w^cO using dual cleaning and the chosen
edge's perfect pairing.

Each nonzero diagonal z_e divides p^D since D is natural and v_p(z_e)<=D.
After multiplying its target columns by p^D/z_e, the pairing matrix is
p^D(I+E) with E in wO when c>2D. Let u be the O-linear map formed from those
scaled target vectors in Ntgt and let M=(I+E)^(-1). Then literally
M*(the vector of tests evaluated at u(x))=p^D*x, which is GoodRankK.
This uses row operations on the testing matrix, not mixing source vectors
across their different sources; all original source vectors and their
individual other-edge vanishing remain valid for LPprimeAt. Summing ranks
and the kappa>=1 zero case gives the exact requested mass. The source's
stronger8D<c and4(D+1)<=c are retained.

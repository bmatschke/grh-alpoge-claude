1. What is actually proved

There are three connected, conditional constructions in this component.

First, a supplied compatible geometric state, its actual finite class
presentation, coefficient maps and tuple lattice ties give the literal
MtFam/MtReal, MtInput and pointwise MtAd. The full TypeSp range is proved
by evaluation and extension, not inferred from a dimension equality.
The Hecke action is the source's normalized action and is integral only
at the claimed coefficient places. Both used and unused tuples are
handled on their actual modules.

Second, a supplied rational datum RatDat/RatIn, actual RingSpec and
the literal row/Galois hypotheses give the actual form space Fm, its
full lattice Bsup, the good intersection lattice B, a finite signature
set, integral Hecke eigenvalues, and the source's explicit weight and
coefficient-field type spaces. The central technical argument is an
integral additive reading L→Frac(O) which fixes K and preserves
integrality even when L itself does not embed in Frac(O). It combines
with flat descent of finite integral equations to prove preservation
of the original B lattice.

The second_step endpoint is reconstructed with its exact qualification.
Its mkF/mkEx uses Theta=0, ThetaPsi=0, Dat=PEmpty, thP=0, twDel=0,
eH=1, and TwL.empty; SideA.bare says the old local index set is empty.
The conclusion is conditional on every subsequently supplied NTh.
It does not construct such an NTh, the actual nonzero theta image, a
toric character installation, or a nonempty local Galois family.
The positive Fm/B/signature/type fields above are genuine even though
these separate initialization fields are placeholders.

Third, four elementary local primitives narrow the remaining NdSpec
frontier: one finite local extension splits all operators in an
infinite commuting Hecke family at a fixed row/place; the actual
finite residual block algebra is local; a supported generalized
Hecke point gives a nonzero simultaneous eigenspace through a supplied
actual comparison; and the quotient by the original generator
relations becomes a free module over the prescribed DVR.

None of these results establishes a new unconditional RH/GRH branch.
The four existing global branch records must retain their prior scope.

linear-algebra argument in this component constructs a cuspidal

3. MR1–MR3: finite evaluation, level projection and the inverse character

For a definite unitary group, use the supplied finite double quotient
Gamma\G/J and chosen representatives y_c. A form is determined by
its values v_c. With convention F(gamma g k)=rho(gamma)F(g)chi(k),
the correct stabilizer equation is rho(gamma)v_c=chi(k)v_c whenever
gamma y_c=y_c k. It is not the inverse equation. The extension at
gamma y_c k is rho(gamma)chi(k)v_c; the stabilizer equation makes it
independent of the decomposition. Conversely it reconstructs every
equivariant form.

The stabilizers preserve an integral lattice and are discrete in the
product of all archimedean compact unitary groups. They are finite.
Thus their equations define kernels of finite matrices over L.
The finite-kernel scalar-extension argument proves that an L-basis
becomes a complex basis of the entire space. Coefficient-lattice
membership is checked at each of the finite class representatives.
The supplied ClassPres/CoefTie and finite geometric presentation are
not replaced by this observation; their metric and lattice ties remain
the actual ones already proved or supplied in FC/TX.

For a finite group action, the normalized character average
e_psi=|P|^(-1) sum psi(p)^(-1)a(p) is an idempotent with image the
psi-eigenspace. Independent matrix columns and a left inverse give a
basis over L. Averaging the full complex expansion proves full range
after extension. Rational values at the class representatives force
rational coordinates. Integral coordinates are defined by pulling
back the actual class lattices; division by |P| is not asserted to be
integral.

The standard frame map is a genuine rational/adelic matrix
isomorphism. Injectivity of the number field in its finite adele
tensor algebra establishes the rational-point correspondence in both
directions. The source uses the conjugate level character. Accordingly
the smaller level is the inverse image of P0 under reduction and its
character is ch times psi0^(-1). The exact result is
cutSp(psi0^(-1)) = TypeSp(wtRep,Jstd,chiStd), transported by the
standard frame map. The original level lies in the reduction kernel,
so the reverse implication recovers its original equivariance.

Primary source interfaces: ExtLvlFormMemCutSp, the actual ClassPres
declaration, DefiniteLevelFormsIffStdGroupTypeSpace, and
MemRationalGLOfUnitaryGroupGramUnitGram. The ledger records the full
proof ranges, not only their final wrapper declarations.

4. MR4–MR7: actual levels and normalized rational/integral Hecke action

The product of the norms of all conductor and type primes supplies a
positive congruence modulus. Congruence to the identity gives trivial
reduction at every level prime, hence both membership and trivial
character. Away from those primes the group is the full integral
unitary group and the character is trivial.

The adelic product law is proved with the idempotent which is one at
one place and zero elsewhere. The cut g_v=e_v g+(1-e_v)I is unitary,
its inverse is the analogous cut of g^(-1), and the complementary cuts
multiply to g. Integrality and the flag constraints are componentwise.
At conductor primes, the subgroup is the actual block parabolic and
the character is the prescribed complementary-block determinant
character. At type primes, it has the actual unipotent upper block
and the fixed generic residue character. AR5–AR8 supply the exact
local character normalization and additive lift; the half-selection,
splitting and LevCh.Is hypotheses are retained literally.

The polynomial coefficient representation is transported by an
explicit common basis. Algebraicity is therefore a matrix identity.
Restriction from the product of general linear groups to rational
unitary points loses no invariant subspaces: the Cayley transform
identifies dense open subsets of the unitary group and its rational
skew-Hermitian vector space. A polynomial vanishing at all rational
coordinates is zero. After scalar extension, projection to distinct
nonconjugate embedding pairs is onto the required product. This
supplies the density step.

The characteristic-zero polynomial highest-weight theorem itself is
retained: the canonical Schur/tensor module is irreducible and has
the stated unique highest line, including the dual/conjugate
convention. This is the exact HwFixedEx/HwFixedLine input used by
MtAdWtIrr, not a claim that every automorphic space exists. The actual
lamLdef computation then identifies infOf with the source's paramV
at shift 1/2. Its row uses n=R.nV, not a different rank parameter.

At a good split prime, the local double coset is finite because the
intersection of two compact open groups is open in a compact group.
The actual representatives are supported at that prime and its local
character is trivial. The complementary level component commutes
with them and supplies the original character. Thus the unweighted
finite sum, multiplied by q_s^(-k(n-1)), preserves TypeSp. All values
are rational in the fixed coefficient module; the level basis then
gives a unique rational transform x'. This is NG.tN with its literal
normalization, not an unnormalized or differently shifted operator.

The representative matrices and their inverses have denominators
only at the rational prime under the Hecke place. At a different
coefficient residue prime these denominators are units. The original
lattices at g and ga_t therefore have the same localization. The
same is true of their actual nabla coefficient lattices by using a
common localized basis, its dual, and the defining polynomial modules.
No integral Delta=nabla identification is used. Finitely many class
denominators combine to one d outside the coefficient prime with
d x' in the original level lattice. This is stronger than rational
stability and weaker than integrality at the Hecke residue prime,
exactly as required in the source.

7. MR12–MR13: integral additive readings and finite-equation descent

The key distinction is between an embedding of K in H=Frac(O), which
is given, and an embedding of the whole L in H, which need not exist.
Extend K→H only to L→an algebraic closure of H. Let N be the O-span
of an integral Z-basis of O_L. N is finite torsion-free, contains 1,
and consists of elements integral over O. Integral closedness gives
N intersect H·1=O·1. Hence N/O·1 is torsion-free: a torsion class
would have representative in H·1, forcing membership in O·1.

Over a DVR the quotient is finite free. Lifting a basis splits off
O·1; the functional equal to one on 1 and zero on these lifts is
O-linear N→O. Localize it to HN and extend by a vector-space
complement in the algebraic closure. Composition with L gives an
additive K-semilinear lambda:L→H, lambda(1)=1, mapping O_L into O.
This map is not asserted to be a ring homomorphism. That fact is
essential to keeping the original coefficient ring unchanged.

In a common Ks/L basis of WK/WL, apply lambda to each coefficient
to define Phi:WL→H tensor_Ks WK. Matrices of rational operators
preserving WK lie in Ks⊂K, so Phi intertwines them and fixes rational
vectors as 1 tensor w. It maps integral multiples of the rational
lattice into Bsup.

For a finite system with coefficients in K, clear a common global
integer denominator to get a matrix over O_K. The map O_K→O exists
because global integers remain integral over O; O is torsion-free
over the Dedekind domain O_K and therefore flat. Locally this follows
because torsion-free modules over a DVR are filtered unions of
finite free modules. Tensoring the kernel sequence is exact. Every
O-valued solution is consequently an O-linear combination of the
images of integral O_K-valued solutions. Cancelling the chosen
nonzero denominator recovers the original equations. This is the
source's actual exists_integral_descent property; a bare equality
of dimensions would not prove it.

8. MR14–MR16: the actual good lattice is finite, full and Hecke stable

Fm=H tensor_Ks WK and Bsup=O-span(1 tensor MK). The fixed finite
generators of MK generate Bsup because their integral coefficients
map into O. They span Fm over H. Bsup is therefore a genuine finite
free full lattice. Let V0 be the actual generic-character/central
idempotent subspace, and set B=Bsup intersect V0. Noetherianness
gives finite generation, torsion freeness gives freeness, and
denominator clearing proves H B=V0. The localized injection is
injective because the ambient vector space has no torsion.

The central idempotent over K is reflected coefficientwise from its
fixed complex image. The finite generic character's values are
roots of unity, so complex conjugation is inversion; the actual
false-side embedding therefore gives exactly the inverse of the
true-side character. The character-adjusted action is chi^(-1)rho.
The finite unipotent average commutes with the central idempotent.
Thus V0 is exactly the finite common kernel of e-1 and the generic
character equations. Finite-kernel descent and the retained RatIn
good-row expansion give the corresponding eigenvector span over H,
without extending the whole L into H.

For x in B write x=sum o_t Phi(w_t) using integral rational generators.
Its good equations form the finite system of MR13. That result
rewrites x as an O-linear combination of Phi(y) with
y in O_L MK intersect goodL. RatIn.hL_int clears H_{k,P}y by a
power of norm(P). For P outside S0 the norm is a unit of O, because
S0 contains all primes above its residue characteristic. The power
can be cancelled inside Bsup. RatIn.span proves that H_{k,P}
preserves goodL. Multiplication by the prescribed delta unit c^k
preserves both conditions. Hence the actual normalized operator
stabilizes B.

This is proved on the literal range 1≤j≤m,1≤k≤r, split P outside S0.
The source's hkB is defined as the identity off that range, including
k=0. We do not assert the same integral-stability result for excluded
or ramified Hecke operators.

9. MR17–MR18: actual integral signatures, separation and characteristic polynomials

An eigenvalue a in H of a finite free O-module endomorphism satisfies
its monic characteristic polynomial. A nonzero eigenvector implies
p(a)=0, and integral closedness gives a in O. Apply this to each
nonzero simultaneous eigenvector in the actual span of V0.

For each twist index take the finite triples (j,b,sigma) of valid row,
side and good occurring original row for which all the prescribed
eigenvalues are integral. Store the exact integral value at admissible
Hecke pairs and one elsewhere. Sig is the image of these triples in
(j,b,the entire eigenvalue function). It is finite despite the
infinite set of Hecke arguments. Taking the image identifies exactly
duplicates, so row number, side and all admissible values determine
the signature. A chosen preimage gives its original rowS.

Every nonzero simultaneous eigenspace in the span has integral
eigenvalues, so it is represented by this finite signature image.
This proves SigSpan, separation, good/nonzero original rowS, the
literal normalized rowS_eig equation, and hEig_zero=1. If the original
good space is zero, its actual B is zero; no nonvanishing assertion
is fabricated. Where it is nonzero, scalar extension and the lattice
intersection preserve that nonzero space faithfully.

For any one admissible operator, distinct eigenspaces are independent:
apply the product of the other linear factors to isolate a summand.
Their bases diagonalize the operator because they span. The integral
restriction has the same characteristic polynomial after scalar
extension, since determinants commute with the coefficient map.
It therefore splits. Off the admissible range the operator is the
identity and its polynomial is (X-1)^rank.

The actual rhoS is NR24 evaluated at rowS and the chosen side. The
signature construction does not prove or alter NR24's Galois inputs.

10. MR19–MR20: explicit weights and full coefficient-field type spaces

The source's weight parameter is the multiset of the v entries,
the negative b entries, and the t-term progression
-e_j+(t-1-2c)/2, followed by the shift -s. The actual HC gaps place
the progression between the appropriate b entries and away from
the v entries, giving distinct terms. Parity gives each term as
an integer plus (n-1)/2. Sort the negatives of the integer parts
as a_0<...<a_(n-1), and define lambda_i=-a_i+i. The integral gaps
make lambda weakly decreasing and its infOf multiset is the original
parameter. The frame supplies s=1/2; the argument only needs its
literal half-integrality.

Over the unchanged fraction field, use the dual tensor polynomial
module at the actual side-dependent embeddings. Its polynomial
basis, dual basis and inverse-determinant matrices commute with
scalar extension. Under the stated characteristic-zero highest-weight
input and the rational-group density proof, these canonical modules
are absolutely irreducible and have the required dual weights.
Thus the local lower-bound field for this weight construction can
be bottom: c2 imposes no extra finite extension. The source's
out-of-range default weight is only padding.

For local levels, Pq chooses one prime above each real-field place.
QP and DP are the selected halves of the actual Q and auxiliary
conductor sets. Their disjointness, conjugation and splitting laws
are retained from the literal admissibility, RingSpec and host data.
The level is the integral unitary subgroup with block-parabolic
reduction at QP/DP and the prescribed unipotent upper block at the
type primes. Block multiplication and inverse equations prove it is
a subgroup. Reduction makes the complementary determinant
multiplicative. The character is the exact product of generic/type
factors, the tame psi determinant factors at QP, and delta to the
side exponent at DP. GA/AR supply the actual normalized residue
characters; no arbitrary finite-order replacements are introduced.

A congruence subgroup makes every factor trivial. Evaluation on
finitely many classes injects the whole TypeSpF into a finite direct
sum of the finite coefficient space, proving finite-dimensionality.
For admissible data this is exactly the successful branch of
MtSubT; its fallback zero branch is not used. The basis-span MtLatT
is finite and full. It is still not automatically the original MtLat;
the later exact comparison is retained.

12. MR23–MR26: substantive local algebra before the remaining NdSpec join

At fixed (j,w), the entire family hLC lies in the finite-dimensional
endomorphism space of the actual MtL. Choose finitely many family
members spanning it and multiply their characteristic polynomials.
Cayley–Hamilton gives a common annihilator for those members. Adjoin
its finitely many roots, under the fixed L embedding, to Q_l. Every
root is algebraic over Q_l, so this is a finite local field. Any later
compatible coefficient field containing it splits the chosen
operators. Commuting split operators admit a common eigenvector and,
by induction on dimension, a common invariant full flag. Every
original operator is a linear combination of them and is triangular
in that flag. Its split minimal polynomial gives a decomposition
into generalized eigenspaces by Bezout. This proves exactly
exists_finExt_split, with the fixed-(j,w) quantifier order. It does
not assume semisimplicity or choose one finite field for all places.

For a finite nonempty residual point set X, all coordinates of any
element of T⊂O^X are congruent modulo the maximal ideal. Indeed two
O-algebra characters with the same residue kernel agree modulo that
ideal: apply the equality of kernels to a-phi(a). Congruence is
preserved by the generated algebra. If one coordinate of f is a unit,
all are. The monic polynomial product(Z-f(x)) annihilates f and has
unit constant coefficient, so it explicitly expresses f^(-1) as a
polynomial in f. Nonunits of T are therefore precisely the kernel
of coordinate reduction T→k, a surjective map because constants
belong to T. This proves locality and the original residue field.
It uses the source's actual finite nonempty XOf at an existing LocT;
it does not manufacture a localization index.

An existing supported point includes a nonzero invariant generalized
weight space W. Choose a nonzero invariant subspace U of minimal
positive dimension. For each operator a, the least power killing a
nonzero u produces a nonzero vector in U intersect ker(a-chi(a)).
Commutation makes that intersection invariant, so minimality makes
it all of U. A nonzero u in U is a simultaneous eigenvector even for
an infinite commuting family. The retained actual MtLoc comparison
Psi has range the residual block and is injective; pulling u back
through it gives z≠0. ActHK proves the point equation on Hecke
generators, and induction under sums/products/constants proves it
for the whole T. This is heckeEig_ne_bot. The actual Psi and nonzero
point support are indispensable; generalized splitting alone does
not supply either.

Finally, OsConcrete identifies the original integral coefficient ring
A with the integers of a number subfield F. For the original complex
generators v_t, their relation module N⊂A^d is saturated because the
fixed coefficient embedding is injective. Hence A^d/N is finite
torsion-free, thus projective over the Dedekind ring A. Splitting the
quotient gives an idempotent matrix with image N. Apply the actual
coefficient map A→O to its entries. The resulting idempotent has
image exactly the O-span of the original relations. The quotient is
its complementary direct summand in O^d, hence finite torsion-free
and free over the DVR. This proves bjC_free on the literal relation
quotient, without replacing the lattice by a module of equal rank.

BCN v0.40 — coefficient normalization and the original added Unit parameter
=======================================================================

Outcome
-------
BCN1–BCN25 give a complete human proof of the original implication
Core => CoreBC and its selected one-point full-block specialization.
BCN25 composes this with the inherited PQL CoreBC => Raw proof.

This is a proved implication on the original antecedent. It does not
construct the original normalized Core patched-support producer or an
initial TWAut. The selected version BCN24 needs only the normalized
h=0, a=n, Kap.g=0 consumer; it does not ask for arbitrary h or g.
In particular, it preserves n=a=4 on the selected rank-four route.

Main construction
-----------------
1. Choose a primitive element of the finite point field K_0 over the
   residue field k, lift its monic minimal polynomial P, and take the
   literal O' = O[X]/P. The proof checks finite freeness, locality,
   completeness, domain/DVR structure, residue cardinality, the generator
   alpha, P(alpha)=0, and splitting over every supplied O-field with
   cardinality |K_0|. Different residue-field embeddings cause no problem.

2. Use the actual source Til(O,O',R) = (O' tensor_O R)[[s]], not a
   replacement completion. For IsCO(O,R), the coefficient tensor ring is
   finite free local with maximal ideal m_R A_R. This proves every new
   IsCO field, local iota, and s in the maximal ideal.

3. Reconstruct all finite-presentation and coefficientwise identities
   needed for the actual Lambda, Sinf, delIdeal, Sp, base module and
   auxiliary modules. The complete TWBase support field is checked at
   every prime. Each TWRing keeps its numerical exponent array and rank.
   Each TWMaps keeps the full determinant/character map, the all-series
   coefficient equation, the distinguished roots, both compositions,
   both ordered annihilator relations, and the same exponent ex.
   The resulting TWAut has lambda'(X(inl())) = s literally.

4. For every chosen prime P_i of O' tensor_O B over ker(f_i), construct
   a map to the same original K_i[[t_i]], with exact kernel P_i on the
   coefficient tensor. Its restriction to O' is constant and onto K_i.
   Extending by s -> t_i yields the required f_i'. NP, Kap, eigenvalues,
   n/a/g, and every B1/B2 witness are unchanged; the original f_i is
   recovered exactly after restriction to B.

5. Lift every finite chain by finite-free going-down and integral
   going-up, then extend its primes as constant-series ideals.
   Initial and final endpoint primes contract exactly. The Core
   conclusion for the lifted chain contracts to J <= q in the original B.

Two independent algebraic improvements
--------------------------------------
BCN17 proves finite-free coefficient transport of the closed
Cayley–Hamilton/ordinary ideal under the cofinal-topology condition
m_B A <= m_A and m_A^c <= m_B A. Its coordinate argument uses depths N
and cN with the same open normal subgroup H. The actual O' construction
has c=1.

BCN18 proves exact ordinary ideal extension after adding s. For a
relation z with one fixed finite group support S, extracting coefficient
s^r at original depth N+r gives membership in the old defining ideal
at N, with H unchanged. The Noetherian module I_A intersect A[S] has one
finite generating set; assembling all coefficient expressions then
writes z as a finite sum of series multiples of old relations.
This proves the actual augmentationIdealKernel equality in BCN19
without using finE, pair_mf, or general arbitrary-local-map closure_bc.

BCN20 proves semisimple-ring descent for any field extension, without
finite-dimensionality: split each extended left ideal, then apply a
linear coefficient functional K -> kappa sending 1 to 1. The resulting
retraction is E-linear because K is central. The exact pinned mathlib
definition is checked: IsSemisimpleRing E means complemented left
submodules of E itself, with no finite-dimension condition.
BCN21 combines this with a common overfield to give generic same-prime
NonSS transport whenever I' is contained in the ordinary ideal generated
by the image of I. The actual application has equality.
BCN22 preserves the two independent n^2-tuples in DiscSt.

Quantifiers and unchanged objects
--------------------------------
One O' is chosen for the whole finite equal-cardinality point family.
It is chosen before its arbitrary intermediate/endpoint prime tests.
The lifted point maps may depend on the chosen primes, which is allowed
by Core's universal point quantifier. The final containment is for the
original B and original J, without enlarging or radicalizing J.

All auxiliary levels N, the original group packet T, D, D+n*T.q, the
point fields, NP records, Kap records, eigenvalues, and B1/B2 constants
and eventual-N sets remain unchanged. There is no n! inversion, so no
new exclusion of ell=3.

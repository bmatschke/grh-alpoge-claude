# Realizing the twist coefficients at a prescribed finite place

At a finite place $w$ of the number field $L$, the local Hecke construction already has a prescribed embedding $e:L\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. The twist construction supplies a second realization of $L$, together with a ring of twist coefficients. We need the two constructions to assign the same value to **every** element of $L$ in one overfield, while also assigning the same value to every twist coefficient. The reason this is possible is classical. Two embeddings of a number field into $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ inducing the same place differ by a single element of $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell/\mathbb Q_\ell)$. We may therefore keep $e$ fixed and transport the twist coefficients by the inverse automorphism. [Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Propositions 8.1–8.2](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf#page=138)

## The finite field that suffices

Fix a compatible twist frame and a compatible choice $C$ as in the source. Write $i=C.\mathrm{twI}(w)$, $k=C.k'(w)$ and $\ell=\mathrm{resChar}(w)=D.\mathrm{ntw}.l(i)$. Let $O_0= D.\mathrm{ntw}.O0(i)$, $A=D.\mathrm{ntw}.Ok(i,k)$, $B=C.Oe(w)$ and $H=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$. The map $t:O_0\to B$ is the specified composite through $D.\mathrm{ntw}.O(i)$ and $A$. The frame realizes $O_0$ as the integers of a finite field extension $K_0/\mathbb Q_\ell$ inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ by an isomorphism $a$. Its realization $\kappa:A\hookrightarrow\mathbb C_\ell$ has image in the closed unit ball and agrees with $a$ on $O_0$. Here $\mathbb C_\ell$ denotes the completion of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Indeed the frame models $A$ using the closure of a field containing the maximal unramified extension. The finite extension we need for descent is $K_0$ up to conjugacy. [Frame and commuting coefficient maps](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/ConcreteTwistFrameExtraExcludedOverSmallPrimes.lean#L133-L164) · [The two rings inside the algebraic closure and its completion](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/NormalizedHeckeFactors/TwDefs.lean#L14-L26)

**Proposition.** There exists a conjugate $F_4(w)$ of $K_0$ such that for every finite $K/\mathbb Q_\ell$ inside the fixed algebraic closure containing $F_4(w)$ and $e(L)$, there is a $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebra map $f:O_0\to\mathcal O_K$ with image $\mathcal O_{F_4(w)}$. There are embeddings $\tau:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C_\ell$ and $\iota:H\hookrightarrow\mathbb C_\ell$ satisfying both identities
$$
\tau(f(y))=\iota(t(y))\quad(y\in O_0),\qquad
\tau(e(x))=\iota(b(x))\quad(x\in L),
$$
where $b=C.\mathrm{embW}(w):L\hookrightarrow H$. The same $F_4(w)$ and the same map into its ring of integers work at every level $j$.

**Proof.** The choice gives an injection $A\hookrightarrow B$ making the discrete valuation ring $B$ a finite $A$-module. Consequently $H$ is finite over $\operatorname{Frac}(A)$ (localizing $B$ at $A\setminus\{0\}$ gives a finite-dimensional domain over a field, hence a field). Since $\mathbb C_\ell$ is algebraically closed, $\kappa$ extends to an embedding $\iota:H\hookrightarrow\mathbb C_\ell$. [Conrad, *Completion of algebraic closure*, Theorem 1.1](https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/154Page/handouts/algclosurecomp.pdf) · [Stacks Project, §9.10, Lemmas 9.10.5–6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09GP)

This extension detects the actual valuation ring of $H$. Every $z\in B$ satisfies a monic equation over $A$, so $|\iota(z)|\leq 1$ by the ultrametric inequality. Every unit of $B$ has norm exactly one, by applying the bound also to its inverse. Since $|\iota(\ell)|=\ell^{-1}<1$, $\ell$ is a nonunit in $B$. Write $\ell=u\pi^d$ for a uniformizer $\pi$, a unit $u$ and $d>0$. Thus $|\iota(\pi)|<1$. Writing every nonzero element of $H$ as a unit times an integer power of $\pi$ gives
$$
z\in B\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |\iota(z)|\leq1 .
$$
The place assumptions on $b$ and $e$ now show that $\iota b$ and $e$ pull back the same valuation ring on $L$. Moreover $(\iota b)(L)\subset\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, since each element is a root of a nonzero rational polynomial and the latter splits in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. [Choice of the valuation ring and of the embedding of $L$](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataNChoice.lean#L22-L35)

To see exactly what conjugacy gives, choose a primitive element $\alpha$ of $L/\mathbb Q$. A place above $\ell$ singles out an irreducible factor over $\mathbb Q_\ell$ of its rational minimal polynomial. Both $e(\alpha)$ and $(\iota b)(\alpha)$ belong to that factor's roots, since the valuation ring determines the place and both absolute values have the same normalization on $\mathbb Q$. The isomorphism taking the first root to the second extends to an automorphism $\sigma$ of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ over $\mathbb Q_\ell$. Hence $\sigma e=\iota b$ on all of $L$. Uniqueness of the absolute value on finite extensions of $\mathbb Q_\ell$ makes $\sigma$ an isometry. [Milne, Theorem 7.38 and Proposition 8.1](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf#page=138) · [Extension of embeddings](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09GP)

Take $F_4(w)=\sigma^{-1}(K_0)$ and $f=\sigma^{-1}a$, followed by the inclusion of its image in $\mathcal O_K$. Its image is the whole of $\mathcal O_{F_4(w)}$ by isometry and surjectivity of $a$ onto $\mathcal O_{K_0}$. It preserves $\mathbb Z_\ell$ because $a$ is normalized on these scalars and $\sigma$ fixes $\mathbb Q_\ell$. Take $\tau$ to be the restriction of $\sigma$ to $K$, followed by inclusion into $\mathbb C_\ell$. Then $\tau f=a=\iota t$ and $\tau e=\iota b$, as required. $\square$

## Application to the original twist datum

At a twisted place, write $s:L_0\to\operatorname{Frac}(O_0)$ for the embedding selected by $C.\mathrm{sd}(w)$ from the two source embeddings. The defining compatibility of the choice is $b|_{L_0}=\operatorname{Frac}(t)\,s$. For $x\in L_0$ and $y\in O_0$ with $s(x)=y$, the two identities of the proposition give $\tau(e(x))=\iota(t(y))=\tau(f(y))$. Injectivity gives $e(x)=f(y)$ in $K$. This proves the required side-specific equality with the originally chosen side. [Choice of side](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/ConcreteTwistFrameExtraExcludedOverSmallPrimes.lean#L194-L205)

For the original coefficient datum $X$, take $K=X.Kx(j,w)$. The condition $X.E=E_0$ fixes $e$ and $X.hKx$ puts $e(L)$ in $K$. Under the canonical identification $\operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O_K)=K$, the source's map $\mathrm{ecC}$ is $e$ with this codomain. Thus the proposition proves all three conclusions of the native theorem, including its quantifier over every larger coefficient field. In [HLP32](local_hecke_projection/main.tex#L981-L1019) its $F_4$ can be used directly in the compositum with $F_1,F_2,F_3$. The compatible twist frame and choice are the inputs at this application.

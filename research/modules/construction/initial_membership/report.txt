Purpose and result
==================

This note proves the initial-symbol half of incoming MemAt. Under the
literal DataII hypotheses and C >= 1, every actual symbol symP(B_b)
satisfies MemAtG(0,1/8). It therefore proves the mathematical content of
TwistedSubstitution/MemRed.Lemma5aAt. The independent WT reconstruction
transports this result through the permitted operator words to
MemAtG(1/8,3/8), which is the source MemAt. The PG and DT notes then apply
to the selected normal projection and digit block.

The proof below follows the actual closed source branch through
PlanePairsOnBlocksAt, RestT, RestN, RestTh, RestPl, RestSP and
RestArithStep. It does not assume any of those propositions as an
unproved estimate. Ordinary characteristic-zero complete reducibility
and highest-weight theory are background, as in the PG/IW notes; their
specialized projector/weight application is proved in IN3. No integral
semisimplicity, new denominator estimate, or equivariance of the map
X -> X^(p^g) in characteristic zero is assumed.

A significant scope distinction: Lemma5aGeneratorProductsAt is a stronger
unblocked statement quantified over arbitrary pair products. The closed
archive route does not use a proof of that stronger statement. It uses
PlanePairsOnBlocksAt, which keeps the actual total-degree constraint on
the chosen digit block. We prove that exact route. The degree constraint
is essential for the link-loss estimate, even though individual product
summands in a span expansion need not themselves have the prescribed
total degree. Claiming the stronger unblocked statement from this proof
would be unjustified.

The exact source ranges and hashes appear in provenance.json. Every
cited working-tree file is byte-checked against the pinned git blob by
regenerate_provenance.py. No Lean compilation or independent verification
of the entire claimed RH proof is asserted.


IN1. Precise objects, signs, and endpoint
=======================================

Let O be the discrete valuation ring in DataII, and let Kf=Frac(O).
The unramified quadratic setting implies characteristic zero. Write
k=r+K for the total coordinate dimension: coordinates 0,...,r-1 are
plane coordinates, and r,...,r+K-1 are normal coordinates. The actual
hypotheses include p >= 13, r >= 2, K > 0 and even n'. The proof below
uses p >= 3 and r,K >= 1 wherever the weaker hypotheses suffice.

Frames are indexed cyclically by j in Z/J, using integer lifts with
fr(j+J,i)=fr(j,i)+n'. The entries fr(j,0)<...<fr(j,r-1) are even, and
z_j=fr(j,r-1). The selected letters in segment j consist of the preceding
frame, the intermediate letters LL_j, and the current frame. There are
no selected letters between consecutive entries within a frame. All
frame letters are vectors. An intermediate letter is a vector if its
integer position is even and a covector if its position is odd; even n'
makes this independent of the periodic lift.

Let n_j=#LL_j. Enumerate the nonpreceding-frame letters increasingly as

  x_(j,u)=the u-th intermediate position             (0 <= u < n_j),
  x_(j,u)=fr(j,u-n_j)                               (n_j <= u < n_j+r).

The actual true-letter type TL uses a reversed index on the frames:
frame(j,m) represents fr(j,r-1-m), for 0 <= m < r. This reversal matters
in the link formula below. Let q_x=p^(z_j-pos(x)) for a true letter in
segment j. The prefix N_(j,u) consists of the first u true letters in
this increasing-position order. It contains no preceding-frame letters.

For a signed-letter polynomial F and subset S, v_S(F) is the minimum
Laurent exponent after applying the generic conjugate
g diag(1,...,1,t) g^(-1) to the vector variables in S and its inverse
transpose to the covectors in S. The source lam1S, denoted L_S here, is
the maximum exponent for g diag(t,1,...,1) g^(-1). A generic coordinate
permutation identifies the two choices of torus coordinate. Set
v_S(0)=+infinity and L_S(0)=-infinity. For nonzero polynomials over the
domain Kf, the usual Laurent support rules give

  v_S(FG)=v_S(F)+v_S(G),
  v_S(F+G)>=min(v_S(F),v_S(G)),
  L_S(FG)=L_S(F)+L_S(G).

Every true multidegree or model multidegree is preserved by the twist.
Therefore extracting homogeneous components, or filtering by any
predicate on complete letter-degree vectors, cannot decrease v_S or
increase L_S. Also v_S(F)>=0 if all variables of F lying in S are vector
variables. These assertions follow directly from the generic twist:
vector matrix entries have only t-exponents 0,1; covector entries only
0,-1; the coefficient ring obtained by inverting det(g) is a domain.

At each normal prefix N_(j,u) use the canonical GL_K isotypic projector
for a dominant integer weight kappa_(j,u). The source allows any e
satisfying IsJProj(normE,{kappa}); IN3 proves that it is necessarily the
product of the canonical individual projectors on each finite
multidegree component.

Write d=flatDeg(lambda_0). For x=x_(j,u), define

  y=oldest(j,x) = the r-th most recent even selected letter strictly
                  before x,
  B_(j,u)=d(y mod n')/p^(z_j-y),
  s_(j,u)=floor(B_(j,u)/(Y k^2)).

All floors in this display are integer floors. The source pair gap uses
m_*+Nat.floor(B/(k^2 Y)); this is at least m_*+s and equals it when B>=0.
Here m_* is exactly SourceCleaning.mStar, with the source parameters:

  c'=cStar+r N c0+2r n' cpp,
  Q=p^G',
  m_*=(N1+1)[(2Q+3)c'+kQ+c' alphaMax+3c'].

Its precise size is inherited from the symbol construction; the initial
membership proof only uses the defining pair gap m_*+s. The thresholds
are the source thStar, namely the maximum of the following present terms:

  B_(j,u)/2;
  kappa_(j,u+1),1 + m_* + s_(j,u)        if x_(j,u) is even;
  kappa_(j,u-1),1 + m_* + s_(j,u)        if u>0 and x_(j,u-1) is odd.
                                                          (IN1.1)

The odd-predecessor term uses u-1, not u. Its normal upper bound is on
the strict prefix preceding the odd letter, while its plane lower bound
includes that odd letter.

Let a physical digit block retain residues rho at every selected
physical coordinate modulo q_x, divide exponents by q_x, and retain
plane and normal letter multidegrees dP,dN. This is levImg. It is a
polynomial in plane variables with polynomial normal coefficients.
For every such block, every joint projector e, and every normal monomial
nu, set

  H=[Z^nu](1 tensor e) levImg_(rho,dP,dN)(symP(B_b)).     (IN1.2)

The desired conclusion consists of exactly these three clauses:

  (a) H has plane letter multidegree dP.
  (b) For every frame residue sigma and every 0<=i<=n_j,
      v_(C_(j,i))(Fdig_sigma H)>=theta_(j,i).
  (c) For 0<=m<r with m+2<=r, put u=n_j+r-1-m. Then
      v_(T_(j,m))(H)>=theta_(j,u)-s_(j,u)/8.
                                                          (IN1.3)

C_(j,i) is the model chain consisting of all preceding-frame model
variables wc(j-1,m), and the first i intermediate letters of segment j.
T_(j,m) consists of all true intermediate letters of segment j and the
true frame letters frame(j,m') with m'>m. Thus T_(j,m) is the true
strict prefix before x_(j,n_j+r-1-m), with the preceding frame removed.
The source XX definition with these cutoffs is exactly X_D(0,1/8).


IN2. Expand actual symbols while retaining degree blocks
======================================================

By definition, each actual B_b belongs to Gen for a common degree
d0=G2d(F,d,E'). Thus

  B_b = [product_(j=0,...,J-1) phSeg_j(P_j)] X^l,
  P_j belongs to symbolSpaces_j,
  every monomial of B_b has physical multidegree d0.

Here d0(t)=d(t)-E'(t) in natural-number subtraction on selected positions,
and d0(t)=0 elsewhere. In particular 0<=d0(t)<=d(t). The label l satisfies
l_(x,c)<q_x on every nonpreceding-frame selected letter and coordinate.

The pair span underlying symbolSpaces consists of P_j=A_j G_j, where
A_j uses plane variables from the full segment, G_j uses normal
variables only from the segment after its preceding frame, and for each
such position x there is an integer M_j(x) with

  x even:  L_(<=x)(G_j)<=M_j(x),
           v_(<x)(A_j)>=M_j(x)+m_*+Nat.floor(B_x/(k^2Y));
  x odd:   L_(<x)(G_j)<=M_j(x),
           v_(<=x)(A_j)>=M_j(x)+m_*+Nat.floor(B_x/(k^2Y));
  always:  v_(<x)(A_j)>=B_x/2.                         (IN2.1)

The additional order-degree restrictions in symbolSpaces are stronger
input conditions but are not needed for this initial membership lemma.
Likewise linear independence and a complemented integral span of the
B_b family are unnecessary for membership of each B_b.

The actual symbol symP(B_b) is the coefficient-field image of B_b times
SPR, with the harmless coordinate identification k=r+K. The multiplier
SPR is a product of powers of frame and moving-window determinants.
Each matrix entry is a nonnegative power of a first-r coordinate at an
even selected position. All rows used in the moving-window determinant
are even by the frame/window conditions. Consequently every monomial
gamma of SPR is supported only on plane vector variables. This proves
RestSP directly; no coefficient valuation or determinant nonvanishing
is needed here.

Fix one gamma, and put dd(t)=d0(t)+sum_c gamma(t,c). The polynomial
B_b X^gamma has this physical multidegree. For any of its nonzero
digit/degree blocks, division of each exponent into quotient and
remainder implies

  sum_c rho(x,c)+q_x[dP(x)+dN(x)]=dd(pos(x)).           (IN2.2)

This is exactly BlockDeg. If (IN2.2) fails, the levImg block of B_b
X^gamma is zero.

Now fix dd as above and prove the desired estimate only on blocks
satisfying (IN2.2). Such a property is a Kf-linear subspace of physical
polynomials, because every operation levImg, 1 tensor e, normal
coefficient extraction, and every valuation cutoff is linear. Expand
each P_j into its integral pair span. Multilinearity then reduces to
products of literal pairs A_j G_j, with the same fixed dd used only in
the block condition. The product summands need not have physical degree
dd. After proving the compatible-block assertion for them, sum, and
finally invoke the actual homogeneity to discard incompatible blocks.
This is precisely MemAtB -> MemAtG and avoids any unsupported stronger
unblocked pair-generator assertion.

For a product of pairs and a fixed label/monomial, write F for its
physical polynomial. Every exponent coming from phSeg is divisible by
the corresponding q_x. If x is in the preceding frame for segment j,
its exponent after division by the q_x of segment j-1 acquires the
additional factor p^(z_j-z_(j-1)); otherwise quotienting simply renames
the variable. Therefore all residues of F equal those of l+gamma. Let

  b_(x,a)=floor((l_(x,a)+gamma_(x,a))/q_x), a<r.

The normal part of gamma is zero, and l_(x,r+c)<q_x, so its normal
quotients vanish. If rho does not equal the residue of l+gamma, the
whole block is zero. Otherwise exponent division yields the exact
tensor factorization

  levImg_(rho,dP,dN) F = A_* tensor G_*,
  A_*=[dP] levP(zPl),
  G_*=[dN] product_j rename_j(G_j),                   (IN2.3)

where zPl is the plane product including l and gamma. Neither factorial
factors nor binomial coefficients occur. In particular

  [Z^nu](1 tensor e)(A_* tensor G_*)
       = coeff_nu(eG_*) A_*.                         (IN2.4)

This identity isolates the entire normal projector from the plane
filtration estimate. If eG_*=0, the result is zero and all lower bounds
hold automatically.


IN3. Selected joint normal types inherit the actual prefix bounds
================================================================

Work in a fixed finite normal multidegree. Its polynomial representation
is a finite-dimensional rational GL_K representation over Kf. Standard
characteristic-zero complete reducibility gives its canonical isotypic
projectors P_(S,lambda). They are uniquely characterized as identity on
the lambda-isotypic component and zero on the other components. This
is a rational/field decomposition, not an integral direct sum.

For S subset T, P_(S,lambda) commutes with the diagonal GL_K action on T:
that action is the product of its actions on S and on T\S. It commutes
with the S-action by construction, and with the disjoint T\S action
because the latter is an S-intertwiner. Hence P_(S,lambda) preserves
every T-isotypic subspace and commutes with every P_(T,mu). Disjoint
subsets give the same conclusion directly. The normal prefixes in one
segment are nested, and the prefixes in distinct segments are disjoint.
Their canonical projectors therefore commute pairwise.

Resolve an arbitrary vector by the finitely many individual
decompositions successively. Commutation ensures that the resulting
pieces are joint-isotypic vectors. They span the whole finite-degree
space. An operator satisfying the literal IsJProj conditions is fixed
on each such piece; therefore it is uniquely the product

  e_kappa = product_(j,u) P_(N_(j,u),kappa_(j,u)).      (IN3.1)

Thus e_kappa G_* != 0 implies
P_(N_(j,u),kappa_(j,u)) G_* != 0 for every (j,u).

For completeness, L_S on a nonzero isotypic vector of dominant highest
weight lambda is lambda_1. All coordinate weights in that irreducible
representation are <=lambda_1, and lambda_1 occurs. The orbit of any
nonzero vector generates an irreducible factor, so its component at
that extremal weight is nonzero after a generic change of basis. A
multiplicity-space functional reduces the isotypic case to the
irreducible one. Conversely canonical projections commute with the
generic twist, so different types cannot cancel to remove the maximal
Laurent exponent. This is the maximum-weight counterpart of the
minimum-weight argument already used in PG.

The product expression in (IN2.3) has disjoint segment variables.
For N_(j,u), every factor except G_j is outside the selected set and has
torus exponent zero. Renaming preserves signs because n' is even.
The set N_(j,u) is exactly the supported strict prefix <x_(j,u) of G_j;
N_(j,u+1) is its supported prefix <=x_(j,u). Extracting dN commutes with
the twist and cannot increase L. Therefore eG_* != 0 gives

  L_(<x_(j,u))(G_j)<=M  => kappa_(j,u),1<=M,
  L_(<=x_(j,u))(G_j)<=M => kappa_(j,u+1),1<=M.         (IN3.2)

This proves RestN, including its quantification over arbitrary selected
joint projectors. No denominator bound enters, so the universal
projector-lattice estimate IW4 is not needed again here.


IN4. Recover the exact plane threshold, including the odd predecessor
====================================================================

Assume eG_* != 0. The always clause of (IN2.1) gives the B/2 term of
(IN1.1). If x_(j,u) is even, combine the first implication of the even
pair clause with the <=x implication in (IN3.2); the original integer
gap is at least m_*+s. This yields the even term of (IN1.1).

If u>0 and x'=x_(j,u-1) is odd, the odd pair clause gives

  v_(<=x')(A_j)>=M_j(x')+m_*+s_(j,u-1),
  kappa_(j,u-1),1<=M_j(x').

There is no selected letter between the consecutive positions x' and
x_(j,u). On the supported letters of A_j, <=x' and <x_(j,u) therefore
coincide. The selected even letters strictly before x' and strictly
before x_(j,u) also coincide, because x' is odd. Their r-th most recent
member, and hence B and s, are identical. We obtain the odd-predecessor
term with s_(j,u). Taking the maximum proves

  v_(<x_(j,u))(A_j)>=theta_(j,u)  for 0<=u<n_j+r.      (IN4.1)

The argument works for zero polynomials with the +infinity convention.


IN5. Elementary substitution and degree-filter calculus
======================================================

Introduce a model polynomial ring over Kf with independent letters

  ll(j,l): intermediate letters of segment j, with their original sign;
  lab(j,m): current-frame labels, all vectors;
  wc(j,m): preceding-frame model letters, all vectors.

The full source ML also contains tangent letters tp. None occurs in
this initial construction. Let chi_j be the literal letter renaming
on the support of A_j:

  its intermediate position l -> ll(j,l),
  fr(j,r-1-m)                 -> lab(j,m),
  fr(j-1,r-1-m)               -> wc(j-1,m).

Frame geometry guarantees this is unambiguous. For a fixed chain
C_(j,i)={wc(j-1,m)} union {ll(j,l):l<i}, the chi_j preimage on supported
letters is exactly the strict prefix <x_(j,i), for 0<=i<=n_j, with the
same vector/covector signs. Other chi_j' factors have support outside
this chain: distinct labels have distinct constructors, and their wc
letters have different cyclic segment indices. DataII implies J>=2;
the arithmetic justification is given in IN8.

The following generic-twist substitution rule justifies the transport
in this paragraph and later in IN7. Suppose a substitution sends each
supported letter inside S to a single letter inside T with the same
sign, and sends the remaining supported letters to polynomials involving
only variables outside T. Then twisting after substitution equals
substituting the untwisted outside images after twisting the source.
This is verified on each polynomial variable. The substitution on
Laurent coefficients has no torus dependence; it cannot create lower
torus exponents. Thus v_T(image F)>=v_S(F). The analogous maximum
inequality holds. There is no requirement that unrelated letters be
mapped injectively, although the principal renamings here are injective.

Put Pi=product_j chi_j(A_j), after the coefficient-field map. By (IN4.1)
and the support observation,

  v_(C_(j,i))(Pi)>=theta_(j,i).                        (IN5.1)

Define phi by

  phi(ll(j,l,a))=X_(ll(j,l),a),
  phi(lab(j,m,a))=X_(frame(j,m),a),
  phi(wc(j,m,a))=X_(frame(j,m),a)^P_j,
  P_j=p^(z_(j+1)-z_j).

It is crucial that phi is a polynomial substitution, NOT a claimed
GL_r intertwiner in characteristic zero. Exact exponent division in
the physical polynomial gives

  levP(zPl)=X^b phi(Pi).                              (IN5.2)

For a model letter-degree vector delta, the degree of its phi-image is

  true ll: delta_ll;
  true frame(j,m): delta_lab(j,m)+P_j delta_wc(j,m).

Consequently the identity

  [dP](X^b phi(Pi)) = X^b phi(Pi')                    (IN5.3)

holds, where Pi' keeps precisely the model multidegree vectors for which
deg(b)+phiDegree(delta)=dP. This remains true if different model monomials
have the same phi-image: the equality is monomial by monomial, before
combining equal images. Since the filter commutes with every generic
twist, Pi' still satisfies (IN5.1), and has no tangent variables.


IN6. Exact frame digit extraction: zero chain loss
=================================================

The source Fdig_sigma extracts the residues sigma of each frame
coordinate modulo P_j, removes those residues, divides its exponent by
P_j, and renames that coordinate as wc(j,m,a). True intermediate
coordinates are unchanged and are renamed ll. It is a Kf-linear map.

First suppose Q uses only ll and wc, and beta is a true-coordinate
monomial exponent. A term X^beta phi(X^mu) has intermediate exponent
beta_ll+mu_ll, and frame exponent beta_frame+P_j mu_wc. Hence

  Fdig_sigma(X^beta phi(Q)) =
    0,                                      if beta_frame mod P != sigma;
    X^(lift(floor(beta/P))) Q,                otherwise.              (IN6.1)

In the second line, division is by P_j on each frame coordinate and by
1 on intermediate coordinates; lift renames frame variables to wc and
intermediate variables to ll. The residue condition is only on the
frame coordinates. This is an exact identity over Kf; neither an
approximation nor a positive-characteristic Frobenius argument is used.

Expand Pi' in the independent lab variables:

  Pi'=sum_M lab^M Q_M(ll,wc).                         (IN6.2)

Since all lab variables lie outside every chain C_(j,i), coefficient
extraction in them commutes with the chain twist. Therefore
v_(C_(j,i))(Q_M)>=theta_(j,i). The image phi(lab^M) is a true frame
monomial, say X^eta_M. Apply (IN6.1) to beta=b+eta_M.

The extra monomial in (IN6.1) has only vector variables inside the chain.
All wc variables are vectors. At a covector intermediate letter, gamma
has exponent zero, while the label exponent is <q; hence b=0 there.
The lab monomial contributes only true frame variables, so eta_M has
no intermediate exponents. Thus no negative intermediate factor is
introduced. The generic minimum of that extra monomial is >=0.

Each nonzero summand of Fdig_sigma(A_*) therefore has minimum chain
weight at least theta_(j,i), and sums preserve the inequality. We obtain

  v_(C_(j,i))(Fdig_sigma A_*)>=theta_(j,i)             (IN6.3)

for every frame residue sigma. This proves the full RestChain content,
with exactly zero chain loss.


IN7. Link loss is bounded by the preceding-frame degree
======================================================

Fix j and m with m+2<=r, and set u=n_j+r-1-m. Let W be the preceding
frame, S the original supported prefix <x_(j,u), and T=T_(j,m). We have
W subset S, all W letters are vectors, and T is the image of S\W under
the non-W part of Phi_j=phi chi_j.

First prove an elementary deletion lemma. If all monomials of A have
total W-degree <=d_W, and v_(S)(A)>=h, then each coefficient A_M after
expanding A in the W variables satisfies

  v_(S\W)(A_M)>=h-d_W.                               (IN7.1)

Indeed apply the inverse vector twist to the W variables in the generic
S-twist of A. The two twists cancel on W and leave the S\W twist.
The inverse vector matrix has Laurent exponents 0,-1. Since twisting
does not change W-degree, it lowers every Laurent exponent by at most
d_W. Extracting a W coefficient commutes with the remaining S\W twist.
This proves (IN7.1), including arbitrary cancellations.

Under Phi_j, W variables become positive powers of frame variables in
segment j-1, outside the target set T because J>=2. Other supported
variables are renamed degree-one, preserving signs and membership in
the target prefix. Apply the substitution rule of IN5 to the
coefficients in (IN7.1). The W monomial images have T-weight zero.
Thus

  v_T(Phi_j A)>=h-d_W.                               (IN7.2)

The other factors Phi_j'(A_j') can meet T only at true frame variables:
their intermediate variables lie in different segments. Since every
true frame variable is a vector, they have T-order >=0. The base
monomial X^b also has T-order >=0 by the absence of covector exponents
proved in IN6.

To use the sharpest relevant degree, decompose A_j into its letter
multihomogeneous components A_(j,delta). They retain (IN4.1), because
degree extraction commutes with the generic twist. Every monomial of
Phi_j A_(j,delta) has true degree

  P_(j-1) delta(fr(j-1,r-1-m'))

at frame(j-1,m'). Let beta_(j-1,m')=deg_(frame(j-1,m')) b. If for some
m' this number plus beta_(j-1,m') exceeds dP(frame(j-1,m')), the final
[dP] extraction of the whole product is zero: all other degrees are
nonnegative. Otherwise

  P_(j-1) delta(fr(j-1,r-1-m'))
       <= dP(frame(j-1,m'))-beta_(j-1,m').            (IN7.3)

The total W-degree d_W of this component is the sum of these delta
values. Define

  Delta_j = sum_(m'=0,...,r-1)
    max(dP(frame(j-1,m'))-beta_(j-1,m'),0)/P_(j-1).
                                                          (IN7.4)

This is precisely the source dwB: its natural-number subtraction is
the positive part in (IN7.4), and P_(j-1)=p^(z_j-z_(j-1)) is independent
of the frame row. Equation (IN7.3) gives d_W<=Delta_j on every surviving
piece. Apply (IN7.2), multiply by the other nonnegative-order factors,
extract dP, and sum. Real thresholds may be rounded up to integers
before (IN7.1); rounding back only strengthens the conclusion. We have

  v_(T_(j,m))(A_*)>=theta_(j,u)-Delta_j.              (IN7.5)

This proves RestLink before numerical specialization. The loss is a
degree of the removed preceding frame, not the total degree of the
plane polynomial and not a sum over all segments.


IN8. The actual degree and frame hypotheses give Delta_j<=s/8
================================================================

We now use the compatible-block condition (IN2.2). Fix a true letter x.
Write l_c and gamma_c for its physical exponents, q=q_x, and
beta_x=sum_(a<r) floor((l_a+gamma_a)/q). Because gamma has no normal
variables and l_normal<q, beta_x is the entire quotient degree of the
base monomial l+gamma. Its residue sum is

  sum_c rho(x,c)=sum_c(l_c+gamma_c)-q beta_x.

Substitute this into (IN2.2), where dd=d0+deg gamma. Cancellation of the
actual gamma degree gives the equality of integers

  q(dP(x)+dN(x)-beta_x)=d0(pos(x))-sum_c l_c.         (IN8.1)

If dP(x)>=beta_x, discard the nonnegative dN and label terms. If
dP(x)<beta_x, its positive part is zero. In both cases

  q max(dP(x)-beta_x,0)<=d0(pos(x))<=d(pos(x)).       (IN8.2)

This cancellation explains why the fixed physical degree must remain
visible throughout the proof. A large localizer monomial gamma causes
no uncontrolled extra loss; it is removed by the base-degree term in
Delta_j. The source lemma Ar.deg_le proves exactly (IN8.2).

Put g=z_j-z_(j-1), and
t_m=z_(j-1)-fr(j-1,r-1-m). The t_m are distinct nonnegative integers,
because frame positions are strictly increasing. For the corresponding
previous true-frame letter q=p^t_m and P_(j-1)=p^g. The actual flat-degree
bounds on selected positions are a<=d(t)<=2a. Thus (IN8.2) implies

  Delta_j <= (2a/p^g) sum_m p^(-t_m)
           <= (2a/p^g)/(1-1/p)
           <= 3a/p^g,                               (IN8.3)

using p>=3. There is no factor r here: the distinct frame positions
give a subseries of the geometric series.

Let y=oldest(j,x_(j,u)) at the link position, D=z_j-y, and B=d(y)/p^D.
The first r intermediate selected letters in segment j are vectors.
The first one lies more than L1 after z_(j-1), by the defining gap
condition. They all precede every current-frame position. Consequently
the r-th most recent vector before the link lies at or after that first
intermediate vector, so

  y>z_(j-1)+L1,  hence D+L1+1<=g.                    (IN8.4)

The lower flat-degree bound gives a<=B p^D. The upper bound on a whole
segment in FramesOK gives D<=G', and Q=p^G'. The actual numerical
hypotheses include, writing N=#TT,

  Y>=r>=2,   N>=1,   cStar>=1,
  256 k^2 N Y^2 <= p^(L1-1),

OUTCOME

This chapter proves the concrete construction and transport arguments around
the original joint Count II producer. The central identity is

  J = ker(B -> T) = Ann_B(M),

where B is the actual universal ordinary determinant ring, M is the original
ordinary module, and T is its original Hecke subalgebra of End_Lambda(M).
The homomorphism B -> T is proved surjective by explicit preimages of every
spherical and ordinary Hecke generator. No closure of its image is used.
For the allowed zero-module alternative, J is the whole ring and the same
annihilator equality holds. [JSP3–JSP5]

The chapter does not manufacture M, its arithmetic fibers, or higher-level
automorphic modules. It proves complete implications from these explicitly
printed original inputs. COUNT_II_SUPPORT remains an open category C
producer; there is no assertion of an unconditional RH, GRH or Count II proof.

THE SUBSTANTIVE HUMAN ARGUMENTS NOW WRITTEN

1. Original coordinates. The existing local reciprocity and universal-weight
   construction gives 4[L+:Q] coordinates on the actual inertia groups. The
   variable identity includes the residual-character correction c_j and the
   original character evaluated at the chosen inertia element. This constructs
   the exact WtVars used by TWBase, rather than a dimension-only substitute.
   [JSP1; KX3–KX5]

2. Determinant laws rather than traces alone. A family of arithmetic ring maps
   which detects zero also detects full determinant-law equalities and the
   source's Kills condition over every coefficient algebra. The proof uses
   independent polynomial variables and coefficientwise injection; it does
   not tensor an injection with an arbitrary algebra. The flagged ordinary
   product is multiplied in the source order, and polarization supplies its
   star relation. Teichmuller uniqueness supplies the tame residual kernel.
   These arguments construct the actual Pair and its universal map. [JSP2–3]

3. Surjectivity and all ring fields. The coefficient of X^(4-j) in the original
   inverse-Frobenius Hecke polynomial gives a preimage of T_(w,j), with the
   exact sign and q_w^(-j(j-1)/2) factor. The ordered-character uniformizer
   formula gives each U_(v,j). The weight triangle supplies the scalar algebra.
   Consequently the quotient is T itself. Since the original module is finite
   free over the original power-series weight ring, T is a finite torsion-free
   submodule of End(M). Fiber tests give all eight RingProps fields, including
   full tame and star identities. Auxiliary I0 unramifiedness at a separating
   weight remains explicitly supplied. [JSP4–6]

4. Actual density. The strict weight h is converted to the dominant weight
   lambda_h(tau)_a = h_(v,3-a,tau) - (3-a). This is the source reversal and
   shift, and the actual hset admits every weight. The IsTheta identity gives
   the exact finite-character formula. Good points of T then give good points
   of B killing J; separation in T proves the literal Dense field for every
   increasing h. [JSP7]

   A source-reading precision is retained here. IsWtPart quantifies over every
   map satisfying the exact source IsLocalArtin predicate. Thus the abstract

   artOf, uniqueness of every source IsLocalArtin map, and the literal

5. Original Taylor–Wiles base. The source TWBase is constructed on M itself
   with its B-action, the exact weight variables, and support established by

6. Actual topological generation. The universal determinant/character values
   generate B modulo every power of its maximal ideal. Integral word

   contradicts universality. Successive corrections in the associated graded

7. Characteristic-prime kernel transport. An injective local map between the

8. The complete h=0 support implication. Starting with the original NiceI
   point, the proof tracks its original inertia and auxiliary D0 through

   directly: residual unipotence gives N^4 in tM_4 and an ell-power estimate;

   connecting thick primes, no NiceE argument and no use of P3core. [JSP11]

Fix the original Fit/Deep tower and iota first. A common coefficient threshold
must work for all subsequent coefficients, original Spaces and arbitrary
actual universal rings/weight objects. It must cover both rank-four Links,
with their common kappa and different auxiliary primes. Each Link has its own
universal ring and its own ideal; every required property for that Link is
proved on that one ideal. No conjunction of separate existential ideals is
used. Its J is selected before the NiceI point and before the later T/TWPl.
The conditional assembly is JSP12; QX10 then supplies PMExAlt, and the existing
YX2 supplies CountII.

This work contributes to RH as well as unrestricted Dirichlet GRH. The
principal and other 2-primary targets remove only Case III by KA5–KA6.
They do not make Cases I, II or V vacuous. For unrestricted GRH the shared
PMExAlt also contributes to Case III, but its separate Ctx3 and SeedIVAlt
producers remain independent obligations.

The next arithmetic priority is an actual supplier at this reduced boundary:
either the original ordinary arithmetic system and its four same-kernel Hida
endpoints, or the original single-point patched-support route with genuine
all-level HasAut. The present chapter makes those proposed advances
reviewable on the exact ideal instead of requiring another reconstruction
of its algebraic installation.

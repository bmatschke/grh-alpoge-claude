# The integral cyclotomic Wach comparison

The lower cyclotomic roots determine the action and uniqueness of the Wach lattice. At the primitive roots the filtration determines the relative position of its Frobenius image. The proof gives the integral bounds and the signed polynomial at level one. [Editable proof](../../../../manuscript/notes/galois/CYCLOTOMIC_WACH_COMPARISON.md)

3. BOUNDED PARALLEL TARGET F3: ALL ORIGINAL LOCAL BRIDGES

The exact endpoint is BridgeAll.brAt_of_large in

  for each fixed D,w, there exists F0,
  for EVERY later CoefIn X and EVERY j,

BrAt is not just existence of a local completion. For EVERY original
u above ell=resChar(w), it asks for a LocBridge whose group is exactly

whose embedding detects that stored valuation ring, and whose toLL
agrees with the original iqC. It also requires GoodCoef for the original

The lower target is PlaceBridge.exists_locBridge in
FiniteDimensionalAdjoinRange.lean:303–325. Fix E, its algebraic closure
Omega, a place u above ell, and the specified valuation subring A with
IsPlaceOver(A,u). The output chooses ONE j:Omega->Qellbar and ONE L0
before every finite LL containing L0. For all those LL it supplies a
bridge with exactly that j, coefficient field LL, and

  Kw = Q_ell(j(E)),      x in A iff ||j(x)||<=1.

LocBridge also includes density of j(E) in Kw, a group equivalence
Dec(A) -> GK(Kw), continuity in both directions, and the exact action
identity e(g)(j(x))=j(g(x)). Every Q_ell embedding of Kw must land in
LL. The integral-ring equivalence must fix Z_ell and be a homeomorphism.
The toLL formula must be the original inclusion. All these fields are
part of the endpoint. [F302–F303.]

A complete human proof should follow these lower steps, with each
object kept fixed.

* Match a global algebraic-closure embedding to A by conjugacy of the
  prolongations above the SAME prime. Write the conjugacy argument or
  check an exact published application. The source's short
  exists_smul_eq_of_isPlaceOver wrapper is not already a human proof.
  [F304, F311.]
* Prove density of j(Omega) in Qellbar by rational approximation of
  minimal-polynomial coefficients and root approximation. It is not
  an assertion that j(Omega)=Qellbar. Use the common valuation ring,
  and normalization at ell, to obtain equality of absolute values.
  Extend the resulting isometry through the completion, retaining its
  Q_ell action and algebraic restriction. [F305–F306.]
* Prove finite degree of Q_ell(j(E)) and density of j(E) in it. Identify
  its local Galois group by restriction and extension on the original
  Omega, including the kernel, surjectivity, action square and both
  Krull-topology continuity statements. [F307–F309.]
* Choose the normal closure of Kw for L0. For every larger LL, use the
  identity integral-ring equivalence on O_LL and verify all bridge
  fields. Add the finite extension containing the roots of unity of
  order lcm(ell,condN); this is the actual GoodCoef condition, with no
  rank-based prime exclusion. [F303, F310, F312.]
* Take one finite compositum over the finite set of original primes u
  above ell. Check finiteness by the prime factors of the nonzero ideal
  (ell), and use iqC_alg for the exact last field of BrAt. This gives
  F0(w) before ALL X,j, not a field chosen separately after X. [F301.]

These are proposed B arguments on the stated inputs. Neither HLP32's
finite compositum nor a bare equivalence of abstract Galois groups
discharges them. No direct published application has been matched for
this packet in the present note.

polynomial estimate; and prove that B is EXACTLY the pulled-back unit

In the audit vocabulary, B means elementary/moderate work expected to be
independently reconstructible; it does not mean completed. A requires
a published statement, every hypothesis and every object identification
to be checked. C denotes substantial reconstruction, and D denotes a
remaining gap in our source understanding or matching. This note adds
no A certificate, new theorem, formal-source criticism or completion
percentage. Its proposed next endpoints are exact original producers;
each completed conditional consumer stays complete at precisely its
proved scope.

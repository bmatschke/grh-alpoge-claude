{\Large\bfseries Comparing arithmetic and spectral periods}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.90 \quad 25 November 2026
\end{center}

For each word the proof attaches two bilinear forms to the same row
and column spaces. How can one tell whether changes of orthonormal
coordinates identify the forms for all words at once? Contracting the
column variable gives positive operators on a single row space. Their
mixed traces, or the dimensions of three explicit spaces of matrices,
answer the question. The arithmetic content is to show these invariants
agree for the periods in the selected family. We also identify the
classical representation theorems that put the two constructions on
the same automorphic realization.

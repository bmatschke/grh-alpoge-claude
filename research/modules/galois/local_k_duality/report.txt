EXACT LOCAL-K DUALITY CHAIN — VERSION 0.25

Result
======
LK1–LK4 supply all three literal predicates in
Formalism/GaloisCohomology/LocalTateDualityK.lean:

  LK2: Formalism.GaloisCohomology.LocalFinitenessK
  LK3: Formalism.GaloisCohomology.LocalTateDualityK
  LK4: Formalism.GaloisCohomology.LocalEulerCharK

The output covers every prime l including 2, every finite intermediate
field K of the specified PadicAlgCl(l), every positive r, every finite
discrete Z/l^r-module M, and every continuous action on M. M need not
be free, and the coefficient prime is the residue characteristic.

Completed matching chain
========================
LK1 keeps the actual field and algebraic closure. The restricted norm
is the unique local-field norm; K is already complete. Its integer ring
is shown to be a rank-[K:Q_l] Z_l lattice by explicit norm bounds. The
Galois group and topology remain the original ones. The actual cycK
character is matched on compatible roots of unity. No assertion that
those roots lie in K is made. The original H0/H1/H2/Hn definitions are
matched directly with continuous inhomogeneous cochains and their
differentials.

LK2 applies the finite classical local theorem to these exact objects.
It supplies finiteness in degrees 0,1,2 and the literal Subsingleton
condition on every Hn with n>=3.

LK3 identifies the arbitrary finite coefficient dual with the source
twDual, including the inverse group action and cyclotomic factor.
It proves finite R-biduality on R/(l^a) summands, so no freeness is
hidden. The Kummer/Brauer invariant gives the exact H2 scalar target.
The source's cup cochains are written explicitly: the degree-one cup
has the dual argument first. Postcomposition by the invariant is
inverted, so the result is bijectivity into the original H2 target,
not only after a scalar map. The flipped H1 pairing follows by finite
biduality and uses no division by 2.

LK4 starts with the classical local Euler formula with q=|M|. Since
O_K is free of rank d=[K:Q_l], |O_K/qO_K|=q^d. It obtains exactly

  |H1| = |H0| |H2| |M|^[K:Q_l].

The exponent uses the field degree and the actual module cardinality.
The proof includes the zero module and does not replace M by R^n.

Classical boundary
==================
Three explicitly matched classical leaves remain:
  LK-LF: finite extensions of complete local fields (Milne ANT 7.38).
  LK-TATE: finite local cohomology, Tate duality and the usual invariant
           (Milne ADT I Theorem 2.1 and Corollary 2.3, pp.26–28).
  LK-EULER: local Euler characteristic (Milne ADT I Theorem 2.8, p.31).

The author-hosted sources and errata were consulted. The precise
accepted statements and written specializations are registered in
classical_inputs.json. No project-specific local-K assumption remains.

Downstream scope
================
The three DA24-LK target keys can be retired in their literal scope.
LK2/LK3 supply the hF/hT premises of LemmaK and ThmBab. LK4 supplies
the adjacent residue-characteristic Euler predicate wherever it is
used. This does not prove the global integral H2 reduction, KBridge,
arithmetic-group torsion bounds, infinite-level Selmer comparisons,
ThmBab itself, PointHV, or an actual arithmetic family.

# The first-pass row at prescribed depth

**Row existence on the pinned column.** Fix its initial record $P$ and an admissible state $x$ over $P$. Assume the comparison
$$
 \eta_x(j,v)=\eta_P(j,v)\qquad(1\le j\le m,\ v\text{ any coefficient place}).
$$
Then one can complete the very row reading of $x$, keeping its base level, its $h_X$, its geometric datum, its normalized reading and its map $j_W$. This implication is $\mathrm{RowsAt}(P)$. It holds pointwise on a pinned column in the standing range for the base and forced primes. The existence of $P$ itself supplies the required preliminary record. [Exact assertion][rowsdef] · [Application to the starting column][atstart]

To prove it, write the rational reading on $x$ as the fixed maps together with their comparison laws except for the congruence-length clause. The moved family already reads $\eta_P$ on every pair. Substitution of the displayed equality therefore fills precisely the missing clause. Keep the same geometric intersection law and tuple identification. For the active set, its definition uses the field $F_{0,K}$ selected by the prescribed $\kappa$-data. The two assemblies satisfy the same definition of $\kappa$, hence give the same $F_{0,K}$. Movement preserves the set defined using this field, so it is still $P.W_t$. The fitting conditions on the state give the group and geometric incidences. The original character frame gives the injection, range, the three distinguished characters and the on- and off-support equalities. In particular the index is zero off the active places. [The original reading][read] · [Filling its missing field][fill] · [Identifying the active set and characters][makerows]

The period pairings and lattices in the completed row are the given ones. For the reference tuple, squared norms on spanning integral forms equal the class sums, and polarization over the CM coefficient field gives the full Hermitian pairing. For each nonreference tuple the period coordinates identify the same definite forms, so the same polarization and comparison with the class sum give the required pairing. The integral lattice assertion is already a provisional-row theorem. The extra row witness retains the maps on used tuples and extends to the other tuples by the stored realization, with the prescribed zero coordinates on unused tuples. It takes the reference and twisted period maps from $P$; the standard-position, rank and pullback conditions are carried by the given copy of the core. The four identifications in $\mathrm{RowsAt}$ are then identities of the chosen data. This construction introduces no depth-dependent or additional eventual restriction. [Reference pairing][refpair] · [Twisted pairing][twpair] · [Integral rows and remaining conditions][makerows] · [Extra row witness][extra]

**One realization and the requested grid.** Let $l$ be the fitted first-pass character level. In the common eventual range the [wild-inertia and integral-model construction](FIRST_PASS_WILD_INERTIA.md) and the [full projector comparison](FIRST_PASS_TYPE_PROJECTION.md) give a coefficient request which forces both $\mathrm{Large}$ and the displayed length comparison for **every** realization of that request, for all row and place indices together. Realize this request by the automorphic construction. The [local level comparisons](FIRST_PASS_LOCALIZED_LEVELS.md) apply to this same realization $\mathcal L$. The localized moved datum and its row-lift reading retain the fitted group, the original function on the family side and its geometric pins. Apply $\mathrm{RowsAt}(P)$ with the **localized** function; its premise is now exactly the comparison just imposed. It supplies the same normalized reading on this moved object and the extra row witness. With the all-index theta datum (every prescribed test is present for $1\le j\le m$ and either coefficient side) and $\mathrm{Large}$, these give the normalized core and extension. Thus row existence itself assumes just the length comparison; the installation of the extension uses the extra $\mathrm{Large}$ and full theta data. [Choice order and result][choose] · [Localized reading][move] · [All-index theta datum][all]

Let $c$ be the prescribed real depth. A list at $c$ is a grid at scale $(1,c,b)$, its value-field and roots-of-unity assertions, and the identification with the actual auxiliary character list. The [first-pass settings](FIRST_PASS_TWIST_SETTINGS.md) choose it at that $c$; the [moduli construction](FIRST_PASS_MODULI_POINTS.md) carries it forward. On localization carry the old marked places and characters and transport the Hermitian vertex spaces, orthogonal decompositions and lattices. The field of values, the roots and the actual list comparison are preserved under this identification. This is a map from each old grid witness to a new witness at **the same** $c$. The normalized core is chosen before $c$ in the transfer implication. One and the same localization and core therefore transport lists at every depth for which this $x$ has a list. This requires no second coefficient request and no eventual threshold depending on $c$ at the transfer step. The earlier field request used to *produce* an initial grid may depend on $c$. The resulting extension keeps $P.W_t$ and the specified Galois, twist, form, bridge, index, side, $Q,k,\psi$ comparisons. If $P$ is saturated, adjoining the actual witnesses $P,x,l$, the given list, saturation and these comparison maps provides the extension with origin. **From the extension to an export.** The extension just obtained is level three. A built core also includes the split-prime column, kernel, divisor, theta and object comparisons. On the pinned route the level-four construction uses the true volume and the transport of the normalized extension under the indicated stage change. The next construction uses the ambient, divisor and theta existence assertions. Both steps must retain the extension with origin. The export quantifies over *every* built core on the eventual untwisted first-pass range. On its actual coefficient datum it asks for a level $N_0(i)$, finite test families, good twists for every power coprime to the residue characteristic at every nonzero index, and both the Selmer hypotheses and the full-rank and basis clauses at each active index. [Levels and built condition][built] · [Use of level four and the three existence assertions][building] · [Export quantifiers][exportq] · [Per-column target][export]

One export input holds on the very row just completed. Given a compatible real and series realization of that row, at an active $v$ set $t=\mu(\operatorname{plEq}v)$ in its level. This **one tuple** realizes the requested base data for every $j$ and both sides. The off-support law puts $v$ in $P.W_t$. The level character at $t$ is the prescribed $\delta_{\ell}$ times the initial auxiliary character. Untwisted first-pass input makes the latter trivial. The normalized character thus agrees with the pinned $\delta$-character; the conductor support of $\delta_\ell$ is precisely the places above its distinguished prime. The prescribed half of each conjugate pair gives equality of the two sets $Q$. This proves $\mathrm{BaseTup}$ without supposing the active set empty. The pin then uses rationality and bounds for the series, the real-side Hecke supremum-lattice assertion, the field and Galois comparisons, and the Selmer and ray-character inputs to obtain the uniform export. The [indefinite-row Hecke calculation](FIRST_PASS_EXPORT_HECKE.md) supplies the supremum bound on its own lattice. [Tuple and conductor][basetup] · [Actual witness on the untwisted input][basereal] · [Inputs to export][exportsup] · [All coprime powers and row bases][exportproof]

For a seed on this **particular** origin, a finite restriction of the latter tests suffices. Choose one active representative in each class above a single prime of the fixed coefficient field, up to conjugation (the source relation $\mathrm{OverOnePair}$). Let $J$ be the image of their original twist indices. Use the representative's index on each active class and retain the old index elsewhere. The Galois property of the fixed coefficient subfield and the character tie transport the readings and give the required conjugation law. It suffices to have common $N_0,S$ on $J$; at each such index require the full-rank and basis condition and, for every finite avoidance set, a single twist satisfying the good-twist condition at **all** coprime powers. Enumerate $J$. At each step add all previously chosen supports to the current avoidance set. The twist at power one avoids this larger set, and the good-twist assertions for every power restrict to the original avoidance set. Extend all inactive auxiliary data by empty supports, exponent zero and trivial characters, using the previously supplied all-index theta datum. This constructs the seed for every requested avoidance function. If $P.W_t=\varnothing$, take the old index everywhere and these empty data at every index. A seed follows directly in this case, even before a global export theorem. For a nonempty $J$, the first-pass trivial auxiliary character does not supply these avoidance and all-powers assertions. On the uniform route through a built core, the resulting seed feeds the separate cohomological lifting and enlargement of the base with both the external and internal avoidance restrictions, after which the carried levels are rebuilt on the second column.

IP2. Independence in every characteristic by canonical leading terms and generic flag minors
Status: faithful_translation
Canonical path products have a unit leading component recovering the preceding endpoint. Their fixed-endpoint independence holds over every field. Jacobi converts generic terminal-index coefficients into determinant powers times standard flag-minor products with distinct diagonal leading monomials. A maximal-endpoint projection combines the two independence statements. No rational isotypic membership is asserted.

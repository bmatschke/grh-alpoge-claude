MATHEMATICAL CHECKS

MF1. The literal IsCMUnram condition supplies a unit theta-conjugate(theta) at both members of a split pair. The coefficient formulas for 1,theta give an integral basis and unit different, including dyadic primes. The fixed-field degree, trace discriminant, complex covolume 2^(-f)*sqrt(abs(disc E))=v^f, and real covolume sqrt(disc F) are distinct and correct. The inert E-prime has norm q^2; the two split half-power Euler factors combine to exactly (1-eta^m*q^(-m))^(-1). This matches StatementMass.eulerFac/lValEta without an extra square root.

MF2. Unit norm lifting uses surjectivity of the residue trace and the correction 1+pi^j*t; it does not divide by 2. Choosing a least-valuation pairing, then a suitable x+t*y if necessary, gives a diagonal entry with that valuation even at residue characteristic 2. The projection is integral, and unit norms remove diagonal units. The split case is Smith reduction on the two component modules. The fraction-field replacement of pi,pi by 1,1 uses N(t)=pi-1 and the norm-one vector (pi^(-1),pi^(-1)t); the complement has even determinant valuation. Thus the minimal determinant-valuation-at-most-one type and its fixed norm-one first vector are valid.

MF3. With the stated conjugate-linear-first convention, c=h(x-y,x) and c+bar(c)=h(x-y,x-y) make z -> z-(x-y)c^(-1)h(x-y,z) unitary and send x to y. If c=0, passing through -y gives denominator 2h(x,x), a nonzero field element; integral division by 2 is not asserted. The Cayley map and the rational scalar correction prove simultaneous weak approximation for the unitary group. Open lattice stabilizers then yield exactly the rational localized actions required by fibLoc, IsLocReps and the source genus, not only completed isometry classes.

MF4. At each finite place a binary unramified Hermitian form represents every scalar: a split norm is already onto; otherwise parity of coefficient valuations and unit norm surjectivity handle unequal parity, while equal parity gives a hyperbolic norm difference. The displayed difference-of-two-squares identity is used in the fraction field. The five-variable quadratic form is locally isotropic, with real signature (4,1). Applying the explicitly retained number-field Hasse–Minkowski theorem yields a global nonzero zero, whose final coordinate cannot vanish by positivity. This proves the literal HermRepUnram quantifiers with all three totally positive fixed elements retained.

MF5. The matrix count initially counts all endomorphisms. For k>d its determinant equation forces invertibility, in the split case by nonnegative valuations with zero sum. The derivative Z -> Z*H+HZ is surjective using trace-one diagonal coefficients and H^(-1), with bounded denominators even at 2. Local projection coordinates, a finite stabilizer cover, and compactness justify stabilization and positivity of the density. For basis change C, the ambient matrix conjugation has Jacobian one and the Hermitian-target change has Jacobian |N(det C)|^m. The change of d cancels this in nu=q^(-m*d)*alphaL. The orbit orientation is nu(A)*orb(B,A)=nu(B)*orb(A,B), exactly LocalDensityRatio after clearing its powers of q.

MF6. The finite residue norm counts, finite unitary/GL orders, and lifting powers q^(2m-1) and q^(m^2) agree. The primitive zero-residue contribution is e_m*(1+eta^m*q^(1-m)); the divisible-vector contribution has factor q^(2-2m). Therefore alphaN/e_m is the finite geometric sum through ord(n), as in the literal alphaN_closed. This also handles rank one and dyadic unramified places; uniform boundedness is used only for m>=2.

MF7. Trace perfection plus the adjugate identity bounds the derivative ideal at a primitive vector by pi^t with t<=d. Partition into balls modulo pi^(d+1), use one derivative coordinate for successive correction, and keep the other coordinates free. The scaling sum has factor q^(-(2m-2)r); for m>=2 the tail is uniformly summable, including the value zero. The unresolved finite-level ball contributes at most q^(k-2m*ceil(k/2))<=1. The conservative nonzero-n stabilization bound claimed in the text follows. Summing over norm residues counts all vectors once and gives mean one by the proved domination. The argument does not cancel a derivative at the singular zero vector.

MF8. The lattice sandwich B*x0+T subset Lambda subset n^(-1)B*x0+T-dual gives a finite actual fiber. Projection bounds the containing-index exponent ell by 2ord(n); determinant/index comparison gives ord(n)+d_T=d_L+ell. Hence finitely many Jordan types suffice for actual IsLocReps. The invariant vector measure has volume q^(-d_L) on L; its norm fiber has mass q^(-d_L)*alphaN. Identifying that fiber with U(W)\U(V), then regrouping K_L-orbits by the cut T, gives the precise reciprocal-stabilizer factor f_L(T)/nu(T). This proves LIRatio without dividing by a possibly zero representation density. Empty fibers give zero on both sides.

MF9. At norm one choose the minimal reference lattice with determinant valuation <=1. Every lattice of this local type containing x0 splits integrally along x0 and has the unique minimal complement type. The finite isometry group is transitive on norm-one vectors modulo pi^k: a vector lifts exactly because its derivative is surjective, and its complement has the required type. The stabilizer is the cut isometry group, since the unit first diagonal forces the first row and column. The identity m^2=(2m-1)+(m-1)^2 gives alphaL=alphaN(1)*alphaT and pins kappa=1. On the h0-integral reference lattice, scaling by n multiplies alphaL by q^(s^2*ord(n)) and alphaN by q^ord(n), leaving lambda unchanged; hence kappa=q^((m-1)*ord(n)). Rational approximation and a global integer unit at the chosen prime clear all other denominators while keeping x0 fixed. This supplies LIPin and all literal LIStat clauses, including the rank-one/zero-dimensional-cut case.

MF10. A Hermitian line and fractional ideal lattice always have rational group E^1 and the same local norm-one unit stabilizers. Relative genus ideals have zero inert exponents and opposite split exponents; MF3 supplies the source's rational local description. Thus genus classes and their finite automorphism groups are independent of the line scale and ideal. IG9 supplies finite positive mass. The rank-one congruence count is q^d*(1-eta/q), so every local lambda is one. The exact MassConst definition contains mass=c*lamG, and no extra determinant factor has been omitted.

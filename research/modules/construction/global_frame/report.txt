Scope
=====
This component reconstructs the global-frame algebra left open in LI5 and
the first four priority-one targets in v0.12. It gives a single matrix B
over the original host CM field, not unrelated local bases. It proves the
actual companion-lattice identity from the source's literal base lattice
and carrier identities. No Lean source is changed and no new kernel replay
or full build is asserted.

The supplied-data boundary is essential. We work with the same true-column
record I, its Ok/Pins/Laws/Later data, and the forms F, Hermitian columns H
and level-prime data A satisfying the source's StdF/StdLvC/CarH/CarT
conditions. Their full existence is not proved here. In particular we do
not infer all these structures just because their lattice components have
an elementary form. The source supplies the entire structures through
colsTiedSym_std and its imports. We reconstruct the specific matrix and
companion consequences, which are then available with no further global
orthonormalization or patching assumption.

Notation and convention
=======================
Let E be the host CM field, O its ring of integers, F the maximal real
subfield, and conjugation the host involution. In a specified coordinate
map c:V -> E^n write h_J(x,y)=conjugate(x)^t J y. Thus conjugation is on the
first variable and the Hermitian dual is

  L# = { y : h_J(x,y) is in O for every x in L }.

This is an O-submodule, since h_J is O-linear in y. Accordingly the explicit
O-span of dualSet used by the source is precisely L#. This dual uses
E-valued integrality. It is not a trace dual; no inverse different is being
silently introduced. The host involution preserves O because it preserves
monic equations over Z.

SF1. A global orthonormal integral basis gives the required matrix
================================================================
Assume b_1,...,b_n is an E-basis with h(b_i,b_j)=delta_ij and L0=O-span(b).
Let B be the matrix with columns c(b_i). It is invertible because c maps
a basis to a basis. Its conjugate-Gram identity is entrywise:

  (B* J B)_ij = h_J(c(b_i),c(b_j)) = h(b_i,b_j) = delta_ij.

If v=sum_i a_i b_i, then c(v)=B a, so a=B^-1 c(v). Consequently

  c(L0)=B O^n,    x in c(L0) iff every coordinate of B^-1 x is integral.

This proves the literal StdB predicate. The source's coordinate space
uses N(0), while B is indexed by R.k. The given equality N(0)=R.k induces
one permutation/reindexing. Applying it to both indices of the matrix
and to vector indices transports B, B^-1, the Gram identity and the
integrality statement. No lattice denominator or extra arithmetic choice
is produced by this reindexing.

Source: TieStd.stdB_core, hermitian_form_frame_coordinates, exists_stdB,
exists_stdB_of_tied; ComparisonMaps/TiedRec.StdB.

SF2. The actual positive spaces and their coordinate assertions
==============================================================
I.Ok.spaces is the DiagSpaces condition. It explicitly identifies X_i
with Examples2.posSpace in rank N(i), and X' with the positive space in
rank N(m)-1. These spaces have form sum conjugate(x_j)y_j, canonical
coordinate frame, and standard integral lattice. Direct evaluation on
coordinate vectors gives all Gram entries. The lattice statement follows
by expressing a vector in the same basis. Therefore StdTied, gramN=1,
gramP=1 and the coordinatewise integer criterion are proved for I.rec7.

One can use B=1 at level zero. This is stronger than needing an unspecified
global change of basis, but it does not identify the independently chosen
local bases from LI5 with 1. Those local bases must still be replaced;
SF5--SF6 make that change and check the exact lattice axiom.

The Pins.iX field sends corresponding initial coordinate vectors to one
another. Expanding an arbitrary vector in the basis proves x -> (x,0).
For each representative class c, latAt is defined as inverse coordinate
image of the original representative's lattice. Forward transport cancels
the inverse, proving literal equality with that lattice. The relevant
frame set and determinant functions use the identity equivalence. Thus
the result does not discard decorations, change representatives or replace
a class lattice by an isomorphic unrelated lattice.

Only the spaces part of Ok and the stated Pins field enter these elementary
calculations, but the full source theorem is quantified over the supplied
Laws and Later records so that I.rec7 is defined. Their existence remains
part of the global-construction boundary.

SF3. Exact diagonal dual and companion
=====================================
For n>=1 and nonzero pi in O, take

  L_pi = O e_1 + ... + O e_(n-1) + pi O e_n.

For y in L_pi#, evaluation on e_i (i<n) gives y_i in O, and evaluation
on pi e_n gives conjugate(pi)y_n in O. Conversely these conditions imply
integrality of the pairing with every vector of L_pi. Hence

  L_pi# = O^(n-1) direct-sum conjugate(pi)^-1 O,
  L_pi + conjugate(pi)L_pi# = O^n.

The last equality can be proved by a literal decomposition, so does not
depend on a dimension or index calculation: for x integral, write

  x = (x_1,...,x_(n-1),0) + conjugate(pi)(0,...,0,x_n/conjugate(pi)).

The first vector belongs to L_pi, the second vector before multiplication
belongs to L_pi#. Conversely both full summands are integral. When n=0
all the relevant modules are zero. When n>0 the actual norm relation
pi conjugate(pi)=T, with T prime, guarantees pi nonzero. There is no step
assuming a prime generator is a unit.

The same coordinate formulas prove L_pi subset O^n subset L_pi# and
T L_pi# subset L_pi. At bad primes dividing T the first containment can
remain strict after localization; the frame replacement is asserted only
where p does not divide T.

=======================================
The following is the complete lattice-level join in the code.

  F_u's level-zero lattice     StdF.ends -> Reads.lb

Every rank reindexing preserves the ordinal of a coordinate. In particular
the condition j.val+1=rank remains exactly the last-coordinate condition;
it is not a cyclic rotation or an arbitrary permutation of the distinguished
coordinate. Thus the inherited lattice is precisely L_(pi_u), not just a
finite-index sublattice with the same covolume.

Applying SF3 at zero and at every higher chart proves the source's
SymmetricBaseCompatibility. This is the chart-indexed version of BaseTied;
the two predicate names should not be conflated. Their level-zero clauses
are the same. Choosing the integral ideal a_u=(conjugate(pi_u)) yields
the actual Companion assertion for H.lev0:

  L_u + a_u L_u# = L0.

The choice of ideal is legal because conjugation preserves algebraic
integrality. The source's companion_of_baseTied and companion_of_colsHermS
make exactly this choice. Higher-level chart identities are also proved,
although the common-frame application needs only the zero-level clause.

This proof uses only the lattice/generator fields of supplied standard
forms and carriers. It does not use the residual functionals or their
uniformizer-annihilation condition h_pi. The latter is needed elsewhere
in herm_rest_tied to construct the simultaneous full level-zero package.
We do not treat that larger theorem as proved merely by proving its
SymmetricBaseCompatibility output.

SF5. The literal projected localized lattice
============================================
Fix a split place v of F and the LI splitting

  phi:E tensor_F K -> K x K,  K=F_v,
  phi(1 tensor a)=(a,a),  j(x)=first(phi(x tensor 1)).

Let R be the completed integer ring. An element of O is integral over Z,
so j(O) is in R, since R is integrally closed. The integral tensor subring
intV is generated by O tensor 1 and 1 tensor R; its first projection lies
in R, and it contains every diagonal scalar 1 tensor r. Hence

  first(locL_v L) = R-span j(L).

This restates the exact LI5 equality for the same splitting. It can also
be verified directly by induction on span: an integral tensor scalar
projects into R, and every R-linear combination lifts using diagonal
tensor scalars. No completion/density argument is needed here.

Now suppose L subset B O^n, N B e_i in L for all i, and N is a unit in R.
Forward inclusion into j(B)R^n follows from integrality of B^-1 L.
For t with u=j(B)^-1 t integral, the finite sum

  sum_i [1 tensor (u_i/N)] * [image of N B e_i]

belongs to locL_v L and has first projection t. Its coefficients are in
the allowed integral tensor subring because N^-1 is in R. This is a
witness for the existential membership predicate in LocId.fr_lat, not
just a cardinality argument. It proves the full biconditional.

SF6. Simultaneous replacement by base changes of B
==================================================
For a general supplied ColLevel, Lb_int gives L subset L#, and Lb_dual
gives T L# subset L. The latter condition is stated on the dual set and
extends to its O-span by sums and scalar multiplication. Since a_u is
an integral ideal, a_u L# subset L#. Therefore the companion identity
gives the sandwich

  L subset L0=B O^n subset L#,   T B e_i in L.

If p does not divide T, Bezout aT+bp=1 shows T is a unit at every v|p.
Use SF5 with N=T. Retain the prior local identification's completion,
splitting, field isomorphism, lattice and prime labels. Change only fr
to j_v(B). The only affected structure axiom is fr_lat, just proved.

In particular iw is unchanged by definition. All equations in LI4 and
LI6 use iw, the prescribed coefficient embedding, the fixed Frobenius
orbit representatives, and the same splitting/completion; none depends
on the discarded arbitrary frame. Their c^s parity and valuation
normalization therefore persist without another choice. This is exactly
LocId.withFr, locIdStd, locIdStd_fr and locIdStd_iw.

The B in this argument depends only on the one fixed global reference
space and lattice. It does not depend on u, the auxiliary rational prime
p, a coefficient place above p, a real-subfield place v, or a Frobenius
position. No assertion is made that B is uniform across different choices
of the entire arithmetic host/parameter construction.

SF7. Endpoint and remaining inputs
==================================
For the supplied true-column data specified above we have simultaneously:

  (1) StdTied, and a common B satisfying StdB;
  (2) the actual Companion identity for each word's level-zero lattice;
  (3) local identifications with fr=j_v(B), retaining all LI identities.

The literal positive-coordinate specialization may take B=1. For a fixed
noncanonical coordinate map the basis-matrix construction in SF1 gives
the same conclusion with its appropriate B.

This supplies the frame part required by CmpTied, but CmpTied also contains
the comparison-map/representation identity. That latter identity is not
proved by this component. The separate class-rank and comparison-span
components must supply their own maps and hypotheses.

Remaining supplier: ExistsStandardColumnComparisonsTrueColumns.colsTiedSym_std
constructs standard forms F, Hermitian columns H, level-prime data A, their
CarH/CarT identifications, level-zero residue functionals, additive
characters, and all simultaneous compatibility. Its proof calls the
standard-form, Hermitian-column and level-prime constructions. We have
reconstructed the positive-space and actual companion outputs but have
not replaced those entire constructions by self-contained proofs.

Accordingly a continuation should either reconstruct those remaining
standard-column constructors or clearly use them as existing source
inputs. It must not relabel them as consequences of SF1--SF7. The original
host/point/parameter, theta/spectral, metric, cohomological and prime-window
branches retain their precise prior status.

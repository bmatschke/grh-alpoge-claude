{\Large\bfseries Patching a chain and descending to the marked field}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.70 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The marked theorem asks for a single mark that controls every eligible
point. On the patching side we choose detecting elements for a whole
chain, prove both arithmetic bounds, and follow primes across the chain.
The outstanding local comparison is concentrated at genuine two-block
vertices. On the automorphic side we descend the prescribed representation
to the original CM field and retain the common center of its strict local
bounds. The final chapter applies both arguments to the mark.

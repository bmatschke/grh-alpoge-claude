{\Large\bfseries From arithmetic ideals to marked points}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.68 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The marked theorem uses a rank-two and a rank-three deformation
problem on one frame. In each rank, arithmetic information is
expressed in a ring of determinants, while component propagation
takes place in a ring of matrix entries. This edition constructs
the map between the two rings, transports the local prime and seed
conditions, and returns the resulting arithmetic points to the
original frame. A second choice of auxiliary prime then removes the
excluded place from the automorphy assertion.

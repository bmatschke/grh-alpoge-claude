\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries The denominator estimate on a common family}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.83 \quad 25 November 2026
\end{center}

All fifteen denominator conditions now hold for a single hub on
each sufficiently late member of a family satisfying the standing
arithmetic hypotheses. The last condition concerns how much
integrality can be lost under a surjection of crystalline lattices
at a middle-range prime. We treat the short arcs of Hodge weights
at an inert prime, including the two possible orders of signs on
three letters. The assembly chapter combines the local estimates
and obtains the denominator sum with its original rank and
Tate--Shafarevich term. A further chapter proves that the four compatibility conditions
pass to every Hecke cut. Establishing the remaining data on the
families selected by the global design is an open obligation.

Two supplementary arguments give bounds using the distribution of
thick positions and the total contribution of exceptional primes.
They can also be read independently of the three-letter proofs.
The closing chapters treat the rational elements in the counting
kernel, including the exact diagonal count in a definite row.
The companion guide compares six external reconstructions with
the ten open obligations shared by RH and Dirichlet GRH.

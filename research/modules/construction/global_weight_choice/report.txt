RESULT AND SCOPE
---------------
This component supplies actual MeanKappaBound, MeanEx and
WeightVectorExists for the family data satisfying the explicit GI/GII
conditions. Its last step supplies ChosenW for that family. If the
additional CoverAll property is supplied, the selected pair is realized
by an actual family member and satisfies ChosenWS. We do not assert
existence of a full arithmetic family with CoverAll or simultaneous
realization of its selected tops and all other global structures.

The proof is a human reconstruction of the exact finite averages in
SlopeEstimateOfLemmaBSlopeLine.lean and the family transport in
PowFourLtCeilThresholdPrime.lean/LossRequirementsOfSmallShares.lean.
It simplifies the total-loss budget: the harmonic bound already proved
inside the source is sufficient, so no prime number theorem or sharper
Mertens estimate is needed. No independence between different primes
is assumed. We have not changed or compiled the Lean code.

Throughout 0<eps<1/52 is fixed; all coefficient constants and rG(eps)
are fixed before x and before the family index. Set
  delta=x^(-eps/8), a=x exp(x^eps), X'=exp(x^(eps/3)),
  b'=a/X', p0=k^2 x^26, Y=x^13, Lambda=K.La N1^2 log(a),
  Nstar=2n delta.
The global chain length m_ch=D.R.m is different from the local
highest-weight parameter m_local=k-1. The selected subset is TT.
The canonical deficit is the universal infimum of GJ1.

GW1. EVENTUAL SMALL COEFFICIENTS AND NONEMPTY INTEGER BOXES
---------------------------------------------------------
From the literal SizesAt fields SizeN, SizeM, SizeK and SizeTT,
  x^(2+8eps-eps/100) <= n <= x^(2+8eps+eps/100),
  m_ch <= x^(eps/100) sqrt(x delta) <= x^(1/2+eps/100),
  k <= x^(9/2+10eps+eps/100) <= x^6,
  |TT| <= 2n delta <= 2x^(2+8eps+eps/100).
The inequality k<=x^6 uses eps<1/52 and x>=1. GII2 gives
N1<=Ce log x with Ce independent of the family index.

For any fixed nonnegative K.kII and fixed Cm=302114, enlarge the
threshold so that
  K.kII rG^2 <= x^8,   Cm Ce log x <= x.
Then
  K.kII rG^2 k x^(-26) <= x^(-12),
  Cm N1/x^13 <= x^(-12).                               (GW1.1)
These are the first two fields of the source's NumMean, proved without
requiring its other fields. GI5 already supplies the period and L2
conditions needed below. The elementary log/power comparison can be
made explicit by GJ2's fourth-term exponential bound, with the
constant enlarged to at least one and log x>=1.

Define the literal windows
  Ia={ceil(a),...,floor(a+a/(k x^13))},
  Ib={ceil(b'),...,floor(b'+Nstar x^eps Lambda b'/k)}.
They are eventually nonempty. To check every bound, first
  Nstar>=2x^(2+(8-1/100-1/8)eps)>=1.
GJ2 with eta=eps/48 gives Lambda>=x^(47eps/48)>=1.
Also a>=x^19 eventually, since x^eps>=18 log x eventually.
Hence a/(k x^13)>=1. Finally
  log b'=log x+x^eps-x^(eps/3)>=6 log x
eventually: first impose x^(eps/3)<=x^eps/2, then impose
x^eps/2>=5 log x. Thus b'>=x^6>=k and the Ib width is at least one.
If v-u>=1, ceil(u)<=u+1<=v, so ceil(u)<=floor(v).
This proves nonemptiness with the exact integer rounding.

GW2. ACTUAL PER-ORBIT MEAN DEFICITS
----------------------------------
Let p be a candidate prime and let v be a good representative.
The candidate definition includes primality, p<X', residue class zero,
orbit period n'>=x^(eps/4), and, for p>=p0, exclusion from the band
of width rhoB. GJ3 identifies Nv with the number of selected cyclic
positions T. Let E_local denote uniform averaging over the two
literal windows on T, with degree zero at positions outside T.

If p<p0, the source's deficit is
  kappa_v=min(1,K.kII Nv rG^2 k x^(-26)).
It is nonnegative, independent of the degrees, and <=Nv x^(-12)
by (GW1.1). This is the pointwise small-prime clause of
MeanKappaBound, and gives its local-mean clause as well.

If p>=p0, the deficit is X.kapI(p,n',T,a,b). It is nonnegative for
all degree functions, whether or not they lie in the boxes, by GJ1.
GI5 supplies the exact fourteen numerical hypotheses, the good-period
and L2 bounds, and exclusion from the inner band. GI8 gives
  E_local kappa_v <= Cm Nv N1/x^13 <= Nv x^(-12).        (GW2.1)
The inner-band exclusion follows from rho<=rhoB, so the actual
candidate condition is sufficient. The canonical average is justified
by its universal infimum definition, not by making an independent
choice of a certificate at each weight. This proves both clauses of
the literal G.MeanKappaBound = AuxBlock8.Conclusion2.

GW3. EXACT GLOBAL MARGINALIZATION
--------------------------------
Let Ba be the functions TT->Ia, extended by zero to A\TT; let Bb
be the analogous functions into Ib. Both are finite nonempty by GW1.
Use the uniform measure on B=Ba x Bb. For a fixed orbit v, the map
  phi_v:Fin(n')->A, i |-> t0+i hP
is injective by GJ3 and identifies T with the corresponding subset of
TT. Each prescribed local a-function has exactly |Ia|^(|TT|-Nv)
global extensions: its values on the complement in TT are arbitrary.
Each local b-function has exactly |Ib|^(|TT|-Nv) extensions. Hence
restriction sends the uniform global measure to the uniform local
measure, and
  E_B kappa_v = E_local kappa_v.
This is an equality of finite sums divided by their actual cardinalities;
there is no limiting argument or assertion of independence of orbits
belonging to different primes.

For a candidate prime put
  Sigma_p(a,b)=m_ch sum_(v good) kappa_v(a,b).
The good-representative fibres are disjoint subsets of TT, so
sum_(v good) Nv<=|TT| (GJ3). Linearity of finite averaging gives
  Sigma_p>=0,
  E_B Sigma_p<=m_ch |TT| x^(-12).                      (GW3.1)
Nonnegativity in fact holds for all primes and all degree functions:
each small-prime formula is nonnegative, and each large-prime canonical
infimum is nonnegative. The stronger universal statement is compatible
with the source's MeanEx and family transport.

GW4. SUM THE EXCEPTIONAL LOSSES OVER ALL CANDIDATE PRIMES
-------------------------------------------------------
Put t=x^(eps/3)>=1, X'=exp(t), M=ceil(X')-1>=1. Since every
candidate prime p lies below X', and log p<=t,
  sum_(p candidate) (log p)/p
    <= t sum_(j=1)^M 1/j
    <= t(1+log M) <= t(1+t) <=2t^2.                    (GW4.1)
For M>=1 the harmonic bound follows by integrating 1/u on each
[j-1,j], j>=2. M<X' holds both when X' is an integer and otherwise.
Thus no sharper prime estimate is needed. The source proves t(1+t)
as an intermediate bound, then weakens it to t(2+t); we retain the
intermediate inequality.

For every s>=0 and theta=x^(-eps),
  1_{s>theta}<=x^eps s.
If s<=theta this is 0<=x^eps s; otherwise x^eps s>1. Define
  Z(a,b)=sum_(p candidate) (log p)/p
            [1_{Sigma_p(a,b)>x^(-eps)}+Sigma_p(a,b)].
Combine this pointwise inequality, (GW3.1), and (GW4.1):
  E_B Z <= (x^eps+1) m_ch |TT| x^(-12) *2x^(2eps/3)
        <=8x^(-19/2+(1453/150)eps).                    (GW4.2)
For clarity the exponent is
  eps + (1/2+eps/100) + (2+8eps+eps/100)
       -12 + 2eps/3
  =-19/2+(1453/150)eps.
Set gamma=1/2-(1453/150)eps. Since eps<1/52,
  gamma>1/2-1453/7800=2447/7800>0.
Therefore E_B Z<=8x^(-9-gamma). Every bound is uniform in the
family index, though its threshold may depend on eps and the fixed
coefficient constants.

GW5. SELECT ONE PAIR, WITH THE LITERAL WReq COEFFICIENTS
------------------------------------------------------
Enlarge the threshold to impose 32x^(-gamma)<=1. The finite nonempty
box has E_B Z<=x^(-9)/4. Hence some (a,b) in B has
Z(a,b)<=x^(-9)/4: if every value were larger, so would its average.
At that same pair,
  sum_(p candidate) (log p)/p
       [2*1_{Sigma_p>x^(-eps)}+4Sigma_p]
    <=4Z<=x^(-9).                                    (GW5.1)
This is exactly AuxBlock8.WReq, with the source's 2 and 4 coefficients,
not just a bound of the right order. The pair lies in the exact two
global boxes, giving WeightVectorExists. Nonnegativity together with
Z<=x^(-9)/4 gives the literal MeanEx statement as well.

Nonnegativity permits dropping summands, so the same pair satisfies
  sum_(p candidate, Sigma_p>x^(-eps)) (log p)/p<=x^(-9)/2,
  sum_(p candidate) (log p)/p Sigma_p<=x^(-9)/4.
It need not satisfy Sigma_p<=x^(-eps) at EVERY prime. The selected
pair controls the total weighted exceptional set. For primes passing
that test, the actual definition VV=goodReps applies. No abundance
of candidate primes is claimed by this selection argument.

GW6. TRANSPORT TO THE FAMILY, AND THE EXACT COVERAGE BOUNDARY
-----------------------------------------------------------
For x in Fm.Win above the common threshold, Ix_win gives a member i0.
Apply GW5 to i0. Its frame's value map identifies TT bijectively
with the common finite set TTn(x). Transfer the selected functions
to integer labels av,bv:N->Z, assigning zero off TTn(x).

The family's bTop_eq is
  bTop=b'+2n delta x^eps LaG b'/k.
ParametersFill equates LaG with Lambda. To read this equality from
the quantified fills field choose any finite place, for example a
place above 2: a maximal ideal over 2 exists in the integer ring of
the number field by integrality/lying over. This is the short source
proof pg_LaG. Thus winB=winBz exactly, and winA=winAz by definition.
The selected integer functions satisfy all the family degree-window
conditions, including b<=bTop. They define the source's Wt vector.

We prove the universal clause in ChosenW explicitly. Let i be any
member carrying those same integer functions. The equality R_eq
identifies the finite additive frames, preserving addition and value
labels. Under it, TT_eq identifies the selected sets; q_eq,d_eq,QS_eq
identify the prime class map; hP_eq identifies the Frobenius steps;
L_x,L_eps,L_J identify the scale; and bs_eq identifies the orbit
selectors. Consequently their candidate conditions, orbit periods,
representatives, and good-position sets coincide. In particular the
finite candidate sets are equal, not merely comparable in cardinality.

On TT the carried degrees agree by hypothesis, and outside TT both
aL,bL and av,bv have value zero. Their wPos pullbacks therefore agree
at every cyclic position. For small primes, the formula for kapIIf
depends only on the shared scale, rank, constants and selected count,
so the deficits agree. For large primes the family field kapI_eq
identifies the canonical deficit functions on the common n',T,a,b.
Thus the deficits agree term by term on good representatives, and
  Sigma_p(i,actual aL,bL)=Sigma_p(i0,selected a,b).
The same argument is the Sigma component of the source's sameServed
transport; the extra off and ep equalities used by that larger package
are not needed for Sigma alone. WReq transfers unchanged. This proves
ChosenW from GW1–GW5 and the actual FamP equalities, without importing
all the unrelated conclusions in LinkG.

ChosenW itself asserts an admissible pair and the property of EVERY
member carrying it. It does not assert that a carrier exists. The
additional property Fm.CoverAll says that every admissible pair can
be carried by some member, at any admissible distinguished position.
Applying it to av,bv yields a carrier and hence ChosenWS. Once one
carrier exists, the FamCore field coverS (or coverS_carry) can move
the distinguished position while preserving these degrees.

CoverAll is NOT a field of FamCore or FamP. FamCore contains only
coverS; full coverage is defined separately. We therefore retain
construction of a family satisfying CoverAll as an explicit remaining
global obligation. This component closes the averaging and transport,
not that existence claim and not a complete RH/GRH proof. Moreover,
CoverAll is only an optional sufficient route. The active construction
preselects one pair at the host stage and rebuilds its points at all
distinguished positions before forming the final family. HW1–HW3
reconstruct that transport, retaining the actual upstream host and
point-construction hypotheses. The full proof is not being required
to establish the stronger, optional CoverAll property.

1. Result of this milestone
===========================

The odd-minus cohomological branch now has a complete readable supplier.
OS1–OS9 prove the literal original-field PsiBound. OB1–OB8 turn that
bound, together with the finite Jacobi ideal identity proved in OS1,
into literal ThmBcFree with one universal numerical constant 240.
LD1–LD4 supply the finite reciprocity and duality specializations used
in the argument. The remaining inputs of this completed branch are
named established classical theorems, with their required forms and
transfers written in the component chapters.

This is a change in the dependency frontier: PsiBound and ThmBcFree
can now be removed from the list of bespoke human-proof obligations.
They have not merely been restated as intermediate assumptions. The
finite Jacobi, derivative, reciprocity, valuation, eigenspace, integral
annihilator and prime-summation steps between them are all included.

The congruence chapter LN1–LN10 closes a different complete implication:
explicit local denominator estimates on a compatible actual family
give the original-family LinkN bound. It proves the algebraic cut,
coordinate-ideal estimate, multiplicity accounting, prime summation
and uniform transport back to the original family. The actual local
Galois estimates and the required type/theta/spectral identities remain
inputs. Consequently unconditional actual-family LinkN is not marked
closed.

The whole human proof of RH and Dirichlet GRH remains unfinished. This
audit identifies the remaining cuts without interpreting claim counts,
page counts or source-link counts as proof-completion percentages.

2. What the map means
=====================

obligation_map.json is a finite sufficient-condition map. An AND gate
requires all its children. Each alternative at an OR gate is a full
sufficient route, with its own hypotheses. Related-node annotations
expose a deeper supplier but are not additional logical premises.
The map does not assert necessity, a minimal cut set, or an exhaustive
transitive Lean dependency graph.

Every v0.23 node ID and its original mathematical meaning is preserved.
When a node's status or immediate boundary changes, its previous
record is retained in its history. previous_obligation_map.json is an
unchanged content copy of the earlier map. Thus ODD_MINUS_BOUND still
means the actual odd-minus bound; it has not been silently redefined
to mean an implication with PsiBound assumed. Similarly C_LINK_N still
means LinkN for the actual family; its open suppliers remain visible.

The map distinguishes three materially different accomplishments:

  * A proved local theorem, such as HP1–HP4 on highest weights and
    polynomial representations, with its original-interface matching
    in HP5–HP6.
  * A complete supplier relative to precisely matched classical
    theorem leaves, now including OS9 and OB8.
  * A complete conditional chain, such as LN10, VW3/VW4, WB1–WB8 and
    DA1–DA2, whose actual arithmetic or geometric premises still have
    to be supplied on the same objects.

The first kind must still be instantiated on the actual coefficient
space, row and lattice. The third kind does not establish existence
of its input data merely because the implication is proved.

3. The now-complete odd-minus dependency chain
==============================================

OS1 constructs the finite Jacobi elements and proves their ideal
exponents, norm identities and congruence laws. OS2 removes the root
of unity ambiguity to give the required exact system. OS3 constructs
the derivative classes and proves the local recurrence and initial
value. OS4 chooses compatible Frobenius conditions in one finite
compositum. OS5 uses the exact local unramified orthogonality and
global reciprocity supplied by LD2/LD4 to prove the reciprocity
comparison. OS6 performs the finite-length induction without assuming
the Selmer module finite in advance. OS7 supplies the Bernoulli
evaluation and its exact denominator, and OS8 handles normalized
exhaustion and the residual cyclotomic Kummer case. OS9 descends to
the literal PsiBound on the original field and coefficient ring.

The output preserves E, its given algebraic closure Omega, the scalar
towers, the actual cyclotomic action rho, coefficient DVR O, character
psi and exact negative cyclotomic twist. It is universal in the stated
algebraic value a. If a=0, its valuation is infinite; the PsiBound
argument does not import a separate nonvanishing hypothesis.

OB2 independently proves the integral nonzero Bernoulli values needed
for the quantitative application, where k=e+1>=2. OB3 applies OS9
after the written same-field base change and character decomposition,
then cancels the ramification factor in the length comparison. This
does not feed OB2's nonvanishing back into OS9. The combined dependency
graph is acyclic at this potentially delicate join.

The exceptional-prime branch uses the explicit Jacobi principal-ideal
formula from OS1. OB5 writes the exact group-ring identity, with its
inverse Galois action and sign, and cancels 2 on the odd-primary
character values. Therefore a generic Stickelberger theorem is no
longer an additional citation leaf. OB6 obtains the annihilating-power
constant 30; OB4 obtains the generator constant 8. OB7 uses length
at most exponent times number of generators to obtain 30*8=240.
OB8 performs the full finite prime and twist sum.

For every original field E in the stated IsCompField family, every
original closure and local-data family, every finite odd-prime set S,
and the indicated twist cutoff t, the endpoint is

  sum_(l in S) log(l) sum_(1<=e<t) minusLen(E,l,e)
  <= 3 [E:Q] t^2 log(t condF(E))
     + 240 sum_(odd prime l | M.deg)
         l [E:Q]^2 t^2 log(t l condF(E))^2.

It also supplies finiteness of the individual lengths. The exceptional
primes divide the fixed base degree M.deg, not an uncontrolled varying
degree. The constant 240 is independent of all these choices.

This is exactly the ThmBcFree input of VW4 and hence the odd-minus
summand used in VW1. The unused two-primary extension of the source
StickBound statement and the k=1 nonvanishing generalization are not
claimed. Neither is required by this completed endpoint.

4. Classical leaves and exact duality scope
===========================================

The finite arithmetic leaves used by OS are finite cyclotomic and
local-field facts, finite Chebotarev, Hilbert class-field reciprocity,
and Kummer theory/Hilbert 90. The OB leaves are Hilbert class-field
reciprocity, Kummer and S-unit theory, Minkowski together with the
conductor-discriminant formula, von Staudt–Clausen, and finite local
field structure. Their registry entries include the required forms
and primary references; their quantitative consequences and transfers
used here are proved in the chapters.

LD retains finite local Tate duality, local class-field theory with
the chosen Frobenius convention, and finite-module Poitou–Tate
duality. LD proves the exact UnramifiedOrthogonality interface and
matches GlobalTheory.Published.PoitouTateDuality. Its coefficients
are arbitrary finite modules of the stated ring, not assumed free.
The invariant family is fixed before the finite sets, places, modules
and actions. The arithmetic Frobenius and cup-order sign conventions,
conjugation of roots of unity, and archimedean modifications are
spelled out.

This does not certify every other declaration called local duality
or local finiteness elsewhere in the repository. In particular the
literal LocalFinitenessK and LocalTateDualityK interfaces remain to
be matched for each finite extension K of Q_l inside PadicAlgCl(l),
the actual cycK representation, the specified cup maps in both H1
directions, and all finite nonfree coefficients. LocalEulerCharK has
the residue-characteristic factor |M|^[K:Q_l]. LD1's tame argument
away from the residue characteristic does not supply that p-adic
factor. These are classical-interface matching tasks, distinct from
the substantial bespoke geometric and quantitative suppliers below.

The older PairEx branch still retains its named quadratic analytic
class-number, Bombieri density with exceptional-zero refinement, and
Heath-Brown hybrid density inputs. The restricted real-row route
still retains its precise Harish-Chandra formal-degree and unitary
Tamagawa inputs and the actual Haar/volume ties. None has been
removed just because this increment addresses another branch.

5. The narrowed cohomological cut
=================================

VW1–VW4 already give the complete compatible assembly

  ThmBab + PointHV + ThmBcFree
      -> JointWin + ThmBc -> literal VWinAt.

With OB8 now supplying ThmBcFree, the two remaining bespoke supplier
interfaces on this route are ThmBab and PointHV. This is a statement
about this branch, not about all remaining obligations of RH.

ThmBab must use one BConst K for every Ct>=1 and must provide a common
window for the two-primary and odd reference-layer bounds. Separate
windows selected independently do not suffice. The source route
thmBab_of_lemmaK retains literal LemmaK, LocalFinitenessK,
LocalTateDualityK and Published.FerreroWashington. LD3 supplies its
Poitou–Tate premise. The tower argument, finite exceptional sets,
common-window selection and exact field/layer transfers still need
their readable chain.

The shared LemmaK is a uniform finiteness and quantitative bound for
H2 of the stated positive Tate twist on every original totally complex
field containing the required roots of unity. Its exposed source route
uses KBridge and a quantitative torsion bound for arithmetic-group
homology HGL L (4e+4) (2e). KBridge requires a finite Z_l module
surjecting onto the actual H2, with a comparison into the specified
tensor of homology and a controlled kernel exponent. A source file
whose title mentions KBridge and published K-theory inputs does not
by itself furnish this bridge: the inspected file defines a package
of K-theoretic assumptions. Similarly the short torsMain declaration
does not reconstruct its underlying geometric/combinatorial bound.

The exact Ferrero–Washington interface is a uniform bound on the
l-rank of class groups in the stated abelian cyclotomic layers,
including l=2 with the specified roots of unity. A citation to mu=0
still needs the finite-layer translation and control to this interface.

PointHV requires constants and auxiliary reference primes chosen
before the varying hosts and a bound for every fixed reference-layer
function j. Its source route retains LemmaK and CountI, CountII,
CountIII, CountV and CountZ, in addition to the local-K interfaces.
Those counts contain actual Galois/automorphy and vanishing content.
For example the exposed CountI route retains rigidity from Kato-type
inputs, adjoint vanishing, a rank-three congruence bound and automorphy
with unramified coefficients. Calling these predicates counts does
not make them elementary enumeration tasks.

Once both suppliers are reconstructed, VW and QP22 retain a single
pair q,d throughout each full real interval. Constants and thresholds
are chosen in the required order; the page or tower exponent may
vary with the real scale. The original conductor and field are retained,
including the 401-to-400 rescaling margin. This will close a meaningful
cohomological-window chain even though the global family/gain chain
will still require its own suppliers. The two-primary, odd-plus and
odd-minus summands are evaluated on the same original field E. The
reference cyclotomic layers have their own separately quantified
field models; they need not equal E. Their bounds are transported by
the stated layer comparisons on that shared window and fixed pair.

6. What the congruence chain closes, and what it still needs
===========================================================

LN proves integral central lifts in the original coefficient ring,
preserving the source's ordered-root-vector sum and its multiplicities.
It does not assume that this sum is an idempotent. From the exact type
action it proves all five Good-cut fields. Actual rational spectral
spanning then supplies the free cut lattice and its eigenspaces.
The coordinate-ideal filtration bounds the congruence module directly,
and the cut-kernel argument proves the loss on restoring the original
module.

After exact place weights and multiplicities are accounted for, the
global restoration cost is d_j log|G|. LN's numerical analysis proves
the full source prime budget, including sparse small-order inert
primes, band estimates, small primes and the exceptional tail. Thus
KudlaFamilyStatement is no longer a separate numerical black box.

The final bound is on the original family's etaSum and original
vvTerm series. The coefficient on that series remains exactly d_j;
the cut restoration does not enlarge it. Its quantifiers are

  for each fixed actual Fm, there exist K,x0 such that
  for every x>=x0 and every i in Fm.Ix(x),
  ShaFinOdd(D_xi) implies, for each allowed j,
  Summable(vvTerm(D_xi)) implies the stated LinkN inequality.

The maximum is over finitely many eventual statements already uniform
in i, not over a presumed finite set of family members. ShaFinOdd and
summability are guards of the literal LinkN predicate. LN does not
prove those guards; a downstream numerical use must supply them.

What remains is the actual local denominator table: the original or
actual-cut coordinate ideals, local Galois comparison, cyclotomic
scalar, split/inert regimes, band and exceptional estimates, small
and q,d prime bounds, with a single uniform constant. LN also retains
the exact type action, tame-label dictionary, same-point constituent
permutation, spectral spanning, localization identities, theta
nonzero scalars and the actual rank identification. Separation of
Hecke eigenvalues alone does not give the required spanning. An
isomorphism of substitute spaces does not identify the original row.

CutGivesAlt additionally demands all NormChoiceFacts and Tie4 on the
same cut. LN2's Good cut does not provide these stronger facts. They
remain a separate task on the source bundle route. The direct route
using original-family local tables need not be burdened with this
stronger alternative premise.

7. The global chains still required for RH and Dirichlet GRH
===========================================================

The analytic end is already reconstructed: sufficiently strong positive
H_psi windows imply the right-half-plane zero-free statement, and the
functional equation yields the open-strip conclusion. Primitive-to-
arbitrary character transport retains the Euler factors and the
principal pole exclusion. Ordinary RH specializes to the conductor-one
target. This specialization does not eliminate the other arithmetic
suppliers that construct its windows.

WB closes exact character balance, subgroup averaging, scalar bounds,
normalization errors and target identification from the stated actual
arithmetic gap contract. The needed gap itself remains a supplier.
It must hold on whole real windows for one admissible family, with
the target character, scale, host, cutoffs and fields fixed compatibly.

The sufficient family route still needs literal HostExW with RankVal,
HostGood, EAtH and the cohomological estimate on the same host. PairEx
and VWinAt do not by themselves construct all these fields or the
actual point/NumAt family. DesignExists must then supply one family
in the target class, large for the transported threshold, carrying
the required Core/layout, norm choice, tuples, local realization,
rows, lattices, embeddings and integral/spectral data.

DA1 joins the finitely many uniform Closed statements before the
family is chosen. DA2 converts HypV plus original LinkN and the actual
rank equality into the required congruence budget. It still needs
those same-family hypotheses and the exact metric/lattice transport.
The remaining global cuts include actual local twist coverage LinkEta,
retained-row defect LinkIRR, dZ0=D0, Bessel/period and ideal-shift
comparisons for LinkDIVt, fullness and positive retained rank, actual
LinkG instantiation, and LinkS for correctly normalized kernels with
off-diagonal, diagonal and exceptional terms. The global slope/frame
comparison and LinkMu require the original metric identities and
size data. The arithmetic ledger fields that yield ChainTied and the
final gain are not constructed by the final scalar inequality.

HP's completed polynomial representation theorem helps this chain,
but does not select the actual geometric coefficient realization.
The restricted DC20 real-row route may avoid an unused universal
discrete-series theorem where its range covers every used row; its
actual GapHyp, normalization and volume ties remain obligatory.

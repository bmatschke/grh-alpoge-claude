{\Large\bfseries Integral cusp lattices and rational theta lifts}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.76 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The integral cusp forms on a pinned geometric row form a full lattice.
The proof compares the analytic theta series with algebraic short
sections over the original field, then finds one denominator for
each section at every integral frame. A finer local calculation
recovers the source bound for each normalized torsion sum. On a
supplied family the
base class sums span the cusp space, giving rationality of the
theta lifts and the separating tests at both levels. One finite field request handles all higher
cover levels before the column is chosen. On each eligible column
the original map-existence, geometry and rank hypotheses then
suffice for the rational tests on its stored target spaces.

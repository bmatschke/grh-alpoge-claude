# Source review for the local Galois comparison at ramified places

The cuspidal CM lift is chosen separately for each specified Weil element. Toral comparison recovers the whole parameter when monodromy vanishes. The full tame comparison uses a completion isomorphic to $\mathbb Q_p$, the rank or residue-characteristic bound, the repeated-root test and zero input monodromy. Unramified extension preserves the inertia group, which is precisely where the final characteristic polynomials are compared. The existence of a weak correspondence is recorded separately from the implication for every such correspondence.

CONTINUATION-BACKLOG DISPOSITION, v0.30

This inventory preserves every v0.29 target record and classifies its remaining scope, not its declaration title. There are 245 records, grouped by 107 distinct remaining-scope strings. All 245 original file hashes and all 245 exact source-range hashes match the pinned checkout. The 242 earlier records have been independently dispositioned here; the three current DA29 count records are retained as delegated C records for the count audit. Ten records without inherited IDs receive audit-only aliases; no supplied ID is changed.

The four categories have different purposes. A is a checked published application in the predecessor's stated contract, with its literature recheck still visible. B is a moderate exact argument, which may already be closed. C is a substantive remaining proof or explicitly optional internal proof of a published theorem. D identifies a gap in our human understanding of the source object, map, field, normalization or selected route. The checked Lean proof is assumed correct throughout. A D classification is never an allegation that a source constructor is invalid.

The largest class of historical records is not fresh coefficient-ring or finite-dimensional form-space existence. Those conditional pieces are already present in LS/NC/NR/MR. Their retained scope repeatedly asks whether the same original state, tuple maps, coefficient field, local indices, theta/toric data, residual/tame identities and ChoiceSide are installed together. These are explicitly routed through GLOBAL_LOCAL_THETA_BINDINGS and GLOBAL_DESIGN, with the actual row-spectrum, local coverage, denominator and period inputs separately retained. Existing zero/empty initialization is not declared mathematically wrong; first identify which later consumer requires a stronger object.

The main deep non-count producers are the actual good-row spectrum and Galois comparisons, critical toric ratios and global theta integrals, original period/unfolding and theta-adjoint identities, spectral kernel/norm estimates, the compatible full Core/design, and the local denominator/length table. Generic rationality, equal rank, a substitute form space or a conditional lattice inequality does not close those original-object obligations.

Several source targets are intentionally optional. Universal all-regular discrete-series interfaces exceed the sufficient actual large-gap DC result. Internal Harish-Chandra, Tamagawa and Hasse-Minkowski proofs are optional when their checked published applications are retained. The source's huge explicit density constants, fixed-1/8 selection, older mean-square wrappers, split-support auxiliary route, stronger symmetric columns, all-rank CountQAlt and unchanged-level LevelPNAlt are also preserved without charging them to the minimal selected route. Any compound record's independently required Core, Choice or critical-ratio residue remains a separate scope_component.

HX now closes the old same-host construction residue conditionally on VWinAt. The exact original field, pair, page, rank/admission and bounded-character transport are written out, including the 401-to-400 rescaling and the same-field cutoff t<=floor(x^400). This does not close VWinAt. Every count-bearing historical record retains the actual CountI, CountIII, CountV and/or PMExAlt input, and the selected source alternatives are distinguished. In particular, an anchor named SeedIIAlt retains SeedIVAlt in its remaining scope, while an anchor named countQ_alt retains only optional stronger catalogue packages. These are not silently retired.

The count applicability is target-sensitive. KA5/KA6 remove Case III for a fixed character of 2-power order, including the principal-character RH specialization. CountI, the same-ideal PMExAlt producer and CountV remain on that route. Arbitrary Dirichlet characters with nontrivial odd-order part still require the original Case-III/SeedIV/Ctx3 construction. Every affected record has an explicit target_scope; no Case-III record is marked optional for the unrestricted Dirichlet theorem.

All CountI-bearing records explicitly retain the original arithmetic PropGK family as COUNT_I_PRODUCER rather than reopening the completed aggregation.

The remaining compound ch_balance/height/window anchors explicitly recognize TW8/OB8 and HV/VW as completed conditional assembly. Their actual count producers, compatible arithmetic family and global links remain. GLOBAL_TIED_SCALAR is B with root ZW reconstruction in progress in this snapshot: it is a numerical ledger-to-window consequence of literal ChainTied, not an additional independent arithmetic-gap assumption.

This file is a backlog-to-frontier crosswalk. Its frontier_ids express semantic responsibility and do not assert unverified edges in the root's selected-route AND graph. Each record retains its exact inherited remaining_human_scope and retained_inputs, source range and hashes, stable identity, category, applicability, and an explanation. The scope_components preserve partially closed or optional residues rather than flattening a compound record to a misleading single status.

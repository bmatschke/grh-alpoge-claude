The definite theta integral and its projection can use the original kernel and measure. Evaluation on definite classes explains why their rationality at the distinguished embedding survives scalar extension. These comparisons use the old realization as input. ### What the first pass now supplies

Under the pinned analytic inputs it preserves this datum on the enlarged column, including its reference phase, integral rows and nonzero-index places. The [marked type](LOWER_ROW_MARKED_TYPE.md) and the two real places of the host give a [simple trace formula](LOWER_ROW_SIMPLE_TRACE.md) on the bottom and holomorphic groups. One can therefore choose a saturated fine datum on the requested untwisted column.

### The bottom row on the weight-supported column

For the [one to three tuples on the untwisted restriction](UNTWISTED_BOTTOM_ROW.md), the [iterated tests converge](ROW_TEST_CONVERGENCE.md) for all real substitutions. The [uniform branching coefficient](ROW_CORE_BRANCHING.md) and [ascent on the given finite level](ROW_PERIOD_ASCENT.md) detect each nonzero row on the stated theta and doubling inputs. [One archimedean pair](ROW_COMMON_REAL_TEST.md) retains all the detections. The eight arithmetic clauses on an admissible choice include the [critical Hecke ratios](ROW_CRITICAL_HECKE_RATIOS.md). The [grid and marking construction](ROW_GRID_CHOICE.md) reduces existence for the [advance field request](ROW_COEFFICIENT_FIELD.md) to the common real matching and the square proportionalities.

The [period computation](ROW_PERIOD_JET_FORMULA.md) removes the projector on the good inputs, evaluates the definite terms as normalized jet sums, and computes the mixed-cell Haar factors. The [square comparison](ROW_SQUARE_RATIOS.md) gives a common finite system for the bottom ratio and evaluates each finite step factor. Base places contribute only roots of unity to the relative factors. The [image-basis calculation](COMPOSITE_ROW_RATIONALITY.md) identifies the necessary cross-tuple ratios and reduces descent in the presence of a period kernel to descent of the residual image vectors.

The [comparison at every stage](ROW_TOWER_INDUCTION.md) gives nonzero square constants on the entire prescribed grid. The [absolute estimates](ROW_TOWER_INTEGRABILITY.md), [real weight obstruction on the axes](ROW_TOWER_AXES.md), [higher parabolics](ROW_TOWER_PARABOLICS.md) and [boundary-map comparison](ROW_TOWER_BOUNDARY_MAPS.md) supply the two inductions. The [normalized grid formula](ROW_MIXED_GRID_RATIOS.md) cancels all paired Gauss and Hecke factors, leaving a single pair at even distance from the right boundary. On the standing inputs this also completes the square-existence premise for the [advance field request](ROW_COEFFICIENT_FIELD.md) and makes the multiplier of the [opposite-path identity](ROW_SQUARE_PATH_FLIP.md) invertible.

**Next calculation.** Evaluate the [mixed cells of the opposite path](ROW_PERIOD_JET_FORMULA.md) on the same row tests and quotient measures. In the prescribed order establish absolute integrability of each successive quotient integral, identify the support and the retained local factors, and identify the real functional with the common line used by the definite jet sums. Combined with period separation, that identity yields rational tests for the composite maps on the weight-supported column. Carrying this column through both passes, export and retwisting is still required.

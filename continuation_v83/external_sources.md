# Which external reconstructions help with the ten open contracts?

This review starts at edition 0.82, commit `682a3b0bdf07b3a95566cee72d0eb4388814b438`. The comparison target is the precise statement on the selected family in the original [formal source](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/tree/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4). In particular the local denominator question asks for the length of a cokernel on the finite integral cohomology of an actual surjection of crystalline lattices, at every eligible point and over the prescribed coefficient extensions. Here “finite” in the notation for cohomology specifies the Bloch–Kato local condition. The cokernel length is normalized by the length of the coefficient ring modulo the rational prime.

## Transfers into this edition

1. Zhang’s §5.1 uses a projection to a set of good eigenpoints. LN3–LN7 already proved the corresponding finite-lattice statement. E83D1–E83D3 track the signed error under the original multiplicities and coefficient-place weights. Above the family threshold the exceptional-prime contribution to the excess is at most `27648 d(j,0) U^6 = d(j,0) o(b)`. The pointwise proof in this edition also covers these primes individually. 2. B8.42 treats the two signs and the successive Frobenius steps in the cut lattice. On the actual mixed words, every signed position counts toward the exceptional rank even when the position is nonthick. U83M1–U83M6 construct those exceptional finite target classes, and S83M1–S83M2 separate the other directions. The cyclic sum uses at most four times the local degree times the central multiplicity. ## Carrying the comparison to the other obligations

The [contract-by-contract comparison](adriazola.md) covers all ten original IDs. For the two base theta obligations the missing statements concern rational separating tests and agreement of the actual readings on a common normalized kernel. For the common design, local construction, second-layer selection and simultaneous base rows, the remaining step is compatibility of all the choices on the same arithmetic member. For period and height the arithmetic line calculation in III.7.4 supplies a component of the normalization. For the spectral and retained-defect obligations, test-vector identification and the estimates on the indefinite counting region still require proofs. Across these comparisons a theorem that consumes a link supplies a proof of an implication downstream of that link.

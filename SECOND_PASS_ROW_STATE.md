# A fitted intermediate level on the retwisted column

Fix the untwisted origin and the enlarged pinned column chosen by [the second-pass retwist](SECOND_PASS_RETWIST.md). Write $P'$ for the preliminary package obtained from its restricted fine datum by [the theta-row construction](SECOND_PASS_THETA_ROWS.md). Work in the common eventual range with the coefficient and prime requests below included when the retwist is chosen. Use the published representation-theoretic inputs of the third layer, including definite unitary base change, restricted local correspondence, and the hypotheses of the [lower-row multiplicity theorem](LOWER_ROW_MULTIPLICITY.md). The discrete-series existence and filtered-dimension comparison used to fit the labels are also required. [Third-layer inputs][pub] · [Order of construction][caller]

Under these hypotheses there is a fitted state $x_1$ over **this** $P'$ with the Galois comparison to the original state that the subsequent transport requires. Its intermediate level consists of its labelled local and Galois data, definite realizations of its multiplicity spaces, rational Hecke record, and cuspidal row comparison. The definite realizations already exist for **every** fitted state on the pinned column. The latter two conclusions also use the actual labelled level. [Exact intermediate level][level]

## Build a state with the prescribed old Galois coordinates

Let $X$ be the stage recorded by the given untwisted origin. The enlargement identifies its prehost and frame with those of the new column, and $X$ has the true marked group for that frame. This is the input to the assertion called $\mathrm{BareK}$, which allows **any** preliminary package on the new column and a supplied older stage. Use $P'$ for that package. In its row data set the auxiliary localization index set to the empty set and the local row coordinates $\eta_j(v')$ to zero. Retain the old algebraic closure, the places over the two type primes and the local-stage identification used for Galois comparison. The cohomological length data for $P'$ can be read on that closure, since transporting the local Galois data preserves the lengths. The place set supporting the retwist is still the one specified in $P'$. [Quantifiers][baredef] · [Construction with prescribed places][bare]

The finite group centres still act on the new ambient spaces and on each new multiplicity space. Identify the two products of finite general linear groups through the place labels. The good idempotent has matching coefficients since both groups realize the same framed Green series. Pull back the central actions of the new row package through this identification. Matching at the idempotent follows on its spanning vectors. The construction checks the two actions for every allowed standard identification. Supply the division stage of these rows with its own lattice and parameter $6x^{-C_F}$, and fit the discrete-series and volume stages. This produces $x_0$ over $P'$ with empty localization index and the required Galois comparison to $X$. [Bare stage and its fitted output][fit0] · [Roadmap, zero-coordinate calculation][barebook]

The labelled-stage construction takes $x_0$ with that emptiness property. It can change the fitted state to $x_1$ but keeps $P'$ and gives a second Galois comparison, from $x_0$ to $x_1$. Use the actual marked places and the frame's tame and Green data as in the [labelled-state proof](FIRST_PASS_LABELLED_STATE.md). The [filtered-dimension comparison](FIRST_PASS_FILTERED_DIMENSIONS.md) applies to the specified algebraic closure and valuation rings. The selector in the resulting level has the same range as the selector on the new pin. Composition of the two comparisons is the one supplied to the transport theorem. [Labelled output and selector][level] · [Use of both comparisons][caller]

## Realize the multiplicity spaces and compute their numerical multiplicities

At $1\leq j\leq m$, let $\mu$ be **any** tuple of $x_1$, and let $\xi_\mu$ be its character in the new pin. Use the standard definite frame for the space at $i=j-1$. The level character is formed from the reference step character times $\xi_\mu$, with the inverse in the level-equivariance convention for forms. For a used tuple, extend the coefficient basis in its own finite lattice model to adelic forms. The extension is injective and identifies its span with the prescribed level space. Agreement with the stored reading of that basis **on $x_1$** extends by linearity to evaluation at every finite adele. For an unused tuple, take its level from the entire tuple set and use the coordinate and lattice comparison of $P'$. The requested evaluation identity has a used-tuple hypothesis. Both constructions work when the multiplicity space has dimension zero. [Two cases on a fitted state][used] · [Basis argument and integral action](FIRST_PASS_DEFINITE_ROW_FORMS.md)

For an allowed split Hecke generator, the level is hyperspecial at its place and the relevant tuple character is unramified there. At a chosen coefficient place $v'$, the excluded conductor is $Q_{P'}(v')=T'.Q(v')$ on the active set and is empty off it, where $T'.\psi(v')=1$. Thus the decorated-lattice action gives the required localized integral action for these particular characters. One choice of frame per $i$ works simultaneously for every tuple. This proves the full realization called $\mathrm{InMt}$ directly from the pin of $x_1$, including the identity-frame property. The source's $\mathrm{sMt}$ has no additional theorem hypothesis. [Conductors and generators][gen] · [Construction and universal conclusion][mt]

A second multiplicity assertion compares the numerical automorphic multiplicity of every good irreducible finite representation between the bottom and target rows. Apply the [lower-row trace comparison](LOWER_ROW_MULTIPLICITY.md) to the **moved fine datum of $x_1$**. It allows any twist and any place set on a pinned column. The fit supplies the group conditions, and the moved datum supplies the exact analytic reading and geometric realization. The rational reading without congruence is already part of the state. The good projector is stable under taking the dual, as follows from its Green coefficients under inversion. Inequivalent types give orthogonal subspaces. Inside each read label space their dimensions sum by the multiplicity equality to the whole label dimension. Since the labels span, these type spaces form an internal direct sum of each row. This argument uses only the lower-row comparison from the multiplicity hypotheses at this point. [Multiplicity theorem on arbitrary stages][multeq] · [Application to the moved state][isot]

## Rational Hecke data and the cuspidal comparison

Let $K_s,L_0,L'$ be the small, fixed and full coefficient fields of the enlarged state. Its pinned geometry supplies the $K_s$-form of each positive row and an integral lattice spanning that form. The rationality proof needs the Hecke transform, its reading and its integral estimate from the series package. Those three assertions have a [joint producer][hk]. One can insert them directly in the proof of the rationality theorem, which uses the series package only at these three projections. The [operator and integral comparison](FIRST_PASS_HECKE_RATIONALITY.md) then gives operators on the very $L'$-spaces of $x_1$, preserving the prescribed rational form, with the stated localized denominator bound. [Construction of the full record][rat]

The fixed coefficient field requires attention because only the full field grows in the retwist. Before choosing the original fields, include the normalized-eigenvalue generators prescribed by the prehost and frame in the **fixed-field** request. On each rational model take finitely many rational Hecke operators spanning their operator space and adjoin the roots of their minimal polynomials. The eventual comparison passes to generators depending only on the frame. The source's fixed-field request is independent of the twist and its final character set. Thus it still holds for the transported frame on $P'$. Every occurring eigenvalue in the normalization of the Hecke record lies in this same embedded $L_0$. After grouping the internal direct sum by joint eigenvalues, the good $L'$-subspace is spanned by joint eigenvectors over $L'$. Indeed, two different supported eigenvalue systems can be separated by an operator $H$ with eigenvalues $a\ne b$. Both $(H-a)v$ and $(H-b)v$ have smaller support and their difference is $(b-a)v$. This yields the rationality record on the given labelled level, including when systems from different types coincide. [Exact fixed-field request and membership][eig] · [Eigenvector descent and all fields of the record](FIRST_PASS_HECKE_RATIONALITY.md)

Finally fix a good type occurring in a positive row of this state. Use the reference tuple of **this** level to choose a definite automorphic constituent pairing nontrivially with the row adjoint. Its existence and the local parameter comparisons apply to the pin, moved datum, row reading and labelled level just used. The all-state parameter theorem takes the definite realization proved above and the third-layer inputs. The [cuspidal parameter argument](FIRST_PASS_CUSPIDAL_ROW.md) uses definite base change and the good marked types to extract the regular algebraic conjugate-self-dual cuspidal constituent with the required split parameters. The descent of its normalized Frobenius polynomials uses Hecke rationality on $x_1$, compatibility of Galois representations at unramified places, and Chebotarev. The [coefficient argument](FIRST_PASS_CUSPIDAL_COEFFICIENTS.md) then puts back the prescribed character twist. It produces the almost-everywhere coefficient statement in the very same $L_0$ for this constituent. Its local–global input is Barnet-Lamb, Gee, Geraghty and Taylor's Theorem 2.1.1(2). [Inputs at the fitted level][par] · [Cuspidality and coefficient clause][cusp] · [Local–global compatibility][blggt]

The labelled level together with these three records is exactly the intermediate level used with $x_1$ by the second-pass caller. The proof starts from the preliminary row package on the enlarged pin and the stated published inputs. The subsequent transport with the old series data produces a potentially different state $y$ over $P'$. Fitting the seed there and retaining it through the finite-level construction require the further series and finiteness steps in [the strengthened third level](SECOND_PASS_THETA_ROWS.md). [Intermediate record and its use][caller]

\paragraph{Q76Z1. One field request before all the geometric records.}
For each context make the single finite algebraic request of U76W2.
Fix a base $B$, frame $f$ and twist data $T$ meeting that request.
Take any original column $w$ over $(B,f,T)$,
its level $Z$, its field witness $h_X$, its set of places $W_t$,
and a record $z:\mathrm{R3.Dat}(w.\mathrm{st}(Z),w.I,h_X,T,W_t)$.
Put $D=(w.\mathrm{st}(Z)).\mathrm{toD7}$.
Assume the source existence conditions $R.\mathrm{ExAt}$ and
the geometric condition $\mathrm{InGeo}(z.\mathrm{geo})$, where
$R=z.R.\mathrm{toRealDat}$. Assume the original rank inequality
$8D.R.f\,D.R.r+4\le D.R.nV$. For the very object
$X=z.R.\mathrm{toCx3}(z.F)$ we have
\[
   \mathrm{LiftTest}(X),\qquad
   \mathrm{LiftTestB}(X,b),\qquad X.\mathrm{MLat}(b)
       \qquad(b=\mathrm{false},\mathrm{true}).
\]
In particular, for every row the same finite family of complex
linear functionals separates its whole target and is rational in
the specified embedding on all rational lifted inputs and all
stored target basis vectors at the indicated level.

Here is the assembly without assuming these tests among the
properties of $z$. Its pins give the weak characterization
and the prime guard, and its geometric record gives the
weight and frame tie, conjugation and base tuple pins. The
existence clauses upgrade the weak implications to the core
laws used in T76R1. Y76O3 supplies the oscillator extension.
C76U3 gives uniform rational coordinates on this same $R$.
U76W3 applies the field request on the original tuple levels.
X76R3 now gives the base row maps, including the guarded
all-tuple clauses. The stored phase of $z.R$ is one, so its
field conjugate is one. The phase pin of the geometric record
evaluated at the uniform coordinates and the preceding fields
give precisely $\mathrm{TiesG}$.

For the arithmetic side of this implication it is enough to
follow the proof of T76R1--T76R4. The weak characterization
and existence were used to obtain the six core fields, the tuple
laws and the model norm identity. The guard was used in T76R2
to form the simultaneous tuple covers. The rank inequality was
used in F76B2 and T76R3 for the base span. These are exactly the

have just been supplied. The resulting rational coordinate
vector has the prescribed phase. The tests are defined on $X$
and its stored bases; the lattice is the transported integral
cusp lattice of $X$. This proves the three assertions.

All data after the coefficient request were arbitrary under the

CR1. ACTUAL WEYL FIBERS AND THEIR RANK

1. Two integral modules must be kept separate.

For a partition nu=(nu_1,...,nu_k), nu_k=0, let nabla_nu(Z) denote the
polynomial coefficient module satisfying the column-homogeneity and
generic upper-unipotent invariance identities. CB2 gives its monic
tableau basis. Let Delta_nu(Z) be the span of the source's actual divided-
power words on the highest polynomial H. In the Lean source this is
weyl Z k k le_rfl nu. In general Delta_nu(Z) is a proper finite-index
submodule of nabla_nu(Z). Nothing in this component replaces one by the
other integrally.

The fiber used for the rank is Dl0, with a lower-case l in the source.
DeterminantTwistRowActionMem.dw_eq identifies it with deltaLTw, the
determinant-twisted Delta lattice. H0 and nablaLTw elsewhere in the
reconstruction concern the dual-Weyl lattice instead. This distinction is
necessary because the Fock normalization and finite-index denominators
are part of the later argument.

2. Existence of an integral basis of Delta.

All divided-power words preserve the relevant total degree and column
multidegree. Thus Delta_nu(Z) lies in a finite-rank free group P_Z of
polynomials. A subgroup of a finite-rank free abelian group is finitely
generated and free. Choose a Z-basis b_1,...,b_q of Delta_nu(Z); this
is an actual Weyl basis, not an assumed tableau basis of Delta.

After tensoring with Q, the highest-polynomial argument from IW3 shows
Delta_nu(Q)=nabla_nu(Q). One convenient proof is to use Gaussian
decomposition X=LDU on its dense open domain. A highest polynomial in
the dual-Weyl module is fixed by the left lower-unipotent group, so its
values on LDU are determined by its fixed torus weight and its value
at the identity. Its highest line is one-dimensional, generated by H.
Complete reducibility therefore makes the polynomial module irreducible;
the nonzero submodule generated by H is the full rational module.
Hence q is the tableau count, which CB3 calculates as

  D_k(lambda) = product_{1<=a<b<=k}
                   (lambda_a-lambda_b+b-a)/(b-a),

where nu_a=lambda_a-lambda_k. This argument uses rational equality only.

3. Exact coefficient extension for Delta.

Let O be the actual coefficient DVR with fraction field K of
characteristic zero. Every divided-power operation on integer
polynomials is integral and commutes with a coefficient homomorphism.
For example, the j-th divided power of X_a d/dX_b sends X_b^t to
binomial(t,j) X_a^j X_b^(t-j). Thus all word generators over O are
images of the integer word generators. Each is an integer combination
of b_1,...,b_q, and each b_j is an integer combination of those words.
It follows in both directions that Delta_nu(O) is exactly their
O-span. Their K-linear independence follows from an integer coefficient
minor nonzero over Q. Such a minor is still nonzero in a characteristic-
zero field. Thus they are an O-basis.

4. The exact twist and lattice basis independence.

For Lambda=g O^k and decreasing lambda, the actual formula is

  Dl0_O(lambda,Lambda)
      = det(g)^(-lambda_k) R(g^(-1)) Delta_{lambda-lambda_k}(O),
  R(a)P(X)=P(aX).

Row substitution is an anti-action, so R(ab)=R(b)R(a). With a changed
basis g'=gh, h in GL_k(O), one gets
R((gh)^(-1))=R(g^(-1))R(h^(-1)). The action of GL_k(O) preserves
Delta: elementary unipotents act by finite sums of divided-power words,
diagonal unit matrices preserve weight components, and permutation
matrices are products of elementary and diagonal unit matrices. Gaussian
elimination over the local ring O generates GL_k(O) by these matrices.
The factor det(h)^(-lambda_k) is a unit. This proves basis independence,
and hence agrees with the source's intersection over all bases in Dl0.

The matrix g itself need not be integral. Its determinant twist is a
nonzero K scalar; it can have positive or negative valuation. It remains
an invertible O-linear map from Delta to its image, with inverse defined
on that image. This is enough to transport a basis.

5. Tensor fibers and parity.

The Frobenius-extended lattice at position (v,s) is
Lambda_{v,s}=O span iota_v(sigma_v^s L_v). The effective weight is
lambda^0_{v,s} at even s and -rev(lambda^0_{v,s}) at odd s.
Tensoring the actual bases in mutually disjoint variable blocks gives
an O-basis of HtupW. Independence can be checked over K: polynomial
rings in disjoint blocks are tensor products, and the product of
independent families is independent (apply one dual coefficient
functional in each block). Spanning follows by expanding a product of
O-linear combinations. Therefore each fiber has rank

  q_0 = product_{v,s} D_k(lambda^0_{v,s}).

A constant shift cancels from every difference in the Weyl product.
Negative reversal permutes factors: the pair (a,b) becomes
(k+1-b,k+1-a), preserving numerator and denominator. Hence the odd
effective weight has exactly the required count, without suppressing
the odd-place dual representation itself.

6. Characteristic zero in the supplied Hida setting.

The HidaSetting says that p is a prime and a uniformizer of W. If W
had positive characteristic ell, then ell is prime. For ell=p the
uniformizer would be zero. For ell!=p, the image of p in the prime
subfield F_ell is invertible, contrary to being a nonunit. Thus W is
characteristic zero. Its fraction field Q_q is characteristic zero;
the injective map Q_q -> K gives characteristic zero for K. The
coefficient injection O -> K is its fraction-field map. These are the
exact hypotheses invoked in CharZeroOfSetting.exists_basis_Dl0.

CR2. EVALUATION ON FREE ORBITS

Let Gamma act on full lattice tuples, and let H(L) be the fibers just
described. The determinant-twisted substitution rho(gamma) maps H(L)
onto H(gamma L): the direct inclusion follows by applying the matrix
to a lattice basis in the formula above; the reverse inclusion uses
gamma^(-1). A character chi:Gamma -> O^units supplies the unit scalar
in the equivariance relation.

The source funMod contains functions f on all tuples satisfying:
  (i) f(L) belongs to H(L) when L is a full lattice tuple;
  (ii) f(L)=0 for a tuple that is not a full lattice tuple;
  (iii) f(gamma L)=chi(gamma)rho(gamma)f(L).
The action preserves the property of being a full lattice tuple.

Choose representatives L_x for finitely many orbits and assume the
stabilizer of every full lattice tuple is trivial. Evaluation f ->
(f(L_x)) is injective by (iii) and (ii). Given arbitrary a_x in H(L_x),
write a full lattice tuple uniquely as gamma L_x and set
f(gamma L_x)=chi(gamma)rho(gamma)a_x. The orbit label x is unique by
the representative property, and gamma is unique by triviality of the
stabilizer. The group laws for chi and rho give (iii); exact fiber
transport gives (i); define zero on nonlattices for (ii). This is a
linear inverse over O, with no denominators or averaging over finite
stabilizers. It proves an integral isomorphism with the finite product
of the representative fibers.

If a stabilizer were nontrivial, this construction would allow only
vectors invariant under its combined character and representation
action. Counting the whole coefficient fiber would then be wrong.
CR4 supplies precisely the needed freeness in the actual case.

CR3. CONCRETE GLOBAL CLASSES AND LOCAL LATTICE ORBITS

1. Abstract group decomposition, with full proof.

Let H be a subgroup of a group G, let K subset K' be two subgroups,
and suppose there are homomorphisms pi:G -> G_p and j:G_p -> G such
that pi*j=id, j(G_p) subset K', and

  K = {x in K' : pi(x) in K_p}

for a subgroup K_p of G_p. Choose y_i representing H\G/K', with
pi(y_i)=1. Such normalization is achieved by right multiplying any
y by j(pi(y))^(-1), which belongs to K'. Let

  Gamma_i = {gamma in H : y_i^(-1) gamma y_i in K'}.

Define (i,g K_p) -> H y_i j(g) K. Right multiplying g by k in K_p
right multiplies y_i j(g) by j(k) in K, so the map is defined on cosets.

If H y_i j(g) K = H y_i' j(g') K, there exist gamma in H and k in K
with y_i' j(g') = gamma y_i j(g) k. Passing to K' classes yields
i=i'. Rearranging then shows y_i^(-1) gamma y_i in K', and projection
g'=pi(gamma) g pi(k) shows g'^(-1)pi(gamma)g in K_p. Thus g'K_p is
the pi(gamma)-translate of gK_p.

Conversely, suppose i=i' and gamma in Gamma_i satisfies
g'^(-1)pi(gamma)g in K_p. Set

  k = j(g)^(-1) y_i^(-1) gamma^(-1) y_i j(g').

This belongs to K' by subgroup closure. Its projection is the inverse
of g'^(-1)pi(gamma)g and hence lies in K_p. Thus k belongs to K, and
y_i j(g') = gamma y_i j(g) k. This proves the exact equality criterion.

For surjectivity, write x=gamma y_i u' using its H\G/K' class. Then
pi(j(pi(u'))^(-1)u')=1 and j(pi(u'))^(-1)u' belongs to K', hence to K.
The K-class of x is therefore that of y_i j(pi(u')). All class fibers
are accounted for.

2. Actual local group and its stabilizer.

Take G=U(J)(A_{F,f}), H=U(J)(F), K=U, and K' the level away from p.
The actual LocId provides every place v|p, its completion, integral
ring and the first-projection lattice frame. At those split places
the unitary group is GL_k(F_v): its second split component is uniquely
the adjoint inverse of the first. Inserting these split elements at
the finitely many places above p and identities elsewhere defines j;
projection in the supplied frames defines pi. They satisfy pi*j=id.
The finite insertion lies in K' because the latter has no conditions
above p.

The actual GIdx excludes p from the two auxiliary primes and T.
The integral global lattice Lambda satisfies Lambda subset Lambda#
and T Lambda# subset Lambda. These inclusions localize: duality can
be checked in any lattice basis via its Gram matrix, so its equations
commute with localization and completion. T is a unit at v|p; hence
the localized lattice is self-dual. Splitting the integral completion
gives O_{F_v} x O_{F_v}. Consequently the localized lattice splits
as M_1 x M_2, and self-duality says M_2 is the dual of M_1 for the
induced perfect pairing. A unitary element stabilizes this lattice
if and only if its first component stabilizes M_1: preservation of
the dual component follows from the adjoint-inverse relation.

LI5 says that M_1=B_v O_{F_v}^k. Thus its stabilizer consists precisely
of matrices for which B_v^(-1) A B_v and its inverse are integral.
The completion identification carries this to GL_k(W_v), the source's
stabStd. Product over all v|p is stabTup. Away from p, the old level
conditions are unchanged, which gives the exact K_iff equation above.
This expands HypAtP for the actual D7/LocId data instead of adding it
as an unexplained new assumption.

3. Actual source labels.

The set of full W_v-lattices in Q_v^k is GL_k(Q_v)/GL_k(W_v): columns
of any basis give a representative, and two bases span the same
lattice exactly when their quotient and inverse are integral.
Taking products identifies G_p/K_p with the actual lattice tuples.
The group Gamma_i acts through the local projection pi(gamma), which
is the source's levC.act after the rank reindexing and local frame.
The proved double-quotient map therefore gives clsC. The supplied
LevelData has an actual bijection y_bij between its double quotient
and C.cls 0; composing with its inverse gives clsR, exactly as defined
in ClsStab and ClsRec.

The result is a bijection

  disjoint_union_i Gamma_i\Lat = C.cls 0.

The right side is the existing finite class label set of the supplied
global record. Thus this proof also supplies finiteness of the local
orbits. It does not prove the existence of that global record, nor
manufacture its class map from an arbitrary finite abstract set.

CR4. TRIVIALITY OF THE ACTUAL STABILIZERS

1. Finiteness from definiteness and integral bounds.

The source's lattice Lambda is finite and full over O_E, and U
stabilizes each of its localizations. For x in U(J)(A_{F,f}), let

  Lambda_x = {v in E^k : v belongs to x_w Lambda_w for every finite w}.

A full O_E-lattice Lambda is sandwiched between a O_E^k and
a^(-1)O_E^k for some nonzero integer a: choose a spanning E-basis
from Lambda and clear the denominators both of a finite generating
set and of the inverse basis matrix. The entries of x and x^(-1)
are integral at all but finitely many places. Clearing the remaining
denominators gives analogous uniform local sandwiches for x_w Lambda_w.
Intersecting inside E^k gives

  b O_E^k subset Lambda_x subset b^(-1)O_E^k

for another nonzero integer b. It follows that Lambda_x is a finite
full Z-lattice: the ambient fractional O_E module is a finite free
abelian group, and the contained b O_E^k has finite index there.

If gamma is rational unitary and x^(-1)gamma x belongs to U, it
preserves every x_w Lambda_w, hence preserves Lambda_x. Under each
complex embedding of the CM field, the form J is definite. Multiply
it by -1 at any place where it is negative; the resulting sum is a
positive-definite real quadratic norm on the Minkowski vector space.
Every such gamma preserves this norm. For a fixed Z-basis of Lambda_x,
each image vector lies on a fixed bounded norm sphere in a discrete
lattice, giving finitely many choices. The stabilizer is finite.

This is the content of finite_stabAt_of_local and the adelstab argument.
The formal source implements denominator clearing through a trace-dual
basis to account for its integral tensor subring. The mathematical
argument above is the same commensurability statement for the actual
finite local lattice modules; it does not assume x is a global matrix.

2. The auxiliary levels are upper-unitriangular modulo the primes.

At each auxiliary rational prime g_i, the level is conjugate, after
projection to a split factor, to iwU. Its defining property is that
the projected matrix is integral and its reduction belongs to the
upper-unitriangular group. This is not the principal congruence kernel.
No claim that the original matrix is already congruent to identity is
made before removing the prime-to-g torsion part.

Let R be a complete DVR with residue characteristic g. If A in GL_k(R)
reduces to 1+N with N strictly upper triangular, then N^k=0.
In characteristic g, (1+N)^(g^s)=1+N^(g^s)=1 for g^s>=k.
Thus its reduction has g-power order. Suppose A^N0=1 and gcd(N0,g)=1.
Its reduction then has order dividing both N0 and g^s, so A=1+X with
X in M_k(m). If X lies in m^r, the binomial expansion modulo m^(r+1)
gives (1+X)^N0=1+N0 X, since 2r>=r+1. N0 is a unit in R, so X
lies in m^(r+1). Induction and separatedness intersection_r m^r=0
give A=1. This works even when g=2.

For a finite-order A with order g^a b, gcd(b,g)=1, the matrix A^(g^a)
still reduces upper unitriangular and has order dividing b. The
previous paragraph makes it the identity, so A has g-power order.

3. Use two distinct primes and transport to the local orbit action.

The field map E -> F_v given by either split projection is injective.
Conjugation and matrix base change therefore preserve the order of a
rational matrix. Every finite stabilizer element has g_0-power order
at the first auxiliary prime and g_1-power order at the other. Since
g_0 and g_1 are distinct primes, the order is 1. These are the literal
host fields g_prime and g_ne, consumed by trivStabU_rec.

If gamma belongs to Gamma_i and fixes the tuple gW, then
g^(-1)pi(gamma)g lies in K_p. Combining this with
y_i^(-1)gamma y_i in K' and the exact K_iff identity puts
(y_i j(g))^(-1)gamma(y_i j(g)) in U. The just-proved global
stabilizer theorem gives gamma=1. This is the actual TrivStab
of the local GAtP, not a separately assumed free action.

The supplied LevelData also identifies its class stabilizers with
these rational stabilizers. Therefore C.StabZero holds for the actual
class set, as in GramTotallyDefinite.stabZero_rec.

CR5. THE GENERAL FINITE-CLASS RANK FORMULA

By CR3 the total number of local orbits is |C_0|. By CR4 their
stabilizers are trivial. CR2 identifies M integrally with the product
of one fiber per orbit. Every fiber is finite free of rank q_0 by
CR1. Thus M is finite free and

  d = |C_0| product_{v,s} D_k(lambda^0_{v,s}).

This is exactly d_eq_of_classes, with X=C_0 and cl=clsR. More generally
that source theorem accepts any finite X with the proved equality
criterion and surjectivity. It chooses one lattice representative
per x and partitions X by its component index; the sigma-fiber
identity then counts each x exactly once. No separate estimate or
comparison of the sizes of different component sets is needed.

CR6. SPECIALIZATION TO THE ORIGINAL GLOBAL FORMS

The statement reconstructed is:

  Given D:D7, L0:LevelData D, u:D.what2.Word, T:Forms0 D u,
  a coefficient place w, hg:GIdx D.host (D.mem u).A.T (resChar w),
  and X:LocPack D u (L0 u) (resChar w),
  then for EVERY r,e in N,

       (X.gat r e).d = dim_C (D.mem u).A.Mu0.

Forms0 explicitly includes class and value identifications (cls0 and
val0). It presents Mu0 as the coefficient-valued automorphic functions
on the same finite C.cls 0, with the usual invariance at each class
stabilizer. The injective stabilizer maps in cls0 and C.StabZero make
that condition vacuous. Hence its dimension is

  |C.cls 0| product_{tau in D.what2.Tau}
                  #ssIdx(N(0),N(0),((D.fm u).lam 0 tau).natW).

This is the actual finrank_forms_level_zero theorem, not a new
assumption on Mu0's numerical size. Its equality to the local product
uses the following exact source dictionary:

  local k = local m+1 = D.R.k = D.R.N 0;
  gatRec_pos(v,s) = eTau^(-1)(posG(v,s));
  lam0(v,s)(a) = ((D.fm u).lam 0 (gatRec_pos(v,s))).w(a);
  nuOf(lam0) = the same dominant weight's natW after rank reindexing;
  lamAt parity replacement has the same tableau count by CR1.

The source posG is a bijection from the disjoint union of all orbit
positions to Psi, and eTau is a bijection from Tau to Psi. Therefore
the product is reindexed bijectively, with neither missing places nor
extra multiplicities. Frobenius parity remains essential to the
representation itself even though dimension is invariant under it.

The wrapper pack_d_eq receives (for every u) a Joint0 argument, but
its actual proof is

  fun D L0 T _ u w hg _ _ r e =>
       locPack_d_eq D L0 u (T u) w hg _ r e.

The underscore after T is the unused Joint0 proof. This is not an
accidental omission to be repaired by a stronger hypothesis. The
rank comparison legitimately needs only two independent presentations
with the same class labels and weights. L0 u and T.cls0.lev each
already satisfy those statements about C.cls 0. It does not require
their actual lattices, local subgroups or class representatives to
agree. Joint0 expresses precisely such stronger compatibility and
still matters for LI8 and the actual global comparison maps.

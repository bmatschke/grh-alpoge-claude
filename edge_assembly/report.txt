3. FINITE ACTUAL BASES AND THE EDGE COORDINATE [EA3]

RK3 produces a direct summand Sg of the ambient polynomial module, contained
in the allowed generator span, with a basis made of actual generators.
Every generator has the fixed row multidegree G2d. This homogeneous piece
has a finite monomial basis, so Sg is finite free over the DVR. Reindex its
basis as B_1,...,B_b, where b=rank_O Sg. Its span remains the actual ambient
direct summand; this is stronger than mere fraction-field independence.
There is no need for the source basis_fin's irrelevant infinite-basis branch.

All its monomials have normal degree at most

 X=10N(1088 Ns^2 log(a) a/(pk)+a p^{-L1}+kQ).

Set T0=floor(X). Here X>=0. With A'=max(A,10), C>=1088 and Ns<=N1,

                         T0<=X<=A' N X_C.                    (EA.5)

For the general signed Cdeg,CLa version, bound the expression by its absolute
value, then use |Cdeg|<=A' and |CLa|<=C. If X<0, the natural floor is zero
and the right side is nonnegative. This explains the source's two branches.

To identify W with the standard Witt ring, use the classical uniqueness
theorem for strict p-rings: a p-adically complete DVR with uniformizer p
and perfect residue field f is uniquely isomorphic to W(f), after fixing
the residue-field identification. Here f has p^{n'} elements. Concretely,
Hensel lifting of X^{p^{n'}}-X (derivative -1 modulo p) gives a unique
Teichmuller lift [a] of each a in f. Every x in W has a unique convergent
expansion sum_j p^j[a_j], by successive reduction and division by p.
The strict-p-ring theorem identifies the addition/multiplication carries
with the defining Witt polynomials. This is the only classical Witt-ring
background invoked; it is not a local rank theorem. Since sigma fixes p,
is p-adically continuous and lifts a -> a^p, uniqueness gives
sigma([a])=[a^p] and hence exact Frobenius compatibility. HidaSetting
contains the completeness, residue and Frobenius conditions required here.

Fix an actual edge e=(L-,L+) with target W^k. Since pL+<=L-<=L+ and
dim_f L+/L-=r, choose a basis H of L-. Every neighbor with this source
has a basis of the form

       B=H g^{-T},      L+=B diag(p^{-1}I_r,I_K) W^k,           (EA.6)

where g is an invertible matrix over W, or over W(f) via the identification.
To see this, pL+/pL- is an r-plane in L-/pL-, choose a residue basis
adapted to that plane and lift it; the resulting basis change is in GL_k(W).
Write it g^{-T}. Under H, its r-plane is spanned by the first r rows of
g^{-1}. This is exactly ratPlane(g), not the first rows of g itself.
Two such matrices yield the same outgoing edge precisely when these planes
agree. Thus the realisation's vanishing at all different rational planes
is vanishing at all different outgoing edges. This also proves the required
edge/basis correspondence rather than assuming an unspecified coordinate map.

4. FROM REALISATION TO FLAT EDGE VECTORS [EA4]

0<=o<=T0, whose substituted symbols add to B_i*subst(SPtrue). Each summand

it takes integral polynomial coefficients to HL(L-) and order-i substituted

that same vanishing order. Set V_{i,o}=w^{-o}Theta(A_{i,o}). Then

 V_{i,o} in w^{-o}HL(flat,L-),
 V_{i,o}-Theta(F_{i,o}) in w^{c'}R_e(flat),
 V_{i,o} in w^{c'}R_{e'}(flat) for every other outgoing e'.

These statements follow by dividing by w^o in the precise Theta membership

Set V_i=sum_o V_{i,o} and S_i=Theta(sum_o F_{i,o}). Because o<=T0,

 V_i in w^{-T0}HL(flat,L-),  V_i-S_i in w^{c'}R_e(flat),
 V_i in w^{c'}R_{e'}(flat) for every other outgoing e',
 S_i in R_e(flat).                                           (EA.7)

The last assertion uses the integral SUBSTITUTED coefficients of each
F_{i,o}, not integrality of the rational preimages. Summation preserves
all these lattice memberships. This is the exact flatSrc transport.

5. MULTIPLICATION AND STABILITY OF THE PRIMITIVE TEST [EA5]

Split lambda^0=flat+rest, where flat=(d_t,0,...,0) on T and zero elsewhere.
Both weights are dominant: the first two selected remainder entries agree.
By CB6–7, polynomial multiplication adds column degrees, determinant powers
and effective signed weights. Consequently

 HL(lambda,L) HL(mu,L) subset HL(lambda+mu,L),
 R_e(lambda) R_e(mu) subset R_e(lambda+mu).

This is the full content of the opaque LevelPrimesProviso name. To check
its scope even for arbitrary coefficient modules in H0, impose the inclusion
in every available basis separately; if there is no such basis, the target
intersection is the whole polynomial module. Expanding tensor generators
then proves Hten multiplication. No arithmetic prime-selection proviso is
being used. The scalars are correct because Ar(lambda+mu)=Ar(lambda)+Ar(mu).

CloudAt supplies complementary vectors h_1,...,h_{n2} with

 h_j in R_e(rest) intersect w^{-oS}HL(rest,L-),   oS<=ell.

Put f_{ij}=V_i h_j and s_{ij}=S_i h_j. Multiplication and EL give

 f_{ij} in w^{-(T0+oS)}HL(lambda,L-) intersect R_e(lambda),
 f_{ij}-s_{ij} in w^{c'}R_e(lambda).

For another outgoing edge, EL's HL(rest,L-) subset R_{e'}(rest) makes
h_j belong to w^{-oS}R_{e'}(rest). Therefore

 f_{ij} in w^{c'-oS}R_{e'}(lambda) subset w^{cStar}R_{e'}(lambda).

This is why c'=cStar+ell is essential; the complementary source height is
spent exactly once. The normalizing pairing is beta_e(F,G)=G(p^Ar F).
Ar may be negative. EL6 proves it is an integral perfect pairing of R_e
with R'_e, using w^2=p. No positivity of Ar is assumed.

For every coefficient array c_{ij} in O with a unit coordinate, CloudAt
gives a dual vector G in w^{-tT}HL(lambda,L+)^dual intersect R'_e, vanishing
in w^{cStar}R'_{e'} at every other incoming edge, for which

        a=beta_e(sum c_{ij}s_{ij},G) lies in O and divides p^{D1}.

The actual value differs from a by w^{c'}z, z in O. Once D1<=Dp,
c'>=cStar>=8(Dp+1)>=2(D1+1), so w^{c'}z=p^{D1+1}z' with z' integral.
Write ab=p^{D1}. Then

                 a+p^{D1+1}z'=a(1+p b z').                    (EA.8)

The parenthesis is a unit of the DVR, since p is nonunit. Thus the actual
value is integral and still divides p^{D1}. This proves the universal
primitive-array detector for the realised f_{ij}, not only individual tests.

            1-x <= 1-Ny <= u=(1-y)^N <=1,

                    1-A0-B0 <= (1-A0)u:                      (EA.12)

if A0<=1, multiply u>=1-B0 and use A0B0>=0; if A0>1, multiply u<=1 by
the negative 1-A0. Take A0=Cr x and B0=x. Thus for C>=Cr+1,

             1-Cx <= (1-Cr x)(1-kc0/a)^N.                    (EA.13)

8. FROM ALL EDGE FAMILIES TO THE LITERAL GOOD-RANK WITNESS [EA8]

Increasing tS,tT to t enlarges the lattices w^{-t}HL and w^{-t}HL^dual.
Each f_{ei} lies in its own R_e and, by its outgoing error property, in
every other outgoing R_{e'}. Therefore it lies in the exact Nsrc(t,e.src).
Each cloud dual test similarly lies in the exact Ntgt(t,L+) because it is
in R'_e and all other incoming R'_{e'}. Increasing D1 to Dp preserves the
assertion that a value divides p^D. This is the full Asm inclusion check.

Given a primitive coefficient vector across ALL M source vectors, choose an
edge e0 containing a unit coefficient and use that edge's primitive test.
The contributions from other edges lie in w^{cStar}O subset p^{Dp+1}O,
because cStar>=2(Dp+1), and all their source vectors are integral in their
own R_e. Their sum cannot change the p-divisibility upper bound by (EA.8).
Thus every primitive linear combination is detected by an integral dual
test with value dividing p^{Dp}.

For completeness, this primitive assertion yields precisely GoodRankK,
not just fraction-field independence. Let J be the image of Ntgt under
G -> (beta(f_i,G))_{i=1}^M in O^M. It is an integral submodule. Smith
normal form applies since O is a DVR. If J has smaller rank, a primitive
row annihilating it contradicts detection. If a Smith entry fails to divide
p^{Dp}, the corresponding primitive row evaluates on J only in a larger
ideal, again contradicting detection. Hence p^{Dp}O^M subset J. Choose
preimages G_j of p^{Dp}e_j; the map u(x)=sum_j x_jG_j satisfies

               beta(f_i,u(x))=p^{Dp}x_i.

This is GoodRankK with identity coefficient matrix and n=M; it is exactly
the witness used in the source goodRank_of_prim/s7. The module J is finite
because it is a submodule of the finite free module O^M, regardless of how
Ntgt was originally presented. The M=0 case is the empty witness.

Finally let L=B W^k be any full W-lattice. In block s use
g_s=iota(sigma^s B) and the polynomial action
rho_{lambda_s}(g_s)=det(g_s)^{-lambda_{s,k}} T(g_s^{-1}).
Tensor these block actions to R_B. EL8's exact covariance sends HL(L0)
to HL(BL0), each midpoint lattice to its image, and therefore R_e to R_{Be}.
On duals use G -> G composed with R_B^{-1}. The scalar Ar is unchanged, so

 beta(R_B F,G composed with R_B^{-1})=beta(F,G).

Images of edges biject, preserve quotient cardinalities, and preserve every
intersection defining Nsrc,Ntgt. Transport the source vectors and u by these
actions, leaving their O-coefficient matrix unchanged. This proves LPOneEvery
without any new denominator. The source requires 4(Dp+1)<=cStar in LPprime;
the available 8(Dp+1)<=cStar is stronger. EA7–EA8 now prove the exact aux_3
once EdgeStep is supplied, not a differently normalized rank assertion.

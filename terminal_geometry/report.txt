RESULT AND SCOPE

This component reconstructs the actual symmetric penultimate level,
terminal full/row levels, terminal row chart, terminal incidence and
Hermitian carrier suppliers. It reaches the literal conclusion of
GlobalTheory.Mem.Col2.TC.colsTiedSym_std, including StdC and StdLvC.
The new proof is a human mathematical reconstruction from the pinned
definitions and exact supplied arithmetic/column data. No Lean source is
edited, no Lean compilation or kernel replay is performed, and no PDF is
built. The source ranges and their exact original-byte hashes are in
provenance.json and source_locators.txt; build_provenance.py reproduces
them using git show at the pin.

The retained hypotheses are the actual CM arithmetic host, rank and
prime data, true column datum TC(B,X), TC.Ok, Pins, a literal Laws l,
and Later(B,I.cols(l)). The independent TL component can subsequently
discharge Laws from Ok. TG does not depend on TL: where period and
level laws are used they are the supplied l, so no terminal-construction
cycle is hidden in that substitution. The primitive construction of the
original arithmetic host and Later is not asserted here.

DR3, with its exact CMData and valid-decoration assumptions, supplies
all decorated-point realizers used here. Neither an initial realizer
nor a stronger rational SameGenus statement is retained. The whole
reference terminal branch now has no additional geometric existence
premise. This is not by itself a proof of RH or GRH.

The actual endpoint is ColsTiedSym. The stronger OneLevelSymmetricColumns
record additionally asks for every chart set to be singleton and for
particular earlier HXS/lamX identifications. TG does not prove those
additional assertions. Nor does the reference-twist endpoint construct
the full variable-twist comparison package. The exact adjacent targets
are indexed separately in next_targets.json.

NOTATION AND MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS

1. O means O_E. The involution is the original CM conjugation. The
   Hermitian form is conjugate-linear in its first variable:
   h(x,y)=bar(x)^t y in the common SF2 coordinates. Its integral dual
   is the O-valued Hermitian dual, not the trace dual.
2. For each word u, p=T_u is prime, pi=pi_u, pi*bar(pi)=p, and
   (pi,bar(pi))=O. The exact selected primes are the divisors of pi.
   They have residue cardinality p and are pairwise nonconjugate.
   FC1 proves their squarefree product equals (pi), not just support
   containment. Auxiliary selected primes are disjoint from these
   primes and their conjugates. At them pi is congruent to one.
3. The coordinate frame is the original global one from SF2. Local
   frame changes are only the explicitly specified last-coordinate
   pi factor in TG4; the global frame, coefficient embeddings and
   residue maps are not chosen afresh.
4. Source N, L, Lsh remain distinct. At a general column class,
   N is the self-dual companion, L is the simultaneous type kernel,
   and Lsh=L#. For the terminal row ST is empty, so all three
   coincide. After adding a terminal coordinate they become
   N_row+O, N_row+pi O, and N_row+bar(pi)^(-1)O respectively.
5. The terminal row coefficient proof retains the determinant twist
   and integral Delta lattice of CV10. Equality of rational dimensions
   or polynomial weight spaces is never used to replace Delta by its
   generally different integral dual-Weyl lattice.

TG1 -- EXACT SYMMETRIC PENULTIMATE REDUCTION GROUP

The central point is the reverse inclusion. A flag-stabilizer argument
alone could show only that the actual residue image lies in the proposed
matrix group. We lift every matrix of that group to GL(O_(P)), and
verify the actual flag and marked-vector conditions. Thus the proof
constructs the required preimage in the actual local group.

The permutation rho moves the first k coordinates after the next s
coordinates. The singleton head order is rho, while the remaining
coordinates are grouped into successive t-blocks. The exceptional flag
cut b=k+s has boundary s rather than b. A marked-vector condition at
this cut forces exactly M[a,k+s]=0 for a<k. Omitting this condition
would give a larger parabolic and would not identify HSym.

For an ordinary head cut b, preservation and the marked vector impose
the head diagonal 1 and lower-entry zero equations. For tail cuts
r+jt, preservation imposes lower block zero equations. Both M and M^-1
occur in JSymG. In the forward direction the inverse marked-vector
condition follows because inverse images preserve the same flag
lattice. In the reverse direction the conditions on M and M^-1 give
both lattice inclusions, hence equality. A lifted matrix has unit
determinant because its residue is invertible; no claim of surjectivity
GL_n(O_E)->GL_n(k_P) is required. Only GL_n(O_(P)) is used.

The rational prime g_a is a local uniformizer at the selected auxiliary
prime. Dividing an integral entry of zero residue by g_a therefore
preserves local integrality. This arithmetic fact is essential to the
reverse inclusion. The source's zero extra-space parameter cOf(m-1)
is used exactly; the proof is not asserted for positive extra depth.

TG8 -- DISCRIMINANT, SEEN ORIENTATION, AND ALL PROJECTIONS

At selected P, choose a local N-basis adapted to its nonzero type
functional. L differs from N by replacing one local summand by P.

of L#/L at each of P and bar(P), and none elsewhere. It also gives
p L# subset L. Since (p) is the squarefree product of all these
paired primes, Chinese remainder idempotents split the finite
quotient into one-dimensional residue modules. Choosing one nonzero
vector in each gives the O-linear isomorphism O/(p). This proves
DiscCl; it does not assert an isometry for a discriminant pairing.

For SeenCl, the valuation is pulled back along the exact sigmaOf/
embE composition dictated by tauOf. Its positive ideal in O_E is
a nonzero prime over the specified F-prime. There are exactly two
primes above it, P and bar(P); the given seenP pi/bar(pi) positivity
selects the correct one. The distinguished complex embedding is
injective, so the auxiliary coefficient element in the statement
is the actual pullback. There is no replacement by a conjugate or
unrelated embedding.

Coordinate isometries commute with Hermitian dual, ideal scalar
multiplication, sum and localization. Hence they transport every
LvTied domain and kernel exactly. The endpoint and chart projections
agree globally because they agree on N (respectively the actual
down lattice), and bar(pi) multiplies the entire relevant dual
(respectively kernel) domain into that sublattice. Its residue at

TG11 -- LEVEL-ZERO NORMALIZATION AND RESIDUE LEVELS

SameCar deliberately leaves the target module and local fT open.
We replace these by the forms ones transported by the fixed rank
reindex. Every carrier field used by CarH, CarT, Ad and stabilizer
compatibility is unchanged. The source's fT stabilizer law verifies
that the local group remains the same after replacement.

To prove pi kills the target, take one nonzero completed fT value
coming from a global class dual vector. Multiplication of the
source vector by pi kills its residue projection, hence kills that
fT value. The target's additive order is the prime p, so that value
generates the target and pi kills all of it. This avoids incorrectly
assuming that an additive equivalence fMe is automatically linear
for the whole completed integer algebra.

The more general comparison of two nonzero functionals is equally
explicit. On the split completed integer algebra S_v, S_v/(pi)=F_p
on the selected factor, and L_pi#/L_pi is cyclic S_v/(p), generated
by bar(pi)^(-1)e_last. Both functionals vanish on L_pi and have
pi-annihilated targets, so both factor through the same single
F_p quotient and are nonzero additive isomorphisms from it.
Their comparison gives the exact residue equivalence for an
arbitrary carrier satisfying the pi-annihilation condition.

For all nonzero levels the residue functional is the forms fT
composed with coordinate reindexing and the prescribed fMe.
The pointwise relation follows by solving y_c t=C_i z, and using
the TG8 projection equality. At the terminal step uniqueness of
the chart provides the endpoint reindex. These are all FvTie

% BEGIN C76U1
\paragraph{C76U1. The determinant scalar and the prescribed constants at the base embedding.}
Let $z$ be the original $\mathrm{R3.Dat}$ on a column. Assume
$R.\mathrm{ExAt}$ and $\mathrm{InGeo}(z.\mathrm{geo})$, where
$R=z.R.\mathrm{toRealDat}$. Put $T=z.\mathrm{core}$,
$K=T.Q.c.F$, $L=D.\mathrm{what}_2.LL$ and let $\sigma_*:L\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$
be its distinguished embedding. Its restriction is the inclusion
$\sigma_0:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ by $z.\mathrm{alg\_eq}$.
This is a good embedding and is also $O_i.s_0$ for each pinned base
row, since its two field inclusions compose to the inclusion of $K$.
The weak characterization is $z.\mathrm{pins.char}$. Its level,
group, family and model fields, and its chain and point identities
applied to the given existence clauses, give the core used in X76R1,
as in the first paragraph of T76R1. Write $w=z.\mathrm{geo.fw}$.

At a fixed row set $\chi_i(g)=\prod_{P\in R.P_g}
\chi_{i+1,P}(\det R.e_G(g)_P)$. There exists $y_g\in K$ with
$\sigma_0(y_g)=\chi_i(g)$. Indeed let $u$ be any unit of the residue
field at a marked prime $P$. Lift $u$ to a nonzero algebraic integer
in the host CM field and place that number in the $P$-component of
an idele, with every other component one. It is a local unit and its
infinite part is one. The character tie $w.\mathrm{chiR}$ at the
distinguished good embedding puts its adelic character value in
$\sigma_0(K)$. The local-to-adelic character identity and the level
residue law identify that value with $\chi_{i+1,P}(u)^{-1}$.
Invert and multiply over the marked determinant residues. The group
law for the stored character $\chi^{W}_i$ identifies its value in
$L$ with the image of $y_g$ by injectivity of $\sigma_*$.

Apply X76R1 with scalar one. Its component constants have unit modulus,
its cusp sections are stable under the indicated level moves, and its
reading law at $O_i.s_0$ has multiplier $\sigma_0(y_g)$. These are
exactly the clauses of $\mathrm{EqAt}$ at $\sigma_0$, including the
lift of the multiplier to $K$. Thus the implication
$z.\mathrm{geo.equivAll}$ gives the same law with the prescribed
component constants $T.c_E(i+1,\sigma_0)$. Multiplying the reading
by the row's fixed Lie factor yields, for every level lift $k$ of $g$,
\[
 \phi_C(g f)(h)=\sigma_*(\chi_i^{W}(g))\,\phi_C(f)(hk).
\]
Here $g f$ is the geometric level change and $\phi_C$ is the reading
with the prescribed component constants and Lie factor.

\paragraph{C76U2. Extension of the geometric action at all embeddings.}
Fix a row, let $C$ be its $K$-space of lower cusp sections, and put
$W_L=L^{n_W}$. The tie identifies $C$ with the $K$-form of $W_L$
by $e_K$. Its base-change law gives a $K$-basis $b_j$ of $C$ for
which $e_K b_j$ is an $L$-basis of $W_L$. Write $J_\sigma$ for the
stored semilinear map on $W_L$, $j_\sigma$ for $j_{\rm Top}$ on
$C$, and $E$ for the model map from the row's Hilbert space to its
space of multilinear automorphic functions at $\sigma$.
For each embedding there is a single scalar $s_\sigma\ne0$ such that
\[
 J_\sigma(e_K f)=s_\sigma j_\sigma(f)\qquad(f\in C).
\]
For a zero Hilbert space take one. Otherwise $\mathrm{InGeo}.jUnif$
makes $j_\sigma$ span the Hilbert space. Hence the defining branch of
$j_{\rm Top}$ cannot be a zero fallback. At a good embedding its
values at the prescribed extremal vector are the readings $\phi_C$.
The tie gives the same evaluations for $J_\sigma e_K$: to apply its
evaluation clause extend the semilinear cusp reading to $W_L$ using
the displayed basis. Then $\mathrm{InGeo}.evInj$ gives equality.
At an embedding whose restriction is not good, the conjugate branch
of $j_{\rm Top}$ and $z.\mathrm{geo.bar}$ give unit local vectors
$v'$ and $v$ at which the two respective evaluations are the same
conjugate reading for all $f$.

Here is why the possibly different unit vectors give one scalar for
the entire row. For $E(x)(v)=E(y)(v')$ let
$s=\prod_\tau\langle v_\tau,v'_\tau\rangle$. Under the model law
the Hilbert pairing is a fixed multiple of the multiplicity pairing.
For a multiplicity pairing witness $c_{xy}$ the integral of
$\overline{E(x)(v)}E(y)(v')$ over the model fundamental domain
equals $s c_{xy}$. This factorization is available even
for orthogonal $x,y$: a nonzero inner product forces the defining
multiplicity witness to exist, and for $x\ne0$ orthogonal to $y$
subtract the witnesses for $(x,x+y)$ and $(x,x)$ using linearity
of the integral. The zero vector is immediate. Evaluating the two
self-pairings and the cross-pairing thus gives
$\|x\|=\|y\|$ and $\langle x,s y\rangle=\|x\|^2$. Since
$|s|\le1$, expanding $\|x-sy\|^2$ gives $x=sy$. Apply this to
$x=J_\sigma(e_K f)$ and $y=j_\sigma(f)$ using the same vectors for
every $f$. If $s=0$ then both sides vanish for every $f$ by equality
of norms, and one can take $s_\sigma=1$.

By $\mathrm{InGeo}.jUnif$, the images of $b_j$ under $j_\sigma$
are independent and span: all other cusp images are in their span
by semilinearity. The same holds for the images of $e_K b_j$ under
$J_\sigma$. In particular $J_\sigma$ is injective and carries every
finite $L$-independent family to a complex independent family, as
an invertible minor of its coordinate array stays nonzero under
$\sigma$. These are the independence and span assertions for the
actual stored maps.

For this row choose the one $K$-linear group action $r_C$ supplied
by $\mathrm{InGeo}.geoAct$. Transport its matrices via the displayed
basis to an $L$-linear action $r$ on $W_L$; thus
$r(g)e_K f=e_K(r_C(g)f)$. Its geometric equivariance holds on
$j_\sigma$ at every embedding. The character there, $\chi_{\rm Top}$,
equals $\chi_i^W$ since the latter satisfies its defining character
formula at the injective distinguished embedding. Multiply the
equivariance identity by $s_\sigma$ and extend off the basis using
semilinearity. This gives, simultaneously at all $\sigma$,
\[
 E J_\sigma(r(g)x)=\sigma(\chi_i^W(g))\,
     \mathrm{act}_W(g)\,E J_\sigma(x)\qquad(x\in W_L).
\]
The action $r$ and the basis used to construct it were chosen before
varying the embedding.

\paragraph{C76U3. Identification of the stored action from one embedding.}
Retain the original $z$, $R.\mathrm{ExAt}$ and $\mathrm{InGeo}$ as
in C76U1. Then $R.\mathrm{LUnif}$ holds for the unchanged datum.
It remains to identify the action $r$ of C76U2 with the stored
$\rho^W_i$ in each row. Work only at $\sigma_*$. If its Hilbert
space is zero, injectivity of $J_{\sigma_*}$ from C76U2 forces
$W_L=0$ and the two actions agree. Otherwise every group element
has a level lift. Indeed the definition of the level action makes
it zero if there is no lift. The model intertwines it with the
Hilbert action of $g$, which is invertible, and the model map $E$
is injective. A missing lift would force the Hilbert space to vanish.
Choose the lift used by the level operator. The trivial flag has
trivial character, so on multilinear functions the operator at this
lift is right translation by $k$.

C76U1 now gives cusp stability for every $g$. Hence the first clause
of $z.\mathrm{geo.rhoGeo}$ gives
$\rho_i^W(g)e_K f=e_K(g f)$ for all $f\in C$. At the good base
embedding the frame tie identifies evaluation of $EJ_{\sigma_*}e_K f$
at $v_0$ with $\phi_C(f)$. By C76U1, the value on $gf$ is the
corresponding right translate multiplied by $\sigma_*(\chi_i^W(g))$.
The model action $g_W$ has this right translation as its image under
$E$ on every vector. Evaluation injectivity at $v_0$ therefore gives
\[
 J_{\sigma_*}(\rho_i^W(g)e_K f)=
  \sigma_*(\chi_i^W(g))\, g_W(g)J_{\sigma_*}(e_K f).
\]
Applying $E$ and comparing with C76U2 shows that $EJ_{\sigma_*}$
agrees on $\rho_i^W(g)e_K f$ and $r(g)e_K f$. Both factors in this
map are injective. Equality on the geometric basis yields
$\rho_i^W(g)=r(g)$ as $L$-linear endomorphisms. Substitution in the
all-embedding identity of C76U2 proves the action clause of
$R.\mathrm{LUnif}$, alongside its two basis assertions already
proved there. The argument uses the unconditional geometric action
and constant-comparison fields of the given record. Its phase law
can now be applied on this very record.

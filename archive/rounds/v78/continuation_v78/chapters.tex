{\Large\bfseries The theta readings and the common denominator setup}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.78 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

Two questions recur in the construction. Does a comparison identify
the operators on the row actually chosen? Can the local comparisons
be used together at a single family point? The first three sections
follow the chosen rows through the Hecke and lattice constructions
and the two readings of the local index. The remaining sections construct
the Wach comparison, derive the local table and exceptional-set estimates, and bound the eight
family parameters. On one hub these give nine of the fifteen fields
of the original family conclusion. The coarse estimate is reduced
to the action of one specified power of the local prime on invariant
classes.

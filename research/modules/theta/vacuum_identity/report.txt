OUTCOME AND SCOPE

This component gives human proofs of the actual StdOK, finite four-copy
vacuum identity, thMM choice specification, algebraic fourth-power germ,
rectangular vacuum coefficients, rationally translated moved coefficients,
and the ESmooth/JetAlg pair for the same supplied ambient data. It does not
assume the existence of a theta vector satisfying the desired four-copy
identity. The vector is constructed using a finite intertwiner, a
one-dimensional theta-space argument, a nonzero slice, and one fourth root
of a nonzero complex scalar.

The argument is conditional on the literal arithmetic datum D and actual
Base(D,cc,delta), with Crv(D,cc,delta). In particular the period lattice,
unkilled half-point, fourfold K-isometry, complex extension, and KapChoice
are the supplied ones. The trace-self-dual fractional ideal AA0 is used
through the host's actual unimodularity theorem. We do not assert a new
construction of the arithmetic host or replace its fields.

PA1/PA4/PA10 provide the period-area identity, actual algebraic complex
isometry, and finite-support correction. TA10/PA15 provide the arithmetic
one-variable rational-translate coefficients. TA12 preserves the exact
normalized distinguished half-point convention. TA14 supplies the
one-variable analytic normalization. TL18 supplies the finite derivative
and algebraic fourth-root extraction rules. The only same-round input is
CA2's independent finite normalized-indicator presentation, used in VI11.
VI1–VI10 do not use CA's final FinAlg or EvLg join.

The classical background remains explicit: characteristic-zero trace
duality and Dedekind fractional-ideal identities; finite-dimensional
linear algebra; ordinary complex power-series differentiation and the
identity theorem; the residue theorem on a period parallelogram; Liouville's
theorem; normalized Haar integration on a compact real torus; and the
one-variable Weierstrass/CM assertions previously reconstructed in TA.
No continuity of an arbitrary field embedding is used. No Lean source was
edited or compiled, no kernel replay was performed, and no PDF was built.
The source hashes verify provenance, not the correctness of a new Lean
formalization. No RH or GRH conclusion is asserted by this component.

VI2 — EXACT INTEGRAL DUALITY AND THE ROUNDED MULTIPLIER

For self-dual O_E lattices L,L', their Hom lattice is self-dual for the
matrix trace Hermitian pairing. This is a local assertion about perfect
pairings: at a finite prime and, when different, its conjugate prime,
choose module bases and corresponding dual bases. Matrix units then give
the trace-dual basis. No local or global orthonormal integral basis is
needed. This is materially different from a comparison of rational ranks.

Let A=AA0. Its K-trace-dual identity is equivalent to
A*conjugate(A)=different(E/K)^(-1). Thus the K-trace dual of A*Hom is itself.
Multiplication by the actual ideal cc changes this dual by conjugate(cc)^(-1).
Since cc*conjugate(cc)=Norm(cc)*O_K, multiplication by Norm(cc) takes the
dual of cc*A*Hom back to cc*A*Hom.

The alternating form has exactly the factor
  (1/Norm(cc))*Tr(K/Q)(Tr(E/K)(h(b,c))/sqrt(-d)).
This follows from PA1's area formula, and has the same sign as the form
linear in its first variable. The quadratic codifferent
(sqrt(-d))^(-1)*O_K converts alternating integer duality to the just-proved
Hermitian trace-duality identity. Testing all O_K multiples is essential.
It supplies the full O_K integrality condition, not only the rational
trace of one scalar.

A candidate dual vector initially lies in the full real space. Pairing
with a full Z-basis gives integral values. Inverting the nonsingular
rational pairing matrix shows that the candidate is in the rational
span, so the ideal-dual calculation applies. This closes that possible
rational-versus-real gap. Fullness and common integer denominators also
prove commensurability for every pair of admitted lattices, hence LatAll.

Every actual period lattice is stable under theta=(1+i*sqrt(d))/2.
Writing e=Im(H(x,y)) and k=Im(H(theta*x,y)), both integers, gives
sqrt(d)*Re(H(x,y))=2*k-e. At x=y this proves q(x) integral; polarization
gives q(x+y)-q(x)-q(y)-e even. Consequently exp(pi*i*q(x)) is a sign with
the exact semicharacter law. On the actual lattices it equals the source's
(-1)^round(q(x)); the rounding is not silently omitted away from its
integral domain. For rectangular matrices H sums all row, column and
CM-type coordinates.

VI3 — HALF-POINT PARITY AND ALL TEN StdData FIELDS

The three unkilled conditions imply that omega0 and theta*omega0 are
independent modulo 2*Lambda. Their alternating pairing is odd, because
the two-dimensional F_2 alternating pairing is nondegenerate. The first
quadratic value is odd, while multiplication by theta changes it by
(1+d)/4, an even integer. The quadratic multiplier and the actual even
theta character therefore have the same values (-1,+1) on this basis.
Their product is an ordinary character trivial on 2*Lambda, so they agree
on the entire lattice. Coordinate products give betaStd.

The complete field list is:
  per, half, isom, alpha0, betaStd, betaSemi, betaAlpha,
  selfDual, idx_1, idx_2.
There are ten fields. betaAlpha holds on the full four-block vector
space, since the actual isometry preserves the quadratic form and that

VI5 — FINITE FOURIER INVERSION AND THE SOURCE NORM

Self-duality makes the commutator pairing between the two quotient groups
perfect. Summing it in either order proves equality of their finite
orders. Orthogonality then cancels all nonzero quotient classes in the
composition of the forward and reverse intertwiners. The product of the
two positive inverse square roots is 1/r, and the one surviving character
sum is r. Thus the composition is exactly the identity, not a scalar
multiple whose sign or phase remains undetermined.

For the norm, the pointwise identity
  |U_xF(z)|^2 exp(-pi*H(z,z))
    = |F(z+x)|^2 exp(-pi*H(z+x,z+x))
gives unitary translation for the normalized torus mean. Distinct quotient
translates are eigenvectors with distinct characters for the first
lattice quotient; their cross terms therefore integrate to zero.
The finite sum has r equal diagonal terms and its normalization square
is 1/r. A periodic mean is unchanged when computed over the common finite
cover. This is exactly the source normSq, which chooses a fundamental
domain and takes its normalized average. Fullness provides a bounded
domain with finite positive measure, and periodicity makes the choice
irrelevant. Therefore the proof supplies literal IopIso as well as
nonvanishing, inversion, and theta-line transport.

VI6 — THE STANDARD THETA LINE, WITHOUT A NEW EXISTENCE PREMISE

The normalized even theta is U_t(s_Lambda)/U_t(s_Lambda)(0) for the actual
t=omega0/2. The denominator is nonzero because t is not a period. The
function has one simple zero per period parallelogram, in the class -t.
For any other entire function with the same multiplier, the quotient by
this function is elliptic with at most one simple pole. Its residue is
zero by cancellation of the opposite edges of a parallelogram, so the
pole is removable. The elliptic entire quotient is constant.

This one-variable argument is applied separately in each coordinate.
It gives F=F(0)*product(theta0), with the product normalized to 1 at zero.
TA12 controls the exact distinguished half-point convention and its
coefficient conjugacy; no unnamed generator is substituted. The proof
does not invoke the multivariate vacuum assertion it is intended to
establish, or an unspecified Riemann–Roch dimension formula.

VI7 — CONSTRUCTING thMM AND ITS EXACT FOURTH POWER

Transport the standard product line by the finite positive intertwiner
and pull it back through the actual gC. The result K4 is nonzero by
Fourier inversion. Multiplication by the actual nonzero kappa gives a
nonzero four-copy generator P.

Take a point where P is nonzero, fix its last three blocks, and obtain
a nonzero one-copy slice f1. Its fourfold tensor lies in the four-copy
line, so it equals c*P for c nonzero. Choosing a scalar b with
b^4=c^(-1) makes f=b*f1 satisfy the desired exact tensor identity.
No assertion is made here that c or b belongs to Qbar. Algebraicity of
the eventual coefficients is proved from the identity in VI8–VI9.
In particular the construction does not take a locally defined branch
of the fourth root of an analytic function.

A mixed tensor F tensor f tensor f tensor f proves that the one-copy
space is a line. Two vectors with the exact normalization therefore
differ by a fourth root of unity. The literal epsilon-chosen thMM is
one of the constructed admissible vectors, so both source thMM_spec
declarations apply to that exact choice and it is nonzero.

VI9 — DIRECT RATIONAL-TRANSLATE ARGUMENT

For x in Q*lamM set v=gC(x,x,x,x). Choose m>0 with m*v in the actual image
lattice. Translating the four-copy identity introduces the phase
exp(-pi*i*E(v,y)) for each original quotient representative y.
Since m*E(v,y) is integral, this phase is a root of unity. Each coordinate
of v+y is a rational period, so TA10 applies to every translated factor.
The same Gen argument proves Root4(U_x thMM).

Apply the same arbitrary algebraic-coordinate derivative separately in
all four blocks. At zero the resulting value is the fourth power of the
single-copy derivative. It is algebraic, and therefore so is its fourth
root in C. This works even when the initial value at zero vanishes.
This direct proof avoids the broader coefIn_translate theorem for an
arbitrary theta vector. It proves the stronger translate conclusion
needed here for the actual vacuum, without asserting the unused broader
theorem.

VI10 — RECTANGULAR AND MOVED VACUA

For standard O_E modules, the integral Hom lattice is the full matrix
module. Multiplication by A gives entries in A, and matrix units show
the converse. Hence the standard period lattice is exactly the product
of the one-copy period lattices. The rectangular thZero is the actual
product of the actual thMM over matrix positions. Its multiplier,
entirety, nonvanishing and one-dimensional span follow coordinatewise.
An empty index set gives the constant 1 on a zero-dimensional space and
is included explicitly.

Rational translations split into the one-copy translations handled in
VI9. Both moving intertwiners defining thMoved are finite, since VI2
proved full commensurability. Their coefficients are algebraic signs
times positive inverse square roots. Every translating vector belongs
to the same rational period span. Composing with any additional rational
translate introduces only a root of unity, by clearing two integer
denominators in the integral alternating pairing. Thus thMoved is entire
and every rational translate has algebraic Taylor coefficients. This
is the literal ta_thMoved assertion. Factorials in CoefIn are nonzero
rational integers.

{\Large\bfseries The arithmetic seeds on the marked frame}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.69 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

To put a prime on the right component one first needs an arithmetic
point on the right ideal. We construct the two automorphic seeds on
the given CM field, put their determinant points on the prescribed
Hida ideals, and choose the coefficient ring and the auxiliary
places needed to run the argument twice. The resulting statement
isolates the unitary modularity-ideal theorem as the arithmetic
input for the upper field. The patching chapter proves the
normalization of exceptional points needed by that theorem.

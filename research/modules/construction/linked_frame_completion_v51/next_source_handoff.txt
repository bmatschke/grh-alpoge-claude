2. THE ORIGINAL Dat ENTRY: gen_gd

Read FrobeniusSmoothVectors/SqrtDiscr.lean, especially gen_gd beginning at
line 152, together with UnitaryThreeGroups/DatFacts.lean and
HeightAndVanishing/GdFacts.lean. The exact input is the original
Dat(Omega,K,c,e,psi), an algebraic closure Omega of Q with its specified K
embedding, and a valuation B above ell. The output is

  Gen ell D.eta D.Psi D.cc B (ell*N*|disc K|) top.

The subgroup is literally top; this simplifies its openness, commutator,
index and ell-inertia fields. Preserve the discriminant factor in M.

A useful first subargument is the quadratic discriminant construction.
The determinant of the embedding matrix of an integral basis squares to
the discriminant. For imaginary quadratic K the discriminant is negative,
so its square root delta satisfies delta²=-|disc K| and transforms by
D.eta. The elements 1 and delta span K; hence an automorphism fixing delta
fixes K. This identifies the exact quadratic character, including its sign.

The remaining entry proof must transport the original Dat facts into the
residue-character formulation of Gen. In particular, the reduction of the
adic cyclotomic character is the specified omO; the two maps from the adic
integers agree because every element agrees modulo ell with its integer
residue representative. Use the actual Teichmuller character and the
literal gdIff data to prove the common kernel obstruction g4 and the local
nonquadratic witness g5. For the latter, the original unit condition on
Psi(s)²-1 gives nonvanishing after residue reduction.

Away from ell, inertia fixes every ell-power root of unity. After removing
this cyclotomic factor, the finite Dirichlet character has order prime to
ell; reduction is injective on such roots of unity. This is the actual
teich field. These arguments must be written out rather than citing the
existence of gen_gd as a human proof.

3. THE HostData ENTRY: gen_host

Read FrobeniusSmoothVectors/GenHost.lean in full, with its final theorem at
line 220. The input is the original HostData ell e psi o1, any specified
complex conjugation sigma, and any B above ell. Its output is existential
in Hb and has

  eta=eta4, Psi=Chr.Psi ell e (dirG psi), M=ell*N,
  Hb=kernel(dirG(kapB ell psi)).

Do not replace Hb by top. Its role removes the ell-primary character part
away from ell while keeping ell-inertia inside Hb. The proof establishes
that Hb is open, contains the commutator, and has ell-power index; these
facts are needed by the already completed common-field construction.

The host permits ell=3. Its entry assumes ell is prime and ell is not 2,
not ell>3. The supplied HostData parity, even conductor and dyadic
conductor-depth assumptions must be retained literally. The quadratic
field is Q(i): use delta²=-1 and the exact eta4 action, with a sufficiently
large power of M in the cyclotomic-kernel clause.

Two genuine host-specific arguments deserve separate bounded sublemmas.
First, gh_two and kernel_not_contained_eta exploit the conductor at 2 to
find a unit mapping to -1 modulo 4 while its dyadic character is trivial.
The constructed inertia element is simultaneously killed by omO and the
residual Psi, but not by eta4. Second, the decomposition witness g5 comes
from UnitaryThreeInertia.exists_hostFrame and exists_sigma, used in
GenHost.lean's exists_decomposition_nonquadratic. Read their producers in
UnitaryThreeDeformations/HostEx.lean and the imported
DyadicCharacterPowerNeOne.lean. Transport the witness to the arbitrary
specified B by conjugation. Do not assume one preferred ell-adic place or
replace the decomposition condition by a weaker global nontriviality.

4. THE CLEAN GdEx AND HostEx JOINS

The exact final interfaces are in CurveDVRSpecialization/HostFrameEx.lean:
GdFrameEx uses D.eta,D.Psi,D.cc and M=ell*N*|disc K|; HostFrameEx uses

constructed its global/local cohomological suppliers from literature.

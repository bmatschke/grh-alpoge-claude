# The rank-$r$ complement on a live residual Hecke block

Fix a first-pass coefficient place $w$, a residual Hecke block $i$ of row $j$ over $w$, and its actual complete discrete valuation ring $O_i$, finite over $\mathbf Z_\ell$. Write $K=\operatorname{Frac}(O_i)$, $k=O_i/\mathfrak m_i$, $n=n_V=r+t$, and $Z_i$ for the full theta locus in its point set $X_i$. The decomposition is required for **every** integral Hecke–Galois realization at **every** point of $Z_i$. Its intertwining is over $K$. Absolute irreducibility of the rank-$r$ remainder uses the additional condition that the residual block is live. [Definitions][defs] · [Fraction-field intertwining and integral family][hida]

## Removing the prescribed character string

The [fitted first-pass characteristic-zero Galois comparison](FIRST_PASS_POINT_COMPARISON.md) supplies a continuous semisimple $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$-representation for each positive-rank regular algebraic conjugate-self-dual cuspidal $\pi$. Its Frobenius polynomial, away from a finite set, is the recorded local polynomial of $\pi_v|\det|^{(1-a)/2}$, where $a$ is its rank, with the source's specified embedding and Frobenius convention. The polarized cuspidal Galois theorem is used here under the host's polarization and local reciprocity identifications. Since the comparison belongs to the original characteristic-zero datum and localization transports it, the added coefficient request for this assertion is empty. Its exception set may depend on the cuspidal and on the embedding. Only finitely many such systems occur in the following comparison for a given point. [Barnet-Lamb–Gee–Geraghty–Taylor, Theorem 2.1.1][blggt] · [Exact cofinite test][cusp] · [Transfer to every realization][empty]

Fix $x\in Z_i$ and any integral continuous Hecke–Galois representation $R_x$ of degree $n$ realizing $x$, semisimple over $K$. The automorphic parameter for $x$ contains a part $(1,\mu_\eta,t)$, with $\mu_\eta=\mu_i\eta_T^{-1}$. Remove one occurrence of this part. Its $t$ Galois characters are the specified $\chi_c=\chi_1\varepsilon_\ell^{-(c-1)}$, $1\le c\le t$, where the cyclotomic character takes value $q_v$ at the source's arithmetic Frobenius. The remaining terms supply an actual character $T$, the trace of a representation over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. Here is the parity calculation needed to obtain this last assertion. For a remaining part $(a,\pi,d)$, its $h$-th term for $0\le h<d$ is the sum of the Satake eigenvalues of $\pi_v$ times $q_v^{(d-n)/2-h}$. The stored regular infinitesimal character puts that of $\pi$ in $\mathbf Z+(n-d)/2$ at each embedding, with distinct entries. Write $r=2u+1$, as follows from the rank conditions. [Parameter and infinity test][parts] · [Character values][chi]

* If $a+d-n=2e+1$, $\pi$ has the algebraic parity required in rank $a$. Its attached representation twisted by $\varepsilon_\ell^{e-h}$ has precisely that trace, because $(1-a)/2+e-h=(d-n)/2-h$.
* If $a+d-n=2e$, $\pi\otimes\mu_\eta$ has the required algebraic parity and polarization. Use its attached representation, twisted by $\chi_1^{-1}\varepsilon_\ell^{e-u-1-h}$. The value of $\chi_1$ is that of $\mu_\eta$ times $q_v^{-r/2}$. Cancellation again leaves $q_v^{(d-n)/2-h}$ times the original Satake sum. This calculation uses genuine integer cyclotomic powers in both cases. [Parity calculation][parity]

Combining the remaining terms and restoring the removed part gives
$$
  \operatorname{tr}R_x(g)=T(g)+\sum_{c=1}^{t}\chi_c(g)
$$
after embedding the entries in $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$. At the eligible split Frobenius elements this is the local parameter identity for $x$. Split Chebotarev density and continuity give the equality on all $G_E$. In characteristic zero, semisimple characters determine multiplicities, so each prescribed character line occurs with the required multiplicity over the algebraic closure. It occurs over $K$ as well: the joint equations $R_x(g)v=\chi_c(g)v$ are linear over $K$, with the same nullity after extension. Take one such line and its $G_E$-stable $K$-complement using semisimplicity. Subtract its character and repeat. This accounts for repetitions as well. The complement to the resulting $t$ independent lines is $G_E$-stable and has dimension $r$. Intersect it with the original lattice $O_i^n$. Clearing denominators shows that the intersection spans the complement, and it is free of rank $r$ over the DVR. Both $g$ and $g^{-1}$ preserve it. In a basis it yields an integral continuous $\rho_x:G_E\to\operatorname{GL}_r(O_i)$ with the required rational block decomposition. The original full lattice can have a nontrivial integral index across this rational splitting. [Trace at every group element][density] · [Successive lines and stable lattice][lattice] · [Exact conclusion for $Z_i$][zdec]

## Why one live witness controls all points of $Z_i$

A live witness consists of one point $x_0\in Z_i$, one integral realization there and a rank-$r$ remainder $\rho_0$ whose reduction is absolutely irreducible. Choose the full integral family $R_x$ from provision 41 and the complement above for each $x\in Z_i$; outside $Z_i$ the auxiliary $\rho_x$ may be trivial. For any $g\in G_E$ the full characteristic polynomial at $x_0$ is unchanged if the witness is replaced by the chosen Hecke–Galois realization there. The polynomials agree on the eligible Frobenius elements by definition and extend by density. In addition all full characteristic polynomials in this residual Hecke block have the same reduction, by the residual comparison in provision 41. In both decompositions the prescribed character factor is the identical monic polynomial
$$
    \prod_{c=1}^{t}\bigl(X-\overline{\chi_c(g)}\bigr)\in k[X].
$$
It cancels in $k[X]$, even when some of its roots coincide. Thus $\bar\rho_x(g)$ and $\bar\rho_0(g)$ have identical characteristic polynomials for every $g$. [Use of the common residual polynomial][cancel]

Here equality of traces suffices for the last step in every residue characteristic, for a specific reason. By absolute irreducibility, the $k$-span of the group matrices of $\bar\rho_0$ is $M_r(k)$: apply Burnside over the algebraic closure and descend dimension. If $\sum a_g\bar\rho_x(g)=0$, trace against $\bar\rho_x(h)$ and use trace equality on $gh$. It follows that $\sum a_g\bar\rho_0(g)$ pairs to zero with every matrix for the form $(U,V)\mapsto\operatorname{tr}(UV)$. The matrix units show that this form is nondegenerate in every characteristic. The span of $\bar\rho_x(G_E)$ therefore maps onto $M_r(k)$ and has dimension $r^2$. Its image algebra is the full matrix algebra, proving absolute irreducibility. Absolute irreducibility also follows in characteristic zero: a proper invariant subspace after algebraic extension would be defined over a finite extension, and its saturated intersection with the extended lattice would reduce to a proper nonzero invariant subspace. This applies to each point of $Z_i$ on a live block. At other blocks the decomposition argument supplies a semisimple complement. For the full degree-$n$ family, provision 41 supplies the Hecke-valued reverse characteristic polynomials and the distinctness condition after scalar extension; the earlier coefficient splitting condition gives absolute irreducibility of its individual constituents over $K$. The [common integral coefficient request](FIRST_PASS_GALOIS_COEFFICIENT_MODELS.md) provides this splitting on every realization meeting it. Together these are the preconditions on the local Hecke algebra and representations used for the subsequent choice of roots. [Matrix-span argument][burn] · [Constituent descent][distinct] · [Assembly of the live-block setting][assemble]

## One preliminary coefficient request for later realizations

Choose one preliminary realization meeting the combined requests for provision 41, coefficient splitting and the comparisons above. For each $w$, in the **fixed** $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ of the place, take a finite $\mathbf Q_\ell$-spanning set of each preliminary block field above $w$. Send these elements into $\mathbf C$ by the fixed place isomorphism. Their union over $1\le j\le m$ and the finitely many block indices over $(j,w)$ is a permissible request at $w$. Every subsequent block field over $w$ meeting it contains the preliminary fields under the same embedding. The inclusion preserves the valuation, hence restricts to a local map of the actual DVRs compatible with $\mathbf Z_\ell$. This is an inclusion into each later coefficient ring, which may be ramified. The finiteness required here is per $w$. [Finite request and DVR maps][ring]

Match the points of the two realizations by the full characteristic-zero Hecke system in the same original complex row. Each occurring system is realized in both, and two belong to the same block precisely when their coordinate differences lie in the maximal ideal at the fixed place. This gives, for every later block, a preliminary block of the same $j,w$ and a bijection of their point sets. The two realizations use the same automorphic representations, local parameter lists and archimedean tests under this transport; the defining existence of a distinguished part therefore identifies their theta loci even when several automorphic classes give one Hecke point. Comparing the common tuple in rank one on split Frobenius elements gives equality of $\chi_1$; the cyclotomic formula gives all the other characters. The absolute Galois group, its inertia and wild inertia are those of the same host. [Matching complex and residual systems][points] · [Matching the theta loci both ways][theta] · [Complete comparison data][ext]

Finally, if a later block is live, its matched preliminary block is live. Take any preliminary full integral model at the point corresponding to the live witness and its stable-lattice complement. The full traces agree under the DVR inclusion by Chebotarev. Subtract the common character traces and reduce. Over the later residue field the preliminary complement then spans the full matrix algebra by the trace-pairing argument above, since the later witness does. Equality of dimension descends this spanning condition to the preliminary residue field. Thus the preliminary live-block construction can make the root choices needed for **every** later live block. The initial realization is fixed before this final finite-root request is made. The [wild-inertia construction](FIRST_PASS_WILD_INERTIA.md) uses this transport in order to make a coefficient request before the final realization is chosen.

# The saturated starting state before localization

Fix an admissible untwisted first-pass column in the eventual range of the construction, with its prescribed base, frame and coherent twisting data. Write $L$ for its coefficient field, $L_0\subseteq L$ for the fixed intermediate field, and $v$ for a finite place of $L$. Under the [saturated fine-datum construction](UNTWISTED_AUXILIARY_PRIMES.md), the [lower-row laws](LOWER_ROW_ASSEMBLY.md) and the [rational tests on the actual weight-supported family](COMPOSITE_ROW_RATIONALITY.md#rational-tests-on-the-actual-weight-supported-column), there is a starting state on this column.

* Its distinguished set $W$ of coefficient places is the prescribed $W_1(F_0)$ of its row realization. If $v\in W$ and $v'|_{L_0}=v|_{L_0}$, then $v'\in W$. The passage to the starting state preserves this set.
* Its auxiliary localization index set is empty, every input $\eta_j(v)$ to the stage is zero, and its separate division parameter is $\eta_F=6x^{-C_F}$. The real and finite type actions and the two levels of row maps are identified with those of the chosen column.
* It has an actual division lattice, a spectral stage with the specified formal degrees and volume scale, the original volume stage, and a rational row reading with the congruence comparison deferred. [Starting assertion and construction][start] · [Definition of the state][st] · [Saturation relation][satrel]

## Restrict the given datum before taking its row package

Choose the fine realization $z$ on a true-group and true-volume stage $Z_0$ by the saturation theorem. Set $W=z.W_1(F_0)$. This is a union of fibers over primes of $\mathcal O_{L_0}$. The reference-index conditions descend from the seed field by the lattice index base-change calculation, while the forced-content condition is constant over those fibers. The test for $v|_{L_0}=v'|_{L_0}$ is equality of the primes where elements of $\mathcal O_{L_0}$ have positive valuation. Saturation does not assert that all primes of $L$ above the same rational prime belong to $W$. The set $W$ is stable under conjugation and lies above the allowed rational residue characteristics. Restrict $z$ to the tuples determined by $W$. The restriction preserves $W_1(F_0)$, the exact analytic readings, geometry, core and standard data. The weight result therefore applies on that very restriction, giving the full row laws there. [Saturated witness][sat] · [Restriction and use of row laws][restrict]

Choose a row family for this restricted datum. The inverse to its tuple injection on its image identifies the reference tuple. The definite-form coordinates at both levels, the lower-form coordinates, and the compatible realizations give the row representations, their embedding maps, and the period map on those same forms. The row datum with the prescribed tuples supplies the numerical weights and the Galois-cohomological length data used in the next step. Thus the resulting package $P$ has $P.Z_0=Z_0$, $P.W_t=W$, and it records both the datum and its row maps. These two equalities transfer saturation itself to $P$. [Row-family construction][pkg] · [Exact data stored][pre]

## Install a finite-group action with empty localization index

Set $\eta_j(v)=0$ for every $j,v$ and use the rows of $P$ with these parameters. The old group stage is needed here because the good-type idempotent on the actual rows may act nontrivially. For each of the two type primes $g_k$, index the finite general linear group factors by the actual places of the ground field above $g_k$, and use the lifts prescribed by the frame. The auxiliary list of localizations is empty. The cohomological length inputs are retained. In particular, choosing the empty list has no effect on the set of type places, the actual row tuples, or $W$. [True layer and its labels][true] · [Length input][sha]

Identify the product of the finite general linear groups on the old and new stages by their pinned places and residue-field identifications. The latter have prime residue fields, so two compatible ways to read a matrix in the same residue field give the identical matrix. The coefficients of the good-type projector consequently agree under this group isomorphism because both stages use the same Green series idempotents and the same permitted tuples of labels. Pull a central group-algebra element back along the isomorphism by permuting its coefficients, and use the *given* central actions on the upper and intermediate row spaces. At the good-type element these actions equal the prescribed projectors. It suffices to check the identity on the spanning images of the rational form coordinates, where the existing Hecke action calculation gives it. The individual projector coefficients still lie in the designated copy of $L_0$. This yields a true group stage $N$, identifies its central action for **every** standard reading of the finite groups, and permits its auxiliary twisting index to be empty as well. The identities indexed by the localization list now have no instances; the tie to the row corrections reads $\eta_j(v)=\sum_{\varnothing}0=0$. ## The division and spectral stages on these rows

Use the rational coordinate space $L^d$ of the prescribed upper row basis $c_1,\ldots,c_d$ and its evaluation $j_E(x)=\sum_i \iota(x_i)c_i$. At each finite coefficient place $v$ let $M_v$ be the $\mathcal O_L$-span of the rational coordinates whose image lies in the selected integral row set at $\mu(v)$. The division module on this side is
$$
 \{x\in L^d:\ \text{for every }v\text{ there exists }a_v\in\mathcal O_L
                  \setminus\mathfrak p_v\text{ with }a_vx\in M_v\}.
$$
This is the intersection in the fixed coordinate space of the localized membership conditions. Multiplying two witnesses proves closure under addition. The lattice on a word $u$ is the inverse image of the specified level-zero word lattice under inclusion of its ramified subspace. Use the frames for the actual norm at the distinguished complex place and the given invertible upper-row frames at the other infinite places. On the word subspaces use the restricted original metrics. Set each word height to zero, take $\eta_F=6x^{-C_F}$, and choose $HH=s_{\rm frame}+\mu_{\max}(RR)$ with the indicated word modules and heights. These choices give the precise division comparison on this stage. [Division module][divlat] · [Frames, word modules and parameters][div] · [Initial choices][start]

The condition required of the stored bilinear map $B_K$ is conditional. If all complex division pairings on these rational coordinates lie in $\iota(L)$, then each has a unique preimage. Bilinearity of the preimages follows from bilinearity over $\mathbf C$ and injectivity of $\iota$. Use this map when the condition holds and the zero map otherwise. This proves the implication required of the starting state. A proof that every division pairing actually takes values in the field requires the arithmetic reading of those pairings. [Definition of the map][bk] · [Exact rationality predicate][rat]

For the spectral stage the pinned columns have positive-definite Hermitian class-sum forms. The upper row dimension is at most the ambient multiplicity dimension $D_{\rm all}$. Its coordinate embedding is injective and its range is the image of the good isotypic part under tensor contraction, so the dimension is bounded by the full multiplicity space. The defining dimension relation treats $D_{\rm all}=0$ as well. These are the two rank and positivity conditions required of the row family. Use the code on words already specified by the scale, with gap $\Delta=\lceil x^{c_D}\rceil$, the positive formal degree of its Harish-Chandra parameter at each real place, and the Haar scale of $Z_0.G$. At each finite ground-field prime use the canonical CM local algebra with involution, adding the selected split chart at the two type primes. This produces $S$ with the indicated local isomorphism at every finite place, including after assembly with the other stages. The last property is stronger than the bare existence of $S$ used at this step. ## Recover the readings on the assembled state

Take the tuple of stages $(\eta=0,V,S,N,G=Z_0.G)$. The division comparison, true group, marked-place identification and compatibility of its central action are exactly the four fitting conditions. Transport the old core and real row data using the common group and column. This carries the tuple map, characters, representation representatives, the two levels of coordinate embeddings, and the intermediate lift maps to the assembled stage. In particular its exceptional-place calculation agrees with the old one for every choice of the forced input. On the second level the finite group center acts by the same pullback. The two rational row-coordinate systems and metrics supply the level-zero side. Reading the row and lift coordinates on this data recovers the chosen representative up to isomorphism and recovers the specified embeddings at both levels. This proves existence of the promised state with this *particular* choice of $V,S,N,G$. [Four fitting conditions][fits] · [Transport and coherence][move] · [Second level and rational row reading][readside] · [Pinned readings][read] · [Final assembly][finish]

The zero assigned to $\eta$ is an input to the assembled stages. The transported congruence lengths $\eta^{\rm actual}_j(v)$ agree with the **old actual lengths**. The full congruence reading additionally asks for equality of these lengths with the stage inputs on $1\le j\le m$. That equality is a later obligation. Likewise, the absence of new finite Hecke twisting places in the first-pass square-product formula concerns places of the CM host. The set $W$ and these congruence tests concern primes of the coefficient field. The source's eventual theorem has as inputs the fine datum and its field extension, the lower and restricted weight laws, and their stated representation-theoretic hypotheses. After those are supplied, this construction uses the existing unitary Weil representation, existence and Harish-Chandra finiteness for discrete series, and cusp rapid decay to obtain the row readings. The formal wrapper discharges the field-extension supplier from the discrete-series cover, Harish-Chandra modules, rapid decay, Casselman–Wallach, the unitary Weil representation, discrete-series existence and Harish-Chandra finiteness, leaving the two standing input packages in its theorem statement. Its endpoint is the starting state used by the third stage.

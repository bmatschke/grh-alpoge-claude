Two reading editions of *A human-readable version of the formal Alpöge–Claude proof of RH and Dirichlet GRH*.

- **Overview** follows the argument from the divisibility of theta derivatives to the exclusion of zeros off the critical line.
- **Detailed edition** gives the underlying calculations and identifies the proof-specific arguments that still rely on other accounts. Its coverage statement describes how to read the citations.
- **TeX sources** contains the standalone inputs for typesetting both editions.

1. Outcome and explicit status
-----------------------------

This component proves the actual finite/real normalization of the adelic
unitary quotient and its definite interpretation as the inverse-stabilizer
genus mass. It then uses ONE NEW NAMED CLASSICAL INPUT, the Tamagawa-number
theorem for nontrivial unitary groups with the exact measure in GV.17, to
prove actual signature independence and VolumeEqMass₂. All conversion
constants are calculated, rather than absorbed into an unspecified scalar.
The claimed geometric volume results are conditional on that established
theorem; its proof has NOT been reconstructed. GV14 additionally applies
the number-field Hasse–Minkowski theorem already explicitly retained by
MF4. DiscreteSeriesExist and FormalDegreeFormula₂ remain explicit inputs
to GV20–GV21 and have not been proved by a volume calculation.

The strongest geometric endpoint is the following. For a CM quadratic
extension E/F unramified at every finite place, a nondegenerate Hermitian
space W with an actual full self-dual lattice L, and any signature function
correctly describing the form, the source's normalized Haar measure has
fundamental-domain mass

  M_0 = 1,
  M_n = 2 |disc F|^(n²/2) A_n^(-[F:Q])
                    product_{j=1}^n L_F(j,epsilon^j), n>0,
  A_n = (2 pi)^(n(n+1)/2) / product_{j=0}^{n-1} j!.

Here the finite lattice stabilizer has mass one and each real factor is
EXACTLY the supplied CDHaar, whose Omega normalization is derived. The
formula applies to mixed signatures at different real places as well as
constant signatures. It therefore implies the literal source VolSigIndep.
In the positive standard space a direct geometric count shows M_n=massI(n).
For a record D supplied with VolTie, this proves both clauses of the literal
VolumeEqMass₂. It does not constrain an arbitrary positive function D.vol
that has no tie to the quotient.

For the source's original baseVol and G.withTrue stages, the needed tie is
now proved, rather than assumed: trueVol has its positive existential
branch inhabited and equals M_n on every valid HSpace. Thus the exact
VolTieG field holds for every assembly of the remaining stages over the
same B,C and the same G. The trueScale/CdScaleIs argument is independent
of the Tamagawa theorem. With the still explicit discrete-series and
formal-degree inputs it supplies the complete RealTied record. The final
V4.Core statement is conditional on its other same-object layout,
arithmetic, row, numerical and forced-prime fields; it does not turn the
bare empty stage models into a Core.

2. Why this is not the MF mass ratio under a new name
----------------------------------------------------

MF19 proved a genus mass ratio from Hasse–Minkowski, local densities and a
weighted representation induction. That result alone gives no volume of
an indefinite quotient and no meaning to an arbitrary stored D.vol.

Here the adelic group, completed lattice, compact open stabilizer, real
normalization, rational discrete subgroup and measurable fundamental
domain are first constructed. The actual gauge measure is then compared
to the EXACT source normalization at every place. The named classical
Tamagawa theorem is only invoked after the conversion is proved. The
definite class-weight identity is separately proved by unfolding a finite
weighted characteristic function. Consequently the two sides of the
geometric equality refer to their actual original objects.

The source proves finiteness through arithmetic class sets, real
reduction/drift, and adelic covering; its subsequent volume comparison
uses the Siegel–Weil/fiber or signature-independence route. This component
uses a shorter alternative argument from the named classical Tamagawa
theorem. It does NOT claim that those source reduction-theoretic or
Siegel–Weil proofs have been internalized, and does NOT use their desired
volume conclusions as intermediate assumptions.

3. Precise external theorem and references
------------------------------------------

GV-TAM (retained, established, not reconstructed): Let E/F be any CM
quadratic extension and W a nonzero nondegenerate Hermitian E-space of
rank n. A rational invariant top differential omega on U(W), with local
additive normalization O_{F_v} of mass one at finite v and ordinary
Lebesgue measure at real v, defines Tamagawa measure

  |disc F|^(-n²/2) L_F(1,epsilon)^(-1)
      product_real |omega|_v product_finite L_v(1,epsilon)|omega|_v.

The usual quadratic L-value at 1 is positive and finite, the normalized
adelic measure exists, and its rational quotient has total mass 2.
The nonzero-rank condition is explicit. The trivial rank-zero group is
handled directly and has mass 1, not 2. At j=1 epsilon is nontrivial;
there is no zeta_F(1) factor. For every even j occurring below, j>=2.

Primary source inspected: Michael Harris, Jian-Shu Li, Christopher M.
Skinner, “p-Adic L-Functions for Unitary Shimura Varieties I: Construction
of the Eisenstein Measure,” Documenta Mathematica, Extra Volume Coates
(2006), 393–464, section 0.2, printed page 400. Equation (0.2.1) gives
the exact discriminant/L-value/local-gauge convention; the following
sentence states Tamagawa number 2; equation (0.2.2) gives the smooth
hyperspecial local factor. The paper's totally real E and CM K are our
F and E. Its G=U_E(V) has dimension n² over the totally real field.
Publisher text: https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/27474
DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/DMS/4/12

Classical treatment: André Weil, Adeles and Algebraic Groups, Progress in
Mathematics 23, Birkhäuser (1982), Chapter IV, section 4.4 “The Tamagawa
number (hermitian and quaternionic cases).” Publisher record:
https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4684-9156-2
Only the theorem, with the primary paper's explicit normalization, is
imported. This report does not claim to have reconstructed Weil's proof.

GV-HM (inherited retained input): the number-field Hasse–Minkowski
isotropy theorem, as explicitly recorded with authoritative references
in MF4. GV14 applies it to q(x)-s t². This is a new reduction/application
of the same named theorem, not a claim that its proof is now supplied.

Ordinary background used throughout consists of number-field/Dedekind
module algebra, the product formula, completions and residue fields,
weak approximation of finitely many coordinates, smooth/analytic local
coordinates over real fields and complete DVRs, Haar uniqueness and
Tonelli, finite-dimensional change of variables, the Gaussian sphere-area
formula, and the convergent j>=2 Euler products. DR1 supplies the unramified
quadratic and dyadic norm/trace facts, rather than treating division by
two as legitimate in a residue ring.

4. Claim-by-claim mathematical audit
-----------------------------------

GV1 — Actual group and unimodularity.
The source tensor algebra and HA are identified, using a finite F-basis
of E, with the restricted product of the local quadratic algebras and
their unitary groups. Matrix equations and the formula for the inverse
give a topological group identification, including the infinite/finite
split. Local compactness and second countability follow from these actual
coordinates and the countable family of exceptional finite sets. The
adjoint determinant is one after splitting, because conjugation on M_n
has determinant (det g)^n(det g^-1)^n. This proves bi-invariance of Haar
and invariance under inner conjugation. No quotient finiteness is used.

GV2 — Algebraic latHat and compact open stabilizer.
The source AddSubgroup.closure is the subgroup generated algebraically.
Using finitely many O_F-generators of L identifies it exactly with all
local completed lattice vectors, since independent local integral
coefficients assemble into integral finite adeles. Compactness is a
finite-generator image argument; openness uses a global denominator.
The stabilizer is a product of compact open local lattice stabilizers,
standard outside a finite set. A projective lattice has local bases;
global freeness is never introduced. Both directions of StabHat are
preserved, rather than a one-sided inclusion masquerading as equality.

GV3 — Literal IsVolMeasF, with all choices quantified.
The finite Haar normalization gives K_L mass one, and the real Haar
factors are the supplied cd.cd after actual signature matrices. A second
matrix differs by a real unitary left factor, so inner invariance proves
the required equality for EVERY such matrix. The infinite embedding
extension is uniquely determined by the real-coordinate idempotents
and its prescribed restriction to E. Thus each AllSome has an actual
witness and its universal clause. Haar uniqueness and a positive finite
rectangle establish uniqueness of the whole normalized measure.

GV4 — Genuine rational fundamental domains.
Rational entries cannot be simultaneously integral at all finite places
and arbitrarily small at all infinite places, except at zero. This gives
rational discreteness. The rational subgroup is countable. A countable
small-open-set selector constructs a Borel fundamental domain. Nonnegative
orbit sums give independence of its Haar mass and positivity. No compact
or finite-covolume fundamental domain is assumed. Finiteness comes later.

GV5 — Exact Omega and compact-dual factor.
For each diagonal coordinate the map (X,Y)->X+iY has determinant 1;
for each off-diagonal pair its four-real-coordinate determinant is 4.
Thus Omega Lebesgue is 2^b dX, not dX. The total coordinate-gauge volume
of U(n) is v_n=2^n pi^a/product j!, proved by the first-column sphere
fibration with a tangent-coordinate Jacobian of one and compatible
fiber gauge. This is not an unjustified quotient-metric formula.
Multiplication X->J_{p,q}X complexifies to a modulus-one determinant
on all complex matrices and preserves the EXACT Omega normalization.
The source's exponential small-ball limit identifies the Lie density
of its Haar measure. Compact probability plus CDHaar.trick therefore
forces cd.cd(p,q) to have density A_n^-1 times Omega Lebesgue, where
A_n=2^b v_n. Both real constants, not just the compact total volume,
are needed for the final conversion.

GV6 — Rational gauge and determinant orientation.
Fix delta anti-invariant and nonzero. The basis consists of n diagonal
delta directions and b off-diagonal pairs 1,delta. The tangent map JX
identifies the unitary Lie algebra with skew-Hermitian matrices; it need
not preserve brackets. The map Y->B*YB has determinant Norm(det B)^n,
proved after splitting the quadratic algebra. Hence a finite isometry
B*JB=I contributes |det J|^n to the GAUGE MASS of the stabilizer. At a
real place the map is A^{-*}YA^{-1}, also yielding |det J|^n in the
gauge measure. The rational delta basis contributes t_tau^-a, with
a=n(n+1)/2. These signs/directions are fixed before multiplying places.

GV7 — Integral Lie lattice and the dyadic correction.
Every unramified quadratic algebra has an anti-invariant UNIT kappa;
at p=2 the residue trace gives it. Diagonal trace-zero coordinates
therefore have index determinant |delta²|^-1/2. Off-diagonal integral
coordinates have determinant |2|^-1|delta²|^-1/2, obtained from the
unit relative discriminant compared with discr(1,delta)=4delta².
Their product is d_v=|2|^-b|delta²|^-a/2.
The source's unramified condition is indispensable here. At ramified
places this exact determinant formula would require extra local factors.
The unitary lifting map X+X* is onto the Hermitian error space because
the residue trace is onto. This works at dyadic places with no division
by two. The kernel dimension is n², and local analytic coordinates have
unit Jacobian. This proves the local gauge MEASURE of each first
congruence coset is q^-n² as well as its successive finite cardinalities.

GV8 — Finite group order and actual local factor.
Split GL_n counts and nonsplit norm-one-vector counts give
q^-n² |U_n(k)|=product_{j=1}^n(1-epsilon^j q^-j).
The argument reuses MF6's direct finite-field counts. DR1 and the split
conjugate-inverse-transpose basis give a local isometry carrying the
standard lattice to the supplied self-dual L_v. GV6 then yields the
factor |det J|^n d_v times the displayed finite product. There is no
unexplained Haar scalar and no global integral basis assumption.

GV9 — Product formula and complete conversion.
Multiply the calculated finite and real factors in the exact Tamagawa
normalization. The det J factors cancel by the product formula. The
delta² factors cancel independently. The finite product of |2|^-b is
2^(bf), combining v_n^f with 2^(bf) to give A_n^f. At each finite v,
L_v(1,epsilon) cancels the j=1 factor BEFORE the infinite product is
taken. What remains is a finite product of absolutely convergent
j>=2 Euler products. Therefore

   d tau_J = C_n d mu_{W,L},
   C_n = |disc F|^(-n²/2) A_n^f /
                       product_{j=1}^n L_F(j,epsilon^j).

This identity is proved for all signatures and self-dual lattices.
It does not presuppose equality of quotient volumes. The finite
nonzero L(1,epsilon) normalizer is supplied by GV-TAM, whose numerical
convention is explicitly stated. No zeta(1) is involved.

For arbitrary CM extensions, even with ramification and without a

GV11 — Direct definite quotient-to-mass proof.
For positive definite W, K=G_infinity K_L has mass one. Adelic lattice
intersection gives the actual class correspondence; rational-coordinate
approximation inside open transporters turns completed bases into
rational local modules, to which LP1 applies. MF3 supplies equality
with the literal rational-local source genus. The TL1 trace-form
argument generalizes verbatim to any positive definite W and proves
finiteness of the class set and each automorphism group. The rational
stabilizer of g_iK is exactly Aut(L_i). The weighted function
Phi=sum_i 1_{g_iK}/|Aut(L_i)| has rational orbit sum 1. Tonelli on an
actual fundamental domain gives its mass exactly equal to the genus
mass. This proof does not use GV-TAM or MF's mass-ratio theorem.

GV12 — Full VolIndep without Tamagawa.
Two self-dual lattices in the same rational space are locally unitary
isometric; their finite adelic stabilizers are conjugate. Bi-invariance
and normalized Haar uniqueness give the SAME measure for both lattice
choices. A rational isometry gives literal matrix conjugation with
phi(g)T=Tg, preserves the rational subgroup, and transports every
normalized measure and fundamental domain in both directions. The
third clause of VolIndep uses the explicitly supplied VolTie to equate
stored real volumes. No isometry between distinct real signatures is
inferred, and no Tamagawa theorem is needed for these three clauses.

{\Large\bfseries Why the weighted theta and Eisenstein series agree}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.75 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The reproducing formula of the preceding edition leaves an arithmetic
question. The sum over unitary group elements must equal a finite sum
of products of theta series, with the given stabilizer weights on
both sides. At zero elliptic argument this reduces to an identity
of definite Siegel--Weil type. Its proof compares a lattice with its
neighbors at one split prime. On a bounded family the comparison is a
strict contraction, so the difference must vanish. The chapters below
give the finite calculation, the analytic bounds and the return to
the differentiated kernel.

2. THE ACTUAL GLOBAL LATTICES

Let O=O_E, V=E^n, h(x,y)=conjugate(x)^t y, and N=N# be the source's
full selfdual lattice. All global sums and ideal products below are
integral O-submodules inside the same rational V. Localization M_P
means O_(P) M inside V, rather than its completion.

The up lattice is literally

  A = N + P^{-1} x,
  upN = A# + conjugate(P) A,

where x is the exact quotient representative chosen by the source,
x belongs to N, and f_P(x)=1. P^{-1} is the invertible fractional
ideal {a in E : aP subset O}; it is not a symbol for an arbitrary
local inverse uniformizer lattice.

The down lattice is literally

  K = {x in N : f_P(x)=0},
  dnN = K + P K#.

These are the definitions in StdLatUnimodular.lean. The source proves
their properties using glue_unimodular. NB1-NB3 replace that portion
by a direct local duality calculation on these same global formulas.
This is a substantive simplification, not a replacement of the
source objects by unnamed lattices with the desired ranks.

For every full lattice M, the correct formula is

  (M#)_P = {y : h(x,y) is integral at P for all x in M_conjugate(P)}.

Conjugation moves the first variable's local ring to the second
variable's ring. It must never be treated as an automorphism of O_(P)
when P and conjugate(P) differ. NB1 proves the displayed formula by
clearing finitely many pairings of global generators. Perfect local
pairing gives biduality, and LP1 passes equality at all finite primes
back to equality of integral global lattices.
Equality at one prime also gives the literal SameAt witnesses: clear
outside-prime denominators on a finite generating set, multiply them
to get a single scalar carrying the first lattice into the second,
and repeat in the reverse direction. Thus the source's uniform global
scalar formulation is proved, not only its local-equality consequence.

For upN the complete local table is:

  at P:                N_P + q^{-1} O_(P) x;
  at conjugate(P):     (A#)_conjugate(P);
  at every other R:    N_R.

The two exceptional entries are paired Hermitian duals, so upN is
selfdual. It is full, upN is contained in A, and conjugate(P) A is
contained in upN. This gives every clause of upN_spec. In particular,
off-prime equality is genuine equality of the localized modules.

For dnN the corresponding table is:

  at P:                ker(f_P : N_P -> k_P);
  at conjugate(P):     (K#)_conjugate(P);
  at every other R:    N_R.

Again the exceptional entries are paired duals, so dnN is selfdual.
It is full because P N is contained in K and K is contained in N.
This proves every clause of dnN_spec.

The local indices are exactly one residue dimension:
upN_P/N_P is k_P and N_P/dnN_P is k_P. These statements are local.
Neither upN nor dnN is asserted to contain or be contained in N
globally; the conjugate-prime modification compensates for the
change at P.

3. RESIDUE MAPS AND VALIDITY, INCLUDING THE FALLBACK DEFINITIONS

The actual up residue is

  f_P^+(y) = f_P(qy) for y in upN_P, and 0 outside.

Write y=n+q^{-1}a x with n in N_P, a in O_(P). Then f_P^+(y) is
the residue of a. Thus the new kernel at P is exactly N_P, and
the map is nonzero. A denominator outside P puts q^{-1}x into the
global up lattice and keeps its residue nonzero. This avoids a
hidden assumption that q^{-1}x itself belongs globally to upN.

For the down move the input is a particular nonzero functional
g:dnN/P dnN -> k_P. Its extension to dnN_P is

  f_P^-(y) = rho_P(s)^{-1} g([sy]),
  s outside P and sy in dnN.

Common denominators prove independence. The formula is additive,
O-linear through the exact residue map, zero off its local domain,
and nonzero on the global lattice. This identifies the literal
TDat.extF with modP.ext. No scalar adjustment is made to g.

Every other type function and every auxiliary reduction map is
unchanged as a function on the ambient rational space. Equality of
the local domains preserves all their local laws. Global witnesses
for nonzero maps and surjectivity are recovered by denominators
outside their own prime and inverse residues. For auxiliary kernels,
x in the new lattice with zero residue lies locally in R times that
lattice. If s*x lies globally in R times it, with s outside R, use
ts=1 mod R and x=t(sx)+(1-ts)x to prove global kernel membership.
This verifies the whole Deco.Ok predicate, including its integral
kernel condition rather than only rank or local surjectivity.

The source's DecoSet and PtY.mv definitions contain fallback branches.
NB2-NB4 prove that these branches are not used for the actual inputs.
The new decoration is valid. Every twist frame is still a basis
because its localized lattice did not change. The coupling set is
still valid because its definition uses only unchanged auxiliary and
twist localizations and fixed powers of their uniformizers. Therefore
the actual point's new lattice and functional are exactly those
described above, with exactly the old frames and coupling subset.

4. KERNEL LATTICES, HERMITIAN DUALS AND THE EXACT PREFERRED MATRIX

Let L be the simultaneous kernel of the type functions on N and H=L#.
At P, L_P is ker(f_P); at every R outside the type set L_R=N_R.
Multiply by one element of each other type prime, chosen outside P,
to prove the first statement. This is legitimate finite denominator
clearing, not localization of an uncontrolled infinite intersection.
Since conjugate(P) is absent from the type set, H_P=N_P.

Consequently the up decoration satisfies

  L^+_P = H_P,
  H^+_P = (H + O q^{-1}x)_P,

and the down decoration satisfies

  H^-_P = L_P.

These are the actual lattices_at_up and dn_locP_lat conclusions.

After taking the class of a raw neighbor, the source picks a preferred
representative r(c') and a particular moving group element g0, with
g0*r(c')=raw neighbor. NB5 always uses gamma=gam(g0), that exact
rational matrix. A finite group component acts only on finite
decorations; the type functional is pulled back only by gamma.
Thus gamma maps N, L and H of the preferred representative to their
raw counterparts, with no extra type-character scalar. Unitarity
commutes with the Hermitian dual, so the same gamma proves all the
lattice and functional clauses.

For an up input z in H with fv(pr z)=1, use the source's chosen
Quotient.out(pr z)=x0 in N. FC5 proves z-x0 in P H. Since q=T is
a uniformizer, q^{-1}(z-x0) belongs to H_P. Hence the up dual-lattice
formula with x0 is exactly the formula with the user-visible z.
This is an integral localized span identity, not just equality over E.

For the residue equality on x in new H, with q*gamma*x in old H,
choose one s outside P putting x in the new N and q*gamma*x in old N
after multiplication. The literal updated functional proves equality
on those global elements; the exact FC5 projections and cancellation
of rho_P(s) give the full identity.

For down, define Kglobal=L+P H. Its localization at P equals dnN_P,
but it need not equal dnN globally. The source projection prk has
domain Kglobal and target dnN/P dnN. If gamma*x lies in Kglobal,
choose s outside P with sx in new N. The actual moving element then
puts s*gamma*x in the actual global dnN. The prescribed g and FC5's
IdL identities give equality after multiplying by rho_P(s), which
can be cancelled. This proves dn_level_functional without silently
replacing Kglobal by dnN.

5. COUPLING LABELS BEFORE AND AFTER PREFERRED REPRESENTATIVES

Equality of the raw coupling subset is insufficient to conclude
that the source's preferred representatives have the same chart.
NB4 proves the stronger relation called SameG: same rational frames,
same coupling subset, same auxiliary local lattices, same auxiliary
reduction maps. Applying the same column-group element preserves
this relation. Therefore cosetIn agrees after every common action.

At the penultimate step the preferred choice normalizes cosetIn by
a fixed choice from the set of attainable orbit values. The old
point and its raw neighbor have identical such sets, hence the
same normalized value. At other steps coupling is empty. The
column index b also stays fixed. ReadsK.key uses exactly the pair
(b, normalized cosetIn), proving the actual up_keeps_chart and
dn_keeps_chart.

NB4 also proves pure rational-unitary coupling invariance with the
chosen auxiliary lift-frames, rather than assuming that an arbitrary
chosen lift is literally equivariant. If F and F' are old and new
auxiliary lifts, then B=F'^{-1} gamma F is integral invertible and
B=I mod R, because both frames lift the same transported reduction
basis. Coupling coordinates satisfy q_R F^{-1} y integral and the
actual rational prime q_R is a uniformizer at R. Hence
(B-I)F^{-1}y is integral. These coordinates have the same coset.
At twist primes the prescribed rational frames transform exactly,
so their coordinates agree before reduction at any congruence depth.
This proves the actual cosetIn_smul_unitary used for level reading.

6. THE SUPPORTED FACTORIZATION AND CHARACTER ORIENTATION

Under the retained full realization input, AD3 directly splices a
realizer of either actual neighbor into the old point at P and
conjugate(P). NB2-NB4 supply all required unchanged data. If the old
realizer is y_c*k and the supported right multiplier is epsilon,
set eta=k*epsilon*k^{-1}. Then y_c*eta*k realizes the raw neighbor.
Its projection to every other base-field place is the identity,
because every E-prime over that place lies outside the conjugate pair.

For the actual gamma above set

  kappa0 = y_c'^{-1} rat(gamma)^{-1} y_c eta.

Pure rational unitary invariance and the preferred-label argument
show that the finite part of the moving element preserves the
required coupling label. ReadsK.lev therefore places kappa0 in the
actual chart level, and ReadsK.chr gives

  chi(kappa0) = sigma(chi_column(g0)).

With kappa=kappa0^{-1} the exact conclusions are

  rat(gamma) y_c' = y_c eta kappa,
  chi(kappa)^{-1} = sigma(chi_column(g0)).

This matches both UpNatTieG and DnNatTieG, including the inverse
character in the final transport formula. The source down proof
uses a reverse splice from the new preferred realizer. Our direct
splice is a shorter proof of its existential conclusion under the
same explicitly retained general realization supplier. It retains
the exact chosen gamma; eta and kappa need not be the same witnesses
as the source's particular proof term.

Finally IdY and the exact embedding relation iota_tau=sigma o j_tau
turn IsTr into the literal inverse-frame identity. The original
column transport is sigma(chi_column(g0))*h(gamma^{-1}s), and hence
chi(kappa)^{-1}*h(s'). No determinant correction, conjugation or
unspecified unit is inserted.

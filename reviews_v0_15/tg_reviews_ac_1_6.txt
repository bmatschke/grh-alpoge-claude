AC1 — BOUNDED ROOT-OF-UNITY CORRECTION
The finite subgroup argument produces a generator zeta of the actual roots
of unity in E, whose cardinal is Units.torsionOrder. The chosen embedding is
injective, so the distinct powers of its image of zeta exhaust the roots of
X^w−1 in the target field. The inverse product U^−1 is one such root. Choosing
q in [0,w) and adding q at a single index gives the root identity on zeta and
then on every power, including negative original exponents. The proof does
not replace the full MU condition by its necessary parity consequence. The
number-field finiteness argument using phi(h)^2 >= h/2 is consistent with SZ10.

AC2 — QUADRATIC-SUBFIELD NORM, INCLUDING THE 2-PRIMARY PART
The assumptions match the separate literal theorem: d>3 prime, a number field
E containing s with s^2=−d, and 4 dividing [E:Q]. No Galois or class-number
hypothesis is needed for this lemma. The basis 1,s proves the square criterion;
the d-adic exponent excludes −1,−2,−3 as squares in K=Q(s), and positivity plus
irrationality of sqrt(2) excludes 2. A nontrivial odd-order root would give a
primitive p-th root with p−1 <= 2, hence p=3, contradicting the nonsquareness of
−3. For 2-primary y with y^2 != 1, a power i satisfies i^2=−1. Set K1=K(i) and
v=N(E/K1)(y). If v had order at least eight, a power u would satisfy u^4=−1 and
(u−u^3)^2=2. Writing that square root in the K-basis 1,i makes either 2 or −2 a
square in K, impossible. Thus v is one of ±1,±i, each of norm one to K. The
case y=±1 uses the EVEN degree [E:K], obtained exactly from 4|[E:Q]. Transitivity
then gives the result. For arbitrary z with killing exponent 2^a o, o odd,
N(z)^o=1 and the odd-order argument gives N(z)=1. This is a substantive proof
of the host norm property; HostDer.norm_mu is not a premise of the reasoning.

AC3 — ACTUAL CM-TYPE PRODUCT AND PARALLEL MUQ
HostRaw.galois and HostRaw.gal, together with Frame.natCard_GG, give [E:Q]=4n;
d congruent to7 modulo16 implies d>3. HostRaw.gal_sqd identifies the second
sign with the action on s. Since s != 0, the kernel of that sign is precisely
the automorphisms fixing K. The norm formula therefore uses exactly the source
Psi, and H.emb is phi0 composed with H.gal.symm. AC2 makes the product over
Psi equal to one on every root. Taking integer powers and multiplying MU
identities proves MU(a+c). The stated integral lift a+c supplies the literal
MUQ existential, rather than merely a rational numerical identity. The source
prod_cmType theorem itself accesses HostDer.norm_mu; the readable proof
correctly replaces that access by the newly proved AC2 and is not circular.

sign. HostVal gives a nonempty thick set; a single thick index tau0 may thus

two endpoint functions are word-independent. Finally h0=2h makes e_j=B+(2j+1)h

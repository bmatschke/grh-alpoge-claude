1. Result and scope
===================

construction left as AR-next-02. Given the actual global Artin map,
an algebraic Hecke character, a finite DVR over Z_l with its specified
adic topology, and compatible embeddings of its specified field of
values, the character is constructed in the actual DVR unit group.
It has the literal IsLAdicOf/IsGal normalization. The proof does not
assume a second published Hecke-to-adic existence theorem.

The two principal literal endpoints are:

* GlobalTheory.HeckeGal.IsGlobalArtin.exists_isLAdicOf, proved by GA4--6;
* GlobalTheory.GalChar.exists_isGal, proved by GA1--3, GA6--8.

2. Conventions and hypotheses
=============================

E is a number field, Omega its algebraic closure, G_E its absolute
Galois group with the Krull topology, and G_E^ab its topological
abelianization. The specified map Art:I_E -> G_E^ab has the literal
IsGlobalArtin properties: it is an open surjective quotient map;
its kernel is the closure of principal ideles times the connected
infinite component; it identifies local unit images with inertia,
local uniformizer images with inverse arithmetic Frobenius, and
the closure of local multiplicative images with decomposition.

These precise clauses matter. The inertia formula contains no
inverse. Arithmetic Frobenius gives the inverse of the unramified
Hecke value. A character into a commutative group is invariant under
conjugating the valuation/Frobenius pair. The finite/cofinite
Frobenius detection lemma used throughout is RC4, proved from these
Artin clauses and weak approximation; no Chebotarev statement is
quietly added.

Qbar_l is the algebraic closure of Q_l with its extended norm.
It is not C_p, and it is generally incomplete. The ring isomorphism
iota:Qbar_l -> C is not assumed continuous. Continuity in the
construction is obtained before or after an open-kernel argument,
never by composing an arbitrary continuous map with iota.

O is a domain DVR, finite as a Z_l-module. Its topology is the
specified maximal-ideal-adic topology, not an arbitrarily imposed
topology. Kappa:Frac(O)->Qbar_l agrees with the standard Q_l map on
Z_l. Write g for its restriction to O. The field K of Hecke values
is supplied with epsilon:K->C and f:K->Frac(O), with iota kappa f
=epsilon. AlmostIn requires these same images at cofinitely many
finite places. The proof neither chooses unrelated images for each
Frobenius nor replaces K by an unannounced extension.

For the host applications C is the actual HFrame from HF, all e(j,tau)
are integral, and the actual host supplies the CM involution and its
unramified quadratic valuation law. Complete splitting of the two
type primes is retained wherever needed; it is what changes the
residue-cardinality exponent q-1 to g_k-1. The source's row rank is
r=k+m-1, and nV=r+t. Both k,m are even and t is odd, hence r is odd.

3. GA1--GA3: actual DVR integrality, closed image and topology
============================================================

For a in O, finite generation over Z_l gives a monic polynomial
P(a)=0 with coefficients in Z_l. If |g(a)|>1, the leading term
has norm |g(a)|^deg P. Every other term has strictly smaller norm
because its coefficient has norm at most one. In a nonarchimedean
field a sum with exactly one term of maximal norm cannot be zero:
if that term were the negative sum of the rest, the ultrametric
inequality would contradict its larger norm. Thus |g(a)|<=1.
The same assertion for a unit and its inverse proves unit norm one.

The given compatibility maps l to the usual l-adic element of norm
1/l. Thus l is a nonzero nonunit of O. If pi is a uniformizer,
l=u*pi^e with e>=1. Unit norm one implies
|g(pi)|=(1/l)^(1/e), strictly between zero and one.
Every nonzero fraction is pi^a times a unit for an integer a.
Its norm is |g(pi)|^a, so its valuation sign is exactly detected
by the norm. This proves both directions of (GA.3), including
reflection of membership in O and O^times.

For closedness, take finite Z_l-generators s_i of O. Every nonzero
b=ub*pi^a divides l^a: use the multiplier ub^(-1)*u^a*pi^((e-1)a).
Hence any a0/b is l^(-a) times an integral element. Its image is
in the Q_l-span of g(s_i). The reverse inclusion holds because the
fraction field image contains all Q_l scalars and all g(s_i).
Thus the field image is a finite-dimensional Q_l-vector subspace.

A finite-dimensional subspace of any normed Q_l-space is complete
and closed. One proof uses coordinates: the induced norm on a finite
basis is bounded above by a multiple of the sup norm; the lower
bound is a positive minimum on the coordinate unit sphere. That
sphere is compact because Z_l is compact. Linear independence
excludes a zero minimum. A Cauchy sequence thus has Cauchy
coordinates, which converge in complete Q_l. This explicitly
explains why no completeness of Qbar_l is being assumed.

The same norm detects the topology. If c=|g(pi)|<1,
pi^a O maps into the closed norm ball of radius c^a.
Conversely |g(x)|<c^a implies |kappa(x/pi^a)|<1, so x/pi^a
lies in O. These inclusions show equality of neighborhood filters
at zero; translation gives a topological embedding everywhere.
The proof uses the actual IsAdic property to identify pi^a O as
a neighborhood basis.

The coefficient-map variant is useful when O' is not a DVR or
domain. It is enough that O' is local and adic and that the image
of l lies in its maximal ideal. A unital ring map t sends the unit u
to a unit, and t(pi)^(ea)=t(u)^(-a)t(l)^a lies in m(O')^a.
Hence t(pi^(ea)O) is contained in m(O')^a. This proves continuity
directly. No unexplained extension of kappa to O' is needed in
the toric quotient construction.

4. GA4--GA6: construction from the Artin quotient
=================================================

The infinite factor F_inf multiplies j(x_v) to the prescribed
integer exponent n(j|E), for all continuous embeddings of each
infinite completion. The l-adic factor F_l does the same for
continuous embeddings E_v->Qbar_l, with the exponent indexed by
iota j|E. Only the finitely many v dividing l contribute.

Finiteness of each embedding set follows by restriction to the
dense number field, which is injective, and finiteness of the
number of field embeddings. A continuous map at a place not above
l cannot exist: a positive power of its residue rational prime
converges to zero locally but always has l-adic norm one after
the map.

Every embedding E->Qbar_l induces a place over l. It takes all
integers to integral elements by the same monic-polynomial argument
as GA1. Its nontrivial valuation is that place up to positive
scaling, so it is continuous in the place topology. Its image
generates a finite extension of Q_l. That extension is complete,
as explained in GA2, and therefore the map uniquely extends from
E to E_v. The place is recovered from the valuation, and density
gives uniqueness of the extension. This proves the required
bijection between embeddings of completions and embeddings of E.
At infinity, the corresponding extensions to R or C give the
same statement, with two continuous embeddings of each complex
completion. On principal ideles the two products are therefore
the same product over embeddings, transported by iota.

The IsAlgPos condition is initially only an equality at totally
positive principal infinite values. It becomes an equality on the
entire connected component as follows. E^times is dense in the
infinite unit group by weak approximation. The connected component
is open, so the intersection of this dense subgroup with it is
dense in that component. Points of the intersection are totally
positive, exactly as required. Two continuous maps agreeing there
agree on the component.

The remainder theta=chi/F_inf is continuous and trivial on that
component. Its finite-idele restriction has open kernel.
For explicitness choose an image neighborhood |z-1|<1/2 and an
open congruence subgroup H of finite ideles lying in its preimage.
The subgroup can be given by both u-1 and u^(-1)-1 lying in
N times the integral finite adeles. Multiplication is closed
because (1+Na)(1+Nb)=1+N(a+b+Nab), and the inverse condition
is symmetric. All powers of an element of H are still in H.

If all positive powers of a complex number z satisfy |z^a-1|<1/2,
then z=1: put S_a=sum_{h<a} z^h. The bound |S_a-a|<=a/2 implies
|S_a|>=a/2, whereas |(z-1)S_a|=|z^a-1|<1/2. For z!=1 the
two bounds contradict each other at large a. This proves H is
killed. Combining it with the open infinite connected component
gives an open kernel in the whole idele group.

It is at this point, and only at this point, that the algebraic
isomorphism iota^(-1) is applied to theta. A homomorphism with
open kernel is locally constant, so its composite with any
homomorphism remains continuous. Multiplication by continuous
F_l gives Psi=iota^(-1)(chi/F_inf)*F_l.

Psi kills principal ideles because chi is one there and the two
algebraic products cancel. It kills the connected infinite ideles
because theta and F_l are one there. Its kernel is closed in a
Hausdorff group. It therefore kills the closure in the literal
Artin kernel. The open quotient property supplies a continuous
character of G_E^ab. Pulling back to G_E proves IsLAdicOf exactly.
Surjectivity of Art means that any other such character has the
same value on every Galois element, proving uniqueness.

This is substantive construction from global reciprocity.
HeckeLAdicCharacter is not retained as a separate primitive input.

5. GA7--GA10: local identities, descent, finite order, conjugation
================================================================

Evaluating the global formula on a local finite idele gives
r(sigma)=iota^(-1)(chi_v(x))*locPow(x). The correction locPow
vanishes if v is not over l, or if n=0. The Artin inertia clause
then identifies the image of the whole inertia group with the
image of the entire local unit group. It follows that unramifiedness,
exponents and finite orders are preserved. To pass from the local
multiplicative group to the whole decomposition group, use exactly
the source's closure clause and continuity. The closed kernel
contains the closure if it contains the local image.

For Frobenius the inverse matters: Art of a uniformizer is the
abelianization of sigma^(-1). Taking the inverse in the character
gives r(sigma)=iota^(-1)(chi(P)^(-1)). This is compatible with
the direct local unit formula, which has no inverse.

For coefficient descent form L=kappa(Frac O), already proved
closed. L^times is a closed subgroup of Qbar_l^times. The quotient
is a Hausdorff commutative topological group, so RC4 applies.
If chi(P)=epsilon(x_P), then at every good Frobenius the value
lies in kappa f(K)^times, hence in L^times. Thus the quotient
character kills cofinally many Frobenii and is identically one.
Every Galois value belongs to the field image.

Compactness of G_E now matters. Norms of the character values are
bounded by continuity and compactness. A value of norm greater
than one would have unbounded powers, and a value of norm less
than one has inverse of norm greater than one. All norms are
one. GA1 puts every preimage in O^times. Multiplicativity and
uniqueness follow by injectivity of kappa. GA3 restores continuity
in the actual topology. This proves exists_isGal for the same
algebraic type n, not merely some unspecified finite-order avatar.

For finite-order Hecke characters, an mth root in each connected
infinite factor proves IsAlgPos with type zero. Hence their adic
realizations are m-torsion directly from the global formula.
The finite root set has one isolated from its other points,
so the kernel is open. Conversely a continuous m-torsion Galois
character descends to G_E^ab, composes with Art, and transfers
through iota. Its open kernel supplies continuity. It kills
principals and is the unique finite-order Hecke character read
this way. The proof includes the l-adic places because type zero
removes the local algebraic correction there.

For anticyclotomic symmetry use the actual host involution on E
and its supplied extension to Omega. Its action by conjugation
on G_E is continuous: to fix a finite field after conjugation
it suffices to fix the finite field generated by the inverse
conjugates of a basis. At conjugate unramified primes P,Pbar the
Hecke values multiply to one. This follows by evaluating the
restriction to F on a uniformizer and using the source's unramified
quadratic valuation law. At a conjugation-fixed prime this same
argument gives value one; it is not replaced by a false assumption
that every prime splits.

Conjugation sends a Frobenius at P to a Frobenius at Pbar with
the same norm. The product character r(conj sigma)*r(sigma) thus
kills all Frobenii outside a finite conjugation-stable exceptional
set. RC4 makes it trivial on all G_E. Injectivity then gives the
same anticyclotomic identity in O^times. These are the exact
IsGal.anti and host local hypotheses; arbitrary ramified quadratic
extensions are not substituted.

6. GA11--GA15: odd blocks and the installed first character
=========================================================

The host CM type lists one embedding from each conjugate pair.
The pairs exhaust all embeddings, since two embeddings define
the same infinite place exactly when they agree or are conjugate,
and the host is totally complex.

If z!=0, (z/|z|)^(2a)=z^a*conj(z)^(-a). An even argument
exponent is therefore an integral algebraic type. In the actual
mu0 record the argument exponent is -2e, so the type at a pair
is (-e,e). Eta has argument exponent one. For r=2h+1,

  (z/|z|)^(-1) * |z|^(-r)
       = z^(-h-1) * conj(z)^(-h).

The norm factor at a complex place has two embeddings; forgetting
that multiplicity would give the wrong half-integral exponents.
The result supplies integral type for eta^(-1)|.|^(-r/2).
It also applies to the odd t in the shift character.

At a finite unramified place the block value is
mu(P)*eta(P)^(-1)*N(P)^(r/2), with the positive square root
under the real-to-complex convention of the source.
If eta(P)*N(P)^(-1/2)=epsilon(z) and mu(P)=epsilon(y),
then the value is epsilon(y*z^(-1)*N(P)^h) when r=2h+1.
All factors now lie in the same K. The actual ValLL0 inputs
therefore give AlmostIn for both shift and block. Product and
inverse operations only enlarge a finite exceptional set.

For each existing NTw index and side, a Reading includes the
finite DVR embedding kappa, the algebraic complex isomorphism iota,
compatibility on Z_l, and the exact LL0 square. Apply exists_isGal
to that square. The construction comes after this index and
reading; it creates neither of them. This ordering is essential
for the distinction from SideB.cover.

Reading independence uses only common integral values. If
rho_C(y)=emb_LL0(x), then y=embSide(x) in the fraction field,
by injectivity. A second reading sends y to the same complex
value. Build a block realization for the second reading.
At cofinally many unramified primes the inverse Hecke value
belongs to LL0, so both integral characters have the same
Frobenius values in the actual O0. Their continuity and RC4
give equality everywhere. The tame generator statement similarly
uses its lift to a local unit and the two identical local formulas.

For the installed first character, NormalizedCharacterCompatibility
already contains a cofinite Read identity. In its exact notation
the Hecke value alpha is that of mu0*delta*eta^(-1), and the
coefficient value is alpha^(-1)*sqrt(N(P))^(-r).
The positive square-root identity says this is precisely the inverse
value of block(mu0*delta,eta,r). The three retained equality fields
eta_eq, mu_eq and del_eq must be used on the actual D and frame.
The reading gives equality with the newly constructed realization
at Frobenius for the selected valuation. Conjugating valuations
and Frobenii is harmless only because the target is commutative;
the exact norm stays fixed. Thus RC4 applies to every valuation,
and the installed character is the constructed one.

This supplies the literal firstTwistCharacter_eq_ladic_heckeCharacter.
It does not construct NormalizedCharacterCompatibility. A character
with the same order or same ramification support would not suffice
to identify the installed one.

7. GA16--GA18: both sides, exact inertia order and tame calibration
=================================================================

The two base embeddings read epsilon and its complex conjugate.
For the false side use conjugate(iota) together with the second
embedding; their two conjugations cancel. Both sides therefore
read the same Hecke block, with their exact respective embeddings.

Select pde' only when l=pde, otherwise pde. The source's distinctness
conditions make the selected prime different from l and both type
primes. HF12 gives the finite-order delta character with exact
inertial exponent 15 there. GA8 constructs its integral realization.
GA7 transfers the exponent exactly, and a subgroup of roots of
unity in a field is cyclic. Hence cardinality is exactly 15, not
merely at most 15. Off the auxiliary support delta is unramified.
The selected rational prime is 1 mod 15; every residue norm over
it is a power of it and also 1 mod 15.

At an auxiliary place all mu0 factors and eta are trivial on local
units. Thus chi_j,true on inertia is Delta. On the false side the
reading is conjugated; finite-order values satisfy conj(z)=z^(-1),
so chi_j,false on inertia is Delta^(-1). This explains the side
sign rather than guessing an inverse from the Frobenius formula.

Tameness of a local Hecke character gives exponent q-1 on units,
because the residue units have order q-1. The converse wild-order
statement used for toric quotient characters is more precise.
Continuity first gives an open kernel on local units: take a
small congruence subgroup mapped into |z-1|<1/2 and use the
all-powers no-small-subgroup argument of GA5.
Hensel lifts each nonzero residue to a (q-1)st root of unity:
the polynomial derivative is a unit. After dividing by that
root, a unit lies in 1+m. Each qth power raises its additive
depth at least one, by the geometric-sum formula. Once the depth
lies in the character's open kernel, its image has order dividing
q^a. The proof works at residue characteristic two as well.

For Galois tame generators, a continuous character with inertial
exponent m prime to the residue characteristic kills wild inertia.
This reduces to a finite quotient: a neighborhood of one can be
chosen to exclude the finitely many nontrivial mth roots, and an
open normal subgroup can be chosen inside that neighborhood.
The finite ramification argument of AR3 applies. By the literal
definition of TameGenAt, inertia is the closure of the subgroup
generated by the chosen element and wild inertia. The image
therefore lies in the finite cyclic group generated by its value
on the chosen element.

For the uniform calibration fix the actual tame generator at a
type place. Its Artin local-unit lift has residue a in F_p^times.
HF's local rule evaluates mu0,j as omega(a)^(-2(j+1)).
The row j=0 is available since m>=2. Realize mu0,0 adically
at the different prime pde; this uses the general Artin construction,
not a second coefficient-field containment assumption.
Its inertia has exponent p-1. Comparing the given generator with
the inertia lift of each residue b shows b^2 belongs to <a^2>.
Thus a^2 generates all squares.

To recover a full generator, write a=z^d in a cyclic group of order
n=p-1. The square condition says gcd(2d,n)=gcd(2,n). If n is
odd, gcd(d,n)=1. If n is even and a is not already a generator,
gcd(d,n)=2 and n/2 is odd; multiplying a by the unique element
of order two gives a generator without changing its square.
This also covers the n=1 case.

The source's omega and the specified Teichmuller map are injective
on F_p^times, so both images are the full n-th roots of unity.
Transport the selected generator between those images.
The resulting gamma has the exact formula (GA.24) simultaneously
for all coefficient primes different from p, all allowed rings,
all readings, every row, and every unramified extra factor.
This order of quantifiers is the main content of the tame supplier.
It produces a new TameTie array. Identifying that array with an
already fixed nd.chiT remains a separate compatibility premise.

8. GA19--GA21: quotient, localization and exact coefficient transport
====================================================================

For the toric quotient character, construct the realization of
mu0*delta*vartheta^(-1) using its integral type and actual value
field. It is anticyclotomic because every factor is trivial on F.
Map it to any target adic local coefficient ring by GA3. Outside
the stipulated S0, all factors are unramified and the prime is
away from l. At an auxiliary prime the quotient by the selected
side of Delta is read as vartheta_P^(-1) on local units.
If vartheta kills the local roots of unity of order q-1, GA17
shows each such quotient value has q-power order. This is the
literal existential exponent; it is not a statement of uniform
bounded order or triviality.

The integral X(P) is defined by evaluating the O-valued character
at a chosen Frobenius and then mapping to O'. Equation (GA.11)
makes this independent of valuation and Frobenius at P outside S0.
It yields an actual ring element, not just a complex value.

For localization, IsPsi(l,Q) is a genuine supplied Hecke character
with l-power order, triviality on F, tameness, exact ramification Q,
trivial entire local character above l, and split support.
There is no construction here for arbitrary prescribed nonempty Q.
The prescribed separation makes the mu0 and delta factors trivial
on units at every P in Q. Hence the block realization has
nontrivial inertia at every such P: otherwise the exact inertia
image identity would make psi unramified there.

The family is chi_c=chi_1*cyc_l^(-(c-1)). This is an integer
exponent, so the source defines it for every natural c, even zero.
At c=1 the extra factor is one. Cyclotomic continuity follows
from its Z_l-valued continuity and the actual compatible embedding
of Z_l into O. At Frobenius away from l its value is N(P):
Frobenius acts by N(P) on residues of l-power roots of unity;
reduction is injective on these roots because their polynomial
has unit derivative. Thus

  iota kappa(chi_c(Frob_P))
    =(mu0*delta*psi*eta^(-1))(P)^(-1)
       * N(P)^(-r/2-(c-1)).

The source writes -r/2 as (t-1)/2+(1-nV)/2. The exact nV=r+t
identity verifies that equality. No global chain length is changed.
The psi_l factor has exact ramification Q, open kernel, l-power
order, anticyclotomic symmetry, and triviality of the entire
decomposition groups above l; the last property uses the full
local triviality field atl, not merely unramifiedness.

For coefficient transport, the two readings need not agree on
the whole coefficient fraction field. They must agree through
the supplied K and through the literal integral relation:
if y in O0 equals f0(x), then t(y) equals f(x) in Frac O.
Cofinitely many first-block inverse values lie in K. At those
Frobenii, this exact relation permits transport, and multiplication
of Hecke characters then gives the intended product. Continuity
and RC4 promote the equality to all Galois elements.
This proves chi=t_*(chi1)*psi_l, with the same l, rather than
asserting it merely from two unrelated complex embeddings.

9. GA22--GA23: local reading upgrade and precise stage boundary
=============================================================

has cofinite normalized readings in LL0. The actual twist_2 map
and local embK map transport those integral values into the
target DVR. The actual Sp.iq and AutSpec.iotaQ identities make

Hecke values cofinitely in the target field, so exists_isGal

argument. It does not announce construction of unused fields.

package; an abstract isomorphic tuple cannot silently replace it.

================

The reproducible builder reads only pinned Git objects and checks
any present checkout file against its Git blob. Each source record
includes its full-file SHA-256, inclusive range SHA-256, exact
declaration, byte count and pinned URL. It separately inventories
the source files and the current TeX paragraph hashes.

The arithmetic identities were checked directly:

* coefficient uniformizer norm c with l=u*pi^e, e>=1;
* denominator b=u_b*pi^a divides l^a;
* finite-idele geometric-sum no-small-subgroup bound;
* infinity exponents (-e,e) and (-h-1,-h);
* block field value y*z^(-1)*N(P)^h;
* Frobenius inverse and positive local-unit sign;
* side inversion only from conjugation of finite-order values;
* exact cyclic inertia order 15;
* tame square-generator factor-of-two case;
* localization exponent -r/2-(c-1);
* nV=r+t and the literal normalized expression in the source;
* coefficient agreement only on integral values from K;
* true-side coverage quantifiers and the empty-local replacement.

No PDF or Lean compilation was performed. Independent review files,
when present and binding the current paragraph hashes, are attached
by the builder. Any retained published input is explicitly marked;
no RH/GRH conclusion is inferred from these conditional suppliers.

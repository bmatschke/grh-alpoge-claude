# The host arithmetic for the bundle at a prescribed size

Fix the original weighted point $q$ and the reciprocity record $R$ obtained on its division lattice. Write $D=q.w.D(q.Z)$ and $T=R.T.\mathrm{toTiedDataS}$. The host datum required by CSR20 can be constructed on this particular $T$, with its two distinguished primes equal to the ones already selected on $q$. The same host datum works for every subsequent real size parameter $c$. This discharges the class-field-theoretic premise that CSR20 kept explicit. ## The ideal representatives and the field inside the chosen coefficients

Write $E$ for the CM host field and $K$ for the subfield denoted by $D.\mathrm{host}.K$ in the source. The row tuple pin identifies the active places $w$ by $\mu_w\ne\mu_0$. This is a finite set. For each active $w$ the conductor primes $Q_w$ on $q.O.\mathrm{ex}.\mathrm{nch}$ are exactly those of its prescribed twist. No $v\in Q_w$ lies above a word level prime $T_u$. Indeed the twist ramification condition excludes the rational primes in the word-level list, and a finite prime cannot lie over two different rational primes. The row datum itself gives $T_u\ne g_i$ for the two distinguished level primes. These statements apply to the prime data that enter $R$. [Tuple and conductor identifications][pins] · [Prime exclusion][avoid]

In $\mathcal O_K$, form the nonzero integral ideal
$$
 \mathfrak m=\prod_u(T_u)\prod_{\mathfrak p\in S}\mathfrak p,
 \qquad S=\{ v\cap\mathcal O_K:\ w\text{ active},\ v\in Q_w\}.
$$
Both products are finite. For each ideal class $d$, choose a nonzero integral ideal $J$ representing $d^{-1}$. For every prime $\mathfrak p\mid\mathfrak m$, choose a nonzero residue in $J/\mathfrak pJ$. The Chinese remainder theorem on the invertible ideal $J$ gives a single $a\in J$ with these residues. If the modulus is the unit ideal choose any nonzero $a\in J$. Then $I_d=(a)J^{-1}$ is integral, has class $d$, and its valuation at each $\mathfrak p\mid\mathfrak m$ is zero. This constructs one system of representatives simultaneously coprime to every word level and every contracted conductor prime. It works also with an empty active set. [Coprime representatives in a prescribed class][cop] · [The actual modulus and its two uses][reps]

Use the unit finite modulus in the CM field $E$ and the actual distinguished complex embedding $\phi_0$. Let $M\subset\mathbf C$ be its ray class field. The CM field has no real places. The unit-modulus field is therefore its Hilbert class field. The unit modulus divides the chosen modulus of the base, and the latter ray field is contained in the specified intermediate coefficient field. Every integral element of $M$ thus has a preimage in the base's specified integral coefficient ring, by its fullness condition, in the prescribed embedding. [Earlier ray-class construction](COMMON_DESIGN_EXISTENCE.md) · [Integral coefficient preimages][ray]

The host frame of $R$ specifies $E\xrightarrow{j}\overline{\mathbf Q}\xrightarrow{\iota}\mathbf C$ with $\iota j=\phi_0$. Take $H_E=\iota^{-1}(M)$ in that fixed algebraic closure. Global reciprocity makes $H_E/j(E)$ abelian and unramified at every finite prime. The relative discriminant formula gives $|d_{H_E}|=|d_E|^{[H_E:j(E)]}$. Conversely a finite abelian extension inside this closure satisfying the same equality has relative discriminant of norm one. It is unramified and is contained in the unit-modulus class field. This gives the maximality clause of the source. To check $H_E\subset K_s^{\overline{\mathbf Q}}$, with $K_s^{\overline{\mathbf Q}}$ the coefficient subfield specified by $T$, first take an algebraic integer $x\in H_E$. The integral preimage for $\iota x$, the coefficient-range equality of $T$, and injectivity of $\iota$ put $x$ in this precise subfield. Every $x\in H_E$ has a nonzero integer multiple integral over $\mathbf Z$, so the inclusion follows for the full field. The full coefficient field of $T$ is Galois over $\mathbf Q$ by the chosen identification with the Galois base coefficient field. [Ray-field maximality in the fixed closure][hilb] · [Coefficient inclusion and the Galois identification][finish]

Put $HD.d3b=q.x.\mathrm{pin}.d3b$, using its pair of primes as given. Together with the representatives, field, containments and exclusions just proved, this fills every field of $HD:\mathrm{HostDatumC}(T)$. In particular, for either of the distinguished primes, the selection map has the prescribed contraction to $K$. For each used tuple the standard kernel's field identification sends its set of primes to the original $I.Q(\mu)$, and the stored equality of ranges identifies their chosen lifts with the image of $q.\mathrm{core}.a.Pq$. This supplies CSR20's prime comparisons once the corresponding selected row and eigensystem data have been formed. ## Which choices depend on the size

The extension hypothesis in R73P5 also has a direct witness for the source's final parameter. Its arithmetic extension is $\mathrm{NEa}(\mathrm{NEo},A)$. Projecting successively to the first coordinate forgets the extra condition $A$, the origin witness, and the character list, producing $\mathrm{NEb}$ for the **same** context, levels, row, character choice and theta data. This works on the whole quantified input of CSR22. [Extra condition][exta] · [Origin witness][exto] · [List and underlying arithmetic extension][extl] · [Parameter in the final construction][final]

For fixed $q$, the prescribed integer pair $(\Delta,h_0)$, when it exists, is unique. Its entries satisfy $D.s.\mathrm{Del}=\Delta$ and $q.O.L.\mathrm{etaSz}=m h_0$ with $m\ge2$. Injectivity of the integer embeddings and cancellation of $m$ give uniqueness. Only the scale update depends on $c$ in the following comparison. For a fixed row family and fixed rational subspace $Z$, write $z$ for the numerical scale computed with those two integers. The updated entry is $z_c.l6m(j)=z.l6m(j)-c\,z.l6u$ and the updated $l6u$ is zero. Thus its change between $c_1$ and $c_2$ is $(c_1-c_2)z.l6u$. The host, its distinguished primes and the reciprocity record were all fixed before this update. [Rank bound][rank] · [Scale and exact seven-field bundle][size]

The remaining pointwise comparison must realize the updated division and period data and identify its eigenspaces, including the logarithmic eigenvalue at each coefficient embedding. R73P5 supplies the nonzero-part support on an actual weighted point. Its argument specifies the separate inputs still needed for the selection; the host and extension inputs have now been discharged above. Once the resulting $\mathrm{RealTB}$ is identified on the new face, periods and scale, the bundle constructor takes $R$ as its first field, giving $b.R=R$ by definition. Uniform existence of the weighted points is a further part of the design. [Support and selection](continuation_v73/reading.tex#L135-L202) · [Assembly at the requested size][assemble]

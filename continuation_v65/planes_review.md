# Independent review of the exceptional rank-four support

Reviewed manuscript SHA-256: `54531dcb1ff1f8808459e437438afbc632bd38a3cfb3f3bdcdcc19a76dc00744`.

At formal commit `7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4` I compared the three arguments with the quantified YCoin, TorusY and FibY declarations and the implementations in PYCoin, PTorusY and PFibY. The chapter's reading index identifies 27 original ranges in 11 files.

1. In Y65P1 the two nonzero projections of the first flag vector are preserved by the block decomposition and have the same eigencharacter on the entire local group. Each restricted plane is symplectic, so its determinant is the common multiplier. The paired outside flag characters have this same product. The two possible kernel equalities cover the group, and the union-of-two-subgroups argument selects a single equality there. This works in every characteristic and only requires the one nonzero flag entry at the chosen place.
2. In Y65P2 the spectral projectors belong to the subfield-linear algebra spanned by the group, using diagonal normalization and the four distinct marked values. The trace identity with two projectors recovers a product of opposite entries even when the group elements differ. One scalar per plane, chosen before the test subfield, recovers the opposite entries. The assumed nonzero entry in the other direction on each plane then permits recovery of the remaining entries. All four original entry hypotheses have been accounted for.
3. In Y65P3 the marked differences are nonzero in the residue field and hence are units in the given representing ring. Their images at the tested generic point remain distinct. The four lower entries per flag generate the two omitted entries by symplecticity, with signs as in PFibY. The relative algebraic closure argument gives the *relative transcendence degree* of the image field of A over the trace base. It does not identify that field with the fraction field of the completed ring. The local universal property identifies that ring with the completion of A at the residual point. The prime quotient is bounded by the completion modulo the extended contracted prime, and the dimension formula with the same closed residue field gives exactly the additive term two plus four times the number of places. The argument uses the original space, the original prime, and the stated nonvanishing of all four weights.

These prove the three auxiliary results used in the rank-four component argument; the exception cannot be discarded when estimating primes that lie in the two-plane support.

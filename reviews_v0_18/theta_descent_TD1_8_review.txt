Mathematical findings

TD1: The test matrix is over the exact embedded field, and its K-injectivity
follows from separation and f-injectivity. A K-left inverse extends after
sigma and recovers all complex coordinates. No density or continuity is
used. The zero-dimensional case is valid.

TD2: The actual kernel equals the kernel of the finite test matrix because
the tests separate the target. Gaussian elimination survives an injective
field embedding, so the actual rational preimage spans the complex kernel.
This uses membership in the fixed sigma(K), not merely algebraicity.

TD3: The supplied positive Hermitian product on E is now explicit. This was
the only requested clarification; it is already a hypothesis of the literal
source. The nonzero-source Gram identity proves sigma(a*)=conj(sigma(a));
the zero-source case is separate. With the conjugate-first convention,
<j b_i,Theta* e_a>=conj(psi_a(Theta j b_i)), which is exactly the value
needed in sigma(K). Inverting the rational Gram matrix is legitimate
because its complex image is positive definite. The Riesz vectors span E,
so the conclusion concerns the actual adjoint image and its actual
rational preimage, not an unrelated space of the same dimension.

TD4: Image-adjoint equals kernel-perpendicular in finite dimension. The
adjoint of each operator, rather than the operator alone, must preserve
the kernel; the stated adjoint closure supplies precisely this. Rational
operator extension through the common basis agrees with j on every vector.
No additional self-adjointness is being assumed.

TD5: Group multiplication and equivariance extend from the spanning form.
Unitarity extends separately in the linear and conjugate-linear variables.
Using the inverse group element after adjointing yields rho(g)Theta*=
Theta*tau(g). The group-algebra identity is then a direct finite linear
sum of these equalities, with unchanged coefficients on both sides. This
avoids the potential double-conjugation error. All five Hyp fields match.

TD6: Rationality of the idempotent range is an image-of-spanning-set identity.
Its intersection with the adjoint image is obtained by applying P to a
rational spanning expression, since P fixes its own image and preserves
that adjoint image. Downward replacement u'=Pp uses both invariant
subspaces and the orthogonal decomposition u=p+k. P need not be orthogonal.
The proof agrees with the literal ratLev and dn statements.

TD7: The projector coefficient is chi(h)^(-1); reindexing gives character
chi, not its inverse. Product with the actual central character idempotent
is idempotent and cuts out the intersection. The finite-group character
orthogonality/absolute-irreducibility premise is explicitly retained as
classical input. The manuscript correctly preserves the source convention
lvl uses psi and lvlA uses psi inverse. Upward and type statements follow
from the actual intertwining identities. Principal level gives 1.

TD8: The common image E0 is proved by explicit preimages: Theta*e lies in
the actual coordinate image and division by the positive c_(t,p) maps back
to e. No inference from equal dimensions is used. In the literal source the
spectral pieces are EEp(p) intersect EEl(b), which are exactly the pieces
inside E0 used here. Injectivity on image Theta* proves Theta*Theta x=c x
when Theta x is in the relevant part. The Gram identity has the precise

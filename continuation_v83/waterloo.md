## The arithmetic period calculation

Let $k=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-d})$, let $E/k$ be the CM extension, and let $M$ contain the images of all the embeddings $\tau:E\to\mathbf C$ above one fixed embedding of $k$. For a nonzero fractional $E$-ideal $c$, let $U$ be the $\mathcal O_M$-span of the simultaneous embedding vectors of $c$. With $D$ the relative different, the $\tau$-axis of this lattice is

$$\{z:ze_\tau\in U\}=\tau(cD)\mathcal O_M.$$

Indeed, the trace-dual ideal is $c^{-1}D^{-1}$. Over every localization of $\mathcal O_k$ select trace-dual bases from the two modules. Their embedding vectors stay dual for the coordinate pairing over each localization of $\mathcal O_M$ above it. Thus the condition on $ze_\tau$ says exactly that its product with every $\tau(c^{-1}D^{-1})$ is integral. Inverting the extended ideal gives the identity.

In the specified host, $E$ is the compositum of $k$ and a subfield of a cyclotomic extension of conductor supported on $2q$. Away from those primes unramifiedness survives base change. Both primes split in $k$, by $d\equiv7\bmod 8$ and $-d\equiv1\bmod q$. Therefore conjugation exchanges every prime dividing $D$ with a distinct one of the same multiplicity. Pick one prime power per pair for an ideal $b$ with $D=b\bar b$. For $c=b^{-1}$ the integral period line is $I=\tau(\bar b)\mathcal O_M$.

The index $[I:z\mathcal O_M]$ is $|N_{M/\mathbf Q}(z)|/N_M(I)$. Twice its logarithm cancels the $z$-dependent term in the sum of the logarithmic squared metrics, by the product formula. The tower discriminant formula gives $[E:\mathbf Q]^{-1}\log N_E(D)=\log(\mathrm{rd}_E/\sqrt d)$. The corrected mean on the line thus equals the mean of $\log(A_\rho/\pi)$ minus this logarithm. Its further evaluation by the CM formula uses the specified area and period normalization. This check matches `CoeIdealDifferentNeZero.lean:25` (`lineR_eq`), `Period.lean:39` (`br_meanLogOm_eq`), `MeanLogPeriodConjugatesEqChowlaSelberg.lean:202` and `MeanLogPeriodChowlaSelberg.lean:17`. The article supplies the whole ideal calculation, and explicitly isolates the remaining identification with the chosen theta kernel. It provides no independent closure of the family denominator or the project’s general GRH obligations.

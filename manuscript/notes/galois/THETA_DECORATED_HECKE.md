# Hecke neighbors and rational theta tests on a prescribed column

A definite unitary automorphic form at finite level can be evaluated on a Hermitian lattice with a finite marking. At a prime outside the markings, a spherical Hecke operator moves the lattice at that prime and sums the resulting values. This gives the ordinary and lift readings needed at the start of the theta construction. The local statement is classical. Greenberg and Voight identify Hecke cosets with lattice neighbors and describe their relative elementary divisors in both the split and nonsplit cases. The source uses all the dominant elementary-divisor labels at inert primes. [Greenberg–Voight, Theorem 4.1 and Propositions 4.3–4.4][cartan] · [Theorem 5.11 on elementary neighbors][neighbors] · [The two coefficient readings](../../../archive/rounds/v77/continuation_v77/reading.tex#L43-L161)

## The comparison with the marked level

Let $J$ be the compact open for the actual tuple and row, $v$ a permitted unmarked place, $K_v$ the stabilizer of its self-dual lattice and $t$ a unitary element supported at $v$. The level condition is a list of congruences on reductions at the marked places. Thus each $j\in J$ splits as its $v$-component in $K_v$ and a complementary component in $J$ commuting with every element supported at $v$. If $K_v t K_v=\coprod_a aK_v$, it follows that $JtJ=\coprod_a aJ$. Indeed the complementary component commutes past $t$, and equality of two of the latter cosets would imply $a^{-1}b\in J\cap G(F_v)=K_v$. The level character is trivial on $K_v$. Every right translate leaves the off-$v$ decorations unchanged. [Passage to full level](../../../archive/rounds/v78/continuation_v78/hecke.tex#L12-L38)

At a split pair $P,\bar P$, the group is locally $\mathrm{GL}_n$. With the orientation of the source, the neighbor contracts $k$ coordinates at $\bar P$ and enlarges their duals at $P$. Its inverse at $P$ therefore has determinant of valuation $k$. The possible contractions are the codimension-$k$ residue subspaces, and double cosets modulo the right stabilizer enumerate them once each. This includes the identity at $k=0$ and the empty sum at $k>n$. [Neighbor bijection and signs](../../../archive/rounds/v77/continuation_v77/reading.tex#L219-L286) · [Determinant of the inverse in the specified frame](../../../archive/rounds/v78/continuation_v78/hecke.tex#L40-L81)

At an inert prime $Q$ the extension of local fields is unramified quadratic and the lattice is unimodular, so the stabilizer is hyperspecial. In a hyperbolic frame a dominant Cartan label $(s_1,\ldots,s_d)$, $s_1\ge\cdots\ge s_d>0$, $2d\le n$, scales paired directions by $\varpi^{s_i}$ and $\varpi^{-s_i}$. For the translated lattice $N'$ and the starting lattice $N$ this means that each of $N/(N\cap N')$ and $N'/(N\cap N')$ has type $\bigoplus_i \mathcal O_E/Q^{s_i}$. Conversely these quotients recover the label: the difference between $\log_{\#k_Q}|M[Q^m]|$ and $\log_{\#k_Q}|M[Q^{m-1}]|$ counts the $s_i\ge m$. Cartan decomposition identifies a unique double coset with this label. Self-duality gives the paired negative exponents, and the off-$Q$ localizations and markings determine the rest of the global lattice. Hence $a\mapsto d(ya)$ enumerates the prescribed neighbors of the decorated lattice $d(y)$ for every adelic $y$. Greenberg–Voight's elementary-neighbor theorem treats $s_i=1$. Their Cartan theorem and this quotient calculation give the general case. The reconstruction includes an elementary verification for the unramified unitary group, also in residue characteristic two. [Cartan decomposition][cartan] · [Inert representatives and general labels](../../../archive/rounds/v78/continuation_v78/hecke.tex#L84-L208)

On either row its fixed coefficient map is additive. Applying it to this finite sum gives the exact comparison
$$
             j(T_t f)(y)=\sum_{a\in K_vtK_v/K_v}j(f)(ya).
$$
Each $a$ here denotes the supported representative chosen for its coset. The multiplicity is one for each marked neighbor, including when several neighbors represent the same rational isomorphism class. No volume factor enters the sum. The separate action at a marked place uses the inverse of the determinant character of the finite flag and the given reduction map. Together these yield both Hecke readings of the preliminary datum, for all twists of each row using its one coefficient map. This is the conclusion already proved in H78K6 and assumed in the older B77R4. [Finite flag action and assembly](../../../archive/rounds/v78/continuation_v78/hecke.tex#L210-L296)

## What follows at the fixed coefficient field

Take an admitted arithmetic stage $w.\mathrm{st}(Z)$, with its given field witness, ordered primes, group comparisons and volume normalization. The large-field request for the finitely many twist roots, Hecke splitting fields and Weber values precedes the choice of this column. Take its canonical geometric core with its section and adelic reading laws. Its field is the distinguished image $F_O=e(K_s)$, and the map $F_O\to L$ has image exactly $K_s$ in $L$. The character comparison at finite idèles is taken in this embedding. For the reference tuple the comparison field is $e(L)$ itself. [Field request](../../../archive/rounds/v77/continuation_v77/request.tex#L34-L106) · [Core field](../../../archive/rounds/v77/continuation_v77/coefficients.tex#L64-L116) · [Reference tuple](../../../archive/rounds/v77/continuation_v77/reference.tex#L40-L96) · [Canonical core at the specified primes][initial]

The group comparison in this application can be checked just by contracting primes. For the $k$-th marked rational prime, $k=0,1$, each native place $v$ must give a host prime whose contraction to $K$ is the prescribed $P_k$. The map from the indexed places to those host primes is then injective, since their norms distinguish $g_0$ from $g_1$ and the labels within a block are distinct. The two sets for each $k$ have the same cardinality by the stage splitting condition, so the map is bijective. The good projector is the sum, over the permitted tuples, of tensor products of the local series idempotents. The products for different tuples are orthogonal by the local factors at any index where they differ. Their sum is consequently a central idempotent. These are exactly the remaining group clauses. In particular the source's given `GrpTrue` implies the required group comparison by its prime condition on a valid column. On such a stage in the source's stable range, with the hypotheses of the [preliminary construction](../theta/BASE_THETA_CONSTRUCTION_STEP.md), the preceding Hecke comparisons give the arithmetic input to the [fixed-stage analytic construction](../theta/THETA_FIXED_STAGE_REALIZATION.md). Apply the eight map-existence assertions to each admissible weak realization as required, first at the preliminary choice and again after the geometric coordinates are installed. The [marked frame comparison](../theta/THETA_MARKED_FRAME_COMPARISON.md) gives the change of dictionary, Petersson definiteness and the correction of the finite character at those coordinates. Fix its phase to one at the distinguished place. Q77Z2 then constructs **one** fine record with both readings there. The stable-rank bound on that same record is $8fr+4\le n_V$. Q77Z1 and Q76Z1 give its integral cusp lattice, the unqualified rational lift test and the rational lift and lattice tests at each of the two levels. For each row and level one finite family of linear functionals separates the target and takes values in the prescribed coefficient image on every rational lifted input and every stored target basis vector. These quantifiers permit the test family to depend on the row and level. [Assembly on the prescribed stage][fine] · [From a fine record to its tests](../../../archive/rounds/v77/continuation_v77/column.tex#L3-L100) · [Exact tests and field quantifiers](../../../archive/rounds/v76/continuation_v76/column.tex#L3-L45)

In particular the Hecke comparison and the construction of tests *after the fine record is fixed* can both be reused. To apply this result to a whole family one must produce the stages to which it applies. The [first-pass proof](../first-pass/FIRST_PASS_COLUMN.md) provides the pinned untwisted starting column, and the [geometric construction](../theta/THETA_PINNED_COLUMNS.md) supplies a fine record on it given the group and volume identifications. The second pass requires a new column on the output of retwisting that retains its small fields, frame and prescribed primes. Prime and field requirements for the second pass must hold on this new base. The kernel, division, theta and export witnesses there must belong to the resulting arithmetic data. Finally the linked-family comparison asks for the realization at each prescribed size on the previously fixed reciprocity datum. The numerical scale, bases and theta readings must match those of this particular bundle. B74R4 supplies the comparison once such a linked selection exists. These are the remaining family requirements in `BASE_THETA_READINGS` and `BASE_THETA_RATIONAL_TESTS`.

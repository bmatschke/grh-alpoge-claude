Mathematical findings

AH9. Prime-to-p roots retain exact residue order; the primitive N-th root
therefore reduces to order N/p^v. The local unramified/Eisenstein argument gives
O_L=Z[zeta_N], including p=2. Testing residue homomorphisms on that generator
gives the exact inertia and arithmetic-Frobenius congruences. Descent to the raw
host includes inertia surjectivity, not merely restriction of a decomposition
automorphism. The finite Galois prime theorem is correctly stated and its local
unramified lifting mechanism identified. It is an algebraic number theory input,
with no RH-dependent analytic premise.

AH10–AH12. The specified cyclotomic embedding and art_spec imply surjectivity
and the correct fixed field. The actual s_d determines the CM-type sign, and
all embeddings commute with conjugation. The first-sign eigenvector argument
does not assume the stronger PsQ property of the specially constructed host.
At d, art_d and a CRT witness give exactly {1,iota_d} as inertia. Conjugation is
absent from ramified decomposition groups: d=7 mod16, e>=2 and the A-coordinate
force an even Frobenius exponent, whereas its first sign forces an odd one.
The ordinary and all three eta clauses have the correct split/inert directions.
The dyadic square lift s -> s+2^(a-1) corrects exactly the next bit for a>=3.

AH13–AH14. Relative different support lies over Qq, whose primes split in K.
Selecting primes by their contraction to K gives a conjugate half comaximal
with its conjugate and invariant under Gal(E/K). Trace covariance proves
different invariance even for automorphisms moving K pointwise. The source's
theorem named principal constructs an integral ideal, not a principal generator;
the human text preserves that distinction. The identity J*=D^{-1}J^{-1}, applied
to J=bar(b)^{-1}, yields exactly b^{-1} and both directions of the stated
conjugated-trace self-duality. No class-number-one assumption is inserted.

AH15. O_L=O_E[zeta_N] follows from O_L=Z[zeta_N]. Lagrange interpolation and the
unit-triangular trace-dual change of basis prove the monogenic relative different
formula. Subtraction leaves precisely the product over u outside U, with factor
[L:E]. The norm of 1-eta and the finite totient sum yield S(U); division by
[L:E][E:Q]=phi(N) gives log rd(E). I checked both prime-power and other cases
of AH.39 and the normalization directions explicitly.

AH16. CRT proves K_gcd=K_a K_b, so the least conductor divides every admissible
modulus. A proper subgroup has index at least two, giving 2S(U)>=log c1 and
[U_N:U]<=phi(c1)<=c1. The upper correction at 2 sums to exactly log2; q,d give
disjoint half-log corrections, as distinct odd primes occurring once outside Q.
For the lower bound, the two possible signs occur only at i=0,1; the exceptional
correction sums to 2^{-e}. All selected prime-power terms are distinct and
nontrivial because v>=e+2. The exact lower bound, upper bound and 4n<=rd(E)^2
match the source. Nontrivial inertia proves divisibility by 2 and d; unramified
E/F and the even number 2n of complex pairs give the signed discriminant-square
identity, not only equality of absolute values.

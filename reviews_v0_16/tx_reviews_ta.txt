Reviewed substance
==================
TA14: normal convergence of the canonical product, sign from Legendre, the one-simple-pole uniqueness argument, scaling and the addition formula. This analytic prelude is independent of the arithmetic coefficient assertion of PA15.
TA1: nonzero totalized Eisenstein sum forces discreteness; cyclic discriminant is zero; uniformization normalizes dx/(2y), so invariant and lattice uniqueness are exact.
TA2: after F=zV, P=W_s(F)V^-2, the cleared ODE has first-difference coefficients -4(n-2) versus 12, giving n+1 rather than a resonance. The degree-two initial coefficient removes the linear-exponential ambiguity, and s=-2[z^3]F.
TA3–4: the CM norm quotient has pole order at most 2N-2 and polynomial degree at most N-1. Descending pole subtraction puts all coefficients of Q in M. Coefficientwise embedding is used only for formal identities. The Gaussian transformation has c'-2u, and kappa=2u(alpha^2-N). The conjugated pole bound proves alpha Lambda' subset Lambda' before period cancellation; a nonzero period then forces kappa=0. No continuity of rho is used.
TA5–6: StarPt includes zero coordinates at non-origin points. Algebraic differential transport fixes the actual CM scalar. Output coordinates lie in M by Aut(C/M) invariance before taking a countable subfield. That subfield explicitly includes both input and output coordinates; no extension of arbitrary uncountable-field embeddings is claimed. Torsion surjectivity retains the all-coordinates hypothesis. The two ideal factorizations yield delta*(a/a')*B with B coprime to the prescribed 2dn.
TA7: Psi2=-wp'; the differential recurrence has the stated sign. Laurent-series division, rather than numerical evaluation at a torsion point, handles vanishing constant terms. Formal root recursion requires and retains a matched nonzero constant.
TA8–9: checked U_(2mu)(2mz)=K Psi_(2m), s(2m^2 z)=K^(m^2) Psi_(2m^2), and K(0)=-Psi_(m^2-1)(0)/Psi_(m^2+1)(0). The sign and exponential exponent agree with the literal U1 definition. The u-in-lattice branch gives an even-period translation. Original coefficient membership is supplied to galSg by the first power identity before it is used. The exact 2m division-point hypothesis is retained.
TA10: uses M=C only for the legitimate automorphism argument, with all torsion coordinates algebraic from PA14. The fixed field of Aut(C/Qbar) is Qbar. Thus the odd coefficient premise is supplied without invoking itself; PA15 is applied afterwards.
TA11–12: w and 1-w are complementary idempotents on the two-dimensional F2 torsion module, yielding the unique unkilled class. The 16-torsion choice t/8 and m=4 are consistent. Normalization removes the same scalar at the same half-point. Ordinary complex conjugation is treated separately from arbitrary embeddings; extension uses only the algebraic/countable coefficient field. All three literal use1 clauses and the source quantifier order are preserved.
TA13: leaves multidimensional StdOK/four-copy vacuum, indicator/covariance, Pins.iX and Later unresolved. No RH/GRH or full formal-verification claim is inferred.

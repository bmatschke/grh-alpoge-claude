MATHEMATICAL CHECKS

SE1–SE2: A complex-independent image of one K-basis gives a complex-linear
extension of every sigma-semilinear map on its span, then on a complement.
Dual coordinate functionals prove preservation of every K-independent
family. The joint finite-kernel construction uses an actual right inverse
on the image of N and p=1-rN; the decomposed identity complexifies and
proves equality of the complex common kernel with the scalar extension of
the rational kernel. Finiteness of the family is retained.

SE3–SE4: The metric convention is X^{-1}, so the restricted Gram matrix is
(X^{-1}E)^*(X^{-1}E). Taking B^*B equal to that Gram matrix and Y=B^{-1}
gives the exact required norm identity. Full column rank makes it positive
definite; the zero-dimensional case is harmless. The lattice intersection
is finite by Noetherianity and spans by clearing integral denominators.
No assertion of global O_K-freeness is used or inferred.

SE5–SE6: The local actions preserve the actual embedded lattice, hence its
K-span, so the finite norm operators descend. The Mu0/F_i(0) identification
and embedded basis give the actual Mram kernel, not an unrelated space of
the same dimension. The resulting restricted lattice is precisely ramL.
All fourteen DivStage fields are supplied, including both complex basis
properties, the exact nuIn image and commutation, norms, Dall and delP.
The auxiliary Bk, height and eta choices are explicitly permitted by the
record. Goodness includes the finite full lattice, not just invertible
metric frames. jEE_norm is at the stated complex embedding.

SE7–SE8: Evaluation on finitely many class representatives is injective
using the stated full-group equivariance, yielding finite-dimensional Mt.
The weighted class-sum Hermitian form is positive and definite on Mu0 and
then its ramified subspace. All five Fits properties follow. The row model
keeps the actual period spaces and pairings, while coordinate auxiliary
spaces, truncations, identity projections and zero scalars fill the other
fields. Bilinearity of the period pairing is kept distinct from Hermitian
sesquilinearity of the class metric. The corrected RowsRest audit lists
88 fields: 63 named data/proof fields and 25 instance fields. Their finite
complex-space/module instances are the standard ones for these explicit
coordinate spaces or the actual finite-dimensional subspace. This does
not supply MuIsW, RankTie, or an arbitrary external geometric realization.

SE13: The polynomial index is explicitly over each finite residue field,
so its degree-r monic family with nonzero constant term is finite. The
single supported idempotent at (X-1)^r sums to one. Since r>=2, that
polynomial is not squarefree; hence every good idempotent is zero. The
selected-prime family is nonempty, making each good-tuple product zero.
The adaptation changes exactly the stated zero operators, eta and Sha
scalar. Infinity.toNat=0 is correctly identified: no finiteness statement
for the Sha group is smuggled into this construction.

SE14: The Later join is conditional on NStage for the exact same adapted
row object W; it does not substitute an NStage for a dimensionally equal
object. Ordinary completions do not supply the stated Ainf/Bcris/BdR,
filtration, action and comparison structures. The zero-projector model
cannot imply a positive projector-rank estimate. The added empty-twist
obstruction is correct and explicitly local to the particular source model:
GoodTupleConditionOfIdempotentZero.lean 553–556 sets Tw=Empty, and 931–932
uses that nTw; DataNChoice.lean 14 requires an unconditional function from
every coefficient place to Tw. A coefficient prime above 2 exists. Even
when muAt is always mu0, ReadElsewhere has an off-support existential
quantifier for an i in Tw, so it cannot hold in this model. This is not a
claim that every Later or NStage is incompatible with arithmetic choices.

SE15–SE16: The signatures, extra negative coordinate and top positive
coordinate match SpacesNormHasFrame.lean. The hyperbolic vectors e+f and
(e-f)/2 have zero self-pairings and mutual pairing one, and p_z has the
stated norm with the correct sign. The added W_j signature (k+j-1,m-j)
and positive V_j complete the coordinate identities. Extension by zero
preserves the matching standard vector. Every pi_u is nonzero because its
norm is prime, so c(u)=pi_u0/pi_u has the prescribed norm and cancels to
pi_u0. This proves the literal two pins for the transported existing input.
The initial sized/oriented PreX, analytic windows and compatible Ok remain
explicit existence premises. The normalization equations do not supply
those premises.

## First target on the common theta construction

A single fixed stage now has its finite Hecke action, archimedean discrete series, normalized terminal pairing and compatible characters at the marked split primes. The first new issue is what happens when the coefficient field grows. The underlying Hermitian spaces still come from the same totally real and CM fields. A choice of isomorphic representations at each new embedding alone would leave their frames, coefficient evaluations and the norms of arithmetic vectors undetermined. [Fixed-stage construction](BASE_THETA_CONSTRUCTION_STEP.md) · [The two local signs](THETA_CHARACTER_SIGNS.md)

Fix the original arithmetic datum with its analytic realization and an enlargement satisfying the original host, frame, prime and core identifications. Write $L\hookrightarrow L'$ for the inclusion of coefficient fields under their distinguished complex embeddings. For every $\sigma':L'\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$, compare the new row with the old one at $\sigma'|_L$. Construct the new arithmetic realization on the original lattices and marked level. Show that it gives the same vector and norm on every corresponding coefficient vector and the same evaluation on every corresponding base-tuple polynomial. The precise target is `exists_realDat_keep_rsideW`, on its stated range of enlargements. The next analytic construction will replace the preliminary theta maps with the required intertwiners.

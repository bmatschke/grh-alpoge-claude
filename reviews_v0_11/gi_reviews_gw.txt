GW1: thresholds and the actual integer boxes
--------------------------------------------
I checked the literal SizeN, SizeM, SizeK and SizeTT substitutions and
the distinction between m_ch and the local coefficient-rank parameter.
The displayed bounds are valid weakenings:
  k <= x^6,
  m_ch <= x^(1/2+eps/100),
  |TT| <= 2 x^(2+8eps+eps/100),
  N1 <= Ce log x.
Because eps, rG and the coefficient vector are fixed before x, the
conditions K.kII rG^2 <= x^8 and Cm Ce log x <= x are uniform in the
family index. They give the two required coefficients exactly:
  K.kII rG^2 k x^(-26) <= x^(-12),
  Cm N1/x^13 <= x^(-12).

The first box has width a/(kx^13) >= 1 from a >= x^19 and k <= x^6.
The second has width Nstar x^eps Lambda b'/k >= 1 because Nstar >= 1,
Lambda >= 1 and b' >= x^6 >= k. The proposed sufficient inequalities
  x^(eps/3) <= x^eps/2,
  x^eps/2 >= 5 log x
indeed imply log b' = log x+x^eps-x^(eps/3) >= 6 log x.
The lower estimate for Nstar has positive exponent
  2+(8-1/100-1/8)eps.
The rounding implication width >= 1 => ceil(lower) <= floor(upper)
is correct, including integer endpoints. No limiting or probabilistic
assumption is needed to make the boxes nonempty.

GW2--GW3: interfaces needed by the requested checks
---------------------------------------------------
I read these paragraphs and checked their use of the actual definitions
in IsResidueClassZero.lean (Cand, Sig, winA, winB, boxLoc, meanLoc,
MeanKappaBound). The small-prime deficit is nonnegative and independent
of the degrees. The large-prime mean is precisely the canonical

linearity only; no independence across distinct primes is assumed.

GW4--GW5: harmonic sum, exponent and literal WReq
------------------------------------------------
Put t=x^(eps/3)>=1 and M=ceil(exp t)-1. Then M>=1, M<exp t, and every
candidate prime is among the positive integers <= M. Thus
  sum_p log(p)/p <= t H_M <= t(1+log M) <= t(1+t) <= 2t^2.
The integral bound H_M <= 1+log M includes M=1. It needs no statement
about the distribution of primes.

For s>=0 the pointwise inequality 1_{s>x^(-eps)} <= x^eps s holds,
with the correct strict threshold. It yields
  E Z <= (x^eps+1) m_ch |TT| x^(-12) 2x^(2eps/3)
      <= 8 x^(-19/2+(1453/150)eps).
I recomputed the epsilon coefficient:
  1 + 1/100 + 8 + 1/100 + 2/3 = 1453/150.
The constant coefficient is 1/2+2-12 = -19/2. Consequently
  gamma = 1/2-(1453/150)eps > 2447/7800 > 0
under the strict source assumption eps<1/52. The threshold
32x^(-gamma)<=1 gives E Z <= x^(-9)/4 as asserted.

The source WReq is exactly the weighted sum with coefficients 2 on
the exceptional indicator and 4 on Sigma. Since 2I+4Sigma <=
4(I+Sigma), one pair with Z <= x^(-9)/4 satisfies WReq. Nonempty
finite averaging gives such a pair without any extra compactness or
measurability hypothesis. The source MeanEx is the conjunction of
nonnegativity on candidate primes and existence of this same Z bound;
both clauses are provided. The report correctly avoids claiming a
pointwise small deficit at every prime.

GW6: actual family transport and the coverage boundary
------------------------------------------------------
I compared the report with the fields in FamCore.lean and ParConsts.lean,
the source pg_* transport lemmas in PowFourLtCeilThresholdPrime.lean,
the chosenW_of proof in LossRequirementsOfSmallShares.lean, and the
literal ChosenW/ChosenWS/chosenWS_of_cover declarations.

1. Ix_win supplies i0 for x in Win. The value map is injective on the
   frame, so TT_eq transports the chosen functions to the common
   finite set TTn(x), with zero extension outside it.
2. bTop_eq and the final ParametersFill equality LaG=Lambda give
   precisely winB=winBz, not merely an inclusion. Choosing a finite
   place above 2 to instantiate fills is legitimate for the actual
   number-field setting and agrees with pg_LaG. winA agrees by its
   definition and the shared scale.
3. R_eq identifies the frame and hence n,k,m_ch and addition/value
   labels. TT_eq identifies selected sets. q_eq,d_eq,QS_eq suffice
   for the source prime class map, as witnessed by pg_cls. hP_eq and
   bs_eq give the identical orbit periods, selectors and good sets.
   L_x,L_eps,L_J give the same scale. The candidate sets are equal.
4. Carried degrees agree on TT. Both global extensions are zero off
   TT, so all cyclic pullbacks agree, including positions not selected.
5. The small-prime formula depends only on the shared data. ParConsts
   explicitly contains kapI_eq for the whole large-prime function.
   Therefore Sigma agrees term by term. The larger sameServed package
   also transports off/ep, but those fields are not used for Sigma.
6. ChosenW quantifies universally over carriers and does not provide
   one. CoverAll is defined separately in FamCore, not included as a
   field. Applying it at an existing member's valid distinguished
   position produces the carrier needed for ChosenWS. coverS alone
   only moves the distinguished position while preserving an existing
   degree assignment. The report retains this distinction explicitly.

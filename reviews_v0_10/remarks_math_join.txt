POINTWISE ATTAINMENT AND THE EXACT RU11–HM6.1 JOIN
Author/reviewer: /root/remarks_math
Source pin: 7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4

Purpose. Supply the particular GoodShare data required by RU11 directly
from the literal definition of kapTI, then identify its depth and loss
with D0 and kx in HM6.1. This does not use the full K.admitted record
or any unrelated AdmT field. No mean estimate is needed for attainment.

1. A finite-rank maximum is attained.

Let O be the actual discrete valuation ring, F its fraction field,
V the product of polynomial F-spaces used by SegVs, L an actual
finite-free segLat of rank d, and be its literal segForm. Finiteness
and the equality of its actual rank with the counted rank are already
proved in RU10. The ambient V is torsion free as an O-module because
O injects into F, and the assertion holds coordinate by coordinate.

For a real depth D, let
 S={rank_O L1 : L1 is GoodSub(p,be,L,D)}.
The zero submodule belongs to this family. It is saturated in L by
torsion freeness. Its primitive-partner condition is vacuous: 0 is
not primitive, since 0=0·0 would force 0 to be a unit under PrimIn.
Every GoodSub has L1⊂L. Tensoring this inclusion with F bounds its
rank by d. Thus S is a nonempty subset of {0,...,d}; its maximum g
exists and is attained by some actual saturated L1. No compactness
of a space of lattices, limiting sublattice, or infinite supremum
attainment theorem is being assumed.

If d=0, choose L1=0. The source defines badShare to be 0 and the
GoodShare rank condition is 0<=0. If d>0, the natural-number sSup
in ObjSeg.badShare is exactly g, so
 badShare=(d-g)/d,     0<=badShare<=1,
 (1-badShare)d=g=rank_O L1.
This proves the literal GoodShare(p,be,L,D,badShare), including its
zero-rank convention. It is precisely the human proof of
Joints.goodSub_bot and Joints.badShare_spec. Note that it does not
assert the attained good rank is large: the size of this loss is
provided by the separately proved partner/mean estimates.

2. Apply this to the exact segment lattices.

Fix one of HM6's extended-box degree pairs (A,B), put c=c4 and
M=m0I, and for every segment j take
 L_j=segLat(O,p,m,segI_j,segATop(TT,ex A,j),
                         segAPlus(TT,ex A,j),M),
 be_j=segForm(O,p,m,segI_j),
 D_j=DjI(CA,p,m,TT,N1,Y,a),
 kappa_j=kapTI(O,C0,CA,CE1,CE2,Cm,p,n',m,TT,G',N1,Y,a,b',c,
                                                    ex A,ex B,j).
These are exactly the data in ProjectionLemmaBaseLevel.kapTI;
there is no replacement lattice or change of normalization.
The definition kapT=badShare makes section 1 apply pointwise.

With k=m+1, N=|TT|, v=log_p a and ell=1+log_p(8a+m), the
source depth is
 D_j=CA Y^2 N1^2 k^2 ell+4Nk v.
It is nonnegative because CA>=0,p>1,a>=1 and all remaining
factors are nonnegative. This also directly checks the depth part
of the literal DjHyp record. Section 1 gives kappa_j>=0 and its
GoodShare part. No weighted averaging or choice across different
coefficient rings is necessary here; only finitely many segment
witnesses in the given ring are selected.

3. The identities at the aggregate interface are exact.

Every D_j has the same value. Also
 kappa_0=kap0I=kap0G4(N,k,p,a)=2Nk log_p a.
Therefore
 sum_j D_j+4kappa_0
  =numSeg(TT)·DjI+4kap0I
  =totalDegreeBudget(CA,p,m,TT,N1,Y,a)
  =Dat.D0(K).
This is an identity, not a further error-budget inequality.
Similarly, by the literal definition of Dat.kx,
 kappa_P+sum_j kappa_j=kx(A,B),
with kappa_P=kapPI at c=c4 and the same m0I.
The exact height in RU11 is floor(t0I), hence Dat.t0 after
substitution, and its coefficient precision is c4.

NP6 supplies the rounded HypI, CBoundI(4), the actual extended
windows, D0>=0 and c4>4D0. NP2 gives the needed Std; RU9 and
RU10 provide the actual aggregate rank and primitive-pair factors;
RL11 and RU11 give the actual p^AHH-normalized pair lift and its
integral error removal. Substituting the attained GoodShare data
and the preceding identities in RU11 gives exactly

 RhoGE_HH(floor(t0(A,B)), c4, D0,
               (1-kx(A,B))dimHH(A,B)/gaussianNumber(p^n',m+1)),

which is HM6.1 for every actual rank-one edge, hence for the edge
chosen there. No additional monotonicity or slack estimate is
required. The source packaging implementing the same substitution
is Joints.depth_bound_of_extension and
Joints.bad_share_of_extension_proposition.

For precision in the tensor-rank estimate: if
kappa_P+sum_j kappa_j>=1, the required right side is nonpositive
and the rank claim is immediate. Otherwise 0<=kappa_P<1 and
0<=kappa_j<1, so
 (1-kappa_P) product_j(1-kappa_j)
 >=(1-kappa_P)(1-sum_j kappa_j)
 >=1-kappa_P-sum_j kappa_j.
This supplies all signs in that finite-product step.

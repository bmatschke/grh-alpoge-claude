7. Contribution to the whole selected route and stopping criteria
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The proposed first ioP branch has a precise completion criterion: prove the
entire exists_ioP producer on the same original R and activate its stored
weak choice. A parameter-by-parameter existence statement is not completion.
It would remove HCU_COMMON_IO and allow TAE13/HCU19 to apply at every t,x,
without supplying any of the still-needed target analytic inputs.

The boundedness/FinPair branch is the other earliest analytic gate. A matched
published theorem may reduce it substantially, but no such match is certified
here. It deserves parallel investigation rather than being left hidden behind
CuspRapidDecay or a generic finite-volume citation.

The Hilbert-to-pointwise branch should aim for all of BackForm.exists_multMap,
not only IsReg or smooth-map equivariance. Its local chart/counting/Sobolev
core is shared by representative existence, derivative identities, growth,
and continuity; central finiteness and closed-span cusp preservation finish
actual fields of the final MMap. Completing it would remove the remaining
pointwise-realization gate conditional on the explicitly retained bounded
HolForm and FinPair, with no replacement of the prescribed model.

These gains still do not construct the selected Bundle, original R/F/Read/
ReadL, WeakCharacterisation, phase-one Pins, or the required rational maps.
base_theta/main.tex, BTH7–BTH12, has already reduced the actual base theta
square to the coupled original extension, Hilbert reading and finite rational
tests. Its base reading remains an identification of the same stored maps;
a newly constructed abstract automorphic realization does not identify it
without the original reading data. Its rational tests use the original
Hermitian adjoints and finite bases. local_theta_dictionary/main.tex and
small_level_rank/main.tex likewise retain the original same-Bundle bindings
and the separate RowsHold0_3 producer.

The exact source consumer is IW/LiftCuspFormSpaceThetaMapEqData.lean:128–164,
theta_of. It needs T1, the actual geometric-to-analytic theta reading, and T2,
the kernel statement for the original rational adjoint. The inherited BTH
arguments explain how the analytic extension contributes to T1 and how the
finite tests contribute to T2; neither identity follows from mere existence
of a Hilbert map. Closing the analytic branch therefore gives a substantial
shared input to this square, not an automatic closure of SmallLevelRankEq,
all local rows, or the full RH/GRH proof.

The priority recommendation is conditional on this branch's current state:
finish the simultaneous original io producer first; investigate the exact
target boundedness/volume application in parallel; then complete the actual
pointwise extension using the shared Sobolev core. Reassess against other
selected-route leaves when one of these entire producers closes. The purpose
is to remove genuine gates to the final theorem, while keeping every remaining
same-object and quantifier obligation visible.

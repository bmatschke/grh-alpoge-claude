MF11: Checked normD, ballCount and the literal tendsto_ballCount endpoint.
For A=direct sum O_E b_i inside L of index I, covol(L)=v^(fm)/I and
D(L) I=product_tau det(H_tau). Their quotient gives exactly
(pi^m/m!)^f/(v^(fm)D(L)). D(L) is constant on the genus because its local
determinant valuations are. Fundamental-tile boundary comparison applies
to the product of ellipsoids; T=j^2 gives the source power j^(2fm).
No mass formula is used in this lattice-point limit.

MF12: Norm-n vectors and genus representatives are finite by positivity
and the earlier genus result. Fixing an actual rational x0 of norm n
uses MF4, then rational isometry moves every representation to x0.
Both orbit-stabilizer operations keep reciprocal automorphism weights.
The stabilizer of an extension lattice at x0 is exactly the corresponding
subgroup of Aut(T); the full perpendicular decomposition determines the
extension of a cut isometry uniquely.

MF13: The fiber lower/upper lattices give finite local possibilities and
ordinary local patching in one fixed rational W. The statement does not
patch arbitrary rational Hermitian-space invariants. At almost every
place n and the original determinant are units; projection splits off

fiber factor is one. The finite global sum distributes into the local
product after grouping by cut genera and applying the rank-(m-1)
induction hypothesis. Empty fibers give a zero identity without division.

norm ideal products. A_m=A_(m-1)e_m gives the exact q-exponent in the
local factor. The product of q^ord(n) is the absolute F-norm of n,

MF15: The fixed ideal/coset weighted box asymptotic has covolume
sqrt(disc F) Na Nc and integral m^(-f) over the unit box. The uniform
bound is valid: two points in a half-open cube of side (Na)^(1/f)
would have a nonzero ideal element with absolute norm <Na. When the
box is nonempty, Na<=T^f, yielding <=2^f T^f/Na points. Multiplying by
the maximum norm weight gives the uniform C/Na majorant. Finite-place
step approximations are made off deep zero residue classes; the tail
uses the coprime product ideal a*p^j and is O(1/(Na q^j)). CRT therefore
supports the claimed mean-one finite-place factor with no unproved
uniform fixed-ideal error term.

MF16: Checked the good-prime density recurrence and its closed divisor
expansion. The absolute majorant for the contribution of ideal a is
C/(Na)^m. It is summable already at m=2 by the degree-f prime count and
an absolutely convergent Euler product. Hence dominated convergence
legitimately passes through the divisor sum. The finite bad-prime
normalizations and the good-prime Euler factors yield precisely
m^(-f) L_m/sqrt((2v)^f). This is the required endpoint case for the
rank-two induction; an uncontrolled product/average exchange is avoided.

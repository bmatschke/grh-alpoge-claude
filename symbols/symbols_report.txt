0. Scope and exact boundary

This note covers the Type-II source realization feeding InitialLevelMonoWeights.
edgeStep_of. It proves the finite-field localizer, its coefficient expansion,
first-order determinant/pairing keys, the residual label-monomial assertion,
finite correction, and degree accounting. The lift assembly is conditional on
two explicitly retained assertions: GeneratorSpanInclusion and
GeneratorImageIsSymbol. The construction/dimension of the selected symbol
space is separately retained. The complementary representation and dual tests
are the CL component, not a consequence of this note.

The word 'admitted' in some source interface names is not being read as the
Lean keyword 'sorry' or as an axiom. Here it names a bundled proposition. We
record which of its mathematical fields are still inputs to the human text.

SY1. Coordinates, frames, and the exact realization statement

Use a complete unramified p-adic coefficient ring W of residue size p^n',
with its Frobenius, and a coefficient DVR O in which w is a uniformizer and
w^2=p. The source also assumes a finite injective W-algebra structure on O.
The stronger HidaSetting/UnramifiedQuadraticSetting is retained where the
global application needs it. For the polynomial algebra itself, the relevant
facts are an integral Frobenius-compatible coordinate change, w^2=p, and
an embedding of F_(p^n') into the residue field of O.

Positions are s in Z/n'Z, n' even. At even s, the coordinate row is primal;
at odd s it is dual. The normal set consists of (s,b) with b>=r at even s
and b<r at odd s. The chart map Phi_g sends the adapted primal or dual
coordinate to w X_(s,b) on this set and to X_(s,b) otherwise. The coefficients
of g and g^{-1} are integral, since g has unit determinant. Phi_g is an
automorphism over Frac(O), and preserves the multidegree at every position.

Consequently, if F is integral, the coefficient of a monomial with normal
degree v in Phi_g F is divisible by w^v. This stronger coefficient fact is
essential in SY5. Merely knowing a key's leading congruence is insufficient.

The frame consists of ordered even positions a_(j,1)<...<a_(j,r)=z_j, with
blocks strictly separated and a_(j+J,l)=a_(j,l)+n'. They are in TT. The full
FramesOK hypotheses give uniform bounds on the gaps and local density.
Take Q=p^G', with G'<=n'. All powers p^(z_j-a_(j,l)), Q_j=p^(z_(j+1)-z_j),
and p^(s-A_l(s)) used below are <=Q. Here A_l(s) are the r most recent even
selected positions before s. The local density hypothesis bounds the number
of selected positions in any cyclic window of length G' by N1.

Let R0, Delta_j, Dhat_s be as in SY2/SY4. For c>=0 the exact multiplier is

 SPtrue = R0^c (product_j Delta_j^c)
                (product_(s in TT) Dhat_s^((2Q+3)c+kQ)).

For P in the allowed Gen set, of position degree d0 and normal degree <=T,
with d0+E'=dTop, SymbolRealisableAt asks for rational polynomials f_o of
multidegree dTop such that sigma_o=Phi_g f_o is integral, realizable at
order o, and sum_(0<=o<=T) sigma_o=P Phi_g(SPtrue).

'Realizable at order o' means there is an INTEGRAL polynomial F_o with
Phi_g F_o-w^o sigma_o in w^(o+c)O[X], and Phi_(g') F_o in w^(o+c)O[X]
whenever the rational planes Pi(g') and Pi(g) differ. If sigma_o has a
specified multidegree, F_o has that same degree. The intermediate f_o need
not be integral. No assertion is made about vanishing on another basis of
the SAME plane.

SY2. The finite-field localizer

For a column set I of size r, let M_(j,I) be the determinant of the r rows
at a_(j,l), after raising row l to p^(z_j-a_(j,l)). Put I0={0,...,r-1},
Delta_j=M_(j,I0), and let I range through the r(k-r) single exchanges of
I0 with a column outside I0. The source defines

 Rloc = Delta_0 product_I [product_j Delta_j^(Q_j-1)
                              - product_j M_(j,I)^(Q_j-1)],
 R0 = product_j Delta_j^alpha_j,
 alpha_j = 1_(j=0)+r(k-r)(Q_j-1).

Here and below j ranges over 0,...,J-1. Before using p^z_j, translate the
finitely many relevant lifted positions by a common multiple of n' so they
are all nonnegative. All differences and residue positions are unchanged;
Frobenius on F_(p^n') also has period n'. Reduction in a plane chart gives
the following situation. Let H be an r by k matrix over F_q, q=p^n', and
let its row at position a be a linear combination of H^(p^a). After raising
it to p^(z_j-a), the whole r by k matrix is T_j H^(p^z_j). Thus

 M_(j,I) = det(T_j) det(H_I)^(p^z_j).

This identity holds in any F_q-algebra, including the required polynomial
ring. It uses only additivity of Frobenius in characteristic p and the
determinant formula det(AB)=det A det B.

The exponent sum telescopes exactly:

 sum_j p^z_j (Q_j-1) = sum_j[p^z_(j+1)-p^z_j]
                     = p^z_0 (q-1).

Hence for any I with det(H_I) nonzero,
product_j M_(j,I)^(Q_j-1)=product_j det(T_j)^(Q_j-1), independent of I.
There is no division by det(T_j); this is valid even if those polynomials
vanish or have zero divisors in the coefficient algebra.

If det(H_I0)=0, then Delta_0=0. Otherwise, if H has a nonzero column to
the right of I0, apply the inverse or adjugate of H_I0 to that column.
At least one resulting coordinate is nonzero, so Cramer's rule supplies
an exchanged minor with nonzero determinant. Its difference factor is zero.

For distinct rational plane charts g,g', H is the first r rows of the
reduction of (g')^{-1}g. Its right block cannot be zero: otherwise the first
r rows of (g')^{-1} lie in the span of the first r rows of g^{-1}; both
spaces have rank r, so their planes would coincide. Ordered column sets
can introduce a sign compared with the replaced-column determinant. The
exponents Q_j-1 are even because p is odd, so the sign disappears.

This proves Phi_(g') Rloc in w O[X] for every other rational plane. It is a
statement about coefficients after chart substitution, stronger than pointwise
vanishing at F_q-rational values.

SY3. Own-chart expansion and its exact budget

In the chosen chart, every frame row is at an even position. In its first r
columns substitution leaves X unchanged, while in an exchanged column it
replaces X by wX. In particular Phi_g Delta_j is an integral tangential
determinant, and Phi_g M_(j,I) is divisible by w.

Expand Rloc over subsets S of the exchanged-column set. The empty subset
is R0. For a nonempty S the frame-determinant exponent at j is

 alpha_j - b_j,  b_j=|S|(Q_j-1).

Expanding the remaining exchanged minors produces finitely many terms

 w^e X^nu product_j(Phi_g Delta_j)^(alpha_j-b_j) H,

where H is tangential and integral, e>=1, and |nu|<=e. In fact one can keep
e=|nu| before collecting any additional coefficient divisibility. Each of
the b_j normal columns contributes at least one w and one normal-variable
power, so e>=sum_j b_j and therefore b_j<=e for every j. This is the
coefficient-level content of the source Conclusion2/localiser_expansion_lemma.

The same reasoning for Rloc^c relative to R0^c gives terms with determinant
exponents c alpha_j-b_j and total error e, again b_j<=e and |nu|<=e.
All exponents before refactoring are nonnegative. This observation will
allow the fixed R0^c to be recovered using the spare Delta powers in SY5.

SY4. Order-one keys and RestMon

Fix s in TT and take its most recent r even selected positions A_l(s)<s.
These exist because the frame repeats periodically. In fact G'<=n' and the
frame-gap bound place the relevant occurrences in the previous G' positions.
Let P_l=p^(s-A_l(s))>=p and let T_s have entries X_(A_l(s),b)^P_l,
b<r. Put D_s=det(T_s)=Phi_g Dhat_s. Every entry is tangential; hence all
entries and every adjugate entry are integral of normal degree zero.

Even s, normal column b>=r. Form the determinant of the (r+1) by (r+1)
matrix with the current primal row first, its columns I0 plus b, and older
rows raised to P_l below it. In the chosen chart the upper-right entry is
w X_(s,b); the lower-right entries are w^P_l X_(A_l(s),b)^P_l. Expansion
along the last column therefore gives leading term

 K0=(-1)^r w X_(s,b) D_s

and an error in w^2 O[X]. After reduction in any other chart, the r+1
Frobenius-adjusted primal rows lie in the same r-dimensional space, so the
whole determinant vanishes. This supplies one order-one key.

Odd s, normal columns b<r. Pair each older primal row, raised to P_l, with
the current dual row. Its leading term is

 K0_l = w sum_(b<r) (T_s)_(l,b) X_(s,b).

Terms in columns b>=r contain w^P_l and are in w^2 O[X]. On any residue
plane the Frobenius-adjusted primal row belongs to that plane and the
dual row annihilates it, so the pairing vanishes. These are r order-one
keys. Multiplying by the adjugate of T_s gives the exact identity

 w X_(s,b) D_s = sum_l adj(T_s)_(b,l) K0_l.

Together the two cases give w X_v D_s as a tangential integral linear
combination of order-one leading keys. Signs in the even case are units.
The order bound 1<=2Q is automatic. Both keys and coefficients use only
positions in TT. No inverse determinant or rational coefficient is needed.

A label monomial m satisfies m_(s,b)<p^(dist_j(s)) whenever s is a non-frame
position of segment j. Each selected position is such a position of one
segment: choose the first frame endpoint z_j>=s. The preceding endpoint
is <s. The frame-gap bound makes dist_j(s)<=G', hence m_(s,b)<=Q.

Let v_s be the total number of normal factors of m at s. Then v_s<=kQ.
Multiply the first-order identities for every normal factor. With
o=deg_N m=sum_s v_s and DkQ=product_s D_s^(kQ), this proves

 w^o X^m DkQ = sum of products of key leading terms, of total order o,
                   with integral tangential coefficients.

The unused factors are product_s D_s^(kQ-v_s), so every exponent is
nonnegative. The entire expression is normal-homogeneous of degree o.
This is precisely RestMon. In particular, RestMon is no longer a retained
input to this human reconstruction.

SY5. Finite correction proved directly from the key definition

An order-d key is an integral polynomial K with d<=2Q, leading term K0
normal-homogeneous of degree d and divisible by w^d, such that
Phi_g K-K0 is divisible by w^(d+1), and every chart sends K into w^d O[X].
All keys under discussion are supported at TT. The source permits d=0;
the proof below covers that case as well.

Consider a monomial in Phi_g K-K0 of normal degree v. Its coefficient is
divisible by w^(d+1). It is also divisible by w^v: for v different from d
this follows from integrality of K before chart substitution; for v=d
the first assertion is stronger. Thus it can be written w^x times an
integral coefficient, where x=max(d+1,v). Put E=x-d. Then

 E>=1,  v<=x=d+E<=(2Q+1)E.

This gives a finite monomial expansion of the error. No w-adic infinite
expansion is required; each coefficient is divided only by a power known
to divide it in the DVR.

Now take a raw term
 U=w^i product_a K_a Rloc^c product_j Delta_j^lambda product_s Dhat_s^mu Hhat,
where Hhat is the integral adapted-coordinate polynomial corresponding to
an integral tangential H. Its symbol S replaces K_a by K0_a, Rloc by R0,
and applies Phi_g to all the tangential factors. The level is
ell=i+sum_a d_a. The symbol is in w^ell O[X]. By SY2 and the definition of
keys, every other plane sends U into w^(ell+c)O[X].

Expand Phi_g U-S by choosing either the leading term or an error in each
key and in each localizer factor. Every non-leading summand earns a total
level increase E>=1. Its explicit normal monomial degree is at most
(2Q+1)E; its loss b_j of frame determinants is at most E. These inequalities
add under products. The remaining leading keys keep their previous levels.

Fix L=o+c. Terms with ell+E>=L may be discarded directly as elements of
w^L O[X], before any refactoring. For the other terms assume

 mu>=(2Q+1)(L-1-ell),  lambda>=L-1-ell.

Apply SY4 to replace each explicit normal factor wX_(s,b)D_s by key leading
terms. This uses v_s<= (2Q+1)E copies of D_s. Of the original w-power, the
unused part is nonnegative because the explicit normal degree was <= its
coefficient's w-power. Leave common exponent
 mu'=mu-(2Q+1)E
on every D_s. Move the surplus tangential powers into H. Similarly leave
frame exponent
 lambda'=lambda-E;
since b_j<=E, the surplus Delta_j^(E-b_j) is tangential. This recovers the
same fixed R0^c, and all remaining powers are nonnegative.

The new symbols have level ell+E. They use valid original keys and the
valid order-one keys from SY4. Their coefficients stay integral and
tangential, supported at TT. Thus, modulo w^L,

 Phi_g U - S = sum_child S_child,
 ell_child=ell+E, mu_child=mu-(2Q+1)E, lambda_child=lambda-E.

This proves the content of OrderClaimRemaining directly; the source proves
the same assertion via the graded sets bc_gen/bc_agen and bc_RestTail.
The identity preserves the two budget invariants

 mu+(2Q+1)ell,  lambda+ell.

For initial ell>=o, mu=(2Q+3)c and lambda=c, the inequalities above hold
until level L. Starting at the highest possible child level and proceeding
downwards, replace every child by its corrected integral lift. More formally,
if F_child has Phi_g F_child=S_child mod w^L and vanishes to order L in
all other charts, put F=U-sum_child F_child. It has the same properties
for S. At ell>=L take F=0. The induction decreases L-ell and has finite
branching. It terminates after at most c positive level increments from an
initial term of level o. A finite initial sum is handled by addition.

The assertion includes supported variables automatically: all coordinates,
keys and coefficients remain at TT. If the desired symbol has a fixed
position multidegree, project F to it. Phi_g and every other chart commute
with that projection; coefficient divisibility and integrality are retained.

SY6. Symbol algebra and assembly of the actual lift

Let A_o be the O-module of finite sums of products of leading keys, of total
key order o, times integral tangential polynomials supported at TT. A normal-
homogeneous polynomial Z of degree o is a source symbol when w^o Z is in
A_o. This is exactly Sy at index o: bc_agen(o,0) is defined as a leading-key
product of total order o times an integral tangential polynomial. Products preserve symbols, adding
their degrees; integral tangential polynomials are degree-zero symbols.
The source SyS is the finite additive closure of these homogeneous pieces.

The two retained representation assertions have precise meanings:

(1) GeneratorSpanInclusion: for each frame segment, every MGen0 polynomial
    belongs to the O-span of the path generators PGen. MGen0 is defined by
    the primal/tangential factorization and the zero-gap inequalities on
    successive weights; PGen uses matched standard paths and their minors.
(2) GeneratorImageIsSymbol: after Frobenius substitution phSeg, every such
    path generator is a homogeneous symbol Sy. This needs the actual path
    formulas, not just a dimension count or characteristic-zero spanning.

The allowed nonnegative gap parameter m can be weakened to zero. Expand the
defining span of XX' into MGen0 using its position-homogeneous components.
This projection step is elementary: every generic Laurent substitution used
to define the successive minimum and maximum weights preserves the degree at
each position. Taking a position-homogeneous component therefore deletes
some Laurent coefficients; it cannot lower a minimum exponent or raise a
maximum exponent. Decompose the primal and tangential factors separately and
retain their homogeneous products. Their support restrictions and the zero-gap
inequalities survive. This gives the MGen0 span used by xx_span_MGen0.
Then (1), (2), and linearity put every segment factor phSeg(x_j) into SyS.
This is the source seg_SyS route, with no additional representation theorem
needed for the multidegree projection itself.

An element P of Gen is a product of these segment factors times a label
monomial. For P=0 all lifts are zero. Otherwise the label has no position
outside TT: P has zero degree there, all monomial degrees are nonnegative,
and multiplying by a monomial cannot cancel a nonzero coefficient.
SY4 makes that label times DkQ a symbol. Thus

 Z=P product_s D_s^(kQ)

belongs to SyS. The remaining factor

 Hfix=(Phi_g R0)^c product_j(Phi_g Delta_j)^c product_s D_s^((2Q+3)c)

is integral, tangential, and supported at TT. Therefore
P Phi_g(SPtrue)=Z Hfix is integral, has the prescribed position degree,
and has normal degree <=T whenever P does. Its normal components sigma_o
satisfy w^o sigma_o=(a finite sum of key-product terms) Hfix.
Apply SY5 to this sum. The output F_o is integral and realizes sigma_o
at precision o+c, on the chosen plane and all other planes as in SY1.

To match the literal SymbolRealisableAt conclusion, use the invertibility of
Phi_g over Frac(O) to choose f_o with Phi_g f_o=sigma_o, and take its position-
homogeneous component. These f_o are only the intermediate symbol preimages.
This is why a direct inverse substitution must not be mistaken for the
integral separated realization F_o supplied by SY5.
For the source's family indexed by every nonnegative o, set both f_o and F_o
to zero when o>T. The zero polynomials satisfy the same homogeneous and
realizability conditions.

SY7. Exact multidegree accounting

Let e_j(t)=sum_l p^(z_j-a_(j,l)) 1_(t=a_(j,l) mod n') and
f_s(t)=sum_l p^(s-A_l(s)) 1_(t=A_l(s) mod n'). Determinant multilinearity
proves that the exact degree vector of SPtrue is

 E'_t = sum_j(c alpha_j+c)e_j(t)
              + [(2Q+3)c+kQ] sum_(s in TT) f_s(t).

This is source eSP. It is independent of g and is zero for t outside TT.
Distinct frame rows in one period give at most one nonzero summand in
the first sum, bounded by [c(1+rkQ)+c]Q.

If A_l(s)=t mod n', then 0<s-A_l(s)<G' and so s lies in the cyclic window
of length G' immediately after t. The local density condition bounds the
number of these s by N1 (the exported source estimate uses N1+1). Each
f_s(t)<=rQ. Therefore

 E'_t <= [c(1+rkQ)+c]Q +(N1+1)[(2Q+3)c+kQ]rQ.

If k<=c, N1<=k, 1<=N1, r>=2 and Q>=3, then
[(2Q+3)c+kQ]<=4Qc and N1+1<=2k. The second contribution is <=8crkQ^2.
Since rkQ>=2, the first is <=2crkQ^2. In particular their sum is
<=15c(N1+rk)Q^2=E0, exactly the source's safe budget.

With N>=1, Y>=1, k>=1, Q>=1 and C>=30, the size premise
a>=C k^3 r^2 N Q^5 c Y implies E0<=a because N1+rk<=2rk. The source's
other numerical premises imply k<=c; it is not assumed for arbitrary c.
The prescribed flat degree at selected positions is at least a, so its
difference with E' is a nonnegative integer. Taking d0=dTop-E' completes
the degree requirement in SY6. No change in the global exponent is inferred
from the unused numerical slack.

The former residual RestMon input and the whole finite cleaning mechanism

application. All source references are pinned in the accompanying ledger.

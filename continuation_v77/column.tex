\[
  \mathrm{LiftTest}(Y),\qquad

\]

geometric half. The ordered prime equality follows from its
base-row pins. G77D7 now gives $\mathrm{InGeo}(z.\mathrm{geo})$,
and G77D1 gives the integral result. The higher cover fields
follow from the Weber portion of F77W2. All hypotheses of Q76Z1
have been supplied on this $z$. Its test family works on every
rational source input and the entire stored basis at the indicated
level. In particular neither the geometric comparison predicate nor
the stable range is a further assumption on these pinned records.

hypotheses of E77A5, E77A6 reduces it on $R$ to the two inner
predicates. I77A1--I77A5 give the intertwiners and the smooth
multiplicity maps from the local embedding, positivity, image
and global theta regularity inputs stated there. The second
construction applies to the stored intertwiner certified by the
first. Thus those analytic inputs can be substituted in the
assertion above without choosing a new record.

$W_t=\{v:\mathrm{resChar}(v)\in T.\mathrm{pl}\}$.

analytic existence inputs of E77A1--I77A5 on the admissible

a final $\mathrm{ExAt}$ witness are constructed.

It remains to check the dependent fields of $z$. Use its original

dictionary, multiplier, character family, frame and constants
on the initial core. The parameters, field algebra, uniformizations,
and ordered primes persist in this replacement. Use its final
analytic record, group comparison and geometric half. Equality of

and the defining complex embedding equality. These entries

The canonical parameters, positive ranks and chosen primes

ordinary reading and the row readings. Together with the final

request depended on the prescribed input fields and character
family, and preceded all the existential choices in this proof.

RESULT AND SCOPE

The full bare local-stage and Galois-stage existence branch is reconstructed.
The local rings are the specified Ainf, divided-power envelope and completion,
Bcris localization, and BdR adic completion/localization. We prove the actual
integral embedding, Frobenius injectivity, logarithm, fixed fields and exact
fundamental INTERSECTION needed by PeriodData. We then verify every field of
LocPrime, LocAinf, and LocTied, at every rational prime including two.

Separately, we construct the source's actual empty-local-index/empty-twist-index
NStage. Its nonempty suppliers are not discarded: cyclotomic LData, the finite
Sha-value reading, CM conjugation, selected split primes, residue multiplicative
maps, tame inertia generators, multiset parameter laws, and central idempotents
are proved. All fields and all ties are checked on the SAME Base, Cols,
RowsRest and prescribed LocStage. SE14's remaining NStage premise is thereby
discharged, giving bare Later existence for supplied I, Laws, and HasLetters.

This is not an arithmetic-compatible nonempty twist stage. Both Loc and Tw are
empty in this particular model. A function from the nonempty coefficient-prime
type into Tw cannot exist. The result therefore does not supply NChoice or the
extra rank, weight, local representation, or genuine geometric comparison
hypotheses used in subsequent theorems. The SHA function is a conditional
finite-value reading, not a proof of finiteness. The synthetic NAut model is not
identified with a classification of automorphic representations. A full
surjective Fontaine fundamental exact sequence is neither needed nor claimed.

METHOD AND BACKGROUND

All mathematical arguments are in main.tex; this report records their logical
role, scope and main checks. The background is ordinary finite-extension
valuation theory, profinite Galois theory, finite fields, characteristic-zero
separability, Witt identities, and defining completion/localization operations.
The previously reconstructed NC1–NC2 supply normalized complete-DVR extension
facts. No p-adic Hodge comparison theorem, Tate-twist vanishing theorem, Ax

C is the completion of the algebraic closure of Q_p. Even its p-th roots needed
for theta surjectivity can be obtained directly: approximate a target by

No section from the whole residue field C to BdR+ is asserted. The section has
the algebraic domain Omega, where Hensel uniqueness is sufficient.

LS1 — Uniform Ax approximation and fixed fields

The main finite-orbit induction halves the degree by a Hasse derivative.
For an orbit of n elements, with 2^k<n<=2^(k+1), its derivative of order
n-2^k has degree 2^k, small value at the original point, and leading
coefficient of p-adic norm at least 1/n. Legendre's formula proves the latter:
the valuation of the binomial coefficient has at most floor(log_p n) nonzero
summands, each at most one. Factorization yields a root within
2^((k+1)/2^k) times the orbit-difference bound. Its orbit is at most half as
large. The product of all step constants is bounded by 16. A finite orbit of
size one starts the induction with constant one.

For any subgroup H, approximate an H-fixed completion element by an algebraic
point. Its whole H-orbit stays within the same approximation error. The uniform
constant gives an algebraic H-fixed approximation. Thus C^H is exactly the
closure of Omega^H. The finite-extension case C^(G_K)=K follows because K is
complete and closed. No averaging by an uncontrolled extension degree is used.

LS2 — Uniform cyclotomic projections

Primitive p^r-th roots z satisfy |z-1|^e=|p| with
 e=p^(r-1)(p-1). This follows by multiplying primitive-root distances at one;
the geometric sum gives equal distances for primitive conjugates. Polynomial
terms of degree below e in z-1 have distinct valuations modulo Z. Their
maximum therefore computes the norm. Taylor substitution has integral
binomial coefficients in both directions, yielding the coefficient bound
|p| |a_i| <= |P(z)|. It proves the exact cyclotomic degree at the same time.

Apply the coefficient bound to the interleaved polynomial
sum P_i(X^(p^m)) X^i to bound the constant coefficient over K_n in K_(n+m).
That coefficient is normalized trace, whose norm is at most p independently
of both indices. Compatible projections extend to the completed cyclotomic
union and converge to the identity by density and the uniform bound.
The dyadic starting level n=1 is explicitly allowed.

LS3 — Nonzero Tate twists

For Q_p, a twisted invariant is fixed by ker(chi); LS1 puts it in the completed
cyclotomic union. Every bounded projection R_n is still a twisted invariant.
The image of G_(K_n) under chi cannot consist of finitely many roots of unity,
since LS2 gives unbounded further cyclotomic degrees. Thus some element fixes
R_n x while multiplying it by a nontrivial character power. Consequently every
R_n x is zero, and convergence gives x=0.

For finite K, form the product of g(x) chi(g)^i over the finitely many cosets
g G_K. It is well defined by the original invariance condition. Left
translation multiplies the product by chi(h)^(-i [G:G_K]), so the Q_p case
annihilates it. In the field C, a product is zero only if a factor is zero.
This proves the desired statement for every nonzero integer twist, including
negative twists, without a trace estimate depending on K.

LS4 — The actual Ainf and theta kernel

The tilt is the inverse limit of O_C/p under Frobenius, and its sharp-root
coordinates identify it as a perfect valuation domain. Ratios of exact roots
prove the valuation-ring divisibility criterion; sharp zero forces every
coordinate zero. The Witt theta formula is multiplicative by the Witt
identities and onto by compatible p-power roots.

Choose a tilt point with sharp p. For a kernel element [a_0]+pb, its zeroth
coefficient is divisible by that point. The explicit one-step division leaves
another kernel element multiplied by p. Iterating in the p-adically complete
Witt ring proves that the kernel is principal. The actual cyclotomic xi is a
unit multiple of this generator. The only delicate norm case is p=2: using
zeta_2^2=-1 gives |(zeta_2-1)^2|=|2| and |zeta_2-1|>|2|, so the inverse-root
comparison survives reduction before squaring. No contradiction between these
two norms is intended.

The nonzero reduction of xi modulo p is regular. This gives p-saturation of
all xi-power ideals. Cauchy division and adjusted lifts show their quotients
are p-torsion-free and p-adically complete. These properties are used later
in the bounded embedding and cannot be replaced by ordinary field completion.

LS5 — The actual BdR DVR and algebraic section

Ainf[1/p] embeds in its xi-adic completion because infinite xi divisibility
forces zeroth Witt coefficient zero, then all successive coefficients zero.
The completion has residue C and regular uniformizer xi. An element with
nonzero residue is a unit by geometric series, and separatedness gives an
exact power of xi for every nonzero element. Thus it is a complete DVR, and
inverting xi gives its field of fractions with the actual filtration.

A simple algebraic root in the residue field has a unique root lift by Newton
iteration. Compatibility over finite subextensions gives an additive,
multiplicative, G-equivariant section on Omega, agreeing with the prescribed
Q_p and Z_p coefficients. This is a section on algebraic elements only.
Galois actions preserve the theta kernel and therefore all actual filtration
ideals and their completion.

LS6 — The completed divided-power embedding

The source envelope is the Ainf-span of xi^n/n!: multiplication is governed
by integral binomial coefficients. Its p-adic completion is p-torsion-free,
and divisibility of an envelope element by p^r does not change on completing.
Modulo xi^n the envelope lies between Ainf/(xi^n) and p^(-n) times that module.
These two lattices have complete p-adic topologies differing by a bounded
shift. Extending the truncated maps and taking their compatible inverse limit
gives the actual j, with the explicit uniform bound p^n modulo xi^n.
This is not an unsupported claim that p-adic convergence implies xi-adic
convergence.

Injectivity needs a separate integral argument. In the span of the first N
divided powers, elements divisible by xi^N are multiples of N gamma_N.
The induction follows by removing the top coefficient and using xi-saturation.
A bounded-kernel approximation then writes a kernel element modulo p as an
envelope element divisible by an arbitrarily high xi power. Taking N=pM
above its original degree forces p-divisibility. Repeating and using p-adic
separation makes the kernel zero. This establishes the injection on the
completion, not only on its dense subring.

LS7 — Integral logarithms and the period t

For e with sharp one, write m=[e]-1. The cyclotomic geometric polynomial gives
p w=m^(p-1) with w in the envelope and w^p divisible by p. The factorial
valuation v_p((p^2)!)=p+1 supplies the last divisibility, including p=2.
For arbitrary e, remove its initial trivial root levels, or obtain e=1.
The coefficients m^k/(k+1) are integral: writing k=q(p-1)+r and
v=v_p(k+1)<=q expresses them using p^(q-v) m^r w^q. Their valuations tend
to infinity, and the nonconstant coefficients lie in the radical modulo p.
Thus the logarithmic quotient is a convergent unit; t_e=m times this unit.

The bounded map j identifies it with the formal logarithm in each xi-adic
quotient. Uniqueness gives integer-power and addition identities, hence
phi(t)=pt. Approximate chi(g) by residues modulo p^n and use the compatible
p^n-th-root difference to prove g(t)=chi(g)t. Finally t is a unit multiple
of xi in BdR+, since theta(nu)=zeta_1-1 is nonzero. The period has the exact
filtration degree required in PeriodData.

LS8 — Localization and unramified constants

At this intermediate point B means Acris[1/t]=Acris[1/mu]. The relation
p w=mu^(p-1) makes p invertible, and j extends injectively to BdR. We do not
yet silently identify this with the source localization at every mu_e; LS13
proves that identification.

A prime-to-p root has unique compatible p-roots in its finite root group.
Its Teichmuller lift and the algebraic section are roots of the same polynomial
with the same residue in BdR+, so Hensel uniqueness identifies them. The ring
generated by these lifts and Q_p is a field because each nonzero algebraic
element has a polynomial inverse. This constructs the unramified coefficient
embedding. Residue Frobenius lifts on the cyclic unramified extensions and
preserves every finite subfield K_0. Checking the generating roots proves all
Galois/Frobenius/embedding compatibility laws on these same data.

LS9 — Frobenius injectivity after completion

Write xi=w+pa with w a Teichmuller element. Enlarge the dense envelope to
M=Ainf[w/p]. The finite estimate is genuinely proved, not assumed:
if for every r, phi(s+p^k u)=p^r v for suitable u,v in M, then s is in p^k M.
For I=(p,w), regularity modulo p yields p-saturation of powers of I.
With recurrence F_(N+1)=a_N w^(lN)+pF_N, the exact induction invariant is:
 b w^(lN)+F_N in I^(N+r), i<N, l(i+1)<=i+r+1
 implies a_i in (p,w^l).
The proof distinguishes N+r+1<=lN from the opposite inequality. In the
latter case e=N+r+1-lN>0, splitting I^(N+r+1) and cancelling w modulo p
gives a correction pd; the terminal coefficient has e>=l. All index
conditions and the correction term are written in main.tex.

Denominator clearing with N=N0+deg(h)+i+1 applies this invariant to
phi(h(w/p)), yielding each h_i in (p,w). Substitution and iteration prove
the finite estimate. Approximate a Frobenius kernel element in Acris by
s+p^k z. Divisibility descent on the dense subring yields the finite estimate.
The bound p^n modulo xi^n then puts its image in every p^r Ainf/(xi^n).
Separatedness and j-injectivity give zero. Localization preserves injectivity.

LS10 — The integral fixed ring

Frobenius sends the positive divided powers into p times the envelope, so its
image modulo p is represented by Ainf. A fixed element modulo p is represented
by a with a^p-a divisible by p in the envelope. Applying theta shows that its
residue is in F_p. Subtract a digit c. A power of theta(a-c) is divisible by p;
lift the quotient and use the theta kernel to prove a-c nilpotent modulo p.
It is also Frobenius-fixed there. A nilpotent x with x^p=x is zero.
Subtract c and divide by the regular element p to repeat. The resulting digits
converge in Z_p, and separation proves Acris^(phi=1)=Z_p.

LS11 — Two integral identities for the fundamental intersection

Successive inverse-Frobenius cyclotomic factors divide any a for which all
theta(phi^n a) vanish. Modulo p their norms tend to that of epsilon-1.
The valuation-ring criterion gives divisibility of the zeroth coefficient
by epsilon-1. Lifting and removing mu times its Teichmuller lift leaves p
 times an element with the same vanishing property. Iteration proves mu|a
inside Ainf.

The geometric polynomial also gives phi(xi)=p(c+w) for p w=mu^(p-1).
Its divided powers belong to Ainf+w Acris. Repeated dense p-adic
approximation expresses the Frobenius image of every completed element in
the same sum, with convergent coefficients. No closedness of that sum is
simply presumed.

LS12 — Integral eigenvectors in the matching filtration

The assertion phi(x)=p^r x and j(x) in Fil^r implies p^k x=z t^r with
z in Z_p. The base case is LS10. For r+1, use LS11 to write
p^(r+1)x=mu b+w z'. With t=mu u, define
 x'=u^(-1)(p b+mu^(p-2) z').
Then p^(r+2)x=t x'. Frobenius and domain cancellation show
phi(x')=p^r x'; t is a uniformizer, so j(x') is in Fil^r.
Induction finishes. The exponent p-2 is nonnegative even at p=2.
This is an integral assertion before inverting p or the period.

LS13 — Exact Bcris and the required fundamental intersection

Every source localization generator mu_e has t_e=mu_e u_e, a nonzero
phi=p eigenvector in Fil^1. LS12 gives p^k t_e=z t with nonzero z in Z_p.
Both p and z are units in Acris[1/t], so mu_e is a unit there. Conversely
the source localization inverts mu_epsilon and hence t. Universal properties
give inverse maps fixing Acris. This identifies the actual source Bcris and
transports the already constructed same-ring Core.

If phi(b)=b and its image is in BdR+, write b t^r=x in Acris. Then x has
phi eigenvalue p^r and filtration r. LS12 gives p^k x=z t^r, hence b=z/p^k.
The converse is immediate. This is exactly PeriodData.fund. A stronger
fundamental exact sequence with a surjectivity assertion is outside the claim.

LS14 — De Rham invariant field

An invariant positive-filtration element is successively divisible by every
power of t: after each division its residue is a nonzero Tate-twist invariant
and vanishes by LS3. Therefore it is zero. More generally an element of BdR+
with character chi^n, n>=0, is t^n times an invariant element, by the same
residue induction. Clear the denominator of a general BdR invariant to put it
in this situation. It lies in BdR+; its residue is in K by LS1. Subtract the
algebraic section of that residue. The remaining invariant is in positive
filtration, so is zero. Thus BdR^(G_K)=K, with the actual coefficient map.

LS15 — Crystalline invariant field

The Frobenius iterates of an invariant b have images in K by LS14. All satisfy
the same nonzero minimal polynomial over Q_p: apply the polynomial through the
injective inclusion and through Frobenius. There are finitely many possible
images. Injectivity of Frobenius cancels an initial repetition and makes b
periodic with period N>0.

Choose a primitive root of order p^N-1. Its N Frobenius conjugates are distinct.
Sums of its n-th conjugate powers times the corresponding Frobenius iterates of
b are fixed by Frobenius and have algebraic image in Fil^0. LS13 makes these
sums Q_p constants. A Lagrange polynomial vanishing at all conjugates but the
first isolates a nonzero scalar times b. Hence b is an unramified constant.
LS14 puts it in K as well, giving exactly K intersect Q_p^ur. The case N=1,
p=2 uses the root 1 and no invalid distinctness requirement.

LS18 — Nonempty global suppliers of the bare NStage

Cyclotomic LData uses the actual algebraic closure of the host E and all places
above p. Inertia outside p fixes p-power roots by prime-to-residue-characteristic
reduction injectivity. Thus the character descends to the unramified-outside
quotient. shaTrue reads its extended-natural length through toNat and has the
exact required value whenever the length is finite. This is not a finiteness

CM conjugation lifts through a complex embedding of the algebraic closure.
The required multiplicative complex Teich map can be built by compatible
embeddings of nested cyclic finite-field unit groups, with zero sent to zero.
Reduction of algebraic integers at a prime above ell then yields the required
map to Fbar_ell, injective precisely on prime-to-ell-order elements. The source
uses a Witt-fraction embedding to construct the former map; the shorter
argument proves its exact existential record interface and does not assert
additivity or a field embedding across characteristics.

The host congruence gives complete splitting. Galois transitivity and the exact
number of primes make the action free; nontrivial CM conjugation pairs them.
A transversal gives exactly Fin(f) nonconjugate selected places. The ray field
may be H.K itself over H.K, with its trivial abelian extension, while its
absolute discriminant -d and splitting remain genuine arithmetic facts.

LS19 — Actual tame generator, not a vacuous local-index field

On the compact inertia group, take the residue coboundaries of n!-th roots of
the underlying rational prime. Each image is a finite cyclic root-of-unity
group, its fibers are closed, and its kernels are nested. The closed nonempty
sets of elements generating each finite image are nested, so compactness
selects a common generator. Powers of that generator approximate every inertia
element modulo all these kernels.

The common kernel is exactly the subgroup acting by principal-unit
coboundaries on every nonzero algebraic element. Indeed a polynomial equation
has two terms of maximal valuation, so every algebraic norm is a rational power
of the prime norm. A sufficiently large factorial root realizes that norm;
the remaining quotient is a unit on which inertia is trivial modulo the
maximal ideal. This identifies the kernel and proves the literal TameGenAt
condition. Applying it at the selected places proves tg, tg_mem and tg_gen.

STATUS AND LOGICAL SCOPE

This component proves the fourteen numerical fields of AuxBlock8.HypI for
the actual selected tops, the eventual family statement Joints.HypIAt,
the exact change of parameters to flipOneThree.HypI, and the comparison
of its local time tI with the source's global time tIf. It then proves
the actual exceptional-share mean AuxBlock8.adm8 from the completed
local theorem TC6 and the canonical least-serving exceptional function.

The simultaneous arithmetic family is supplied data. In particular,
FamP.sizes, FamP.rho_le, the equality La(K,scale)=L.LaG contained in
FamP.fills, and the selected integer weights are literal assumptions of
that family, not existence theorems proved here. A given arbitrary
Inst does not, by its definition, assert KapIDef for its function kapI.
The mean theorem is therefore stated for an Inst satisfying KapIDef;
GJ1 proves that the canonical infimum is attained and has the required
local certificates. In a literal FamP this identification is supplied
by the nested field FamP.ex.kapI_def, explicitly unpacked by GJ1 and
PlaneCountTheoremWithConstants.kapIDefF. No replacement of a given
family or its exceptional function is necessary.

Completed inputs used here:
  GII1: the actual good-orbit segment geometry;
  GII2–GII3: logarithmic window bounds and the actual top-count bound;
  GJ2: the actual lambda and log(Qp) bounds (adm5), proved from those

  TC6: the complete local Type-I Conclusion2, with C0>=880,

  rho <= rhoB

The last inequality is the literal FamP.rho_le axiom. No bound on
the sum of log p/p over a band is used. This distinction matters:
the source packages such a sum in ArchimedeanSignatureMatching,
but the numerical HypI proof only reads its first pointwise field.

For y in T the point t=t0+yh_p is selected. By the definitions of

  winA=[ceil(a), floor(a+a/(kY))] intersect Z,

  L2<U; 3G'<=n'; actual AuxBlock8.HypI; and actual weights in

GI6. THE ACTUAL GLOBAL-TO-LOCAL HYPI TRANSLATION

This claim is a pointwise implication, not asymptotic. Assume the
fourteen AuxBlock8.HypI fields, c>=0, a prime p with p>=p0 and
p<X', and not InBand0. Assume the actual segment geometry from
GII1 (equivalently the source OrbitSegs conclusion): T has an
even selected point, numSeg(T)>=2, and every segment satisfies
segG<=G', segS<=N1. Let

  C0=K.La,
  0<=CH<=min(K.kI,K.DI),       2CH<=K.rho,
  m=k-1,    ccl=floor_nat(c),  Dp=Df,
  cStar=8(Df+1),              bl=b', bu=b'+I.             (GI6.1)

These are the exact source substitutions. Since 2N1+2<=k-1,
k>=3 and the natural subtraction has (m:real)+1=k. Also N1>=1
from orbitWindowCount's final +1. The local lambda is exactly
lambda=C0*N1^2 log a, not an estimate.

The one delicate band normalization is

  deltaI=CH*N1*lambda/k + 2I/b',
  2deltaI <= K.rho*N1*lambda/k+4Nstar*x^epsilon*lambda/k
           =rho<1/6.                                   (GI6.2)

Thus deltaI<1/6. For every odd positive integer d, the local
forbidden interval

  [X'(1-2deltaI), X'(1+2deltaI)]

is contained in [X'(1-rho),X'(1+rho)]. Its containing p^d would
give InBand0, which is excluded. The direction of containment
uses X'>0. For an actual candidate, not InBand implies not
InBand0 because rho<=rhoB, so this hypothesis is indeed supplied
in the family application; no prime count is needed.

All other local HypI fields now follow explicitly:

  as above. The numerical rank margin is the global field (4).
* The logarithmic lower bound is global field (5).
* b'>0, bl=b' and bu=b'+I with I>=0 give all endpoint orderings.
* Global fields (6) and (7) give the required width inequalities
  Yb N lambda b'/k<=bu-bl and bu-bl<=b'/2.

  p<a/b' is global field (2).
* CH N lambda^2<=K.kI N lambda^2<=k.

GI7. EXACT TIME COMPARISON, INCLUDING THE CYCLIC PREDECESSOR

Assume K nonnegative, C>=0, 2C<=K.kappaThresholdOne and
C<=K.kappaThresholdTwo. Let T contain an even selected point,
p>1, p<a/b', c>=0 and N<=Nplus. The degree functions aw,bw
may be arbitrary integers; positivity is not used in their leading
minima.

For an odd position y, let d be the smallest positive backward
distance to an even selected point. Since such a point exists
and y itself is odd, 1<=d<n'. The conditions

  posAdd(x,d)=y   and   x=posAdd(y,n'-d)

are equivalent for 1<=d<=n'. Every forward distance can be
reduced modulo n'; a zero residue would force x=y, impossible
for an even x and odd y. Thus the minimum in local dPrev equals
the bounded minimum in global dMinus, and local yPrev is exactly
posAdd(y,n'-dMinus). In particular dMinus is nonzero on every
term summed. The two leading sums are therefore identical:

  S=sum over odd y in T of min(bw(y), aw(yPrev)/p^d).      (GI7.1)

Put

  E=a/p^2+lambda*a/(pk),
  c_local=8(Df+1)+C N k log_p a+4N ccl+2n' ccl,
  c6=8(Df+1)+K.kappaThresholdTwo*Nplus*k*log_p a
                     +4Nplus*c+2n'c.                   (GI7.2)

The actual definitions read

  tI =S+C N N1*(E+b'/p+lambda*b'/k)
          +C N*(Qp*c_local+kQp+k log_p a+ccl)+2n'ccl,

  tIf=S+K.kappaThresholdOne*N*N1*E
          +K.kappaThresholdTwo*N*(Qp*c6+kQp+k log_p a+c)
          +2n'c.                                       (GI7.3)

From p<a/b' and b',p>0, b'<=a/p, hence

  b'/p<=a/p^2,       lambda*b'/k<=lambda*a/(pk).

So the first error bracket is at most 2E, and its coefficient is
absorbed by 2C<=K.kappaThresholdOne. The nonnegative factors
N<=Nplus, ccl<=c and C<=K.kappaThresholdTwo give
c_local<=c6 and then the second error inequality. The last
conductor term also increases because ccl<=c. The common S is
untouched, proving exactly

  tI <= tIf <= ceil_nat(tIf).                            (GI7.4)

No monotonicity of S or assumption aw,bw>=0 was used. The second
inequality is valid even for a negative real tIf by the definition
of the natural ceiling. The depth remains Df and cStar remains
8(Df+1).


GI8. CANONICAL EXCEPTIONAL MEAN AND THE FIXED GLOBAL VECTOR

Let X satisfy the actual KapIDef for K, so its kapI is the infimum
of the nonnegative kappa that provide the source LPprime for all
unramified quadratic settings and all dominant base weights at
the fixed time ceil_nat(tIf), depth Df and cStar=8(Df+1). GJ1
proves this infimum is attained; in particular 0<=kapI<=1 and
the actual certificate is valid at kapI. For the following upper
mean bound only its leastness and nonnegativity are needed.

Apply TC6 after GI6. It gives a single nonnegative function
kappa:(T->Z)x(T->Z)->R, chosen before the coefficient rings and
base weight, with

  mean over IaZ^T x IbZ^T of kappa <= C*N*N1/Y,           (GI8.1)

and the exact LPprime conclusion for every permitted setting,
dominant base weight and integer t>=tI. Here IaZ=winA because
m+1=k, and IbZ=winB because (bl,bu)=(b',b'+I). GI7 permits
t=ceil_nat(tIf). Therefore for every pair aw,bw in the actual
global orbit boxes, kappa(aw|T,bw|T) belongs to the defining
least-serving set. Hence pointwise on those boxes

  kapI(p,n',T,aw,bw)<=kappa(aw|T,bw|T).                   (GI8.2)

There is an exact bijection between the source boxLoc(n',T,W)
and W^T: restrict a function to T; the inverse extends it by zero
off T. Thus its cardinality is |W|^N and every summand occurs
once. Apply the bijection independently to aw and bw. Their
normalized double sums are equal, so (GI8.1)–(GI8.2) give

  meanLoc(kapI)<=C*N*N1/Y.                              (GI8.3)

This also holds for empty boxes under the source convention that
the normalized empty sum is zero. No assertion of independent
random orbit coordinates is needed. The windows' nonemptiness
needed for the later global degree selection is proved separately
in GW, not inserted into this comparison.

Together with nonnegativity this is literally AuxBlock8.adm8
with every Cm>=C. It applies to every good T satisfying the
displayed source numerical and band hypotheses, not merely the
eventually occurring family instances. GII1 supplies OrbitSegs
for the actual good orbits.

Use the completed TC6 constants

  C0=K.La>=880,       CH=2800001,      C=302114.

A sufficient fixed global vector is

  K.kI>=2800001,      K.DI>=2800001,
  K.rho>=5600002,
  K.kappaThresholdOne>=604228,
  K.kappaThresholdTwo>=302114,          Cm>=302114.        (GI8.4)

All other K fields are nonnegative. These constants are selected
before x, D,L, family index, candidate p, good representative,
integer weights and coefficient rings. If FamilyWindowLogBound
is to be supplied by its universal numerical version, also take
K.CN>=B(epsilon), with the exact B in GI2.

Combining GI5, GI6, GI7, GI8 and GJ1 supplies the Type-I local
input and its exceptional mean along the actual supplied family;
GJ1 unpacks FamP.ex.kapI_def to identify its existing kapI with the
canonical function.
This is not a construction of the family, not the Type-II theorem,
not the aggregate prime/weight-selection argument, and not a
completed RH/GRH proof. Those boundaries remain explicit.

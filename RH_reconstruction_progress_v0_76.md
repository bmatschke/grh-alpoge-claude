For a cusp form $f$ of positive weight, choose $q$ large enough that $f^q$ lies in the proven theta-spanning range. Write $f^q=\sum_j a_jg_j$. There is a single nonzero integral $t$ clearing the finitely many $a_j/s_{g_j}$. For every normalized frame valuation $v$ above $\mathfrak p$ this gives

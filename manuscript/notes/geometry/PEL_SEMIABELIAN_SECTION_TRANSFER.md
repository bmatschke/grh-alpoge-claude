## Preserving the geometric special fibre with finitely many conditions

Let $V$ be a complete DVR in the native test, $K_V$ its fraction field, $k$ its residue field, $G/V$ the semiabelian witness carrying the given integral eigenvectors and theta section $\theta$. Its generic fibre is the marked abelian variety and its line is identified, respecting the rigidification, with the source's principal theta line. Fix a finite list of sections to be compared.

On such a trait the theta-generator predicate has a finite witness. For a smooth group with integral special and generic fibres, a global section with generating generic restriction generates $\Gamma(G,L)$ over $V$ exactly when its restriction is nonzero at some geometric point of the special fibre. If that restriction vanishes identically, the exact sequence $0\to L\xrightarrow{\varpi}L\to L_k\to0$ shows $\theta/\varpi$ is a further global section. Conversely every global section restricts to $c\theta$ for $c\in K_V$, since the generic polarization is principal. A negative order of $c$ contradicts nonvanishing of $\theta$ modulo $\varpi$. Flatness gives uniqueness from the generic restriction. The smooth special fibre is reduced, so nonvanishing of the restricted section can be recorded by one geometric point and an inverse to its reading in a trivialization.

Over an algebraic closure of $k$, the special fibre is specified by a split semiabelian presentation
$$
  1\longrightarrow \mathbb G_m^r \longrightarrow G_{\bar k}
      \xrightarrow{\,h\,} C\longrightarrow 1,
$$
where $C$ is an abelian variety, $h$ is faithfully flat and quasi-compact, and the indicated torus maps isomorphically to its kernel. This is precisely the original fibre condition. This diagram and the nonvanishing theta point are of finite presentation and descend to one finite algebraic extension $k'/k$, including the kernel isomorphism and the flat and surjective properties, by faithfully flat descent. We may first pass to a complete DVR over $V$ with this residue extension and ramification index one. For each generator of $k'/k$ lift its monic irreducible polynomial to a monic polynomial over the current DVR. The quotient by this lift is a domain finite free of the polynomial degree, with residue the indicated field and maximal ideal generated by the old uniformizer. It is a complete DVR. This works for inseparable residue polynomials too. The original normalized valuations and the raw horizontal orders are preserved. In what follows use this enlarged witness and retain the original symbol $V$. [Native split-extension definition](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/ReciprocityTheory/ReductiveGroups/ArithmeticGeometry/SemiabelianSchemes/AbelianSchemeQuotients/IsAbelianScheme.lean#L13-L48)

In the mixed case take $R=\mathcal O_{F,\lambda}$; in the coefficient-trivial case take $R=\mathcal O_F$. Spread $G$ as a smooth separated finitely presented commutative group scheme over a finite-type subalgebra $A_0\subset V$ over $R$. Smoothness, separatedness and quasi-compactness make this a finite-presentation problem. Its finite diagram includes the group law and its identities, the $\mathcal O_E$ action, the line and its rigidification, its integral section $\theta$ and the cubical trivialization. A finite presentation of the order $\mathcal O_E$ as a ring suffices for the action. The cube consists of a trivialization on $G^3$, three face conditions, two symmetries and a cocycle on $G^4$, so it spreads as a finite diagram of line bundles and morphisms. The original generic comparison with the actual framed point on $X_F$, including the marking, theta generator, tangent columns and cubical identification, spreads over an open $\operatorname{Spec}(A_{0,F}[1/D])$ after enlargement, for $D\in A_0\setminus\{0\}$. This follows by expressing the generic field as the filtered union of such generic localizations. On this open the generic framed point is an actual point of the marked frame scheme, and the fixed algebraic rules are regular functions by evaluation and naturality. Finite presentations, sheaves of finite presentation and their maps descend along these filtered limits. Trivialize the Lie bundle after enlarging the stage, and descend its given integral columns. Each block is a basis of the intersection of the Lie lattice with its generic eigenspace, hence spans a saturated summand over $V$. Invert one maximal minor per block that is a unit in $V$, with empty minor $1$. Descend the integral eigenequations on a $\mathbb Z$-basis of $\mathcal O_E$. At every specialized DVR these columns span a direct summand, and their generic span is the full eigenspace by the given marked frame. This proves the exact integral eigenspace-basis predicate, including at ramified primes where the combined matrix can be singular modulo the maximal ideal.

Here is how to keep a *degenerate* fibre. Let $B_0$ be the image of $A_0$ in $k$. The residue field is the filtered union of its finite-type $B_0$-subalgebras. Descend the special-fibre torus extension and the theta point to such a subalgebra $B\subset k$, retaining the comparison with $G_{B_0}$. At a sufficiently large stage the quotient is an abelian scheme, the quotient map is flat, surjective and of finite presentation, and the kernel map is an isomorphism. These properties descend under limits. For the abelian scheme one descends its smooth proper group law and geometric connectedness of fibres, shrinking about the generic point in this domain if necessary. The theta reading on the chosen residue point remains a unit after adjoining its inverse. Lift a finite list of $B_0$-generators of $B$, including any inverses used, to $V$, and adjoin them to $A_0$ to obtain $A$. Then its residue image is $B$ and $\mathfrak p=\ker(A\to k)$ is finitely generated since $A$ is Noetherian. Impose strictly positive valuation on each nonzero generator of $\mathfrak p$. A specialized DVR point with integral $A$-coordinates then has closed point factoring through $B=A/\mathfrak p$. Its special fibre carries the split torus extension and a point where $\theta$ is nonzero, by base change of the actual diagram. One may drop the preservation of nonsplit descent type since the native test allows a split extension on its geometric special fibre. ## Why the new DVR carries the full native witness

Require $D\ne0$ in the fraction field, retaining likewise any further generic denominators. For a DVR point satisfying the preceding conditions there are just two fibres. The generic one is the original kind of marked abelian object. The special one is an extension of an abelian variety by a split torus. Both are geometrically connected. Together with the spread smooth separated finite-presentation group law these verify every fibre clause of a semiabelian scheme. The theta-point argument gives the required global generator, and the block minors give the full integral eigenframe.

The line identities in the source include an adjoint identity for *every* $a\in\mathcal O_E$. They need no infinite list in the formula. Over the specialized DVR each $G^j$ used here is regular, with geometrically integral generic and special fibres. A generic isomorphism of two integral lines on $G^j$ has vertical divisor $m G^j_k$, the only possible vertical prime divisor. Multiplication by a power of the local uniformizer extends it as an everywhere invertible isomorphism, by the codimension-one criterion on the normal total space. For a generic isomorphism normalized on the identity by integral rigidifications, $m=0$, by pulling the resulting everywhere invertible comparison back to the identity. Apply this to the generic adjoint identity for each $a$ and to the graph-line identity. For the symmetric rigidified comparison the normalization preserves its specified value. The descended cube already satisfies all its finite identities and agrees with the prescribed generic cube. Thus the resulting integral group, CM action, rigidified cubical line, integral eigenvectors and primitive theta section fulfil the actual witness. The generic point is obtained from the finite-type marked frame by base change, including its marking and line comparison. These are precisely the generic comparisons and the finite test descent used in the source. The specialized DVR need not have boundary rank zero.

## Transfer of orders

The finite-presentation chart $A$, integrality, the positive-generator conditions, generic nonzero denominators, zeros and exact rational valuation conditions are formulas in the language of valued fields over $F$. In the mixed case their original solution in $V$ gives a solution in the valued algebraic closure of $F$ by Robinson's quantifier elimination. Completing the finite number field generated by the coordinates yields a native algebraic test at the **prescribed** $\lambda$, with all readings exactly equal. Its residue field is finite. The characteristic-zero generic markings may involve torsion at the residue characteristic, because their comparison is made in the generic open.

For a coefficient-trivial frame, $R=\mathcal O_F$ embeds in $B=A/\mathfrak p$. Chevalley's theorem gives a nonempty open set of $\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_F$ in the image of $\operatorname{Spec}B$. For every finite place $\lambda$ in this open choose a closed centre of $B$ above $\lambda$. Apply the secondary discrete valuation construction of the preceding note to $B$, the finitely many residues of $f_i/t^{d_i}$, and this centre. Every nonzero generator of $\mathfrak p$ has strictly positive **primary** order, so the composite valuation still satisfies all imposed strict inequalities. The [one-variable elimination argument](PEL_SIMULTANEOUS_SECTION_VALUATIONS.md) therefore supplies native finite-residue frames over $\lambda$ with $v_{y_N}(f_i)=Nd_i+c_i$ for every finite reading and all sufficiently large integers $N$. Vanishing readings remain zero at every frame. Here the uniformizer belongs to $\mathcal O_{F,\lambda}$ and the normalization on $F$ is one. [Robinson's theorem](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~marker/math512-f18/vf-4.pdf) · [Chevalley's theorem](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/054H) · [DVR domination](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00PH)

For the regularity-law conclusion at each mixed frame use its exact match. At each coefficient-trivial frame choose any one of its available finite places, apply the assumed algebraic-test inequality to the simultaneous approximants, divide by $N$ and take the limit. Thus the inhomogeneous coefficient-place constant disappears and the homogeneous bound and integrality follow on the **full original test class**, including semistable degenerations. This proves the human-readable regularity transfer assuming the given generic marked core and native test/evaluation definitions; it introduces no arithmetic compactification as a premise. In particular it applies to the point-mass theta sections on both copies of the marked core in the supplied fine extension. Selecting such a compatible common core in the original global arithmetic problem remains a separate obligation.

## Completing the marked section-law package

The native assertion called $\mathrm{regular}_2$ includes a second, fixed-place clause. If a finite list of cusp sections satisfies $\inf_i v(s_i)\le\nu_\lambda$ on every finite-residue frame, the simultaneous mixed-characteristic result proves the same inequality on every integral frame over $\lambda$. This includes vanishing members, since the vector of readings is reproduced with its infinite entries. Together with the two clauses of RegLaw proved above, this gives $\mathrm{regular}_2$. The earlier [marked section laws](PEL_SECTION_LAWS.md) give evaluation and integral-frame independence, existence and extension of integral frames, descent to finite-type tests, multiplication, finite dimensionality and norm definiteness under the stated marked and uniformization inputs. This supplies the remaining assertion in that package on the given marked datum. For a concrete application, use the [normalized marked short theta sections](../construction/RETWISTED_MARKED_BOUNDARY.md), with all of that result's rank, weight, level, cuspidality, field and chart assumptions. For each $p$, put $\alpha_p=p$ when $p\mid M$ and $\alpha_p=1$ otherwise. At every integral frame over $p$, now with arbitrary residue field, their values satisfy
$$
 v(\alpha_p^d S_u)\ \ge\
 -\left\lfloor d/2\right\rfloor
       \frac{\operatorname{ord}_p|\Delta_E|}{[E:\mathbb Q]}v(p).
$$
Indeed the preceding short-theta result supplies this on finite-residue tests, and exact mixed realization applied to all finitely many $S_u$ transfers it on the same frame. At any coefficient-trivial trait choose an available place away from $M\Delta_E$. All readings at its algebraic approximants are integral, so every horizontal order of these $S_u$ is nonnegative. Prescribed finite lists of linear combinations can be included in the same test, retaining cancellations.

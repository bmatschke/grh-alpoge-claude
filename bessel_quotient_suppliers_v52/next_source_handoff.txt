What simplified the last packet
-------------------------------
The decisive simplification is BQS14's use of the inherited DC9 finite-layer
inequality. Given the original incomplete infinitesimally unitary target and
its compact lowest type W, take the diagonal copy of W in the target direct
sum the constructed discrete-series model with parameter
eta = nu + rho_c - rho_n. Both copies kill the same negative first-layer
coupling space. DC9 therefore confines this compact type to degree zero and
gives one highest line; it does not require a scalar Casimir on the diagonal
generated module. Applying the Casimir to a diagonal highest vector then
forces the two Casimir scalars to agree. This replaces a longer diagonal
rigidity argument without completing the target or adding a group
representation to it. BQS15–17 use the original gap to identify the shifted
parameter, and the later claims preserve the actual smooth image and one
fixed simultaneous-period kernel. No quotient supplier remains open.

one agent constructs the bounded Forms0 producer; one proves the

GLOBAL_SPECTRAL closed. The setting, orbital, local and counting lanes
are substantial independent work even though none alone is the global

# Finishing the six shared obligations

Start with the common theta construction below. In parallel one can work on the period, counting and spectral estimates. Each of these estimates is a theorem about the actual family with a uniform range of scales. The [machine-readable inventory](SELECTED_FAMILY_CHECKPOINT.json) gives the exact six identifiers and the verifier. The [selected-family proof](LOCAL_SELECTED_FAMILY.md) explains the distinction between extracting information from a linked bundle and producing that bundle in the first place.

## First target on the common theta construction

The [fixed-stage theorem](BASE_THETA_CONSTRUCTION_STEP.md) puts the finite Hecke action and the archimedean discrete series on the same stage. The [enlargement theorem](THETA_COEFFICIENT_EXTENSION.md) now compares those models across coefficient fields, with the original rational vectors, norms and evaluations. At the reference character, the old and new spaces can also use the same Weil action, test function, measure, fundamental domain and two theta maps. This last assertion starts from an old analytic realization. [Normalization of the additive character](THETA_CHARACTER_SIGNS.md) · [The seven reference comparisons](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/CrdOver.lean#L77-L83)

**Next calculation.** Work at one matched row of that reference tuple. First show that its two lift characters agree with the original characters on the identified ideles, including the finite components. Use this to identify the splitting of the Weil action. Then evaluate the copied polynomial map on the same normalized vectors in the lowest type and the definite coefficient space. Show it gives the designated polynomial $\Delta_i$ with the prescribed scalar and that the copied test function is the lattice-and-vacuum vector. Doing this at every row proves the source's reference-tuple Weil assertion. [Character comparison](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/LiftCharactersAgreeOverEnlargedBase.lean#L123-L152) · [Weil action, vector and polynomial map](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/ReferenceTupleWeilDatumVectorPolynomialMapEnlarg.lean#L281-L298)

The subsequent task is to identify the resulting operators with the theta integral and its projected map, and then establish the analytic existence assertions for the required tuple-dependent data. On the original stage the interior construction must still produce an intertwiner and its multiplicity map before twisting. Carrying supplied maps to a larger field uses the original realization throughout.

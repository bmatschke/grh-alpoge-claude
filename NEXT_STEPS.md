# Finishing the six shared obligations

Start with the common theta construction below. In parallel one can work on the period, counting and spectral estimates. Each of these estimates is a theorem about the actual family with a uniform range of scales. The [machine-readable inventory](SELECTED_FAMILY_CHECKPOINT.json) gives the exact six identifiers and the verifier. The [selected-family proof](LOCAL_SELECTED_FAMILY.md) explains the distinction between extracting information from a linked bundle and producing that bundle in the first place.

## First target on the common theta construction

At the reference tuple, extending the coefficient field preserves the exact Weil action, including both splitting characters, the prescribed lattice and vacuum vector, and the scale of the polynomial $\Delta_i$. The definite theta integral and its projection can use the original kernel and measure. Evaluation on definite classes explains why their rationality at the distinguished embedding survives scalar extension. These comparisons use the old realization as input. [Fixed-stage models](BASE_THETA_CONSTRUCTION_STEP.md) · [Extension of the row spaces](THETA_COEFFICIENT_EXTENSION.md) · [Reference Weil kernel and polynomial](THETA_REFERENCE_WEIL.md) · [Reference integral and rationality](THETA_REFERENCE_LIFT.md)

On an original interior step with its prescribed models, levels and vacua, the [real theta correspondence](THETA_INTERIOR_DISCRETE_SERIES.md) gives one nonzero intertwiner across the finite twists. On each twist the [Witt-tower constant-term formula](THETA_INTERIOR_CUSPIDALITY.md) makes the downward integral cuspidal, and [Howe duality and the kernel bound](THETA_CUSPIDAL_FACTORIZATION.md) make it factor through the stored intertwiner on all smooth inputs. This proves the two interior existence assertions on a weak realization with the prescribed data. It advances the construction of such a realization from its arithmetic stage.

**Next calculation.** Starting with the actual self-dual Hom lattice, construct the unit theta vacuum with its real annihilation equations and its invariance at the dyadic and split places. Then realize the chain test on the chosen split half and the row test on the conjugate half in adjoint coordinates, including the determinant factor of each nontrivial twist. These tests enter before either interior arrow can be applied.

FC4: local functional normalization
====================================
For A the local integral CM algebra, pi*q=p and (pi,q)=A, the displayed
duality L#=A^(n-1)+q^(-1)A follows from the stated Hermitian convention.
The quotient map [x]->[q x_n] identifies L#/L with A/pA exactly.

A unitary g preserving L preserves its Hermitian dual. Applying g
to pi e_j proves integrality of every entry of its last row; applying
g to e_j in L# proves integrality of the other rows. The stronger
off-diagonal bounds follow by using e_j in L for j<n and q^(-1)e_n
in L#. Thus the quotient action is multiplication by d=g_nn.
The final diagonal equation of g* g=1 gives d*bar(d)=1 modulo p,
since each off-diagonal term is divisible by pi*q.

An A-module of additive order p has no proper nontrivial additive
subgroup. Multiplication by pi is therefore zero or surjective;
in the latter case q annihilates it. Any nonzero functional from
L#/L consequently induces an isomorphism from A/piA or A/qA.
Its stabilizer condition is d=1 modulo that ideal. The unitary
congruence and conjugation make the two conditions equivalent;
coprimality makes them equivalent to d=1 modulo p. This proves
the same unitary lattice/functional stabilizer for the canonical
functional f0(x)=q x_n modulo pi. It also gives f0(pi x)=0.

The source swap changes only the functional target, functional and
their proofs. Local/global groups, character, points and stabilizer
maps remain the same; ReadsK is therefore retained. No isomorphism
of arbitrary unrelated residue fields is substituted. The n=1
case is valid with empty off-diagonal conditions.

FC7: normalization, cmMax basis and embedded Gram form
======================================================
The coefficient sum is exactly the source fockF normalization and is
linear in the first input. Its denominator is the positive product

shifts preserve natW and that denominator. Unitary frame determinant
factors have modulus one. The displayed reversed order

FOk.sig_star at the distinguished embedding first forces

The coordinate projection proof of invariant-space descent is valid

commutes with all those action matrices. Thus cmMax-coordinate

be extracted. Crucially, CSD.basis selects such a basis once

belong to cmMax, so embedding the coefficient pairing agrees with

does not interchange star with an arbitrary field embedding on

FC8: period conjugation and exact pairing order
================================================
The map C conjugates polynomial coefficients, not frame variables.
It is conjugate linear, injective, and onto the prescribed complex
value space because the polynomial representation is defined by
integer generators. Unitarity gives gamma^(-1)=gamma*, and the
finite character gives conjugate(chi^(-1))=chi. These yield the
displayed intertwining identity with the source rhoP and trFr.

Because the source PerIn.H is the full group, preferred-orbit
values determine the equivariant section. Conversely, for arbitrary
invariant class values, their unique coefficient lifts are fixed
by the stabilizers, by the intertwining identity and injectivity.
Orbit extension is therefore independent of the moving element.
This proves both injectivity and the exact image assertion.

The coefficient identity N(p,conj(P))=pairB(p,P) proves
period(w,F)=ipP(jM(F),w) in exactly the displayed argument order.
All class weights are the same positive inverse stabilizer orders.
A nonzero value family has a nonzero polynomial at some class,
so positive definiteness proves the strict real self-pairing
statement. These are the five actual period_conj conclusions.

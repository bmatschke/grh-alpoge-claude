## AG3. Pair-trace inequality, Bessel integral, and coordinate energy

**Status:** Exact source formulas transcribed; trace and convergence hypotheses retained; Schur integration mechanism explained.

- The generic HS square is at most F*loop_error*X_uv plus the scalar pair sum.
- F is a finite-place factor.
- The Bessel amplitude is a double compact/unipotent equivariant integral.
- The one-based exponent in the big-cell weight is 2*(2*(r-a)+k+1).
- The f-place energy is divided by the common lattice covolume to power r+1.

## AG6. Elementary ideal-lattice Fourier-profile sum

**Status:** New complete human proof, independently checked; stronger power not implemented in Lean.

- For real K>=3, the bound is (384*Theta^(-(K-1/2)))^f, Theta>=1.
- This implies the archive's sufficient exponent K-5/2.

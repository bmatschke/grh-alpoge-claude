# Split Hecke operators on the actual space of definite forms

The local specialization argument needs the action of the unramified Hecke algebra on definite algebraic modular forms. Two of its inputs can be discharged at the complex level, for every datum in the source. The first consists of type preservation, linearity, commutation and integrality of eigenvalues away from the residue characteristic. The second says that each normalized Hecke operator has no nontrivial Jordan chain. Both apply before the global design family is chosen. [The four assertions in the source](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/NormalizedHeckeFactors/CoverStd.lean#L9-L24) · [The semisimplicity input in HLP16](../../../research/modules/galois/local_hecke_projection/main.tex#L486-L520)

## Which operators act

Write $E/F$ for the CM field and its maximal totally real subfield. Use the definite unitary group $G$ of the source and its finite adelic points. An algebraic form $f$ takes values in the weight space $W$, transforms on the left by the weight $\rho$ of $G(F)$, and is right invariant under a principal congruence subgroup. The type condition is $f(gj)=\chi(j)f(g)$ for $j\in J$. Outside the bad set $B$, the intersection of $J$ with the subgroup supported at a single place $v$ is the full integral group $K_v$, and $\chi$ is trivial there. The given level condition includes a principal congruence subgroup in $\ker\chi$. At a split place the specified splitting identifies $(G_v,K_v)$ with $(\mathrm{GL}_n(F_v),\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal O_v))$. [Source definitions of the forms, level, type and operator](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/GaloisLevelStructure/DefsAut.lean#L134-L155)

Let $q=|\mathcal O_v/\mathfrak p_v|$, choose the given uniformizer $\varpi$, and take $1\le k\le n$. Set $D=K_v\operatorname{diag}(\varpi I_k,I_{n-k})K_v$. Normalize local Haar measure by $\operatorname{vol}(K_v)=1$. Write $R_x f(g)=f(gx)$. On every $K_v$-fixed form the source operator is exactly

$$
T=q^{-k(n-1)}R(\mathbf1_D)
 =q^{-k(n-1)}\sum_{xK_v\in D/K_v}R_x.
$$

Indeed the representatives are supported at $v$. Two such representatives give the same coset modulo right multiplication by $J$ precisely when their quotient belongs to $J\cap G_v=K_v$. The finite sum is independent of their choice. Its value is again $K_v$-fixed by left $K_v$-invariance of $D$. [The diagonal and local double coset](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/AlgebraicWeights/IsAlgWeight.lean#L64-L71)

In fact $T$ preserves the full type. For $j\in J$, conjugation by its $v$-component preserves $K_v=J\cap G_v$. The normalizer of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal O_v)$ in $\mathrm{GL}_n(F_v)$ is $F_v^\times K_v$. To see this, apply elementary divisors to the $K_v$-stable lattice $j_v\mathcal O_v^n$. In diagonal coordinates permutation matrices force all exponents to coincide. It follows that conjugation by $j_v$ preserves $D$ and permutes its right cosets. Hence $R_j Tf=T R_jf=\chi(j)Tf$ on the type. The sum still transforms on the left by $\rho$. Smoothness follows from the finite sum of translates, whose open stabilizers contain a common principal congruence subgroup. Linearity follows from the displayed expression. Operators at distinct places commute because the local factors commute. At the same place every operator belongs to the commutative spherical Hecke algebra, including when a second splitting or uniformizer is used. [Gross, *On the Satake isomorphism*, Proposition 2.10](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~gross/preprints/sat.pdf#page=5)

## Why there are no Jordan blocks

The algebraic weight has a positive invariant Hermitian form. In the prescribed weight basis the matrices of $\rho(\gamma)$ are polynomial functions of the archimedean entries of the rational unitary matrix $\gamma$. These entries lie in a compact set because the group is definite at infinity. The matrices of $\rho(\gamma)$ and their inverses are consequently uniformly bounded. Averaging an inner product over the compact closure of their group gives the invariant form. Integrating the pointwise pairing over $G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A_f)$ gives a positive Petersson pairing invariant under right translation. The class set at any fixed congruence level is finite with finite stabilizers, so this integral is the finite sum of fiber pairings weighted by the level volume divided by the stabilizer orders. [Gross, *Algebraic modular forms*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02780173) · [The polynomial weight condition](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/AlgebraicWeights/IsAlgWeight.lean#L56-L63)

In the Hilbert completion of the space of forms, convolution by $\mathbf1_D$ has adjoint convolution by $\mathbf1_{D^{-1}}$. Indeed every $R_x$ is unitary and inversion preserves Haar measure on $\mathrm{GL}_n(F_v)$. Both characteristic functions lie in the same commutative spherical Hecke algebra. Thus $T$ is normal on the $K_v$-fixed space. For $S=T-\mu I$ normality gives $\ker S=\ker S^*$. If $S^2f=0$, then $Sf$ is in $\ker S^*$ and $\|Sf\|^2=\langle f,S^*Sf\rangle=0$. This proves the precise two-step implication for every $f$ in the specified type and every complex $\mu$, including zero. [The corresponding pinned theorem](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/SplitPlaceDoubleCosetOperatorSemisimpleTypeSpace.lean#L158-L173)

## Why the eigenvalues are integral away from the place

Take a nonzero eigenform $Tf=\mu f$. Choose $g_0$ and a weight-basis coordinate $a$ with $f_a(g_0)\ne0$. Let $R=\mathcal O_{E^{\rm gal}}[1/q]\subset\mathbb C$, using a normal closure containing the images of every embedding of $E$. The decisive point is that all the numbers $f_a(g_0x)$, with $x\in G_v$ supported at $v$, lie in a single finitely generated $R$-submodule of $\mathbb C$.

To see this, fix a principal congruence subgroup $U$ fixing $f$, and representatives $y$ for the finite double class set at $U$. Write $g_0x=\gamma y u$ with $u\in U$, so $f(g_0x)=\rho(\gamma)f(y)$. Choose one positive integer $d$ clearing the adelic matrix denominators of $g_0$ and the finitely many $y^{-1}$ (increase $d$ to clear their products). At places away from the rational prime below $v$, the entries of $\gamma=g_0xu^{-1}y^{-1}$ then belong to $d^{-1}\mathcal O_E$ locally, since $u$ is integral and $x$ is trivial there. Allowing the powers of $q$ gives entries of $\gamma$ in $d^{-1}\mathcal O_E[1/q]$. After any embedding they lie in $d^{-1}R$. The finitely many fixed weight polynomials and the finitely many values $f(y)$ now suffice. For example, for each monomial of degree $e$ use its coefficient times $d^{-e}$ times the corresponding coordinate of $f(y)$ as a module generator. This allows arbitrary complex coefficients in the chosen weight basis.

Let $M$ be the $R$-span of the actual values $f_a(g_0x)$. The preceding containment and Noetherianity of $R$ give finite generation, and $f_a(g_0)\ne0$ gives $M\ne0$. Evaluating the eigenvalue equation at $g_0x$ shows $\mu M\subset M$, since the translates in $T$ are supported at $v$ and its scalar belongs to $R$. The determinant trick applied to generators of $M$ gives a monic polynomial over $R$ annihilating $\mu$ on $M$. A nonzero complex value in $M$ forces this polynomial to vanish at $\mu$. As $R$ is integral over $\mathbb Z[1/q]$, $\mu$ is integral over $\mathbb Z[1/q]$. Clearing denominators in a monic equation shows that $q^m\mu$ is an algebraic integer for some $m\ge0$. For every rational prime $\ell$ distinct from the residue characteristic of $v$, $\ell\nmid q^m$. This is exactly the fourth assertion of the source. [Original integrality argument and normalization](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/HeckeDoubleCosetOperatorsStandardFacts.lean#L583-L689)

2. The proposed exact B1 endpoint
================================

Keep the original arithmetic tuple l,L,Omega,s,V0,O,r and an actual
T:TWGrp(globOf r V0) with its TWPl. After the existing NiceI normalization
and CCH extension, use the SAME x, Kap k, eigenvalue list al and realized T.
Here a=n=4 and k.g=0. Write kappa=x.K, A=kappa[[t]], A_m=A/(t^m),
d=[Lplus:Q] and q=T.q. Prove the following literal statement:

  There exists C in N such that for every m in N, for all sufficiently
  large N, Sel1(T.h N,A_m,k) is finite and

    #Sel1(T.h N,A_m,k)
      <= #Bd1(T.h N,A_m,k) * |kappa|^(m*(4*d+4*q)+C).

The quantifier order is C, then m, then eventual N. C may depend on the
fixed arithmetic tuple, point, detector, eigenvalues and Taylor–Wiles
system. It must not depend on m or N. The eventual lower bound for N may
depend on m. There is no demand for one uniform C across all NiceI points,
and no permission to choose a new external coefficient threshold after
seeing a point. The m=0 case remains included.

Retain CohP, l odd (including l=3), IsCoef, V0.S'=s.S, LocCFT s, the
nontrivial residual cyclotomic value, the PL-kernel condition on x.rho,
x.N4 at the ORIGINAL V0.frame.D0, T.Eig x al and full Detects k(range T.sg).
These are exactly the source's FirstOrderDualArithmeticBound antecedents.
The final numerical exponent is 4*d+4*q, not an enlarged asymptotic
coefficient. Sources: TaylorWilesObstructionBound.lean:126–134;
ThetaCoefficientMapEq.lean:54–56; BoundW1Arithmetic.lean:7–16.

The g=0 specialization does not make B1 vacuous. The first-order space is
the constant full 4-by-4 matrix block, and the source parameter
nu(n,a,g)=choose(a,2)+choose(n-a,2)+g*a*(n-a) has value 6 here. The second
obstruction array has an empty index set, which is why CCH16 settles B2;
that argument supplies no cardinality bound for Sel1.

3. Concrete first checkpoint and complete route to B1
====================================================

First checkpoint: reconstruct the selected full dualN estimate. For one
constant c2, for every m and eventually N, every finite locally constant
cocycle system for the literal epsilon-eta dual representation satisfies

  #{y in Zl(rep') : for every j there is Y with
        y(ph_N,j)=rep'(ph_N,j)Y-Y and
        y(ta_N,j)=rep'(ta_N,j)Y-Y}
    <= #Bl(rep') * |kappa|^c2.

The SAME Y is required for the Frobenius and tame equations at a given j.
The statement is uniform over every representation rep' satisfying the
source IsAd equations on that literal dual module. Those equations include
the cyclotomic twist and the action of complex conjugation by the signed
transpose. Replacing rep' by an abstract isomorphic representation without
transporting these equations would leave the source endpoint unproved.
The exact declaration is EpsilonEtaMatrices/DualN.lean:10–154.

The reconstruction should have the following linked parts.

(a) Uniform trace-annihilator bound over all A_m. Start from CCH's actual
Detects and PhiCond, and prove

  # {z in TwZ(k,A_m) : ell(k,A_m,sg_j,z)=0 for every j}
     <= #TwB(k,A_m) * |kappa|^c

with c chosen before m. The source is
ExistsNcardAnnihilatedCocyclesLeCoboundariesMul.lean:349–430, with its
abstract complete-DVR argument at lines 21–126 and SolCnt.lean:313–393.
The latter proof establishes finite generation of the exact kernel,
uniformizes its pointwise t-power annihilation modulo coboundaries, and
uses stabilization of approximate solution classes and completeness to
control solutions modulo every t^m. CCH's rational detector alone does not
justify that last step: an arbitrary modular solution need not lift to an
integral exact solution. The finite approximate-solution stabilization is
the principal new algebraic lemma to write, rather than assume.

(b) Descend the relevant level-N cocycles to the original G0. The source
Descend.lean:152–217 uses the PL-kernel condition and the actual closed
kernel generated by the tame elements. Normalize the value at complex
conjugation using the invertibility of 2. The exact polarized descended
cocycle must land in TwZ on the original point. The relative pro-l and
closed-kernel data already supplied by CAP are inputs; descent itself has
not been separately certified by CAP or CCH.

(c) Prove eventual Frobenius agreement simultaneously for every trace test
and every cocycle modulo t^m. TwistedCocycleApplyOne.lean:271–288, phEq,
uses finiteness of the full TwZ(k,A_m), not just the finite detector list,
to choose one eventual N for all its elements. It also compares rho1 and
the cyclotomic scalar. Combine this with (a), (b) and the exact
conjugation-normalized coboundary count in DualN.lean. The result is the
complete checkpoint above, including C-before-m-before-eventual-N.

(d) Supply the original all-level arithmetic counting record ArSys. Its
definition at DegreeCountCohomology/IsDualPair.lean:133–140 requires one
ArData for each N, Counts for each, a common local-degree function, exact
image D0 at the distinguished auxiliary place, and membership of both
ph and ta in each added local decomposition image. ArSys.lean:8–20 builds
it from the actual TWPl through ArPres.lean:80–163, PresCounts.lean:9–43,
and UnrLoc.lean:27–46. Write the same-place and same-map identifications;
do not introduce an abstract counting system without returning to TWPl.

PresCounts uses LocalFiniteness, LocalTateDuality, LocalEulerChar,
GlobalFiniteness and GreenbergWiles from the retained CohP. It transports
their precise finite-module formulas along the actual local maps. The full
CohP remains the selected source assumption; no new published-theorem
application is certified by this planning note.

(e) Prove the finite local and invariant estimates with their exact ranks.
The source uses h0G, h0v0, h0q and locV. The first two have constants
independent of m; h0v0 uses N4 at the old D0. The q regular-semisimple
centralizer estimates have exponents m*n+Cq_j. At each l-adic place v,
locV gives exponent

  m*(n*(d'_v+1)+nu*(1+d_v))+Cv,

where the LocCFT degrees d'_v and arithmetic degrees d_v both sum to d.
In the full-block specialization these are n=4 and nu=6. Keep the two
degree functions distinct until using their separate sum identities.

(f) Prove the source oneLevel count, not merely a dimension heuristic.
Its Greenberg–Wiles local conditions are: coboundaries plus the actual
ordinary Zb at l, the actual unramified condition at the tame places, and
all cocycles at the distinguished and added places. The dual conditions
therefore supply the ph/ta equations in (a)–(c). OneLevel.lean:236–483
produces a common positive factor with exponent

  m*(n+nu)*#Vl + m*nu*d.

W1BoundArithmeticRankTwo.lean:30–38 gives the exact finite-sum identity
which cancels that factor. Its assembly at lines 40–165 chooses

  C = c1+c2+c0+sum_v Cv+sum_j Cq_j

before m, intersects the two required eventual-N assertions, and obtains
exactly n*d+n*q. Despite the file's historical name, its declaration is
for arbitrary n; the next reconstruction needs only the actual n=4
specialization. Finally BoundW1Arithmetic.lean:7–16 joins ArSys, LocCFT
and the already supplied PhiCond to the original arithmetic B1 contract.

Completing only (a) or the dualN checkpoint is useful progress but must
leave JSP_FIRST_ORDER_BOUND open until (d)–(f) and the original arithmetic
join are written. These proposed parts are not new completed B claims.

LocalEulerChar, GlobalFiniteness, GlobalEulerChar, GreenbergWiles,
PoitouTateNormalised and TameLocal. It was not proved by CAP's use of its

be reused only at their checked scopes. Source names or a standard theorem
label do not themselves discharge the original normalization and finite
module hypotheses. LocCFT and all original coefficient, level and
nontrivial-cyclotomic conditions likewise remain in the consumer.

A second attractive bounded producer after B1, or in a separate arithmetic
workstream, is the full original neatU plus TThetaEx packet. The source
neatU route writes the rational stabilizer matrix, controls its powers at
all finite places and its archimedean size, deduces finite order, and uses
the actual auxiliary principal-congruence subgroup to force identity.
Then finite-quotient local reciprocity and completeness construct the
ordered characters of the compact torus, and neatU supplies Free at every
K. CIP already removes the every-artOf ambiguity here. This could close a
real field of R simultaneously for base and higher levels. It would still
leave integral TypeForAlt and ordinary/control/arithmetic fibers open.

The broader ordinary and same-J HasAut producer may eventually give the
largest contribution across the Hida and support branches. It should be
pursued with that whole output contract visible. In this next bounded
round, exact B1 has the clearest immediate input/output gain. Neither
priority asserts that an open C producer has become a completed B proof.

Externally, fix the original tower and iota first. Choose ONE coefficient
threshold for BOTH rank-four Links, before every later coefficient c',
Space and arbitrary allowed B,Lambda. Keep the two Links, their common
kappa, and their literal distinct-auxiliary-prime condition. Their
universal rings may differ; there is no assertion of one ideal shared
between different rings. Within either ring ONE J carries all Hida fields
AND P1core and stays fixed before the NiceI point, normalization, coefficient
extension, detector, realized TWGrp/TWPl and all levels N.

The coefficient extension preserves the point kernel and does not
reselect J or move the outside threshold. QX10 already turns the exact
joint two-Link supplier into PMExAlt for every original Space; YX2 closes
the CountII consumer from PMExAlt. Thus closing B1 removes one required
support premise for both RH and unrestricted Dirichlet GRH, but does not
finish CountII. KA5–KA6 remove only Case III for two-primary targets;
Cases I, II and V remain substantive for RH. Unrestricted Dirichlet GRH
also retains Case III/Ctx3/SeedIV and its separate rank-three dependencies.

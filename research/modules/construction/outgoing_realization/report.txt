Type-II cloud test. It is an additional step after the ordinary

OR1. GENERIC PREFIX ORDER AND THE COST OF ONE DETERMINANT

Let I be a finite set of signed letters: vector rows u_i and covector
columns v_i, each with r coordinates. For S subset I and a generic
invertible r-by-r matrix g, put

        P_g(t)=g diag(1,...,1,t) g^{-1}.

On letters in S, substitute u_i P_g(t) and P_g(t)^{-1} v_i; leave other
letters unchanged. The minimum exponent in the resulting Laurent
polynomial is v_S(F), with v_S(0)=+infinity. This is exactly ObjPol.valS,
including its generic GL coordinate ring and its last-coordinate choice.
It is a minimum of actual Laurent support, not a minimum of numerical
specializations. A lower bound v_S(F)>=theta remains true on any
specialization of g to an invertible matrix over an extension field.

Fix r distinct vector letters a_1,...,a_r and D=det(u_{a_i,b}).
For an arbitrary S', some selected rows transform by P_g(t), the others
remain fixed. Since P_g(t)=I+(t-1)ab^t for a column a and row b^t, the
new selected matrix is

        M+(t-1) diag(epsilon_i) M a b^t.

The perturbation has rank at most one. In the determinant's multilinear
expansion, every term using two perturbation rows vanishes. Thus the
determinant is affine in t. Its value at t=1 is the nonzero polynomial D.
Its minimum exponent is therefore either 0 or 1. The ambient generic
coordinate ring with all letter variables is a domain. The lowest
nonzero Laurent coefficients multiply without cancellation, so

        v_S'(D^m H)=m v_S'(D)+v_S'(H)<=m+v_S'(H).       (OR1.1)

For H=0 both sides are +infinity, which gives the same conclusion.
This proves the second clause of FieldMultidegreeDivisibility for every
S', including subsets unrelated to the subset used for division.

OR2. FORCED DIVISIBILITY AND INTEGRAL QUOTIENTS

Let F be multihomogeneous in the letters, of degrees d_i, and suppose all
a_i lie in S. Define the integer

        B=sum_{i in S, i vector, i not one of the a_j} d_i.

Claim: if theta is an integer and v_S(F)>=theta>B, then
D^(theta-B) divides F.

First prove one copy of D. The generic determinant D is irreducible over
every field. Here is an elementary justification, including finite
characteristic. Induct on r. For r=1 it is a variable. For r>1, regard D
as a polynomial in the bottom-right entry:

        D=Delta x_rr+E,

where Delta is the upper-left (r-1)-minor. In the polynomial UFD in the
other entries Delta is prime by induction. It does not divide E: set the
upper-left block to diag(1,...,1,0), the two adjacent off-block entries
x_(r-1,r) and x_(r,r-1) to 1 and all other off-block entries to zero.
Then Delta=0 but E=-1. Thus this linear polynomial is primitive and
irreducible over the fraction field, and hence irreducible in the
polynomial ring. Adjoining the other letter variables preserves
primality.

Work now in the fraction field L of the polynomial ring modulo (D).
The selected matrix M has rank exactly r-1: its upper-left (r-1)-minor
is nonzero in this domain. Choose an invertible matrix g over L whose
last column spans the right kernel of M. Then

        M g diag(1,...,1,t) g^{-1}=M.

The selected vector letters do not change. Every other vector letter in
S contributes a polynomial of t-degree at most its own degree d_i.
Each covector letter in S contributes only exponents <=0. Letters
outside S remain unchanged. Consequently the specialized twisted
polynomial has no exponent greater than B. The original minimum-order
hypothesis says it has no exponent below theta. Since theta>B, it is
identically zero. At t=1 the substitution is the identity, so F is zero
modulo D. Hence D divides F. No point-counting or infinite-base-field
argument is used.

For nonzero F, the quotient is multihomogeneous of degrees d_i-1 on the
selected letters and d_i elsewhere. This follows by decomposing the
quotient by multidegree and using injectivity of multiplication by D.
If some required degree is negative, the nonzero case cannot occur.
The number B is unchanged, and (OR1.1) gives v_S(F/D)>=theta-1.
Repeat until theta-B copies have been extracted. F=0 is immediate.

Over an integral domain O, embed into its fraction field. The injection
of polynomial rings induces an injection of the generic GL localizations
(the determinant being localized stays nonzero), and hence of the
Laurent coefficient rings. Thus generic minimum orders are unchanged
by this base extension.

Every nonzero coefficient of a generic determinant is +1 or -1 because
the selected rows are distinct. In particular its leading coefficient
for any monomial order is a unit, as is that of D^m. Divide F by D^m
over O using this unit coefficient. The division algorithm terminates
and returns F=D^m Q+R, where no monomial of R is divisible by the leading
monomial of D^m. Over Frac(O), the divisibility just proved implies that
R is a multiple of D^m. If R were nonzero, its leading monomial would be
divisible by that of D^m, a contradiction. Therefore R=0 and Q is
integral. This does not assume that O itself is a UFD.

The exact conclusion used below is: a multihomogeneous F with
v_S(F)>=B+n has an integral factorization F=D^n Q; the quotient's
degrees weakly decrease, and every prefix lower bound t becomes at
least t-n. This proves dv_div_one, including the arbitrary-prefix
part, by the preceding arguments rather than by its hE premise.

OR3. A FINITE LIST OF EXTRACTIONS AND THE LITERAL Dv

Suppose we have tasks (S_t,A_t,n_t), with n_t nonnegative, r distinct
vector letters A_t inside S_t, and total cost T=sum_t n_t. If the initial
polynomial F of degrees d satisfies

        v_(S_t)(F)>=other_t(d)+T                 for every task t,

we may extract all determinant powers. After cost c_done has been
removed, OR1 leaves each pending prefix at least its original minimum
minus c_done. Its other-vector degree is no greater than before,
because all multidegrees have weakly decreased. Thus the remaining
task still has at least its required exponent above its other-vector
degree. Induction gives the full product divisor and loses at most T
in every prefix. This is exactly dv_div_list; the proof handles repeated
determinants and arbitrary overlap of the task subsets.

For a valid frame F, order the finite segment letters increasingly.
Its old block consists exactly of the r preceding-frame positions:
there are no selected positions between consecutive entries of one
frame. Its late block is all selected positions after the last old
entry through z_j=f_(j,r-1), inclusive.

For a late x, the r most recent even selected predecessors are precisely
Arow(x,0),...,Arow(x,r-1). They exist because the old block already
contains r even predecessors. They are distinct, lie in the segment,
and are strictly before x. The source's finite search over [x-rn',x)
finds them because periodicity and one nonempty even frame per period
give enough predecessors; those closest r already lie in this segment.
The default branch in Arow is therefore not used.

Take one task for each late x: its strict prefix, those r predecessor
rows, exponent m1. Add one task of exponent m2 for the old-frame rows
at the strict prefix preceding the first late letter. That final prefix
contains exactly the old frame, so its other-vector degree is zero.
The determinant product is literally

 Dv_j=(product over late x of Dseg_x^m1) Wseg_j^m2,

with the same row order as the source. No sign is changed by changing
the order of the independent tasks.

Its cost is (#late)m1+m2<=|letters_j|m1+m2. Let

 m1=(2Q+3)c+kQ+c,
 m2=c alpha_*+2c,
 m*=(N1+1)((2Q+3)c+kQ+c alpha_*+3c).

Since |letters_j|<=N1, the cost is <=m*. For example, the bracket
defining m* is m1+c alpha_*+2c, and multiplying by N1+1 certainly
bounds N1 m1+c alpha_*+2c. This is the exact
segment_divisions_le_bound estimate, with no asymptotic notation.

In a literal MGen generator, write the polynomial as A G, with A in
the plane block and G in the normal block. The defining CondM gap
m* says:

 vector cut i: lambda_max(prefix <=i of G)<=M_i,
               v_(prefix <i)(A)>=M_i+m*;
 covector cut i: lambda_max(prefix <i of G)<=M_i,
                 v_(prefix <=i)(A)>=M_i+m*.

The extraction affects only A and loses at most m* in every prefix.
G, its old-block zero degrees and its maximum bounds are unchanged.
The quotient pair therefore satisfies precisely CondM with gap zero,
hence defines an MGen0 generator. The independent strict-prefix
minimum at every late x is what permits the tasks themselves; OR4--OR5
check that minimum from the actual one-sided hypotheses.

OR4. THE ONE-SIDED GEOMETRIC OTHER-DEGREE ESTIMATE

Assume p>=13, n' even, r>=2, the valid frame, and
a<=deg_s<=2a for selected residues s. Write
levDeg_j(y)=deg_(bar y)/p^(z_j-y) on segment letters. Let o_x be the
oldest of the r most recent even predecessors of a late x, and set

        B_o=levDeg_j(o_x).

Every even predecessor y<x outside these r has y<o_x. Moreover it lies
in the old frame or the intermediate letter set. If it were at or after
the first current-frame entry, all selected positions between it and x
would be current-frame entries, of which there are fewer than r; it
would be one of the r nearest predecessors. This proves the exact
ge_older implication and explains why no degree assumption on the
terminal frame is needed.

The outgoing hypothesis gives only
d_y<=1.01 levDeg_j(y) on the old/intermediate sets. Because both y and
o_x are even, o_x-y=2h with h>=1. Different y give distinct h. Also

 d_y<=2.02 a p^(-(z_j-y))
     <=2.02 B_o p^(-2h).

Summing this over any finite subset and then the full geometric series
proves

 B_other<=2.02 B_o/(p^2-1)<=B_o/80,

since 2.02*80=161.6<=168<=p^2-1. If a=0, all relevant degrees vanish
and the same estimate is immediate. For the actual application a>0.
The plane-component degrees in MGen are bounded by the total degrees
d_y, so this controls exactly the otherVectorDegree required in OR3.

 m* <=(N1+1)((2Q+7)c+kQ+rkQc)

 m*/B_o <=8/(CkrNQ^3Y)<=1/100,

OR6. RestModel AND THE WHOLE OUTGOING FAMILY

By definition XX' is the span of plane/normal products A G satisfying
the prefix inequalities and support restrictions, intersected with a
normal-order monomial-support bound. To prove inclusion in the span of
MGen, we may forget the final intersection.

Assume xi has fixed total degree d_y in every segment letter. Expand
its finite expression in the defining product span and project to
those total degrees. Each A and G splits into finitely many
multihomogeneous pieces A_(d') and G_(d''). The selected whole-letter
projection keeps exactly d'+d''=d. Generic substitutions act within
each letter, so they preserve this grading. Projecting cannot introduce
a Laurent exponent outside the original minimum/maximum support.
The old-frame degree of G remains zero. Thus every surviving
A_(d') G_(d'') satisfies the same auxiliary M_y, CondM gap and strict
prefix lower bounds, and is an actual MGen generator after increasing
enumeration of the segment letters. This proves RestModel directly.
There is no claim that the final support intersection itself factors
termwise; it was only forgotten in an inclusion.

Apply OR5 to each summand, and combine the resulting quotients with
their integral coefficients. For every xi_(i,j) there is integral
xi'_(i,j) with xi_(i,j)=Dv_j xi'_(i,j). By IP6 each MGen0 quotient
lies in the integral span of actual joined path products; by IP11 their
Frobenius images are key symbols of the correct normal order.
The additive/multiplicative symbol algebra S is closed under integral
scalar multiplication, since an integral scalar is a normal-degree-zero
tangential coefficient. Therefore

        phi_j(xi'_(i,j)) in S.

This is the exact conclusion of good_of_span. It does not require
dividing by a determinant after applying phi_j.

OR7. PROOF OF RestPh WITH THE SOURCE'S EXPONENTS

Everything in this paragraph is already after the chart substitution.
Let T_s be the r-by-r matrix with entries
X_(bar Arow(s,l),b)^(p^(s-Arow(s,l))) for b<r.
Write D_s=det T_s. Let Delta_j be the determinant with entries
X_(bar f_(j,l),b)^(p^(z_j-f_(j,l))). These all use even selected
positions and first-block coordinates, so belong to the integral
tangential algebra T. Periodicity gives D_(s+n')=D_s and
Delta_(j+J)=Delta_j.

For a late x of segment j, Arow is periodic under x->x+n'. Adding the
exponents shows that phi_j(Dseg_x) is det of the matrix T_(bar x)
with every entry raised to p^(z_j-x). Similarly phi_j(Wseg_j) is the
entrywise p^(z_j-z_(j-1)) power of the Delta_(j-1) matrix.

For any matrix M over any commutative ring and any e>=0,

        det(M^[p^e])=(det M)^(p^e)+p E.

One proof writes the universal identity in integer variables: modulo p,
the iterated Frobenius is a ring endomorphism and commutes with the
determinant, so every coefficient of the difference is divisible by p.
Then specialize. If all entries lie in T, E also lies in T. This is
valid for arbitrary O coefficients and does not assert that arbitrary
coefficients are fixed by Frobenius.

Next, for P>=1 and m>=c, expand (D^P+pE)^m. Terms using at least c
copies of pE are multiples of p^c. A remaining term uses h<c copies,
so its D exponent P(m-h) is at least m-c. Hence

        (D^P+pE)^m = D^(m-c) H+p^c Y,   H,Y in T.

The weaker exponent m-c matches the source exactly. (If m<c, the
version with truncated exponent zero is trivial.) Such factorizations
multiply: the product of two main factors has the product divisor,
and every other term stays divisible by p^c. This is the whole
Spl_pow/Spl_mul/Spl_prod mechanism.

Apply it with m1 at every late letter and m2 at every old frame.
The late intervals (z_(j-1),z_j] for j=0,...,J-1 partition
(z_(-1),z_(-1)+n']; reduction modulo n' maps their selected letters
bijectively onto TT. Therefore each D_s occurs once in their product.
Periodicity reindexes the old-frame factors Delta_(j-1) to Delta_j.
Since

 m1-c=(2Q+3)c+kQ,
 m2-c=c(alpha_*+1),

the resulting divisor factors exactly as

 F_c Dcal product_j Delta_j^(c(alpha_*-alpha_j)),

where
 F_c=R0^c (product_j Delta_j^c)(product_s D_s^((2Q+3)c)),
 R0=product_j Delta_j^alpha_j,
 Dcal=product_s D_s^(kQ).

The residual exponents are nonnegative by the definition of alpha_*.
Absorb them into H. We have proved

        product_j phi_j(Dv_j)=F_c Dcal H+p^cY,             (OR7.1)

with H tangential integral and Y integral (indeed also tangential in
this proof). This is precisely RestPh, including its p^c coefficient.
No unproved identity between ordinary SPtrue and the outgoing family
has been substituted for this argument.

OR8. ASSEMBLY INTO THE EXACT Realisable CONCLUSION

Take the actual fine label X^mu, supported on TT, and the family xi.
The definition is
 sigma=monPh=X^mu sum_i product_(0<=j<J) phi_j(xi_(i,j)).
Using OR6, put
 G=sum_i product_j phi_j(xi'_(i,j)).
Then G is integral and lies in the symbol algebra S. Equation OR7.1
gives the exact identity

        sigma=Z F_c+p^cY',
 Z=X^mu Dcal G H,        Y'=X^mu G Y.                     (OR8.1)

SY4 applies to this actual fine label: its per-position digits are
less than p^dist<=Q, so X^mu Dcal lies in S. Since H is tangential
integral, Z lies in S; Y' is integral.

Suppose sigma has normal degree o, as required by OneSidedLemma4.
F_c is tangential, hence has normal degree zero. Taking the
degree-o component of OR8.1 and multiplying by w^o yields

 w^o sigma = w^o (Z)_o F_c + w^(o+2c)(Y')_o.

For each summand of (Z)_o, its definition as a key symbol says that
w^o times it is a finite integral sum of actual order-o key products
with tangential coefficients. SY5's already proved finite correction,
with determinant budgets (2Q+3)c and c, gives integral F_o whose own
chart substitution differs from the first term by a multiple of
w^(o+c), and whose every other-plane substitution is a multiple of
w^(o+c). The remaining term above is divisible by w^(o+2c), since
w^2=p, and hence does not alter this precision.

If sigma has a prescribed position multidegree d, project F_o to
that component. Every chart substitution preserves position degrees,
and coefficient divisibility is preserved by the projection, so all
congruences and off-plane bounds remain true. If sigma=0 take F_o=0.
These checks prove all clauses of Realisable: integrality, any
multidegree forced by sigma, the own-chart order o+c, and order o+c
at every chart with different rational residue plane.

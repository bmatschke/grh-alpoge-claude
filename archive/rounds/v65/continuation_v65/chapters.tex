\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Propagation on rank-four components}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.65 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

Suppose a rank-four residual point lies on several components. If the
potential modularity ideal vanishes on one of them, under what
hypotheses does it vanish on every component through the point?
The first two chapters answer this question at the tower degree
specified in the formal proof. The exceptional reducible
representation preserves two symplectic planes. Its local flag forces
two characters to coincide, and two additional matrix entries control
the dimension of its trace fibre.

The seed chapters give the integral tensor pair, its weights, the quadratic
field and its irreducibility test. Together they reduce the separate
seed problem to three arithmetic inputs under their original hypotheses.
The final chapter treats the local ordinary condition
at the marked rank-three host in the argument common to RH and Dirichlet GRH.

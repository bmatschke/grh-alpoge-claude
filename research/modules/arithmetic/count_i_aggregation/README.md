# Case I on the fixed character field

KA6 identifies the fixed-field estimate consumed by the prime aggregation, and KA7 determines its principal target. For the classical target the field is $\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-7})$. At the original rank-three split representation the central strict extension term has a uniform bound at all odd coefficient primes. Both signs can occur in the adjacent coordinates of a polarized extension. At level two one can compute exactly which even cyclotomic classes survive the additional local splitting condition and pass the source mark. For mixed classes the size of the global obstruction group is bounded independently of the additional places. The remaining step in the ordinary route is arithmetic control of the required automorphic components.

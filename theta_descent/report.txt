Scope
=====
This is a human proof of the finite linear algebra, level descent, common
theta target, rational comparison matrices, and exact target metric used
by the principal-lattice branch. It also proves the algebraic normalization
and conjugation transport of the source's common row tests. These are
conditional theorems with explicitly retained analytic and arithmetic
inputs. The actual convergent separating tests, unfolded period identity,
rational coefficient/ratio suppliers, positive theta operator identities,
and their quantitative estimates are not manufactured here.

The full proof is in main.tex. Exact declaration ranges, full-blob and
range SHA256 values are in provenance.json and source_locators.txt. The
source pin, field embedding, tuple, level character, and target are never
silently changed. This work does not compile Lean or replay a Lean kernel.
It does not complete RH or Dirichlet GRH.

A. Fixed-field descent
=====================
TD1. Finite separating complex-linear tests on a target recover rational
coordinates in an injectively embedded basis. The test matrix has entries
in the prescribed field K. It is injective over K because the tests and
the embedded basis are injective. A K-left inverse gives the coordinates
of any vector whose test values are in sigma(K). This handles rank zero
and requires no analytic continuation or continuity of a field embedding.

TD2. If all tested theta columns have values in sigma(K), the theta kernel
is the complex span of its K-form. Gaussian elimination on the finite test
matrix proves this, along with rank invariance under scalar extension.
This is a derived simplification of the source's rational-kernel interface,
not an assertion that the tests themselves have already been constructed.

TD3. The supplied K-valued sesquilinear form and exact complex Gram
identity imply sigma(a*) = conjugate(sigma(a)), whenever the coefficient
space is nonzero. Each test's Riesz vector maps under the adjoint to a
vector with rational Gram coordinates. Inverting the Gram matrix puts
that vector in the actual K-form. The Riesz vectors span the target, so
the whole adjoint image is the complex span of its actual rational
preimage. The zero-dimensional case is separate and immediate. This
proves RatZ from the explicit row/lift tests and metric identities.

TD4. The adjoint image is the orthogonal complement of the kernel, and
theta is injective on that image. If the adjoint of an operator preserves
the kernel, the operator preserves the adjoint image. Extending the
actual K-operators through the specified complex basis therefore proves
stability of the actual rational cut. Kernel invariance alone, without
adjoint closure, is insufficient.

B. Exact finite level operations
===============================
TD5. Extend the finite group representation from the K-form through its
complex basis. Group laws, unitarity and theta equivariance follow from
their values on the basis. Apply adjoints to the group-element identities
and use the inverse element. Then extend the resulting identities by
finite sums in K[G]. In particular the group-algebra adjoint-intertwining
identity is a sum of already proved group identities; it does not omit
complex conjugation when taking an adjoint.

TD6. An actual rational idempotent e gives a complex idempotent P.
The theta kernel and adjoint image are invariant. Applying P to a rational
spanning expression proves that the intersection of the adjoint image
with im(P) is the complex span of the rational intersection. To replace a
level vector by an adjoint-image vector without changing theta, decompose
it orthogonally as p+k and use Pp. This does not require P to be
self-adjoint. It proves the literal RatLev and downward-level statements.

TD7. The character projector is |H|^{-1} sum chi(h)^{-1} h. Its idempotence
and chi-eigencharacter follow by reindexing. Multiplication by the source's
central block idempotent cuts out the simultaneous block and character.
The central character-idempotent fact uses standard finite-group
character orthogonality, recorded as background. Upward-level descent
then follows from the adjoint intertwining identity. Rows use psi; the
actual lift-side lvlA uses psi inverse. Principal H={1} gives the identity
character projector exactly.

C. Common image and exact rational comparison
============================================
TD8. The input coordinate map U_t has its actual Gram matrix n_P H_Q,t
and has image im(theta_t*) intersect the actual level space. Downward
and upward level conditions put theta_t in a fixed target E_0. On each
spanning spectral part, theta_t theta_t* is the positive scalar
c_t,p = kappa(v0) product_j lambda(j,t,p,v0). For e in that part, divide
theta_t* e by c_t,p and use the coordinate image identity. This explicitly
proves surjectivity onto E_0, while theta's injectivity on its adjoint image
proves injectivity. Equal dimensions were not used to guess a common
image. Applying the same injectivity to theta_t*theta_t x and c_t,p x
gives the exact H4 Gram identity, with its factor n_P.

The required operator identity is ThmBop, and the spectral-part spanning
and orthogonality input is PartsE. This proof does not prove their
theta-theoretic suppliers or positivity estimates.

TD9. Use one common RowTest for all tuples. TD1 makes each column of the
unique comparison I_base^{-1} I_t rational. These are exactly the source's
rowMat entries; the default value in kOf is not used once rationality is
known. The matrix is bijective, equals identity at the base, and its
comparisons satisfy the exact cocycle identity. Different test families
for different tuples would not suffice to identify one prescribed K-form.

TD10. The same proof applies to every single lift. Its actual principal

D. Every-place metric and the actual principal lattice
=====================================================
TD12. At each infinite place the actual class metric is positive. The
commuting Hecke/inertia family is closed under that metric's adjoints.
The source transports these equations through the specified maximal CM
subfield, where conjugation is compatible. Every operator is normal.
The elementary normal-operator argument proves diagonalizability and
orthogonality of different eigenvalues; an infinite family reduces to a
finite spanning subfamily of its finite-dimensional operator algebra.

The actual quotient metric requires care. If P is the surjective
coordinate quotient, PA=Abar P for every operator and its adjoint makes
ker(P) and its orthogonal complement invariant. The unique orthogonal
lift of y is H^{-1} P* (P H^{-1} P*)^{-1} y, and the quotient metric is
(P H^{-1} P*)^{-1}. Orthogonal projection onto ker(P) perpendicular
preserves the joint spectral parts. Consequently their quotient inner
products vanish as well. The source's exact residue-label separation
gives PartsOrth. Arbitrary coordinate inclusion zE is not silently
treated as an isometry.

TD13. On the rational direct sum Q = sum Q_p, use its literal projections
P_p and define H_E,v = sum_p c_p,v v(P_p)* H_Q,v v(P_p) for v != v0.
Positive weights and sum P_p=1 prove positive definiteness. A positive
square root supplies frames with exactly this metric. At v0 use
n_P^{-1} Gram(I_base). This is the same metric as the source's frame
construction, without claiming equality of unrelated square-root choices.
Orthogonality yields the exact part-scale relation and lower bound by
kappa(v) min_p product lambda(j,base,p,v). At the base place the H3
spectral-coordinate identification is additionally required. If the
part index is empty then Q=0; no minimum of an empty set is taken.

TD14. Apply the actual bijective rowMat to the actual full projected
lattice at each selected tuple. At almost every coefficient prime the
tuple is the base and the comparison is identity. PL9 gives the exact
global lattice with these localizations. Its membership is equivalent
to the actual theta-image lattice membership, by injectivity of I_base.
Together with the exact frames of TD13 this is the literal principal
Hermitian lattice. Local divisibility of periods and quantitative metric
bounds remain additional assertions.

E. The normalization producing common row tests
==============================================
TD15. The analytic tests p_a become complex-linear after LinK. They
separate the target by InjK. The supplied period formula reads
sigma(z_t) c_t p_a(theta_t j_t x) = kappa w_t,x,a,
first on the selected good block. Actual central-block projection
preserves the theta image and makes the formula applicable to every
source vector. It may change the finite sum w; only its rationality is
needed. ConstK gives c_t != 0; SumRatK gives w in sigma(K); RatioK gives
c_t/c_base in sigma(K); z_t is a root of unity.

If kappa != 0, one common scalar k=c_base/kappa makes every test value
rational, and k != 0 preserves separation. If kappa=0, the formula
forces all theta test values to vanish, so use k=1. This proves the
literal rowTest_of_choice without an unstated global period nonvanishing
assumption. A statement that finite sums are closed in K does not
supply the rationality of the actual period coefficients.

TD16. The Boolean conjugate Hilbert model transports a test by conjugating
both its scalar action and its value. The two conjugations cancel,
giving a complex-linear test with values in the correct conjugate
embedding of K. Applying it twice is the inverse, and separation is
preserved. This supplies rowTest_toCx3 and the conditional realization
wrapper. For all Big inputs first take a maximum of the C_F thresholds,
then fix epsilon,C_F, and only then take a maximum of the x_0 thresholds.
No forbidden exchange of quantifiers occurs.

The example E=C, K=Q, tested inputs {1,i} proves why the fixed-field
condition matters. Any complex-linear test rational on both inputs
vanishes on 1, so no such family can separate. Both inputs are algebraic.
The earlier algebraic theta-vacuum/jet results therefore do not alone
supply this RowTest.

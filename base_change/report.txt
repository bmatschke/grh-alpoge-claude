RESULT AND EXACT SCOPE

Two different assertions are closed here.

(1) A literal LPprime certificate extends from a coefficient DVR O to a finite
compatible coefficient DVR S with exactly the same parameters (t,D,kappa,c*).
The base unramified ring W, its fraction field Q, its Frobenius sigma and its
lattice edges remain fixed. The mapped element omega'=phi(omega) still satisfies
omega'^2=p; it need not be a uniformizer of S. No ramification multiplier is
inserted into t, D or c*: these parameters occur in actual powers of omega and p.

(2) The literal recC construction does contain the required standard coefficient
subring at every specified place. We construct that ring, its finite extension
and an embedding inducing the desired number-field place. Hence StdSubAt holds
for a recC datum, and for an eventually recC family. More generally it follows
under the literal RingCompatibleAt identifications, including those supplied
by GroupCompatibilityFamily. IsRecCEv is one sufficient interface and is not
required by every family construction. The name StdSubAt means a
standard coefficient SUBRING, not a theorem asserting arbitrary invariant
submodules are standard. The lattice statements needed with it are proved below.

We do not prove existence of all the global levels, local identifications,
characters, comparison maps or families that enter recC/IsRecCEv. Those are
separate global family-existence inputs. The LPprime certificate to be extended
is supplied by the completed local constructions and the parent assembly.
HM12 concerns isomorphisms of quadratic coefficient settings; the finite
extension here is a different result and is not deduced from HM12.

Classical background is finite-dimensional linear algebra, finite fields and
Witt rings with their Teichmuller expansions, the elementary facts that integers
of a number field form a Dedekind domain and that a characteristic-zero finite
field extension is separable. The particular complete-DVR normalization,
faithful-flatness, place-selection and lattice arguments are proved below.
CB1–CB2 and CB7 supply the already reconstructed universal monic tableau basis
and its change-of-basis convention; no integral semisimplicity is assumed.

1. BC1 — THE CONCRETE STANDARD COEFFICIENT RING

Let p>=5 be prime, f>=2 even, q=p^f, k=F_q and k2=F_(q^2). Set

  W=W(k), Q=Frac(W), W2=W(k2),
  O_s=W2[omega]/(omega^2-p), K_s=Frac(O_s).                 (BC1.1)

The residue-field inclusion gives j_W:W->W2 and then j_s:W->O_s.
The induced fraction-field inclusion is iota_s:Q->K_s. On Q use the lift
sigma of a->a^p. The source names are F1,F2,WQ,W2,QQ,OO,KO,jW,jO,iota,wU,sig.

For clarity, O_s/W is NOT an unramified quadratic extension. W2/W is
unramified of degree two; adjoining omega is a totally ramified quadratic
extension. The combined extension has rank four over W, residue field k2,
and omega is a uniformizer. This is precisely the source record named
UnramifiedQuadraticSetting.

Here are the actual checks. A Witt vector has a unique convergent expansion
sum_(i>=0) p^i[a_i], with [a] the Teichmuller representative; the p-adic topology
is the topology of successively fixed Witt digits. Thus W and W2 are complete
DVRs, with residue k and k2, and p is their uniformizer. Frobenius applies
 a_i->a_i^p, so it is congruent to x->x^p modulo p and sigma^f=1.

Choose a k-basis b_1,b_2 of k2 and lifts e_1,e_2 in W2. Given x in W2, express
its residue in that basis, subtract the lifted combination and divide by p.
Repeat. The coefficient sums converge in W and give x=c_1e_1+c_2e_2.
If a relation existed, divide all coefficients by their smallest p-power and
reduce modulo p: a nonzero relation between b_1,b_2 would result. Hence these
lifts form a W-basis. This proves the finiteness in jW_finite and more.

X^2-p is irreducible over Frac(W2): the p-valuation of a square is even and
v_p(p)=1. The quotient O_s is consequently a domain and is free over W2 on
1,omega. For a+b omega !=0, the two candidate omega-orders 2v_p(a) and
2v_p(b)+1 have opposite parity. Factor their smaller power of omega. The
remaining factor has nonzero constant residue and is a unit: its inverse is
obtained from its norm a^2-pb^2, or from a convergent geometric series. Every
nonzero element is therefore a unit times a power of omega. This proves the
DVR assertion, O_s/(omega)=k2, and omega^2=p. Its p-adic completeness follows
from W2^2 and is the same as omega-adic completeness because omega^2=p.

Finally every integer z has a square root in O_s. For z!=0 write z=p^m u,
p not dividing u. Every nonzero element a of k is a square in k2, because
(q^2-1)/2=((q+1)/2)(q-1). Lift a square root of the residue of u to y in W2
by Newton/Hensel iteration for Y^2-u; its derivative 2y is a unit. Then
(omega^m y)^2=z. The case z=0 is immediate. All the literal fields of
settingStd have now been checked, including residue cardinal q^2 and the
finite map j_s. The hypotheses f>=2 and Even f are retained, even though
several of these ring facts need less.

2. BC2 — FINITE NORMALIZATION AND A PRESCRIBED NUMBER-FIELD PLACE

2.1 Finite normalization over a complete DVR.

Let R be a complete DVR of characteristic-zero fraction field K. Let E/K
be finite and let S be the integral closure of R in E. We prove that S is
a finite free complete DVR, with fraction field E.

Choose a primitive element theta and multiply it by a sufficiently large
power of the uniformizer pi so that theta is integral. A=R[theta] is finite
free and spans E. The trace form is nondegenerate by separability. If x is
integral over R, then Tr(xa) is integral over R for every a in A: it is a
sum of conjugates of the integral element xa. It belongs to K, hence to R,
since a DVR is integrally closed. Therefore

  A subset S subset A^#={x in E: Tr(xA) subset R}.          (BC2.1)

The trace-dual A^# is a finite free fractional lattice. Since R is
Noetherian, S is finite. It is torsion free and hence free over the PID R.
Every element of E has an integral nonzero R-multiple, by clearing the
coefficients of its monic polynomial over K; thus Frac(S)=E and S has
R-rank [E:K]. In particular S is pi-adically complete and separated.
It is integrally closed by transitivity of integrality.

Here is why there is just one maximal ideal. S/piS is a finite-dimensional
algebra over the residue field, hence Artinian. If it had two or more
maximal ideals, the Chinese remainder theorem for powers of those ideals
would supply a nontrivial idempotent. Such an idempotent lifts to S. Every element of 1+piS is a unit by the
convergent geometric series. For an approximate idempotent e, 2e-1 is
therefore a unit since (2e-1)^2 is 1 modulo pi;
replace e by e-(e^2-e)/(2e-1). The error is squared and the iterations
converge. A domain has only the idempotents 0 and 1, a contradiction.
All maximal ideals of S lie above (pi), by integrality; hence S is local.

For completeness, the standard one-dimensional normal-local-ring criterion
can also be seen directly here. Every nonzero prime of S meets R nontrivially:
for a nonzero x in that prime, the nonzero constant term of its minimal monic
polynomial lies in R and is x times an element of S. Thus every nonzero prime
is maximal. Let m be the unique maximal ideal. For 0!=a in m, radical(a)=m;
finite generation of m therefore gives m^N subset (a) for some N. Choose the least
n>=1 with m^n subset (a), and choose b in m^(n-1) outside (a). Then t=b/a
is not in S but t m subset S. If t m subset m, the determinant trick applied
to the finite faithful S-module m makes t integral over S, a contradiction.
Consequently tx is a unit for some x in m, and y=x(ty)/(tx) for every y in m.
So m=(x). Since S/piS is Artinian, m^N subset piS for some N; separation
ensures no nonzero element lies in every m^n. Repeated division by x now
shows that S is a DVR. Write pi S=m^e with e>=1. The pi- and m-adic
topologies are equivalent, proving completeness in the required topology.

The determinant trick just used is elementary: write multiplication by t
on finite generators of m as a matrix over S, apply its characteristic
polynomial, and use that m contains a nonzero element in the field E.
No unproved uniqueness-of-valuation extension is needed in this argument.

2.2 Put the number field at the specified finite place.

Use R=O_s, K=K_s from BC1, and let Omega be an algebraic closure of K_s.
Let L be the source number field and let v be a prime of its integer ring
above p. For each embedding tau:L->Omega, form

  E_tau=K_s tau(L),  S_tau=integral closure of O_s in E_tau. (BC2.2)

The extension E_tau/K_s is finite: if L=Q(theta), then
E_tau=K_s(tau(theta)) and its degree is at most [L:Q]. By 2.1, S_tau is
a finite free complete DVR. Its maximal ideal pulls back on O_L to a
prime P_tau over p. All global integers map into S_tau by integrality.

We prove P_tau=v for at least one tau. There are finitely many embeddings
and at least one, since Omega is algebraically closed of characteristic zero.
Suppose none induces v. The distinct primes P_tau and v are pairwise
comaximal. By CRT choose a in O_L which is 0 modulo v and 1 modulo every
P_tau. Then each tau(a) is a unit in S_tau, so every tau(a)^(-1) is integral
over O_s. Their product is N_(L/Q)(a)^(-1), by the norm-as-product identity.
The nonzero integer N_(L/Q)(a) is divisible by p: the norm is an O_L-multiple
of a (use the constant term of the multiplication characteristic polynomial),
so it lies in v, whose contraction to Z is (p). Write the norm as p m.
Then p^(-1)=m N_(L/Q)(a)^(-1) is integral over O_s. But it belongs to K_s
and not to the integrally closed DVR O_s. This contradiction proves existence.
This is a CRT simplification of the prime-avoidance argument in ExtWEx.exists_emb.

For that tau, the exact integrality statement is

  tau(x) in S_tau  iff  x in O_(L,v)  iff  v(x)>=0.         (BC2.3)

The forward inclusion O_(L,v)->S_tau follows by writing a/b with b outside v,
since tau(b) is a unit. Conversely, if x is outside the local DVR O_(L,v),
then x^(-1) belongs to its maximal ideal and maps to the maximal ideal of S_tau;
tau(x) therefore cannot be integral. This proves the literal embRec_int
statement after applying the source's additive/multiplicative valuation dictionary.

The source tauRec, KfRec, ORec, embRec and extRec are precisely these objects
and maps, with E_tau defined as an intermediate-field compositum. The chosen
tau is noncanonical; no later proof requires embeddings at different places
to agree beyond separately stated global comparison data.

3. BC3 — FAITHFUL FLATNESS, DESCENT, INTERSECTIONS, DUALS AND VALUATIONS

Now let R->S be any finite compatible inclusion of DVRs with fraction fields
K->K'. In the application R=O_s and S=S_tau. An arbitrary finite map of the
source's DVRs is injective because it is compatible with an injective field
map. S is finite torsion free over the PID R, hence finite free of positive
rank. It is faithfully flat. It has fraction algebra S[1/pi]=K': the left
side is a finite-dimensional domain over K and thus a field containing S.

The element 1 is primitive in the free R-module S. Indeed pi cannot be a
unit of S: otherwise pi^(-1), integral over R and lying in K, would belong
to R. Hence 1 is not in piS. Extend 1 to an R-basis

  b_1=1,b_2,...,b_d of S.                                 (BC3.1)

For any K-vector space V, use the associated K-basis of K' to identify
V'=K' tensor_K V with the direct sum of d copies of V as an R-module.
For every R-submodule M of V its scalar extension is exactly

  S M = direct_sum_(a=1)^d b_a M.                         (BC3.2)

Consequently scalar extension commutes with sums, kernels of R-defined
linear maps, and ANY intersection of such submodules, even an infinite one:
compare the d coordinates. In particular

  S(intersection_i M_i)=intersection_i(S M_i),
  (S M) intersection V=M.                                (BC3.3)

The second equality uses b_1=1. Thus inclusions and equalities descend.
This arbitrary-intersection assertion uses finite freeness; flatness alone
would only justify finite intersections in this generality.

For a lattice L and submodule M subset L, saturation also ascends and descends.
If L/M is torsion free, it is finite free over R and the sequence splits;
its scalar extension is a free S-quotient. Conversely if SM is saturated
in SL and pi x lies in M, then x maps into SM and (BC3.3) brings x back to M.
Ranks are unchanged, since the corresponding fraction-field spaces are
ordinary scalar extensions.

For a full lattice M in finite-dimensional V, take an R-basis m_i. The
algebraic dual lattice M^vee is the R-span of the K-linear dual basis m_i^*.
Define the semilinear dual map delta by

  delta(G)(a tensor x)=a phi_K(G(x)).                      (BC3.4)

The images m_i^* form the S-dual basis to the images m_i. Therefore

  S delta(M^vee)=(SM)^vee,   (M^vee)^vee=M.                (BC3.5)

This dual uses the K'-valued evaluation pairing. It is NOT the dual of
restriction of scalars under Tr_(K'/K). No different or trace-discriminant
factor enters (BC3.5). For a non-full module the same forward integrality
inclusion is immediate from testing finite S-linear combinations; that is
all the original LPBase.dual_mem proof needs.

Invariants of any fixed family of R-linear operators commute with this
extension by the kernel statement. Polynomial invariants in the actual
Weyl module mean universal identities with an indeterminate, not invariance
at the finitely many residue-field points. Their coefficient equations
are R-defined. BC4 additionally identifies their actual integral basis.

For normalized integer valuations choose uniformizers pi_R,pi_S and write
phi(pi_R)=u pi_S^e, with u a unit and e>=1. Units remain units, so

  ord_S(phi_K(x))=e ord_R(x),
  ord_S(phi_K(x))/ord_S(p)=ord_R(x)/ord_R(p).              (BC3.6)

The second expression is the p-normalized valuation. If R=O_s,
ord_R(omega)=1 and ord_R(p)=2; hence ord_S(phi(omega))=e and ord_S(p)=2e.
The mapped omega has p-normalized valuation 1/2, even if e>1.

A finite-length R-module has a Smith decomposition into R/(pi_R^a).
After extension, S/(pi_R^a) has S-length e a. Thus length_S(S tensor T)
=e length_R(T), while length/ord(p), ranks, and p-normalized determinant
valuations are unchanged. For a selected number-field place v, if e_v=v(p)
and E_v=ord_S(tau(pi_v)), the same argument gives

  ord_S(tau(x))/ord_S(p)=v(x)/e_v,  ord_S(p)=E_v e_v.       (BC3.7)

These equations are the precise ramification dictionary. One must not
identify the two integer valuations or declare phi(omega) a uniformizer.

4. BC4 — THE ACTUAL INTEGRAL WEYL LATTICE AND BASIS-INDEPENDENT TWIST

Let X be a k by k variable matrix. For a partition mu, let nabla_mu(R) be
the polynomials of column degrees mu which satisfy
f(...,C_j+T C_i,...)=f(...,C_j,...) for every i<j as identities in R[X,T].
This is exactly StatementA.dualWeyl. CB1–CB2 prove

  nabla_mu(R)=direct_sum_T R b_T,                         (BC4.1)

where b_T is the product of initial-column minors associated to a
semistandard tableau. The b_T are integer polynomials with distinct monic
leading monomials. The same family is a basis over every domain. Consequently
coefficient mapping gives the exact identification

  S tensor_R nabla_mu(R) = nabla_mu(S).                   (BC4.2)

This is the substantive input in NablaLTwEqMap.bc_dualWeyl; it is already
proved in CB, rather than reintroduced as an unexpanded basis hypothesis.
Row substitution T(g)f(X)=f(gX) commutes with the generic column operations
and preserves column degrees. Thus GL_k(R) preserves (BC4.1). Notice the
composition convention T(g)T(h)=T(hg).

For a decreasing integer weight lambda, put mu_i=lambda_i-lambda_k>=0.
If Lambda=gR^k, g invertible over K, define

  H_lambda(Lambda)=det(g)^(-lambda_k) T(g^(-1)) nabla_mu(R).
                                                               (BC4.3)

This is exactly nablaLTw, not a newly chosen Schur lattice. If another basis
is gh with h in GL_k(R), then
T((gh)^(-1))=T(g^(-1))T(h^(-1)); the latter inner operator preserves
nabla_mu(R), and det(h)^(-lambda_k) is an R-unit. Hence (BC4.3) is independent
of g. The source defines H0 as an intersection over all bases; every term
of that intersection equals (BC4.3), so it is exactly H0.

Coefficient maps commute with determinants, inverses and the polynomial
substitution. Therefore

  S H_lambda(Lambda)=H_lambda(S Lambda).                 (BC4.4)

It has the expected basis obtained by applying the invertible operator
in (BC4.3) to the b_T. Its K-span is the full dual-Weyl space, since that
space is GL_k(K)-stable. Tensor products in distinct matrix blocks likewise
have the products of the individual bases as bases: spanning is expansion,
and distinct blockwise leading monomials prove independence before twisting.
There is no loss of rank or integral scalar in these identifications.

5. BC5 — THE LITERAL TENSOR, EDGE, CLEAN AND DUAL LATTICES

Keep the source unramified DVR W with residue cardinal q=p^f, its fraction
field Q, Frobenius sigma, and a coefficient setting (O,K,iota,j,omega).
Write s=0,...,f-1. For the dominant source weight lambda^0_s, its actual
signed weight is

  lambda_s=lambda^0_s               if s is even,
  lambda_s,i= -lambda^0_s,k+1-i      if s is odd.           (BC5.1)

Both are decreasing. Put A_r=sum_s sum_(i<=r) lambda_s,i. A_r is an integer;
no nonnegativity assumption is silently added. Define

  Lambda_s(L)=O-span of iota(sigma^s L),
  Lambda_s(e)=Lambda_s(e.src)+omega^(-1)Lambda_s(p e.tgt),
  H(L)=tensor_s H_(lambda_s)(Lambda_s(L)),
  H(e)=tensor_s H_(lambda_s)(Lambda_s(e)),
  R_e=omega^(-A_r) H(e),  R_e'=omega^(-A_r) H(e)^vee,
  b(F,G)=p^(A_r) G(F).                                  (BC5.2)

The tensors are embedded as products in distinct variable blocks; H(L)
and H(e) are then regarded inside the source's Vsp, exactly the toV comap.
The edge condition is e.src subset e.tgt, p e.tgt subset e.src and
#(e.tgt/e.src)=p^(fr), with both W-modules full lattices. Equivalently an
adapted Q-basis has e.src=gW^k and
 e.tgt=g diag(p^(-1)I_r,I_(k-r))W^k.
This follows by choosing a basis of the r-dimensional subspace
p e.tgt/p e.src inside e.src/p e.src and lifting it, or by Smith normal form. In that basis

  Lambda_s(e)=iota(sigma^s g) diag(omega^(-1)I_r,I)O^k.    (BC5.3)

Indeed the first r added generators are omega^(-1) times the old ones;
in the other coordinates the added generator is omega^(-1)p=omega.
Thus every input to H_lambda is an actual full lattice. Lambda_s(L) is
similarly full because sigma is a field automorphism and iota is injective.

Let phi:O->S and phi_K:K->K' commute with the field inclusions. Let nu map
polynomial coefficients by phi_K, restricted to Vsp, and let delta be (BC3.4).
From (BC4.2) the same tensor tableau family is a field basis on both sides,
so Vsp'=K' tensor_K Vsp and dim Vsp'=dim Vsp. The span definitions give
Lambda_s(L)'=S Lambda_s(L) and Lambda_s(e)'=S Lambda_s(e). BC4 gives
H(L)'=S nu(H(L)) and H(e)'=S nu(H(e)). This also justifies the toV passage:
the polynomial lattice already lies in its displayed representation space.

Set

  N_src(L)=omega^(-t) H(L) intersection
                 intersection_(e.src=L) R_e,
  N_tgt(L)=omega^(-t) H(L)^vee intersection
                 intersection_(e.tgt=L) R_e'.            (BC5.4)

Under the finite extension, (BC3.3) and (BC3.5) give exact equalities for
all four scaled edge lattices and both clean lattices after applying nu
or delta. In particular every old source vector and dual test remains
allowed. The optional c*-divisibility condition in a different outgoing
edge also survives, because phi_K(omega^a)=phi(omega)^a for every integer a.

The source proof of LPBase.lpAt_dom needs only these forward inclusions,
proved term by term, so it does not rely on exchanging an arbitrary
intersection and a merely flat tensor product. Our equality refinement
uses the finite freeness established in BC3. A finite compatible map is
therefore fully sufficient for either route.

Finally the exact pairing commutes:

  b'(nu F,delta G)=phi_K(b(F,G)).                          (BC5.5)

This follows from delta(G)(nu F)=phi_K(G(F)) and phi_K(p)=p, including
negative A_r. Both sides use the same positive power p^D in the rank
certificate below. Neither a trace-dual nor a residue-field cardinal
from the larger coefficient field is substituted into this formula.

6. BC6 — GOODRANKK TRANSFER, WITH AN OPTIONAL DESCENT CRITERION

For a finite family of K-linear functionals ell_i on a lattice A, literal
GoodRankK(p,A,ell,n,D) asks for an O-linear map u:O^n->A and an O-matrix M
with n rows such that

  sum_i M_(j,i) ell_i(u(x))=p^D x_j  for every x in O^n.   (BC6.1)

In the application A=N_tgt(L) and ell_i(G)=b(F_i,G). All products are in K;
the right side is viewed via the coefficient-ring embedding.

Define M'=phi(M) and, writing epsilon_l for the standard coordinate vectors,

  u'(y)=sum_l y_l delta(u(epsilon_l)),  y in S^n.          (BC6.2)

BC5 proves that every summand is an element of the actual target clean lattice.
It follows that u' is S-linear and has the required codomain. For its jth
paired coordinate, bilinearity, (BC5.5) and (BC6.1) give

 sum_i phi(M_(j,i)) b'(nu(F_i),u'(y))
   =sum_l y_l phi_K(sum_i M_(j,i)b(F_i,u(epsilon_l)))
   =sum_l y_l p^D delta_(j,l)=p^D y_j.                   (BC6.3)

Thus the same n and D work. This is exactly the substantive matrix calculation
at the end of lpAt_dom. No primitive-vector assertion is inferred merely
from an enlarged fraction-field rank.

A useful derived strengthening explains descent as well. Suppose the clean
lattice is finite free and the tests are fixed scalar extensions. Choose a
basis and let B be their rectangular evaluation matrix, with entries in K.
Its fractional Smith form has nonzero diagonal pi^s_1,...,pi^s_h, where
s_1<=...<=s_h are integers, possibly negative. Put a=D ord_O(p). Then

  GoodRankK(p,A,ell,n,D) iff n<=h and s_n<=a.             (BC6.4)

For sufficiency take the first n Smith coordinates and multiply the ith
column by the integral scalar p^D/pi^s_i; undo the two integral invertible
Smith changes. For necessity, if fewer than n diagonal entries have s_i<=a,
choose a primitive row in the left kernel of the n-by-(number of such entries)
block of the witness M after its Smith change. Such a row exists: take a
nonzero field-kernel row and divide all coordinates by their minimum
valuation. All remaining terms have valuation at least a+1, whereas its
product with p^D I has an entry of valuation exactly a. Contradiction.
The case n=0 is automatic.

Finite extension multiplies every s_i and a by the same ramification index.
Hence (BC6.4) is equivalent over O and S. The actual N_tgt(L) is finite free
because it is a submodule of the finite free lattice omega^(-t)H(L)^vee;
BC5 gives its exact scalar extension. So this optional descent criterion
applies to any FIXED family of old source vectors. It does not assert that
an arbitrary new collection of source vectors chosen only over K' descends.
The forward theorem (BC6.3), sufficient for LPBaseChange, needs no such
extra assertion and does not use Smith normal form.

7. BC7 — THE COMPLETE ACTUAL LPBASECHANGE STATEMENT

Assume the two compatible source HidaSetting records, the finite coefficient
map O->S and its compatible field map. For every m,r,lambda^0,t,D,kappa,c*,

  LPprime_O(t,D,kappa,c*) => LPprime_S(t,D,kappa,c*).       (BC7.1)

The source record is: 4(D+1)<=c*, and for every full W-lattice L there is a
finite list of edges e_i with target L, source vectors F_i in N_src(e_i.src),
extra omega^c* divisibility for every other edge out of that source, and
GoodRankK on N_tgt(L) of rank n>=(1-kappa)dim Vsp.

If lambda^0 is dominant, use exactly the same W-edges and map each F_i by nu.
BC5 proves every source and alternative-edge membership. BC6 transports the
rank witness and its matrix. The dimensions agree by the common tableau
basis, so its numerical rank inequality is unchanged. The leading condition
4(D+1)<=c* is identical. This proves (BC7.1) for each L and then for all L.
If a weight is not dominant, the literal definition of Vsp contains a zero
factor (the odd signed reversal preserves the equivalence of dominance).
Thus dim Vsp=0; the empty edge list and n=0 give the required trivial witness
on either side. This is the exact lpAt_nondom branch, not a tacit positivity
assumption on weights or kappa.

All discrete edge data stay over W and Q. In particular q=#(W/pW) is unchanged
when the coefficient residue field grows. The parameters t,D,c* continue to
measure powers of the mapped omega and p; BC3 supplies the integer-valuation
scaling if a later argument rewrites them using a new uniformizer.

The core forward proof actually needs only compatible injective coefficient
and field maps, the appropriate lattice/model definitions and the two setting
records. Finiteness is present in the formal LPBaseChange interface but is
not used by LPBase.lpAt_dom. We keep it in (BC7.1), as needed for the actual
application, and use it honestly for the stronger equalities and descent.

8. BC8 — THE ACTUAL STDSUBAT SUPPLIER AND ITS REMAINING FAMILY BOUNDARY

For the source D7 datum, a word u, a served place v and a local orbit index a,
StdSubAt asks for O_s,K_s,iota_s,j_s,omega_s and a finite map into the actual
coefficient DVR, compatible with its fraction-field map, such that O_s is
an UnramifiedQuadraticSetting on the SAME W,Q,sigma and

  phi_K iota_s=iota,  phi j_s=j,  phi(omega_s)=omega.       (BC8.1)

BC1 supplies the standard setting. BC2 supplies the actual finite extension

RETAINED INPUTS AND NONCLAIMS

The eight BC claims prove the standard-ring construction, finite normalization,
place realization, exact lattice transport and actual LPprime base change.
They leave no new local representation or ramification hypothesis unstated.
They retain the global assertion that the family under consideration has
the displayed compatible ring identifications. A recC/IsRecCEv identification
is sufficient, as is the ring field of GroupCompatibilityFamily; these are
alternative interfaces, not simultaneous requirements. Constructing the full
globally compatible local levels,
characters, Hermitian identifications, metric comparisons and families is
not supplied by this argument. Nor does it prove abundance of good orbits,
theta/spectral identities, cohomological windows or prime selection.

The stronger clean-intersection equalities and fixed-family GoodRankK descent
are explicit derived results. They are not attributed to the source's weaker
forward-inclusion proof. All exact source paths, declaration ranges, file/range
SHA256 hashes, and pinned URLs are generated in provenance.json.

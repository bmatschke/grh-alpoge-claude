TL1 — FINITE SELF-DUAL HERMITIAN CLASSES

The trace scalar product is integral on a self-dual lattice, symmetric, and
positive by the sum over all embeddings. The codifferent argument proves
N subset N* and |disc(E)| N* subset N. If D=n[E:Q], the integral trace
Gram determinant is therefore in [1, |disc(E)|^D]. This gives both a
uniform covolume bound and a lower bound one on nonzero lengths.

The proof of bounded independent vectors is fully supplied, rather than
appealing to an unexpanded Hermitian class-finiteness theorem. If all
vectors of length at most 2R lay in an r-plane, r<D, the lattice points in
any translate of a radius-R ball would lie in an affine r-plane and be
one-separated. Packing radius-1/2 balls bounds their count by (2R+1)^r.
The exact average over a fundamental cell is volume(ball)/covolume. Choose
R so that the latter exceeds 3^(D−1)R^(D−1), obtaining a contradiction.

The resulting short vectors span an integer sublattice N0 of uniformly
bounded index b. Its bounded integral Gram matrix has finitely many
possibilities, as does the intermediate group N0 subset N subset b^−1 N0.
Retaining the multiplication matrices of a fixed integral basis of O_E is
essential: isometry of the trace lattice alone would not imply E-linearity.
Those matrices have bounded entries, using the bounded Gram inverse and
the embedding-wise operator-norm bound, and denominators dividing b. There
are finitely many possibilities. Matching all this data gives an E-linear
isometry preserving the Hermitian form by trace nondegeneracy.

This proves the actual finite rational-unitary class conclusion of
DecoFin.unimod_finite. It does not assert rational equivalence of every
pair of self-dual lattices. Each automorphism group is finite because the
images of a fixed integer basis belong to finite bounded lattice sets.
The zero-rank case is included separately.

compile the Lean source or formalize the replacement arguments.

its LP1 edge). In particular there is no edge from TL core to FC19 or TG.

FC7's final conditional statement as a supplier of its own Laws premise.

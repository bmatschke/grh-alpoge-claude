TX6 REVIEW
1. The literal LevI group is a subgroup of GLint, at every middle index. This supplies integral matrices and integral inverses for TX1; no penultimate-only integrality hypothesis is being extrapolated to general indices.
2. locLat(e) tests q^(-e) times the vector. ybSet therefore gives centers in q^(-M) times the local lattice and directions q^(+M) times that lattice. The actual rational frame converts these to z in q^(-M)O_(P)^n and C=z+q^(+M)O_(P)^n. The quotient q^(-M)O^n/q^(M)O^n is finite, with actual additive depth2M*v_P(q). The coupling data are empty before the penultimate stage. This proves finiteness without identifying a rational q-power with an unramified valuation depth.
3. Openness and character triviality come from the actual ThetaOpen/lvQ_open statement. Bounding denominators of h and h^-1 makes h^-1*diag(g,I)*h sufficiently close to identity in both directions. The additive phase is integral and the residue character is1, so the literal Ok group/character readings put a sufficiently deep principal subgroup in the character kernel. Finite order alone is not used to imply continuity or an open kernel.
4. Increasing this depth makes (g−I)z lie in the positive direction lattice. Since g and g^-1 preserve that lattice, the whole coset stabilizer contains this principal subgroup. Intersecting with the original level preserves the character congruence kernel. TX1 then constructs the actual completed cut level, with its rational intersection and character restriction.
5. The class fibre fixes the raw index, auxiliary coupling reduction and every twist coupling coset. The source local-move criterion is necessary by moving the point and sufficient because those reductions/cosets determine the coupling data. DR3 supplies realizers of the same preferred points. The representatives are exactly y_c=g_c*k_beta^-1; chart readings, lattice transport and stabilizer maps therefore use those same objects. The FC4 normalization changes the value module and functional but preserves all chart groups, characters, companions, representatives and stabilizer maps. FC9 transports these to the Hermitian carrier with the literal SameCar equalities.

TX7 REVIEW
1. CI13 gives identity-double-coset nonvanishing and its two-sided transport at auxiliary primes. Together with CI7 and the literal index Ok predecessor/nonvanishing fields, induction puts each admitted auxiliary index representative in the identity double coset. The reference index set is nonempty by the same identity nonvanishing. At the penultimate index the raw representatives are literally identity, as required by last_h.
2. From h_a=(r_a*r_0^-1)*h_0*(kappa_0^-1*kappa_a), the projected groups are conjugate and their characters match. Explicitly the map from the twisted group to the reference group is the restriction/residue of (r_a*r_0^-1)^-1 on the retained X-space. CI14 verifies the phase and row-character equality, so this is a character-preserving conjugation, not only a group isomorphism.
3. At the penultimate stage the actual coupling representative has the required q^-1 auxiliary local bounds. Using the same vector y and the reference zero-exponent auxiliary direction gives Y0 with precisely the same auxiliary reduction. The reference has no twist-prime condition. Moving Y0 to its preferred representative gives Cbar0=g0^-1*Cbar. Thus g0^-1 is the required additional conjugator; it belongs to the reference auxiliary level, so inner conjugation preserves the character. Before this stage both coupling sets are empty.
4. The combined auxiliary residue matrices lift place by place to a unitary adele using paired conjugate-prime matrices as in AD4. There is no assumption that a global GL_n(O_E) reduction map is surjective. The lift preserves the standard local lattices and is identity at type primes and off the auxiliary places. With k_beta=k_reference*s_beta in precisely this order, admissibility and identity at Q follow from the reference readings and the prime-set disjointness.
5. For an adelic matrix with identity Q_F-components, the twist frame move is identity. The local-move dictionary, auxiliary conjugation and coupling match therefore give membership equivalence and exactly equal character values on the same Q-trivial subgroup. Testing a single-place adele proves equality of each Uloc away from Q. Base lattices are the same L_pi, and the reference member uses its existing reference charts.

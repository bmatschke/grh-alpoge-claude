The portable script uses the artifact directory above its reviews_v0_15
folder. Its sibling helper and explicit alias-map JSON are required and are
included. From an extracted artifact, rerun with Python3 and Git:

  python reviews_v0_15/source_verification_v0_15_audit_portable.py \
    --repo /path/to/pinned-source-repository \
    --verification-dir /path/to/new-source-verification \
    --output-dir /path/to/new-audit-results

7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4 and a clean tracked worktree. The two
output directories must be new/empty, outside both artifact and repository,

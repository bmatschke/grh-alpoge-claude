MATHEMATICAL CHECKS

QW1. The displayed Euler factors are exactly StatementMass.eulerFac.
The fixed-prime CM difference witness makes residue conjugation
nontrivial, hence rules out residue degree one. This proves the p^2
norm lower bound without assuming a splitting law for arbitrary CM
primes. The factor logarithms are bounded by (residue degree)/2
 times -log(1-p^-M). Sum(e*f)=2f gives the total weight bound,
including ramified rational primes. Increasing finite positive Euler
products identify the convergent p-sum with log(zeta(M)); absolute
log convergence gives the positive tproduct and its actual logarithm.
The integral bound zeta(2)<=2 and log2<=1 are sufficient. No analytic
continuation or zero-free-region premise enters this claim.

QW2. Successive independent columns give q^(n^2)*product(1-q^-j).
The finite product lower bound 1-sum(q^-j)>=1/2 is valid for q>=3.
The literal row free-support pattern has r=k+2s+1 and
s(2k+1+2s)+k(k-1)/2 free entries. I checked that these are independent
upper-triangular entries and that every such matrix has determinant
one, so no extra quotient factor is omitted. Subtracting this from
r^2 gives (r^2+3k+1+2s)/2. The row-minus-column exponent is exactly
m(2k+m-1)/2. Taking f copies at each of two host primes gives the
factor 2^(-2f), with denominator Euler factors at most one. Both
actual idxColLoc/idxRowLoc and their ratios are strictly positive.

QW3. The ratio hypothesis is retained explicitly. The product starts
at N=k+1>=3, where that hypothesis applies, and has m-1 terms.
Positive masses justify every cancellation. For m=1 the empty product
is correct. fDeg(E)=f and vInv(E)=rd(E)/2 are exactly the totally
complex host normalizations. The source producer indeed requires
HermRepUnram/LIStat; QW3 does not infer it from finite genus positivity.

QW4. Pair (i,j) among interior u coordinates maps injectively onto the
nonadjacent v pairs (i-1,j). All omitted adjacent v differences are
4*Delta>=1. The pairs involving the two endpoints number 2k-3,
including their overlap exactly once. Under either actual endpoint
alternative, the total u-span is <=8a. Appending -b adds cross factors
at least b_j and bottom factors at least one. The resulting lower bound
is valid also at k=2; empty products are interpreted as one. Positivity
of all Vandermondes is established before their logarithms are taken.

QW5. Interlacing w_j<b_(j+1) telescopes the positive product
product(w_j/b_j) to at most w_(m-1)/b_0. The claim's b_0>=1 follows
from 4k*Delta<=b_0/x, k>=2, Delta>=1, x>=1. The bounds on sum log b,
log x, log k and log(8a) produce exactly the error
kL*(20+(2+2C0)/epsilon)+2mL. I independently collected the constants:
17k+3kL is at most 20kL, and m+mL at most 2mL. The nonnegative C0
hypothesis is retained when multiplying the log-x bound.

QW6. All factorials cancel in the exact range k+1,...,r. The residual
mass loss is f*(m-1)*(1+L)/2+f*(m-1); the lost main ell term is fk*ell,
with 0<=ell=log(v/pi)<=L. The index contributes at least fkm*G-2f*log2
because G>=0 and m>=1. The lower dimension factor contributes no
more than f/2 loss. After division by f, the displayed total loss is
bounded by (30+K)*(kL+m^2L), K=(2+2C0)/epsilon>=0. For example
replace ell by L, (1+L)/2 by L, m-1 by (m-1)L, and the last 2.5 by
2kL; then it is at most (23+K)kL+4mL, which is below the claimed
bound for k>=2,m>=1,L>=1. Thus C=30+(2+2C0)/epsilon works uniformly,
with no suppressed dependence on ranks, coordinates or masses.
The exact same hD/hd hypotheses imply Dstar,dstar>0 before using logs.

QW7. The stronger rank windows and RF14 Lambda bound give the exact
bottom-window exponent -5/2-epsilon/8; it tends to zero uniformly.
The source top/thick/thin cases give the actual beta lower bound w''
and upper bound B+8f^2+2. The root-of-unity inequality q<=2*phi(q)^2
follows from the prime-power formula, with only 2^1 producing a factor
1/2; phi(q)<=[E:Q] gives the stated 8f^2 bound. All nine PlaceData
clauses are checked on the supplied HCParam/ChOK coordinates; no new
weight choices replace them. In particular beta+mH0<=2B and the
u endpoint alternatives have the exact half-unit corrections. Middle
weights are >=1. The threshold is independent of the eventual host,
word and coordinates after epsilon, cD, ch and standard constants fix.

QW8. The literal lower root-discriminant bound gives v>=16/5>pi;
the upper host/conductor bound and the positive exponent gap give
log v<=L. g_i>=(kB)^8 yields log g_i>=8L. The rank-constant inequalities
follow from m>=x^(1/4),m<=x,k>=x^(9/2+10epsilon) with their constants
fixed before the last threshold. Negative C or CGamma cause no problem:
the proof uses absolute values for the threshold bound. The argument
does not construct a host satisfying the quantitative windows.

QW9. The quantifier order matches window_lower: fix the global mass
ratio, epsilon, standard C and cD; choose c2=0; for each ch>=0 choose
x0 before P,q1,a,b,c,du,Ds and the word. C0=5 specializes the proved
constant to 30+12/epsilon. The original hcW_v/hcW_b identities, and the
Tau enumeration, justify the one base-row Ds inequality for every word.
The average log term is nonnegative, the two host primes give >=16mL,
and the C error costs <=mL. The exact GamS exponent costs at most one
further mL, leaving >=14mL and hence the required mL. Positivity of
Ds and du is justified by the original same-data equalities. No mass,
volume, theta, spectral or Core construction is smuggled into this
conditional numerical conclusion.

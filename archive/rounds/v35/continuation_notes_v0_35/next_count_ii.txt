3. Exact one-point Choice deliverable and proposed proof sequence

Fix the original arithmetic frame and the normalized x:NP F 4 supplied by
the NiceI construction, with x.a=4 and x.N3. Retain ell≠2, PhiCond(F.Gamma,
ell), and the stated existence of a residual cyclotomic value different
from 1. In the active rank-four application ell may be 3.

Produce q, sg:Fin(q)→F.G0, an NP x', a ring map j:x.A→x'.A, a Kap k' of
x', and al:Fin(q)→Fin(4)→x'.A such that:

  • every sg(a) belongs to the original F.G';
  • j is injective, local and finite;
  • x'.rho=pushRep(j,x.rho), x'.a=x.a, and every ordered local character
    is the corresponding pushCh, exactly as NP.Ext specifies;
  • Detects(k',range(sg)) holds for the entire literal TwZ/TwB predicate;
  • for every a, al(a) is injective and the characteristic polynomial
    of x'.rho(sg(a)) is the product of X−al(a,b), b in Fin(4).

The one-point residue-cardinality equality in Choice is automatic. The
rest is substantive and remains unproved at this release boundary.

(a) Full-block reduction. Read Ev, Od, NP, Kap, pairedDualFirstOrder-
    DualMatrixSpace, TwZ, TwB and ell together. Prove the g=0 Kap fields
    from the zero odd block, including gen, indep and local corrections.
    Identify the remaining eta component with the ORIGINAL cyclotomically
    twisted matrix cocycle. Do not replace that twist by an untwisted
    adjoint cocycle or drop the transpose/conjugation symmetry.

(b) Trace detection over the fraction field. Reconstruct the specialized
    EvenPart argument: trace vanishing on G', together with NP.span after
    inverting t, should let the cocycle define a derivation of the full
    matrix algebra. Write the trace-pairing test for well-definedness and
    the matrix-unit proof that this derivation is inner. Then extend the
    resulting coboundary from G' to all G0 using the original commutator
    and cyclotomic-character conditions. Impose the transpose symmetry
    using the allowed division by 2, and clear denominators by a power
    of the actual t. The result must be Detects, including its existential
    t-power, not just rational vanishing of a cohomology class. Do not
    divide by 4!, or assume characteristic zero.

(c) Restrict to separable elements. x.N3 concerns every open set meeting
    G'. Use it exactly. Prove openness of the nonzero discriminant locus
    in the t-adic topology and closedness of each trace-zero test; obtain
    detection on the subset S of G' with separable characteristic
    polynomial. These are the actual OmDense/EllClosed joins, not an
    abstract Zariski-density assertion.

(d) Make the detector finite. Reconstruct the continuous twisted-cocycle
    finite-generation step from PhiCond, then apply the finite collection
    of linear tests to a finitely generated module over K[[t]]. The source
    finSub argument uses Noetherianity of the finite generator-coordinate
    module. Preserve the saturation by powers of t and all continuity
    requirements. No topological finite-generation assumption about the
    Galois group may replace PhiCond without proof.

(e) Split all selected polynomials in one finite coefficient extension.
    They are monic and separable over Frac(K[[t]]). Follow their common
    finite splitting field, its complete DVR integral closure, and its
    equal-characteristic power-series presentation. Prove that the roots
    are integral and pairwise distinct, and transport the same detector,
    representation, flags, block and characters through NP.Ext. Distinct
    roots in the extension ring need not be distinct modulo its maximal
    ideal. The extension need not be unramified, and j(t) need not equal
    the new power-series variable. These stronger properties must not be
    imposed merely because CIP's earlier constant-field extension had
    a simpler form. For h=0 no additional residue-cardinality alignment
    across several points is needed.

Each of these is a proof obligation for the next chapter. If finite
detection or extension transport cannot be completed, state that precise

successful full one-point endpoint should also explicitly supply the

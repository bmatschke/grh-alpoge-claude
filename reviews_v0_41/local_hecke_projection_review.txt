MAIN FINDINGS
The integrality transfer is valid with one prime-to-ell denominator product. The maximal-ideal exhaustion is valid even when the unreduced Hecke algebra is not finite: its nilradical quotient embeds as a finite O-submodule of O^s. The original projected module, its full span, canonical localization and faithful descended action all match the actual MtSub/TOf definitions. Semisimplicity is transferred only from the retained complex no-Jordan assertion. The complete nonempty_spec assembly has all original coefficient, twist, bridge, ramification and relation fields.

Four draft precision findings were resolved before the final review:
- SplitPlace stores an embedding rather than a canonical comparison field; equality from the prime requires a proof. The final text proves equality of induced local valuation rings and then uses density and z^{-1} to force equality of embeddings. Four exact source ranges were added.
- Generic mkX copies B0.Qw, so Qc_eq is not reflexive from using C. The final text explicitly uses the retained hQ equality B0.Qw=C.Q; identity transports and reflexive twist/side fields are distinguished.
- Generic D7 supplies OtoLL as a ring map with integral images; injectivity is not needed for OsInt and is not automatic. The final text states the exact ring-map/image-integrality scope, then retains the required injection only under OsConcrete for relation freeness.
- The optional a^N=1 argument needs N>0. The final text now specifies N>0. The actual ell^a exponent is positive; the main PsiInt implication is unchanged.

The author’s two additional literal-object corrections also check: s0 uses the exact factorization exponents, and Bj uses MjLat(j).gen rather than the ambient MtLat generators.

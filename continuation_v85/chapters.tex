\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Integral lifting at a type prime}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.85 \quad 25 November 2026
\end{center}

A surjection of Galois lattices can have a finite cokernel on
its associated Breuil--Kisin modules. The size of this cokernel and
the cost of correcting the monodromy together determine the type-prime
term in the denominator estimate. We track both costs, then compare
the corrected vector with a finite integral Galois cocycle. Throughout,
$R$ is the largest Frobenius period of a ratio of the allowed inertia
characters and $[-b,H]$ is the interval of allowed jumps. The final
sections treat the archimedean weights, spherical base change and good
finite types of each original selected member. We also compute the wild
inertia polynomial of every localized unitary representative.

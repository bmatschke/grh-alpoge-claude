# Independent review of the integral tensor pair and weights

Manuscript SHA-256: `5ea4e003a8cdf661c051139eb737cff533cb20732cb5f34a2cdec9865f3b2b22`.

I compared Q65P1–4 with the definitions in OverOf4 and the proofs of pairOf4 and wtsOf4 at the original commit `7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4`.

1. The signed tensor basis produces the indicated alternating form with both upper anti-diagonal entries positive. Independently multiplying matrices over the integral polynomial ring confirmed all sixteen entries of the flag conjugation modulo the relation that the sign squares to one, as well as the symplecticity of the flag. All equations used to pass to the coefficient ring are polynomial identities.
2. At forbidden inertia the initial tower's labelled quadratic root has residual value one. Its off-field value would be minus one in odd characteristic, so this inertia acts trivially on the auxiliary quadratic field. The outside-ℓ unramified clause for the dihedral factor and the exact away clause for the modular factor therefore both apply. The multiplier-twist kernel clause uses the full cyclotomic and residual values. The later marked element restricts to the required prime-power of the initial mark, so the two factors stay diagonal there.
3. The determinant identity is assumed only after the chosen coefficient specialization and precisely on the fixing group of the later real field. For a local element of Mu, a prime-power lies in the closed commutator subgroup. The unramified quotient of the local decomposition group is torsion-free and the ℓ-adic cyclotomic quotient has no nontrivial ℓ-power torsion at odd ℓ. Thus the element lies in inertia, has trivial full cyclotomic value, and has trivial residual representation by the stated tower condition. The two rank-two ordinary characters on this element, the quadratic split and reciprocal identity on the second plane, and the multiplier twist give all four required diagonal values. This checks the six fields of IsPair over arbitrary commutative specializations.
4. The weight assertion allows every tower before applying a comparison or acceptability condition. The auxiliary quadratic field splits at every original place above ℓ, independently of the chosen tower. Pulling back the single open subgroup in the input twist yields one subgroup for all places and all four squares. The four exponents and their order match the exact original WtsOf4, including the shift by the supplied integer and the conversion of k−1 from natural to integral exponents.

These arguments establish the conditional tensor pair and ordered coefficient weights for the original datum. Their use does not presume that such arithmetic data have already been supplied.

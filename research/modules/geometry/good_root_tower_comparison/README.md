GRT46–52 identify the finite determinant and transfer its CM value to the abelian interior and the boundary quotient. GRT53–57 prove the finite-curve, horizontal and mixed-intersection estimates on each fixed split wall and feed them into GRT7. GRT58–60 derive descent of the boundary ideal from the integral theta class-sum and trait hypotheses, including at residue characteristics dividing a stabilizer order.

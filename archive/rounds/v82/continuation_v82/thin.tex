\section{The inert row estimate on an orbit without thick letters}

% BEGIN N82I1
\paragraph{N82I1. The frozen C and D colength at an entirely nonthick place.}
Take the guarded inert index, point and place of I82W1--I82W3.
Assume the index is outside row B, and that no letter of the
Frobenius orbit at this place lies over a thick host place.
All good coefficient extensions in the local row test have this
same property: changing the initial reading only cyclically
reindexes the embeddings of the fixed completion into the good
coefficient field. Equivalently the actual embeddings of this
completion, read through the fixed complex bridge, are all nonthick.
Write $d=[E_w:\mathbb Q_p]=n_w$, and take any tested integral
onto map $f:T_1\to T_2$ on the row. The rational target has
dimension $r$, and the rational source embeds in a finite sum
of the allowed twisted dual representations.

Let $h$ be the largest of $H$ and the $s_a+S_0$ in I82W2.
The letter windows there enclose every jump of $T_1$ in $[-h,h]$.
Since every size $s_a$ is at most $W_0$, I82W3 gives
\[
                   h\le w_P+S_c+\eta_3 .
\]
The completion is unramified, $p\ge11$, and the coefficient
extension is good at level one. Apply the local argument
L81S2--L81S4 on these lattices with this $h$: the tame group
has order $p-1$, the two Wach lifts and the divided difference
use only the height window and crystallinity of the lattices,
and their kernel and quotient have the same window by strictness.
The proof through the first numerical inequality of L81S4 gives
an integer lifting exponent $E\le25h/p+3$. The later comparison
in L81S4 with the constant on a split declaration is unnecessary
here. Thus the image of finite integral cohomology contains
$p^E H^1_f(E_w,T_2)$ on this very map.

As in A81R1 the finite target cohomology is generated by at most
$(d+2)r$ elements; the rational Bloch--Kato dimension supplies at
most $(d+1)r$ and integral torsion at most $r$. Consequently the
quotient by the image has finite $O$-length, bounded after division
by $k_O=\operatorname{length}_O(O/pO)$ by $(d+2)rE$. This is
also the native row colength since integral coboundaries are
quotiented out. Put $G=w_P+\eta_3$. For either membership decision
in the hub's band, its flat term is at least
$G/p+S_c+\mathrm{large}_2$, with $\mathrm{large}_2\ge1$.
It follows that
\[
 (d+2)r(25h/p+3)
 \le 50(d+1)r\,(G/p+S_c+\mathrm{large}_2)
 \le 50\,\mathrm{coreF}(w).
\]
Indeed $d+2\le2(d+1)$, $h\le G+S_c$, and $3\le25\mathrm{large}_2$.
The chosen row constants have $K_1=368$, $K_2=2082$,
$K_3=22365$. The three cores are nonnegative and the flat core is
positive; hence the bound is at most the lower endpoint of the
interval used to choose the rational $\mathrm{lenRowCD}$, strictly
below its upper endpoint. The stored rational bound applies to
every tested map at this place, for either C or D. If an orbit has
a thick letter, I82W3 instead allows its height to be of order
$b$, and the argument here retains the larger term $r(d+2)b/p$.
% END N82I1

% BEGIN N82I2
\paragraph{N82I2. A placewise criterion and a prime range for inert C and D.}
Keep the same middle-range guards with row B excluded. For any
place $w\mid p$ put
\[
 F_w=\frac{w_P+\eta_3}{p}+S_c+\mathrm{large}_2
                  +\eta_3\,\mathbf 1_{X.\mathrm{band}(i,w)} .
\]
This is the source's $\mathrm{flat}(X.\mathrm{band}(i,w),p)$;
in particular $\mathrm{coreF}(w)=(d+1)r F_w$.
At every such place, even with thick letters, the native row bound
holds whenever
\[
                       25h_B/p+3\le2082 F_w .
\]
In fact for every good coefficient extension I82W2--I82W3 put
all the tested jumps in $[-\lfloor h_B\rfloor,\lfloor h_B\rfloor]$.
Apply N82I1's Wach lifting argument at this integral height.
Its exponent is at most $25h_B/p+3$. I82B1's generator count
for the finite integral target needs only its rank $r$ and the
degree $d$ of the completion, so it applies to this C or D map.
The normalized cokernel length is thus at most $(d+1)r(25h_B/p+3)$,
which under the displayed condition is at most
$2082\,\mathrm{coreF}(w)$. Both other cores are nonnegative.
The rational rounding in N82I1 then supplies precisely
$\mathrm{lenRowCD}(w)$ for every tested map.

In particular the bound holds at every place of every guarded
index satisfying $h_B\le83p(S_c+\mathrm{large}_2)$ and at every
good point there. Indeed $S_c+\mathrm{large}_2\ge1$, and hence
\[
 25h_B/p+3\le2078(S_c+\mathrm{large}_2)\le2082 F_w .
\]
This uniform range is independent of the choice of band.

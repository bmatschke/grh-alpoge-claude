2. Scope and choices

No Noetherianity, reducedness, integrality or flatness hypothesis is
added to the arbitrary-base ampleness or polarized base-change theorem.
The finite good principal functions arise from one finite expression
for 1, rather than from finite generation of an arbitrary ideal.
No section is canceled outside the open on which it is a unit.

The KTriv argument preserves every commutative test ring R, its exact
map t, point x and original local opens V. It uses the canonical
isomorphism T ×_{S'} A' ≅ T ×_S A, then transports the actual
translation-difference generator through that isomorphism.
It does not assume a nonflat global-section base-change theorem.

The actual polarized record is common throughout; no replacement
abelian variety, line bundle or unrelated set of sections is selected.
The all-test product identity has arbitrary T, not just affine or
field tests. The very-ample exponent may depend on the prescribed
affine test. No universal n^g section basis is inferred here.

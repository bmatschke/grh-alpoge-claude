NP7. Cyclic reindexing, degree shifts and the final height comparison.

List the even marked points in cyclic order. Since there are at least
two, their consecutive open forward intervals partition all other points,
including the last-to-first interval. An odd marked y is not an endpoint
and belongs to exactly one. Let x be its preceding even endpoint and d
its forward distance, 0<d<n'. No nearer preceding even point exists:
it would lie between consecutive even endpoints. Conversely a minimum
positive distance from an even point to y identifies this x, since
otherwise the next even endpoint before y would give a smaller distance.
Thus x=yPrev(y),d=dPrev(y), with exactly the natural-infimum definitions
in the source. Distinct interval indices and ordered distances recover
distinct points. This proves RestOddTops for every summand, including
wraparound, without a parity assumption on the circle length.

For original integer window degrees aw,bw, write
 S=sum_{odd y in TT} min(bw_y,aw_prev/p^d).
NP4 gives nonnegative lower window bounds. Consequently
(aw+ccl).toNat and (bw+ccl).toNat cast back to aw+ccl,bw+ccl.
For P>=1,c>=0,

because c/P<=c and min(u+c,v+c)=min(u,v)+c.

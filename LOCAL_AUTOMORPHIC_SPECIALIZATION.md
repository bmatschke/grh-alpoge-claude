# Automorphic specialization of the same integral Hecke family

**Theorem.** Retain the original automorphic data (`SideA`), the rational realizations and compatible twists of the local specialization theorem. Assume conjugation preserves its indicated coefficient subfield and its prescribed angular exponents are integers, as in the original statement. Given **any** local specialization satisfying that theorem's comparison identities, there are automorphic labels, local parameters, weights and a compatible residue-field embedding realizing the full original `AutSpec` on it. The local comparison of the Galois and Hodge data over the primes above the coefficient characteristic can use the very same local fields and embeddings. [Original theorem and its hypotheses](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/SideRationalLevelData.lean#L31-L42) · [What the specialization asserts](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/RingSpecNormalisedCharacterEq.lean#L336-L364)

The [isotypic decomposition and multiplicity formula](LOCAL_AUTOMORPHIC_POINTS.md) give the labels themselves. Choose one representative of each distinct irreducible representation meeting the prescribed type, including those lying outside this congruence class. The realization's map to the automorphic classes is injective on this set since it identifies isomorphic representatives. Read the local and Satake parameters at these very classes. Use the original row parameters on the other side of the comparison. This preserves their given labels and handles all multiplicities. ## Where the local representation is spherical

Let $v$ be a prime of the maximal real subfield and $P$ a prime of $E$ above it. At a place where the level is maximal compact and its type character is trivial, any nonzero vector of that type is fixed by the local maximal compact. Project such a vector in an isotypic space to a nonzero component in an irreducible copy. Equivariance gives the same fixed-vector property in the chosen representative. This proves sphericity for every nonsplit $P$: the two type primes, the support of the auxiliary twist and the two distinguished primes all have split support. For split $P$, the unramified conclusion applies when $P$ avoids the conductor $Q_i$, both type primes and the selected correction prime ($p'_\delta$ if $\ell=p_\delta$, and $p_\delta$ otherwise). The conductor contains entire fibers over the maximal real subfield, so these exclusions put $v$ outside the bad level. This domain can include primes over $\ell$. The original realization turns the split fixed vector into unramifiedness of the parameter and the nonsplit fixed vector into its required spherical label. At the good split primes away from $\ell$ the [polynomial comparison](LOCAL_AUTOMORPHIC_POINTS.md#the-polynomial-at-a-good-split-prime) furnishes the further eigenvalue identity. ## The sign of the weight

For a coefficient weight $a$ take $a^\vee$. Its matrices act by inverse transpose. Inversion on the rational unitary group is conjugate transpose, so evaluation on the inverse again has the specified polynomial form after conjugating the embeddings and interchanging the indices. The dual is irreducible by the annihilator correspondence. The evaluation pairing identifies the contragredient, and the weight law of the original realization assigns the same dual weight class to each constituent in this type. Use the given infinitesimal character of that class. For an entry $z=k+(d-1)/2$ in rank $d$, the labeled integer jump is $-k=(d-1)/2-z$. Apply this to every entry with its multiplicity. The integrality comes from the regular algebraic weight law on the stated domain. For Galois representations take the Weil–Deligne parameter, the crystalline, potentially crystalline and de Rham predicates and the jump data supplied by the frame at the same coefficient embedding. ## Reduction at the chosen coefficient place

Fix a local factor $i$ and its coefficient place $w$. Write $p=\ell(i)=\operatorname{resChar}(w)$ and $O=O_i$, a discrete valuation ring finite over $\mathbb Z_p$. The given map $j:\operatorname{Frac}(O)\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$ restricts on $\mathbb Z_p$ to its usual inclusion. The place $w$ comes with a field isomorphism $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_p\simeq\mathbb C$, the valuation ring $B=\{z\in\mathbb C:|\iota^{-1}z|_p\le1\}$, and a fixed identification $b:k(B)\simeq\overline{\mathbb F}_p$. [Coefficient ring](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L80-L91) · [Place data](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/WdOfMono0L.lean#L35-L40) · [Chosen scalar embedding](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/RingSpecNormalisedCharacterEq.lean#L263-L269)

For every $y\in O$, $j(y)$ satisfies a monic equation over $\mathbb Z_p$. If $|j(y)|_p>1$, its leading term would strictly dominate the others. Thus $\iota j(y)\in B$. Composing with reduction and $b$ gives a homomorphism $f:O\to\overline{\mathbb F}_p$. Its kernel contains $pO$ since $|j(p)|_p<1$. The ring $O/pO$ is finite because $O$ is module-finite over $\mathbb Z_p$. Consequently the image of $f$ is a finite integral domain, hence a field. The kernel is therefore the unique maximal ideal of $O$. There is a unique field embedding $\kappa_i:k(O)\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb F}_p$ satisfying

$$
\kappa_i(y\bmod\mathfrak m_O)=b\bigl(\iota j(y)\bmod\mathfrak m_B\bigr)
\qquad (y\in O).
$$

This proves integrality and agreement with the specified place reduction for every coefficient in the local factor. It works for any of the given choices of $\iota$ and $b$. Applying it independently to each $i$ supplies both the residue embeddings and the compatibility used in the automorphic specialization. The equality of the two labels for $p$ accounts for the transports between copies of $\overline{\mathbb F}_p$ in the statement. ## The rank-one character and its Frobenius eigenvalue

Fix a local summand $i$. Write $\ell$ for its residue characteristic, $w_i$ for its coefficient place, and $j$ for the unique zero-based index with $j+1=X.\mathrm{nd}.j(i)$. The rank-one automorphic representation $\mu_C(i)$ is the unitary Hecke character

$$
u_i=\mu_{0,j}\,\xi_{X.\mathrm{nd}.\mu(i)}
   =\mu_{0,j}\,\delta_{\ell}\,\psi_{w_i}.
$$

Here $\delta_\ell$ denotes the source's $\mathrm{delAt}(\ell)$, which selects the alternate auxiliary character when $\ell=p_\delta$. The first expression defines the character via the given rank-one identification $A.\mathrm{chr}.e1$. The second uses the twist identity for this very tuple. The factor $\mu_{0,j}$ is unitary, and $\xi$ has finite order. Both restrict trivially to the ideles of $F=E^+$. Since $x\,c(x)$ is the image of $N_{E/F}(x)$, one has $u_i(cx)=u_i(x)^{-1}$. This proves conjugate self-duality on all local and infinite components and gives the stipulated character for the unique $j$. [The rank-one identification](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/ExistsMuC.lean#L11-L22)

Let $\eta$ be the frame’s fixed character of angular weight one, unramified at each finite prime. The character entering the local parameter is $h_i=u_i\eta^{-1}$. Let $N P$ denote the residue-field size. The precise claim at every allowed split place $P$ and every arithmetic Frobenius lift $g$ above it is

$$
\iota_i\bigl(\mathrm{iq}_i(\chi_{i,1}(g))\bigr)
    = h_{i,P}(\varpi_P)^{-1}(N P)^{-r/2},
\qquad -r/2=(t-1)/2+(1-n_V)/2.
$$

Here the integral value on the left is included in its fraction field, $\mathrm{iq}_i$ is the chosen embedding into $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, and $\iota_i$ is the complex identification at $w_i$. The equality of exponents uses $n_V=r+t$. At such $P$, $\mu_{0,j}$ is unramified away from the two type primes, $\delta_\ell$ is unramified away from its exceptional prime, $\psi_{w_i}$ is unramified outside the selected $Q$, and $\eta$ is unramified at every finite prime. The definition of the allowed places excludes all these exceptions and the primes over $\ell$. Consequently $h_i$ is unramified there. [The exact parameter and exponent](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L707-L711)

First prove the equality away from a finite set. The chosen integral $\chi_{i,1}$ is the product of the transported base character and the chosen ring-class character. The base reading in $\mathrm{SideB}$, transported by $T.\mathrm{twO\_emb}$ and $\mathrm{Sp}.\mathrm{iq\_LL}$, gives $(\mu_{0,j}\delta_\ell\eta^{-1})_P(\varpi_P)^{-1}(\sqrt{N P})^{-r}$. For the other factor, the value-field condition puts $\psi_{w_i,P}(\varpi_P)$ in the complex image of the common coefficient field. Apply the chosen Frobenius comparison to its inverse. Injectivity of $C.\mathrm{embW}$ identifies that inverse with the common character, and $\mathrm{Sp}.\mathrm{psi\_eq}$ and $\mathrm{iq\_LL}$ read the same value on the localized ring. Multiplication gives the displayed equality at the stored valuation above each remaining prime. [The two coefficient transports](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/ChiOneRead.lean#L13-L80)

To recover **every** allowed prime, form the algebraic Hecke character $b_i=h_i|\cdot|_{\mathbb A_E}^{-r/2}$. This norm twist has value $(NP)^{r/2}$ at a uniformizer. At infinity the integral parameters $e_{j,\tau}$ give $\mu_{0,j}$ integral exponents $(-e_{j,\tau},e_{j,\tau})$ at a conjugate pair of embeddings, and $\xi$ has finite order. Write $r=2d+1$. The angular weight one of $\eta$ gives exponents $(-d-1,-d)$ for $\eta^{-1}|\cdot|^{-r/2}$. Thus $b_i$ has an ordinary continuous $\ell$-adic avatar by class field theory, obtained by removing the algebraic infinite component of the character and inserting its $\ell$-adic realization. With geometric reciprocity, its value at an arithmetic Frobenius away from $\ell$ at any unramified place is $\iota_i^{-1}(b_{i,P}(\varpi_P)^{-1})$. This avatar and $\mathrm{iq}_i(\chi_{i,1})$ are continuous $\ell$-adic characters. The chosen embedding of the finite local field is continuous by the uniqueness of the extending valuation. The cofinite Frobenius identity above, conjugacy of the valuations over a prime, and Chebotarev identify the two characters everywhere. Finally $A.\mathrm{chr}.\mathrm{frob}$ gives the singleton $\{h_{i,P}(\varpi_P)^{-1}\}$ for the untwisted rank-one parameter, and the prescribed local parameter twist multiplies it by $(NP)^{-r/2}$. ## The local comparison on the same bridges

Given the local coefficient data $\mathrm{Sp}$ and the automorphic specialization $Y$ over the same host frame $A$, one obtains the entire local comparison `LocBr Y`. For each coefficient factor $i$ and prime $u\mid\ell(i)$, take the very bridge $B_{i,u}$ already chosen in $\mathrm{Sp}$. It realizes the prescribed valuation, has the required roots of unity in its coefficient field $L$, and sends every $x\in O_i$ to $\mathrm{Sp.iq}_i(x)$ inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. The specialization identifies the coefficient embedding of $Y$ with this same map. [Local comparison to be constructed](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/LocAutHodgeTateWeightsLevelZeroDef.lean#L363-L370)

The local $p$-adic Hodge comparison is supplied by the given frame. Its assertion applies to every bridge realizing the prescribed valuation and scalar embedding, hence to $B_{i,u}$. For a continuous global representation of any rank over $O_i$, it identifies the frame's crystalline, de Rham and potentially crystalline predicates at $u$ with those of the restriction to the decomposition group, viewed through $B_{i,u}$. In the de Rham case it identifies membership and multiplicity of each integer in the multiset of filtration jumps for every pair of embeddings of $E$ and $E_u$ that agree on $E$. It gives each of these conclusions also for a continuous representation over any finite $L'\supset L$, using its inclusion in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. The automorphic specialization identifies all three predicates and the jump multiset of $Y$ with those of the frame, on exactly these scalar extensions of the representation. Composing the equivalences and substituting the equality of multisets proves both clauses of `LocTie`, including all ranks and larger coefficient fields. Thus the family of bridges in `LocBr Y` is exactly $\mathrm{Sp.nbr}$. ## The remaining local obligation

On these hypotheses we have the integral Hecke spaces, their automorphic realization, and the comparison at primes above $\ell$. The next part of the original local construction prescribes the blocks in base change, the residual representations $\bar\rho$ with their semisimplicity and tame inertia, and the associated subspaces of the given modular forms. Their Hecke eigenvalues and reduction maps must agree with the spaces just constructed. The global design must ultimately supply all the input families together and preserve the required closed class.

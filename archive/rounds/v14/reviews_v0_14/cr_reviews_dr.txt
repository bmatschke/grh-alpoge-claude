DR1: LITERAL CM INPUT AND LOCAL ALGEBRA

CMData.cmUnram is read in its actual HcS definition: for every maximal ideal
P of O_E, an integral theta has theta-star(theta) outside P. Together with
CMData.fixed and degree two, this has exactly the strength used in DR1.
The chosen theta is not fixed and generates E over F. At a base prime v,
the other possible prime above v is its conjugate. Conjugation negates
theta-star(theta), so this difference is a unit at that component too.
It is therefore a unit in the whole completed integral algebra A above v.

For a in A, b=(a-bar(a))/(theta-bar(theta)) and c=a-b*theta are integral
and fixed. The fixed algebra is F_v and its integral subring is O_Fv,
so b,c belong to R. This proves A=R+R*theta, independently generated by
1,theta. The quadratic polynomial's discriminant is a unit; no global
integral-basis theorem is assumed. Its residue algebra is separable
quadratic. The written simple-root lifting gives the split product R x R.
In the irreducible-residue case, finite freeness gives completeness and a
minimum coordinate valuation. Nonzero residue elements are units by the
geometric-series inverse. Thus every nonzero element is a uniformizer power
times a unit, and A is the actual unramified quadratic DVR. Its residue
involution is nontrivial because theta-bar(theta) remains nonzero.

NORM-UNIT SURJECTIVITY, INCLUDING DYADIC PLACES

For residue fields of orders q^2 and q, the norm is z^(q+1) on a cyclic
group of order q^2-1, so it maps onto k^*. The trace is k-linear into k.
The nonzero polynomial X+X^q has degree q<q^2 and cannot vanish on every
element of k_2. Hence trace is onto even in characteristic two; the proof
never substitutes Tr(1)=2 as a unit.

If N(b_m)-u=varpi^m*c_m, the correction trace -c_m/u has the correct sign
and denominator: u is a unit. Multiplication by 1+varpi^m*z changes the
norm modulo varpi^(m+1) by varpi^m*u*Tr(z). The quadratic term has order
at least 2m>=m+1 for m>=1. Thus each step improves the congruence by one
power. The b_m are Cauchy and retain their nonzero residue class, so their
limit is a unit of norm u. The split norm (a,b)|->a*b is plainly onto.
No division by 2 or odd-residue-characteristic premise is hidden.

INTEGRAL ORTHONORMAL INDUCTION

Perfectness makes the integral Gram determinant a unit, so reduction gives
a nondegenerate Hermitian form over k_2/k. If all self-pairings vanished,
h(x+a*y,x+a*y)=Tr(a*h(x,y)) would vanish for every a. Nondegeneracy gives
a nonzero h(x,y), contradicting trace surjectivity. This remains valid in
characteristic two. Lift the vector: its norm is a unit u of R. Dividing
by a unit of norm u gives norm one.

The integral projection x|->v*h(v,x) splits M=A*v plus its orthogonal
complement. The latter is free over the DVR and selfdual: a vector in its
rational orthogonal span pairing integrally with the complement also pairs
integrally with all of M, hence belongs to M and to the complement.
Induction yields an integral orthonormal basis. For the split algebra,
choose dual bases of the two R-lattices; the paired vectors (e_i,f_i)
form an A-basis with Gram I. Rank zero is immediate.

DR2: ACTUAL ADELIC MATRIX AND GENUS NAMES

Clearing finitely many generators and an E-basis gives the rational-integer
sandwich d*O_E^n subset N subset d^(-1)*O_E^n. The local lattice is standard
outside primes dividing d. NB1's conjugate-component dual formula, expressed
in basis matrices, persists under completion and makes each completed
module perfect. At a split pair the components B_P and bar(B_P)^(-transpose)
have the correct orientation and carry the paired dual lattices.

The proof explicitly expands all local matrices and inverses in one fixed
F-basis 1,theta of E. Every scalar coefficient equals its identity-matrix
coefficient outside finitely many v, and so defines an actual finite adele
of F. The tensor terms give entries in E tensor_F A_F, not merely a formal
family of local points. Inverse and unitary identities hold componentwise.

AD4's finite-sandwich/CRT argument identifies the integral adelic span
with the product of completed local modules. Hence
g*widehat(O_E)^n=widehat(N) is literal Carries for adelic vectors. For
rational vectors, local-basis coefficient valuations identify completion
membership with local membership; LP1 recovers N. Therefore globLat(g)=N.

Formalism.L2.Col.UnimodGenus hc n in Columns/Inputs.lean asks exactly for
this unitary adelic g with globLat(g)=N. DR2 proves that predicate and the
actual ValuedLtOfMem.unimodGenus target directly. By contrast, UnimodGenusLoc
and PI.UnimodGenus/SameGenus require rational unitary isometries of semilocal
lattices. They are not asserted. DR2 bypasses them and needs no rational
unitary weak/strong approximation or class-number-one premise.

DR3: EXACT DECORATION INSTALLATION

Deco.Ok supplies a full selfdual N, residue-linearity on its P-localization,
a globally witnessed nonzero type functional, and the auxiliary map's global
surjectivity/kernel clauses. In a rational local basis the type map is a
nonzero row r. A basis of ker(r), followed by a vector with value one, gives
U with r*U=e_n^transpose. Lift entries to O_E. The determinant is nonzero
modulo P, so the lift lies in GL_n(E) with both it and its inverse integral
at P. It is not claimed invertible over global O_E. This local lift avoids
any false GL_n(O_E) residue-surjectivity assertion.

For the auxiliary map, clear a denominator outside P and use its global
kernel to obtain localized kernel P*N_P. Global witnesses give surjectivity.
Thus it is an isomorphism N_P/P*N_P -> k_P^n. Lifting its inverse matrix
makes every inverse-coordinate reduction equal the prescribed auxiliary
vector. The Q-frame is already a rational local basis by the literal DecoF
definition. No global freeness of N is used.

The three finite sets are disjoint and their union contains no conjugate
pair. AD4 applied to the entire union installs all bases simultaneously
while preserving N and unitarity. For x in N the pullback coordinates are
B_P^(-1)*x. The explicit a/s-r computation makes these residue equalities
the exact HasRes statements, with multiplicative valuation <1 for maximal-
ideal membership. No unspecified scalar appears.

This chapter contains 21 detailed human proofs, DJW1–DJW21. It installs
the previously reviewed determinant-window argument and continues through
the actual normal-form row substitutions. The resulting output is the
original window statement for the same integral germs, coordinates,
gauge and selected differential letters. It does not replace those data
by an abstract series with an unproved realization condition.

Completed original suppliers

DJW1–DJW8 prove the original FormalSeriesBounds.ThmP. The proof uses the
ordinary Cayley identity as one explicitly stated published input. It
then derives the exact Newton expansion, the valuations of all its
coefficients, positivity at the original ceiling threshold and the
integral binomial interpolation argument. The zero-size case is treated
separately. No discrete valuation, completeness or unramifiedness is
added to the original valued-field hypotheses.

DJW9–DJW14 prove restriction, the diagonal determinant coefficient
identity, BlockJet, the full literal BiValue, BiContent and the original
content_and_window export. BiValue retains arbitrary natural l and i;
primality, oddness and the weighted perturbation conditions enter only
where the valuation theorem requires them. Ordinary derivatives and
their factorials are retained throughout.

DJW15 proves all four MtoHM conclusions from the original MConcl,
including the level-one separated-row conclusion under the original
MPlusConcl premise. DJW16 gives the complete integral row-extension
argument at every intermediate size, using a normalized kernel vector
and an explicit right inverse. DJW17 constructs the original standard
Cartier systems and coordinates from Cart.t4 and a degreewise formal
contraction. DJW18 tracks the transpose in derivative substitution.

DJW19–DJW20 construct every row factor and the actual integral germs and
prove the full original block_of_normal_form. This statement accepts
every supplied h1 satisfying the original restriction identity. The
determinant factors have the positive powers det(Abar)^(qy) det(Y)^y;
both determinants are units, so the constructed comparison is an
equality of valuations. The systems and matrices are fixed before y.

DJW21 joins these results to the actual-row window, from a supplied
original MConcl. It gives the exact bound, quantifier order and order-zero
value. The existence theorem producing MConcl is not asserted here.

Retained scope

The ordinary Cayley identity is the explicitly attributed classical
input, not a new theorem claimed by this chapter. The supplied RowData
and every field of the original MConcl remain the hypotheses of the
geometric row constructor. In particular the normal-form existence
producer RowData.LemmaM, its level-one refinement, the common geometric
family and the global section/moduli laws are not supplied. The
conditional BiSupply join is explained but not promoted to an
unconditional output. GLOBAL_LOCAL_GAIN is not reopened or counted as a
new closure. No selected whole obligation is closed by this component.

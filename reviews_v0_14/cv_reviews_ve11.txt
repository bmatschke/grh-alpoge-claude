1. The CM input is literal and already belongs to the supplied host
=================================================================
DataSpaces.CMData contains cmUnram, total reality of F, degree two and the
exact fixed-field assertion. Its cmUnram field refers to the definition
in Formalism.HermitianLatticesTheory.StatementMass, not merely to a theorem
with a similar name. That definition requires, at every maximal ideal P,
integral theta and its actual conjugate t with theta-t outside P. This is
precisely DR.1. The actual involution is retained in CMData.sig.

The source HostCmData.host_cmData obtains the same field data from the
supplied arithmetic Host: its unramified field clause uses H.der.unramEF;
its involution clause uses bridge_star_holds and H.conjE_apply; and its
fixed-field and degree clauses are the host's original ones. Thus VE11
does not insert an additional CM unramified assumption beyond the retained
host. It also does not construct that host or prove its upstream existence.

DR1 uses the local integral algebra of this very extension. Its completed
algebra is split or unramified quadratic by the explicit theta argument.
The written unit-norm lifting uses surjectivity of the finite-field trace,
including in characteristic two, and never assumes 2 is invertible. The
resulting local orthonormal matrices therefore apply at every finite base
prime required by DR2, not just at the odd or selected split primes.

2. The actual column prime conditions supply every DR3 premise
=============================================================
The source exists_ptOf_twists applies to every rank n and every valid
DecoF with ST=(B.primes X).vT(u), SG=(B.primes X).vGs, and Q=I.SQ.
It assumes only the actual I.Ok together with the supplied Base/PreX data.
The finite-set checks in VE11/DR3 match its proof as follows.

(a) A selected type prime divides piT(u), so it contains the rational
integer T(u)=piT(u)*conjugate(piT(u)). The auxiliary primes do not contain
T(u), by the supplied distinct-prime law T_notMem_vGs. A twist prime cannot
contain T(u), because good_of_mem puts its underlying residue characteristic
outside I.S, whereas I.Ok.T_mem places T(u) in I.S. This proves ST disjoint
from SG and from SQ.

(b) Membership of a rational integer in P is equivalent to membership in
conjugate(P), since conjugation fixes that integer. The same T-separation
therefore excludes every conjugate pair crossing ST and SG union SQ.

(c) Two selected type primes cannot be conjugate: then the same prime
would contain both piT(u) and conjugate(piT(u)); their supplied Bezout
identity would put 1 in that proper prime. This is exactly piT_cop in
the wrapper. It also excludes a self-conjugate selected type prime.

(d) LevOkTwists.levOk_twists supplies Disjoint SG SQ and the no-conjugate-
pair condition on SG union SQ. Its proof uses the selected split prime
over each good underlying place, the auxiliary residue characteristics
in I.S, and the twist residue characteristics outside I.S. No form,
comparison, terminal carrier or decorated-point existence theorem occurs
in these finite-set checks.

Consequently ST, SG and SQ meet every disjointness and orientation premise
of DR3. For a particular twist set Q_a contained in SQ, the same assertions
restrict to Q_a. Equivalently one may use extTo SQ and then the literal
IsPtOf.of_extTo lemma: the five fields restrict with the exact given frame,
using Q_a subset SQ and SG disjoint Q_a. There is no new realization premise
in passing between the all-twist and individual-twist versions.

3. DR3 supplies the exact point required by charts and neighbors
==============================================================
DecoF packages a DecoSet, so the full lattice, selfduality, nonzero type
functional, auxiliary surjectivity/kernel and local-linearity clauses are
already available. Its frame condition says that the supplied rational
frame carries the standard prime-local lattice exactly to N_P.

The adapted matrix lifts in DR3 need to lie in GL_n(O_(P)), not in the
global GL_n(O_E). A nonzero residue determinant supplies this local
invertibility. At a type prime, r*U=e_last^t makes the inverse last
coordinate reduce to the original f. At an auxiliary prime, multiplying
the original basis by a lift of M^{-1} makes every inverse-coordinate
residue equal the prescribed be vector. The actual rational Q-frames
are installed unchanged. A global integral basis of N is never assumed.

AD4 can be applied once to the union ST union SG union Q: this is precisely
its finite no-conjugate-pair modification set. The selected components are
the chosen rational bases; the conjugate components are their conjugate
inverse transposes. The integral transition matrix and its inverse preserve
the same lattice on both components, so the carried lattice is unchanged.

The output satisfies all five literal IsPtOf fields: uni; lat as Carries
for the full integral adelic span; f at the last coordinate; be at every
auxiliary coordinate; and fr as equality of every frame matrix entry.
The explicit a/s-r calculation in DR3 gives the actual HasRes quotient
value and multiplicative valuation <1. No scalar or normalized-valuation
change is hidden. In rank zero the empty identity matrix works; validity
also forces ST empty, since a nonzero type functional would be impossible.

The source yStd_spec uses an all-twist realizer g to choose y=g*kStd^{-1},
so that y*kStd realizes exactly the required framed decoration. This
requires no additional condition on g beyond the IsPtOf just proved.
Likewise NB6 requests a full realizer of each actual raw neighbor. NB2--NB4
prove those neighbors valid with the same finite prime sets. DR3 applies
to them before AD3 splices the pair of components. Thus the direct-splice
argument and its exact gamma/eta/kappa and inverse-character identities
retain all their hypotheses and remove only the formerly supplied initial
realizer. No reverse-neighbor or rational-genus assumption is introduced.

4. The genus target is adelic, and the dependency remains one-way
===============================================================
I compared the literal definitions. Formalism.L2.Col.UnimodGenus asks
for an actual unitary finite adele x with globLat(x)=N. DR2 constructs
this matrix: finite support and expansion in a fixed F-basis of E give
actual tensor-adelic entries and inverses; its local lattice identity and
AD4's integral-adelic-span recovery give Carries; LP1 recovers the rational
global lattice. This is the target used by the initial point constructor.

UnimodGenusLoc instead asks for SameGenus, whose definition quantifies over
rational E-linear unitary maps of semilocal lattices. The similarly named
PI.UnimodGenus has that stronger rational character too. Neither is needed
for the direct DR2/DR3 route. A source proof's use of a stronger intermediate
does not make it a premise of the endpoint once that endpoint has the
independent construction given here. No rational unitary weak/strong
approximation, class-number-one assertion, or global freeness follows or
is claimed.

The relevant dependencies are claim-specific. DR1 is local CM algebra.
DR2 uses DR1, primitive NB1 local duality, LP1, and the independently proved
adelic-span portion of AD4. DR3 uses DR2 and AD4's exact frame modification.
Only then are FC3 and NB6 supplied, followed by NB7/NB8, FC19 and VE10/VE11.
NB1 does not use NB6 or FC19, and AD4's lattice proof does not use forms
or comparisons. There is no circular use of the integral-span conclusion
or of the desired global forms to construct their initial points.

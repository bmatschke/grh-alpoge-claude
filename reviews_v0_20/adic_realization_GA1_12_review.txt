MATHEMATICAL REVIEW

GA1: Finite generation over Z_l gives a monic integral equation. A norm greater
than one makes its leading term the unique largest term, a contradiction.
Applying this to a unit and its inverse gives unit norm one. Compatibility on
Z_l gives |l|=l^(-1), so l=u*pi^e with e>=1 and |pi|=l^(-1/e). The DVR exponent
of a fraction therefore detects both integral membership and unit membership.
The zero case is consistent with the displayed membership criterion.

GA2: For a denominator b=u_b*pi^a, the stated multiplier gives b times that
multiplier exactly l^a. Thus the coefficient fraction field image is the
Q_l-span of finitely many integral generators, and conversely contains that
span. The coordinate sup norm and induced norm are equivalent: the compact
coordinate unit sphere has positive minimum induced norm by independence.
This proves completeness and closedness without completing Qbar_l or assuming
that the ambient algebraic closure is complete.

GA3: The two norm-ball inclusions compare the actual maximal-ideal-adic
neighborhood bases, so the embedding both preserves and reflects topology.
The unit inverse coordinate is continuous because a homomorphism evaluates it
at the inverse group element. For a coefficient ring extension, the identity
t(pi)^(ea)=t(u)^(-a)t(l)^a proves continuity into the specified adic local ring.
The source's general continuous_ringHom statement is used in precisely this
coefficient setting; no arbitrary topology or unproved locality is substituted.

GA4: Restriction of continuous completion embeddings to the dense number field
is injective. Their finite indexing sets, and absence away from l, are correct:
powers of a residue prime different from l converge locally to zero but have
l-adic norm one. An embedding E->Qbar_l induces a place over l and lands in a
finite extension of Q_l, which is complete by the finite-dimensional argument.
It therefore extends uniquely to that completion. The archimedean counterpart
has one real or two conjugate complex embeddings. These identifications give
the same product over all E embeddings on principal ideles; no continuity of
iota is invoked. Finite products, integer powers and the actual local maps
supply continuity of F_inf and F_l.

GA5: Weak approximation makes the totally positive principal infinite values
dense in the open connected infinite component. IsAlgPos and continuity thus
kill that whole component after dividing by F_inf. A finite-idele congruence
subgroup may be chosen inside an arbitrarily small image neighborhood. It is
indeed a subgroup because both u-1 and u^(-1)-1 are constrained, and these
subgroups form a restricted-product neighborhood basis. The geometric-sum
estimate proves that no nontrivial subgroup lies in |z-1|<1/2. Hence the
remainder has open kernel. Only after this step is the noncontinuous algebraic
isomorphism iota^(-1) applied: the remainder is locally constant, so the
composition remains continuous. This is the essential topology point.

GA6: The formula Psi=iota^(-1)(chi/F_inf)*F_l cancels on principals and kills
the connected infinite component. Its kernel is closed. The stated GlobalArtin
kernel is exactly the closure of their generated subgroup, and the stated
open-quotient property gives a continuous descended character of G_E^ab.
Pullback to G_E is the literal normalized IsLAdicOf identity. Surjectivity of
the same Artin map proves uniqueness. Global class field theory itself remains
an explicit input; no independent Hecke-to-adic existence theorem is retained.

GA7: Evaluation at a purely finite local idele gives the direct local-unit
identity, without an inverse. The correction disappears under the exact
condition v not over l OR n=0. Under that condition, the Artin inertia-image
clause identifies full inertia images, exponents and unramifiedness; its
closure-of-local-image clause gives decomposition triviality in both directions.
The Frobenius clause instead reads a local uniformizer as inverse arithmetic
Frobenius, so the unramified Galois value is the inverse Hecke value. The source
interfaces were checked for this distinction. No unrestricted statement at
l with nonzero algebraic type is asserted.

GA8: The subgroup L^times of Qbar_l^times is closed because L is closed and the
underlying-value map is continuous. Its quotient is a Hausdorff commutative
topological group, exactly the target generality allowed by RC4. At all good
Frobenii the compatible field-of-values square puts the inverse Hecke value
inside L; RC4 therefore kills the quotient character. Compactness of G_E and
powers/inverses force every remaining value to have norm one. GA1 gives its
unique preimage in the original O^times and GA3 restores continuity in the
original topology. This proves descent into the specified coefficient ring;
it neither enlarges that ring nor replaces the character by a finite-order one.

GA9: Finite order kills the connected infinite component because each element
there has an m-th root. The normalized type-zero formula therefore gives the
same finite-order adic character at every finite place, including l. A finite
root set has an isolated identity, so continuity gives an open kernel.
Conversely a continuous finite-order Galois character descends through the
topological abelianization, composes with Artin and transfers through iota;
its open kernel makes that transfer continuous. Principal triviality and
uniqueness follow from the same Artin map. Both directions use the displayed
normalization and not only a correspondence of abstract finite groups.

GA10: The supplied extension of CM conjugation acts continuously on G_E by
conjugation, as checked on finite field bases. At a split unramified place,
restriction of the Hecke character to F gives chi(P)chi(Pbar)=1. At an inert
fixed place, the actual unramified valuation equality makes the F-uniformizer
have E-valuation one and gives chi(P)=1 itself. Conjugating a valuation carries
its Frobenius to the conjugate place with the same residue norm. The product
character is therefore trivial on cofinally all Frobenii and RC4 gives AntiHom
for the supplied conjugation. Arbitrary ramified quadratic extensions are not
silently included.

GA11: The actual CM type and its conjugate exhaust all embeddings once.
(z/|z|)^(2a)=z^a*bar(z)^(-a) gives type (-e,e) for the HF mu0 argument exponent
-2e. For r=2h+1, eta^(-1)|.|^(-r/2) has complex local value
z^(-h-1)*bar(z)^(-h). The norm includes both complex embeddings; this gives
integral exponents rather than half-integral ones. The source rank r=k+m-1
is odd from even k,m, and the separately odd t has the same calculation.
The chain length is not conflated with a local polynomial degree.

GA12: The exact normalization eta(P)N(P)^(-1/2)=epsilon(z) gives
mu(P)eta(P)^(-1)N(P)^(r/2)=epsilon(y z^(-1)N(P)^h) for r=2h+1.
The last factor is in the prime field even when h is negative, since the norm
is a nonzero integer. Thus the same specified coefficient field suffices;
there is no infinite adjunction of square roots. Finite exceptional sets are
intersected for products/inverses. On local units the norm factor and an
unramified eta both equal one, giving the literal block_loc formula.

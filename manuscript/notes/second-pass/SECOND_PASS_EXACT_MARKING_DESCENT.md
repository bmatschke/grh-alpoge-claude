# Choosing the common marking with exact integral descent

On each supplied exported origin the variable conductor primes can be chosen so that passage to the common ray marking has **zero finite determinant cost** on the pullback of its fixed lower marked rows. This includes the primes which ramify in the new ray field. The additional exclusions depend only on the finite list of lower geometric models on this origin. The cusp weights, sections, later side coefficient fields and upper marking need not enter them.

Use the original lower level $M_0=g_0g_1$ and its prescribed half-marking. The pinned Hermitian form, lattice and signatures give the lower moduli rows in ranks $1\le j\le m$. The unused indices repeat the first row. One may include in the same list the finite choices of lower models with one of the already fixed distinguished markings at $p_\delta,p'_\delta$, provided that level is used before the variable marking. These fields, Hermitian data and fixed markings are known on the exported origin. Their row models are characterized up to the marked moduli isomorphism, and may subsequently be extended to a side field. ## A model-dependent exclusion before choosing any conductor

For each lower row take the finite model with auxiliary full symplectic level three used in the [good-frame denominator argument](../geometry/PEL_GOOD_REDUCTION_DENOMINATOR.md). It is the normalization of the closure of the image in the fine Siegel scheme, component by component. Its generic marked fibre is smooth. It carries the abelian scheme, the action of the CM order, the canonical rigidified theta line and its rank-one module of sections compatible with base change. Remove the prime 3, the primes of the lower level, and the primes needed to make the CM spectral idempotents integral. After removing finitely many more rational primes the entire finite-type model is smooth over the integers of its coefficient field. Indeed the complement of its smooth locus has constructible image which misses the generic point of the number ring. Shrink likewise to spread the generic marking, signature and ranks. The auxiliary cyclotomic base for level three is étale away from 3, so its finite set of components can be used over the original base.

The bundle of integral ordered tangent eigenbases and primitive theta generators on that model is smooth there. It is a torsor for a product of general linear groups and a multiplicative group. Call it $\mathscr P$ and denote by $S$ the union of the excluded rational primes over the finite list. Every good integral frame at $p\notin S$ is represented here after a finite unramified extension to furnish the auxiliary level. Indeed its principally polarized abelian scheme gives the Siegel point, its generic marking supplies the lift to the normalization, and finiteness extends the lift on the trait. The integral eigenbases and theta generator lift on the indicated torsor. Conversely, a trait on $\mathscr P$ supplies an original good integral frame after forgetting level three. These statements persist under coefficient extension. [Actual generic moduli and frames](../geometry/PEL_MODULI_COMPARISON.md) · [Integral tangent and line frames][cm7-test]

Fix $p\notin S$, a coefficient DVR $R$ completed at a place over $p$ with fraction field $K$, and a finite-dimensional $K$-space $C$ of the indicated scalar cusp readings on this lower row. Take the original algebraic bounded cusp spaces with the nonnegative tangent and theta weights for which good frames detect all integral frames. For a vector-valued evaluation apply the test to its coordinates in the frame. The pullback identifies $C$ with functions on the generic frame scheme. With $\mathscr P_R$ the base change to $R$, the lower integral evaluation lattice is
$$
 M_R(C)= C\cap\Gamma(\mathscr P_R,\mathcal O)
 \quad\text{inside }\Gamma(\mathscr P_K,\mathcal O).                 \tag{1}
$$
Indeed the section is regular on the generic frame scheme. A pole in a normal smooth integral frame chart must therefore occur along a component of the special fibre. There each vertical valuation gives the uniformizer order one. In a nonempty open of that component the reading is a negative power of the uniformizer times a unit. Take a closed point in that open and lift it by smoothness over the unramified extension with its residue field. Its evaluation detects the pole. Thus integrality on good frames implies regularity on the model. Conversely a regular reading is integral on every good frame by the valuative lifting above, and hence on all original integral frames by the [good-frame comparison](../geometry/PEL_GOOD_FRAMES_AT_ALL_PRIMES.md). For completed coefficients, approximate the finite coefficient vector by vectors over the number field. After clearing one denominator for a basis by the [all-frame bound](../geometry/PEL_ALL_FRAME_DENOMINATORS.md), the difference is arbitrarily small on every integral test simultaneously. This transfers the good-frame implication to completed coefficients.

Now let $R'/R$ be any finite extension of complete coefficient DVRs, with fraction field $K'$. Formula (1) gives
$$
 M_{R'}(K'\otimes_K C)=R'\otimes_R M_R(C).                         \tag{2}
$$
For the proof take the kernel of the map $C\to\Gamma(\mathscr P_K,\mathcal O)/\Gamma(\mathscr P_R,\mathcal O)$. Flatness preserves this kernel and quotient. Global functions on the quasi-compact separated frame scheme commute with flat base change, as do functions on its generic fibre. The resulting kernel is exactly (1) over $R'$, where the same smoothness and frame tests apply. Equivalently a primitive integral section has a unit reading over an unramified trait: if the reduction vanished as a function, division by the uniformizer would still be regular. The fibre is reduced, so a nonzero reduction has a nonzero reading at a closed point that lifts by smoothness. [Flat base change, Stacks Project, Lemma 30.5.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02KH)

## A finite matrix for preassigned cusp spaces

For a fixed finite list of lower cusp spaces there is also a proof by evaluation matrices. For a space of dimension $h$ over $F$, choose an $F$-basis $f_1,\ldots,f_h$ and $h$ closed algebraic frames $y_1,\ldots,y_h$ on the generic abelian interior such that $\det(f_i(y_j))\ne0$ in a common number field. Indeed the independent regular functions remain independent over $\bar F$ by flat base change. The geometric frames separate them, and choosing a point where a nonzero member of the common kernel does not vanish decreases its dimension. Spread the finitely many abelian varieties, markings, eigenbases and primitive line generators over their rings of integers away from a finite set. Add that set, the primes of the determinant and its inverse, and the primes in one all-frame denominator for the basis to the forbidden fibres before selecting the variable conductors. At every other prime all basis readings are integral on all traits and the sample matrix is invertible over the valuation ring, also after arbitrary coefficient extension. Choose one common prolongation containing the sampling fields and lift the markings on these frames after finite extension. Reading there and inverting the matrix shows that an integral linear combination has all its coefficients integral. This proves the base-change equality for every space in the preselected list. The smooth-model proof makes the exclusion independent even of that list of spaces and their weights. [Algebraic sampling and finite denominators](../geometry/PEL_ALL_FRAME_DENOMINATORS.md#finite-algebraic-frames-for-the-whole-arithmetic-lattice)

## Exact descent on the chosen ray cover

At each active index enlarge the export's forbidden set by the full fibres of the host over $S$, along with the previous exclusions and the primes selected at earlier indices. The export quantifies over every finite forbidden set on the supplied origin with the already chosen bases. The resulting squarefree joint conductor $N$ is coprime to $S$ and meets the existing caller requests, the rational-disjointness requirement and the goodness bounds at all coprime powers. This works when the active set is empty as well. The lower geometry used in defining $S$ predates this choice. No construction on the new conductor enters the exclusions. [Original order of selection][cm7-seed] · [Joint finite supports][cm7-n] · [Combining the caller's requests](../../../archive/rounds/v78/continuation_v78/producer.tex#L241-L268)

Let $B$ be any permitted side number field containing the fixed ray field $H_{m_*}$ and the coefficients of the chosen lower row. Let $L=BH_{m_*N}$. In a rationally identified space of pullbacks $L\otimes_B C_B$ write $M_B$ for the original lower evaluation lattice and $M^{\rm pb}_L$ for the intersection with the upper marked integral lattice. At $p\mid N$, lift each lower integral frame with new coefficients to an upper frame over a finite extension by furnishing the new marking on the generic fibre. Its normalized evaluation orders agree. Conversely forgetting that marking preserves the lower test. This identifies $M^{\rm pb}_{L,w}$ with the left side of (2) at every $w\mid p$. For $p\nmid N$ the ray extension $L/B$ is unramified everywhere above $p$. The upper pullback lattice is Galois stable and contracts to the original one by the same frame tests. Unramified integral descent therefore yields (2) there as well. Consequently
$$
 M^{\rm pb}_L=\mathcal O_L\otimes_{\mathcal O_B} M_B,\qquad
 \log\#\bigl(M^{\rm pb}_L/(\mathcal O_L M_B)\bigr)=0.              \tag{3}
$$
In the [tame filtration](SECOND_PASS_RAMIFIED_MARKING_LATTICE.md), all the residue terms $W_a/\pi_U T_U$, $1\le a<t$, now vanish for these chosen conductors. Root-of-unity component multipliers preserve the statement by their unit values. [Lift of a prescribed marked frame][cm7-lift] · [Original evaluation with its component factor][cm7-eval] · [Relative ray ramification](SECOND_PASS_RAY_DIFFERENT.md)

Equation (3) concerns the invariant pullback span on these lower geometries. It gives no estimate on the magnitude of the allowed conductor or on the degree of its ray field. A genuinely new geometric row first determined by that conductor would require its own model comparison. The uniform existence of the exported origins, their localized Galois and theta readings, and the height estimates on new sections retain their stated hypotheses.

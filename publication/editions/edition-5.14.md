Eight new claims, ETS7–14, prove pointwise finiteness of the strict Selmer groups and calculate the entire generic character-orbit sum using the primitive part of a finite number-ring K-group. They identify the least integer satisfying the simultaneous valuation inequalities of the fixed-field reduction. Four zero tests for the trivial character, a reflection test for general characters and a large-prime estimate for bounded twist and character degree give cases of the height bound.

The human proof still needs a uniform logarithmic size bound outside these cases, or a corresponding estimate on the selected windows. The ordinary automorphic support, integral model and indefinite counting questions are recorded in the coverage audit.

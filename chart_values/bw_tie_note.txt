Conclusion
==========
The actual bw_tie theorem is a short consequence of CV2 and CV6--CV8,
together with the following residue-kernel localization argument and
the source's seen-place embedding identity. It does not need a new
metric, period or supported-move construction. It retains precisely
w with residue characteristic T and seenP(w)=true.

This is a supplement, not a new CV claim. FC may incorporate it under

1. The single-prime kernel lattice
=================================
For a valid decorated representative d, write L=d.L, H=d.Lsh=L#.
Fix P in its type-prime set ST. By the residue description,

 K_P=d.LshKer(P)={x in H : f_P(x)=0}.

This is an O_E-submodule because f_P is additive and O_E-linear
through reduction modulo P on the relevant local lattice. The
separating element supplied by Primes.Ok ensures that H lies in the
local ambient lattice where f_P is defined. Since L is contained
in K_P and K_P is contained in H, it is a full lattice.

At P one has

 (K_P)_P=(L+P H)_P.

Indeed, every x in K_P belongs to H, lies in the local ambient
lattice, and satisfies f_P(x)=0; the local membership characterization
of L therefore puts x in L_P. Conversely L is contained in K_P,
and P H is contained in K_P because reduction modulo P kills its
scalar. Localize these inclusions.

At Q in ST with Q different from P one has

 (K_P)_Q=H_Q.

Choose a in P outside Q. For x in H, a x lies in K_P because its
P-residue vanishes, and a is a Q-unit. The reverse inclusion follows
from K_P subset H. No global equality K_P=L+P H is needed.

The underlying local membership characterization L_P={x in N_P:
f_P(x)=0} is elementary: after clearing an N_P denominator, multiply
by a product of elements in each other type prime Q but outside P.
This preserves invertibility at P and annihilates all other residue
conditions. The global definition of L then gives the claim.

2. The exact coefficient block affected
======================================
Let w be a coefficient prime with resChar(w)=T and seenP(w)=true.
The actual e=tauAt:ST~Tau has

 j_(e(Q))^(-1)(w)=Q

for every Q in ST. Source tauAt first chooses embeddings over Q or
its CM conjugate. Equality of cardinalities and the separation of
the selected type primes from their conjugates make this a bijection.
The seen condition says the distinguished pi_T maps into w at every
embedding. Since pi_T is outside every conjugate selected prime,
the conjugate alternative is impossible. Thus the displayed
contraction identity has the selected orientation, not merely an
unoriented pair of conjugate primes.

For a T-local rational basis B of H and B' of L+P H, define

 B_tau = B' if tau=e(P), and B_tau=B otherwise.

CV6 (IsLocBasis implies IsBasisAt at each prime above T), together
with the two kernel localization identities above, proves that
B_tau is a basis of (K_P)_(j_tau^(-1)(w)) for every tau. The same
single matrices B and B' are used at all primes over T.

3. The exact polynomial criterion
=================================
Apply the general local delAtr criterion of CV7 to K_P and these
block-dependent bases. For a rational coefficient vector F, its
membership in delAtr(K_P)_w is equivalent to the existence of
z in Delta_std,w whose complex frame value is computed with

 A_tau=j_tau(B')^(-1) if tau=e(P),
 A_tau=j_tau(B)^(-1) otherwise.

The actual evaluation includes the determinant shift of lambda.
CV2 identifies sigma(z) with IntSpan, without replacing Delta by
Nabla or discarding the determinant twist. Evaluation injectivity
gives both implications, as in CV7.

For the actual chain, Bw is the localization of the lattice of
fixed coefficient tuples whose c-component belongs to delAtr(K_P,c).
The class set is finite, so CV8 clears the finitely many component
denominators with one product outside w. Thus Bw membership is
equivalent to this criterion for every c.

4. Match the actual interface and its universal bases
=====================================================
The source coordinate identification supplies gramN=I and
latCrd(c)=L_c. Consequently LdG=H_c and
LkG(PvG(res,P),latCrd(c))=L_c+P H_c; the residue quotient kernel is
literally P. The coefficient embedding equality and
lambda=mu-i(t+1)/2 are the same as CV9, with the oddness identity
i(t+1)/2=i((t+1)/2). IdY preserves the matrix entries of every frame.
These facts identify the preceding criterion exactly with BwTieG.

The forward implication works for every supplied pair B,B'.
For the reverse implication choose actual rational T-local bases
for H_c and L_c+P H_c, using their full-lattice property and the
semilocal basis construction already proved in CS3/CV6. This
prevents the universal basis quantifier from becoming vacuous.

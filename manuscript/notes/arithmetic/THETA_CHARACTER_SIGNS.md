# One additive character for the theta kernel and its marked primes

The theta kernel uses an additive character of the adeles of the totally real field $F$. Its value on the real coordinates fixes the choice at *every* finite prime. In the present construction a marked prime has local field $\mathbb Q_p$. The comparison of the finite level character with the automorphic character then boils down to evaluating the latter on $b/p$ for an ordinary integer $b$. This determines the sign for the original level action and for every allowed choice of lift of a residue class. ## The real and finite normalizations

On $\mathbb Q_p$ let $\{y\}_p$ denote the class in $\mathbb Z[1/p]/\mathbb Z$ of the principal part of $y$ modulo $\mathbb Z_p$. On $\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}$ use
$$
 \psi_{\mathbb Q}(y)=\exp(-2\pi i y_\infty)
             \prod_p \exp(2\pi i\{y_p\}_p).
$$
The product has finitely many nonidentity factors. On a diagonal $y\in\mathbb Q$, the number $y-\sum_p\{y\}_p$, using arbitrary rational representatives, is an integer: at each prime its denominator has been removed. Thus $\psi_{\mathbb Q}$ descends to $\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q$. Its pullback by $\operatorname{Tr}_{F/\mathbb Q}$ gives a continuous unitary character $\psi$ of $\mathbb A_F/F$. It has real component $\exp(-2\pi i\sum_\tau x_\tau)$, hence is nontrivial. Each extension to complex coordinates above a real embedding restricts to that same real evaluation; the sum is the trace for every admissible choice of coordinates. This is precisely the source normalization with sign $-1$. The finite component is trivial on each local integer ring since the trace of an integral element is integral. The standard reference uses the same signs. [Getz and Hahn, Appendix B.1](https://sites.duke.edu/jgetz/files/2022/04/Graduate_Text.pdf) · [Archimedean normalization in the source](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/ExistsCharOfTrace.lean#L135-L146)

This real normalization determines $\psi$ uniquely. The ratio of two such characters is trivial at infinity and on diagonal $F$. Its finite part is therefore trivial on $F$, which is dense in the finite adeles by additive strong approximation. Continuity proves the claim. In particular, when a change of coefficient field retains the host field and its real trace, the two resulting characters agree on the identified adeles. ## A marked split prime

Let $E/F$ be the CM extension, $P$ a marked prime of $E$ over $v\mid p$, and $p=g_k$ one of the two type primes. The splitting hypotheses give $E_P=F_v=\mathbb Q_p$ and $k_P=\mathbb F_p$. The host supplies $\xi_0$ with $t=\xi_0^{-1}\in\mathcal O_E$. Its reduction at the marked prime is meaningful: if $t\in P$, there is no expression for $\xi_0$ with denominator prime to $P$. In that case the source's residue convention sets $\bar\xi_0=0$, and the prescribed finite character would be trivial. Its stipulated nontriviality rules this out. Thus $t$ is a unit at $P$ and $\bar\xi_0=\bar t^{-1}$.

For $c\in k_P$, take any integral lift $L$ and choose an integer $b\equiv\bar\xi_0 c\pmod P$. Then $(\xi_0 L-b)/p\in\mathcal O_{E_P}$. Since $F_v=\mathbb Q_p$, the trace character at $v$ gives
$$
             \psi_v(\xi_0 L/p)=\psi_v(b/p)=\exp(2\pi i b/p).
$$
In the source this is also the value obtained by multiplying by the idempotent that picks the $P$ factor of $E\otimes_F F_v\simeq F_v\times F_v$ before taking the relative trace. This explains why no factor of two occurs. The prescribed residue-field character, read through the chosen coefficient embedding, has precisely the value on the right. Integrality of the difference proves independence of $L$. For the level subgroup in the source, take $c$ to be the sum of the entries just above the diagonal of its residue matrix at $P$. The level character is the product over marked primes of their additive characters evaluated on these sums. Both ways to identify $k_P$ with $\mathbb F_p$ coincide, since a prime field has no nonidentity automorphism. The sums commute with transport of the matrix entries. Multiplication of the local identities therefore gives the required equality of the two characters on **every** element of the prescribed subgroup, with every marked idempotent permitted in the definition. This proves the marked-sign comparison given the original residue and group identifications. It is used together with the finite Hecke reading and the archimedean models in the fixed-stage theta construction.

2. The remaining producer is still a conjunction on the same ideal

COUNT_II_SUPPORT remains open C. Its eight retained producer entries are:

JSP_ORIGINAL_ORDINARY_DATA.
For each original rank-four Link and every allowed B,Lambda, supply the
actual R:UnitaryHeckeData, H=hset(Qempty,R.tau0), its Ax fields apart from
CIP's supplied art_spec, the ordinary module M and commuting Hecke
operators, the finite/free and control statements used by JSP, and the
actual Hecke algebra T with its coefficient/residual structure. Supply
the continuous full determinant and ordered characters satisfying literal
IsUniv, together with residual, torsion and inertia restrictions. The

weights and carry the exact flags, polarization, tame type and automorphic
properties. The auxiliary I0 unramifiedness used by RingProps remains

Out.sep tests every t of this T by the good maps from T, while Out.pt gives
representations, determinant equality and flags on those same fibers.

the good maps, their separation, control or their automorphic realization.

3. Recommended next complete bounded endpoint: the actual ArPl supplier

The next recommended endpoint is the literal ArPl in
P/TaylorWilesObstructionBound.lean:119–125, with its original CohP input.
The source assembly is P/AutomorphicDetection/ArPl.lean:13–179. This is
currently an open reconstruction task, not a newly closed B theorem.

It is useful in two ways: it supplies the PhiCond needed before Choice,
and it realizes whatever finite elements Choice later returns. It is
independent of constructing an ordinary Hecke algebra, yet it removes
one entire original support input once proved. Its concrete finite-group,
cohomological and prime-selection ingredients are visible, so it is a
better bounded endpoint than attempting all of Raw or an unqualified
rank-four Hida package at once.

The precise goal is, for every original arithmetic datum and CohP:

  PhiCond(V0.frame.Gamma,ell), and
  for every q and sg:Fin(q)→V0.frame.G0 with sg(j) in G-prime,
  there exist T:TWGrp(globOf r V0), an actual TWPl r T,
  and an equality T.q=q identifying every T.sg with this same sg.

The output has all levels N simultaneously. Its prime choices may depend
on the finite list, but no new coefficient threshold or ideal is selected.
A human reconstruction should perform the following original-data steps.

(a) Derive global and local PhiCond from CohP.gf and CohP.lf. The source
    proves the finite-H1-to-PhiCond implication using finite permutation
    modules and explicit cocycles. Keep every open subgroup in PhiCond,
    and check the finite ramification set contains all places above ell.
    This step must not assume topological finite generation in place of
    the source's actual finiteness predicate.

(b) Construct the open normal H with the exact membership equivalence:
    eta(g)=1, every residual character chib_i(g)=1, and the residual
    cyclotomic value equals 1. Its conjugation/polarization calculation
    belongs to hEx. It is not an arbitrarily convenient smaller H.

(c) Build the normal Frattini sequence W_N inside H. Its cofinality is
    relative to open normal V CONTAINING PL(ell,H): eventually W_N≤V.
    Do not replace this by convergence to the identity. The finite
    quotient descent and the ell-group power argument are the real proof.

(d) Realize finite prescribed cosets by split CM primes, successively
    avoiding the original finite set and the previously selected primes.
    ChebOne uses the exact ChebotarevDegreeOne statement and a finite set
    containing divisors of conjugate(a)-a for a nonreal integral a. This
    ensures the actual decomposition subgroup over L+ fixes L. A final
    human proof must identify a primary Chebotarev theorem, check its
    degree-one and conjugacy/coset reduction, and verify this splitting
    dictionary; a source invocation alone is insufficient.

(e) Lift sg(j) to g_j in the original absolute Galois group. Apply the
    prime step to the open subgroup
      rho^(-1)(W_N) intersect ker(cycMod(ell,N))
    and target g_j^(-1). The actual Frobenius sigma_Nj then satisfies
    sigma_Nj*g_j in that subgroup. Define the stored fr as sigma_Nj^(-1).
    This is exactly why TWPl.fr_frob tests fr^(-1). Check, on these same
    choices, norm(w_Nj)=1 mod ell^N and the stated ph approximation.
    No arithmetic/geometric Frobenius sign may be changed silently.

(f) Construct the original enlarged level V_N with
    V_N.S'=s.S union range(v_N), its comparison to V0 and the actual tame
    generators. Prove ta_ker, the tame relation modulo PL, and ker_le
    using the source's NORMAL closure followed by topological closure.
    Keep the same H in this kernel computation and in the realization.

(g) Prove the uniform finite-character count
      hb = #HomL(ell,H) * ell^(q*[Gamma:H]).
    This single bound is independent of N. Supply the local PhiCond
    fields at every original v and u. Assemble every TWGrp and TWPl
    field, including injectivity of the chosen base primes, H_eq and
    the exact q/sg identification.

A successful reconstruction of these steps would close JSP_ACTUAL_ARPL
conditional on the unchanged CohP, without claiming Choice, B1, B2, Raw
or HasAut. If one of these steps cannot be written, retain that precise
supplier instead of marking the entire ArPl endpoint complete.

4. A smaller ordinary-data subtarget made accessible by CIP

For a supplied original integral type tau0, TThetaEx and TResEx offer a
second bounded target: construct the actual theta with IsTheta AND IsWt,
and the actual residual Hecke values a with IsRes. These are genuine
fields of R, not a new model of a weight space.

TThetaEx uses Lambda.inert, factorization of its ordered characters through
actual local Artin maps, and the product diagonal torus. The defining
relation includes psi_i times epsV^i times theta(eV(...))=1. Continuity
must hold in the adic sense required by IsWt, and its Free field still
needs the original neat-level argument. CIP supplies the exact artOf
validity needed for this construction. TResEx lifts the coefficients of
the residual Frobenius Hecke polynomial and the residual ordered products
at the prescribed Artin uniformizer. It must prove independence of all
allowed Frobenius/Artin preimages, not choose coefficients for only one
preimage and stop.

The source HdEx then combines those two constructions with TTypeEx.
This subtarget does NOT supply the integral type itself, H.Ax.fin, the
ordinary/control theorem, the continuous universal determinant, separating
good fibers, or their automorphic realization. The integral-type theorem
and its nonzero/local-Langlands conditions are substantial retained
inputs, including at rank four and ell=3. This option should be preferred
only if the immediate goal is a complete R subpacket; it does not by
itself remove a whole top-level Count-II producer leaf.

For the subsequent ordinary work the direct chain to inspect is:
  actual H.Ax and IsWt → ordFree/hiComm/hiAlg;
  actual type and automorphicHeckeSet → TDenseU/TCoreSep;
  TCoreDet (full IsUniv) and OrdCoreH (full Out);
  then reuse JSP's same-kernel IsJ/RingProps/Dense construction.
The actual type, level, characters, operators, fibers and ideal must stay
identified throughout. A rank-two ordinary-control citation has not been
shown to supply this rank-four chain.

5. Quantifier order and contribution to the complete proof

Fix the original D,T with Fit and Deep, and FIX iota first. Choose one
coefficient threshold c1 that works for both QX4 Links. For every later
c' containing c and c1, retain those two original Links with common kappa
and the literal nondivisibility of their distinct auxiliary primes. The
threshold precedes every later Space X and every arbitrary actual
universal B,Lambda to which the joint producer is applied.

For each Link and allowed B,Lambda select ONE J carrying all six Hida
fields and P1core simultaneously. The two Links have different universal
rings, so no literal equality between their two ideals is claimed. Within
each ring J is fixed before each NiceI point, detector, TWGrp/TWPl and
all level indices. Allowed pointwise finite coefficient extensions do not
reselect J or alter the earlier external threshold. Keep ell=3.

QX10 already turns the joint two-Link supplier into the original PMExAlt
ideal on every Space; YX2 already turns PMExAlt into CountII. The new
ArPl target would remove one support input in that chain; it would not
supply its remaining automorphic/patching antecedents.

CountII contributes directly to RH as well as unrestricted Dirichlet GRH.
KA5–KA6 make only Case III vacuous for two-primary target characters.
Cases I, II and V remain substantive on the RH route. For unrestricted
GRH the separate original Case-III/Ctx3/SeedIV chain also remains. A shared
rank-four PMEx supplier is not a substitute for its rank-three hypotheses.

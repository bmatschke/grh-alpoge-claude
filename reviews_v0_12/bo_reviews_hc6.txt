1. Boundary cases and hard-case parameters.
   For n=0 the empty set satisfies the totalized source statement. For
   n>0 with Q=n*exp(-32*sqrt(log n*log L))/L<=1, {0} satisfies the strict
   location condition 4L*0<n and the vacuous NoRel condition.
   Otherwise, writing x=log n, y=log L>1/2 and s=sqrt(xy), Q>1 implies
   x>32s+y. Hence s/y>32, S=floor(s/y)>=32 and s>16. The bounds
   Sy<=s<(S+1)y, a=(x-y-3)/S<=2s, and a>=y+7 are valid. In particular
   a>=y+7 follows by S<=R=s/y and
   a>=Ry-y/R-3/R>y+7 for R>32,y>1/2.
   Consequently exp(a)>=8L, d=floor(exp(a)/(4L)) satisfies
   exp(a)/(8L)<=d<=exp(a)/(4L), and d>=1. The shortened budget is sound:
     3+S(7+y)+S+2a <= 3+21s < 32s.

2. Sphere pigeonhole and the signed base-expansion argument.
   There are at most S(d-1)^2+1<=Sd^2 norm-square values in the box
   {0,...,d-1}^S. Thus one sphere has at least d^S/(Sd^2) points.
   The base B=4Ld encoding is injective and lies below B^S. Because
   B^S<=exp(aS)=n/(L exp(3)) and exp(3)>4, every encoded value obeys
   the required strict 4L*b(v)<n.

   For nonzero signed integers i,j with |i|,|j|<=L and i!=j, every
   coefficient of j(b(v2)-b(v1))-i(b(v3)-b(v1)) has absolute value
   below 2Ld<B. Reducing modulo B and dividing repeatedly proves
   coefficientwise equality; there is no hidden positivity assumption
   on i or j and no carrying ambiguity. Three distinct encoded points
   would therefore give three distinct collinear vectors on one sphere.
   Equivalently, equal norms yield
     i(i-j)*sum_m(v3_m-v1_m)^2=0,
   which forces v3=v1 and then v2=v1. This is precisely literal NoRel.

3. The explicit constant 32.
   The inequality log(8L)<=7+y, together with log S<=S and the budget
   above, gives
     exp(3)*(8L)^S*S*exp(2a)<=exp(32s).
   Combining d^S>=exp(aS)/(8L)^S, d^2<=exp(2a) and
   n=exp(aS)*L*exp(3) proves exactly
     |T|>=n*exp(-32*sqrt(log n*log L))/L.
   No additional density or analytic theorem is imported here.

4. Uniform large-x specialization and deletion of zero.
   With n=o*n0, o<=Co, x^h/2<=n<=Co*x^h and h=2+8epsilon, the report
   correctly obtains x^h/(2Co)<=n0<=Co*x^h. The literal expression
   Lwv=10*ceil(405 log x)+2*ceil(30/epsilon)+22 yields
     2<=Lwv<=(4084+60/epsilon)*log x
   for log x>=1. Thus log E=O(sqrt(log x*log log x))=o(log x),
   E=exp(32*sqrt(log n0*log Lwv)), with constants fixed before x and
   all Frames. This gives 4Co*Lwv*E<=x^epsilon eventually.

   One clarification was requested and applied: that upper asymptotic
   estimate alone does not explain the separate lower inequalities.
   The report now explicitly derives E>=2 from
   log n0>=h log x-log(2Co), log Lwv>=log 2, and derives
   x^(-epsilon)*n>=1 from n>=x^h/2 and h-epsilon>0.

   Put Q0=n0/(Lwv*E). The three estimates give
     Q0>=4*x^(-epsilon)*n>=4.
   Erasing zero loses at most one member, so the remaining size is
   at least Q0-1>=Q0/2, hence at least Q0/E and Q0/4. These are
   respectively the source lower bound with constant 64 and the
   required lower bound x^(-epsilon)*n. The strict location and NoRel
   conditions pass to this subset.

5. Final cardinality cap and scope.
   The remaining set is nonempty because its target size is at least
   one, and consists of positive natural numbers. Its cardinality is
   at most its largest member m. Since 4Lwv*m<n0, this implies exactly
     4Lwv*|T2|<=n0.
   This final step would not follow in this form for a set containing
   zero, so the erasure is mathematically accounted for, rather than
   merely cosmetic. CT2=64 is uniform and source compatible.

   T2 is a set of natural-number shifts with NoRel, distinct from the
   Bernoulli-selected TT subset of the Frame group. Neither TT's biased
   coset shares nor any probabilistic independence is used to assert
   T2's properties. The reviewed text preserves this distinction.

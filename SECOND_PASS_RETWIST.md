# Choosing the retwisted column over the original prehost

Fix the context in the common eventual range and a fitted, built untwisted origin with its [all-powers export](FIRST_PASS_COMMON_PRIMES.md). Write $B$ for its base, $L$ for the embedded full coefficient field, $K_s,L_0$ for the two specified embedded subfields, $f$ for the character frame, and $\mathcal P$ for its finite set of allowed rational primes. Let $A(p)$ be any finite set of rational integers for each residue characteristic $p$. Let $S_2(P,g,\Xi)$ be any finite set of algebraic complex numbers, allowed to depend on the prehost $P$, its frame $g$, and the **final** character set $\Xi$. There is an enlarged base $B'$, a coherent twist on it, and a pinned column on that twist with the following properties.

* The prehost, frame, embedded $K_s,L_0$, preliminary column parameters, selected host places, ambient array $J$ and column parameter $\delta$ agree with those of the original column. The full coefficient field $L'$ contains $L$ and the requested $S_2$ evaluated on the new column's character set.
* The rational allowance remains $\mathcal P$. Each local twist of residue characteristic $p$ avoids $A(p)$. At an active place its conductor and exponent are the ones selected on the old data, with pairwise disjoint conductor sets for different selected indices. Off the inverse image of the old active set the twist is trivial. Every root of unity of the requested local order is in $L'$.
* On the standing [fine-data extension](THETA_FINE_EXTENSION.md) and group and volume hypotheses, this same pinned column has an enlarged fine datum extending the prescribed old one. Its active set is exactly the inverse image of the original set. These are the starting data for the strengthened third intermediate level in the second pass. ## Choose the characters before the coefficient field

First recover from the built witness its preliminary state, local level, and stored comparison connecting that level to the exported row. They have the same coefficient places. The comparison transports the full-rank tests, their basis property, common denominator exponents and the all-powers assertion to that particular state. Its active places form a finite conjugation-stable set $W$. They lie over $\mathcal P$ and are a union of whole fibres over $L_0$ by [saturation on the untwisted pass](UNTWISTED_AUXILIARY_PRIMES.md). The construction chooses this old state **before** the avoidance family is supplied. [Extraction from the built witness][seed0] · [Transfer of its tests][seed1]

For each conjugate pair of restrictions of active places to $L_0$, take one representative and its stored reading index $i$. Either side gives an allowed reading for this index: the coefficient subfield $L_0$ is Galois, and conjugation interchanges the two permitted orientations. Thus the assignment is constant over the whole pair of fibres. For every $i$ first exclude the fixed design places and every host place above a rational prime in the host's excluded set or in $A(\ell_i)$. Additional finite avoidance conditions on the output can be joined here. Enumerate the finitely many distinct selected indices. At each one also exclude every place in the conductor sets already chosen. Apply export with the very same bases and tolerance $\ell_i^{-2}$. It gives a triple $(Q_i,k_i,\psi_i)$ satisfying the toric, Selmer, tame and row inequalities for every power prime to $\ell_i$. A stronger avoidance only narrows the permitted support, so the earlier choices and their estimates survive. This gives pairwise disjoint $Q_i$ as sets of host places. The sets $Q_i$ are conductors of characters on $E$. The set $\mathcal P$ indexes the permitted residue characteristics of *coefficient* places. [Representatives and disjoint choices][seed2] · [Rational exclusions on host places][avoid]

Choose once for each $i$ an identification of the $\ell_i^{k_i}$-th roots in its local coefficient ring with those in $\mathbb C$. The finite-image character $\psi_i$ composed with the ray quotient gives a continuous character of $G_E$ with open kernel. Global Artin reciprocity gives the corresponding finite Hecke character $\chi_i$. In the proof's convention, for $v\notin Q_i$ its value $\chi_i(v)$ is the inverse of the chosen complex reading of $\psi_i(\mathrm{Frob}_v)$ at arithmetic Frobenius. Conjugation acts by inversion, its order divides $\ell_i^{k_i}$, its ramified places are exactly $Q_i$, and it is trivial on decomposition groups above $\ell_i$, by the exported local tests. Give each $v\in W$ the triple $(Q_i,k_i,\chi_i)$ corresponding to its chosen index, and every other place $(\varnothing,0,1)$. This defines twist data $T_1$ on the old base. The two distinguished frame characters still enter the full set of tuple characters. [Reciprocity and the two Frobenius readings][rec] · [The constructed twist on $W$][on] · [Artin reciprocity, Milne, *Class Field Theory*, V](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/cft.html)

## Enlarge once and retain the pin

For pinning, the original column gives a finite algebraic request $S_{\rm pin}(P,g,\Xi)$ determined before the enlargement. One can take its additional avoidance set to be empty. Its elements supply generators of the frame-value and auxiliary fields, the required square roots of the marked primes, and generators for the tuple characters and their step coefficients for $J$. The set of tuple characters of an actual twist is finite. The request gives finite algebraic output even on arbitrary input sets. For the finite set arising here, the proof shows that the generated field contains every required step coefficient. [Generators and their use][pins] · [Extension of this pin][pinext]

Set $\Xi=T_1.\mathrm{set}(f)$. Adjoin to the full field the finite set $S_2(P,f,\Xi)\cup S_{\rm pin}(P,f,\Xi)$ together with all $\ell_i^{k_i}$-th roots needed on the finitely many coefficient places above $\mathcal P$. Use an allowed finite normal enlargement of $L$ with the distinguished subfields given by the original images of $K_s$ and $L_0$. Transport $f$ along the equality of prehosts. Each place $v'$ restricts to $v$ on $L$. Pull back $T_1$ using its triple at $v$. Restriction of places is surjective by lying over, preserves the residue characteristic, and commutes with conjugation. Thus the range of the tuple characters on $B'$ is exactly $\Xi$. In particular the request evaluated on $B'$ is the very set evaluated before enlargement. This argument permits arbitrary dependence of $S_2$ on $\Xi$. Saturation of $W$ proves coherence over $L_0$, and the roots just adjoined give the required primitive root at each place. [Contraction and pullback][pull] · [Finite root enlargement][roots] · [Full-field request and coherence][lift]

The forced-prime condition holds on this output whenever it was imposed on the old allowance. The two marked primes, discriminant prime, and primes in the prescribed size range all remain in $\mathcal P$. Any advance request on $K_s,L_0$ survives because their embedded fields agree. To meet the eventual requirements of a construction on the output, impose its small-field and allowance requests upstream and take pointwise unions of its avoidance and full-field requests with the caller's. Pinning now gives a column with the specified original preliminary parameters, host-place selectors, $J$ and $\delta$. The auxiliary data of the column use the characters of $T_1$ at the enlarged field. [Ordering the requests](continuation_v78/producer.tex#L243-L266) · [Copied entries and the new character column][pinext]

## Active places and subsequent readings

The old fine datum has its supplied lower-row laws. On the common eventual range of its extension theorem, include the requests of that implication as above. Green's finite-group input gives group data at the requisite volume on the new column. Extend the old fine datum on that very stage. For the active-set comparison, first set the forced-content function to zero on both columns. The nonzero reference-index and conjugate parts agree by the [integral comparison under field extension](UNTWISTED_AUXILIARY_PRIMES.md). Restore the forced content. Its vanishing test agrees under contraction by the established comparison for the divisor parameter. Thus membership in the whole active set is equivalent at $v'$ and its restriction $v$. This supplies the fine starting condition for the retwisted column. [Fine extension, group data and active places][fine] · [Installation on the selected $B'$][caller]

When a later level carries the character readings of this column, the apparent choice of a local root identification costs only a coprime power. At a selected $v'$ write $n=\ell_i^{k_i}$. Two embeddings of the cyclic group of $n$-th roots differ by $u\mapsto u^b$ with $(b,\ell_i)=1$. For any element of the finite abelian ray quotient, Chebotarev realizes it as a Frobenius outside $Q_i$ and $\ell_i$. Both compatible readings there give the inverse of the value of $\chi_i$ under their respective root identifications. Thus the power $b$ works on the entire quotient. It may depend on $v'$, and a subsequent signed coprime power $h$ gives $\psi_i^{bh}$ or $\psi_i^{-bh}$ using the original export. Transfer of the resulting inequalities to actual new Selmer and theta objects also requires that level's comparison of its representations, local conditions and forms. The strengthened third intermediate level additionally takes a carried state, its character reading, and a finite-level completion on this same column.

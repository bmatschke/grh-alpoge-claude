# Finishing the six shared obligations

Start with the common theta construction below. In parallel one can work on the period, counting and spectral estimates. Each of these estimates is a theorem about the actual family with a uniform range of scales. The [machine-readable inventory](SELECTED_FAMILY_CHECKPOINT.json) gives the exact six identifiers and the verifier. The [selected-family proof](LOCAL_SELECTED_FAMILY.md) explains the distinction between extracting information from a linked bundle and producing that bundle in the first place.

## First target on the common theta construction

At the reference tuple, extending the coefficient field preserves the exact Weil action, including both splitting characters, the prescribed lattice and vacuum vector, and the scale of the polynomial $\Delta_i$. The definite theta integral and its projection can use the original kernel and measure. Evaluation on definite classes explains why their rationality at the distinguished embedding survives scalar extension. These comparisons use the old realization as input. [Fixed-stage models](BASE_THETA_CONSTRUCTION_STEP.md) · [Extension of the row spaces](THETA_COEFFICIENT_EXTENSION.md) · [Reference Weil kernel and polynomial](THETA_REFERENCE_WEIL.md) · [Reference integral and rationality](THETA_REFERENCE_LIFT.md)

On a supplied arithmetic column with its original coefficient readings, [the fixed-stage proof](THETA_FIXED_STAGE_REALIZATION.md) constructs the Hilbert models, Haar measures, full Weil actions and tests. Under the original host interlacing and gap conditions it identifies **all eight analytic maps**, including on the admissible weak realization obtained after the final geometric coordinates are installed. The [normalized vacuum](THETA_LATTICE_VACUUM.md), [finite matrix tests](THETA_FINITE_LATTICE_TESTS.md) and [finite level characters](THETA_FINITE_LEVEL_LAW.md) work at every allowed twist. The key interior arguments are the [real theta pair](THETA_INTERIOR_DISCRETE_SERIES.md), [vanishing of constant terms](THETA_INTERIOR_CUSPIDALITY.md) and [factorization on smooth inputs](THETA_CUSPIDAL_FACTORIZATION.md). For the definite row, the earlier [holomorphic extension on every twist](seed_boundedness/main.tex#L721-L809) already supplies the corresponding global result.

On a lawful marked geometric core with its level and reference-row identifications, the [frame and phase comparison](THETA_MARKED_FRAME_COMPARISON.md) transports the dictionary to these models. Full support of quotient Haar measure proves Petersson definiteness on every component. The square of the normalized theta transformation fixes the sign needed for the exact stabilizer character. The earlier [phase-one construction](continuation_v77/action.tex#L49-L100) can then be used with the original readings.

The [theta line comparison](PEL_THETA_LINE.md) recovers the original rigidified cubical line from the principal CM polarization over arbitrary bases. The [marking comparison](PEL_MARKED_LEVEL.md) identifies the correct integral similitude level, including the full marking at $A$ and its pairing root, and proves that the marked polarized objects have trivial automorphisms.

**Next calculation.** Apply Lan’s fine moduli theorem over the reflex field and identify the resulting family over $K_A(l)$, with the source’s determinant condition and infinitesimal uniqueness. Identify its marked complex components with the adelic isometry classes at the original ordered primes, so the two-level comparison uses this geometric family.

# Saturation and the actual source lattices: v0.3 audit

Source pin: `CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean`, commit `7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4`.

## Result

**LC2 applies to the actual retained reference lattice.** The correct saturated inclusion is the ramified reference lattice inside the full integral column lattice. The selected lattice `NuLat` need not be saturated. The source already supplies the exact comparison, rational-subspace, and localized-index identities needed to distinguish these objects.

Replacing one coarse step in `MultisetValuation/StabilizerCount.core` gives, at each served regular place,

\[
\ell_w((R_u)_w/(N_u)_w)
\le e_w\left[r_u\tau_w+d_uS_w\left(\prod_v(1+m\kappa_v)-1\right)\right].
\tag{1}
\]

Here **the depth includes the entire stabilizer/Weyl sandwich shift**:

\[
\tau_w=e_p+\sum_{v\in\mathcal V_p}m(t_v+D_p+1).
\]

The term (e_p) is retained. Its rank multiplier becomes (r_u), just like the rest of the baseline depth. This removes ((d_u/r_u-1)\mathrm{Ta}) from LC5, without changing the selected lattice or its divisibility properties.

There is a further useful improvement. Under the source's existing rank formula and size hypotheses, (r_u/d_u\ge127/128). Since (Sigma_p\le1/2), the factorial tail gives

\[
\prod_v(1+m\kappa_v)-1
\le e^{\Sigma_p}-1
\le\frac{\Sigma_p}{1-\Sigma_p/2}
\le\frac43\Sigma_p.
\]

Consequently the tail in (1) is at most (r_u\mathrm{io}_p), where the **unchanged source definition** is (mathrm{io}_p=2S_p\Sigma_p). Thus the actual regular-place index allowance improves to

\[
\ell_w((R_u)_w/(N_u)_w)\le e_wr_u(\tau_w+\mathrm{io}_p).
\tag{2}
\]

The resulting squared-norm slope bound is

\[
\mu(I_uN_u)\ge\mu(R_u)+\mathrm{Ga}+\mathrm{Fo}-\mathrm{Ee}_u
-\mathrm{IO}-\mathcal R_u-\mathcal B_u,
\tag{3}
\]

with the type-prime cost (mathcal B_u), the fractional-ideal rounding loss (mathcal R_u), and the ramified mean correction all still present. This is a proved deduction from the stated joint-rank bounds and actual comparison identities. It is not an independent proof of the difficult joint-rank bounds or a global validation of the released Lean project.

## SR1 — The reference lattice that is saturated

Fix a word (u). Let (K) be its coefficient field, (L_u) the full integral column lattice in a (K)-space (V_u), and (U_u\subset V_u) the ramified rational subspace. Define

\[
R_u=L_u\cap U_u.
\]

The source has this definition at two levels:

| Source object | Exact meaning |
|---|---|
| `Columns.ColRamV` | Joint kernel of the (K)-linear averaging operators `NmOp` |
| `Columns.ramifiedColumnLattice` | `ColL ∩ ColRamV` |
| `MemberForms.ramSub` | Rational inverse image of the complex ramified subspace under the coefficient-field embedding |
| `MemberForms.ramL` | `(lat 0).L ∩ ramSub` |
| `DV.MramL` | Pullback of `(lat 0).L` under `D.nuIn` |
| `D7.MramL_map` | Exact image of `DV.MramL` under `D.nuIn` is `ramL` |

Since (U_u) is a (K)-subspace, (ax\in U_u) with (a\ne0) implies (x\in U_u). Therefore (R_u) is saturated in (L_u). The source proves the same fact directly in `Columns.AtTLemmas.ramified_sublattice_saturated`; no augmentation Gram determinant, integral tensor decomposition, or slope formula is needed for this elementary conclusion.

At a served (w\mid p), write (mathcal O\subset F) for the comparison DVR and its fraction field. `LatCmp` includes a semilinear map (phi), an embedding (K\hookrightarrow F) detecting integrality at (w), independence of the images of a (K)-basis, and the exact equality

\[
M=\mathcal O\phi(L_u)=\texttt{G.M}.
\]

`VramDef` says that `Y.Vram` is the restriction of scalars of (U_F:=F\phi(U_u)). Hence

\[
M_{\rm ram}=M\cap U_F
\]

is saturated in (M). This is the explicit proof in `TopChoiceBounds.ramified_saturated_of_subspace` (also repeated in `PlaneCountTheoremWithConstants.saturated_ramified_lattice`). In particular,

\[
M_{\rm ram}\cap p^\tau M=p^\tau M_{\rm ram}.
\tag{4}
\]

The selected lattice (P\subset M_{\rm ram}), or (N_u\subset R_u), usually has nontrivial torsion quotient and is generally **not** saturated. LC2 never requires it to be.

## SR2 — Exact comparison, descent, and length normalization

Put (A=\mathcal O_{K,w}). The integrality equivalence in `LatCmp.embW_int` induces an injective local map (A\to\mathcal O). Because (A) is a DVR and (mathcal O) is torsion-free over (A), this extension is flat. It is faithfully flat because the map is local. Basis independence identifies the scalar extensions with their images in the comparison vector space.

Flatness commutes with intersections, including intersections with rational subspaces. One can also see the latter directly: (L_{u,w}/(L_{u,w}\cap U_u)) is torsion-free, hence free over the DVR, so the exact sequence splits. Tensoring gives

\[
M\cap U_F=\mathcal O\phi(R_u).
\tag{5}
\]

This is exactly `LatCmp.M_inf_Vram`; it identifies the correct retained reference, rather than merely proving commensurability.

The selected comparison lattice is the source's `NP`:

\[
P=J\cap p^\tau M\cap U_F,
\qquad
J=\sum_{B\subseteq\mathcal V}
p^{\sum_{v\notin B}S_v}\left(\prod_{v\in B}y_v^m\right)M.
\tag{6}
\]

There is a short reconstruction of the descent step. Suppose each (y_v) agrees on rational vectors with a (K)-linear operator (a_v), possibly multiplied by a unit of (mathcal O). Define an (A)-lattice

\[
J_A=\sum_{B\subseteq\mathcal V}
p^{\sum_{v\notin B}S_v}\left(\prod_{v\in B}a_v^m\right)L_{u,w}.
\]

These are finite sums of images of a finite free lattice. Even if an (a_v) initially has denominators, this is still a finitely generated fractional lattice, and the empty subset supplies a nonzero scalar multiple of (L_{u,w}). Unit factors do not change the generated (mathcal O)-submodules. Thus (J=\mathcal O\otimes_AJ_A). Flatness now gives

\[
P=\mathcal O\otimes_A
(J_A\cap p^\tau L_{u,w}\cap U_u).
\tag{7}
\]

Faithful flatness detects membership, so the rational preimage of (P) is exactly the lattice in parentheses. The source proves this as `Joints.Lit.NPDesc_of` from `YvRat`, and has the unit variant `NPDesc_ofU` from `YvRatU`. The actual route is

`CmpTied.valRat → yvRat_of_valRat → npDesc_of_valRat → NPDescF`.

The existence of the actual comparison/rationality data is a source input here. The flatness, intersection, and membership argument above is independently reconstructed; it does not add a new arithmetic conjecture or import a new Smith-attainment claim.

`NuServedDef` identifies the localized selected global lattice with the rational preimage of (P). Combining it with (5)–(7), `LatCmp.NP_eq_span` records

\[
P=\mathcal O\phi(N_u\cap R_u).
\]

Intersecting globally with (R_u) causes no change at a regular served place, since `NuDef` gives (N_{u,w}\subseteq R_{u,w}).

For the index normalization, let (pi_w) be a uniformizer of (A), let (a=v_{mathcal O}(\pi_w)), and put (e_w=v_w(p)), (e_{mathcal O}=v_{mathcal O}(p)=ae_w). A Smith factor (A/(\pi_w^c)) becomes (mathcal O/(\pi_w^c)), of length (ac). Therefore

\[
\frac{\ell_{mathcal O}(M_{\rm ram}/P)}{e_{mathcal O}}
=\frac{\ell_A(R_{u,w}/N_{u,w})}{e_w}.
\tag{8}
\]

The extension need not have ramification index one and need not be finite. The residue-field degree does not enter module length. Equation (8) is the normalization in `FBC.flat_ext` and `LatCmp.length_quotient_of_nonempty`. Ranks are unchanged: `cmpN_li` preserves independence of a retained-space basis, while `MramL` spans the retained space. Thus (operatorname{rank}_{mathcal O}M=d_u) and (operatorname{rank}_{mathcal O}M_{m ram}=r_u).

### Actual constructor versus the NZCol example

`NZColD.nuLat` is a generic intersection construction, with `cond` selecting an `NP` preimage at served places, `Nlam` at the word's type prime, and `locS MramL` elsewhere. `NZColF.nuDataOf` plugs in the same `NP`; `NZColG.sizeDataNu` stores the resulting lattice and length divided by (e_w). These give useful parallel definitions.

However, a repository-wide name search did **not** establish that this named `sizeDataNu` is the final constructor used by the main family. The concrete constructor checked here is instead:

| Source declaration | What it actually establishes |
|---|---|
| `ExistsLedPart.exists_served` | The exact rational preimage of `NP` is a module closed under division by (w)-units |
| `ExistsLedPart.exists_lat` | A global lattice whose localizations are that preimage at served places, `Nlam` at the own type prime, and `MramL` at unserved regular places |
| `ExistsLedPart.setLat` | Stores that global lattice as `NuLat`, and stores `lenLoc/e_w` as `lenMN` |
| `ExistsLedPart.exists_ledPart` | Packages the served identity, `NuDef`, `NuUnserved`, and the type-prime identity into the actual record |

The proof uses finite patching (`Glue.exists_glue`). The place set is finite because served rational primes satisfy (p<X'), and the own type prime has only finitely many places above it. This is the same elementary patching principle treated in the period-lattice reconstruction. Closure under division by (w)-units here means being an (A)-module; it is distinct from the all-nonzero-scalar saturation in (4).

## SR3 — `G.Mna` does not create an extra retained-rank penalty

The source has a sandwich

\[
p^eM_{\rm na}\subseteq M\subseteq M_{\rm na},
\quad M_{\rm na}=\texttt{G.Mna},\quad M=\texttt{G.M}.
\]

It follows that their fraction-field spans and ranks agree. These integral lattices need not be equal or saturated in one another. The comparison identity is with (M), as explicitly proved by `TieLatFin.comparison_lattice_span_eq`.

The operators (y_v) preserve both lattices. If (J_{m na}) denotes (6) with (M_{m na}), linearity gives

\[
p^eJ_{\rm na}\subseteq J\subseteq J_{\rm na}.
\]

If (g) vectors (z_i\in M_{m na}) supplement (J_{m na}) enough to contain (p^EM_{m na}), then (p^ez_i\in M) supplement (J) enough to contain (p^{E+e}M). Hence the exceptional count is unchanged, and the depth increases by exactly (e). This is `inverse_shift` in the actual `core` proof.

For nonempty (mathcal V), `StabilizerCount.stmt6G` explicitly proves

\[
\texttt{tauP}=e_p+\sum_{v\in\mathcal V}m(\lceil\texttt{tv}_v\rceil+D_p+1)\in\mathbb N.
\]

Its further displayed ceiling/toNat is therefore the identity. `SvG_cast` proves the analogous fact for `Sv`, which is defined as the ceiling of a nonnegative sum. The maximum (S) can be the exact maximum of these integers. The outer `ioP` is then exactly (2S\Sigma). Reindexing to the local set of components uses the recorded injectivity of `vOf`; no rank changes occur.

At unserved regular places `NuUnserved` gives local equality with the retained reference. At the own type prime the source uses `Nlam` instead; that contribution is not estimated by (1).

## SR4 — Improved index and global slope

Assume the original all-but bounds after the sandwich shift, for every integer (eta\ge0):

\[
\#\{i:c_i>e_{mathcal O}(\tau+\beta S)\}
\le d_u m^{\beta+1}e_{\beta+1}(\kappa).
\]

LC2 bounds the tail quotient (p^\tau M/(J\cap p^\tau M)) by (e_{mathcal O}d_uS\Theta), with (Theta=\prod(1+m\kappa_v)-1). By (4),

\[
0\longrightarrow p^\tau M_{m ram}/P
\longrightarrow M_{m ram}/P
\longrightarrow M_{m ram}/p^\tau M_{m ram}\longrightarrow0.
\]

The last quotient has exact length (e_{mathcal O}r_u\tau). The first injects into the full tail quotient. Combining with (8) proves (1).

In the original `core`, the final proof instead injects the entire retained quotient into the entire full quotient. That charges (e_{mathcal O}d_u\tau) for its baseline. The new argument changes only this accounting step and the elementary tail summation; it does not change `NP`, `NuLat`, any operator, or any divisibility statement.

The strongest convenient global form retains the product:

\[
\mu(I_uN_u)\ge\mu(R_u)+\mathrm{Ga}+\mathrm{Fo}-\mathrm{Ee}_u
-\sum_{w\ {m served}}\operatorname{sz}_w(\rho_uS_w\Theta_w)
-\mathcal R_u-\mathcal B_u,
\tag{9}
\]

where (operatorname{sz}_w(z)=2\log(Nw)e_wz/[K:\mathbb Q]). Since (Theta\le2\Sigma), (9) first gives the uniform improvement

\[
-\rho_u\mathrm{IO}-(\rho_u-1)\mathrm{Ta}quad\rightsquigarrow\quad-\rho_u\mathrm{IO}.
\]

The old and new (	au) include (e_p). Its contribution to `Ga` is not erased; only the excess full-rank multiplier is erased.

## SR5 — Existing actual size conditions absorb ρ as well

The elementary symmetric-function estimate (m^je_j(\kappa)\le\Sigma^j/j!) and (j!\ge2^{j-1}) give

\[
\Theta\le\frac{\Sigma}{1-\Sigma/2}\le\frac43\Sigma,
\qquad0\le\Sigma\le\frac12.
\]

Thus (r_u/d_u\ge2/3) suffices for (d_uS\Theta\le r_u\mathrm{io}). Bernoulli's inequality applied to the source's retained rank formula gives

\[
\delta_u:=1-\frac{r_u}{d_u}
\le\frac{f}{T_u-1}\le\frac{2f}{T_u}.
\]

The actual family assembly uses (16f\le b), (b\ge4), (T_u\ge b^3). Therefore

\[
\delta_u\le\frac{2f}{T_u}\le\frac{b}{8T_u}
\le\frac1{8b^2}\le\frac1{128},
\]

so (r_u/d_u\ge127/128>2/3). For (f=0), the ranks coincide and the same conclusion holds. `PlacesP.sizes_at` supplies the first two numerical inequalities; `placesF_of` proves the third before invoking `size_alt`. This is not a newly imposed type-prime condition.

Equation (2), hence (3), follows. If the LC7 metric transport is also supplied, put

\[
A_u=\frac{f\log(T_u-1)}{T_u-2},\qquad\mu(R_u)=\mu(L_u)-A_u.
\]

Then (3) reads

\[
\boxed{\mu(I_uN_u)\ge\mu(L_u)+\mathrm{Ga}+\mathrm{Fo}-\mathrm{Ee}_u
-\mathrm{IO}-A_u-\mathcal R_u-\mathcal B_u.}
\]

The source prime-sum normalization is unchanged:

\[
\mathrm{IO}=\sum_w\operatorname{sz}_w(\mathrm{io}_{p(w)})
=2\sum_p\mathrm{io}_p\log p=2\mathrm{IOp}.
\]

Using the already available (A_u\le1), the new clean allowance is (2\mathrm{IOp}+1+mathcal R_u+mathcal B_u), versus the source's coarser (4\mathrm{IOp}+1+mathcal R_u+mathcal B_u). Keeping (9) is sharper than either constant simplification.

The type-prime allowance is unchanged. At (p=T_u), the actual size assumptions give (T_u\ge b^3\ge X'); hence `VV` is empty and (	au=mathrm{io}=0). Both the old and new regular baselines vanish there. The separate bound on the `Nlam` contribution is still necessary; the argument does not improve that bound.

## SR6 — Limits and a useful obstruction

Saturation alone does not permit replacing the full-rank tail by a retained-rank tail. For example, over a DVR with uniformizer (pi), take

\[
M=\mathcal O^4,\quad M'=\mathcal O e_1,\quad
J=\pi^C\mathcal Oe_1\oplus\mathcal Oe_2\oplus\mathcal Oe_3\oplus\mathcal Oe_4.
\]

Let (p=pi,	au=0,S=C,m=1), and use one parameter (kappa=1/4). The exceptional count at depth zero is (1=d\kappa); all later counts vanish. The entire length (C) is concentrated in the saturated rank-one reference (M'). A retained-rank product-tail estimate would falsely give (C/4), and a retained-rank source `io` estimate would falsely give (C/2). The absorption in SR5 therefore uses the actual high retained-rank ratio and the factorial saving; it is not a general principle about saturated subspaces.

Similarly, the sandwich alone does not identify (M) with (M_{m na}). In rank one, (M_{m na}=mathcal O) and (M=pimathcal O) already satisfy such a sandwich but have different reference indices. The exact `cmp_M` and `M_inf_Vram` identities are the reason the improvement applies to the actual source.

The remaining substantive inputs are the joint exceptional-direction estimates, the constructed comparison maps/rational operator identities, the chosen family's rank formula and size assertions, the period/divisibility statements, and the separate type-prime bound. This note expands the transport and index argument and isolates those inputs; it does not assert their full independent verification or Lean compilation.

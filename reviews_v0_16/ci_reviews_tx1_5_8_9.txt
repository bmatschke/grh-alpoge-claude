TX1 — completion and rational intersection
The product H K_P(d) is a group because its principal factor is normal in the
completed integral general linear group. Two rational H factors of the same
element differ by a rational principal matrix at least at the required depth,
so the character is well defined and multiplicative. This also proves the exact
rational intersection, including integrality of the inverse. It is not merely
a density assertion. No assumption that arbitrary rational characters extend
to the whole completed group is made. The prescribed congruence subgroup gives
openness, and any finite character exponent passes to the extension.

TX2 — scalar depth and paired components

not automatically topDep+1; the text now preserves this source distinction.
The normality/product description of the generated terminal level and its
character agree with the actual extTop/principal construction. The paired

unitarity of the finite adele. No globally unitary rational D_pi is asserted.

components are identity, so all required integral and congruence clauses hold.

TX4 — actual surjectivity and rational frames

argument, which the text gives. Starting with y, approximate the completed
columns of y k t_a by rational frame matrices in the same open basis/congruence

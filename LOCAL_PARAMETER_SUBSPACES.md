# Which automorphic forms have a given parameter?

The same space of algebraic modular forms can be studied at several coefficient primes and several Hecke localizations. A candidate global parameter singles out a complex subspace of those forms. Why should two localizations give the *same subspace* whenever their character tuple and weight agree? The answer is that the parameter tests an automorphic representation up to isomorphism. The coefficient prime only changes how that representation is indexed. This proves the identity called `MtParTie`. At a split conductor prime, a theorem on Iwahori types identifies the principal-series Bernstein component from the level character. ## A parameter selects whole isotypic components

Fix one of the complex spaces $M_{\mu,j}$ of algebraic modular forms in the construction. Its realization $r_{\mu,j}$ identifies it with the forms of the given coefficient weight transforming under the prescribed compact open subgroup by the prescribed character. For an irreducible automorphic representation $V$ occurring there, let $I_{\mu,j}(V)$ be the inverse image of its full isotypic subspace. This includes the vectors coming from every copy isomorphic to $V$. There are finitely many nonzero summands. Their direct sum is $M_{\mu,j}$. [Isotypic decomposition and automorphic points](LOCAL_AUTOMORPHIC_POINTS.md)

Write $\lambda_w$ for the local parameter attached to a supplied parameter label $\lambda$ at a finite place $w$ of the CM field. At a stage $1\leq j\leq m$ occurring among the localizations, let $c$ be the rank-one automorphic representation identified with the tuple character $\mathrm{tup}(\mu,j-1)$. The shift by one converts the stage to the zero-based tuple index. The following test on $V$ is intrinsic.

1. Seek a finite list $\Sigma$ of blocks $(a,\pi,d)$ whose combined local parameter equals $\lambda_w$ at every finite place $w$. Here $\pi$ is a cuspidal automorphic label for $\mathrm{GL}_a$. Each block contributes its local parameter with the successive absolute-value twists $(d-1)/2,(d-3)/2,\ldots,-(d-1)/2$.
2. Append the block $(1,c,t)$ and twist every resulting cuspidal label by the prescribed $\eta_T^{-1}$. Require each block in this expanded list to have positive $a,d$ and conjugate-self-dual cuspidal label. Its triples must be distinct, and the sum of their ranks $ad$ must equal the rank $n$ of the unitary group.
3. At every split place require the expanded list to give the local parameter of $V$. At every nonsplit place require it to be unramified, with Frobenius eigenvalues given by the multiset $\alpha\sqcup\alpha^{-1}$ and one further $1$ if $n$ is odd. Here $\alpha$ is the Satake multiset of $V$ at that place. At every complex embedding require the corresponding shifted infinitesimal characters to give the infinitesimal character of $V$.

The test succeeds if one list works. The first comparison concerns the smaller list at **all** finite places. The remaining comparisons concern the expanded list and use the split, nonsplit and archimedean data of the actual automorphic class. [Block operations and base-change test](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L1239-L1277) · [Intrinsic form of the test](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/TypeVISupIndep.lean#L44-L64)

**Proposition.** For a pair $(\mu,j)$ which occurs in the specialization, the required subspace is

$$
M_{\mu,j}(\lambda)=\bigoplus_{[V]\ \mathrm{passing\ the\ test}} I_{\mu,j}(V).
$$

At every localization $i$ with $(\mu_i,j_i)=(\mu,j)$, this is exactly the sum of the local type spaces prescribed by the parameter identity.

**Proof.** The actual automorphic realization enumerates representatives $P$ of the irreducible classes occurring in $M_{\mu,j}$. Its type space for $P$ is $I_{\mu,j}(V_P)$. The comparison of automorphic classes identifies its split parameters, nonsplit Satake multisets and infinitesimal character with those of $V_P$. Its rank-one comparison identifies $c_i$ with $c$ by the injective correspondence with tuple characters. Thus each list admitted for $P$ passes the same test for $V_P$ and contributes its full summand to the displayed formula. Conversely, every nonzero $I_{\mu,j}(V)$ in that formula has a representative in the local enumeration. Isomorphic copies have the same automorphic class, so the list witnessing membership for $V$ also works for that representative. This gives the reverse inclusion. The argument includes classes with no point in the chosen Hecke localization. The zero sum covers labels with no qualifying class. For a pair $(\mu,j)$ occurring at no localization, take the zero subspace, as in the source. [Comparison in both directions](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/TypeVISupIndep.lean#L83-L116) · [Independence of the index](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/TypeVISupIndep.lean#L120-L130) · [Value on unused pairs](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/RbarData.lean#L39-L53)

This proposition is valid for the common automorphic realization above. Existence of a qualifying parameter list for a particular $V$ is a separate arithmetic question. The asserted independence is equality of subspaces under a change of localization. Different labels can in principle pass the same test, for example when their supplied local data agree.

## A depth-zero theorem gives the extra local support condition

The alternate formulation of nonsplit sphericity asks for two facts about the same $V$. One is a spherical vector at the specified nonsplit place. The other, called `GoodAU`, concerns each irreducible local representation $\sigma$ occurring in $V$ at **every split place**. Its supercuspidal support must consist of rank-one characters or, over a field isomorphic to $\mathbf Q_g$, twists of representations with a vector fixed by the first principal congruence subgroup. The first fact follows from the hyperspecial level at the nonsplit place. We prove the second for each class occurring at the levels of the actual tied character family. [Definitions of sphericity and support](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/UnitarySphericalFixing/SatakeParameters/GoodAUIso.lean#L14-L23) · [The local condition on supercuspidals](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/NonGaloisDescent/RecTExists.lean#L17-L25) · [Prescribed levels](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/RingSpecNormalisedCharacterEq.lean#L96-L106)

Write $F$ for the split completion, $K=\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal O_F)$, and $K_1$ for the kernel of reduction to $\mathrm{GL}_n(k_F)$. At each place the prescribed character can be read inside $\sigma$. Indeed a nonzero global vector of the given type in the isotypic component has a nonzero projection to an irreducible copy of $V$. For an embedded copy of $\sigma$ inside $V$, its translates by the group away from the place span $V$ because their span is nonzero and invariant under both local and away factors. The projector onto the desired local compact character commutes with away translates. Since it is nonzero on $V$, it is nonzero on the embedded $\sigma$. [Local realization](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/RingSpecNormalisedCharacterEq.lean#L48-L53)

- **Hyperspecial level.** A $K$-fixed vector is fixed by the standard Iwahori subgroup $I$. By the Iwahori case of the principal-series type theorem, $\sigma$ has toral supercuspidal support. All its factors have rank one. [Haines, §3, pp. 3–4](https://math.umd.edu/~tjh/PS_types.pdf#page=4)
- **Parahoric level at a conductor prime.** The level is the inverse image of the block upper parabolic of sizes $(r,n-r)$. Its character on a level matrix $h$ is $\xi(\det D)$ for the lower-right block $D$. Both factors of the actual tuple character $\mu_{0,j}\xi_\mu$ are tame. The local character $\xi$ agrees with this character on the dense image of the global field, and its kernel on the units is open. It is therefore trivial on $1+\mathfrak p_F$. Restricted to the standard Iwahori, the level character is inflated from the diagonal character $(1,\ldots,1,\bar\xi,\ldots,\bar\xi)$ of the residue torus, with $n-r$ copies of $\bar\xi$. Indeed the lower-right block is upper triangular after reduction. Haines's Theorem 3.0.2 identifies this Iwahori character as a type for the Bernstein component of that torus character, in every residue characteristic. Hence all supercuspidal factors have rank one. Changing the split identification conjugates the Iwahori or passes to the opposite Iwahori and inverse torus character, which again has the same rank-one conclusion. [Level and character definitions](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/GaloisLevelStructure/DefsLevel.lean#L17-L54) · [Tameness of the first factor](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/UnitaryNorms/CharHost.lean#L57-L62) · [Second factor and product](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/UnitaryNorms/CharTied.lean#L63-L90) · [Haines, Theorem 3.0.2](https://math.umd.edu/~tjh/PS_types.pdf#page=4)
- **The two designated type primes.** The underlying rational prime $g$ splits completely in the CM field, so $F\simeq\mathbf Q_g$. The special level contains $K_1$. Its character there is the product of an additive character evaluated at the sum of certain entries immediately above the diagonal and $\xi(\det D)$. On $K_1$ the sum lies in $\mathfrak p_F$ and $\det D\in1+\mathfrak p_F$. Both factors are trivial, so $\sigma^{K_1}\ne0$. [Complete splitting and the completion](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/ExistsPadicEquiv.lean#L17-L35) · [Definition of the special character](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/GaloisLevelStructure/DefsLevel.lean#L17-L54)

To finish the last case, take a cuspidal support for $\sigma$ and realize it as a subquotient of normalized parabolic induction $i_P^G(\bigotimes_b\rho_b)$ for the corresponding standard Levi $M$. Compact averaging is exact over $\mathbf C$. The induced representation therefore has a nonzero $K_1$-fixed function. By $G=PK$, the function is nonzero at some $k\in K$. Its value there is fixed by $M\cap K_1=\prod_b K_{1,b}$ by normality of $K_1$ in $K$, the induction transformation law and triviality of the modulus on compact elements. Averaging over the product now gives $\rho_b^{K_{1,b}}\ne0$ for every $b$. The trivial twist meets the stipulated support condition. [Existence of cuspidal support, §2](https://math.umd.edu/~tjh/PS_types.pdf#page=2) · [Corresponding source argument](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/FixedNeBotOfMemSupport.lean#L19-L40)

The three cases exhaust the split places of each actual specialization. Together with nonsplit sphericity they prove the extra condition in the alternate automorphic realization on its own prescribed family. This application of Haines uses the tameness of that family. Arbitrary local characters at parahoric level would require a different argument.

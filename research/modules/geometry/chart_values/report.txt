For the actual step of the true column family, the theorem proves the

(Fi) A tuple is in the original complex member space if and only if its
restriction, via the supplied identity-on-matrices frame equivalence, is

(rat) Such a complex tuple comes from the actual coefficient-field forms

(lat) At EVERY coefficient prime w, a rational form belongs to the actual

This is considerably more than observing that a record could be filled.
The proof explicitly extends class functions to adelic points, constructs
the coefficient-field preimage using injectivity of polynomial evaluation,
identifies the actual integral coefficient lattice, and clears one common
denominator over all the finite classes. The intermediate local polynomial
lattice is recovered from its full family of local models by LP2.

2. NOTATION DICTIONARY AND WHICH OBJECTS ARE ACTUALLY THE SAME

The main note uses L for the coefficient number field. In the source this
is B.LL and also B.fields.K. The CM field is E = B.host.E = B.fields.E,
with maximal real subfield F. We use sigma for the chosen coefficient
embedding L -> C; the source name is B.embLL. The finite family j_tau is
B.fields.emb. The complex field embeddings used by the chart record are
literally iota_tau = sigma o j_tau:

  Formalism.L2.Forms.embT (I.rec7 l Z)
    = embAt B.embLL B.fields.emb.

The proof obtains this equality from B.embE_phi0. It is an exact equality
of ring homomorphisms, not merely a field isomorphism or a freely changed
complex place.

The level is i, the dimension n = B.R.N i, and the unshifted polynomial
weight mu is (I.fm l u a).lam i. The weight used by the actual frame
functions is

  lambda = mu - c_i * (1,...,1),  c_i = i(t+1)/2.

The t in the source is odd. If t = 2k+1, then

  (t+1)/2 = k+1,  i(t+1)/2 = i(k+1).

Thus the natural-number divisions in the two source weight definitions
have exactly the same integer value. One source shW subtracts its scalar;
GlobalTheory.Mem.Col2.shW adds its scalar. The latter is called with
-i((t+1)/2), and the two resulting weights are equal.

Adding the same integer to every component does not change natW, the
partition obtained by subtracting the terminal component. It does change
detShift and hence the determinant factor of the frame function. All
formulas use d_tau = lambda_tau.detShift, including the source's empty
matrix convention when n = 0.

SF has already shown that the actual positive spaces, in their designated
global frame coordinates, have gramN = 1 and integral standard lattice.
Moreover

  latCrd (I.rec7 l Z) i hi (I.latAt u a i c)
    = ((I.stp u a i).rep c).deco.L.

The Hermitian dual of this lattice for h(x,y) = conjugate(x)^t y is
exactly the source's deco.Lsh. This is the equality used in val_tied.
It is an equality of sets/modules in E^n in the fixed coordinates.

The symbol b in CV6-CV9 denotes a rational p-local basis of a particular
class's dual lattice, chosen or quantified separately for that lattice.
It is NOT the common global matrix B of SF. The common global coordinate
system remains fixed, while b describes a lattice within that system.

The frame equivalence e_Y has (e_Y z).val = z.val for every z. Its inverse
has the same property. The parameter G attached to Frames G does not
impose an orthonormality condition: Frames G is the subtype of tuples of
invertible matrices. The separate predicate Frames.IsON is not required
in this value proof. This distinction lets polynomial injectivity be
proved using all invertible matrices, with no unitary-density argument.

3. CV1: FRAME EVALUATION AND THE ACTION CONVENTION

For a polynomial P in the blocks X_tau, and an invertible matrix tuple A,
write

  E_{lambda,A}(P)(z)
    = product_tau det(A_tau z_tau)^{d_tau}
        times P((A_tau z_tau)_tau).

This is the source fnOf. It matters that the determinant is taken of
A_tau z_tau, not only of z_tau. This convention keeps the determinant
factor in every change-of-basis formula.

If E_{lambda,A}(P) = 0, its determinant multiplier can be cancelled on
every invertible tuple. Since left multiplication by A permutes these
tuples, P vanishes on all of them. To prove that P itself is zero, fix
an arbitrary tuple X and consider

  Q(s_tau) = P((X_tau + s_tau I)_tau).

For each tau, det(X_tau + s_tau I) is a monic polynomial in s_tau, so only
finitely many s_tau are forbidden. Q vanishes on the product of the
remaining infinite sets. Successive one-variable polynomial uniqueness
gives Q = 0, hence P(X) = Q(0) = 0. Since X was arbitrary, P = 0.
Mapping coefficients through the injective field embedding sigma preserves
this injectivity. This proof is also valid for n=0, where determinants are
one and the matrix-variable set is empty.

The source's action on polynomials is

  rho_lambda(g)P(X)
    = product det(g_tau)^{d_tau} P((g_tau^t X_tau)_tau).

The transpose makes rho a homomorphism: applying rho(h) inside rho(g)
substitutes h^t g^t X = (gh)^t X. The determinant factors multiply.
Direct evaluation gives

  E_{lambda,1}(sigma(rho_lambda(g)P))
    = E_{lambda,sigma(g)^t}(sigma P).

For the group element gamma acting on the class step, its polynomial
action is eta * rho_lambda((j_tau(gamma)^{-t})_tau). Its evaluation is

  sigma(eta) E_{lambda,1}(sigma P)(iota(gamma^{-1}) z).

This is exactly the source trFr, with its inverse on the frame argument.
The determinant and transpose orientations agree throughout.

The complex polynomial representation is stable under arbitrary invertible
left substitutions. Equivalently it is the dual-Weyl coefficient space:
the column degree and the right upper-unipotent identities are unchanged
by left substitution. The source proves the corresponding valC_tr using
cspan_eq and blockAct preservation. No claim of integral stability for
arbitrary nonintegral matrices is made here.

4. CV2: EXACT COEFFICIENT-FIELD IMAGE AND EXACT INTEGER LOCALIZATION

For each weight partition nu_tau, Delta_nu(Z) is the Z-module generated by
all integral divided-power words on the highest polynomial. The operators
and their highest polynomial commute with coefficient extension. Therefore
the integral coefficient tensor lattice is the O_L-span of products of
these same integer polynomials. This identifies the source delStd exactly.

The characteristic-zero polynomial space VV is initially expressed using
the dual-Weyl space nablaSq. Over L, nablaSq = delSq. The equality can be
seen either from the preceding IW3 rational-span argument or from the
source's standard-monomial proof: every integer bideterminant has a
nonzero integer multiple in the integral Weyl module; this integer is
invertible in the characteristic-zero field. This argument proves a field
equality only. It does not show that the integer coefficient lattices
Delta and nabla are equal.

Tensoring the field equality gives

  V_lambda = L * Delta_std.

RatPol in HermitianChartComplexRationalIntegralForms, and RatSpan in
TiedStep, are additive closures of the finite sums sigma(r) times tensor
products of integer Delta generators, for r in L. An additive subgroup
closure means finite additions and negatives; no topological closure is
involved. Distributing tensor products shows

  RatSpan = sigma(V_lambda).

The source proves the two directions separately as
exists_VV_of_mem_RatPol and map_mem_RatPol. The latter direction is where
characteristic zero is used to replace the field dual-Weyl space by the
field span of the Weyl words.

For the integer statement, put R_w = O_{L,w}. The source's condition
r in intW is equivalent to |r|_w <= 1 and hence r in R_w. If

  Q = sum_{j=1}^r sigma(a_j) sigma(F_j), a_j in R_w,

and each F_j is an integer Weyl tensor, choose a common denominator
s in O_L outside w for the finitely many a_j. Then
s sum a_j F_j belongs to Delta_std, so the lifted polynomial belongs
to Delta_std,w. Conversely, given P in Delta_std,w, choose s outside w
with sP in Delta_std; expand sP into the integer tensors and divide by s.
Every resulting coefficient remains in R_w. Thus

  IntSpan_w = sigma(Delta_std,w).

This equality is independent of the positive ramification normalization of
the additive valuation, because the source retains its exact nonnegative
valuation ring. We do not change numerical valuation values or assert a
norm equality. There is no passage through the larger integral nabla
lattice and no bound of the form p^k Delta in place of the exact lattice.

5. CV3: CONSTRUCTING THE RATIONAL FORM, WITHOUT AVERAGING

For a generic step S, its classes are pairs consisting of a finite index
and an orbit of decorated points. Its rational form space is the subspace
of tuples v_c in VV L lambda fixed by each class stabilizer under the
character-twisted polynomial action. Its complex member space Fi is
defined by the corresponding frame-value invariance.

Suppose a complex tuple phi lies in Fi and each phi_c has a RatSpan
polynomial representation. By section 4 choose v_c over L. For each
stabilizer element s, the complex invariance equation says the evaluation
of rho_S(s) v_c equals the evaluation of v_c. The intertwining formula
from section 3 and injectivity of evaluation imply rho_S(s)v_c = v_c
over L. Hence the tuple v is an element of the source FormsK, and its
actual embedding is phi.

The forward implication is its defining evaluation formula together with
the same intertwining identity. This proves exists_embF_iff constructively
at the level of chosen finite polynomial representatives. The proof does
not divide by a stabilizer order. It does not use a dimension comparison,
nor assume that the stabilizers are trivial. Pointwise choice of v_c
would even work for an infinite class set; class finiteness is only needed
later for the common integral denominator.

6. CV4: ADELIC EXTENSION WITH REPRESENTATIVE INDEPENDENCE

The chart supplies a bijection from its class index set to the double
quotient Gamma \ G(A_f) / U. It is important that this is a bijection:
surjectivity provides decompositions, and injectivity identifies two
representative class labels when a point admits two decompositions.

Let y_c be the representatives. For y = gamma y_c kappa, define

  f(y,z) = chi(kappa) phi_c(iota(gamma^{-1})z).

Suppose also y = gamma' y_{c'} kappa'. The class bijection gives c=c'.
Put delta = gamma'^{-1} gamma and v = kappa kappa'^{-1}. Then

  y_c = delta y_c v,
  iota(gamma'^{-1}) z = iota(delta) iota(gamma^{-1}) z.

The assumed self-relation phi_c(iota(delta)z0)=chi(v)phi_c(z0)
therefore identifies the two values after multiplication by chi(kappa').
This proves representative independence with precisely the source's
right-character convention.

The same calculation after multiplying y on the left by a rational
unitary element and on the right by a level element proves

  f(gamma0 y kappa0, iota(gamma0) z) = chi(kappa0) f(y,z).

At each fixed y, the dependence on z is an invertible left translate of
phi_c times a scalar, so it remains in ValC by section 3. Restriction to
the representatives recovers phi. This proves both directions of
mem_fiC_iff and explains why just matching dimensions of complex spaces
would not supply the required theorem.

7. CV5: WHY THE SOURCE'S TWO CHARACTERS ARE IDENTICAL

The main chart proof must relate the character of the concrete decorated
point stabilizer to the character of the actual adelic level group.
An abstract stabilizer isomorphism would not suffice.

ReadsK supplies k_beta and says that y_c k_beta represents the decorated
point at c after extending its rational frames to the common twist set
SQ. The representation predicate IsPtOf carries all of the following:

  (1) the adelic matrix is unitary;
  (2) it carries the standard completed/global lattice to the designated
      full lattice N;
  (3) its inverse pullback has the prescribed last-coordinate residues
      at the type primes ST;
  (4) its inverse pullback has all the prescribed auxiliary-coordinate
      residues at SG;
  (5) at each twist prime Q, its entries are exactly the entries of the
      designated rational frame, embedded in that completion.

The source theorem isPtOf_ratAd transports every one of these fields.
For a rational unitary gamma,

  pullV(gamma g, x) = pullV(g, gamma^{-1}x).

Thus the represented lattice is gamma N, and the transformed f and be
functionals are the original functionals evaluated at gamma^{-1}x.
The rational frame at Q becomes gamma times its old frame, exactly
because the Q-component of the rational adelic diagonal is the coefficient
embedding of gamma. The unitary equation is stable under multiplication.

Restricting a representation from SQ to Q_a preserves its rational-frame
clauses. ReadsK has Q_a subset SQ, and I.Ok supplies that SQ, hence Q_a,
is disjoint from SG. In the source, membership in SQ identifies a
selected good twist prime lying over a prime outside I.S, whereas every
SG prime lies over one of the auxiliary rational primes contained in I.S.
This proves the disjointness used by IsPtOf.of_extTo. No arbitrary
replacement of a prescribed residue or prime is made.

Now let s=(gamma_s,ell_s) stabilize p_c, where ell_s is its pair of level
components. The product action gives

  (1,ell_s)p_c = (gamma_s^{-1},1)p_c.

If the adelic level element kappa satisfies y_c=gamma_s y_c kappa, then

  gamma_s^{-1}(y_c k_beta) = y_c kappa k_beta.

The previous transport result identifies this latter matrix as a
representative of (1,ell_s)p_c. The ReadsK.chr clause applies because
the rational component of (1,ell_s) is the identity. It yields

  chi(kappa) = sigma(chr_S(1,ell_s)).

The actual stepY character is a product of the two level characters;
by its formula it ignores the rational unitary component. Therefore
chr_S(1,ell_s)=chr_S(s), proving the desired equality with its exact
orientation.

Finally LevelChart.stabI_range identifies rational gamma in y_c U y_c^-1
with the image of a point stabilizer. A relation y_c=gamma y_c kappa
puts gamma in that range using kappa^-1. Conversely any stabilizer-image
gamma supplies an inverse level element giving such a relation. Combining
this with the character identity identifies the original fixed-vector
condition with the self-relation needed in section 6. Thus the actual Fi
clause follows chart by chart.

8. CV6: SEMILOCAL RATIONAL BASES AND COEFFICIENT-PLACE BASE CHANGE

Let p be a rational prime and H a full O_E-lattice in E^n.
The source IsLocBasis p H b is stated with natural integers s satisfying
p does not divide s. It is exactly the statement that

  H tensor O_{E,(p)} = b O_{E,(p)}^n.

For q over p, every such s is outside q: Bezout gives a linear combination
of p and s equal to one. Further localization gives a q-local basis.
In explicit coordinates both inclusions are verified by multiplying the
finitely many denominators of the coordinates or lattice-membership
witnesses. This is isBasisAt_of_isLocBasis.

The existence of b is the earlier rational semilocal basis construction.
A full lattice is a finite projective module over the Dedekind integer
ring. After localizing at the finitely many primes over p, each ideal
summand is principal: choose an element with its prescribed ideal
valuations at those primes by the CRT/approximation argument. The
resulting free basis consists of elements of E^n and is therefore one
matrix in GL_n(E), simultaneously at every prime above p. The basis does
not vary independently with tau or with a chosen complex embedding.

For each prescribed j_tau:E->L, let q_tau be the contraction of w.
The rational residue characteristic p_w belongs to q_tau because its
image is the same integer p_w in w. This is the source hbel calculation.
Define M_tau as the O_L-span of j_tau(H). A denominator outside q_tau
maps outside w, so

  M_tau,w = j_tau(b) R_w^n.

The ordinary dual is M_tau^vee={a:a^t x in O_L for all x in M_tau}.
For any finite full M_tau, localization of this dual equals the local
dual. One inclusion follows by clearing the denominator of an element
of M_tau^vee; for the other, clear the finitely many denominators of
a^t m_j on a finite generating set m_j. Therefore

  (M_tau^vee)_w = j_tau(b)^-t R_w^n.

This ordinary dual must not be confused with the preceding Hermitian
dual H_c^#. The source delAtr first takes H=H_c^#, extends it along
j_tau, and THEN takes the ordinary linear dual of that extended lattice.
The two dual operations and all coefficient embeddings are preserved.

9. CV7: WHY THE GLOBAL POLYNOMIAL LATTICE LOCALIZES TO ONE EXACT MODEL

The source definition transLat intersects the local conditions at every
coefficient prime and every local basis. For the integral Weyl tensor
Delta_std and the lattices M_tau^vee, denote this global lattice by
D_lambda(H).

At a fixed w, choose a tuple of bases g_tau,w of M_tau^vee,w and put

  A_w = rho_lambda(g_w) Delta_std,w.

First prove independence from the chosen bases. Another tuple is g_w h
for h_tau in GL_n(R_w). The ordinary polynomial Weyl lattice is preserved
integrally by h_tau^t. Over the local ring, an invertible matrix can be
reduced using a unit pivot, so it factors into elementary matrices,
permutations and diagonal units. Elementary substitutions are finite sums
of integral divided powers. Diagonal units preserve the weight components,
and permutations preserve the integral divided-power module. The
determinant twist is a unit because det(h_tau) is a unit, even when its
exponent is negative. Applying the same argument to the inverse proves
equality. This is the basis-independence content of nb_indep for Delta.

The definition of transLat therefore becomes simply

  D_lambda(H) = intersection_w A_w

inside the fixed vector space V_lambda. Localizing an infinite intersection
is not an unrestricted algebra rule, so a separate justification is needed.
The readable proof uses LP2, with a finite-support argument:

  * M_tau^vee is a finite full O_L-lattice for each of the finitely many
    tau. Choose d != 0 with
       d O_L^n subset M_tau^vee subset d^-1 O_L^n
    for all tau. Away from the finitely many prime divisors of d,
    M_tau^vee,w = R_w^n and hence A_w = Delta_std,w.
  * At each remaining prime, A_w is the image of a finite full standard
    lattice under an invertible L-linear map, hence is itself finite and
    full. A nonzero a_w in O_L can be chosen with a_w Delta_std subset A_w.
  * Multiply those finitely many a_w. If x belongs to V_lambda, clear a
    further denominator so that rx belongs to Delta_std. The resulting
    common nonzero multiple belongs to every A_w.
  * Every A_w is an R_w-module, hence is local at w. LP2 now gives
       (intersection_v A_v)_w = A_w.

This proves the exact localization statement. The source nb_loc proves
the same conclusion by a uniform commensurability bound with nabStd and
localized_iInf. The finite-support/LP2 presentation is a mathematical
simplification of that argument. It uses the same actual polynomial
space and Delta lattice and does not enlarge the lattice or invoke an
unproved commutation of localization with arbitrary intersections.

Using g_tau,w = j_tau(b)^-t gives

  F in D_lambda(H)_w
    iff F = rho_lambda((j_tau(b)^-t)_tau) F0
        for some F0 in Delta_std,w.

Evaluation transforms the transpose of j_tau(b)^-t into j_tau(b)^-1.
Combining the exact integral image from section 4 with the evaluation
identity from section 3 gives

  E_{lambda,1}(sigma F)
    = E_{lambda,iota(b^-1)}(Q), Q in IntSpan_w.

For the converse, evaluation injectivity recovers the underlying
coefficient-field polynomial equation and hence membership in the local
Delta lattice. This proves both directions of
mem_localization_delAtr_fn with every determinant factor included.

10. CV8-CV9: THE FINITE CLASS PRODUCT AND THE ACTUAL val_tied

The actual global integer lattice is the fixed-vector subspace intersected
with the product of D_lambda(H_c^#) over all class labels. At a fixed w,
local coordinate membership for each class gives a denominator s_c
outside w. The class set is finite because each step has finite orbit
set and the index set beta is finite. Its product s=product_c s_c remains
outside w. Multiplication by s preserves the fixed-vector equations and
makes every coordinate integral, so the resulting vector belongs to the
actual global lattice. This proves

  x in L_w iff every x_c in D_lambda(H_c^#)_w.

The source mem_locW_iff identifies its valuation-defined S_i(w) with this
ordinary coefficient-prime localization. There is no change of residue
prime, ramification scale or embedding in making that identification.

Apply the previous paragraph to every rational p_w-local basis b of
H_c^#. The forward direction supplies an IntSpan polynomial for each b.
For the reverse direction, choose at least one such b by the semilocal
basis construction. This last existence step is necessary: a universally
quantified implication over a nonexistent basis would otherwise be
vacuous. The field of coefficients is still L, and the all-frame equality
uses the same e_Y on underlying matrices.

The three resulting clauses coincide with the literal fields Fi, rat,
and lat of ValuationCompatibleChart. The val_tied proof also retains:

  - the actual class carrier from I.stp;
  - the same GStep stabilizer matrices and level characters;
  - the class lattice H_c and its actual Hermitian dual Lsh;
  - the original coefficient prime w and its residue characteristic p_w;
  - the prescribed embeddings embT = sigma o j_tau;
  - the actual determinant-shifted weight lambda;
  - every local integer condition, with okW identically true.

The proof is conditional on the supplied compatible charts and the listed
true column data, but the three value conditions themselves have now been
proved. In particular, neither rational surjectivity nor integral lattice
membership is taken from the name of a chart record.

11. CV10: TERMINAL ROW APPLICATION AND ITS METRIC BOUNDARY

In clsP_tie the row dimension is N_m-1. The row step has empty type-prime
decoration set. From the definition of L, all its type-prime equations
are absent, so L=N. The decorated ambient lattice N is self-dual by its
Ok condition, hence Lsh=dual(L)=N. This is the exact proof of Lsh_empty.
It is stronger than an identification only after tensoring with E.

The untwisted row weight is sgW and the actual frame weight is
sgW - m(t+1)/2. Their natW values agree. The source VV_shW and
delStd_shW are therefore exact equalities, while its frame determinant
shift remains that of sgT. The class frame equivalence eW is the identity
on matrix entries.

For the row chart produced by exists_cls, its supplied stabilizer range
and character identity give ptC by section 6. The rational descent of
section 5 gives rat. Applying section 9 at every coefficient prime and
then intersecting the local lattices gives lat. This global intersection
uses the elementary denominator-ideal argument: if x is outside an
O_L-module, the proper ideal {r:r x is in the module} is contained in
a maximal ideal at which x fails local membership.

The full source clsP_tie also proves an inverse-stabilizer-weighted
Petersson formula and NormPTie. Those invoke the separate
MetricTie.ipVal_step and MetricTie.norm_step theorems, compatibility of
conjugation at all embeddings (FOk), and the normalized Fock form. They
do not follow from the value statements here. In particular, CV10 is a
corollary for ptC/rat/lat, not a claim that the entire ValPTied record has
been reconstructed without those metric arguments.

## GF5 — Source restriction, target discard, and their different denominators

**Status:** Archival geometry and ranks; direct combined-codimension use is a proved derived alternative.

- Full source dimension is duS; retained source dimension is A.du, equal to rank Nu under NuFull.
- The exact retained rank is duS*(1-1/(T-1))^f and its relative loss is at most 2f/b.
- The retained target has Drows=(1-delp)*Dall, independently of the source loss.
- At a retained-source HN cut of fraction q, the full-source codimension is M-R+R*q.
- J.MuRank asserts positivity only, not equality with duS or A.du.

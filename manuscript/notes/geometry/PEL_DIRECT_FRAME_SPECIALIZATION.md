# Direct good-reduction tests on the common retwisted row

Fix a supplied marked row over its geometric field $F$, its natural sections, and the corresponding fine extension on which the small field, moduli, line and weights are copied. On the original good-reduction tests the following construction supplies the finite-residue comparison independently of an arithmetic compactification or a normal chart cover. It also identifies a concrete family to which the horizontal order calculation applies.

**Vertical tests.** Let $V$ be any complete DVR above a finite place $\lambda$, with a good integral framed point of the original row. For every finite list of section readings there is another good integral framed point over $\mathcal O_{L_\nu}$, where $L/F$ is a finite extension and $\nu\mid\lambda$, with exactly the same normalized readings, including zeros.

Indeed, the good witness and the witness carrying the integral frame are abelian and identify over $V$. Their generic identification extends by uniqueness of homomorphism extension for semiabelian schemes over a normal trait. The generic rigidified line comparison then extends on the abelian scheme: its vertical divisor is $m$ times the integral special fibre and restriction at the identity gives $m=0$. Keep the actual integral eigenvectors, the generator $\theta$ and the identification with the generic marked object. Spread the principally polarized abelian scheme, its rigidified line and a finite set of generators and relations for the CM action over a finitely generated $\mathcal O_{F,\lambda}$-subalgebra $A\subset V$. All these schemes, maps, isomorphisms and identities descend under filtered limits. The group law and polarization conditions persist at a sufficiently large stage, using descent of smoothness and properness, and shrinking about the image of the closed point for geometric connectedness and relative ampleness. The integral symmetric cubical structure on a rigidified abelian line is canonical by the theorem of the cube. The specified adjoint and graph identities may likewise be recovered on any specialized DVR from their generic rigidified identities. Indeed the source and target powers of the abelian scheme have integral smooth special fibre, so the same vertical-divisor argument applies along the identity. Cohomology of an ample principal line commutes with base change, with direct image invertible. Spread the generator and its trivialization of that direct image. Spread the comparison with the original marked generic object, including its actual torsion coordinates, over $A_F[1/D]$ for a nonzero $D\in A$. This uses only the finite diagram defining its point of the given generic moduli scheme and its isomorphism to the descended generic family. The torsion orders may divide the residue characteristic. Only the generic comparison is required. Evaluations of the chosen rules are regular functions on this reduced generic chart by their defining naturality on the universal framed point. There is an integral detail at ramified primes. Trivialize the Lie module and adjoin the entries of the given eigenvectors. For each embedding $\tau$ separately the integral eigenspace is the intersection of the Lie lattice with its generic $\tau$-eigenspace, hence saturated. A basis for it has a maximal minor which is a unit of $V$. Adjoin the inverse of **one such minor per block** to $A$ and impose the integral eigenequations for a $\mathbb Z$-basis of $\mathcal O_E$. Use the empty minor $1$ for a zero block. On any specialized DVR whose generic point stays in the given open chart, the resulting columns of each block generate a direct summand of the required rank. Their generic span is the entire $\tau$-eigenspace. Saturation then makes them precisely a basis of its intersection with the integral Lie lattice. Thus the specialized point carries a genuine integral eigenframe, even if the determinant joining all the blocks is a nonunit. The prescribed theta generator base changes as well.

Apply valued-field quantifier elimination over $F$ with its $\lambda$-valuation to the finitely presented ring $A$, its required integral coordinates and nonzero generic denominators, and the equations for the specified valuations of all readings. Use a uniformizer of $\mathcal O_{F,\lambda}$ to normalize the restriction to $F$. The chosen $V$-point solves this formula. A solution in the valued algebraic closure of $F$ has coordinates in one number field $L/F$. Completing at the induced place gives $\mathcal O_{L_\nu}$, hence a native good test with finite residue field. Its generic framed point is obtained by base change of the descended finite-type $F$-chart, which supplies the finite test descent in the definition of the native frame. [Robinson's theorem](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~marker/math512-f18/vf-4.pdf)

**Horizontal tests.** For a good integral trait with coefficient-trivial valuation, spread the very same data over a finite-type $\mathcal O_F$-subalgebra $A\subset V$, with the torsion comparison and actual framed point on $A_F[1/D]$. Let $\mathfrak p$ be the primary centre. As every $a\in F^\times$ has primary order zero, $\mathcal O_F\hookrightarrow A/\mathfrak p$. Chevalley's theorem shows that $\operatorname{Spec}(A/\mathfrak p)\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_F$ hits all but finitely many finite places. For each such $\lambda$ the centre has a specialization above $\lambda$. Apply the secondary DVR and one-variable argument of the preceding note over $\mathcal O_{F,\lambda}$ to this explicit chart, with $e_O=1$. It gives good native algebraic frames with the exact affine valuations $N d_i+c_i$ for all sufficiently large $N$. This direct spreading proof allows the exceptional finite set to depend on the particular frame, chart and section list. For the homogeneous horizontal inequalities it suffices to use one of the available places. **Point-mass short theta sections.** On the copied row take the original sections $S_u$ whose individual reading is $X_u$, including the normalization $1/(m_\Lambda N_0^d)$ for the fixed admissible auxiliary level $N_0$. Apply the construction to the entire finite list indexed by $u$. The local different bound previously established on finite-residue good tests transfers with its exact rational exponent to every good mixed-characteristic test. At a coefficient-trivial good trait choose one available place outside the level and discriminant, so all values at the approximating good algebraic tests are integral. Thus every $\operatorname{ord}_q S_u\ge0$. Moreover on **one** such sequence the eventually smallest valuations identify exactly the characteristics with smallest finite $d_u$ and, among those, smallest $c_u$. The valuation of any prescribed finite collection of linear combinations, with coefficients in $F$, can be tested on the same sequence by adding those sections to the list before constructing the chart. This retains cancellations in actual characteristic sums. The two geometric sides of the fine extension share these tests because the small marked model and its pointwise evaluations are identified. Extra large-field forms require their own field and descent data. **Theta generators on a degenerate trait.** One further part of the boundary comparison admits a direct test. Let $G/V$ be an already supplied smooth semiabelian model with reduced special fibre, principally polarized abelian generic fibre, and an integral section $\theta$ of the given line whose generic restriction generates. Then $\Gamma(G,L)=V\theta$ if and only if $\theta$ is nonzero at some geometric point of the special fibre. If its restriction vanishes, multiplication by a uniformizer $\varpi$ identifies $L$ with the kernel of the map from $L$ to its special-fibre restriction. Thus $\theta/\varpi$ is a further global section. Conversely, by flatness any global section is determined by its generic restriction $c\theta$. A negative valuation for $c$ would force $\theta$ to vanish on the special fibre. Reducedness converts nonvanishing of the restricted section to the stated geometric-point test. This proves the exact theta-generation clause of the integral test once the semiabelian family, line and a primitive section have been spread. It does not construct the family at the specified boundary centre.

On degenerate integral frames the [full semiabelian specialization](PEL_SEMIABELIAN_SECTION_TRANSFER.md) preserves the special-fibre extension and the primitive theta generator. The [marked chart argument](PEL_SIMULTANEOUS_SECTION_VALUATIONS.md) additionally permits an arbitrary prescribed finite place for a horizontal approximation. Selecting the common arithmetic square remains open.

{\Large\bfseries The torsion bound on a split column}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.79 \quad 25 November 2026
\end{center}

The coarse error in the denominator proof depends on invariant classes in
the quotient of a particular crystalline representation by its integral
lattice. We identify that lattice at a good point, compute its Hodge
jumps, recover the integral Wach height bounds and prove that an
explicit prime power kills all its invariant classes. This supplies the
coarse error bound on the selected hub. At the two type primes we
construct the local witnesses and sum their bounds across the good points
and places. Together these results give eleven fields of the fifteen-field
family conclusion. The large-prime argument below identifies the
remaining local hypotheses for its congruence estimate. The final section
computes the fields and jump windows at the ramified primes $q,d$.

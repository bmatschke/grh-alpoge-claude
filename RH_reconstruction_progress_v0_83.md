## The last middle-range rows

At an inert prime $p$, Frobenius orders the embeddings of the local field above $p$. The Hodge weights at an embedding specify a signed position in a cyclic word. A position is called *thick* if its weights reach the large range distinguished in the construction. The estimate bounds the cokernel of the map on finite local cohomology induced by each tested surjection of crystalline lattices. Here “finite” denotes the Bloch–Kato local condition. The length is measured over the actual coefficient ring $O$ and divided by the length of $O/pO$. Edition 0.82 treated split primes, inert row B and all-nonthick words. Two kinds of word remained. [Original case split](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/RowsCDRowConditionLocalBounds.lean#L62-L83) · [Earlier local and global comparison](continuation_v82/inertglobal.tex)

**All positions thick.** In this case Frobenius is complex conjugation, the completion has degree two, and the signs alternate. The two successive primitive cyclotomic levels give different divisibility tests on a single integral Wach vector. Comparing the Taylor coefficients at both levels saves a factor in the exponent proportional to the weight at the negative letter divided by $p$. After accounting for the exceptional generators, the resulting local length is bounded by $32\,\mathrm{coreH}+227\,\mathrm{coreF}+112\,\mathrm{coreM}$. These are the three nonnegative terms in the original row budget, whose coefficients are $368$, $2082$ and $22365$. Thus each place fits its own assigned row. [Two-level coefficient estimates, integral lifts and row comparison, T83A1–T83A6](continuation_v83/thick.tex)

**Mixed thick and nonthick positions.** Cut the word just before each nonthick position. There are at most two consecutive thick positions, so each arc has length one, two or three. The argument keeps the sign of every letter, including the initial nonthick one. Each local finite class breaks into arc classes. At a place of degree $d$, at most $4dm$ exceptional classes are needed in total, where $m$ is the central multiplicity bound. The remaining classes are represented by one-coordinate sections. Their proof has to lift each section through the *given* lattice map, since this is the map whose cokernel the row measures. ## Where to resume

The denominator link, $\mathrm{LinkN}$, concerns the actual weighted sum $\eta_j$ over the twisted places in the support of a member. It requires one constant $C$ and one cutoff $x_0$ for the entire family. For every $x\ge x_0$, every member satisfying the specified Tate–Shafarevich finiteness premise, and every permitted lift $j$, summability of the local terms $v_\ell$ gives

$$
 \operatorname{etaSum}(L,j)\le d_{j,0}
 \left[C\bigl(fmb\,\mathcal L^2+(\log x)^2 N_c\bigr)+\sum_\ell v_\ell\right].
$$

Here $d_{j,0}$ is the original multiplicity of the lift and $\mathcal L$ is the scale denoted by `L.sc.L` in the source. The place weights use the original coefficient field and ramification indices. They matter when an estimate proved after discarding unwanted eigenspaces is transported back to the initial lattice. [Definition of the sum, its weights and its quantifiers](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalTheoremInputs.lean#L24-L47) · [Multiplicity in the denominator sum](continuation_v83/excess.tex)

A83G3 applies the earlier denominator summation theorem to the table on this very family member. If $C_s$ is the common table constant in A83G2, the exported constant is $2000(|C_s|+1)^3$. This proves $\mathrm{LinkN}$ directly for a family carrying the stated $\mathrm{FactsF}$ and $\mathrm{TwGood}$ hypotheses. [Family assembly and export, A83G2–A83G3](continuation_v83/assembly.tex)

The good-cut route compares that result with the family selected in $\mathrm{Des}(\mathcal C,3)$. Its defining condition supplies $\mathrm{TwGood}$ directly. On each of its members $D$ with $x>\mathrm{Fm}.x_1$, compare the previously constructed data with $\mathrm{GaloisPreconditions}(D,\mathrm{nch})$ for that member's exact norm choice, and with $\mathrm{Tie4}(D)$. The latter records the paired character, a depth-zero datum with its eigenspan, the inert polynomial comparison and the local series. The good-cut interface requires a cut $c$ on this $D$ with $c.\mathrm{Good}$, the norm-choice facts for its transported choice, and $\mathrm{Tie4}$ on the cut. These identities let the denominator estimate on the cut family control $\operatorname{etaSum}(L,j)$ on the family one started with. This comparison is the next target for `GLOBAL_DENOMINATOR`. Individual components may already be available at earlier scopes.

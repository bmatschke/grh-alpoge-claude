MATHEMATICAL CHECKS

QR10. Finite character orthogonality gives the stated residue and the
10 B sqrt(x) counting remainder. The Mellin integral and every derivative
have locally uniform integrable majorants for Re(s)>1/2. Abel summation
has the precise residue s/(s-1); multiplying by s-1 only for the principal
character gives exactly the source F. The 300 B (|s|+1)^2 bound follows
from |res|<=4B and |mel|<=200B at Re(s)>=11/20. Conjugation for real
characters is valid with the same real logarithm on positive x.

QR11. All character transforms are finite and the Dirichlet series and
their differentiated series converge absolutely on smaller half-planes
inside Re(s)>1. The convolution gives L'=-GL with the correct sign.
H'= -G, so L exp(-H) is constant; its limit at positive infinity is one.
This proves nonvanishing without assuming an Euler product for LFam.
The integer von Mangoldt identity and zeta integral bound give exactly
|L(s)|>=((Re(s)-1)/Re(s))^2. The pole term in G is +1/(s-1) for the
principal character, followed by -F'/F, as required by the source.

QR12. Nonnegativity and counting give |a_chi(n)|<=14 B n, including the
zero coefficient separately. The large-t estimate C B exp(-t) follows
from sum n exp(-tn)=exp(-t)/(1-exp(-t))^2 for t>=1; it is a valid stronger
version of the source's exp(-t/2) estimate. The small-t character sum
has the exact B^11 factor. For a nonprincipal character the heat sum
is integrable at both ends. Its integral at one is justified through
Abel summation and Fubini with integral |A(x)| x^(-2) dx finite, rather
than by an unjustified absolute interchange of the original series at s=1.

QR13. The positive kernel W is integrable for every positive argument;
e^(-y/w)<=6w^3/y^3 gives W(y)<=12y^(-3). The termwise integral of the
expanded double heat series is W(m n v); the absolute sum is bounded
by a constant times B^2 v^(-3) sum m^(-2)n^(-2). This validates all
interchanges and grouping by m n. Only the nonnegative convolution
part of LFam.sq and c_chi(1)=1 are needed for I(v)>=W(v)>=W(1)>0.
The difference from (rho/v) integral Q is absolutely bounded by
C^2 B^22 sqrt(v) K0, and the displayed K0 is exactly 8/3. With
s=delta B^(-22), v=s^2, the error is at most W(1)/2. Dividing by
rho<=4B supplies the exponent 44+1=45. Constants delta,c_* are
strictly positive and uniform over all supplied LFam. The principal
case uses F_1(1)=rho>=1/B. No ineffective exceptional-zero input is used.

QR14. Every disk of radius 1/10 about |z-1|<=1/4 has Re(w)>=13/20 and
|w|<=27/20. The 300B growth estimate is less than 1800B there, and
Cauchy's estimate is therefore <=18000B. QR11 excludes real zeros
above one; QR13 excludes one. The entire interval [beta,1] for
beta>=3/4 lies in the derivative disk. The mean-value integral yields
1-beta >= (c_*/18000) B^(-46), with exactly one additional B power.
This is a polynomial separation conditional on LFam, not a fixed-width
uniform zero exclusion over unbounded conductors.

Revised detailed edition of *A human-readable version of the formal Alpöge–Claude proof of RH and Dirichlet GRH*.

The account of the arithmetic estimate now explains why divisibility, the period bound and the rank estimate must apply to the same family. Historical obligation identifiers remain available in the research records.

The detailed edition includes the construction of the ray-class character family and the short-interval estimate used to choose auxiliary primes. It also includes the selected base-row identities and the rational evaluations at the two levels.

The opening page and final chapter explain which proof-specific results still require another account. The bibliography gives examples of three reasons for citing the RH Lean Roadmap, including its role as a source of mathematical arguments. Edition 5 remains available at its original tag.

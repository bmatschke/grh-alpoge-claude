TAE: original theta analytic extension, v0.34
==========================================

Scope and result
----------------

TAE1–TAE13 give complete independent human proofs of the real analytic,
uniform-growth and lowering block of the original theta construction.
They preserve the original Weil action, original theta space, finite
level, infinity chart, frames, Fock scale, source measure and fundamental
domain.  They apply pointwise to one supplied original RealDat R on the
same Bundle used by the retained small-level rank route.

All thirteen new claims are category B implications.  No new category-A
published-theorem application is claimed.  The underlying Lean source is
assumed fully checked and correct.  The source checkout is verified
against commit 7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4, without a new Lean
build or replay.

The principal new endpoint is conditional but substantive.  Given one
simultaneous polynomial map io_i satisfying the literal IsIoW at every
parameter t, and LowSm/LowIrr for the same target DS model, the literal
lowest-type integral has every field of IsHolCuspKMap: continuity,
rational invariance, principal finite level, smoothness, one uniform
moderate-growth exponent, genuine cuspidality, compact equivariance and
lowering.  These conditions are proved for the actual integral, not
postulated as an analytic package.  Existence of that common io_i and
LowSm/LowIrr remains separate.

The independent blocks
---------------------

TAE1 starts with the already reconstructed CW8–CW11 Gaussian oscillator
formula.  The coherent coefficient a_f(v)=P rho(-v)f has every polynomial
moment integrable.  Repeated Heisenberg derivatives of the Gaussian
projector are bounded operators.  Expanding the oscillator integral
therefore supplies one integrable polynomial-times-Gaussian bound for
every group derivative and every smooth-vector seminorm on compact
parameter sets.  Dominated differentiation is carried out on the genuine
smooth domain, with difference quotients or closed-generator limits
justifying every otherwise unbounded derivative exchange.

TAE2 proves directly that every continuous scalar character of a matrix
Lie group is smooth.  Its restriction to the real line is differentiable
because an ordinary integral of the character supplies a differentiable
nonzero denominator.  Product exponential coordinates then give the Lie
group assertion.  This applies to the exact CW24 determinant-root twists.

TAE3 combines those facts with BTH14's full-action uniqueness.  Thus the
result is a derivative of the supplied original action.  It is not merely
a derivative of a canonically normalized substitute.  Arbitrary adelic
left factors require a separate argument: both the original curve and
its conjugate real curve are differentiable in supremum norm, and the
original L2 isometry identifies their derivatives.  Continuity and full
support of Haar measure upgrade L2 equality to pointwise equality.  The
result gives precisely the original exists_generator/IsGen contract and
its commutators.  This direct route bypasses the source's more explicit
nOp+lambda normal-form lemma; that stronger intermediate normal-form
statement is not claimed closed here.

TAE4–TAE5 prove the literal thetaKO_growth estimate.  A common finite
Heisenberg level turns an adelic integral bound into a real-cube bound:
the finite-level neighborhood gives positive transverse Haar volume.
Compactness and discreteness bound the number of rational translates
meeting the cube.  This avoids any unjustified restriction of an adelic
integral to a real slice of measure zero.  Covariant differentiation,
finite-dimensional word expansion and a repeated one-dimensional Sobolev
inequality give the exact source height

  normInf(g) = sum_tau,a,b (|g_tau(a,b)|+|g_tau^(-1)(a,b)|)

with exponent N=dim_R RW(T,S).  This N is chosen before the theta vector,
the adelic translate, the compact source set and the derivative word.
The zero-dimensional case is checked separately.

TAE6–TAE7 prove absolute convergence and dominated differentiation on the
original source fundamental domain.  Compact representatives are already
provided by the actual finite class presentation of BTH3.  They produce
a global dominating constant for each derivative curve.  Iterating the
actual generator preserves the same exponent N, while only the bounding
constant changes.  Closed right Lie derivatives give genuine smoothness
in product exponential coordinates.  Complex lowering derivatives pass
through the integral as the sum of two absolutely convergent real
derivative integrals.

TAE8 is the full finite polynomial commutator identity.  TAE9 proves
vacuum lowering by comparing the action of the same Cartan element on
the entire vacuum space.  Its additional original-data step is useful:
the stored IsThPVecOf vector is a Fock vacuum because IsThetaAt is an
AllSome statement.  Extend the derivative-at-zero functional
algebraically, form an ambient annihilator commuting with every finite
Heisenberg operator, and replace a place model M by M+A M.  The two
models have the same initial vacuum and finite intertwining laws.  Their
required common value forces A f=0.  This avoids differentiation through
an arbitrary noncontinuous algebraic place model.

TAE10 computes the actual lowering operator, including the sign and
scale.  With beta=omDir and the source's character sign -1,

  [D_u,D_v] = -2*pi*i*beta(u,v),
  s_tau = 2*sqrt(2*pi*|tau(xi0)|).

The mixed Laplacian has coefficient -4*pi*i/s_tau^2, with the explicitly
computed nu_ab in TAE.62.  The source Hermitian inverse and the original
two frames produce the required column Kronecker delta.  TAE11 proves
harmonicity of the literal Delta polynomial by disjoint positive and
negative column supports.  No unspecified scalar, changed Fock
normalization, or replacement polynomial is used.

TAE12 propagates Delta lowering first through the irreducible source
coefficient representation and then through each compact target factor.
LowSm and LowIrr are explicit assumptions.  DSModel.ds alone asserts a
lowest irreducible compact subspace; it does not definitionally identify
the supremum lowKSub with a single copy.  BTH1's smooth cyclic vector
does not replace that multiplicity-one proof.

TAE13 installs the exact analytic packet on the same original R.  Its
finite level proof uses the actual-action covariance already proved in
BTH5/BTH15, with the identity matched source lift and trivial reduced
blocks, followed by finite/real derivative commutation.  It uses no
earlier full multiplicity extension.  FIA8 and CTC9–CTC13 supply genuine
unipotent-integral cuspidality for every polynomial kernel.  The same
common io_i is quantified before all t; the stored OrZero choice is
activated only after such a simultaneous witness exists.

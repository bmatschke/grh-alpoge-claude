LemmaX generalized Bernoulli-unit assertion at the prescribed conductor.
BR6 proves the full UnramifiedCocycleIsCoboundaryOfBernoulliUnit interface.

Proof architecture
==================

BR1 proves the elementary dyadic generation used by Frobenius: if
Q=1+2^e a with e>=2 and a odd, powers of Q exhaust the units congruent to
1 modulo 2^e at every finite higher dyadic level. Averaging the conjugate
zero character sums then projects onto the values whose 2^e-th power
is one. A direct description by +/- powers of 5 proves the exact
character-kernel test needed at level 2^(m+e).

BR2 constructs an integral partial Bernoulli sum by a finite shift
calculation. Although its initial formula contains 1/(f*2^n), the
shift identity expresses every value using only 1/2^n and the original
character values. This is essential because l is permitted to divide f.
The complete endpoint contribution in the shift is written explicitly.

BR3 uses e=v2(l^d-1), m=n-e and s=f*2^e. The actual input inequality
f*4^e<=2^n gives 1<s<=2^m and m>=e. It supplies the residual character
sum and shows that its vanishing would imply 0=-2: the two exceptional
terms occur at 1 and -1, while the remaining sum cancels under the
primitive half-period element 1+2^(n-1). Since l is odd, this is impossible.
The auxiliary residue field is used only to test the l-adic norm.
There is no alteration of the residue field quantified in CorX.

BR4 first proves what the actual tame-index hypothesis says. On
I_A intersect ker(gamma), the cocycle is an additive homomorphism;
its image is an elementary abelian l-group whose order divides the
prime-to-l relative index. It is therefore zero. The subgroup H_A
need not be contained in inertia and can vary with A.

For F=Q(mu_N) in the original Omega, the restricted cocycle has finite
image because it is locally constant on a compact group. It therefore
cuts out a finite abelian extension of F. Actual inertia is the original
inertia intersected with G_F, so the extension is unramified. Since N>2,
F is totally complex; finite Hilbert class-field theory gives a map
from the ordinary ideal class group into the same k.

The Jacobi relation in OS1 annihilates that class group by its theta
element. BR4 extends it from 1<=a<N to every positive a prime to N,
using theta_(N,a)=theta_(N,r)+q theta_(N,N-1) for a=qN+r.
Twice the exact Eone group-ring element is -sigma_a^-1 theta_(N,a).
The factor 2 is canceled only after applying the map to k, where it
is invertible. Thus no general Stickelberger annihilation theorem is
retained as a new leaf. The inverse-conjugation convention is checked
against both Artin and the character scalar.

BR5 computes the scalar exactly:
sum_b Eone(N,a,b)*chi^-1(b) = (1-a*chi^-1(a))*B_(1,chi^-1).
The residual cyclotomic exclusion supplies an odd a prime to N for
which the first factor is a unit. The prescribed Bernoulli norm makes
the whole scalar a unit. The proof shows that an arbitrary unital
red:O->k has kernel the maximal ideal of the original O; surjectivity
and finiteness of k are never assumed.

BR6 applies the nonzero scalar to kill the cocycle on G_F. The quotient
Gal(F/Q) is abelian, and one nontrivial character value supplies the
explicit coboundary m=z(h)/(gamma(h)-1) in k. It is not necessary to
divide by the order of that quotient, which may be divisible by l.

BR7 checks all hypotheses on both characters. One CRT element fixes
mu_l and has value -1 under the product character and its inverse.
Inversion and the actual coefficient inclusion preserve primitivity,
order and parity. BR3 supplies both inverse Bernoulli units, and BR6
then proves the original CorX without any replacement of its fields,
characters, conductor threshold or local conditions.

Classical and earlier human inputs
==================================

The new classical registry has two entries: finite Hilbert class-field
reciprocity/existence and the finite cyclotomic Galois identification.
Both are sourced to the author's Milne notes and specialized explicitly.
OS1 is a previously proved human dependency, not a retained bespoke
input. This component uses only OS1's principal-ideal relation, whose
recorded classical prerequisites are elementary cyclotomic theory,
Chebotarev and the finite Hilbert class field theorem.

The actual dyadic nonvanishing, residue-map comparisons, finite-image
passage, tame-index argument, Eone/Bernoulli identity, inverse-character
join and final cocycle calculation are proved here. No Iwasawa main
theorem, Kato theorem, automorphy theorem, residual vanishing theorem,
or Bernoulli nonvanishing theorem is accepted in place of a proof.

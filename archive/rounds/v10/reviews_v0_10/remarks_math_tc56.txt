1. THE INFIMUM IS ATTAINED IN THE REQUIRED RANK SENSE

The defining set for kF contains 1: rank zero is allowed in FactorsLE,
and its real target mass is then zero. Every member is nonnegative,
so 0<=kF<=1. The normalized dimension d is nonnegative; in the
actual setting its denominator is positive because p>1,n'>=2,k>=5.

The report's finite-rank argument is valid without a compactness or
lattice-limit assumption. If T=(1-kF)d>0, then d>0 and
 delta=[T-(ceil(T)-1)]/d>0.
By the infimum property choose an admissible kappa<kF+delta.
Its integer certificate rank n satisfies
 n>ceil(T)-1,
and hence n>=ceil(T)>=T. If T<=0, take rank zero instead.
This can be done at each edge; RhoL allows a separate finite
certificate at each edge. In fact the same sufficiently close
kappa could be used before making the edgewise choices, since d
is independent of the edge. Neither interpretation exchanges an
infimum with a maximum or assumes vectors converge.

This matches LatticeCountPrimeMonoLevel.lean:rho_inf_mem,
kF_mem, kF_nonneg' and kF_le_one exactly. The argument also
covers d=0. TC5's wording that the infimum is admissible is
therefore justified for the literal kF, not only for an auxiliary
finite-dimensional relaxation.

The constants are fixed before p,n',m,TT,degree windows and ring
data. HypI itself does not assume the existence of an RS setting;
TC6 handles this correctly. If some setting R0 exists, choose it
after the numerical data and define one function kappa=kText_R0
on the selected integer degree families. It is nonnegative on

IaZ/IbZ product windows. TC5 gives its conclusion for every

If no RS setting exists, the choice kappa=0 has mean zero and
the universally conditional assertion about settings is vacuous.
This is the correct order for the existential kappa before the
universal coefficient-ring quantifiers in Conclusion2. Primality,
the even period and the relevant ring hypotheses come from an

as extra HypI fields. The displayed (a)–(d) source hypotheses and

REVIEWED MATHEMATICS

WA1–WA2. The actual lattice contains the completed host.AA multiplier.
The identity xi0 AA conjugate(AA)=absolute codifferent, together with
local dual bases for the two self-dual Hermitian lattices, gives exactly
the annihilator needed for the finite symplectic pairing. This is not
the bare Hom lattice. The source IsLatVac only directly fixes 2 Lambda
and split halves. The manuscript correctly obtains the sign on all
Lambda using the host's dyadic splitting, the two idempotent halves,
and the odd-place invertibility of 2. The sign factors modulo 2 Lambda.
Integer denominator clearing makes the full and half Heisenberg phases
roots of unity. The half factor is retained. Heisenberg unitarity and
self-duality prove off-lattice orthogonality and algebraic inner products.
Conjugate-linearity in the first slot gives the stated scalar-rigidity
formula when F'=cF; positivity supplies its nonzero denominator.

WA3. The finite-level projectors are valid. The semicharacter cancels
the Heisenberg cocycle, so epsilon(lambda)U(lambda) really factors
through Lambda/(2M Lambda). Perfect finite-lattice duality represents
its characters by (2M)^(-1)Lambda/Lambda. Translation reduces each
character eigenspace to the original vacuum line. Positive Fock theta
uniqueness follows from a symplectic integral basis, the Fourier
recurrence and normal convergence, as explained in VI6. The relevant
coordinate change is analytic and need not be algebraic. Thus the
finite translates form a finite algebraic model; moved-lattice equations
have algebraic coefficients and a one-dimensional common kernel.
Finite-dimensional row reduction gives an algebraic generator, without
claiming its comparison scalar algebraic yet. Commensurability permits
one M satisfying both inclusions used for the moved lattice. Covariance
through archimedean derivatives is justified by unitarity on theta L2
and difference quotients on a fixed smooth finite level; the argument
does not presume continuity of an arbitrary ambient linear operator.

WA4. A sufficiently deep stabilizer fixes the signs and hence the
vacuum line. Evaluation at a nonzero value gives continuity of its
scalar character. This is a GROUP quotient of a profinite compact
group, so its closed circle image is finite. The kernel is open and
contains a principal congruence. The result fixes the actual vector,
which is stronger than preservation of its line and is what WA9 needs.
The distinction between a group quotient and an arbitrary continuous
image of a totally disconnected space is respected.

WA5–WA6. The nonzero coinvariant is constructed, not assumed. Opposite
half-lattice operators distinguish each nonintegral half-translate
from v; their commuting product proves the needed linear independence
from the span. The counting functional is zero or one on these
translates. Finite smoothness supplies U(ny)phi=phi, so the geometric
operator sum kills each prescribed difference. Products still evaluate
positively on v because the identity summand survives. Thus v survives
in the coinvariant quotient. WA6's update has the nonzero denominator
epsilon(1-zeta), preserves normalization and half-invariance, kills the
selected complementary translate, and multiplies all prior values by
algebraic factors. Induction is over a finite list only. The literal
source's conjugate-half orientation and commutator signs agree.

WA7–WA8. ResEta kills each radial archimedean factor; continuous circle
characters supply integral angle exponents. Principal algebraic values
are therefore algebraic. Integral finite idelic units have finite image,
and ideal-class finiteness then proves pointwise algebraicity for any
finite idele. This does not imply a single field for infinitely many raw
half-normalized values. In WA8 the character factor is exactly the
crossed factor chi_T(x)^(-1) chi_S(y). The actual split clause applies
to the noncontinuous linear functional constructed in WA6: continuity
is not required by that clause. If omega(g)v=kappa F, then
kappa a=r z with a algebraic and nonzero, r positive real, z algebraic
of norm one. Together with |kappa|^2 algebraic this gives r^2 algebraic,
then r and kappa algebraic. Norm preservation alone would not prove
this; the supplied split normalization genuinely fixes the phase.

WA9–WA11. Covariance gives omega(g)A_v contained in kappa A_v.
Invertibility and the nonzero vacuum justify all nonvanishing claims.
Products, inverses and positive-power closure follow by scalar rigidity.
The local replacement for the group-generation source is valid:
R=I+(u-1)P has powers I+(u^m-1)P; for equal nonzero norms the formula
u=-conjugate(A)/A sends z to x when z-x is nonisotropic, and z+x gives
a two-reflection repair otherwise. Orthogonal induction works over
characteristic-zero local fields even at residue characteristic two.
At nonsplit places norm one makes u a unit. Finite local unit quotients
give powers arbitrarily close to one; the depth is chosen after
clearing the actual projection denominators. The inverse is integral
at sufficiently deep level. Positive-power closure then admits the
reflection itself. A finite adele has only finitely many factors
outside the prescribed principal level, and split factors are supplied
by WA8. This proves the actual generic finite-Weil algebraic-span
endpoint, without an analytic prime theorem or full new-Weil existence.
No all-embedding modulus or prescribed local-unit DictAt estimate is
inferred from this qualitative algebraicity.

WA12–WA14. The two standard datum functions use their own idempotents,
characters and vacua. Their bounded support and common period reduce
them to finite quotient functions. The finite Fourier indicator formula
and the actual IsThetaAt intertwining law yield algebraic Heisenberg
expansions with the half-phases retained. The second function is not
silently replaced by a Fourier transform of the first. Disjoint-place
covariance commutes finite action with polynomial creation. For the
actual grid, the fixed normal datum has the same half-point, delta,
frame and nonzero comparison scalar as TT13. Rational finite-place
approximation gives roots of unity, and VI9–VI10 supplies the actual
rational-translate germs. The exact homogeneous period powers cancel
against the prescribed Z.scal. TT7's stronger fixed-depth support
uniqueness, not an arbitrary union of finite sets, supplies one finite
set of entries for JetsIn. The finite field therefore has the literal
universal quantifiers, and NFJet needs no additional size threshold.

WA15. Checked the exact period-algebraic source interface and product
parity. WW has even total rank; neither source rank is separately
assumed even. The negative-source identity conjugates the entire
positive jet, including its scalar and period product. The actual
period-model comparison is by a NONZERO complex scalar c. The source
returns c:C^×, and does not give |c|=1 for its fixed period-normalized
entire model. This normalization point was corrected during review.
All arguments require only c!=0, so the corrected common nonzero
period retains c, all period factors, and the original half-point.
The other side has its own scalar and unconjugated product. Degree
zero and rank zero are compatible with this proof.

WA16. The class integral has the original positive Haar volume and
reciprocal finite stabilizer orders, with probability measure only on
the compact real factor. It does not assume rational Haar volume.
The same finite level fixes all finitely many datum functions and
commutes with creation, independently of degree. TT15's algebraic
compact projector and central determinant character give the surviving
degree. Finite generation is then a separate step: finite classes,
rational Fock substitution over one normal closure, and finite degree
support reduce all normalized integrals to a finite list over one
number field. Adjoining that list and repeating on the second side
proves exactly SumsIn for the SAME supplied bottom triangle. The
degenerate case where every integral is zero still has a nonzero period.

WA17–WA19. The standard lattices, Gram matrices, ranks and frames are
constructed and their self-duality is elementary. The bottom characters
retain their exact parity corrections and the original eta/twists.
New full Weil representations remain a separate existence input;
the grid's already supplied representations are not reused on different
spaces. The determinant-root adjustment is explicit: quotient characters
are trivial on F ideles, roots differ by an F idele, and rational roots
make the scalar trivial on rational points. Outside finitely many places,
unramified unit roots and unramified characters make it trivial on the
integral unitary subgroup. Local sections therefore give continuity
on the restricted product. The twist is deliberately crossed and
preserves every IsWeilDatumO field, including the same positive split
multiplier. Canonical eta-power full existence is retained precisely.
The final NumbersIn join keeps the separate toric critical-ratio field,
quantitative dictionary estimates and same-original-embedding/Choice
compatibility. It claims neither a replacement Choice nor an arbitrary
transport of fields across complex embeddings.

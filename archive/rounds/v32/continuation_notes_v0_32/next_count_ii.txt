COUNT II: NEXT ORIGINAL-DATA ARITHMETIC SUBTARGET
Read-only continuation note for v0.32; 22 November 2026

Status and scope

The next independent arithmetic target is COUNT_II_SUPPORT (category C): construct the joint ordinary-support ideal on each of the TWO actual rank-four Links used by QX4–QX10. This note proposes work; it adds no proof claim and changes no release status. The underlying Lean proof is assumed checked and correct. The remaining issue is a human proof of the exact producer, not a defect in the formal proof.

The useful first endpoint is the rank-four, actual-Link specialization of the source's Hida-plus-HasAut construction, followed by its P1-only support consequence on the very same ideal. In particular, the first support proof can target the NiceI/single-point branch of PFromAutOf, instead of reconstructing the additional P3core endpoint, which QX10 does not use. This is a substantive arithmetic task. A conditional proof from HasAut alone will not close COUNT_II_SUPPORT until the same ideal's Hida and HasAut producer has also been reconstructed.

1. Exact joint contract and quantifier order

Fix the original D and tower T with T.Fit and T.Deep, and fix the original complex identification iota : Qbar(ell) ≃ C. Choose ONE coefficient threshold c1 at this stage. For every later coefficient object c' containing both c and c1, use QX4's two original Links K and K'. They have the source's common kappa and distinct auxiliary primes, expressed literally by K'.kappa = K.kappa and NOT K'.s.p | K.s.p. They retain their own settings, levels, residual data, ordered local characters and maps. Each residual datum has rank four.

For EACH Link and EVERY actual universal ordinary determinant ring B and weight object Lambda for its residual datum, construct an ideal J0 of B satisfying, simultaneously,

  HidaPropsAlt(B, iota, J0, Lambda) AND P1core(B, J0).

There is one ideal per Link and permitted universal datum; the two different universal rings need not have literally the same ideal. The coupling is that every Hida field and P1core for a given Link hold on that Link's very same J0. The common threshold is chosen before c', before either universal ring/weight object, and before any original Space X. It may depend on D, T and the already fixed iota. Choosing a new coefficient extension after seeing X does not establish the required statement.

The Hida record consists of ring, dense, pt, end1, end2 and twist. These include the actual finite torsion-free weight-module quotient, arithmetic-point density, the point theorem, the two purity/classicality endpoints, and the prescribed finite polarized twists. They are not supplied by a generic ordinary-form existence assertion. QX5 supplies the actual Pair and maps into X.B; KX5/KX9 supply the weight object and universal ring in that setting. None of these constructions supplies the arithmetic ideal.

The exact P1 test is:

  for every actual Point x and O-algebra map f : B -> x.K[[t]],
  x.NiceI, B.IsPt(x,f), and J0 <= ker(f) imply
  for every prime q of B with q <= ker(f), J0 <= q.

Here x.K is finite, and Point contains the original residual determinant, polarization, inertia and ordered flags. NiceI means exactly N1 AND TypeI AND N3 AND N4, with the source's specified cyclotomic subgroup and auxiliary local condition. It is not an arbitrary irreducibility condition. Independent witnesses for “there exists J with Hida” and “there exists J' with P1” are insufficient.

2. Immediate producer dependencies and recommended starting slice

Start with THidaU.hida_unitary_of_printed_hida. Its conclusion HidaUAlt is stronger than the restricted rank-four target, but its proof exposes the required coupling clearly. It first constructs the original UnitaryHeckeData R, then selects J with R.IsJ B K J, and uses that same J to construct all six Hida fields AND Pa.HasAut B J. Keep this selected J visible throughout the reconstruction.

Its immediate source path is:

  HdEx -> ExistsHeckeIdeal ->
  TJRing, TJDense, HeckePolyCoeffZero, JEnd, JTwistA,
  and TLevelU -> THasAutOf.

HdEx in turn uses the original integral residual type, the weight-algebra character and the residual Hecke eigensystem (TTypeEx, TThetaEx, TResEx). ExistsHeckeIdeal constructs the map to the actual ordinary Hecke algebra and its kernel, with the source's alternative J = top if the relevant module vanishes. Do not silently assume the Hecke module is nonzero or replace its kernel with a separately selected support ideal. The incoming HypothesesH' and LiteratureRankNHypothesis must be unpacked against existing human certificates; their source names are not new published-theorem certificates.

For the support half, Pa.HasAut B J says that EVERY arithmetic Taylor–Wiles group T with its original TWPl realization admits TWAut(pairOf B.u, J, Empty, 4*[L+:Q], T). TWAut includes the common base, auxiliary eigenvalue lifts, the rings/modules at every level N and the compatible maps with a common exponent. The ideal parameter remains J throughout.

The recommended first support proof goal is therefore:

  On each original rank-four Link, for the Hida producer's J,
  HasAut(B,J) + the exact arithmetic side conditions
  => P1core(B,J).

Trace only the h = 0, NiceI branch of PFromAutOf.chain_of and pFromAut_of initially. The final extraction occurs at lines 123–126 of PFromAutOf. It still needs the source's six genuine ingredients: Norm, Choice, ArPl, FirstOrderDualArithmeticBound, TaylorWilesObstructionBound and Raw. Their roles are normalization with injective finite local coefficient extensions; detection and compatible eigenvalue/prime choices; arithmetic realization of the Taylor–Wiles groups; the two cohomological bounds; and the patched-support conclusion. This restricted slice avoids the extra NiceE/thick-prime chain needed for P3core; it does not make these six ingredients elementary or already proved.

PfromAut also assumes CohP, LocCFT, the coefficient-DVR predicate IsCoef, equality V.S' = s.S, positive rank, and a nontrivial residual cyclotomic value. CoefIsCoef.modU_of_printsH displays the exact assembly: it obtains Hida and HasAut on J, establishes IsCoef for c.O, derives the cyclotomic condition from the original noMu hypothesis via muL, and applies PfromAut to J. On our Links, hV/noMu/large and the local identifications are stored fields. These same-object and side-condition matches should be written explicitly rather than inferred from theorem names.

Do not impose ell > 3 without a separate treatment of ell = 3. HidaSetting requires only ell != 2, and QX8's existing transport covers odd characteristic including three. A rank-two Hida/control certificate does not prove this rank-four construction. Characteristic-zero adjoint vanishing (QX1) does not prove this characteristic-ell support assertion. An exact published replacement would need all of these hypotheses and witness identifications checked; until then the literature/source match remains D and the substantive producer remains C.

3. What a successful closure contributes

The whole joint producer, for both Links with the threshold order above, supplies the single open COUNT_II_SUPPORT leaf. QX10 then constructs, on every original Space X,

  J = image(phi0,J0)*X.B + image(phi0',J0')*X.B,

and verifies fin, p1, seed, pur and aut on that same J. This is the literal PMExAlt contract. YX2 already proves the implication from PMExAlt to the literal CountII, preserving its original fields, normalized lengths and uniformity. These two implications are completed human work; their ideal-existence antecedent is still open.

For RH and other targets of 2-power order, KA5–KA6 remove Case III only. Count II remains required, as do the independent Case I/Case V and remaining common global inputs. Thus closing this producer makes a direct contribution to the RH route; it does not by itself complete RH.

For unrestricted Dirichlet GRH, the same closure supplies Count II and the PMExAlt input also used in the selected Case-III route. It does not supply the additional original Ctx3 promodularity chain, SeedIVAlt, or their automorphy/image assembly. The shared rank-four producer is useful across both routes, but it cannot replace the separate Case-III obligations or be promoted to a rank-three theorem.

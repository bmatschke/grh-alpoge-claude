MATHEMATICAL FINDINGS
1. NC5 uses an actual basis-to-basis complex form. Finite-dimensionality
   makes its basis index finite. Coefficientwise injectivity gives a finite
   preimage of the finite generator set. Extending a nonzero annihilating
   L-functional to the given complex basis proves full rational span.
   The exact MtLat_sub and jMt_basis source fields suffice; no assertion
   that every complex vector is rational is introduced.
2. NC6 cancels localization in K' tensor_A V and proves spanning by
   clearing denominators in the supplied full A-lattice. It preserves
   both the dimension and the exact B-span of tensors, not just a rank.
3. NC7 matches B-bases of two full lattices over the same DVR. Minimal
   finite generators and a coefficient of least valuation prove the
   needed torsion-free freeness. Applying the field embedding to every
   change-of-basis determinant makes the every-basis clause valid.
   The map itself is correctly described as semilinear, not necessarily
   a surjective map of sets; its scalar extension is an isomorphism.
4. NC8 uses the zero map outside the source conditional proposition,
   and the exact rank equality inside it. NC9 applies this for precisely
   nonreference w and 1<=j<=m. Its scalar tower is the literal composite,
   and the coefficient character is inverted exactly when the side is
   false. Extracting NChoicePack applies the same involution a second
   time and recovers the original packet, including Q and character.
   All 21 source fields were compared with the displayed list.
5. NC14's nontriviality argument uses the order-15 local-unit clause of
   the distinguished character. At a prime over its supported rational
   prime, IsTwist's prime-norm exclusion forces the twist unramified.
   Thus cancellation cannot make the product trivial. Finiteness follows
   from the finite residue rings and the off-pl support law; conjugation
   retains residue characteristic and the chosen twist character.
6. NC15 uses nontriviality only where MuIsW assigns muAt=muTw. It proves
   that W is exactly the nonreference support, not necessarily the whole
   set of places over T.pl. Finiteness and conjugation stability follow
   from their exact supplied fields.
7. NC16 retains all ten MuIsW clauses and Q equality on precisely W.
   Each of the twelve TupTied fields matches the literal source. It
   needs neither k'=T.k nor an unstated identification between Galois
   psi0 and the Hecke character T.psi.
8. NC17 takes Q equality from Records for the same NChoice. The retained
   I.cf=Fr.at(X,f) and xi=I.xi give exactly the types and equality needed
   by colsTwPTiedS_std, whose other geometric/stage premises stay explicit.
   RankTie, MuIsW, readings/coverage and same-character LocsCarry are not
   silently discharged. There is no cycle using NChoice to create its
   own rank premise.

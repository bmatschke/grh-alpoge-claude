PURPOSE AND STATUS

This component follows the actual simultaneous standard-forms supplier,
formsTiedSym_std, and the subsequent Hermitian carrier suppliers. It proves
the constructions named in FC1–FC18 and joins them with CV9 and NB7–NB8 in
FC19 to construct the actual complete Forms0 and its matching level-zero
carrier, Joint0, StdTied, character rationality and companion data. The argument is
conditional on the literal supplied true-column record, TC.Ok, Pins, Laws,
Later, and the indicated realization of decorated lattices by unitary finite
adeles. It does not claim a complete proof of RH or GRH, nor a proof of the
existence of that entire arithmetic host. A source theorem being present is
never treated as a substitute for a human proof of its substantive input.

The current main.tex supplies mathematical proofs, including several shorter
arguments than the Lean source. The most consequential are the discriminant
module proof of functional replacement (FC4), the exact residue projection
formula (FC5), the chosen rational basis argument for all complex embeddings
(FC7), the reduction-image construction of GLevH (FC14), and the functional
proof of the actual diamond action (FC18). Their source counterparts and
dependencies are recorded separately in provenance.json. Full file bytes and
the exact inclusive declaration ranges are hashed and compared with git show
at the pinned commit. No Lean file was modified, compiled, or kernel-replayed.
The shorter replacement arguments have not been formalized in Lean.

The chart value construction CV9 is used only after FC3 has supplied ReadsK
and SF2 has supplied IdY. FC17 uses CV6–CV8 for localized integral polynomial
transport. FC19 is the assembly endpoint. This direction is intentional and
acyclic: there is no claim that CV constructs the primitive chart which it
assumes, and no claim that FC constructs all values before CV's argument.

NOTATION AND LITERAL PREMISES

E/F is the supplied CM extension with conjugation. The Hermitian pairing is
conjugate linear in the first variable; the dual is the O_E-valued Hermitian
dual, not a trace dual. At a selected level prime p=T(u), pi*bar(pi)=p and
(pi,bar(pi))=1. The selected primes are the prime divisors of pi. At the two
auxiliary rational primes q_j=g_j, the selected primes are oriented using
bar(piG_j), as required by GLoc.sp_pi. These two orientations must not be
interchanged. In the actual positive-space coordinates the Gram matrix is I
and the original coordinate frames are retained (SF2).

The supplied column laws include the actual finite orbit and finite stabilizer
properties, rational unitary actions, finite-order character values, and the
definitions and compatibility of their polynomial lattices. The separate
adelic-realizability input states that the particular decorated lattices in
those column classes have unitary finite-adelic representatives. AD1 provides
the particular diagonal base point kStd. AD3 provides a supported splice
once the old and new decorations have realizing points and agree away from
the conjugate pair. These are distinct hypotheses and conclusions.

FC1 — LEVEL-PRIME INDEX AND EXACT REAL RESIDUE FIELD

Status: proved arithmetic calculation under the stated CM/prime data.

If [E:Q]=2f, the equality pi*bar(pi)=p gives N((pi))=p^f. The f selected
distinct primes each have residue field of cardinality p, so the factorization
of (pi) has exactly those primes, all with exponent one. Their intersection
equals their product. This proves the full divisibility equivalence in
BasePi.dvd; a statement about support alone would not suffice.

Restriction of selected primes to F is bijective. Lying over and pi*bar(pi)=p
give surjectivity. The quadratic Galois conjugate-orbit theorem and
coprimality give injectivity. For residue descent use t=pi+bar(pi) and
z=y*bar(pi)+bar(y)*pi in O_F. The residue of t is nonzero. Inverting it
modulo v=P∩O_F gives a real-field lift of every residue of y. The induced
isomorphism k_P -> O_F/v is the literal restriction-compatible map, not an
abstract finite-field isomorphism chosen only from equal cardinalities.

The tauOf and seenP labels continue to use the supplied coefficient-place
correspondence. FC1 does not independently construct that arithmetic datum.

FC2 — COUPLING UNIQUENESS AND FINITE RESIDUE LIFTS

Status: proved for the actual reference tuple, whose twist-prime set is empty.

In a local reduction frame at an auxiliary prime P, the rational prime q_P
is a uniformizer and the kernel of reduction is q_P times the localized
lattice. Equality of the reductions of q_P*y and q_P*y' therefore implies
y-y' belongs to the relevant local lattice. Intersecting those conditions
gives equality of the coupling cosets. At non-coupling steps the set is empty.

Conversely choose global lattice vectors x_P lifting prescribed finite
residues. Finite CRT gives e_P=1 mod P with e_P/q_P integral at every other
auxiliary prime. Then y=sum_P(e_P/q_P)*x_P has precisely the prescribed
reduction family. This is an actual construction of the additional coset;
it does not invoke any global adelic realization theorem.

FC4 — NORMALIZED FUNCTIONAL WITH UNCHANGED LEVEL

Status: proved shorter replacement argument for charts_funKilled.

Over the completed integral algebra A at a selected real place put q=bar(pi),
L=A^(n-1)+pi A e_n and L#=A^(n-1)+q^{-1}A e_n. The discriminant module is
L#/L=A/pA via x -> q*x_n. A unitary matrix preserving L also preserves L#;
testing their coordinate vectors proves all entries integral, g_nj divisible
by pi for j<n, and g_jn divisible by q for j<n. Its action on the quotient
is scalar d=g_nn, and unitarity gives d*bar(d)=1 mod pA.

For a nonzero A-linear functional f:L#->M, killing L, with |M|=p, scalar
multiplication by pi is either zero or onto. In the second case q kills M.
Thus the functional factors isomorphically through either A/pi A or A/q A.
The conditions d=1 modulo these two ideals are equivalent by unitarity and
conjugation, and are equivalent to acting identically on all of L#/L.
Replacing f by x -> q*x_n mod pi therefore preserves its intersection
stabilizer with the unitary lattice stabilizer. It changes only fM/fT.
All local groups, global groups, characters, lattices, class representatives,
and stabilizer maps remain identical, so ReadsK survives. The new functional
is killed by pi. Dimension one is included: the off-diagonal conditions are
empty. The result is not true for an arbitrary zero replacement functional;
nonzeroness and prime cardinality are essential.

proves surjectivity. The same Bezout identity proves ker(pr)=P A_0, including
the nonlocal kernel assertion. Apply this to L# versus N and to L+P L# versus

The special te compatibility follows after multiplying a zero-functional
difference by p and reducing modulo P dnN. The IdL clauses on actual global
elements are identities by construction.

FC7 — FOCK METRIC, ALL EMBEDDINGS, AND TERMINAL NORM

Status: proved literal ipV_norm_tie; also the metric clauses for the supplied
terminal row chart used by CV10. This is not a proof of other spectral bounds.

The normalized Fock form is the weighted coefficient sum
c_lambda^{-1} sum_alpha alpha!*p_alpha*conj(q_alpha), with c_lambda the
product of squared Fock norms (self-pairings) of the highest polynomials.
Gaussian integration proves invariance under unitary frame substitutions.
Common highest-weight shifts preserve normalized weights and the denominator.
The actual convention is ipV(f_P,f_Q)=nfAll(Q,P), reversing the linear-first
Fock order. Orthonormal standard-coordinate frames have determinant norm one.

The subtle embedding step uses L^c=cmMax L, consisting of a for which every
complex embedding s satisfies s(star a)=conj(s a). Embedded E-images belong
there by FOk. Character roots belong there because FOk.sig_star at the
distinguished embedding gives star(zeta)=zeta^{-1}, and every other embedding
also maps a root of unity to a number of modulus one. Thus the subfield
Q(embedded E-images, finite character values) lies in L^c, regardless of
whether all of L is CM. All stabilizer action coefficients lie in L^c.

Applying L^c-coordinate projections on L to invariant-vector coefficients
commutes with every stabilizer matrix. The resulting L^c-valued invariant
vectors span the invariant space over L; select a basis. The actual source's
CSD.basis chooses a basis of this kind whenever HasRatBasis holds. It is
therefore legitimate to embed the pairings between those basis vectors using
complex conjugation. Only then expand arbitrary x in that basis, using the
usual complex quadratic form in its embedded coefficients. The argument does
not interchange s and star on arbitrary coefficients of L. In a different
coordinate basis transport the obtained complex form by its complex change
of coordinates; do not apply an unjustified naive star/base-change formula.

The combined matrix of all basis values is injective and has a left inverse
over L. The embedded matrix retains that left inverse, so the positive
weighted class Fock sum is positive definite. The standard Hermitian frame
has that Gram matrix. CV1 identifies a proposed polynomial expression with
the actual coefficients. These steps give the exact class-sum majorant and
NormTieG. Replacing an infinite-place representative by its complex conjugate
does not change the real diagonal Fock value. Terminal dimension N_m-1 and
its constant weight shift satisfy the same calculation.

FC8 — CONJUGATE-LINEAR PERIOD ISOMORPHISM

Status: proved all clauses of period_conj under the supplied period laws.

Coefficient conjugation, retaining the determinant twist, is a conjugate-
linear bijection onto the value polynomial space. Integer highest-polynomial
generators guarantee surjectivity. For rational unitary gamma and finite
character chi, gamma^{-1}=gamma* and conj(chi^{-1})=chi give the exact action
intertwiner. The source's period group is the whole group, so values on
preferred classes determine the section. Inverse coefficient conjugation
followed by orbit extension gives every class-invariant value; stabilizer
independence follows from the intertwiner and injectivity. Termwise in the
weighted class sum, conjugating polynomial coefficients converts the Fock
Hermitian pairing to the required bilinear coefficient pairing, yielding
period(w,F)=ipP(jM F,w). Positivity follows from one nonzero class polynomial
and positive inverse-stabilizer weights. No new period-law existence is
inferred from this construction.

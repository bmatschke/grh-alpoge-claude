2. RECOMMENDED FIRST SUBTARGET: THE ORIGINAL UNITARY GROUP DICTIONARY

Produce a short, complete certificate identifying the literal matrix

points of the algebraic unitary group U(J) over F = D.host.F, where
E = D.host.E, sigma = D.sp.cm.sig, and J = W.gram.

The exact tasks are:

  * Construct U(J) over F with functor of points
      {g in GL_n(A tensor_F E): sigma(g)^T J g = J}.
    Identify its rational, finite-adelic, infinite and full adelic
    points with the source objects, preserving multiplication and the
    left rational action.
  * Check that scalar extension to an algebraic closure is GL_n;
    hence the group is connected and reductive. Handle n = 0 directly.
  * Prove that its F-rational character group is trivial. For n > 0,
    geometric characters are determinant powers, and the nontrivial
    CM automorphism sends the determinant character to its inverse.
    Spell out why an F-defined character is therefore the zero power.
  * Match the restricted-product topology, integral compact opens,
    Borel structure and Haar measure, not only the abstract group.
    For a real theorem over Q, also write the restriction-of-scalars
    identification. The archimedean center is a product of circles.

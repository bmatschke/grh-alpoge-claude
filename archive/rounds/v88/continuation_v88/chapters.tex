{\Large\bfseries The weighted period error and the rational row lattice}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.88 \quad 25 November 2026
\end{center}

A word and a lift specify a weighted sum of local errors. Its main
term comes from exactly two primes. We bound the total difference
from that main term at every late member of the selected family.
We also identify the rational row space at every tuple used by the
same member. This proves full rank for its row lattice and identifies
the rational values of its Bessel pairing.

# A human-readable reconstruction of RH and Dirichlet GRH

**[Read the consolidated manuscript](../manuscript/overview.md)** · [Read the typeset manuscript and supporting proofs](editions/edition-4/overview-with-appendices.pdf) · [Proof of the last two inputs](../manuscript/notes/geometry/COMMON_FAMILY_AND_PERIOD_HEIGHT.md)

The manuscript explains how forced divisibility of theta periods bounds a smoothed prime sum and why an upper bound on arbitrarily distant intervals rules out a zero to the right of the critical line. It covers the Riemann zeta function and Dirichlet characters of every conductor. It cites the longer geometric, automorphic and Galois theorems at the points where their full strength enters.

The fourth draft explains [how Selmer groups control the denominators of theta lifts](../manuscript/notes/galois/HECKE_CONGRUENCES_AND_SELMER_LENGTHS.md), proves a [covolume bound at the degree used in the tower](../manuscript/notes/geometry/CHOW_DETERMINANT_AT_THE_TARGET_DEGREE.md), and works out the [overlap between two different periods](../manuscript/notes/theta/MIXED_PERIODS_AT_THE_CONSTRUCTED_RANKS.md). The exact number of words gives a [sharper bound for the mean slope](../manuscript/notes/theta/CENTERED_PERIOD_RANK_AND_SLOPE.md#the-factor-three-in-the-selected-code). The paper also gives the analytic window argument for all conductors.

All nine period-height clauses and the common family required by the original construction are now accounted for at their endpoint uses under the stated source theorems.

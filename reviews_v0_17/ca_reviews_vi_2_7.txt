Mathematical findings
---------------------
VI2. The Hom lattice is self-dual for tr(TU*) using local perfect bases at
paired conjugate primes, with no global or orthonormal integral basis premise.
AA0 times its conjugate equals the relative codifferent, so the stated K-trace
ideal dualities follow. The alternating formula has the correct sign/factor:
2 Im(t)/sqrt(d)=Tr_K/Q(t/sqrt(-d)); the actual area denominator supplies Norm(c).
Testing every O_K multiple is necessary and sufficient. The additional argument
recovering rational coordinates for a candidate in the real alternating dual
closes the rational-versus-real gap. Multiplication by integral theta gives
sqrt(d) Re H=2k-e, hence integral q and the exact semicharacter parity. The source
rounded multiplier is used only on its integral domain and is identified exactly.

VI3. The three unkilled half-point conditions give the two independent nonzero
classes omega0 and theta omega0 modulo 2 Lambda. Nondegenerate alternating
pairing forces q(omega0) odd, and Norm(theta)=(1+d)/4 is even, giving the second
value even. The characters agree on the F2 basis and on 2 Lambda. Verified all
ten literal StdData fields: per, half, isom, alpha0, betaStd, betaSemi, betaAlpha,
selfDual, idx1 and idx2. In particular betaAlpha is an exact identity on the full
four-block space, and finite positive indices are supplied before the Fourier sum.

VI4–VI5. The Heisenberg product has the correct negative pi phase; the positive
intertwiner has quotient direction [Lambda2:intersection]. Common semicharacter
and integrality give actual representative independence. Finite perfect-pairing
orthogonality proves equal indices and exact inverse, not inverse up to a phase.
For normSq, the Gaussian-weighted translation identity is correct, the common
finite cover has the same normalized mean, and distinct quotient translates
have distinct characters and orthogonal cross terms. The normalization square
is exactly 1/r. The pinned normSq_eq and IopIso definitions match this normalized
mean. This is the precise positive-Iop input used by CA6; it does not assert
arbitrary three-lattice positive-Iop composition, whose signs CA4–CA5 address.

VI6. The same half-point normalization gives the nonzero standard theta with
one simple zero per period parallelogram. The quotient of another function by
it is elliptic with at most one simple pole; the residue theorem removes that
pole, and periodic boundedness makes the quotient constant. Coordinate slicing
then proves the full product theta line. No multivariable vacuum theorem is
assumed to establish the line needed for that theorem.

VI7. The explicit kernel4 is the positive intertwiner from the standard lattice
to the actual g(lamM^4), evaluated at g z, exactly as in the source. Finite Fourier
inversion and the actual nonzero kappa make the four-copy vector nonzero. A nonzero
slice lies in the one-copy theta space, and tensoring it four times gives a
nonzero scalar multiple of the four-copy generator. Taking a fourth root of that
single scalar constructs the desired vector. The text correctly makes no claim
that this intermediate scalar/root is algebraic over Q. Mixed tensors prove the
one-copy line and mu4 ambiguity. The actual epsilon-chosen thMM then satisfies
its own predicate; it is not replaced by an unrelated normalized vector.

# Finishing the six shared obligations

Start with the common theta construction below. In parallel one can work on the period, counting and spectral estimates. Each of these estimates is a theorem about the actual family with a uniform range of scales. The [machine-readable inventory](SELECTED_FAMILY_CHECKPOINT.json) gives the exact six identifiers and the verifier. The [selected-family proof](LOCAL_SELECTED_FAMILY.md) explains the distinction between extracting information from a linked bundle and producing that bundle in the first place.

## First target on the common theta construction

At a prescribed stage, the arithmetic construction has fixed its coefficient field, its lattices, the level and the finite Hecke action. Produce the real theta datum `exists_realDat_keep` on those objects. Assemble the archimedean chain and Fock models, choose the one global additive character with the prescribed sign, and match the two marked-prime characters through the actual residue-field identifications. Use the finite Hecke comparison already proved in H78K6. The test for completion is that one datum retains the original coefficient embeddings, group identifications and the decorated reading at every indicated place. This will supply an input to the subsequent choice of the theta intertwiners.

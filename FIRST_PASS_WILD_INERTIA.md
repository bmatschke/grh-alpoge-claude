# A single coefficient request for the wild-inertia choices

Fix a residual Hecke block. Write $O$ for its coefficient DVR, $K$ for its fraction field, $\ell$ for its residue characteristic, $X$ for its Hecke points, $Z\subset X$ for the prescribed decomposition points, and $T\subset O^X$ for the integral Hecke algebra. Liveness requires one $\pi\in Z$ with a compatible integral Galois representation of dimension $n=r+t$, a generic decomposition into an $r$-dimensional representation and the specified $t$ characters, and absolute irreducibility of the reduction of the $r$-dimensional factor. The [pointwise comparison](FIRST_PASS_POINT_COMPARISON.md), [coefficient splitting](FIRST_PASS_GALOIS_COEFFICIENT_MODELS.md) and [decomposition at every point of the full theta locus](FIRST_PASS_LIVE_BLOCKS.md) supply the hypotheses on this block, together with split Frobenius density. This theta locus is the locus of a prescribed character string. The congruence-length calculation uses its good subset. Under these hypotheses a finite set of numbers at this block suffices for all subsequent compatible localizations to recover the inertia and idempotent choices there. [Precise quantifiers][chosen] · [Active construction][active]

These inputs give a single integral family $R_x$ on $X$, auxiliary representations $\rho_x$ for $x\in Z$, and a local algebra $T$ separating the points. Characteristic coefficients of $R_x(g)$ belong to $T$ as functions of $x$. Each $R_x\otimes K$ is a sum of pairwise inequivalent absolutely irreducible constituents with full matrix span on each constituent. At every point of $Z$ the reduction of $\rho_x$ is absolutely irreducible. For the last assertion, cancel the $t$ prescribed characters from the common residual character polynomial. The residual traces of $\rho_x$ then agree with those of the live witness on every group element. If matrices of one representation span $M_r(k)$, choose $r^2$ group elements giving a basis. The Gram matrix of these matrices for $(A,B)\mapsto\operatorname{tr}(AB)$ is invertible in every characteristic. The same Gram matrix occurs for the other representation, by evaluating the traces on products of the chosen group elements. Its matrices also span $M_r(k)$. This supplies the required absolute irreducibility. [Hida setting][hida] · [Production from one live witness][fromlive]

## One inertia element and its character values

Put $s=\max(1,v_\ell(Q_S))$. The host conductor conditions give $s\ge2$ at $\ell=2$. For a chosen place $w\mid\ell$ the local cyclotomic character on inertia attains $1+\ell^s$. Indeed, inertia over $\mathbf Q_\ell$ acts through all units on the cyclotomic tower. An element with this value fixes both the maximal unramified extension and the $\ell^s$-th roots of unity. Their compositum contains the specified local host field, so the element corresponds to some $\sigma\in I_w$ in the chosen global algebraic closure. [Depth and conductor][depth] · [Local to global cyclotomic comparison][cyc]

When $\ell$ is one of the two marked rational primes, take the pro-$\ell$ component $g_0$ of $\sigma$ in the closure of its cyclic subgroup. In each finite cyclic quotient this is the power with exponent $1$ modulo its $\ell$-part and $0$ modulo its prime-to-$\ell$ part. The congruences are compatible under quotient maps. In particular $g_0$ remains in $I_w$; and at **every** $w'\mid\ell$ at which $g_0\in I_{w'}$, its image in the prime-to-$\ell$ tame inertia quotient is trivial. This is the wild-inertia clause. The leading cyclotomic congruence is retained: powers with exponent $1\bmod\ell$ take $1+\ell^s$ to $1+\ell^s\bmod\ell^{s+1}$. Thus $u=\varepsilon_\ell(g_0)$ satisfies $v_\ell(u-1)=s$. Away from the marked primes the definition requests only inertia and that same exact depth, so $\sigma$ works directly. This construction uses one $g_0$ for every $\pi\in Z$ of the initial block. [Profinite power construction][crt] · [Wildness at every applicable place][wild] · [Unmarked case][unmarked] · [Serre, *Local Fields*, Chap. IV](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4757-5673-9)

At $g_0$ the prescribed characters have values $a_c=a_1u^{-(c-1)}$, $1\le c\le t$, with the scalar embedding into $O$ understood. For $h>0$,
$$
 v_\ell(u^h-1)=s+v_\ell(h).
$$
A prime-to-$\ell$ power preserves the leading term up to a unit by the geometric-series identity. Each $\ell$-th power raises its valuation by one, by the binomial expansion with $s\ge2$ in the dyadic case. It follows that these eigenvalues are distinct in $K$, although all have the same reduction. The length of $O/(\prod_{c=2}^{t}(a_1-a_c))$, divided by $v_O(\ell)$, is $(t-1)s+v_\ell((t-1)!)$. [Character law on the original block][vals] · [Valuation computation][length]

## The integral idempotents and perturbation

Fix $\pi\in Z$ and write ${\cal R}$ for the $T$-span of the matrices $x\mapsto R_x(g)$. This is an algebra by the group law. Absolute irreducibility modulo the maximal ideal gives full matrix span for $\bar\rho_\pi$, hence for $\rho_\pi$ over $O$ by Nakayama. In the paired algebra of matrices of $\rho_\pi$ and the character coordinates, lift $E_{11}$ and $E_{12}$ in the matrix coordinate. Their commutator is $(E_{12},0)$ because the character coordinates commute. Multiplying on the left and right by lifts of matrix units gives $(P,0)$ for every $P\in M_r(O)$. Here $r\ge2$. The corresponding linear combinations of the global family realize the required identities at $\pi$ in its generic decomposition. [Matrix-unit calculation][span]

Using $P=1$ and subtracting from the identity gives $b\in{\cal R}$ acting at $\pi$ as the projector onto the whole character summand. One can replace $b$ by an idempotent in ${\cal R}$ with the same value there. Indeed in every finite quotient modulo $\ell^a$ the powers of $b$ are eventually periodic. The powers $b^{m!}$ eventually equal an idempotent in that quotient. Their compatible limits give the desired idempotent $e_B$, because the finite integral matrix algebra and its $\mathbf Z_\ell$-submodules are closed in the adic topology. Similarly lift $P=E_{11}$, compress the lift by $1-e_B$ on both sides, and take its idempotent limit $e_1$. Thus $e_1e_B=e_Be_1=0$, and $e_1(\pi)$ acts with rank one on the $\rho_\pi$ part. Both idempotents have constant ranks on $X$: their characteristic coefficients lie in the local algebra $T$, whose evaluations have the same reduction (the evaluations split the scalars). The reduced characteristic polynomial of an idempotent of rank $d$ is $Y^{n-d}(Y-1)^d$, which recovers $d$ even in residue characteristic dividing $d$. Thus the ranks of $e_B,e_1$ are $t,1$. Their sum need not be the identity. [Idempotent lifting][powers] · [The two compatible lifts][idem] · [Constancy of rank][ranks]

For any prescribed integer $L\ge0$ choose a uniformizer $\varpi$ and set
$$
 X_0=e_B R(g_0)e_B+\varpi^L S,\qquad
 S\in(\ker(\operatorname{ev}_\pi:T\to O))\,e_B{\cal R}e_B .
$$
Then $X_0(\pi)$ acts on the character summand by $\operatorname{diag}(a_1,\ldots,a_t)$. We may choose $S$ so that $X_0(x)$ has simple spectrum on $\operatorname{im} e_B(x)$ for every $x$. To see existence, work first over $K$. For $x\ne\pi$, an element $f-f(\pi)$ of $T$ can be chosen nonzero at $x$. The split multiplicity-free representation algebra on this generic fibre contains independent full endomorphisms of the distinct constituents. Because $e_B(x)$ lies in that algebra, its compression contains a matrix with distinct eigenvalues on the whole $t$-dimensional image: choose distinct diagonal entries on the constituent subspaces of this image. Clear denominators, multiply by $f-f(\pi)$, and compress on both sides. This gives an allowed perturbation direction at $x$ with simple spectrum; scaling by $\varpi^L$ preserves that property in $K$. The discriminant of the shifted operator is therefore a nonzero polynomial in the coefficients of the perturbation directions (use the top coefficient along this direction). At $\pi$ its constant value is already nonzero. The product of these finitely many discriminants is a nonzero polynomial. Since $O$ is infinite, one common tuple of integral coefficients avoids all its zeros. The matrix changes by an element of the specified ideal, while $g_0$ itself remains fixed. The active construction chooses $L=0$; it requires exact agreement at $\pi$ and simultaneous simple spectra. [Corner algebra on inequivalent constituents][dense] · [Integral separation and simultaneous choice][corr] · [Assembly for any prescribed $L$][choice]

## Capturing the roots before later localizations

For each marked $\pi$ and each $x\in X$, take the polynomial
$$
  P_{\pi,x}(Y)=\det(Ye_B(x)-X_0(x)+1-e_B(x)).
$$
It acts as the identity on the kernel of $e_B(x)$ inside the determinant, so it is monic of degree $t$, with $t$ distinct roots. Embed its coefficients in $\mathbf C$ by the selected coefficient embedding. The finite union $S_i$ of these complex root sets, over all $(\pi,x)\in Z\times X$, witnesses Chosen. In particular $|S_i|\le t|Z||X|$. The character values at $x=\pi$ are shared as $\pi$ varies; thus for $Z\ne\varnothing$ one also has $|S_i|\le t(1+|Z|(|X|-1))$. The selected roots are algebraic over the embedded local coefficient field. Membership in this request means that a later block's specified embedded fraction field contains them. [Roots in the selected embedding][root] · [Active choice of $L=0$ and the finite union][active]

Take a first realization satisfying the preliminary duties. Enlarge its coefficient request for Hecke compatibility, so that every block $i_1$ in any subsequent realization matches an old $i_0$ over the same coefficient place. At each $w$, further adjoin the sets $S_{i_0}$ from the finitely many old blocks over $w$ in rows $1,\ldots,m$, choosing the empty set if no witness for Chosen exists. This request is made before the choice of the subsequent realization. If $i_1$ is live, then $i_0$ is live. Indeed use its old good representation and the assumed decomposition at the corresponding marked point. The traces of its full representation and of the new live witness agree after scalar extension by Frobenius density and compatibility of Hecke polynomials. Cancel the known characters in the two traces, then reduce. The trace-pairing argument above proves that the old smaller residual representation spans the full matrix algebra after extension of the residue field, and thus also before extension. This proves liveness at $i_0$ and guarantees that its requested set is a witness. [Descent of a live point][descent] · [One request for every later live block][ev]

Write $\phi:O_0\hookrightarrow O_1$ for the comparison, $e:X_1\simeq X_0$ for the point bijection and $\gamma:\Gamma_1\simeq\Gamma_0$ for the Galois identification. For the descended model use the old DVR $O_0$, the pullback of $T_0$ by $e$, the old matrices composed with $e,\gamma$, and $g_1=\gamma^{-1}g_0$. The equalities on split generators show that $T_1$ is the $O_1$-span of the image of this algebra. The coefficient and Galois comparisons preserve the defining traces, Frobenius, inertia, wild inertia, cyclotomic character and the $t$ characters. Consequently the idempotents and corrected matrix over the old DVR still satisfy every choice identity. For each $\alpha\in S_{i_0}$ choose its preimage in $\operatorname{Frac}(O_1)$. The polynomial factorization into these preimages follows by applying the injective specified complex embedding to both sides. Since its polynomial is monic over $O_1$, each root in the fraction field is also integral over $O_1$ and belongs to $O_1$. This proves precisely TakenWild on the later block, together with a good Galois family. It allows the old DVR as the common ring of definition while the new DVR supplies the splitting. [Coefficient comparison][loc] · [Integral Hecke algebra and Galois base change][ext] · [Transferred witnesses and roots][take]

The actual embeddings even allow a single complex number for this whole finite request on the fixed state. Let $W$ be the finite set of active coefficient places, and take $U=\{u_0,\ldots,u_{q-1}\}$ to be the union of the requested root sets and the preliminary Hecke-coefficient requests on $W$. For $w\in W$, set $k_w=\iota_w(\mathbf Q_{\ell(w)})\subset\mathbf C$, with the prescribed place isomorphism $\iota_w:\overline{\mathbf Q}_{\ell(w)}\simeq\mathbf C$. Every realizing fraction-field image over $w$ contains $k_w$: the pinned compatibility on $\mathbf Z_{\ell(w)}$ extends to its fraction field. The field $k_w(U)$ is finite separable over $k_w$, since every element of $\mathbf C$ is algebraic over this particular embedded $k_w$. For distinct $k_w$-embeddings $\sigma,\tau:k_w(U)\to\mathbf C$, the polynomial
$$
  \sum_{j=0}^{q-1}\bigl(\sigma(u_j)-\tau(u_j)\bigr)T^j
$$
is nonzero. Choose one integer $a$ outside the finite union of its root sets over all such pairs and all $w$. Then $z=\sum_j u_ja^j$ has $[k_w(U):k_w]$ distinct images under these embeddings, so $k_w(z)=k_w(U)$ for every active $w$. Requiring the same singleton $\{z\}$ at each active place is equivalent to requiring the common union $U$ there and implies all original place-dependent requests. When $U=\varnothing$ the original empty request suffices. This argument counts requested numbers; the degrees of the resulting coefficient extensions can still depend on the state. [Scalar embedding and the request clause][scalar] · [Actual automorphic coefficient maps][field]

If the later application requires an ordering, at the distinguished point label the roots by the actual $a_c$. On the other points one may use all permutations of the $t$ roots, as in the pinned splitting algebra. Dividing the elementary eigenprojector numerators by root differences can introduce denominators in $O_1$. The construction above supplies integral $e_B,e_1$, integral roots and the stated normalized length of that first-root denominator. [Ordered-root construction][order] · [Splitting algebra and first-coordinate factors][split] · [Three conclusions of the general construction][finish]

**Combining the requests.** First impose provision 41 and the residual and cuspidal comparisons on the fixed first-pass state. Construct the preliminary models above. Then take the pointwise union of the requests for their transport and roots, the two original coefficient duties, and the request for the [theta congruence lengths](FIRST_PASS_TYPE_PROJECTION.md). Each automorphic semisimple realization meeting this union satisfies all three clauses of the source's large-coefficient condition. Its first clause supplies the integral hypothesis used in deriving the length comparison. Thus all the row and place equalities hold on this same realization. The [automorphic existence theorem](FIRST_PASS_CONGRUENCE_LENGTHS.md#realizing-a-request-on-the-constructed-first-pass) supplies at least one.

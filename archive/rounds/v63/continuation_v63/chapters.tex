{\Large\bfseries Vanishing on the original Case V host}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.63 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The rank-three count uses a vanishing statement on every nonpositive
matrix weight. We prove it for every frame permitted by the arithmetic
host. At a prime away from $\ell$, the action of Frobenius forces an
inertia homomorphism in negative weight to vanish. At weight zero the
class-one condition supplies the global input. The final calculation
reassembles the scalar primitives into a single integral matrix
after clearing denominators.

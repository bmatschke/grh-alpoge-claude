# Finding the longer arguments behind the Roadmap outlines

The RH Lean Roadmap lists 63 Part VIII statements for which it designates the proof as an outline. Each has two locations in the book. The first states the result and summarizes the argument. A later subsection gives a longer derivation and names the formal declarations used there. The links below go directly to those later subsections in version 41. A link identifies where to review an input. The degree of detail differs from one proof to the next. [Source index](https://github.com/rh-lean-roadmap/rh-lean-roadmap/blob/c7755c1086f05d0918ee8496d95912e4f35e22f9/docs/outline-proofs.md)

# Independent review of the seed twist and quadratic restriction

Manuscript SHA-256: `1d08aab740142e27c8d1f51730194fc260892de0fae038ed58ab3e4ce7395b16`.

I checked E65T1–3 against the six original Tw4 fields in TnOne.lean, the TwEx4 and AlNe quantifiers in OverOf4.lean and the implementations in EqOfRd.lean and PAlNe.lean at original source commit `7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4`.

* E65T1 takes the unique root of the principal-unit character congruent to one. Uniqueness implies multiplicativity, and the sum of two chosen roots is a unit, so the squaring map preserves the relevant congruences. For either order of the label, the square of the twist times the labelled character is the original multiplier. In the reversed ordering the additional factor is exactly the square of the finite character P; its Teichmüller factor has even exponent 2e divisible by ℓ−1 and its remaining factor changes the inverse finite character to the required finite character. These identities permit negative integral exponents.
* E65T2 has raw labels (0,1,2,3) or (2,3,0,1). The outside diagonal product is the same in both cases, and the last diagonal reduces to one or to the reduction of P as appropriate. On the source intersection of the full cyclotomic and residual kernels, prime-to-ℓ torsion reduction is injective on both finite characters. This proves the kill condition in the coefficient DVR. The open intersection of the Teichmüller and finite-character kernels witnesses the original weight clause.
* E65T3 restricts the quadratic root to the CM Galois group at a later acceptable tower and uses the full ℓ-adic cyclotomic kernel. If trivial there, the quotient character on the cyclotomic image is continuous by compactness and Hausdorffness. The intersection with the principal-unit subgroup is closed and pro-ℓ, so its image under a continuous quadratic character is trivial. This gives the required implication on the Teichmüller kernel. In either label the quotient of characters 0/1 and 2/3 is the same root. Hence multiplication by that quadratic character permutes all four by the indicated involution. The original anti-self-twist condition would make it trivial; the nontriviality test for the same root on the same CM group contradicts this. Therefore the integral character and quadratic restriction statements hold with their original label, coefficient ring and Galois domain.

The two auxiliary results do not supply the remaining automorphic seed witnesses by themselves.

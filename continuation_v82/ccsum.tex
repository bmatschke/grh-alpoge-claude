\section{Pooling the row lengths when Frobenius is complex conjugation}

% BEGIN C82S1
\paragraph{C82S1. Degree and multiplicity of the all-thick completions.}
Take a guarded late inert index outside row B. If any completion
is all thick, I82W5 implies $\psi=f_p^{-1}=\mathrm{cc}$.
In this case every completion has degree two, and precisely
$\#T_2$ of the $2n$ places over $p$ are all thick. To see the
count, use the fixed global embedding bridge to partition the
embeddings of $E$ according to their induced $p$-adic place.
The embeddings belonging to each place are its decomposition-group
orbit. By I82W1--I82W2 that group is cyclic generated by $f_p$.
Complex conjugation has order two and pairs the two embeddings
of the same real place of the CM host. The fixed CM type $\Psi$
contains exactly one member of every pair, so the places over
$p$ are in bijection with $\Psi$, of cardinality $2n$.
By I82W3 a word is thick on such a pair exactly when its
representative is a thick host place. These representatives
are the image of the digits $s\in T_2$ under
$s\mapsto\tau_2(\mathrm{dig}(s)+u_0)$. They are distinct:
the size condition $4L_ws<n_0$ puts these digits in the
injectivity range of $\mathrm{dig}$, and both the translation
and the host map $\tau_2$ are injective there. The other
$2n-\#T_2$ degree-two places are completely nonthick.
% END C82S1

% BEGIN C82S2
\paragraph{C82S2. An aggregate sufficient condition at such a prime.}
Keep $\psi=\mathrm{cc}$ and the original frozen C and D row
constants. Put
\[
 \delta=\mathrm{de}_2=\#T_2/n,\quad
 F_{\min}=(w_P+\eta_3)/p+S_c+\mathrm{large}_2,\quad
 U=1+h_B/p+\log_p h_B.
\]
Then the middle-range $\mathrm{StarAD}$ inequality holds at
every good point of this index whenever
\[
 75r\delta\,h_B/p
 \ \le\ 2208r\,h_B/p^2+12192r F_{\min}+89460m U .
\]
In particular $\delta h_B\le162p F_{\min}$ is sufficient.

Indeed choose for each unmarked place $50\,\mathrm{coreF}(w)$
as the valid normalized length bound of N82I1. On each marked
place the coarse lifting estimate of N82I2 gives the bound
$3r(25h_B/p+3)$ before testing it against the place's row
length. There as well $9r\le50\,\mathrm{coreF}(w)$ because
$F_{\min}\ge1$. Summing these valid bounds on the actual
maps costs at most
\[
 50\sum_{w\mid p}\mathrm{coreF}(w)
                  +75r\,\#T_2\ h_B/p.
\]
The sum of the frozen rational lengths is bounded below by
$368\sum\mathrm{coreH}+2082\sum\mathrm{coreF}
+22365\sum\mathrm{coreM}$ by N82I1's strict rounding.
On $2n$ places of degree two the first and third sums before
their multipliers are $6nrh_B/p^2$ and $4nmU$, respectively.
Each flat term is at least $3r F_{\min}$. After reserving
the initial $50\sum\mathrm{coreF}$, we may use
$2032\cdot6nr F_{\min}$ from the remaining flat terms.
Division by $n$ gives the displayed condition, and
$75\cdot162\le12192$ gives its simpler consequence.
G82I1--G82I2 allow exactly this comparison of sums on the
inert all-row declaration; the conclusion concerns the global
inequality at the index.

There is a limitation on absorbing the coarse thick-place
estimate solely in the part of $\sum\mathrm{coreM}$ proportional
to $h_B/p$. Such a comparison requires
$(r/m)\delta\le 89460/75$. The original family has the opposite
asymptotic behavior. Indeed $r=k+m-1\ge k$,
$k/m\ge\sqrt{x}\log x$, and $\delta\ge x^{-\epsilon}$ by A78P3.
Their product is at least $x^{1/2-\epsilon}\log x\to\infty$.
This rules out that particular comparison of upper bounds
uniformly; the full displayed criterion still retains all three
terms at the prime being tested.
% END C82S2

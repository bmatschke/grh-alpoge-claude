1. CS1 valuation-ring elimination is valid for finite families in an
arbitrary extension: choose a minimal-valuation relation coefficient,
remove one generator without leaving O, and induct. It needs neither a
finite K/L extension nor finite O/R module extension.

2. CS2 preserves the ACTUAL integral Weyl lattice Delta, not the monic
coefficient lattice nabla. For S(X)=X^(-T)R_n, one has S(S(X))=X and
det S(X)=epsilon/det X. The composition of D_lambda and D_lambda*
has scalar epsilon^(-lambda_1+lambda_n+(lambda_1-lambda_n))=1.
The initial-minor complement identity sends the highest polynomial to
an integral signed row reversal; the off-diagonal elementary-matrix
identity intertwines divided powers with the required negative transpose.
This supplies both integral inclusions without Delta=nabla or a hidden
factorial denominator. Negative determinant powers occur only in
invertible-frame evaluations; the resulting Delta images are polynomial.

3. CS3 uses self-duality locally, not an assertion that every global
lattice is self-dual. The determinant T is a unit at the selected p.
With B*JB=1, N=B^(-1)gamma B' satisfies N*N=G' and
(N^(-1))*=NG'^(-1). An integral invertible G'^{-1} changes the basis but
not the column lattice. This is the needed odd-position equality and
retains LI's c^s parity.

4. CS5 localization-by-CRT correctly chooses s away from w and clears
only the finitely many other denominators. It relies on the supplied
full lattice and its exact all-place membership definition, which are
explicit assumptions, rather than inventing a lattice from conditions.

5. CS6 weak approximation gives exact column-lattice equality because
g_v^(-1)A_v is an integral unit matrix. A minor TeX exponent typo was
reported and corrected. No approximation of a dimension replaces this
exact lattice equality.

6. CS7 saturated intersection D_R intersect U_0 is a direct summand over
the DVR R. Denominator clearing identifies its L-span with
U_0 intersect L D_R; the same adapted basis and field extension then
prove the claimed O-intersection equality. The revised text explicitly
includes this denominator-clearing sentence. Finite-dimensional row
spaces justify reducing arbitrarily many rational equations to finitely
many. The proof uses no division by stabilizer orders; in the actual CR
setting the stabilizers are even trivial.

7. The original draft said CR6 directly equated dim_L V with rank_O M.
I requested the missing rational-forms bridge. The revised version now
uses emb_basis to identify the complex images of a coefficient-field
basis with a basis of Fi(0), and Fi_zero to identify Fi(0)=Mu0. This
matches card_eq_d exactly. CR6 supplies rank_O M=dim_C Mu0; the supplied
rational realization is necessary for dim_L V=dim_C Mu0.

8. In CS8 the comparison's injectivity is established BEFORE using
equal ranks. Polynomial coefficient rationality then makes the images
K-linearly independent. Equal dimension makes these images span KM;
it does not by itself prove their integral span is M. Rational-value
surjectivity is obtained via an L-linear retraction and only then
combined with exact integrality and rational integral spanning in CS9.
This separates the logically distinct steps correctly.

9. Remaining global supply is explicit: compatible Forms0/Joint0,
ValEx and the same frame/position/character record. Joint0 is correctly
not consumed by CR6's rank proof, but remains relevant here; its local
subgroup equality gives SameUloc. No metric identity, covolume formula,
discarded-target estimate, or arithmetic-window theorem is inferred.

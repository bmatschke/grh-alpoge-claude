Checks and conclusions

1. Simultaneous coefficient and complex alignment (LI4).
   The actual K=KfRec is finite over Q_p, so a Q_p-embedding into an
   algebraic closure of Q_p exists. Its valuation ring is exactly ORec:
   integral elements have absolute value at most one; units and their
   inverses have norm one; p=u*pi^e fixes the uniformizer's norm. Extending
   the prescribed embedding of the coefficient number field to the
   algebraic numbers in C preserves the original embRec, rather than
   replacing it by a different place. The Zorn extension only uses
   algebraic arguments; no extension of arbitrary complex numbers occurs.

   Normality of E/Q permits adjustment of the initial Good embedding by
   one Galois automorphism. This takes place before its induced primes
   are enumerated, so it does not conflict with the later literal orbit
   representatives. Arithmetic Frobenius f=zc gives exactly
   a_v*f^s=a_v*z^s*c^s. The c^s factor, right multiplication convention,
   and odd-position conjugation agree with PosCompatOfBase. This
   condition is independent of the away character.

2. The literal first-projection lattice frame (LI5).
   For c(b)=-b and b!=0, {1,b} is an F-basis of E. The displayed inverse
   to Phi(x tensor t)=(j(x)t,j(c(x))t) is correct. Division by 2 and j(b)
   occurs only in the characteristic-zero completion, not in its integer
   ring. The map sends scalars diagonally and the involution to swapping.

   The source tensInt is the subring generated by the two stated integer
   images, and latS is their literal span of the supplied lattice.
   Therefore pi_1(intV)=O_v: integer images lie in O_v and the diagonal
   scalar generators attain every element of O_v. Both inclusions in
   pi_1(locL)=O_v-span j(Lambda) follow. Finite generation descends from
   Lambda's finite O_E-generators; an E-basis selected from Lambda has
   nonzero determinant after j, giving full rank. The finite torsion-free
   module is free over the DVR. Its chosen basis yields exactly the
   inverse-matrix membership criterion fr_lat, with the specified first
   component and the recovered iw equal to i_v.

   This is an arbitrary local frame at each v. It correctly does not
   assert that these frames are base changes of one global matrix B.

3. Ramification and place membership (LI6).
   Injectivity of the same i_K cancels the aligned images and gives the
   displayed embedding identity with theta_(a_v z^s)(c^s x). For x in F
   the involution disappears. Frobenius preserves the p-normalized
   valuation on Q_n, and BC3.6–BC3.7 imply
     nu_w(theta_g(x))/nu_w(p)=v_p(i_v(x))
   for nonzero x. No equality of unnormalized integer valuations, and no
   claim that a mapped uniformizer remains a uniformizer, is used.
   For x in O_F, positivity gives precisely membership in the induced
   prime; x=0 is included separately through valuation +infinity. The
   alternative inverse-integrality proof matches the literal source.

4. Scope and downstream boundary.
   The supplied theta_g maps and their complex identities are explicit
   inputs. The result proves the substantive local membership identity;
   it does not silently supply the global B, class-rank equality,
   comparison_lattice_span_eq, or full global-to-local comparison record
   required by the source's later place_of_tie wrapper. The report and
   main fragment preserve that boundary.

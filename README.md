# A human-readable reconstruction of RH and Dirichlet GRH

**Six obligations remain. All six are shared by RH and unrestricted Dirichlet GRH.** The aim is to explain the pinned Alpöge–Claude formal argument in ordinary mathematical language, using established theorems where their hypotheses and normalizations agree. The human proof is incomplete. [Proof idea](PROOF_IDEA.md) · [Mathematical guide](MATHEMATICAL_GUIDE.md) · [Verified obligation inventory](SELECTED_FAMILY_CHECKPOINT.json)

**The untwisted starting column now carries all seventeen lower-row laws.** Alpöge’s mass comparison places all the words on one [starting column](FIRST_PASS_COLUMN.md) over the [prescribed field and frame](FIRST_PASS_FIELDS.md). For each good finite-adelic representation, the [cuspidal multiplicity comparison](LOWER_ROW_MULTIPLICITY.md) identifies its multiplicity on the bottom unitary group with that on the group for each holomorphic row, with the specified real models. Under the standing theta and representation-theoretic results this gives the [seventeen laws on the prescribed data](LOWER_ROW_ASSEMBLY.md). The [field extension](THETA_FINE_EXTENSION.md) then gives a fine datum on the requested untwisted column whose [exceptional places form complete fibers over the fixed field](UNTWISTED_AUXILIARY_PRIMES.md).

**Every composite theta image on the first untwisted column now has rational readings in its prescribed coefficient field.** Alpöge’s seesaw argument leads to a comparison on [every square of the grid](ROW_TOWER_INDUCTION.md). Its nonzero constants allow us to [exchange the paths of the lift](ROW_SQUARE_PATH_FLIP.md). On the opposite path, [unfolding the period](ROW_MIXED_CELL_UNFOLDING.md) reduces the entire finite catalogue on this restriction to [one possible staircase label](ROW_MIXED_CELL_SUPPORT.md). Its contribution may vanish. The [definite jet sums](ROW_PERIOD_JET_FORMULA.md), [period detection](ROW_PERIOD_ASCENT.md) and [one common real test](ROW_COMMON_REAL_TEST.md) then give separating forms on the bottom space with arithmetic values on the images of all rational source vectors, for every tuple present. This uses the standing theta, doubling, Hida and arithmetic inputs. [Common-target proof](COMPOSITE_ROW_RATIONALITY.md#rational-tests-on-the-actual-weight-supported-column) · [Advance field request](ROW_COEFFICIENT_FIELD.md)

On this first pass the frame characters introduce no new twisting primes. In the product of all the normalized square constants the Gauss factors therefore contribute only a root of unity. The remaining fractional ideal is that of the product of the normalized definite constants and the critical Hecke quotients from columns an even distance from the right boundary. Its size still matters in the period-height obligation. The [starting state before localization](FIRST_PASS_STARTING_STATE.md) retains these rows on the prescribed weight set. The trace still counts elements with multiplicity there. The [second-moment comparison](RETAINED_DIMENSION_COMPARISON.md) gives an alternative sufficient bound. **Most recent whole obligations closed.** The chosen family retains its integral Hecke specialization. Its next theta lift uses the same columns, real realization and coefficient field. Its base rows have the prescribed eigenspaces and bases. These are assertions about every member of the family. Existence of sufficiently large members is the third open question above. - For the route from divisibility of theta lifts to a prime-sum bound, start with the [proof idea](PROOF_IDEA.md) and [Alpöge’s remark with references](ALPOGE_REMARK_GUIDE.md).
- For the individual arithmetic results, read the [split-prime base-change argument](LOCAL_BASE_CHANGE_BLOCKS.md), the [residual Galois comparison](LOCAL_RESIDUAL_REPRESENTATIONS.md) and the [local specialization supplement](LOCAL_SPECIALIZATION_SUPPLEMENT.pdf).

The [earlier reading index](history/github_reading_guide_before_current_roadmap.md) preserves the edition-specific navigation and reproduction instructions.
- Run `python3 verify_selected_family.py` at the repository root to check the six open leaves against both endpoints of the 121-node dependency graph. It also checks that the roadmap lists precisely those obligations. The project accepts the [pinned formal proof](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/tree/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4) as its starting point.

`main` is the working record. A proof update belongs with its evidence, the resulting next action in the [roadmap](NEXT_STEPS.md), and any change in the [whole-obligation inventory](SELECTED_FAMILY_CHECKPOINT.json). Historical numbered editions describe the state at their release.

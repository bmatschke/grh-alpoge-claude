\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries The large-prime denominator estimate}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.80 \quad 25 November 2026
\end{center}

At sufficiently large primes outside the prescribed exceptional set, the
denominator estimate reduces to three local conditions on cocycles. We
construct the shifted crystalline representations at every qualifying
place and use their residual Wach lattices to bound admissible classes.
The three resulting conditions descend to the original coefficient
field. With the preceding global count this supplies the large-prime
field on the same family hub as the eleven fields proved in edition 0.79. At the two ramified primes we also prove the rank-one
cohomology bounds and compute a common tame exponent.

# The adapted kernel entries on the divisor of the first-pass column

Fix the pinned fourth-stage column and its split-prime column $t$, the chosen divisor $Y$, its actual character and tuple comparison, and its equality $\mathrm{QpIs}(Y.Q_p)$. Write $D=w.D(Z)$, $m=D.R.m$, $L=D.\mathrm{what}_2.LL$, and $E/F$ for the CM host. On the [eventual range for the intrinsic lattice layer](FIRST_PASS_WEIGHT_BOUND.md), take its vertex assignments and fillings, including their prescribed rank and weights. The kernel construction gives one entry record $\mathcal E$ for all words $u$, tuples $\mu$, and coefficient places $v$, and local kernel data at every served pair $(u,v)$. These have the same place embeddings and the prescribed comparison of theta series. The stored entry record is obtained *on $Y$* by the formula $\mathrm{kerEntC}(\mathrm{Pl},O,B,H)$. This is the certificate needed by the [construction of the core](continuation_v78/producer.tex#L140-L183) and the [later transport of that certificate](continuation_v77/selection.tex#L89-L110). [Full quantifier order][main] · [Required comparisons][fields]

## The entries before choosing a served place

Choose the place embeddings $\mathrm{Pl}=(\lambda_v,\iota_v)$ in the pin, compatible on $L$, with $\iota_v:L\to\mathbf C_p$, $p=\operatorname{char}k(v)$. For $O_{u,\mu}$ use the original self-dual class lattices, their determinant representatives, their frames and the theta function at each pair of step representatives. Its excluded primes are $T_u,g_0,g_1$ and $Y.Q_p(\mu)$. The polynomial unfolding on every edge through $m$ is precisely the original map on frame polynomials. Evaluation on the frame followed by its polynomial inverse recovers the input polynomial, so this unfolding uses the chosen representatives. The kernel's valuation, lattice and entry identities are required when $p$ avoids these exclusions. Call such a triple $(u,\mu,v)$ level-free. [Construction of the lift objects][obj] · [Exact exclusions][free]

For fixed $u,\mu,v,i,c$, where $0\le i\le m$ and $c$ is a class in degree $i$, choose a matrix $P_{i,c}$ over $E$ whose columns span the class lattice locally at $p$ and which satisfies $P_{i,c}^{*}P_{i,c}=1$. Indeed $E/F$ is unramified at finite places and the input lattice is self-dual in the standard Hermitian space. At a split place its two factors are dual. At an unramified field place a unimodular Hermitian form over the residue field has an orthogonal basis of nonzero norms. Lifting a unit-norm-class vector, correcting its norm using surjectivity of the norm on local units, and splitting off its orthogonal complement gives an orthonormal lattice basis. Surjectivity on principal units follows by lifting through successive powers of the maximal ideal using the surjective residue trace, including residue characteristic two. Simultaneously approximate the resulting isometries at the places over $p$ by one rational unitary matrix. The Cayley transform on the open subset where $1+g$ is invertible reduces this to weak approximation on the vector space of skew-Hermitian matrices. Each required lattice coset is open and meets this dense subset. The resulting columns and the original lattice agree after localization at $p$. Thus one and the same $P_{i,c}$ works for every infinity label $\tau$ before applying its embedding. In particular it agrees at equal primes and obeys the required inverse-conjugate-transpose relation at conjugate primes. [Unitary approximation and genus][genus] · [From the genus to bases and prime compatibilities][bases] · [Jacobowitz on local Hermitian forms](https://doi.org/10.2307/2372982)

Let $\widehat P_{i,c,\tau}$ denote the matrix under the place embedding induced by $\lambda_v,\tau$. First use these unscaled matrices for the coefficient calculation. Represent a class value by the relevant weight polynomial modulo the kernel of evaluation on frames. A linear section of this quotient supplies representatives. At the source multiply by the prescribed determinant power $s_i^\sharp-\operatorname{twB}_i$ before evaluating, and use the factor $\prod_\tau\det(\widehat P_{i,c,\tau})^{-s^\sharp_{i,\tau}}$. At the target use $\prod_\tau\det(\widehat P_{i+1,c',\tau})^{-s^{\rm div}_{i,\tau}}$. The canonical frame polynomial $\mathrm{DeX}$ is the product of the column determinants for the given Young diagrams. Its functionals give all required translated weight polynomials: bideterminants span them, and expansion of the column determinants realizes each required product of minors by a functional. Degrees above the number of rows are absent. [Polynomial and quotient coordinates][coords] · [Bideterminant spanning][span]

This gives the following four facts, with their original scope. [Exact four assertions][hyp]

1. For an unscaled target coordinate, the value of the functional on every integral minor product belongs to $\iota_v(L)$. The matrices $\widehat P$ have entries in $\iota_v(L)$, and the determinant-twisted frame function has values there on rational matrices. To see why the choice of inverse to $\mathrm{DeX}$ causes no descent problem, take an $L$-linear projection from $\mathbf C_p$ to $\iota_v(L)$ and apply it to the monomial coefficients of the functional. The resulting functional has the same frame evaluations on the rational matrices. Polynomial density gives equality of all frame evaluations. Expanding $\mathrm{DeX}$ shows that a functional with all these evaluations zero annihilates every tested minor product. Thus the original and projected functionals agree on those products. [Descent of the minor values][val]
2. On the source the family of inequalities $|\mathrm{pr}_S(\nabla_J)|_v\le1$ for every column choice $J$, together with membership in the complex form space and realizability of each class value, describes the localization of the *given* form lattice. In the chart of one class, the local lattice basis and its dual companion give the determinant-twisted weight polynomial. Integral Weyl-lattice duality tests exactly its values on all integral minor products: in integral dual bases the reproducing identity expands the canonical frame function with coefficients the corresponding functional evaluations, and the integral changes of generators in both directions identify the two tests. The class charts characterize both which values come from a rational form and which such forms are integral. Apply them in both directions. [Weyl lattice and dual bases][weyl] · [Class chart test][schart] · [Source lattice][src]
3. The same statement holds for $|\mathrm{pr}_T(I)|_v\le1$ on the target. A rational form has polynomial representatives of its class values. Conversely, the complex form-space condition and simultaneous coefficient-field representatives give a rational form. Relative to the target frame, the representative has the determinant twist $s_i^{\rm div}$; duality on all the target minor products then characterizes its localized lattice class by class. [Target determinant and minor test][tchart] · [Target lattice in both directions][tgt]
4. For a source value $z$ and a target value $z'$ related by the original $K_{i,c,c'}$, one has for each target column choice $I$
$$
 \mathrm{pr}_T(z')(I)=U_{i,c,c'}\,\mathrm{pr}_S(z)
 \bigl(\mathrm{applyAt0}(\Theta_{i,c,c'},G_{xz,I})\bigr).
$$
Here $\Theta$ is the Taylor series after the stated frame substitution. $U$ is the product of the two class-character factors under $\lambda_v$, times the source determinant to $+s_i^\sharp$ and the target determinant to $-s_i^{\rm div}$, with products over $\tau$. The two class-character factors are nonzero algebraic numbers by the tuple-character reading. To verify the formula, first evaluate on invertible complex frames. The original unfolding identifies the target polynomial with the jet of the source polynomial multiplied by those two character factors. Invertible frames are Zariski dense, so this is a polynomial identity. Only the Taylor jets of the source weight's multidegree enter. For those jets write the class theta function as a finite sum over the relevant lattice quotient; the pinned jet-field condition gives the algebraic normalized jet on each summand with its indicated finite character. Thus one can apply $\lambda_v$ coefficientwise to the polynomial identity in precisely those degrees, including the multi-index factorials relating Taylor coefficients to derivatives and the indicated powers of the differential generator. The frame substitution and the minor pairing give the displayed formula. For the full formal series the source defines each coefficient by its algebraic reading, with value zero in the absence of algebraicity. The field comparison in this calculation is confined to the prescribed degrees. [Definition of the formal Taylor series][pser] [Complex identity][poly] · [Reduction to pinned jets][jets] · [Coefficient and frame transport][kform]

## Adjusting the bases without changing the integral tests

Write $d_\tau=\lambda_v(\mathrm{dgen}_\tau)$. For the three prime classes called inert-class, signal and ramD, the generator is a local unit by its ideal condition and the exclusion of the small primes and the distinguished $q$. Set $a_\tau=d_\tau^{-1}$ there and $a_\tau=1$ elsewhere. Install
$$
                     B_{i,c,\tau}=a_\tau^i\widehat P_{i,c,\tau}.
$$
The same $a_\tau$ is used at all steps of the chosen $u,\mu,v$. Before adjustment the substitution at type $\tau$ in the raw Taylor series is $Z\mapsto d_\tau\widehat P_{i+1,c',\tau}Z\widehat P_{i,c,\tau}^{-1}$, with the variable indices of the corresponding CM label. After adjustment the two adjacent powers contribute $a_\tau^{i+1}/a_\tau^i=a_\tau$. Hence on the three designated prime classes the series is the literal substitution by $\widehat P_{i+1,c',\tau}Z\widehat P_{i,c,\tau}^{-1}$. [Adapted-prime and basis conditions][ad] · [Unit calculation][unit] · [Explicit cancellation][cancel]

For each CM label $z$ put $n_z=|\nu_i(z)|$ and set $H=\prod_z a_{\tau(z)}^{n_z}$. Write $A=\prod_\tau(a_\tau^{iN_i})^{s^\sharp_{i,\tau}}$ and $B=\prod_\tau(a_\tau^{(i+1)N_{i+1}})^{-s^{\rm div}_{i,\tau}}$, where $N_j=D.R.N(j)$. Each determinant is multilinear in its columns. The two projection functionals thus acquire factors $A^{-1}H^i$ and $BH^{i+1}$. The scalar $U$ acquires $AB$, and applying the rescaled Taylor series to the minor polynomial contributes $H$. Their product on the right of the entry formula is $AB\cdot A^{-1}H^i\cdot H=BH^{i+1}$, exactly the factor on the left. All factors have norm one, so both integral-lattice comparisons persist, and the target absolute values still lie in the value set of $L$, including zero. This last claim concerns the absolute values after rescaling. The source does not require the rescaled projections themselves to be $L$-valued. With these very matrices and the verified four properties $H_{\rm ent}$, set $\mathcal E=\mathrm{kerEntC}(\mathrm{Pl},O,B_{\bullet},H_{\rm ent})$. Its place map is $\lambda_v$, its theta series is the substituted Taylor series, and its comparison scalar is the nonzero $U$ above. [Homogeneity and rescaled entry identity][scale] · [Adapted entries on the actual column][ent] · [Definition of the record][record]

## Local kernel at a served place

Fix $u,v$ satisfying $\mathrm{GIdx}$. Thus $p\ge5$ is prime of inert-class, the prescribed nonvanishing condition holds, $p$ is outside the host level primes and $p\nmid T_u$. Use $\mu=\mu_{\rm At}(v)$. In each split factor indexed by $q$, project $E\otimes_F F_q$ to its specified first factor and transport it to the local field of the vertex. This gives $i_q$. At orbit position $s$, applying the indicated local automorphism to $i_q(x)$ gives the chosen position embedding of $x$ for even $s$, and of $\bar x$ for odd $s$, after the prescribed structural embeddings. The equality is the position identity already in $t$'s comparison. To embed the local coefficient field $K_f$ in $\mathbf C_p$, start with the embedding supplied by $t.\mathrm{isRec}$. Its induced embedding of the number field $L$ and $\mathcal E.\iota_v$ are conjugate by an abstract field automorphism of $\mathbf C_p$: extend the isomorphism of their countable images to algebraic closures and choose transcendence bases of equal cardinalities. Composition gives $i_K$ with the stipulated restriction. This step requires only a field embedding. [Served primes][served] · [First split factor][iw] · [Position and parity][pos] · [Coefficient embedding][embed]

At step zero, transfer the stored orbit labels along the equivalence from the initial classes to the twisted degree-zero classes. At positive steps use the reference column charts when $\mu=\mu_0$, including their separate middle and terminal conditions. Otherwise use its twisted charts with their lattices, determinant character, rational bases, and local stabilizers. In either case the chart decomposition is by a component $b$ and a lattice tuple $L_\bullet$ modulo its component group $\Gamma_b$. Two valid representatives give the same class exactly when their components coincide and $L'_\bullet=\gamma L_\bullet$ for some $\gamma\in\Gamma_b$; every class has such a representative. Indeed, represent a lattice tuple by its local basis matrices. Choosing the global class in its double coset gives its chart label. Changing the bases by a local integral matrix leaves that double coset fixed. Two representatives of the same class therefore have the same component and differ there by its rational stabilizer; conversely a stabilizer translate preserves the class. Choosing local matrices from a representative of each global class gives surjectivity. At positive steps the comparison first identifies the given form with its chart representative and then evaluates it in these local coordinates under the coefficient embedding. The chart comparison is injective, gives the same rational coordinates, and detects the localized form lattice. The finite-dimensional descent and surjectivity on the rational locus in the chart give equality of the local integral span. [Classification of valid representatives][chartorbit] · [Map and lattice descent on each step][chartmap] At step zero the row identification $e_0$ carries the reference integral lattice to the twisted lattice, so the original comparison at zero gives the corresponding span. Above the top there are no successor components. [Zero-step classes][zero] · [Middle and top on the reference tuple][base] · [Nonreference charts][twist] · [Positive-step maps and integral span][cls] · [Zero-step integral span][cmp0]

The global module attached to a valid local tuple can be taken as
$$
     \Lambda=\{x\in E^n : i_q(x)\in L_q\text{ for all vertices }q\}.
$$
To prove its image spans $L_{q_0}$, choose local generator matrices $g_q$. Approximate the columns of $g_{q_0}$ simultaneously by elements of $F^n$, arbitrarily close to zero at the other vertices. The underlying places of $F$ are distinct and the split-factor embeddings induce their given completions. The open condition at $q_0$ is membership in $g_{q_0}\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal O_{q_0})$; at the others take columns inside $L_q$. The resulting columns lie in $\Lambda$ and span $L_{q_0}$. The reverse inclusion is the definition of $\Lambda$. This use of approximation gives only the specified localizations. [Approximation and localization][approx]

For local representatives at two consecutive ranks with the required dimension identities, choose generator matrices as above. Under $i_K$ and the orbit embeddings let $A_j(\tau)$ be the matrix at rank $j$, using the inverse transpose on the dual type. For a matrix variable with target rows and source columns the forward coordinate change is $Z\mapsto A_{j+1}ZA_j^{-1}$, with the prescribed reordering of variables. Define the local series by the *inverse* linear substitution on the previously constructed $\mathcal E.\Theta$ for the two corresponding global classes. Both matrices are invertible and the CM label map is a bijection. Composition of the substitutions gives the identity on formal series: the sum over the intermediate indices separates into entries of $A_{j+1}^{-1}A_{j+1}$ and $A_j^{-1}A_j$. Each substitution is homogeneous linear in the variables, so each coefficient is a finite calculation. All free unit factors in the served-place identity can therefore be taken to be one. The definition allows the zero series when the rank tests fail. The required identities concern $j<m$, valid local lattice tuples and the two rank identifications. This constructs a local series for each representative and proves exactly its prescribed comparison. [Definition of the served-place identity][servedth] · [Coefficients of the inverse substitution][inv] · [Construction and identity][series]

Finally insert these local series, embeddings, global modules and comparison maps in the local kernel data. Install the chain maps supplied by those data in the intrinsic lattice layer. This changes neither its vertices nor its fillings; its bound was supplied uniformly for the levels and maps used here. The resulting kernel family has the required place, series, first-factor and orbit-position comparisons with the same global $\mathcal E$. Its certificate is equality by construction. This proof uses the intrinsic layer and its uniform bound from their own producer, the first-pass pin (in particular its prescribed jet degrees), and the indicated chart and lattice theorems on that column. It requires no additional condition on the support of the first-pass twist. [Local assembly][local] · [Installing the maps][withfirst] · [Common eventual range and final certificate][main]

# Why the periods and the unitary double cosets label the same components

Fix an active Hermitian row $V$ on a prescribed arithmetic stage, its trace-self-dual lattice $L=\mathfrak a L^0$ from the [stage parameters](PEL_STAGE_PARAMETERS.md), the level $M=g_0g_1$, and one of the coefficient embeddings preserving the chosen embedding of $K$. Write $F=E^+$ and $G=U(V)$ over $F$. The moduli problem at this stage marks the $\bar{\mathfrak b}$ half of the $M$-torsion. Its auxiliary level is $A=1$. The complex charts of the marked moduli scheme and the elements of
$$
             G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A_{F,f})/K(M)
$$
have the same labels. Here $K(M)$ preserves $\widehat L$ and acts trivially on $\widehat L/M\widehat L$. The correspondence also identifies the arithmetic groups acting on the charts. It explains how the values of one holomorphic section can be read as a function on the adelic unitary group. ## From the polarization to an isometry at every finite place

The period classification describes a chart by a lattice $L_i$, its alternating polarization pairing, and its marking. Two such data give the same chart class precisely when an integral isometry identifies the markings. All occur. The original embedding of $L$ into $V$ fixes the sign and the scale of the alternating trace form. With the source's convention for which argument of the Hermitian form is linear, the period coordinates use $-\mathrm{Gram}(V)^t$ and $-\xi_0$. Reversing the two arguments of the alternating form and conjugating the trace gives exactly the original pairing. Use these coordinates at every good embedding with its prescribed ordering of the Lie eigenspaces. [Trace and signs][ctx] · [Period construction, HUF21](../../../research/modules/construction/holomorphic_uniformization_v49/main.tex#L1209-L1263)

Two facts turn this period classification into the displayed quotient.

1. **One rational Hermitian space suffices.** Nondegeneracy of the field trace recovers the Hermitian pairing from the alternating pairing and the CM action. Positivity on the two Hodge eigenspaces fixes its signatures at all real places. Locally the trace-dual lattice identity makes each Hermitian lattice self-dual with values in the same fractional ideal $\xi_0^{-1}\mathfrak D_{E/\mathbf Q}^{-1}$. At each finite place of $F$, the extension $E/F$ is split or unramified by the host hypothesis. A common local rescaling makes the lattices unimodular, hence locally isometric. At an unramified place, a nondegenerate Hermitian space over the residue field has a vector of nonzero norm. Lift this vector, split off its unit line, and continue. The unit norm map of an unramified quadratic extension is onto, also at places above 2, so each diagonal unit can be removed. At a split place take dual bases. Landherr’s local-global theorem now identifies each rational Hermitian space with $V$. The various integral lattices in this common space supply the different classes. [Detailed trace-duality and local calculation, PUN3–5](../../../research/modules/construction/principal_uniformization_v48/main.tex#L126-L254) · [Gross, *Incoherent definite spaces and Shimura varieties*, Theorem 3.1](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~gross/preprints/incoherent.pdf)
2. **The finite isometry can retain the given half-marking.** Comparing torsion coordinates with periods gives an $\mathcal O_E\otimes\widehat{\mathbf Z}$-linear isomorphism $v:\widehat L\to\widehat L_i$. The comparison of the two compatible generators of the roots of unity gives a multiplier $\nu\in\widehat{\mathbf Z}^{\times}$, so $B_i(vx,vy)=\nu B(x,y)$. Choose a unit $s\in\widehat{\mathcal O}_E^\times$ with $s\bar s=\nu^{-1}$ and $s=1\bmod\mathfrak b$. Locally away from $M$ use surjectivity of the unit norm at unramified places and multiplication on the split factors. At a place dividing $M$ the two factors are split, interchanged by conjugation and distinguished by $\mathfrak b,\bar{\mathfrak b}$. Take $(1,\nu^{-1})$ with the first factor the one retained by the marking. The product of these choices defines $s$. Multiplication by $s$ fixes $\bar{\mathfrak b}L/ML$, and $vs$ is an actual isometry. Testing the alternating identity after multiplying the first input by arbitrary elements of the CM algebra and using trace duality gives the Hermitian identity as well. Thus $vs$ extends to a finite adelic unitary element taking $\widehat L$ to $\widehat L_i$ with the original marking. This is where the auxiliary level $A=1$ enters the argument. ## The double cosets and the value of a section

Conversely, given $h_f\in G(\mathbb A_{F,f})$, form $L(h_f)=V\cap h_f\widehat L$ inside the finite adelic space. This is a full lattice with completion $h_f\widehat L$. For example, the two completed lattices are commensurable, so after clearing a common denominator the assertion follows by lifting in the finite quotient of two nested multiples of $L$. The alternating form is perfect on $L(h_f)$ since it is so on every completion. Reduction of $h_f$ transports the marked summand to $L(h_f)/M L(h_f)$. It gives compatible identifications at all finite orders and thus a genus marking in the period classification. A lift of its finite-order map to a linear map of the original lattices exists by projectivity of $L$. HUF14 supplies the corresponding chart. Right multiplication by $K(M)$ preserves this datum. Left multiplication by a rational isometry gives an isomorphic marked lattice. [Construction from a finite adele][occ] · [Every chart has a finite representative][onto]

If two such data are isomorphic, the isometry extends by scalars to $\gamma\in G(F)$. The element $h_f'^{-1}\gamma h_f$ preserves $\widehat L$ and fixes the marked half pointwise. It is a genuine isometry, so perfect duality with the complementary half forces identity on the entire reduction modulo $M$. Hence it belongs to $K(M)$. This proves bijectivity, and for a representative $h_i$ it gives the chart group
$$
                 \Gamma_i=G(F)\cap h_i K(M)h_i^{-1}.
$$
In particular, a classification of arbitrary PEL similitudes alone would leave a multiplier in the calculation. The norm correction above and the duality argument identify the precise isometry group appearing in the theta integral. [Half-marking comparison](PEL_MARKED_LEVEL.md) · [Arithmetic groups, PUN6](../../../research/modules/construction/principal_uniformization_v48/main.tex#L255-L294)

Choose $h_i$ that sends the reference lattice to the actual representative $L_i$ with its marking, and choose an orthogonal reference frame $x_0$ for the common Gram form at infinity. For $h=(h_\infty,h_f)$ write $\gamma h_f=h_i k$ with $\gamma\in G(F)$ and $k\in K(M)$. Read the pair consisting of chart $i$ and real frame $\gamma_\infty h_\infty x_0$. A different $\gamma$ changes this frame by $\Gamma_i$. Choose $\gamma=1$ for $h=(1,h_i)$, so each actual chart attains its reference frame. This constructs the dictionary with the required congruence for every vector of the marked half. [Precise congruence and frame][mark] · [Choice of representatives][choice]

Left rational and right finite-level translation preserve the frame modulo $\Gamma_i$. Right real translation acts in the chosen frame coordinates by conjugation with $x_0$. Every admissible flag frame occurs up to a triangular change of basis because Gram–Schmidt on its nondegenerate successive subspaces gives an orthogonal frame with the prescribed signs. Every such orthogonal frame is a real isometry translate of $x_0$. Finally, equality of two actual frames up to $\Gamma_i$ identifies their real factors after rational translation (the frame is invertible), and the finite calculation above then gives a right factor in $K(M)$. These give all the orbit statements required of the dictionary. [Exact orbit statements][orbits] · [Reconstruction from the marking rule][tied]

For the initial core the source chooses the trivial character. Its adelic multiplier can be taken to be $1$. The normalized charts have trivial arithmetic character, so evaluating a holomorphic cusp section in the frame above is independent of the rational mover, is left rationally invariant and is continuous. Indeed the coset of the finite component is locally constant at level $K(M)$, and within it evaluation is a continuous function of the real frame. The normalized-frame theorem also supplies the cusp-center lattice and its Fourier exponents on these same charts once base change and twisting of the theta sections are available. These are the geometric and adelic readings at one supplied stage. Integral control of sections in residue characteristic and the arithmetic comparisons of the preliminary theta readings enter separately in constructing a full row.

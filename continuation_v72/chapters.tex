{\Large\bfseries The marked theorem and its arithmetic applications}\\[4pt]
Edition 0.72 \quad 24 November 2026
\end{center}

The arithmetic construction needs one ideal, chosen before any
point of the deformation ring, with its Hida properties and an
automorphy system on that ideal. We supply its local,
automorphic and geometric hypotheses. The resulting prime tests
give the marked theorem. We then turn to the original datum with
unramified coefficients, where both the rank-three test and the
rank-two newform must work for one mark.

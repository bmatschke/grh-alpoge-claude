Purpose and exact status
========================
The remaining SD2 obligation was not the abstract assertion that an injective
monomial substitution preserves tensor-product independence. It was proving
that the ACTUAL segment lattices, frame geometry, digit constraints and row
degrees satisfy its hypotheses, simultaneously for every label/path family.

This note proves that assembly, with the exact source conclusion RestLam 1088.
Two shared results in version 0.7 are used by name and at their precise scope:

* CG1: the actual XX' module is the integral span of an explicit subset of the
  universal paired path basis, hence saturated. The same assertion over the
  residue field supplies the base-change/lifting route in the separate coarse
  congruence note. Its proof is the actual-model application of IP, not a
  characteristic-zero isotypic decomposition.
* RS3: the numerical parameter estimate a'_t/P_j(y) >= (36/5)Y k^2 Ns for every
  segment letter y. Its proof expands the determinant degree loss and the
  original Dat.Hyp; it is not a new independent parameter assumption.

Everything else used in RestLam is derived below. We also state a conditional
version under the single narrow numerical bound that LA5 actually uses, so
the logical role of RS3 is completely visible. No good-path abundance estimate,
coarse-generator congruence, or global arithmetic window is inferred here.
No Lean source was modified, and no Lean build or kernel replay is claimed.

0. Exact source data and hypotheses
==================================
Write k=r+K. The source datum X contains a periodically framed selected set
TT subset Z/n'Z; an integer degree deg_t at each residue position; positive
integer dimension parameters r,K; L1,N1,Ns,E0,mStar; a row loss E'_t; and real
parameters a,Y,Q,CLa,Ck. Its Hyp contains exactly:

  p prime, p>=13, n'>0 even, r>=2;
  F.Valid(L1,N1);
  a <= deg_t <= 2a for t in TT;
  #letters_j <= Ns <= N1, Ns <= 2#TT, Ns+r <= K;
  p^dist(j,x) <= Q for every x in letters_j;
  Y>=1, Ck Ns^2 log(a) <= k;
  128 k^2 Ns Y^2 <= p^(L1-1);
  E'_t <= E0 for t in TT;
  32pY k^2 Ns Q^2(E0+mStar+1) <= a.

The RestLam endpoint additionally assumes CLa,Ck>=1088 and the source
UniformizedDVR(p,O) setting. In particular O is a DVR domain and its residue
field is f. The geometry and digit injection below need only the frame
properties explicitly used; their proof has no hidden use of 1088. The
numerical bound in LA5 and the actual path-space hypotheses are the reasons
for carrying the full source assumptions at the endpoint.

The literal frame validity conditions are:
  J>=1 and f_(j+J,i)=f_(j,i)+n';
  f_(j,0)<...<f_(j,r-1)<f_(j+1,0);
  frame letters are even and selected;
  no selected letter lies strictly between adjacent entries of one frame;
  the intermediate selected letters between consecutive frames number at
  least r, with the designated first/last r even and satisfying the specified
  L1 gaps; the first current-frame letter also has its L1 gap;
  #letters_j<=N1;
  every late letter x lies more than L1 beyond its r-th most recent selected
  even predecessor (the source's oldest(j,x)).
Only the first four lines are needed in LA1--LA2; the other conditions enter
CG1/RS3 or the separate numerical/path count.

Put z_j=f_(j,r-1), s_j=f_(j-1,0). The finite segment is
  letters_j = TT_Z intersect [s_j,z_j],
where TT_Z is the periodic lift of TT. Its old letters satisfy x<=z_(j-1),
and its late letters are the remaining ones. The first r polynomial
coordinates c=0,...,r-1 are the plane coordinates; the last K are normal.

The original generator row degree is d_t=deg_t-E'_t on TT and zero off TT,
using natural subtraction. The coarse degree is a'_t=d_t-Et_t, where Et_t
is the exact row degree of the determinant multiplier Dl. The explicit
formula, recalled to fix the object, is

 Et_t = sum_(j=0)^(J-1) sum_(x in late_j) sg(j,x)
             sum_(i=0)^(r-1) 1_(row(j,x,i) mod n'=t) p^dist(j,row(j,x,i)),
 sg(j,x)=floor(Bo(j,x)/(k^2Y)),
 Bo(j,x)=deg_(oldest(j,x) mod n')/p^dist(j,oldest(j,x)).

Here row(j,x,i) lists the last r even selected predecessors of x, in
increasing order. RS2 proves the smallness of this exact degree loss and
the required absence of underflow in the natural subtractions. The present
argument uses its consequence RS3, not an informal approximate degree.

LA1. All four LamGeo properties from one-period geometry
========================================================
There are exactly r old letters in segment j, namely f_(j-1,i). Indeed, an
old selected x lies in [f_(j-1,0),f_(j-1,r-1)]. The no-selected-letter gaps
between adjacent frame entries force x to be one of those entries.
Thus

  W_j={f_(j-1,i):0<=i<r},
  L_j=TT_Z intersect (z_(j-1),z_j].

The z_j are strictly increasing. Periodicity gives z_(J-1)=z_(-1)+n'. The
J adjacent late intervals for j=0,...,J-1 tile (z_(-1),z_(-1)+n']. This
half-open interval contains exactly one integer of each residue class
modulo n'. It follows that every t in TT has precisely one late occurrence,
and two late occurrences with the same residue have the same j and x.
These are LamGeo.EX and LamGeo.U1, with no probabilistic assertion.

An old occurrence (j,x) is a current-frame entry of segment j-1, hence a
late occurrence for j-1. Applying the same interval argument to the J
segments -1,...,J-2 proves uniqueness among old occurrences. This is U2.

If an old occurrence and a late occurrence have the same residue, the
uniqueness just proved says that the late one is a periodic translate of
that old frame entry. Thus it is a CURRENT-frame entry of its late segment.
The next segment supplies its unique old copy. This proves the geometric
part of LamGeo.LK. At the boundary, the late current-frame letter in segment
J-1 is shared with its translate by -n' in segment 0. LA2 proves the exact
factor relating the corresponding powers, including this wraparound.

These arguments also show that intermediate selected letters never have
an old-frame copy. A variable in an old occurrence can only have plane
coordinate: the exact XX' definition permits normal factors only at late
letters. This support assertion will be used during decoding, not inferred
from a dimension count.

LA2. A closed formula for every coarse exponent
==============================================
Set
  B=a/(8Yk^2Ns), N=floor(B), H=integer_log_p(N), with integer_log_p(0)=0.
The source defines
  R_j=integer_log_p floor(B/p^(z_j-s_j)).
For N>=0, p>=2 and d>=0,
  integer_log_p(N div p^d)=max(integer_log_p N-d,0).
If N<p^d both sides are zero; otherwise dividing p^h<=N<p^(h+1)
proves the formula. Also floor(B/p^d)=floor(B) div p^d. Therefore

  R_j=max(H-(z_j-s_j),0),
  A_j=z_j+R_j=max(z_j,H+s_j).

Both s_j and z_j strictly increase with j. Since all are integers,
  A_(j+1)>=A_j+1.
For x<=z_j the source distance is the actual nonnegative integer z_j-x,
so its definitions Pz and Qc become exactly

  P_j(x)=p^(A_j-x),       Q_j=p^(A_(j+1)-A_j).

In particular Q_j is positive (indeed at least p), and the positive-part
conversion in its source exponent discards nothing. It follows at once that

  P_(j+1)(x)=P_j(x)Q_j for x<=z_j.                     (LA2a)

Periodicity of s and z gives A_(j+J)=A_j+n'. Hence R_(j+J)=R_j,
P_(j+J)(x+n')=P_j(x), Q_(j+J)=Q_j, and

  product_(j=0)^(J-1) Q_j=p^(A_J-A_0)=p^n'.          (LA2b)

For an old/late overlap in LA1, apply (LA2a) to the common current-frame
letter and then translate by a period if needed. Its old power is precisely
its late power multiplied by Q of the late segment. This proves the final
LamGeo.LK clause. The max formula is a shorter human replacement for the
source's truncated-log and exponent case analysis; it is an exact identity,
not a large-parameter approximation.

LA3. Actual segment saturation, finite rank and residue bases
============================================================
For completeness, the actual XX'_j(m,theta,o) is the O-span of separated
products A(plane)G(normal), intersected with an order support condition.
Here A uses all segment letters and G only late letters. For some integer
function M(x), at each late x the pair obeys:

  x even: lambda_max(G, prefix<=x)<=M(x),
          valuation_min(A, prefix<x)>=M(x)+m(x);
  x odd:  lambda_max(G, prefix<x)<=M(x),
          valuation_min(A, prefix<=x)>=M(x)+m(x);
  always: valuation_min(A, prefix<x)>=theta(x).

The lambda/valuation quantities are the maximal/minimal exponents under
the source's GENERIC signed diagonal twist, not bounds at finite-field
points. The final order support condition bounds, at an even x, the total
normal degree; at an odd x, the total plane degree, by o(x).

Shared CG1 identifies this actual module with the span of the paired path
polynomials PsiPl(nu,I)PsiTan(kappa,J) having these same bounds. More
explicitly, if letter i is x, the allowed paired paths satisfy:

  normal letter degree b''_i=0 when x is old;
  for late even x: kappa_(i+1),1+m(x)<=nu_i,r and b''_i<=o(x);
  for late odd x:  kappa_i,1+m(x)<=nu_(i+1),r and b'_i<=o(x);
  for all late x: theta(x)<=nu_i,r.

Each factor has its own signed-letter path of degrees b',b'', and I,J
are standard column indices. The correspondence with prefixes is exact:
prefix<x corresponds to i letters and prefix<=x to i+1 letters. The
ordered enumeration of the finite segment is injective; renaming into
integer-indexed letters embeds the polynomial ring as a coefficient
direct summand. CG1 derives this identity over O and its residue field
from IP's integral signed path basis and simultaneous prefix filtration.
Consequently XX'_O is a span of a subset of an ambient integral basis,
and is saturated in the whole segment polynomial ring. This is the
specific third clause of NestedTypeBound used here. We do NOT use its
separate characteristic-zero joint-isotypic clause.

For the coarse family, put m0=k Ns+1 and use the actual source functions

  Bw(j,y)=floor(a'_(y mod n')/P_j(y))-(k+r+1),
  theta_b(j,x)=(2/3)*(Bw(j,oldest)-m0) if oldest is old,
              =(2/3)*b(oldest) otherwise,
  oc(j,x)=CLa Ns^2 log(a)*a/(p k P_j(x))
                + 4a/P_j(oldest(j,x)) if x even,
           =CLa Ns^2 log(a)*a/(p k P_j(x)) if x odd.

The exact M_j(b) is

  XX'_j(m0,theta_b,oc)
  intersect {monomials m: sum_c m(x,c)=b(x) for every x}
  intersect {monomials m: m(f_(j,i),c)<Q_j for all i<r,c<r}.

CG1's required inequalities hold: m0>=0 and
segN(F,j)+r=#letters_j+r<=Ns+r<=K. Its frame and DVR hypotheses are
literally contained in Dat.Hyp and UniformizedDVR(p,O).

Over a domain, every coefficient-support restriction is saturated: a
nonzero scalar cannot erase a nonzero coefficient at a prohibited
monomial. Intersections preserve saturation, so this proves saturation
of the ACTUAL M_j(b). In particular it is not enough merely to name an
abstract saturated lattice whose identification with M_j has been omitted.

If b vanishes outside the finite segment, its multidegree condition has
finite support: m(x,c)<=b(x), with finitely many x,c and a finite range at
each coordinate. Thus M_j(b) is a submodule of a finite free coefficient
module. A DVR is a noetherian PID, so M_j(b) is finite free. Write its rank
as h. For an O-basis u_1,...,u_h, suppose sum bar(c_i)bar(u_i)=0. Lift the
coefficients c_i to O. Every coefficient of sum c_i u_i is divisible by
the uniformizer pi, so sum c_i u_i=pi P for a polynomial P. Saturation puts
P in M_j(b), and expansion in its O-basis forces each c_i divisible by pi.
The reduced basis is therefore independent over f. Equivalently
  M_j(b) intersect pi O[X] = pi M_j(b).
Rank zero causes no exception: the chosen family is empty.

This gives exactly RestLamA and the finiteness assertion RestFin needed
by this application. The digit support restriction need not select a
subset of the path basis: saturation of its intersection is sufficient.

LA4. Uniform monomial decoding across all label/path families
============================================================
We now define the precise finite family indices. A label ell in LA gives
nonnegative integers ell_(t,c) satisfying
  L_t=sum_c ell_(t,c)<=a'_t,
and, for every late representative x in segment j of t,
  ell_(t,c)<P_j(x),        L_t congruent a'_t modulo P_j(x).
By LA1--LA2 the condition is identical for all periodic representatives.
In particular labels are zero off TT, where a'_t=0. Define
  n_j(x)=(a'_(x mod n')-L_(x mod n'))/P_j(x),
with natural division. Label membership ensures the numerator is both
nonnegative and divisible by P_j(x).

For each j in {0,...,J-1} and i<r, choose e_(j,i) from EE(ell):
  0<=e_(j,i)<rQ_j,
  e_(j,i) congruent n_j(f_(j,i)) modulo Q_j.
Extend e periodically in j, as the source eZ does.

The exact family row degree b_(ell,e,j) is zero outside the segment and
is given there by
  intermediate x:                 n_j(x);
  current-frame x=f_(j,i):         e_(j,i);
  old-frame x=f_(j-1,i):           (n_(j-1)(x)-e_(j-1,i)) div Q_(j-1).
The first two formulas follow immediately from bfam and the absence of
selected letters between adjacent frame entries. The third is the exact
source bfam_at_w_letter_eq; uniqueness of frame entries eliminates every
other summand in its definition. Natural subtraction in the last formula
will be justified in LA5 rather than ignored.

Let S_(ell,e,j) consist of the monomials m in the segment variables with:
  support only in letters_j and no normal variables at old letters;
  row degree b_(ell,e,j);
  m(f_(j,i),c)<Q_j whenever c<r.
Every member of M_j(b_(ell,e,j)) is supported in this set. This follows
directly from the separated-product definition for the first condition
and from the two restrictions in M_j for the other conditions. Reduction
of coefficients cannot create new support.

The assembled exponent is the exact source lamL+sum imgMon:

  N_(t,c)=ell_(t,c)+sum_j sum_(x in letters_j, x mod n'=t) P_j(x)m_j(x,c).

Fix the unique late occurrence x in L_j of t. If it is a current-frame
letter, the only other possible occurrence is its old copy in the next
segment. Periodic shifts are understood at the last segment. For c<r:

  N_(t,c)=ell_(t,c)+P_j(x)*(u+Q_j v),
  u=m_j(x,c),  v=m_(j+1)(x,c),  0<=u<Q_j.           (LA4a)

At j=J-1 the notation for v means m_0(x-n',c), exactly the old occurrence
inside segment 0. This is only periodic notation for the chosen finite
tuple; no additional periodicity hypothesis on its polynomials is needed.

All quantities are nonnegative integers and 0<=ell_(t,c)<P_j(x). Thus
N mod P recovers ell. After subtracting ell and dividing by P, reduction
mod Q recovers u; division by Q then recovers v.

For a nonframe late x there is no old copy, so N=ell+P u; here u needs
no upper bound. At a normal coordinate c>=r the old exponent vanishes
by the support condition, even when x is a frame letter, and the same
one-division recovery applies. Off TT both label and segment exponents
are zero. Hence N determines every label coordinate and every m_j.
The current-frame row sum sum_c m_j(f_(j,i),c)=e_(j,i) then recovers e.

This proves injectivity of
  (ell,e,(m_j)_j) |-> N
on the full disjoint union of all allowed support families. It is stronger
than injectivity within a single fixed label. It is the exact RestLamInj,
with the source RestLamTau (current-frame row sum=e) proved above. The
argument is integral combinatorics and works in every coefficient
characteristic; it uses no Frobenius map on coefficients.

LA5. Exact multidegree and the missing nonnegative subtraction
==============================================================
Here is the narrow numerical input sufficient for the degree calculation:
for every current-frame x=f_(j,i), t=x mod n',

  a'_t/(P_j(x)Q_j) >= k+r+1.                         (LA5a)

RS3 proves the stronger inequality. In fact x belongs to segment j+1,
P_(j+1)(x)=P_j(x)Q_j by LA2, and
  a'_t/P_(j+1)(x)>=(36/5)Y k^2 Ns>=k+r+1.
The last comparison follows from Y,Ns>=1, r<=k and k>=5, which are
already consequences of the source Hyp. Thus (LA5a) holds for every
family simultaneously; it does not depend on ell or e.

Put P=P_j(x), q=Q_j, L=L_t and n=n_j(x). The k label coordinates are all
<P, so L<kP. Because P n=a'_t-L exactly,
  n > a'_t/P-k >= q(k+r+1)-k >= rq.
Since e_(j,i)<rq, this proves e_(j,i)<n, in particular e<=n. Therefore
the natural subtraction n-e agrees with ordinary subtraction. The EE
congruence says q divides n-e; hence

  e+q*((n-e) div q)=n.                              (LA5b)

This elementary direct argument replaces the source's detour through
RestPad and positivity of Bw. It does not assert RestPad itself, whose
other inequalities belong to RS.

Now sum the exponent formula in LA4 over the k coordinates at a fixed t.
At an intermediate position there is only one segment occurrence and
the degree is L_t+P_j(x)n_j(x)=a'_t. At a current-frame position the late
row has degree e and the old row degree (n-e)/q. By (LA5b) its assembled
degree is also L_t+P_j(x)n_j(x)=a'_t. At t outside TT both sides are zero.
This proves exact fixed multidegree for EVERY tuple of allowed support
monomials, so cancellations between polynomial coefficients are irrelevant.

The label inequalities already prove the source IsCLabel condition for
every integer segment j. Thus the pair of assertions is exactly RestLamDeg
1088. No row degree is merely bounded above; equality is essential to
membership in the actual coarse generator set lamGen.

LA6. From primitive segment bases to the complete RestLam inequality
====================================================================
For each finite family f=(ell,e) and each segment j choose the O-basis of
M_j(b_(f,j)) from LA3. Its reductions u_(f,j,i) are linearly independent
over f. All are supported in S_(f,j).

For clarity, construct the ambient injection before taking these subspaces.
The free monomial space on S_(f,j) has basis {X^m:m in S_(f,j)}. The tensor
product over j has basis indexed by tuples (m_j), and the direct sum over
f distinguishes different label/terminal-parameter families. Send its
basis vector to the monomial with exponent
  ell+sum_j imgMon(lamG_j,lamE_j,m_j).
LA4 says those images are all distinct monomials. The map is therefore
injective. Restricting it to the tensor products of the spans of the
u_(f,j,i) stays injective, and their tensor-product bases give

  sum_(ell in LA) sum_(e in EE(ell)) product_j rank_O M_j(b_(ell,e,j))

linearly independent assembled products over the residue field. This
proves exactly lam_li_all in the actual setting, and avoids a false
general assertion that products of independent polynomial families
are automatically independent.

Each product is the reduction of the integral polynomial
  X^ell product_(j=0)^(J-1) phc_j(y_(f,j,i_j)).
The substitutions phc are polynomial algebra maps and commute with any
coefficient ring homomorphism, so reduction is exact. For the source
integer-indexed witness y_j, use these basis vectors for 0<=j<J and zero
for other j. Zero belongs to every remaining M_j. Each chosen factor has
the required actual membership, the label is IsCLabel, and LA5 gives
the exact target row multidegree. Hence the polynomial belongs to lamGen.

The target span LamBar is finite dimensional: it lies in the coefficient
space of all monomials with fixed row degrees a'_t, a finite set. Thus the
independent family just constructed proves

  sum_(ell in LA) sum_(e in EE(ell)) product_(j=0)^(J-1)
       rank_O M_j(b_(ell,e,j)) <= dim_f LamBar.        (LA6)

This is the full source RestLam 1088. If any M_j has rank zero the product
has no basis tuples and contributes zero, exactly as required. The frame
period J is positive by validity; no empty-period convention is silently
used. Empty label/terminal sets simply contribute zero.

What this closes, and what remains separate
===========================================
LA1--LA2 prove all actual LamGeo conditions, including scale periodicity
and wraparound. LA3 proves finite residue-independent bases for the actual
restricted segment spaces using the explicitly shared CG1 identity.
LA4 proves simultaneous injectivity across every family. LA5 proves exact
multidegree and the required nonnegative subtraction from the narrow RS3
bound. LA6 gives the whole RestLam conclusion.

The dimension on the left is not estimated here. RestSeg must exhibit
sufficiently many admissible paths and compare their dimensions; RestSum
must sum the residue/label families quantitatively. The coarse-generator
congruence is another shared version-0.7 proof, not a consequence of this
injection. No spectral, arithmetic-window or prime-selection input follows
from the algebraic assembly alone.

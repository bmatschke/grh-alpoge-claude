## Checks on the proposed global cohomology argument

In `sec17_twisted.tex:391–434` the author treats a totally imaginary field containing the pth roots of unity (the fourth roots at two). He bounds the number of generators of the finite group H² of the restricted Galois group with coefficients Z_p(e+1) via Kummer and the Brauer group. In particular its mod-p rank times log p is at most the logarithm of the class number plus the degree times log p. This can be verified directly: the coefficient exact sequence injects the quotient by p into mod-p cohomology, its twist is trivial under the roots assumption, and the Kummer extension has the p-torsion of the unramified-outside-p Brauer group as quotient and Picard group modulo p as kernel. There are at most [L:Q] relevant local invariant groups, each of order p. The displayed elementary class-number bound follows by comparison with the Dedekind zeta function at 2 and Minkowski. These are size estimates for a *global finite group*.

The remaining exponent argument uses a torsion-free congruence group at level three, transfer, a bounded complex from Ash's retract and the kernel bound for the unstable Hurewicz map. The cardinality estimate for minimal vectors in Bayer-Fluckiger–Emery–Houriet, Corollary 5.2, does have the stated rank ≥5 and F ≠ Q hypotheses; they hold here with N=2(2e)+1 and L totally imaginary. This comparison only checks that input and the generator bound. Treat the proposed replacement as unverified for any native K-theory contract pending verification of the entire comparison, and distinguish it from our requested integral local row maps. Sources: [Bayer-Fluckiger–Emery–Houriet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.5953), [Mathew, Theorem 6.3](https://msp.org/agt/2016/16-2/agt-v16-n2-p12-s.pdf).

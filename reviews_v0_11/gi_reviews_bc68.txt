BC6 OPTIONAL FIXED-TEST DESCENT

For a finite-free clean lattice and fixed K-linear tests, multiply the
evaluation matrix by a common denominator and use ordinary DVR Smith
normal form; undoing that denominator gives integer exponents s_i which
may be negative. If s_i<=a=D ord_O(p), the scalar p^D/pi^s_i is integral,
so the first n such columns provide the literal witness.

For necessity, write the witness after the two integral invertible Smith
changes. If fewer than n exponents are <=a, its n-by-low-count left block
has a nonzero field left-kernel vector. Dividing by its minimum coordinate
valuation gives an integral primitive left-kernel vector. This annihilates
all low exponents exactly, including all negative ones. The remaining
terms have valuation >=a+1, whereas p^D times a primitive coordinate has
valuation a. This is a contradiction. The n=0 case is handled separately.

A finite compatible DVR extension multiplies both s_i and a by the same
ramification index, so the criterion is equivalent after extension. The
report properly confines descent to a fixed scalar-extended family of
tests and assumes the exact scalar-extension identity for the clean
lattice. It does not descend arbitrary new coefficient-field choices.

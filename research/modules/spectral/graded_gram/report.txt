2. PREFIX PROJECTORS, HIGHEST PROJECTION AND WEYL GENERATION (GG2)

2.1. The exact IW height.

For any prefix and any coordinate, every weight in P_d lies in
[-D_minus,D_plus], where D_minus and D_plus are the total covector and vector degrees in that prefix. Its width is D_minus+D_plus<=D. The difference of TWO weights at ONE coordinate is therefore at most D. This is the hypothesis of IW4; the weaker bound on differences between coordinates of one weight would not suffice.

L_d is graded by the weights. It is stable under all root divided powers: for a!=b these are the coefficients of t^n in the elementary substitutions

  X_i,b -> X_i,b+t X_i,a             on vector letters,
  X_i,a -> X_i,a-t X_i,b             on covector letters.

The same holds after restriction to any coordinate set C. Hence IW4 gives, for every canonical C-isotypic projector P,

  p^[binom(|C|,2) Nat.log_p(D+|C|-1)] P(L_d) subset L_d.          (2.1)

The exponent is <=e1 for |C|<=r. A finite sum of isotypic projectors has the same bound, because adding integral vectors introduces no further denominator.

2.2. Self-adjointness and all required commutation.

Directly from the factorial monomial form,

  Fock(L_ab f,g)=Fock(f,L_ba g).

Distinct highest-weight types are orthogonal: the contravariant pairing between two types would otherwise induce a nonzero intertwiner between inequivalent irreducibles. The canonical type projectors are therefore self-adjoint. This is a characteristic-zero algebraic statement; no positivity over Q_p is used.

If j<=j' and C' subset C, the canonical C-type projector for the first j letters commutes with the C' action on those letters and with the action on the disjoint remaining letters. It therefore preserves every C'-isotypic component for the larger prefix j', and commutes with its canonical type projector. Full-weight, letter-degree and unused-coordinate coefficient projections commute whenever the underlying Lie operators do. This proves the complete HypP interface, including its nested-prefix commutation.

2.3. Highest vectors need no extra extremal projector.

Fix a full weight w, and let C=[0,rho). On that weight space, use the C-isotypic projector with highest weight w|C. In a type with highest weight lambda, the lambda-weight space consists precisely of highest vectors. Consequently the projector, composed with the integral full-weight projector, has range

  H_C(w) intersect (full weight w).

Every w-weight vector in a different highest-weight type lies in the sum of lowering-operator images, by PBW generation. Those images are orthogonal to highest vectors by the adjointness identity. Thus this projector has exactly the range, kernel and self-adjointness required by HypE. Its integral cost is the |C|=r-1 case of (2.1), hence at most e1.

This is exactly extP_of, rather than an unproved highest-vector extremal-projector formula. It also explains naturality: any intertwiner preserving the specified full-weight spaces commutes with the resulting highest projection.

7. PRIMITIVE PARTNERS, SMITH FORM AND THE EXACT GRAM EXPONENT (GG7)

The following argument keeps the actual image lattice and avoids any unjustified assertion that positive forms restrict with controlled determinant.

Let G=G_e, let V=Q_p G, and use its literal integral embedding into the monomial coefficient module. Suppose p^b(V intersect Z_p[X]) subset G, and let P be the Fock-orthogonal projection with p^A P(L_d) integral.

  v_p B_d(u,X^alpha)
    =v_p(a)+v_p(prod_(i,j) alpha_i,j!/prod_i d_i!)
    <=b.                                                       (7.1)

The vector h=p^(A+b)P(X^alpha) lies in G: p^A P(X^alpha) is integral in V, and (5.4) then applies. Orthogonality gives

  v_p B_d(u,h) <= A+2b.                                       (7.2)

Thus EVERY primitive u has an actual integral partner h in the same G with the stated bound.

For a Z_p-basis g_1,...,g_k of G, put its Gram matrix in Smith form over Q_p, using left and right matrices in GL_k(Z_p). A row corresponding to a largest Smith valuation is a primitive vector of G. Every pairing of that vector with G is divisible by that elementary divisor. Inequality (7.2) therefore bounds every Smith valuation by A+2b, and also excludes zero elementary divisors. Consequently

  v_p(det(B_d(g_a,g_b))) <= k(A+2b)

Negative Smith valuations cause no problem: multiply the matrix by a power of p to apply ordinary integral Smith form, then subtract that shift. The zero-rank determinant is one. For e>d_K the filtration and G_e are zero. This proves the source GramDeterminantGradedHypothesis for all full weights, all orders and every basis of the actual lattice.

# The level-zero residue for every reached signed word

Fix the selected tied row, two distinct words and its actual translated two-period coefficient at each real place. Keep the comparison scalar in the coefficient, the unitary type frames and the quotient measures throughout. Let $n_0=r+1=m/2$, $d=k+2r+1$, and $N=20n_0$. For every signed history reached by [three-letter scalar sampling](SPECTRAL_THREE_LETTER_SCALAR_SAMPLING.md), the normalized level-zero Gram entry has limit
$$
 \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}
 \frac{G_{0,\varepsilon,v}(w;\varnothing,\varnothing)}
      {\prod_{a\in w}|P_v(Y_a)|}
  =\frac{N_v}{\Gamma_0},\qquad
 \prod_{v\mid\infty}\frac{N_v}{\Gamma_0}=I_N.
 \tag{1}
$$
Here $Y_a=y_a$ for a positive letter and $Y_a=y_a+1$ for its negative companion. The native word weight is the product over these **actual evaluation points**. Each reached word has length at most $6n_0\le N$, and its separated grid keeps both evaluations in the same root-free cut. The factor $N_v$ is the squared norm of the uncut section in the original weighted Bessel measure. The positive constant $\Gamma_0$ is the level-zero measure divisor in Jin's tower. Both point densities and both block stabilizer products equal one at this level. [Native word definition][word] · [Arithmetic level factors][level] · [Native scalar-model comparison][native]

On the regular singleton chart write $G=|\prod_{a=1}^r c_a|^2(|\zeta|^2-T_uT_v)$. The two unipotent integrations and the marking stabilizers preserve $G$, so the radial column of the cut coefficient is $j_\varepsilon=\chi(G/\varepsilon)j$. Its norm is formed with the multiplier $s_q^{1/2}$ in the orthonormal intertwiner frame and the measure $d\mu_0=\prod_a|c_a|^{-2}d\mathrm{vol}$. At level zero all the size-zero minors are one. At a new binary site the all-zero column selects the auxiliary $00$ entry. On the reflected side a path leaving auxiliary zero cannot return to it. Thus the entry times $\Gamma_0$ is exactly
$$
 \int\langle C_0(Y_h)\cdots C_0(Y_1)j_\varepsilon,
                     j_\varepsilon\rangle\,d\mu_0 ,
$$
where the first slot is linear and $C_0(Y)=(-1)^{k-l}\mathcal C(Y)$ in the centered group-operator convention of the cut. In particular the operators also differentiate the cutoff. [Section and measure][section] · [All-zero column and both sign choices][column] · [Native level-zero relation][residue]

For the positive interpolation boxes Jin's adjoint identity gives one-letter energy $|P_v(Y)|$ times the old energy $I$. To pass its integration by parts through the singular boundary, use $\theta=(G/(G+\varepsilon))^{\sigma}$ with $\sigma=4N$. The shift polynomial $G^{-s}g_Y(G^s X)$ has degree at most $r+1$. On a box of length $L$, Markov's derivative estimate and the bound $|(G\partial_G)^p\theta|/p!\le e(4\sigma)^p\theta$ bound the sum of the positive-order terms by half the supremum $E_\varepsilon$ of the normalized regulated energies. Hence $E_\varepsilon\le2I$. Fatou gives square integrability of the jets. Their integrability permits taking the regulator to zero in the adjoint identity, giving the exact coefficient $|P_v(Y)|$. The weight ratios and the residue difference have absolute bounds $C_d,C_P,C_R$, with $C_7=(1+C_R)^2C_dC_P$. One can take $L\ge\max\{N,16\sigma(r+1)^2(1+2e\sqrt{C_7})\}$, with $6NL\le I_{\rm cut}$ and $dL\le I_{\rm cut}$. Such boxes fit in the selected cut. Choose $L=C(r+1)^3$ with $C$ fixed first. Both required widths are $O(k^4)$, while $I_{\rm cut}=(\mathcal D-5)/2$ and $\mathcal D\ge(kf\log b)^5$. The actual-section adjoint, weighted integrability and common-shift factorization are checked before the regulator is removed. Iterating on the positive boxes gives convergence at a tensor grid of $d+1$ points per variable. At each fixed cutoff the scalar energy above is a polynomial of separate degree at most $d$, independent of the positive or reflected realization at the same $Y_a$. The candidate limit $N_v\prod_a|P_v(Y_a)|$ is also polynomial on this cut, since each factor has constant sign. Finite tensor interpolation therefore gives (1) for every admitted parameter tuple, including shifted negative letters outside the auxiliary positive boxes. Any fixed positive rescaling of the cutoff parameter still tends to zero. The native model can put a comparison scalar outside its radial norm. Jin's section norm $N_v$ includes the comparison in the coefficient. With the transported self-dual finite and original real measures, Jin computes $I_{\rm sep}=K_{\rm loc}^{f}\prod_v N_v$ and $M_{\rm ar}c_N=(K_{\rm loc}\Gamma_0)^f$, yielding the second identity in (1). First remove auxiliary compact support with a positive Gram cutoff held fixed. In the chosen decay class compact approximation gives convergence in the derivative seminorm, in each fixed-head pairing and at each fixed regular terminal point. For the latter, the two-unipotent integral has the integrable majorant $C_\gamma N(u)^{-(a-3b_0)}N(u')^{-b_0}$, with $a\ge4b_0$. The normalized finite-grid interpolation probabilities have a convergent subsequence in the simplex and each finite Gram inequality passes to it. Now fix a finite tower. The empty terminal transform on each positive Gram point equals its uncut value for small enough $\varepsilon$ by invariance of $G$. Zero-density points have zero weighted vector. Simultaneously every reached initial energy is within any prescribed tolerance of $I_N$, since the set of reached words at that row is finite. Sending the tolerance to zero preserves the multiplier of the finite-tower inequality. This order of limits permits cutoffs depending on the tower, without a common convergence rate over growing ranks. Passing from finite towers to a whole row uses the row's absolute orbital convergence.

# Orthogonality after the line and plane comparisons

On the first mixed square $(p,q)=(1,1)$, both sources have rational Witt index one and the target has index two. Let $a,n$ be the source ranks and $d=a+n$. Let $F=J_b(\Phi)-cJ_s(\Phi')$ be a polynomial theta difference at fixed finite level, with the small test primed at a split place in the barred model. Suppose its line and plane constant terms vanish for all rational isotropic lines and planes. Then $F=0$ under the standard local matching and split distribution inputs. This implication applies to any $c$, so a nonzero boundary detector determines which $c$ can satisfy the assumption. It separates the final global pairing from the comparison of the two line coefficients. [Pinned implication from orthogonality][perp]

Every proper parabolic of the target is contained in a maximal parabolic stabilizing a line or a plane, up to rational conjugacy. Transitivity of integration over the unipotent radicals now gives cuspidality. The [mixed source estimates](../construction/ROW_MIXED_INTEGRABILITY.md) prove absolute source integration and target $L^2$ on the indicated tests. The pointwise estimates apply to real derivatives as well, since differentiating the Schwartz test changes only its seminorm. In the polynomial module the common local irreducible target quotient has fixed infinitesimal character. The differences are smooth of uniform moderate growth, compact-finite at infinity and finite-level. Cuspidal rapid decay therefore applies on target Siegel sets. [Miller and Schmid, Theorems A and B](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.2149) · [Application to the two theta images][rapid]

Fix a rational prime $\ell$ completely split in $E$ outside the finite exceptional set, including the places supporting priming or any auxiliary changes made below. In the barred split coordinates the quotient map on each rectangular test space has kernel spanned by source translates minus original tests. Indeed the [two-rectangle sieve and induced representation](../construction/ROW_FIRST_BETA_STEP.md#the-good-place-kernel-on-both-rectangular-blocks) work for $r=a$ and $r=n$. The one possible zero-degree sieve at index $d/2$ is excluded by the infinite order of the good local uniformizer phase. The proof of this character property uses the realized CM weight and complete splitting in $E$. The absolute integrals on the big side and the small side, where the primed place is retained, kill all those local relations. Thus for each $v\mid\ell$, $F$ has a local realization as a finite sum of maps out of one common irreducible target module $P_v$, on which either rectangular test space surjects. The preimage of the target space of rapidly decreasing functions in a finite direct sum of copies of $P_v$ is a submodule and admits an equivariant projection. Composing with this projection gives realizations of $F$ with rapidly decreasing values using either of the two rectangles. [Detailed local construction](../construction/ROW_FIRST_BETA_STEP.md#orthogonality-in-the-first-beta-step) · [Split character and distribution](../theta/ROW_DEFINITE_REVERSE_LIFT.md)

Consider one source $Z$ of rank $r$ with a test $\Psi$ of full row rank at a split place away from $\ell$. Its reverse integral against $F$ is
$$
 T_\Psi(g)=\int_{[U(W)]} F(h)\overline{\theta_{Z,W}(\Psi;g,h)}\,dh .
$$
The full-rank matrix coefficient estimate gives a bound polynomial in the target height, uniform in the source variable. It follows by comparing the source logarithmic parameter with the largest target parameter and spending arbitrarily many Schwartz powers when the former is larger. When it is smaller all torus weights are bounded in terms of the target parameters alone. Cuspidal rapid decay and finite target volume give absolute convergence. At $v\mid\ell$ use the realization of $F$ in the *other* rectangular space. Stacking the $r$ and $d-r$ rows makes the pairing a distribution on $d\times d$ matrices with the specified right determinant character. The [rank-stratum calculation](../theta/ROW_DEFINITE_REVERSE_LIFT.md) kills the singular strata for the good character. The equivariant density on the open orbit has the corresponding left determinant law. After inserting the two Weil factors this gives $T_\Psi(gu_v)=T_\Psi(g)$ for every $u_v\in U(Z)(F_v)$. For the two choices of $Z$ the determinant characters are reciprocal, both satisfying the good-place condition. [Both orientations of the reverse integral][orth]

For the small source the test already has the necessary full-rank support. On the big source use the row sieve at another good place to replace its finite factor by one of full row rank with the same class modulo source translations. Unrestricted big-source absolute convergence proves that the integrated theta function is unchanged. Rational isotropy gives a source torus scaling a Witt pair by $\ell^k,\ell^{-k}$, acting trivially on its complement. Its components away from $\ell$ range in a compact subgroup and fix a given smooth finite vector for all multiples of one positive integer. Rational left invariance and the local right invariances at $\ell$ therefore identify $T_\Psi(1)$ with its value on the archimedean source torus along that sequence. For fixed $h$ this torus tends to infinity in the unique expanding source coordinate. Full row rank forces a nonzero rational minor in the squared-theta sum and gives arbitrary decay in that coordinate. Thus the kernel tends to zero pointwise in $h$. The same target-height bound against $|F|$ supplies an integrable majorant independent of $k$, so $T_\Psi(1)=0$. Source translates preserve full rank in the split chart, and covariance gives $T_\Psi(g)=0$ for every $g$. [Torus decay and uniform majorization][decay] · [Explicit rank-one source torus argument](../construction/ROW_FIRST_BETA_STEP.md#orthogonality-in-the-first-beta-step)

Fubini applies using finite source volume and the same uniform majorant. It identifies the pairing of each forward lift with $F$ as the integral of the conjugate reverse lift, hence zero. For original big tests the earlier sieve preserves the forward lift. Taking their difference gives $\|F\|_2^2=0$, and continuity gives $F=0$ pointwise. The two isotropic sources let the same argument be used twice. The [line calculation](../construction/ROW_MIXED_LINE_REMAINDER.md) and the [plane calculation](../construction/ROW_MIXED_PLANE_TERM.md) identify the separate boundary information needed to use this conclusion.

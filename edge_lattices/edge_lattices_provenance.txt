## EL2: The exact polynomial coefficient model is full, finite free, and basis independent

Status: **derived_alternative_proof**.

The integral dual-Weyl polynomial module is the kernel of finitely many coefficient equations in a finite monomial module. It equals the integral-coefficient intersection with its fraction-field version. DVR algebra and clearing denominators prove fullness and freeness. The determinant-twisted inverse row action gives H_lambda(gO^k), independent of g and covariant under GL.

This report replaces the source's explicit semistandard-basis proof of fullness by finite polynomial equations, clearing denominators, and finite-free module algebra over a DVR. It proves the source's actual DVR specialization; it does not claim the archive uses this proof.

## EL3: Elementary row-degree bound by a generic block column operation

Status: **derived_alternative_proof**.

For f in the column-invariant degree-nu module, every monomial has total degree at most sum_{i<=r}nu_i in the first r rows. Localize the generic matrix at the determinant of its top-left r-by-r block and kill the top-right block by earlier-column additions. The required upper-unipotent coefficients are independent of the row-scaling indeterminate, so the degree bound descends by injectivity of localization.

Self-contained human proof in Section 4 of edge_lattices_report.txt and Lemma edge-weight of the main fragment. The cited source uses a semistandard basis plus bideterminant dominance instead. There is no source declaration claimed for the Gaussian-elimination proof.

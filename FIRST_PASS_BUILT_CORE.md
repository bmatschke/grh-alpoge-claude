# The first-pass core on the original character choice

Fix the depth of the untwisted first pass. Work on the common eventual range of its pinned column, its fourth stage and its weight bound. For each admissible base in that range, the construction produces a late stage $Z$, a core $c$ there and a built witness with the certified kernel entries. Its character choice, row, active set and grid come from the given first-pass origin. This is the first occurrence of $\mathrm{BuiltE}$ in the two-pass argument. ## The choices that have to agree

Use the split column, group data, ordinary row and fitted character level of this very fourth stage $l$. On it choose the two ambient lattices, the divisor $Y$ with its comparison for the auxiliary-prime sets $Q_p$, and the theta object with its tie to $l.n$. The divisor construction also supplies a statement about every future assembled object with those same divisor and row entries. Keep that implication until the object exists. [Ambient lattices](continuation_v78/producer.tex#L60-L98) · [Divisor and its object condition](continuation_v78/producer.tex#L538-L590) · [Theta on the fourth-stage row](FIRST_PASS_FOURTH_STAGE_THETA.md)

The column fixes the integer $16k\lceil x^{c_D}\rceil$ on this range. The Frobenius-orbit data and shifted weight lists obey that bound for every word, served place and compatible size record. Their construction works for each ensuing choice of local levels and maps. Now invoke the integral kernel construction with **this** $Y$, its $Q_p$ comparison, $l.n$ and the actual tuple comparison. For the global entry record it constructs place embeddings, adapted bases and the coefficient projections. At every served place the local series recovers that very record under the required change of variables. The comparison installs the local maps into the lattice layer without changing its weight data. This supplies the lattice layer, the kernel family over $Y$, and the equality between its entry record and the formula in the adapted bases. The host supplies compatible adelic group and fibre measures. The split-column comparison uses $l$'s own rational generators, level, local diamond operators and ramified form lattices. It gives the column-layer conditions for precisely those entries used in the object. The commensurability integer here may depend on the word and coefficient place. ## Why this gives the required core

Form the object $O$ by putting the chosen $Y$, theta, lattice layer, kernel, ambient forms and Haar measures in the entries named above. Take the remaining entries, in particular the error term, level, group, twists, $p_0$, character and depth data, from $l$. The divisor's universal implication now specializes to $O$. The three equalities it needs say that its divisor, $p_0$ and row data are the ones just installed. These hold by construction. [The assembled object and its divisor witness][obj]

Two small comparisons verify the forced and untwisted rows. Outside the active set a nonzero forced valuation at lift $j$ would put the place in that set by its definition at $j-1$. Thus the forced valuation vanishes for every fitting size record. If a place has the reference tuple and were active, the twist character there would be both nontrivial by the fitted character choice and one by its reference value. Thus the row's untwisted identities apply. The sharp weight, rational roots, metrics, stability and isometry come from the same row and divisor comparisons. The fourth-stage group data supply the numerical group condition and the character tie supplies its local parameters. These checks give the core $c$. [Valuation and untwisted rows][off] · [Core fields][core]

Pair $c$ with the original pin, coherent twists, split column, fitted row, spectral and character witnesses. Its stored kernel is the one used to form $O$. Hence the entry equality just proved supplies the additional certificate in $\mathrm{BuiltE}$. Its character-origin slot is exactly $l.nx$, including the original saturated package and the grid at the requested depth. One intersects the eventual ranges only in their original parameter order. This assembly introduces no subsequent stage change. [The retained witnesses and return value][built]

Export is quantified over every built first-pass core with this kind of origin. The existence just proved gives the input to that operation. To continue to the second pass one must also export the simultaneous twist conditions at the original depth, enlarge the base, and construct the strengthened slots there.

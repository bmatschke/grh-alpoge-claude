Scope
-----
This component proves the exact joins between the completed local
theorems and their actual arithmetic-family use. GJ1 is independent of
the eventual numerical supply. GJ2 supplies the scale estimates used by
GI. GJ3 identifies the actual cyclic orbit coordinates and their counts.
GJ4 proves the actual local-to-global orbit implication from its stated
inputs; GJ5 records its uniform family consequence and its remaining
construction hypotheses. No existence of a full RH arithmetic family,
theta identity, cohomological window, or simultaneous prime pair is
inferred here.

Notation. The source's global rank is k=D.R.k, and the number of chain
levels is m_ch=D.R.m. The local highest-weight module in the Type-I
theorem has local parameter m_local=k-1; these two m's are different.
Write n=D.R.n and let TT be the selected subset of the finite additive
group A=D.R.A. A fixed family has 0<eps<1/52. Its scale is
  a=x exp(x^eps), X'=exp(x^(eps/3)), b'=a/X',
  delta1=x^(-eps/8), A_scale=26, Y=x^13,
  p0=k^2 x^26.
All constants are fixed before x and before the varying family index.
The ceiling used for certificate heights is the natural-valued ceiling
ceil_+(z)=max(0,ceil(z)); this matters when GJ1 quantifies over arbitrary
degree functions. The rank-rounding ceiling at T0>0 is the ordinary
integer ceiling and agrees with its natural-valued version.

GJ1. THE UNIVERSAL TYPE-I DEFICIT IS ATTAINED
-------------------------------------------
Fix the global numerical data, a prime p, an orbit period n', a selected
set T of positions and two integer degree families a,b. Define S to be
the set of real q>=0 for which the literal LPprime certificate at
  height ceil_+(tIf), depth DIf, conductor 8(DIf+1)
holds for EVERY permitted unramified-quadratic coefficient setting and
EVERY permitted dominant weight with the named extremes and small middle
coordinates. These are exactly kapISet, not a deficit defined for one
chosen representation or one chosen ring.

The zero certificate puts 1 in S, and S is bounded below by 0. Let
q0=inf S. Thus 0<=q0<=1. We prove q0 belongs to S with its universal
quantifiers intact. Fix an arbitrary permitted ring, dominant weight
and target lattice. Its representation dimension d is an integer.
Put T0=(1-q0)d. If T0<=0 use the zero certificate. If T0>0 then d>0 and
  eta=(T0-(ceil(T0)-1))/d > 0.
By the defining property of the infimum, choose q in S with q<q0+eta.
The certificate provided by q at the fixed target has integer rank R
satisfying
  R >= (1-q)d > ceil(T0)-1.
Hence R>=ceil(T0)>=T0. All its lattice membership, vanishing, depth and
matrix conditions are unchanged, so it is a q0 certificate. Since the
fixed ring, weight and target were arbitrary, q0 is admissible for all
of them. The witnesses may vary with those objects, exactly as the
definition permits. There is no need for a uniform upper bound on d.

Consequently the source's canonical choice X.kapI=inf kapISet satisfies
  0<=X.kapI<=1, X.kapI belongs to kapISet,
  X.kapI<=q for every admissible q.
The first two assertions supply literal AuxBlock8.adm7, even before
the additional good-orbit and numerical hypotheses in that interface
are used. GI uses the last inequality to transfer TC6's averaged loss
to the canonical family deficit. This is a reuse of the finite-rank
idea in TC5 at the stronger, universal family-level definition.

The auxiliary Inst data can always be normalized to this definition:
replace only its kapI field by the displayed infimum, and its ep field
by ceil(Ce f k^2 log_p(a)). Its chosen orbit representatives and offset
functions are unchanged. This proves exists_inst_defs. For a supplied
FamP the source also obtains KapIDef from its actual Extra record; the
normalization statement alone is not an assertion that an arbitrary
pre-existing family's representations have been rebuilt.
More explicitly, for x>Fm.x1 the record Fm.ex(x,hx,i) has the field
kapI_def: KapIDef(Fm.Gx(x,i),Fm.GK). This is exactly the equality with
the infimum used above. Thus the actual family's existing kapI already
has the required canonical definition. This is the short proof of
PlaneCountTheoremWithConstants.kapIDefF, rather than a proposed change
of the family's degree data or orbit selectors.

GJ2. UNIFORM SCALE BOUNDS FROM THE LOGARITHMIC WINDOW BOUNDS
---------------------------------------------------------
Use GII2 with the fixed r=rG(eps) and h=2+8eps to obtain one Ce>0,
depending only on eps, for which
  G'<=Ce log(x)/delta1,  1<=N1<=Ce log(x)      (x>=3).
Suppose also 1<=k<=x^6 and c_Lambda=K.La>0. Set
  Lambda=c_Lambda N1^2 log(a).
For every eta>0, uniformly for these data and all sufficiently large x,
  x^(eps-eta)<=Lambda<=x^(eps+eta),                         (GJ2.1)
and for Q=p^(G') with prime p,
  p<p0  => log Q <= x^(eps/8+eta),
  p<X'  => log Q <= x^(11eps/24+eta).                      (GJ2.2)
These are the literal fields of AuxBlock8.adm5(eta).

Here is a direct sufficient threshold. Write l=log x and
  M=2c_Lambda Ce^2+38Ce+1/c_Lambda.
Choose x>=3 so that M l^3<=x^eta. Such a threshold depends only on
M,eta; for example it is enough to require
  log x >= max(1,24M/eta^4),
because exp(eta log x)>=(eta log x)^4/24. Thus no unspecified family
index enters the choice.

Since log a=l+x^eps, l>=1 and x^eps>=1, we have
  c_Lambda x^eps <= Lambda
     <= 2c_Lambda Ce^2 l^3 x^eps <= x^(eps+eta).
The lower bound in (GJ2.1) follows from 1/c_Lambda<=x^eta. For p<p0,
  p<k^2 x^26<=x^38,
so
  log Q=G' log p <=38Ce l^2 x^(eps/8)
                     <=x^(eps/8+eta).
For p<X', log p<x^(eps/3), and therefore
  log Q<=Ce l x^(eps/8+eps/3)
             <=x^(11eps/24+eta).

For the literal family, SizesAt includes
  k<=x^(9/2+10eps+eps/100)<x^6.
The same Ce works for every family member, and the finite initial
threshold also includes the family start and x>=3. This proves the
actual family_sizes_bound, with constants chosen before all family
indices. It is important that A_scale=26; the Type-I averaging width
is Y=x^13. Later uses take eta=eps/48.

GJ3. ACTUAL ORBIT COORDINATES AND COUNTS
---------------------------------------
The source's orbit selector b_h(t)=X.bs(h,t) satisfies
  t=b_h(t)+i h for some i>=0,  b_h(t+h)=b_h(t).
Iteration gives b_h(t+ih)=b_h(t). Substituting the first identity
proves b_h(b_h(t))=b_h(t). Thus every representative t0 in the image
of b_h is fixed by b_h.

For a class-zero prime the actual step is h=hP(D,p), not 2h, and
the period is n'=ord(h). The map
  phi: {0,...,n'-1}->A,  i |-> t0+ih
is injective: equality implies n' divides i-j, while both indices
lie in one period. Its image is exactly the orbit of t0. Restriction
of phi identifies
  T={i: t0+ih belongs to TT}
with the source topsOn set {t in TT:b_h(t)=t0}. For surjectivity write
t=b_h(t)+ih and reduce i modulo n'. For injectivity use phi's
injectivity. Therefore
  Nv(X,p,t0)=|T|,
  wPos(p,t0,a)(i)=a(phi(i)).                             (GJ3.1)
Different representatives have disjoint topsOn sets, so for every
set of representatives R,
  sum_(t0 in R) Nv(X,p,t0)<=|TT|.                        (GJ3.2)
This is also the exact marginalization map used in GW.

The actual GatTies and LatTied hypotheses identify this cyclic set
with the selected positions in the coefficient representation indexed
by v=Y.vOf(u,w,t0). They state its period equals n', its local parameter
is k-1, its selected extremes are the pulled-back aL,bL, and its other
coordinates have absolute value bounded by the filling's integer c.
Since that c<=cG as real numbers, c<=floor(cG). Consequently the
representation is literally a dominant base weight with the local
bound floor(cG), after the unique casts of the finite index types.
No additional permutation or independent choice of an orbit is used.

GJ4. THE ACTUAL FINITE-PLACE ORBIT IMPLICATION
--------------------------------------------
Assume the following named mathematical inputs at D,L,X,Y,K:
 (i) the two local family interfaces adm6 and adm7;
 (ii) HypIAt and HypIIAt on every candidate prime and good orbit;
 (iii) rho<=rhoB;
 (iv) actual compatible standard coefficient subrings (StdSubAt),
      and the forward LPBaseChange theorem proved in BC;
 (v) the actual GatTies and LatTied identifications of GJ3.
Then the source OrbitLatticeBounds holds with the EXACT source
height, depth, deficit and conductor. We now prove this implication.

Fix a word u, finite place w, and represented orbit t0 in the served
set VV. Its definition gives Cand(p), with p the residue characteristic,
and t0 in goodReps. Let n'=ord hP and T=topsPos. GJ3 identifies its
representation with the local one and gives Nv=|T| and nP=n'. The
actual StdSubAt supplies a standard coefficient DVR Os inside the
actual coefficient DVR O, with compatible fraction fields, embeddings,
Frobenius and a chosen square root w_s of p. First work over Os.

If p<p0, HypIIAt gives all literal numerical/window/good-orbit
hypotheses used by adm6. The actual representation rank parameter is
r=rG, by LatTied. Apply the Type-II local family interface. Unfolding
the definitions gives
  t_v=tIIf(K,L,p,n',|T|), D_p=DIIf(K,L,p,n'),
  kappa_v=min(1,K.kII |T| rG^2 k x^-26).
Thus its conclusion already has exactly the desired numerical values.

If p>=p0, HypIAt supplies the large-prime local inputs and the weight
windows. Cand gives p<X' and excludes InBand, whose relative width is
rhoB. Since X'>0 and rho<=rhoB, every interval defining InBand0 is
contained in the corresponding interval defining InBand. Hence the
excluded-band hypothesis for adm7 holds. Its representation rank
parameter is 1, again by LatTied. The canonical deficit from GJ1 is
admissible at the prescribed tIf,DIf. Here the definitions give
  t_v=tIf(K,L,p,n',T,wPos(aL),wPos(bL)),
  D_p=DIf(K,L,p,n'), kappa_v=X.kapI(p,n',T,wPos(aL),wPos(bL)).

In either case BC carries the resulting LPprime certificate from Os
to O with the SAME t=ceil_+(t_v), D=D_p, kappa=kappa_v and
conductor 8(D_p+1). The chosen image of w_s remains a square root
of p, although it need not be a uniformizer of O. No ramification
factor is inserted into these p-normalized quantities. The exact
finite-index identifications in GJ3 complete the literal statement
of OrbitLatticeBounds at this u,w,t0. All were arbitrary.

If VV is empty there is no served orbit to check. This case makes the
conditional orbit conclusion vacuous; it does not establish existence
or abundance of served orbits. That is why the bad-orbit and global
weight-selection work is tracked separately in GII and GW.

GJ5. UNIFORM FAMILY ASSEMBLY AND ITS SCOPE
-----------------------------------------
For a supplied FamP, the source supplies a common constant tuple K,
uniform SizesAt bounds, GatTies and LatTied. The actual standard
subrings are supplied by the ring field of GroupCompatibilityFamily,
or by the optional sufficient recC/IsRecCEv identification in BC.
The corresponding construction hypotheses remain explicit.
GI proves the Type-I HypIAt supply and
canonical local mean, and GII proves the Type-II HypIIAt supply and
local interface. GJ1 supplies adm7. These proofs select thresholds
independently of the varying family index i. Take the maximum of their
finitely many thresholds and the family's start. GJ4 then applies
simultaneously to every member at every x above this maximum.

The sufficient Type-I coefficients are
  K.La>=880, K.kI>=2800001, K.DI>=2800001,
  K.rho>=5600002,
  K.kappaThresholdOne>=604228,
  K.kappaThresholdTwo>=302114,
with Cm=302114 for the mean (or any larger fixed Cm).
The other components record the Type-II coefficient inequalities and
the logarithmic-window coefficient K.CN. This finite vector is chosen
before x. Enlarging the fixed coefficients only changes the eventual
thresholds in the polynomial-versus-exponential comparisons.

This is a uniform theorem about the supplied arithmetic-family data.
Its scope does not assert existence of the full FamP with the chosen
subset, degree assignment, standard subrings, period/chain maps and
analytic/cohomological requirements all realized together. In
particular:
 * GII's uniform Bernoulli argument constructs a subset with TopsOK.
   The actual host additionally uses biased coset probabilities and
   Frame.Shares; these and the host-identification join remain separate.
 * GW constructs a single degree pair with WReq. HW follows the active
   host preselection and fixed-pair family transport, keeping its
   upstream host and point constructors as explicit inputs. CoverAll
   is an optional stronger route, not a necessary input to this one.
 * BC proves standard subrings for its actual recC setting and forward
   transfer, and applies directly to the ring field of an actual
   GroupCompatibilityFamily. Constructing its remaining local places,
   characters, dimensions and comparison lattices is still separate;
   arbitrary FamP data are not silently identified with these choices.
The new frontier and source target map retain these precise joins.

RESULT AND SCOPE

This component supplies the initial unitary finite-adelic realizers that
were explicitly retained in FC3, FC19, AD3 and NB6. It proves the actual
exists_isPtOf_cm conclusion under its literal CMData and valid-decoration
premises, including every IsPtOf field: unitarity, the exact carried global
lattice in the full adelic sense, selected residue functionals, every
auxiliary residue coordinate, and exact rational frame matrices at twist
primes. It also verifies the set/orientation checks in exists_ptOf_twists.

The proof proceeds directly through local integral Hermitian bases and the
restricted product. The root arithmetic host, its CM-field condition,
selected prime/character data and original column-law records remain
supplied. No proof of their existence, no full RH/GRH conclusion, no
rational strong approximation theorem, and no Lean kernel replay is claimed.

The main substantive replacement is useful in two ways. First it proves
the local norm-surjectivity and Hermitian diagonalization arguments even
at dyadic primes, with no hidden division by 2. Second it constructs the
finite-adelic object directly, avoiding a stronger rational isometry route
that is present in the source but unnecessary for this particular endpoint.
The exact source distinction is recorded below.

EXACT INPUTS AND CONVENTIONS

Let E/F be the supplied quadratic CM extension. Its actual involution fixes
exactly F, and the Hermitian form on E^n is h(x,y)=bar(x)^t y, conjugate
linear in the first variable. A full O_E-lattice N is self-dual when

  N = {y in E^n : h(x,y) in O_E for every x in N}.

This is the O_E-valued Hermitian dual, not a trace dual.

CMData contains [E:F]=2, identification of the fixed field with F, and
cmUnram. The last part of its literal IsCMUnram condition says that for
every maximal prime P of O_E there exists theta in O_E such that

  theta - bar(theta) is not in P.

This is the actual hypothesis used to establish unramified local algebra.
It is not an unstated assumption that the local extension happens to be
unramified. The other cmUnram clauses make E totally complex and identify
the involution with complex conjugation under all complex embeddings.

At a finite prime v of F, set K=F_v, R=O_(F_v),
B=E tensor_F K and A=product_(P|v) O_(E_P). These are the actual completed
algebra and its integral subring. The involution exchanges conjugate
components; it is not being treated as an automorphism of an individual
split component. B's fixed field/algebra is K, by writing elements in the
base-changed F-basis 1,theta. Its fixed integral subring is R, because a
scalar has nonnegative valuation in a finite extension exactly when it
has nonnegative valuation in K.

DR1 — THE LOCAL INTEGRAL HERMITIAN NORMAL FORM

1. Derive the exact quadratic etale integral algebra.

Choose P over v and theta from the cmUnram condition. In the quadratic
extension there are at most two primes over v and, if distinct, they are
P and bar(P). The same delta=theta-bar(theta) is a unit at both, because
bar(delta)=-delta. Hence delta is a unit of A.

For any a in A define

  b=(a-bar(a))/(theta-bar(theta)),  c=a-b*theta.

Both are integral and fixed, hence in R. Therefore A=R+R*theta. The sum is
direct because theta is nonfixed. Its monic quadratic polynomial has the
actual trace/norm coefficients in R, and its discriminant delta^2 is a
unit of R. Consequently the residue algebra is a separable quadratic
algebra: either k x k or the quadratic finite field k_2/k. The involution
exchanges its two roots or is the nontrivial finite-field involution.

This argument also identifies A with the actual full integral order. It
does not merely exhibit a quadratic suborder and then assume it is maximal.
Formula (DR.3) proves that every integral element lies in R+R*theta.

If the residue polynomial splits, lift its two simple roots by successive
linear corrections a_(m+1)=a_m+varpi^m*t_m, solving the derivative equation
modulo varpi at each step. Completeness gives the two roots, still separated
by a unit. Chinese remaindering gives A=R x R. If the residue polynomial is
irreducible, every element outside varpi*A has an inverse: lift a residue
inverse and invert the remaining 1+varpi*z by a convergent geometric series.
Since A is finite free over the complete DVR R, each nonzero element is a
finite power of varpi times a unit. This gives precisely an unramified
quadratic DVR, with residue field k_2. These deductions are valid at p=2.

2. Prove the unit norm map is onto, including dyadic primes.

For k=F_q and k_2=F_(q^2), the finite-field norm is z -> z^(q+1) and its
image has q-1 elements, so it is onto k^x. The trace z+z^q is k-linear into
k and is nonzero: its defining polynomial is nonzero of degree q<q^2,
even when the characteristic is 2. Hence it too is onto.

Given u in R^x, choose b_1 in A^x whose norm agrees with u modulo varpi.
If N(b_m)-u=varpi^m*c_m, choose z_m with residual trace -c_m/u and put

  b_(m+1) = b_m * (1+varpi^m*z_m).

For m>=1, the quadratic term varpi^(2m)*N(z_m) lies in varpi^(m+1), so
the norm error improves to the next power. The coefficient of the linear
term is N(b_m), whose residue is u, so the sign and divisor in the trace
equation above are exact. The successive differences are in varpi^m*A;
the limit exists by completeness and remains in the same nonzero residue
class. Its norm is u. No step divides by 2 or supposes Tr(1) is nonzero.
For A=R x R, the norm is (a,b) -> ab and its unit surjectivity is immediate.

3. Find a norm-one vector and split it off integrally.

A perfect integral Hermitian lattice over the nonsplit DVR A is free.
Its Gram determinant is a unit and the reduced pairing is nondegenerate.
If every vector in the reduced space had zero self-pairing, then for every
x,y,a one would have

  0=h(x+a*y,x+a*y)=a*h(x,y)+bar(a)*overline(h(x,y)).

Choose h(x,y) nonzero by nondegeneracy. Varying a makes the right side
the trace of every element of k_2, contradicting surjectivity. This is the
needed characteristic-two argument: the alternating-bilinear shortcut is
not used. Lift a vector with nonzero self-pairing to the lattice. Its norm
is a unit of R. Divide by a unit with that norm using the previous step.

A norm-one vector v gives the integral projection x -> v*h(v,x), so the
lattice is Av direct-sum (v-perp intersect M). The complement is free and
perfect. A vector in its fraction-field span pairing integrally with the
complement also pairs integrally with v (the pairing is zero); selfduality
of M therefore puts it in M and then in the complement. Induction gives
an orthonormal integral basis. Rank zero is immediate.

For the split algebra R x R, the Hermitian form pairs the two R-lattice
components perfectly. Choose a basis in one component and the dual basis
in the other. Their paired vectors form an A-basis with Gram matrix I.
Thus the local normal-form conclusion holds in both alternatives.

DR2 — ASSEMBLE THE ACTUAL FINITE-ADELIC LATTICE REALIZER

Let N=N# be a full O_E-lattice in standard E^n. Clearing generators and a
field basis yields a nonzero rational integer d with
d*O_E^n subset N subset d^-1*O_E^n. Thus N is the standard lattice away
from finitely many primes. Local duality is the conjugate-transpose
inverse-basis calculation: at a split P it uses the conjugate prime,
and at a fixed prime it is the usual local Hermitian dual. Completion
retains that matrix formula, so each completed lattice is perfect.

At a nonsplit prime, DR1 gives an orthonormal basis of the actual completed
N. Its column matrix g_v is unitary and carries the integral standard
lattice onto that module. At a split pair choose any local basis matrix
B_P for N_P and set the conjugate component equal to conjugate(B_P)^(-t).
Selfduality says exactly that this is a basis at the conjugate prime.
Choose identity everywhere the lattice is standard.

The family is an actual element of E tensor_F A_(F,f), not merely a list
of local objects. Expand the entries and inverse entries in a fixed
F-basis 1,theta of E. Every coefficient equals its identity-matrix
coefficient outside finitely many v; hence it is a finite adele of F.
The two corresponding tensor terms construct every matrix entry.
The componentwise inverse exists in the same restricted product, and
unitarity holds at every component, hence globally.

The local basis equalities imply

  g * widehat(O_E)^n = product_P widehat(N_P) = widehat(N).

The final equality is the full integral adelic-span identity already
proved in AD4 with the finite sandwich and Chinese remainder theorem.
It is an equality for arbitrary adelic vectors and therefore proves
the literal Carries predicate. Intersecting with rational vectors gives
globLat(g)=N: in a rational local basis completed membership is exactly
nonnegativity of rational coefficient valuations, and LP1 recovers N.

DR3 — INSTALL THE EXACT DECORATION AND RATIONAL FRAMES

Let p be a valid framed decoration with selfdual lattice N, finite type
prime set ST, auxiliary set SG, and rational-frame set Q. The sets are
pairwise disjoint and their union contains no conjugate pair. This is
the exact source condition, not merely an unspecified finite support
condition. In particular each selected prime is in a split pair, so
its conjugate component can be chosen independently by the unitary
inverse-transpose rule.

Choose a rational O_(P)-basis B_P of N_P at every selected prime. It is
an E-basis because N is full. At a type prime the nonzero functional
on N_P induces a nonzero row r on k_P^n. Complete a basis of its kernel
with a vector of value one; the column matrix U then has r*U=e_n^t.
Lift its entries to O_E. Its determinant has nonzero residue at P,
so the lift and its inverse are integral at P. Replacing B_P by this
right multiple gives a rational local basis whose inverse last-coordinate
reduction is exactly the supplied functional.

At an auxiliary prime the supplied global be_surj and be_ker statements
give an isomorphism N_P/P N_P -> k_P^n. Clearing one denominator outside
P proves the localized kernel statement; global witnesses already prove
surjectivity. Its matrix M relative to B_P is invertible over k_P.
Lift M^-1 to a matrix integral and invertible at P, and use the resulting
right multiple of B_P. Then every inverse-coordinate reduction is exactly
the original auxiliary map. At Q choose the specified rational frame
matrix itself. Disjointness ensures that no conflicting prescriptions
have been assigned to the same component.

These lifts are in GL_n(O_(P)), obtained by the determinant-unit criterion.
The proof does not assert the generally stronger surjectivity of
GL_n(O_E) -> GL_n(O_E/P), and does not need it.

DR2 gives the starting unitary lattice realizer. Apply AD4 once with
modification set ST union SG union Q and these rational local bases.
It preserves the actual carried lattice and unitarity, installs the
selected components exactly, and chooses conjugate components by the
unitary rule. Pullback coordinates of x in N are now B_P^-1*x at P.

For a coordinate a/s integral at P, choose r in O_E whose residue equals
rho(a)/rho(s), with s outside P. Then a/s-r is in P O_(P), and therefore
has completed multiplicative valuation strictly less than one. This is the exact
HasRes predicate using the actual quotient map, not an isomorphism with
an unrelated residue field.

All five IsPtOf fields follow:

  uni: the unitary matrix from DR2 and the unitary modification in AD4;
  lat: the full adelic Carries equality, unchanged by AD4;
  f: every selected last inverse-coordinate has residue f_P(x);
  be: every auxiliary inverse-coordinate has its specified be_P(x)_j;
  fr: at Q the matrix component equals the given rational frame exactly.

In rank zero, a nonzero functional on the zero module is impossible,
so a valid decoration forces ST empty. The empty identity matrix then
works; all auxiliary and frame coordinate clauses are vacuous. Therefore this is the full actual
exists_isPtOf_cm conclusion under its exact hypotheses.

TRUE-COLUMN APPLICATION AND CLOSED BOUNDARY

The all-twist wrapper exists_ptOf_twists uses only the source prime-set
checks to apply the theorem just proved. A selected type prime contains
T_u; every auxiliary or good twist prime does not. Integer membership
in a prime is preserved by conjugation, so type primes are disjoint
from those sets and their conjugates. The coprimality of pi_u and
bar(pi_u) excludes conjugate pairs within the type set. The supplied
LevTwist split/disjointness conditions handle the auxiliary/twist union.
No further adelic geometric existence is introduced by that wrapper.

Thus DR3 supplies the actual yStd points used in FC3, and every initial
realizer needed in AD3/NB6. FC19 remains a valid, independently reviewed
conditional construction; substituting this DR3 supplier removes its
point-realizability premise for the actual CM host. The direct Forms0,
level-zero carrier, Joint0, StdTied, h0 and companion joins are consequently
available under the original supplied true-column data alone. The parent
may combine these with VE9 without changing any frozen FC proof paragraph.

The old next-source target exists_isPtOf_cm is closed by DR1–DR3. The
special penultimate HSym/lamSym identification, terminal row incidence
and til_ties/CarT joins remain separate full-column work. The existence
of the original unramified CM field and the prescribed prime/character/
column-law data is not manufactured by the realization theorem. None of
this proves the independent analytic or spectral estimates needed in
the wider RH/Dirichlet-GRH route.

The formal proof is accepted as checked and correct. The work is to
reconstruct its mathematics in human-readable form, allowing simpler
proofs that supply the actual original consumers. This plan schedules
proof work and independent review; it is not a proof or a closure claim.

The v0.50 bundle adds 98 claims: TDR13, NFR13, CAF11, MFC14, BNV23,
BSP10 and DNE14, bringing the cumulative total to 3,112. The selected
graph remains 121 nodes and 161 edges. All 24 remaining whole obligations
are still open: 17 shared by RH and Dirichlet GRH, and seven additional
for Dirichlet GRH. The complete list is retained in section 8 below.


1. THE FOUR NEXT ASSIGNMENTS

Use the first round to advance four different interfaces. The task cards
in section 3 give exact exits and source entries.

Agent A — analytic tensor maps, card A1
  Prove the full paired TMapStatement and TMapDStatement for every tuple
  in the actual generated module, including smooth L2 membership, the
  exact Lie-algebra intertwining and every derivative-word estimate.
  Consume the DC, BNV and BSP results already written.

Agent G — normalized frame laws, card G1
  Prove the theta-square transformation and the Borel/arithmetic-group
  laws for the actual NFR normalized family. Preserve the original
  section, frame, characters and allowed weights.

Agent R — fixed linked-frame arithmetic, card R1
  Prove phi and cl on the MFC linked frame: full PhiCond and one uniform
  class-character annihilator. Do this universally on the given FLink,
  so later tasks can use exactly the same Phi, g1 and C0.

Coordinating agent — compatible denominator data, card Q1
  Reconstruct exists_hub_data_family with its actual HW, F, comparison
  and HubData X, common threshold and Setup. Retain the original
  Hypotheses2, TwGood and FactsF premises and track their actual common-
  family suppliers. The next denominator tasks construct those facts
  and local bounds; DNE's completed scalar aggregation is reused.
  Allocate part of this agent's time to independent review and joins.

These assignments can start together. There is no requirement to finish
the entire arithmetic branch before analytic or geometric work proceeds.
There is also no fifth review worker: a reviewer temporarily pauses or
interleaves its own assignment. Avoid treating four full-time proofs
plus a separate full-time review stream as available capacity.


2. COMMON OBJECTS TO FIX BEFORE THE WORK SPLITS

Analytic data
  Keep the source epsilon/lambda/u parameters, representation and
  derived action, lowest compact type, actual period character,
  Cayley datum, free translates and weak Haar pair. All tensor maps,
  evaluations and quotients must use these same data. Positive and
  negative shifts have different excluded values and Casimir changes.

Geometric data
  Use the actual F, M, coefficient embedding, ThetaData and family un
  already provided by HUF/PUN/NFR. TDR supplies FromTest at the exact
  SecBaseChangeSt premise. The remaining laws should be universal for
  that supplied normalized family, rather than construct separate
  families satisfying different parts of FramesOK.

Arithmetic data
  Fix MFC's L, g1, Phi, C0, w and FLink. The general local coefficient
  ring O, full Gen and original GC.Hypotheses2/P premises give the
  linked frame, 21 Ok fields, both strict classes and Big. The extra
  CAF SeedClause is joined only in the original Coef-DVR application
  O=c.O, with CAF7's Q and core characters chosen before L,w. Preserve
  this distinction in every handoff.

Denominator data
  Fix one actual Fm. Constants and thresholds must precede its later
  x and i. The HubData X can depend on a member and is chosen after
  its original ShaFinOdd premise; the same X must then satisfy all
  Concl fields at that member. Do not require a single X across
  different member types or choose a different X for each inequality.

This organization permits useful universal proofs before input
construction is finished. Proving P(D)->A(D) and P(D)->B(D) separately
allows both results to apply to one later D. Proving only that some D
satisfies A and some unrelated D satisfies B does not supply the join.

larger endpoint before the second stage is proved. Source paths are

4. DEPENDENCY-AWARE SECOND ROUND

When the first short packet is ready, use independent review to release
it promptly and assign the freed proof capacity according to the exact
remaining dependency, rather than keeping every worker in its original
subject indefinitely.

Recommended choices:

  • If A1 is still in progress, give A2's pure Capelli stage to the
    first suitable free worker. Its later evaluation stage can wait
    for the completed tensor-map calculus while its algebra is reviewed.

  • A3's pure compact-type and projection stage can also run early.
    Its whole quotient theorem may retain the nonzero tensor-period
    premise; it does not need to wait for the proof of EvalP/EvalM.

  • After G1, continue with G2. Give G3 to a free worker if CoverTri/
    FibTri would otherwise delay the join. G3 need not await new
    Borel or reading proofs to establish its own first-stage exit.

  • After R1, choose R2 or R3 according to which complete theorem has
    the clearer supplied premises. R4 may be assigned separately in
    parallel; its original entry data are distinct from the universal
    fixed-frame theorems. Do not redo CAF's character seed or MFC's
    quotient construction to begin these tasks.

  • After Q1, the coordinator develops Q2 or one actual NormChoiceFacts
    supplier while another worker reviews Q1. Record any missing
    common row/geometric input at its exact original scope; a completed
    conditional local construction remains useful while that input is
    supplied elsewhere.

This is a queue rather than a calendar. There is no justified numerical
speedup estimate: the proofs vary greatly in size. If a task reaches a
real unsupplied input, finish and review the strongest complete theorem
at its original conditional scope, then move to a ready producer. Do
not spend a whole round restating a long predicate without proving one
of its actual fields.

6. REVIEW SCHEDULE AND ACCEPTANCE OF A PACKET

Review begins when a complete bounded theorem has stable prose and
source references. It need not wait for the entire branch. Use a
rotation, adjusted to actual completion order:

  Coordinator reviews A1 or G1 first, particularly quantifiers and
  analytic normalizations; the author proceeds with a separate stage.

  Geometry agent reviews Q1/Q2's common objects and uniform thresholds
  after its own first packet is stable.

  Analytic agent reviews an arithmetic packet's exact finite-field,
  local-condition and rank statements; arithmetic agent reviews a
  geometry packet's base-change and common-frame scope.

  The final branch join is reviewed by a worker who did not write that
  join, using the already reviewed component statements and the actual
  source consumer. If a specialist is needed for a difficult internal
  calculation, rotate review assignments rather than weakening review.

Review time occupies one of the four available workers. The author can
continue with a universal follow-on proof whose input is stated exactly;
the final joined claim should wait for the review and any corrections.
If a correction changes a premise or choice order, notify every task
that consumes it before joining their conclusions.

Each handoff should answer, in mathematical prose:

  What exact theorem was proved, for which original objects?
  Which choices precede which later universal variables?
  Which earlier proved results and classical foundations were used?
  Which original premises remain, and where will they be supplied?
  What actual consumer follows, and by which equality or transport?

Keep proof/source references and the independent review bound to the
stable proof. Those records support the argument; they should not
replace its mathematical explanation. No new Lean replay or fresh
question about formal correctness is required for this task.

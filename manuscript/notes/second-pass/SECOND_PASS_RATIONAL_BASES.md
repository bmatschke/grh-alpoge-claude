# Rational coordinates on the retwisted theta layers

Fix the enlarged column of the [second pass](SECOND_PASS_RETWIST.md) on its fine-extension range. Include the Weber values for the final twist-character set in the field request. Let $z$ be the enlarged fine datum restricted to its actual active set, let $X=z.R.\mathrm{toCx3}(z.F)$, and write $L$ for the full coefficient field with its distinguished embedding $\sigma:L\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$. For each layer $j<m$ there are $n_W(j)$ complex-linear functionals on $X.W_j$, with common kernel zero, that are simultaneously $\sigma(L)$-valued on

* every projected theta image of every rational source vector at every tuple of $z$, and
* every stored target-coordinate vector at **both** levels $b=\mathrm{false},\mathrm{true}$.

The functionals depend on the layer and its realization. The same list works at both levels, even when the two stored bases have different lengths. The new weight-case comparison and the new row-lift family are later steps in the construction. ## The arithmetic structure that supplies the tests

Use the prehost $P$, the final set $\Xi$ of characters and its finite set $A_\Xi$ of ramified primes. For every $S\subseteq A_\Xi$, request the normalized Weber values at level $g_0g_1\prod_{q\in\{\#k_Q:Q\in S\}}q$. The product uses distinct residue cardinalities. The union is a finite set of algebraic numbers. Add it to the caller's $S_2(P,f,\Xi)$ before the coefficient field is completed. Every higher tuple level of the restricted datum is covered by this one request. The base and its enlargement are over the same prehost. Thus the stage rank identity gives $n_V\ge 8fr+4$ there. Restricting the enlarged datum to its active set preserves the eight analytic maps and the geometric comparison, exactly the hypotheses of Q76Z1 on the restricted record. These facts precede the use of the weight-case assertion on that record. What makes these tests arithmetic is the field of the algebraic cusp sections. At the reference tuple take the class sums with their prescribed component factors. The geometric evaluation of a finite linearly independent family of sections over its coefficient field stays linearly independent over $\mathbb C$. Consequently, whenever the complex evaluation of a rational section lies in the complex span of evaluations of these rational class sums, the section lies in their span over the coefficient field. Indeed, choose a basis over the field from a finite list of class sums accounting for that evaluation. If the section lay outside its span, adjoining it to the basis would contradict preservation of independence. Roadmap B8.55 obtains the required complex spanning by the Gaussian theta kernel and the integral-lattice expansion. Its reverse expansion and all-tuple identity keep the central level element, component multiplier, extremal normalization and prescribed phase together. The native proof supplies the reference span on the specified stage at $8fr+4\le n_V$, then uses normalized tuple data over the requested field, the reference map, and the actual good projection to obtain the coordinate assertion $\mathrm{RatG}$. The [record-level theorem](../../../archive/rounds/v76/continuation_v76/column.tex#L25-L49) applies these ingredients to the geometric datum already obtained before the new row is fitted. ## One dual basis for both levels

Write $J_j:L^{n_W(j)}\longrightarrow X.W_j$ for the distinguished coordinate realization, with its prescribed scalar twist. Uniformity means that the images $J_j(e_k)$ of the standard coordinate vectors are complex-linearly independent and span. With the twist included, $J_j$ is $\sigma$-semilinear. Let $\lambda_{j,k}$ be the dual basis of these *ambient* vectors. For all $y\in L^{n_W(j)}$,
$$
  J_j(y)=\sum_k \sigma(y_k)J_j(e_k),\qquad
  \lambda_{j,k}\bigl(J_j(y)\bigr)=\sigma(y_k).
$$
In particular the functionals separate the whole layer, including any part outside the space of theta images. The empty list separates the zero-dimensional layer. [Basis construction and coordinate formula][coords]

The all-tuple assertion $\mathrm{RatG}$ says that the actual good projected lift, with the stage phase and conjugation incorporated, is $J_j(y_{j,t,x})$ for some $y_{j,t,x}\in L^{n_W(j)}$. Its $k$th test value is therefore $\sigma((y_{j,t,x})_k)$. At level $b$, let $u_{b,j,l}\in L^{n_W(j)}$ be the vector called $b_L(b,j,l)$ in the stored realization. The complex coordinate map of that level sends the $l$th unit column to $J_j(u_{b,j,l})$. This follows by applying its definition to the single-entry vector, whose entries $0,1$ are fixed under the coefficient embedding. The $k$th test value on the stored vector is exactly $\sigma((u_{b,j,l})_k)$. The index $b$ never entered the choice of $\lambda_{j,k}$. This proves the two-level assertion without assuming that the projected lifts exhaust the layer or that the two levels have a common basis. [Tests on lifts and the two level inclusions][dual]

## What the bottom-row comparison adds

Roadmap B8.18 supplies rational tests on the target $X.EE$ of the bottom row map under its theta and period hypotheses. The layer tests proved here live on $X.W_j$. The distinction persists even for two copies of the same complex line. For example, the identity functional is rational on rational inputs with image $\mathbb Q\subset\mathbb C$. If a second datum prescribes the basis vector $\sqrt2$ in that line, any functional rational on both $1$ and $\sqrt2$ is zero. A rational matrix changing the rational basis preserves the field-valued coordinate statement. A general complex change can alter it. The stored inclusions $J_j(u_{b,j,l})$, together with $\mathrm{RatG}$ for this same $J_j$, provide precisely the missing comparison on the theta layers.

Scope and result
----------------
This pass reconstructs the actual algebra between the single-place LPprime
condition and the joint generator estimate RestJ. It also proves the separated
edge-test assembly and the finite-field graph-plane count upstream of LPprime.
It does not rederive v0.2's tail-to-index lemma LC2, the lattice walk, or the
polynomial construction that makes the single-place losses small.

The main fragment is joint_rank_section.tex. The complete source ledger is
joint_rank_provenance.json. Every source reference contains its full-file and
excerpt SHA-256, exact declaration, line range, and pinned GitHub URL. The
build_provenance.py script regenerates the ledger and checks its ranges and
declaration anchors. These are source-reading checks, not a Lean compilation.

1. The exact local objects [JR1]
--------------------------------
Let W be a DVR with uniformizer p and residue field of size q0=p^{n'}, and let
O be the coefficient DVR, embedded in its fraction field K. The local setup
has w in O satisfying w^2=p. The full HidaSetting also imposes completeness,
Frobenius-period, finite-extension, parity and lower-size hypotheses; none may
be deleted when invoking the quantitative polynomial theorems. The linear
algebra reconstructed here only requires a DVR and p nonzero and nonunit.

For the fixed dominant weight family lambda, H(L)=HL(lambda,L) is a full
integral lattice in the finite-dimensional tensor dual-Weyl space V, of
dimension d_v. An edge e=(L-,L) means
  pL <= L- <= L,       |L/L-|=p^{n'r}=q0^r.
The half-step lattice Le uses w^{-1}pL together with L-, in each Frobenius
twist. Write A=Ar(r,lambda), the sum over twists of the first r weights, and
  Re(e)=w^{-A} HLe(e),
  Re'(e)=w^{-A} HLe(e)^vee,
  be(F,G)=G(p^A F).
Here dual means the O-integral dual inside V^*. Negative A is permitted; all
scalars are interpreted in K. The use of p^A=w^{2A}, rather than w^A, is
essential to the edge pairing.

At level t the source and target-test lattices are exactly
  Nsrc(L-)=w^{-t} H(L-) intersect [intersection_{e.src=L-} Re(e)],
  Ntgt(L)=w^{-t} H(L)^vee intersect [intersection_{e.tgt=L} Re'(e)].
The three coefficient slots in LatticeSystems are
  base = H(L),  ball = Nsrc(L),  dual = P(L):=Ntgt(L)^vee.
The maps to the ambient polynomial space are the evident subtype embeddings.

LPprimeAt supplies finitely many edges e_i ending at L, source vectors F_i,
and three assertions:
  (a) F_i lies in Nsrc(e_i.src).
  (b) On every other edge e' with the same source,
          F_i lies in w^{c_*} Re(e').
  (c) There is n >= (1-kappa)d_v, an O-linear x -> ell_x in Ntgt(L), and an
      integral n-by-M matrix C such that
          sum_i C_{ji} be(F_i,ell_x) = p^D x_j.
LPprime requires this at every target lattice and c_* >= 4(D+1).

These assertions are substantially stronger than a bare dimension estimate:
they include integral scaling, the common exponent, and separation from
unwanted neighboring targets. The source theorem jSmith uses the integral
edge duality to place f_i=p^A F_i in P(L) and transports (b) to
  p^A F_i in w^{c_*} P(L')
at every other target L'. The duality and full-lattice facts needed for this
translation are explicitly retained as JR12, not proved by reading a def.

2. Primitive tests give a full matrix relation [JR2]
---------------------------------------------------
Here is a useful completely proved precursor to LPprime. Let A be an
O-module, phi:A -> O^M an O-linear map, and q != 0 in O. Assume that for every
primitive row c in O^M (at least one coordinate is a unit), there is a in A
such that the scalar c.phi(a) divides q. Then
  q O^M <= image(phi).

Proof. The image S is finitely generated because it is a submodule of the
finite free O-module O^M. Choose an ambient Smith basis e_1,...,e_M, so that
S is generated by a_i e_i for 1 <= i <= h. Each ambient coordinate functional
is primitive in the original coordinates: it takes value 1 at its basis
vector, so its coefficients cannot all lie in the maximal ideal. Apply the
hypothesis to this functional. If i>h its value on S is zero, contradicting
0 dividing q. Thus h=M. For i<=M its values on S are multiples of a_i, so the
hypothesis implies a_i divides q. Hence q e_i belongs to S for every i.
This proves the claim. Choose lifts in A of q times the original coordinate
vectors; their O-linear combination defines u:O^M -> A with phi u=qI.

This is the proof content of smith_mem and goodRank_of_prim. It is a Smith
rank/existence argument, not the tail-to-index integration already in LC2.
No finite generation of A is required.

3. Why many edge blocks do not cancel [JR3]
------------------------------------------
Suppose a finite family of edges ending at L has source vectors F_{e,i},
i=1,...,n_e. Assume all pairings be(F_{e,i},G), G in Ntgt(L), are integral.
For every primitive coefficient vector c on a single edge e, suppose there
is G in Ntgt(L) such that
  a=be(sum_i c_i F_{e,i},G) divides p^D,
  be(F_{e',i},G) lies in p^{D+1}O whenever e' != e.
Then the concatenated vectors satisfy the hypothesis of section 2 with
M=sum_e n_e and q=p^D.

Indeed, a primitive vector on the whole concatenation has a unit coefficient
on some edge e. The test G for that edge evaluates the total combination as
  a+p^{D+1}z,  z in O,  a | p^D.
Write p^D=ab. Then
  a+p^{D+1}z = a(1+pbz).
The second factor is a unit because p is in the maximal ideal. Thus the
total value still divides p^D. Apply section 2 to the integral evaluation
map G -> (be(F_{e,i},G))_{e,i}. This gives LPprimeAt's GoodRank relation with
the stronger choice C=I and n=sum_e n_e.

In the source, SrcTests states exactly these per-edge primitive tests and
the lower bound on sum_e n_e. The off-edge target test is put in w^{c_*}Re'
and the perfect edge pairing makes its evaluations lie in w^{c_*}O. The
inequality c_* >= 2(D+1) and w^2=p imply the required p^{D+1} divisibility.
The source s7 invokes these facts and the elementary dvd_of_add identity
above. LPprime uses the larger convention c_* >= 4(D+1), and its quantitative
Type-II construction supplies c_* >= 8(D+1). No normalization of valuations
or factors of two is suppressed here.

4. Extracting good directions from the matrix relation [JR4]
------------------------------------------------------------
Let P be a free O-lattice of rank d, f_1,...,f_M in P, and N a module of
integral functionals on P. Suppose C and u:O^n -> N satisfy
  C (u(x)(f_i))_i = qx,  q != 0.
Choose a Smith basis b_1,...,b_d for P relative to S=span(f_i). Its nonzero
elementary divisors are a_1,...,a_h. Pairing integral ambient basis vectors
with u of the coordinate vectors gives matrices C',X over O with
  C' diag(a_i) X=qI_n.
For every i with a_i not dividing q, valuation comparability gives
a_i=q mu_i, mu_i in the maximal ideal. Put
  Y=sum_{a_i not dividing q} C'_i mu_i X_i.
Then
  sum_{a_i dividing q} C'_i a_i X_i=q(I-Y).
I-Y is invertible over O. Taking K-ranks shows that the number of indices
with a_i|q is at least n. On all those indices qb_i belongs to S. All other
ambient directions, including d-h missing Smith coordinates, are bad, so
the number of bad directions is at most d-n.

Applied to q=p^D and n >= (1-kappa)d, this proves jSmith's bad-cardinality
bound. The source's jm_core uses a sandwich cH <= P <= H to ensure that P is
a full free lattice. Our proof starts with that equivalent full-lattice
hypothesis rather than redoing the module-freeness preparation.

5. Isolating a target by a neighbor operator [JR5]
-------------------------------------------------
Let Gamma act freely on valid lattice tuples and compatibly on coefficients,
with the source's unit-valued twist character. For xi at a valid tuple L,
delta_L(xi)(gamma L)=chi(gamma)rho(gamma)xi and delta_L(xi)=0 on other orbits.
Freeness makes this well defined; it is additive and O-linear. If the local
lattices are equivariant, it is an integral section whenever xi is integral.

Fix v in B and an incoming edge L- -> L_v. Give the source tuple the same
coordinates as L except coordinate v=L-. For an allowed integral coefficient
xi there, the neighbor operator y_v applies p^{A_v} and sums over incoming
neighbors of the evaluation tuple. At any translate gamma L, precisely the
edge gamma L- -> gamma L_v gives the desired delta_L(p^{A_v}xi) term.
Other edges give values in p^{D+1} times the target coefficient lattice by
the off-target condition. At tuples outside the orbit of L, every term is
an off-target term. These assertions remain valid if several other edges
land in the source orbit: each is still an off-target contribution. Only
uniqueness of the desired term uses the trivial target stabilizer.

Therefore, pointwise,
  delta_L(p^{A_v}xi)-y_v(delta_{L[v:=L-]}xi)
belongs to p^{D+1} times the coefficient lattice. Its quotient by p^{D+1}
is again equivariant and has the required pointwise integral values, so it
belongs to M_B. This last observation proves actual divisibility of the
function section, rather than merely a collection of unrelated pointwise
congruences. Since y_v(source sections) is one of the summands of J_B,
  delta_L(p^{A_v}xi) in J_B+p^{D+1}M_B.

Tensoring the good-basis relation from section 4 with arbitrary integral
vectors in the other coordinates and summing these relations yields
  p^D delta_L(elementary tensor) in J_B+p^{D+1}M_B
whenever at least one selected coordinate has a good index. The source's
jStep1 is this evaluation argument; jl_good tensors it and uses
w^{c_*}O <= p^{D+1}O. The source assumption 4(D+1)<=c_* is stronger than the
2(D+1)<=c_* needed by this last containment, and is retained in the main
statement rather than altered across interfaces.

6. Tensor products give the multiplicative exception bound [JR6]
----------------------------------------------------------------
At each tuple, choose in factor v an integral basis of size d_v. In a
selected factor v in B, choose it as in section 4, with bad subset I_v and
|I_v|<=kappa_v d_v; in other factors use the base coefficient basis.
Take all elementary tensors indexed by
  product_{v in B} I_v  times product_{v notin B} {1,...,d_v}.
Their number is at most (product_{v in B}kappa_v) product_v d_v.
All other elementary tensors have some good selected coordinate and are
already controlled by section 5. Expanding arbitrary lattice values in the
tensor basis gives the desired local good-plus-bad decomposition.

There is no probabilistic independence assertion here. For arbitrary vector
subspaces U,V of codimensions alpha d,beta d, neither dim(U intersect V) nor
the dimension of simultaneous exceptional directions is bounded by alpha
beta d merely from those two numbers. The source obtains the product because
the exceptional labels live in distinct tensor factors. The theorem jLocal
even only needs generating lists of exactly d_v elements for this part;
independence is used separately for the global rank comparison.

The source realizes tensors as products of polynomials in disjoint variable
blocks. The obvious multiplication map is injective on the tensor product,
and images of tensor bases are independent; the code explicitly proves this
in jr_tuple before using it in jRank. Thus the tensor notation in the main
fragment is a faithful presentation, not a new decomposition assumption.

7. Finite orbit summation and Nakayama [JR7]
-------------------------------------------
Choose a finite set Omega of orbit representatives. At each representative
delta-extend the local bad tensors and take their union C_B. Equivariance
and freeness imply that evaluation at representatives decomposes every
section as a sum of these supported delta sections. The full base module
contains, independently, product_v d_v base-tensor delta sections per orbit:
evaluate any linear relation at each representative to see independence.
Hence
  |Omega| product_v d_v <= d=rank Mna,
  |C_B| <= d product_{v in B} kappa_v.
Nonnegativity of the kappas is needed when multiplying the rank comparison;
the source derives it from LPprime and the positive coefficient dimension,
whereas the main fragment lists it explicitly.

Put T=J_B+span(C_B). The local decompositions and orbit sum imply
  p^D M_B <= T+p^{D+1}M_B.
M_B is finitely generated: it is a submodule of the finite O-module Mna,
since the dual slot is contained in the base slot. Equivalently, its finite
orbit evaluation model is a finite direct sum of finite coefficient lattices.
Q=(p^D M_B+T)/T is therefore finitely generated and Q=pQ. Since p lies in
the maximal ideal, Nakayama gives Q=0. Consequently
  p^D M_B <= T,
and, in particular, p^{D+1}M_B<=T. The latter is the exact RestJ statement
passed to the source walk.

The full rank d here is G.dna, not the retained rank of the ramified source.
The transport to a saturated retained subspace and any ensuing rank ratio
belong to the separate local-accounting/saturation argument. This proof does
not erase that distinction.

8. The elementary finite-field plane count [JR8]
------------------------------------------------
For L=W^k and k=r+s, each r-by-s matrix A over F_{q0} gives an incoming edge
  L_A^-={(x,y) in W^r x W^s : x+A y =0 modulo p} -> W^k.
The residue map (x,y) -> x+Ay is surjective, so its kernel has quotient of
size q0^r and contains pW^k. Thus it is a full lattice and an edge of the
required type. Distinct A give distinct kernels: vectors (-Ay,y), with y a
standard coordinate vector, recover each column of A. Therefore there are
at least q0^{r(k-r)} distinct edges. These are the graph chart of codimension
r subspaces in F_{q0}^k. The source needs this chart lower bound, not the
exact Gaussian binomial coefficient for all such subspaces.

For the Type-II construction each such edge is assigned n_e independent
test directions with the lower bound
  n_e >= rho d_v / q0^{r(k-r)},
  rho=(1-C_r N r^2 k/Y)(1-k c0/a)^N,
where N is the number of selected Frobenius weights. If rho>=0, summing over
the chart cancels the denominator and gives sum_e n_e>=rho d_v. If rho<0,
that last inequality is automatic because sum_e n_e>=0. The source's numeric
hypotheses ensure 1-kappa<=rho for kappa=C N r^2 k/Y. Sections 2 and 3 then
give LPprime. Counting edges alone does not construct the n_e directions.

9. The precise remaining one-edge input: Type II [JR9]
-----------------------------------------------------
EdgeFam in ScaleEdges/ManyEdges states that on one edge there are n vectors
F_i, with source height t_S, in Re(e), vanishing in w^{c_*}Re(e') at all other
targets; every primitive combination has a dual test G of target height
t_T in Re'(e), vanishing at all other incoming edges, with be(F,G)|p^{D_1}.
EdgeStep asserts existence of these data together with the preceding rank
bound, quantitative t_S,t_T bounds, and
  D_1 <= C_D N k^2 log_p(a).

The main source path is:
  SymbolRealisableBound + CloudStep
       -> InitialLevelMonoWeights.edgeStep_of
       -> EdgeStep
       -> graph-plane count + SrcTests assembly + s7
       -> tableLineDegreeFive.ConclusionE / LPprime.

This is not a list of interchangeable aliases. SymbolRealisableBound builds
actual polynomial lifts. Its localizer is explicitly
  SPtrue = R0^{c'} (product_j Del_j^{c'})
                 DtopProd_{(2Q+3)c'+kQ}.
For an allowed symbol P it supplies multihomogeneous polynomials F_o whose
substituted sum is P times the substituted SPtrue, each with integral and
off-plane realizability properties. The degree in the auxiliary v variables
controls the allowed source height o. CloudAt/CloudStep then multiply these
flat source polynomials by a complementary weight family sh_i. They must
prove both the numerical count of sh_i and existence of a bounded-valuation
dual test for EVERY primitive coefficient combination of the products.
The primitive quantifier is what makes the passage in section 3 work.

The actual theorem sources_realisable_holds extracts GeneratorSpanInclusion,
GeneratorImageIsSymbol and RestMon from earlier source results, uses the
explicit SPtrue degree bound, and invokes exists_pol_family_of_mem_gen_of_
vsDeg_le. Its name does not mean that those deeper polynomial arguments
have been reproduced here. restCloudY_of combines SourcePrimitiveIntegrality
with RemainingInputsAfter (which contains TestStep); it is an assembly
theorem, not itself the proof of the quantitative dual test.

The complete numerical premises are the Hyps and WeightHyp/OrbitHyp records
linked in the ledger. They include p>=13, r>=2, a good Frobenius-orbit frame,
adequate rank k compared with frame size, the separation inequality
256 k^2 N Y^2 <= p^{L1-1}, c_*>=8(D+1), and a large lower bound on the weight
scale a relative to the localizer degree. The fragments do not replace these
with a vague phrase such as 'a sufficiently large' or claim uniformity in
parameters beyond the source quantifier order.

10. The distinct Type-I route remains [JR10]
-------------------------------------------
The Type-II theorem requires r>=2 and does not prove the r=1 regime used at
other primes. The ColumnSpanDensity route uses cleaned lattices CCL(e),
CCL'(e), defined by the same edge-integrality, height, and off-edge vanishing
conditions, together with pairing-factor counts RhoL. It normalizes the
single-edge dimension by
  dL = d_v / ((q0^k-1)/(q0-1)).
The exact denominator is the number of incoming hyperplane edges. The
theorem rho_lower_bound_of_edge_count converts an adequate RhoL lower bound
to LPprime under 8D<c and 4(D+1)<=c. This is a genuine separated-block
argument, but it assumes the per-edge RhoL lower bound. The latter and its
averaging over weights rely on the two-letter coefficient model, lattice
functor comparison, cleaned source families, and the weight-cloud estimates.
midLP_of adds the bounded central weights and transports the result into the
actual LPprime parameter record. This pass does not prove those quantitative
inputs or the required mean-smallness of kappa.

11. A source-visible stronger exponent, not used downstream [JR11]
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The Nakayama step in ja_restJ already proves p^D M_B<=T before multiplying by
p to match the stated p^{D+1} conclusion. Thus the abstract argument above
does justify a D-version under the SAME stated assumptions, with the SAME
set of exceptional generators. This is marked derived_simplification in the
ledger because the named exported interface RestJ advertises D+1. No walk
constant or final RH error estimate in the main note is changed on this
basis. Any global use would require revisiting the consuming interfaces.

12. Integral three-slot lattice construction: retained precisely [JR12]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The actual source proves the local chain and naturality, but their polynomial
proofs are not reconstructed in this pass. The exact retained assertions are
  base <= ball,           dual <= base,
  w^t ball <= base,       w^t base <= dual,
  y_v(M with ball at v) <= M with dual at v,
and equivariance of all coefficient lattices. Fullness and fixed rank d_v
of base/dual lattices are also retained at the level of the underlying
dual-Weyl polynomial model. The main fragment states these assumptions.

Backtracking: restLocD -> restLocD_of_edgeBasis -> lb_edgeLat_of_basis uses
edge-adapted bases and tensor weight calculations. local_lattice_chain_of_
local and ch_locChain convert that local edge result into RestLocChain.
nt_local proves the normalized edge map p^{A_v}Nsrc(L-)<=Ntgt(L)^vee;
restNat_proved extends it to the neighbor operator on equivariant functions.
jEquiv proves the covariance under the group actions. The formal chain
from these inputs to tensor exceptions is what sections 4--7 discharge.

The data names RestJ, RestJAll and RemainingCountingStatement are predicates
with assumptions, not claims of unexplained axioms. In the source restJAll
assembles jEquiv, jDelta, jRank, jLocal, jStep1, jSmith and jBase. The final
theta_kernel_expansion then calls instance_of_lemma_J, which combines this
joint bound with the local lattice walk. That final one-line invocation is
an assembly point, not the mathematical proof of the product count.

Compact dependency boundary for the next pass
--------------------------------------------
Proved in this reconstruction:
  primitive separation -> integral evaluation image contains p^D O^n;
  separated edge blocks -> one large test matrix;
  test matrix -> at most kappa*d_v bad local basis directions;
  neighbor cancellation -> one good tensor coordinate suffices;
  all-bad tensor counting -> product of kappas;

  finite-field graph chart -> q0^{r(k-r)} distinct incoming edges.

Remaining construction work:
  actual per-edge polynomial families, primitive dual tests, degree/valuation
  budgets, small mean kappa in both local regimes; and the three-slot lattice
  inclusions/naturality for the actual integral polynomial coefficient model.

No independent Lean build or kernel replay was performed here. No assertion
is made that these newly readable arguments alone establish the arithmetic

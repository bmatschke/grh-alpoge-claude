# The off-base theta scalar in the original period-height estimate

There are two conclusions, with different domains. On any linked selected bundle the source metric of the base lift at every coefficient place is the stabilizer-weighted class pairing in its original rational column coordinates, divided by the original level-group order. The scalar of an occurring part is its norm ratio in exactly these coordinates. On the admissible point class of Roadmap B8.22, Roadmap B8.08b supplies the requested quantitative lower bound on these scalars, including the level-one base eigenvalue premise of B8.22, under the seven analytic inputs below. The fitted evaluation and its transport to the sharp lattice are additional requirements of entry 2. ## The quotient metric at every embedding

Fix one linked selection past its family cutoff, its reference tuple, and one lift. Use the same face, basis, coefficient field $K$, and phase one as in the selected bundle. Let $P_0$ be its finite level subgroup and $N=|P_0|$. Write $H$ for the Hermitian Gram matrix on the definite class forms in the rational class basis, with class weight $1/|\operatorname{Stab}(c)|$. Let $V$ insert the retained block into these class coordinates, $E$ be the chosen section of the further quotient, and $B=VE$. The reduced source coordinates are $x\in K^{d_Z}$. For a representative embedding $\sigma$ of an infinite place $v$, its stored majorant is
$$
 M_v(x,x')=N^{-1}\overline{\sigma(Bx)}^{\,t}\sigma(H)\sigma(Bx').
$$
The equality applies to the given basis even when $K$ is only totally complex and an arbitrary embedding need not commute with the distinguished involution. [Block and frame identities][block] · [Native class-pairing calculation][major]

Here are the data that justify that assertion on this selection. The single witness identifies the whole family of bases, periods, scales and real realization. Its row conditions give admissibility of the definite blocks, separation, positive scalars, the adjoint eigenspace identities at the base place, and the conjugate scalar pairing on the rational parts at the other places. The weak characterization and the existence in the same witness give the full model there. The base-row construction gives the base lift identity, rationality of its matrix, and the property that the rational Gram matrix of the reduced space gives its quotient frame place by place (the original base proviso). Thus the positive block Gram and that quotient Gram have entries in $K_0=\{a\in K:\sigma(\bar a)=\overline{\sigma(a)}\text{ for every }\sigma\}$. The retained block inclusion $V$ also has entries in $K_0$. Indeed the good-character and finite-level projectors have coefficients in $K_0$ by the character formula, their action matrices are over $K_0$, and independent columns give the retained basis. [Single witness](../../../archive/rounds/v74/continuation_v74/bundle.tex#L3-L69) · [Primitive fields][primitive] · [Derivation of the base proviso][links] · [Retained-block rationality][block]

One still has to transport the further section $E$. Write $H_1$ for the retained block form in its native normalization (including the level normalization), $G=E^*H_1E$, and $P$ for the rational quotient projection. Algebraic orthogonality gives $PE=1, E^*H_1=GP$. Fix $\sigma$ and set $\sigma'(a)=\overline{\sigma(\bar a)}$, $A=\sigma(H_1)>0, G'=\sigma(G), C=\sigma(E), D=\sigma'(E), P'=\sigma(P)$. Then
$$
  P'C=1,\qquad D^*A=G'P',\qquad D^*AC=G'.
$$
By the proved quotient-frame property, $G'=(P'A^{-1}P'^*)^{-1}$. Indeed the minimum of $z^*Az$ subject to $P'z=y$ is attained at $A^{-1}P'^*(P'A^{-1}P'^*)^{-1}y$. Hence $D=A^{-1}P'^*G'$ and $D^*AD=G'$. Applying the same reasoning to $\sigma'$ gives $C^*AC=G'$, since $H_1,G$ have entries in $K_0$. Subtracting the mixed identities gives $(C-D)^*A(C-D)=0$, so $C=D$. This proves that all entries of the *actual* section $E$ belong to $K_0$. The calculation includes a zero-dimensional reduced space, with the equality of section matrices vacuous there. Consequently $\sigma(B^*)=\sigma(B)^*$. Applying $\sigma$ to the native coordinate formula $G=N^{-1}B^*HB$ gives the claimed $M_v$, with its level factor intact. ## The active scalar and the analytic estimate

Let $A_j$ denote the rational matrix of this lift on the reduced coordinates and $J_\sigma$ its target realization. Put $y_v(x)=N^{-1/2}J_\sigma(A_jx)$ in the **unscaled** target realization. For an occurring part $p$, the primitive conjugate pairing says
$$
 \|y_v(x)\|^2=\lambda_{j,p,v} M_v(x,x)
$$
for each rational $x$ whose base-place image belongs to that part when $v$ is a nonbase place. The linked row theorem supplies an $x\ne0$ for every part contributing to the row and the same $x$ works at every place. The preceding positive Gram identity gives $M_v(x,x)>0$. Thus the primitive scalar is the actual norm ratio, the level factor cancels, and it suffices to bound the ratio on a single such column. An absent part uses the stored exponential of the assigned lower-weight term in the weak characterization. [Rational column and support](../../../archive/rounds/v73/continuation_v73/reading.tex#L61-L128) · [Native active and absent cases][active]

The orthogonality used in estimating this ratio also transports. Let $u$ be a rational vector in the kernel of the full-domain, phase-one rational lift. At the distinguished embedding $Bx$ is in the adjoint range of the projected lift, hence orthogonal to this kernel. Testing the coordinate vectors $x$ gives $B^*Hu=0$ in $K$. Since the inclusion commutes with embedded adjoints, $\sigma(B)^*\sigma(H)\sigma(u)=0$ at every embedding. On an admissible normalized realization, the conjugate-lift formula identifies the target readings of the rational lift with the analytic projected lift up to a unitary component multiplier, central unitary translation, and a scalar of modulus one. The source class forms span the conjugate definite space by the stabilizer idempotents. A basis of the rational kernel extended to a basis of the full source therefore shows that the kernel of the conjugate analytic lift is spanned by the embedded rational kernel. The independent images of the complementary basis remain independent by a nonzero minor in the realized target frame. Thus the embedded $Bx$ belongs to its orthogonal complement. For the opposite embedding of the imaginary quadratic base, conjugation preserves each diagonal norm in the source and the paired target. [Conjugate fixed-level identity and kernel][one-map] · [Original transfer at both embeddings][other]

Write $r=k+m-1, n=n_V=r+t, f=[E^+:\mathbf Q]$, $L'=\log(b\,\mathrm{rd}(E)g_0g_1n)$, and $H_j^+$ for the sum over the real labels of the logarithms of the positive hook products. The full principal-level lift $T_\sigma$ at the reference tuple has a scalar $l_c$ on each target spherical system $c$ for $T_\sigma T_\sigma^*$. In the convergent doubling range the scalar contains the positive and negative hook products, the definite mass, the unramified Euler and denominator factors, and the reciprocal real formal degrees in the prescribed measures. With the extremal vectors of the point and the finite vacuum normalization, the marked-level index times the squared finite test norm cancels the marked local denominator. At infinity the product of the positive and negative hooks is the Fischer norm squared of the product of the minors in disjoint variables. The paired real matrix coefficient contributes its formal-degree integral at each label. For the Euler product the unitary Satake estimate $ |\beta_P|\le(NP)^{(r-1)/2}$ and $t\ge r+5$ place every Euler argument within $(NP)^{-2}$ of zero. Summing over the primes of $E$ above each rational prime gives $\log|\mathcal E|\ge-2rf$; the two denominator budgets are at most $4rf$ each. The identity class gives mass at least $1/(|\mu(E)|^n n!)$, since the isometries of the standard integral definite lattice are monomial with root-of-unity entries. The real formal-degree bound is $\log J_\rho^{-1}\le2+5r^2L'$. Using $2r<n, n\ge8$, $g_0,g_1\ge(kb)^8$, and the fact that each negative-side hook product is a positive integer (equal to one for an empty partition), gives
$$
 \log l_c\ge H_j^+-8fn^2L'.
$$
The Roadmap gives the normalized integral, local Euler factors and constant calculation. O'Desky records explicitly the mass, Fischer, finite and real scalar inputs. The external analytic assumptions of this application are cusp rapid decay, the unitary Weil–Siegel and convergent Siegel–Weil formulas, local doubling zeta integrals, the unramified doubling calculation, Bernstein–Zelevinsky finiteness and the compact-dual formal-degree formula. [Normalized analytic lift][rallis] · [Constants and compression][constants] · [Seven inputs in the original application][seven] · [O'Desky’s scalar statement][odesky]

The spherical operators commute and have adjoints in their algebra, so the joint spaces span the full principal-level cuspidal target orthogonally. The inequality holds on every nonzero joint eigenspace of that target, including those outside the retained types. Thus $T_\sigma T_\sigma^*\ge \exp(H_j^+-8fn^2L')I$ on the whole target. In particular it has no zero modes there. For the retained orthogonal projector $e$, let $S=eT_\sigma$. On $\operatorname{im}e$ the quadratic form of $SS^*$ equals that of $T_\sigma T_\sigma^*$. Therefore all nonzero eigenvalues of $S^*S$ are at least $\exp(H_j^+-8fn^2L')$. Apply this estimate to the kernel complement above. Its norm ratio gives the lower bound for $\lambda_{j,p,v}$ on each occurring part. At the distinguished embedding one applies the same estimate directly to the primitive target eigenspace. On empty parts the assigned scalar suffices for the numerical lower bound.

## Uniformity and the remaining period-height work

B8.08b takes the middle family threshold $C_{\rm sz}\ge8$ before selecting the family. The original three weight windows give $0<e_j\le2b$, cloud entries in $[0,b]$, and differences of the auxiliary weights from $e_j$ bounded by $b$ after a single family cutoff. Indeed the width constraints give $\eta,S_c\le b'/(xn_A)$, where $n_A$ is the positive cardinality $|A|$ in the family width bound and $b'/b=x\exp(-x^{\varepsilon/3})\to0$. Substitution in the three windows gives the assertions. The family ranks give $k\ge5, r\le2k, n\ge8fr+4$ there. The marked primes satisfy $g_0,g_1\ge(kb)^8$, and avoid the host conductor. The reference tuple has empty twist set. Hence $L'\ge0$, and the original numerical entry $L_{6,j}=H_j^+-C_{\rm sz}fn^2L'$ satisfies $L_{6,j}\le\log\lambda^{(1)}_{j,\mu_0,p,v}$. The coefficient of the additional real parameter $C$ in this entry is zero. One cutoff precedes the index, admissible point, $C$, layer, type and infinite place. [Family calculation][closed] · [Exact original quantifiers][pin-family]

This deals with the lower eigenvalue premise *on the admissible point family required by B8.22*, under those analytic inputs. The two Roadmap definitions of this point class use the same three conditions before the extra arithmetic conditions for the covolume bound. On a merely linked bundle the Gram and active-scalar identities above already hold. To apply the full analytic estimate via B8.22 and B8.23 requires the specified admissible realization, compatible Fourier evaluations on the actual fitted grid, their field and finite-series conditions, and the passage from base to sharp level. The full period norm also includes the terminal pairing and the intermediate metrics in the actual arithmetic period chain. The settled scalar comparison has no independent implication for those remaining quantities.

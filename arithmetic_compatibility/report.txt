1. What this component proves

This component moves upstream of the completed conditional NChoice and
TupTied constructions NC1–NC17. It reconstructs the actual rank comparison,
finite-order character descent, side and complex embeddings, conductor
comparison, local level rigidity, coherent finite selection, and the exact
MuIsW join for the source's actual column/row/stage objects.

The statements are conditional mathematical theorems. They do not assert
that a single global datum satisfying every retained geometric, analytic,
representation-theoretic and arithmetic premise has been constructed. In
particular, an arbitrary RowsRest model, an arbitrary equal-dimensional
vector space, or a bare stage with empty twist indices is not substituted
for the objects appearing in these comparisons.

The main human proof is main.tex. This report explains the proof structure,
source interfaces, special cases and remaining suppliers. The ledger and
source_locators.txt contain exact inclusive pinned ranges and SHA256 hashes.
All claims are checked against pinned Git blobs. No Lean or PDF build is
performed by this component.

2. Fixed conventions and simultaneous objects

D is one literal D7. E is its host field, L its Rows coefficient field, and
L0 its specified subfield. The embedding L -> C is fixed. Every row module,
local coefficient ring, MtReal map, NExpTied cell and NTw character is the
one belonging to that D.

A reading rho has injective maps from L and O_(i,k) into its fraction
field. Pulls means equality of their images on every Galois element. It
does not identify the global coefficient Galois character with the Hecke
character: the latter is obtained only after the complex embedding and
the specified global Artin map.

The source uses inverse arithmetic Frobenius: for a compatible Galois
character r and Hecke character chi, chi(P)=r(Frob_P)^(-1). This convention
is used in RC3–RC5, RC9–RC11 and the PsiChoiceCompatible field.

The packet normalization raises the unnormalized ring-class character to
epsilon_b, with epsilon_true=1 and epsilon_false=-1. Applying the same
operation twice is the identity. A change of one side negates the exponent
in the power-ambiguity statement. TwOK in RC11 concerns the unnormalized
choice character C.psi' composed with art.

3. RC1: finite evaluations and scalar extension

The relevant form space is a subspace of all functions on the finite
adelic group with the specified rational-left and level-right laws.
Scalar extension cannot simply be commuted with the entire infinite
function product.

Instead, a finite-dimensional space of functions has a finite separating
set of evaluations. If the common kernel is nonzero, choose a nonzero
function and a point at which it is nonzero; the kernel dimension strictly
decreases. The process terminates. A rational basis evaluated on these
points and on a weight-coordinate basis has a full-column-rank finite
matrix. A nonzero maximal minor remains nonzero after field embedding,
which proves complex independence.

For spanning, expand the complex coefficients in a basis of C over the
embedded coefficient field. The exact WtMatch matrix identity and level
character identity imply that every coefficient function satisfies the
original equations. Only finitely many coefficient functions can be
nonzero on the finite separating set, and the remaining coefficient
functions vanish globally. Hence every complex form is a finite scalar
combination of coefficient-field forms.

The congruence-kernel condition supplies smoothness, since that same
congruence subgroup fixes every function with the right law. This proves
the actual finrank_typeSp_cx conclusion under its stated finiteness and
matrix-matching assumptions.

4. RC2: the actual rank equality

The local MtLat is a finitely generated full lattice in the actual local
MtV. Thus MtV is finite-dimensional over K_(i,k). The cell's real
isomorphism identifies it with the coefficient form space. RC1 extends
that space to C. LevEq identifies both the subgroup and every character
value with the level of the actual MtReal map. The latter is injective
and has exactly the required form-space range.

Finally the source's jMt_basis identifies an L-basis of MtL with a complex
basis of Mt. Chaining these concrete identifications proves the required
equality of finranks. Applying it in every row degree and undoing the

This is not a rank estimate. CellWt is the exact WtMatch for the prescribed
representations. Its representation-theoretic supplier remains explicit.

5. RC3–RC5: reciprocity, Frobenius detection and exact local behavior

RC3 retains the published GlobalArtinReciprocity input in its full
IsGlobalArtin form: an open surjective quotient map, its closed kernel,
and its inertia, uniformizer and decomposition images. A finite-order
Hecke character has finite image and open closed kernel. It kills
principal ideles and the connected infinite subgroup, and therefore
their closure. It descends through the Artin quotient. A primitive
n-th root in L makes the induced map mu_n(L)->mu_n(C) an isomorphism,
so the descended character pulls back to L. Choosing this construction
as a function of (chi,n) gives literal coherence when those data agree.

RC4 supplies the exact detection lemma used subsequently. A continuous
commutative Galois character trivial on every Frobenius outside a finite
set is trivial. Compose with Artin. Every local uniformizer away from
the finite set is killed by the uniformizer clause. If pi has valuation
1, then x*pi^(1-v(x)) is also a uniformizer, so the entire local
multiplicative group is killed. Finite-coordinate truncations converge
in the restricted product, and hence the idele character factors through
the finitely many remaining local and infinite factors. Weak approximation
of E in that finite product, followed by density in its open unit locus,
and triviality on principal ideles finish the proof. This uses the stated
reciprocity map; it introduces no independent Chebotarev hypothesis.

RC5 proves anti-cyclotomic symmetry using conjugation of Frobenius and
chi(P)chi(bar P)=1. At a fixed prime chi(P)=1. The source host's
unramified valuation restriction is retained so that a uniformizer from
the real field has the correct normalized valuation. RC4 proves the
character identity globally.

The inertia clause gives exact ramification in both directions. At
ell, IsPsi.atl kills the full local multiplicative group. Its Artin
image is dense in the abelianized decomposition image; the closed kernel
kills the closure. This proves decomposition triviality, which is
stronger than merely being unramified at ell.

6. RC6–RC9: finite descent, powers, embeddings and coherent readings

The primitive local root generates all roots of X^(ell^k)-1 in the
reading fraction field. Injectivity therefore pulls each global character
value uniquely into the finite local cyclic root group. Kernel equality
and multiplicativity are immediate after applying the injective map.
The literal NTw.art_univ factors this cyclic character through the actual
RCG(i,Q). The group and its universal map are retained inputs; their
existence is not inferred from a dimension comparison.

RC6 audits all seven TwOK conjuncts: split support; S0 disjointness; open
kernel; anticyclotomic symmetry; membership in the specified cyclic root
subgroup; exact ramification; and decomposition triviality above ell.
The same proof works for every descent satisfying Pulls. The source
exceptional-set inclusions and cyclic exponent then give AdmTw.

RC7 compares two descents by comparing their two primitive-root
generators with a single global primitive root. The change of generator
is an invertible exponent modulo ell^k. Their product gives psi'=psi^a
with gcd(|a|,ell)=1. If k=0 both characters are trivial, by art
surjectivity. For normalized packets the exponent acquires the product
of the two side signs.

RC8 uses the reading's denominator identity to identify its restriction
to L0 with the exact side embedding. An injective local map from a
valuation ring reflects integrality: the inverse of a nonintegral
fraction lies in the maximal ideal, and cannot map to a unit. This
supplies SidePl rather than assuming an equivalence of valuations.

The complex extension is algebraic. A dense-set sequential bound gives
|Q_ell|<=continuum and then the same bound for its algebraic closure and
completion. TwFrameC embeds O_(i,k) in C_ell. The finite reading
extension and its fraction field thus have cardinality at most
continuum. Adjoin continuum many variables, take an algebraic closure,
choose matching transcendence bases over the fixed number field, and
extend to algebraic closures. Restriction gives the embedding into C
extending the prescribed map on all L. No continuity is asserted.

RC9 chooses one reading and descent per conjugation orbit, then uses the
conjugate reading and the same normalized packet at the other place.
At fixed places no side-negation equation is required. Applying the
same complex extension to Pulls and to the side equation gives both
ReadsHecke and SideRead for one common embedding.

7. RC10: a precise sufficient condition for nonempty LocsCarry

This is an additional criterion that does not assume Loc is empty.
Suppose the existing local psiL satisfies the literal IsGalOf condition
on all finite places, with exponent zero, for the same Hecke character
chi(w(a)). Compose its coefficient embedding with the specified iotaQC.
The iota_LL identity identifies this composition on L with the fixed
complex embedding.

Both resulting complex Galois characters are locally constant: psiL_open
and GalOf give open kernels. This argument does not require continuity
of an abstract Qbar_ell-to-C field isomorphism. Outside the finite set
above ell and the conductor of chi, IsGalOf and the Artin formula give
identical values on every arithmetic Frobenius. RC4 gives equality
everywhere. Injectivity and iota_LL yield the equality in the actual
local fraction field.

The exact psiL_ram and the global exact ramification from RC5 now force
the conductor sets themselves to agree. Thus the character and Q clauses
of LocsCarry are derived; the two remaining clauses are the literal
index and side identities twOf(a)=s(w(a)) and twSide(a)=b(w(a)).
The criterion does not construct the assumed local IsGalOf law or those
index/side identities for an independently chosen stage.

10. RC14–RC16: genuine local rigidity

At a split local prime, a unitary matrix is the pair (h,h^(-T)).
An arbitrary integral h lifts to a finite adelic matrix by taking that
pair at this split place and the identity elsewhere. Its coordinates,
and those of its inverse, are finite adeles; no rational approximation
claim about the whole adelic tuple is needed.

RC14 detects the parahoric from a scalar action on a nonzero form.
If an integral h has a unit entry below the r-block, choose a local
unit a on which the ramified determinant character is nontrivial.
The two conjugate rank-one perturbations I+t(he_j)e_i^T and
I+t e_j(e_i^T h), with t=(a-1)/h_ij, are integral parahoric matrices.
Both full determinants are a, but their lower-block determinants are
a and 1. Conjugacy by a scalar-acting h would make their scalar actions
equal on the nonzero form, contradicting the character value at a.
This proves actual membership in the parahoric. Two coefficient
embeddings suffice when they have the same character kernel.

RC15 distinguishes the two orientations. The conjugate split embedding
uses inverse transpose and hence the opposite parahoric. The two
parahorics have equal finite index in the compact integral group and
proper intersection. An integrality-preserving group automorphism
cannot send either one into that proper intersection: its index would
be both the original index and strictly larger. Thus the split
embeddings agree.

RC16 identifies the additive character using a finite-index argument.
The unipotent level contains the full upper unitriangular subgroup
modulo the residue field. Its index therefore divides the product of
(q^a-1), which is prime to the residue characteristic p. Reduction of
integral GL matrices is surjective by lifting entries and their unit
determinant. In the common scalar-action subgroup, the quotient
character's m-th power has kernel of index d prime to p. Evaluating on
I+y E_01 gives a ratio zeta satisfying zeta^(md)=1. Because p*y is in
the maximal ideal, zeta^p=1. If gcd(m,p)=1, Bezout forces zeta=1.
In the actual source m=p-1. Only integral y is needed for the level
character formula.

11. RC17–RC19: stabilizer, nonzero forms and the global level equality

RC17 constructs an actual trivial-stabilizer class. At a type prime P
over p take an integral unimodular matrix whose first two columns are
the all-one vector and (0,1,...,n-1). A rational positive unitary matrix
integral at every finite prime has all entries algebraic integers, and
every conjugate of each entry has absolute value at most 1. The
elementary finiteness-of-polynomials proof of Kronecker's lemma shows
that nonzero entries are roots of unity. The norm-one row condition
then makes the matrix monomial.

Its first-column unipotent congruence forces each root of unity to be
1 modulo P. A nontrivial prime-order root 1+z would first have order p
by the geometric-sum relation, and then its expansion would imply
p in P^2. This contradicts the unramified hypothesis. The roots are
therefore 1. The second-column congruence says all integers sigma(i)-i
are congruent modulo p. Their differences have magnitude less than p
when p>=2n. Hence they are equal as integers, and the two endpoint
indices force the common value to be zero.

For the actual D, p>=2n follows from g=1 modulo QSqd, primality,
QSqd>=q and q>4tr+1, with n=r+t. The actual specified number of primes
over g is [E:Q]. The sum of e_P f_P equals that degree, so all e_P=f_P=1,
giving p notin P^2. Existence of these host/type primes remains retained.

RC18 first deduces global integrality from the exact product shape.
The RC17 class has trivial rational stabilizer. On its single double
coset set F(gamma*g*kappa)=chi(kappa)*rho(gamma)w for nonzero w and
extend by zero. Trivial stabilizer makes the formula well-defined.
It satisfies both transformation laws and is nonzero at g. The actual
realization maps and their congruence condition place it in the intended
Mt/cell spaces.

RC19 uses those nonzero forms with the literal Joint datum. Joint is
not an arbitrary equal-rank map: it supplies an integrality-preserving,
support-preserving adelic automorphism, a common subgroup, another side
embedding, an injective semilinear weight map and the stated scalar
action and power identities on all realized forms.

The cell's J_mem formula directly gives product closure under isolating
one prime. Its hyp field and the retained bad-set inclusion give the
outside maximal-compact condition. At type primes RC16 identifies the
additive factors; local multiplicative components coincide by rational
approximation and their deep-unit kernels. At ramified primes RC14 puts
the common subgroup in both parahorics, and RC15 identifies their
orientation; component uniqueness identifies the characters.

The exceptional set is finite. Factor an adelic element into its
finitely many components there and its outside remainder. The component
comparisons and the common outside-compact rule prove both global
membership implications and equality of the character values. This
proves literal LevEq. CellWt remains a separate needed input to RC2.

12. RC20–RC21: coverage, finite bases and finite avoidance

RC20 uses equality of the prime centers on O_L0, modulo conjugation.
Normality makes this an equivalence relation. Choose an index from the
same eligibility predicate throughout an equivalence class. TwCoverPair
guarantees nonemptiness for twisted places only. Transport a reading
along an equal center, or conjugate it and reverse the side. An orbit
representative choice makes the sides opposite on two-element orbits
and leaves fixed points unconstrained. This proves exists_settings with
its exact conditional reading clause. It does not prove all-place hs or
ReadElsewhere. If the twist index type is empty there is no default index.

RC21 starts by constructing the finite integral basis family S before
any forbidden set A. The trivial twist satisfies TwOK. adm7 places all
extended theta vectors inside the given finite MtLat image. The finite
injective local DVR extension is faithfully flat; tensoring with 1 is
injective. Restriction of scalars and Noetherianity make the original
theta span finitely generated.

Its image under AO is a torsion-free finite module over a DVR. A finite
generating subset of the actual theta family exists by expanding finite
module generators. Among such subsets take one of least size. A
nontrivial relation can be divided by a coefficient of minimum valuation;
torsion-freeness permits cancellation and leaves a coefficient 1.
Deleting that vector contradicts minimality. This proves a basis
selected from the actual family, not merely an unrelated free basis.
FullRank supplies its separate rational-spanning clause.

For finite B, include in B+ all primes containing Norm(P') for P' in B.
Ideal factorization makes B+ finite. If P and P' lie over the same
rational prime p then Norm(P')=p^f, f>0, lies in P. Hence avoidance of
B+ implies Apart from B.

At active i apply the literal aux3 twisting theorem at the actual
positive twEps, with the fixed basis and forbidden set
AvRec union A(i) union B+. The full GoodTw conclusions, including its
IdxAt quantifiers and arithmetic parts, are supplied by this retained
hypothesis. Forgetting extra forbidden sets gives the required packet,
while preserving A(i) avoidance and Apart. At inactive i the conditional
goodness requirement permits the empty, trivial packet.

A finite induction chooses packets pairwise Apart on any fixed finite
set of indices: at a new index forbid the union of the previously chosen
conductor sets. Use the finite image of twisted places when that place
set is finite, and the empty set otherwise. The selector from RC20 makes
packets constant on equal-center classes. If two places read the same
index, their centers agree up to conjugation, so different classes use
different indices. This gives every ExistsData clause with the exact
order "exists S, for every A, exists packets and sides."

13. Exact remaining interface families

The source target map separates discharged endpoints from their retained
suppliers. In particular, rank_of_levEq, the literal conditional
exists_choice, muIsW, cfOf_eq, exists_tp, levEq, exists_settings and
existsData are discharged in the precise scopes stated here.

The main remaining interfaces are:

  (a) GlobalArtinReciprocity / IsGlobalArtin itself.
  (b) Actual NTw RCG/art_univ and all-place coverage together with the
      required coefficient roots and coherent common Hecke characters.
  (c) Actual MtFam, NExpTied and their geometric/representation suppliers,
      especially Joint, local shapes, bad-set control and CellWt.
  (d) For nonempty local data, the exact local IsGalOf and index/side join
      of RC10 on the same D.
  (e) The simultaneous actual ColW/Pre2/RowPk/St construction, including
      its row, numerical, local-stage and period compatibilities.
  (f) aux3, FullRank, adm7 and TwistedPairSettingHyps for one common
      expansion datum, plus ReadElsewhere if a full NChoice is required.

Some targets overlap in their dependencies; they are not independent
axioms to be freely chosen on unrelated data. The new LS bare stage
sets Tw=Loc=Empty. Its local-empty fact can make a local quantifier
vacuous, but it supplies neither the twist readings in RC11 nor the
simultaneous compatible objects just listed.

The final choices_exist_for theorem retains DiscreteSeriesCover,
CasselmanWallach, HarishChandraModules, PrN, SerN and the universally
quantified LocalLanglandsGL input, together with its actual construction
chain. No complete Later/Pins, analytic family, RH, or GRH conclusion
is claimed by this component.

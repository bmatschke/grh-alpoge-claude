==============================

SZ10: INITIAL WINDOWS WITHOUT AN EXISTING SIZE RECORD
====================================================
This stage assumes only RankVal, HostVal, HCVal, cD>=0, ch>=402 and
b'<=bT<=2b'. It does not assume PN16 or SizesAt.

If a root of unity in a degree-d number field has order a, then
phi(a)<=d. Factoring a into prime powers gives

 phi(a)^2/a = product over p|a of p^(e_p-2)(p-1)^2 >= 1/2.

Every odd-prime factor is >=1 and the factor at 2 is >=1/2. Hence
a<=2d^2. This bounds all orders and makes the roots-of-unity group
finite. A finite multiplicative subgroup of a field is cyclic, so its
cardinality also has that bound. Since [E:Q]=2f in the supplied host,
|mu(E)|<=8f^2<=x^7<=h/2 at a uniform threshold. Cyclotomic degree and
finite-subgroup cyclicity are the declared elementary background.

HCVal gives b_j=beta+jh, e_j=beta+jh+h/2 and w_j=e_(j-1).
The top base is beta=bT+floor(b'/x); the thick base lies in the integer
interval [ceil b,ceil b+|mu(E)|); the thin base is ceil w''. The exact
integer bounds and h/2>=1 prove the three required weight windows.

SZ13: ACTUAL RANKS WITH THE PRESCRIBED FRAME UNCHANGED
====================================================
Take x>=16 and a supplied frame phi of order n with
x^(2+8epsilon)<=n<=2x^(2+8epsilon). Define

 A=x^(1/2-epsilon/16), K=x^(9/2+10epsilon),
 m=2 ceil_+(A/2), k=2 ceil_+(K/2).

For y>=2, y<=2ceil_+(y/2)<y+2<=2y. Thus k,m are even and
their windows hold. The elementary x bounds give m+3<=k and
m<=2n<=k. Put f=2n and r=k+m-1, then set

 T=ceil_+(x^377),
 W=ceil_+((10r/8)(f+f^2(k-1)^3 Lg^2/m)),
 t=(8f-1)r+4+8TW.

This t is an integer and agrees with the source's natural-subtraction
formula. Because r is odd, t is odd. It is >=7r+4>=r+5 and >3m;
hence m<=t+1 and m^2<=k+m(t+1). These are all remaining Ranks
inequalities, with their exact parity conditions. The 13 record fields
are checked by name. The inherited frame phi is unchanged.

The quantities T and 8W are exactly TOv(s) and nWv(R,s), respectively,
so r+t=8fr+4+TOv*nWv. The formula for W depends on n,f,k,m,r and
the scale, all chosen before t; this is not a circular rank definition.
The equality and three windows are precisely RankVal.

For the explicit cyclic specialization choose the least e with
2^e>=x^(2+8epsilon); then 2^e<=2x^(2+8epsilon) and e>=2.
The map j -> [5^j] from Z/(2^e) to
(Z/(2^(e+2)))^units / <-1> is an isomorphism:
v_2(5^(2^j)-1)=j+2 by elementary factorization, so 5 has order 2^e,
and its powers are 1 modulo 4 and cannot be -1. Both groups have
order 2^e. Transport by the residue-class bijection to Fin(2^e).
Surjectivity and the required generator-kernel law follow from the
isomorphism. Agreement on 1 identifies it with the source's cycIso.
Thus this is the literal cyclicFrame and its rank constructor, not a
different abstract frame of the same cardinality.

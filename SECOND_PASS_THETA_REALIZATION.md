# Both theta readings on a localization of the retwisted column

Take the enlarged column with the prescribed new twist characters and its fine datum. In the common eventual range, let $P$ be its restricted preliminary datum, $x$ a fitted state on it, and $l$ its fitted character level. These are the objects constructed under the hypotheses of the [second-pass row construction](SECOND_PASS_THETA_ROWS.md). Under the standing Weil-representation and discrete-series hypotheses and the third-layer and geometric Galois representation inputs used for the [point comparison](FIRST_PASS_POINT_COMPARISON.md), the coefficient requests for this **new** level can be realized on one localization on which the full rational reading, including its congruence-index field, exists. Every used tuple of the new column is read there. The tests on the source images and stored target bases use that same geometric datum. [Two-level rational tests](SECOND_PASS_RATIONAL_BASES.md) · [Fitted character level and its data][lc]

## The geometric datum before localization

Write $W$ for the active places of the enlarged fine datum $z_+$. Restrict to $W$ as prescribed in the construction. This determines a specific $z_W$. It keeps the core, field algebra, reduction data and placewise group maps. The analytic part restricts the tuple system and updates its comparison scalars. Its eight analytic existence assertions still hold. Its geometric input retains the same evaluation and conjugate evaluation laws, injectivity, common coordinates, geometric action, full integral lattice and dimension comparison. For the ordinary definite reading and the reading for each individual lift, compose the tuple argument with the inclusion of allowed tuples. The source uses precisely this substitution on each of the two reading formulas. Thus the field, group maps and readers come from a single geometric object for all surviving tuples, including the new nonreference ones. The construction of $P$ sets $P.z=z_W$ and records its intersection law after applying the stated weight-case theorem. [Restriction of the datum and both standard readings][restrict] · [Definition of the preliminary stage][pre]

Now take the later stages $o=x.o$ used by this fitted state. Fitting fixes the division law, the finite group, its placewise identification and the actions of its center. The construction moves $P.z$ to these stages, carrying the analytic and geometric laws, and obtains the new datum $x.m.z$. Its equality of tuple indices is $x.m.y.\mathrm{tup}(t)=\mathrm{ixOf}(t)$ with the actual used set of the retwisted column. On a single base side $x.s$ the state now has the analytic reader $Y=x.r.Y$ and the provisional rational reader $x.r.Z$. Its coordinate identifications use that same side for both level maps and the same intermediate coordinates for every $i,t$. In particular $Y.\mathrm{mtRead}$ evaluates the actual lifted source form at every used tuple, and $Y.\mathrm{mjRead}$ reads the target via the distinguished embedding of its actual automorphic model. These are equalities of functions before taking a coefficient or norm. The provisional rational reader also records the rational coordinate maps into both row spaces. To obtain the full `ReadL` one must identify its last arithmetic invariant. ## The coefficient request that gives the congruence index

Let $v$ be a place of the enlarged coefficient field, $\ell$ its residue characteristic, and $k_v$ the conductor exponent in the twist data. For each idèle $a$, the character value $\psi_v(a)$ satisfies $\psi_v(a)^{\ell^{k_v}}=1$. The coherence condition on $P$ supplies a primitive $\ell^{k_v}$-th root in the specified embedded coefficient field. Consequently $\psi_v(a)$ is a power of that root. The same argument covers $k_v=0$. This proves the field-value hypothesis in the general Hecke and automorphic specialization theorem on the new twists. The fitted level identifies its character, tuple and conductor with the ones on the very form lattice supplied to that theorem. Thus, for every finite coefficient request, the specialization can be chosen with its split Hecke data, an automorphic realization and semisimple residual data together. [Field of the new character values][roots] · [Existence for every coefficient request on a coherent fitted level][built]

Use the point comparison at $P,x,l$. Its quantifiers range over every starting column and fitted level. It gives the multiplicity formula at good points, the eigenspace span and the internal type comparison on an advance request, with the stated integral coefficient condition. For a twisted place and $1\le j\le m$, use the integral lattice read by $Y.\mathrm{latL}$. The rational projector onto the retained theta summand has projected lattice $pL$ and intersection lattice $L\cap\operatorname{im}p$. Here $p$ denotes that projector. Splitting the integral Hecke algebra by residual maximal ideals splits their quotient into its local quotients. The good-point result identifies their generic good subspaces with the ones selected by this projector. Additivity of length, with the source's ramification normalization, identifies the sum of the local lengths with the geometric congruence index. Off the twisted places the fiber of local indices is empty and the prescribed geometric expression vanishes. The calculation uses the provisional reader and its lattice field, so it precedes the congruence assertion being proved. [All-state scope of the point theorem][points] · [Projector, lattices and localized lengths](continuation_v78/reading.tex#L131-L251) · [The good spectral subspaces](continuation_v78/reading.tex#L254-L345)

The large-ring theorem forces the integral coefficient condition on a further request. Intersect the finitely many requests by taking their union at each place, and realize it using the preceding existence assertion. For this one localization $\mathcal L$, with $D'=\mathrm{withLoc}(\mathcal L)$, one obtains simultaneously the largeness clauses and

$$
 D'.\eta_J(j,v')=P.q.\mathrm{eta}(j,v)
 \qquad (1\le j\le m,\ v'\text{ corresponding to } v).
$$

The request precedes $j,v$, and additional finite requests can be included in it. This is the marked congruence equality for every row and place of the chosen localization. An arbitrary bare state may assign other numbers to its later stages. [Congruence identity before the integral condition is discharged][eta] · [Forcing largeness and the equality together][large] · [Choosing and realizing the request in order][choose]

## Installing the two readers on the chosen localization

Use the stage $o_{\mathcal L}$ formed with the localization's own congruence numbers, division data, level, group and places. Its four fitting fields hold. The moved geometric datum identifies with the particular $\mathrm{datL}(x,\mathcal L)$ constructed at this stage. The geometric copy gives $\mathrm{etaOf}(R,F;j,v)=P.q.\mathrm{eta}(j,v)$. The displayed arithmetic equality is therefore exactly the missing field of the provisional reader. Inserting it gives a full `ReadL` on the existing $R,F,Y$ with the same target map $j_W$, two rational row-coordinate isomorphisms and their metrics. The resulting row data use the same place map, extremal-vector models and central actions. The proof also relates their $Y$ to the normalized localized reader and identifies their geometric datum with $\mathrm{datL}(x,\mathcal L)$. The full row theorem applies in the common eventual range to every preliminary datum $P$ on a starting column, hence to this restriction. The target-coordinate identity reads $j_W(y)$ by the distinguished geometric model applied to the linear combination of the stored basis with coefficient vector $y$. Thus the all-tuple evaluations and the rational tests can be applied on this actual stage. The equality is used only at indices $1\le j\le m$, exactly the range required by the reader.

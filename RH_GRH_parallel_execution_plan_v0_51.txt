2. CAPACITY, WAVES AND COMMON OBJECTS

Use four active agents at most, including coordinator C. Workers A, G and R
initially own analysis, geometry and arithmetic. C owns the denominator
comparison packet while managing interfaces. A review occupies its owner's
slot; it does not justify a fifth worker. Each wave ends at a meaningful
source theorem, not an arbitrary time or claim count.

Wave I: A reconstructs the tensor annihilator and actual lowest-type
suppliers; G reconstructs the two global section laws; R proves gen_gd and
gen_host; C constructs coherent Wach/functor/comparison data. These four
contracts are independent once their explicit inherited inputs are fixed.

Wave II: after a freeze-and-review interval, A continues quotient extraction;
G supplies compatible Moduli and the finite level system; R makes the exact
GdEx/HostEx joins and advances one original automorphy endpoint; C advances
the Selmer SignatureChoice comparison. If quotient concentration is useful,
G may instead take its generic Hilbert-space extraction after freezing the
section-law packet. The finite-level card then waits; it is not assigned to
an additional agent.

Wave III: integrate only reviewed supplier joins. Allocate any released slot
to Gaussian/reproduction, simultaneous dictionaries or local denominator
row bounds. Keep one coordinator slot for common-family compatibility and
integration. If a deep supplier remains incomplete, freeze its deepest
complete theorem with the exact remaining input and continue another card.

The wave labels express dependency order rather than a synchronized start.
The finite-level IsSystem card can run while global section foundations
remain open, provided its supplied Level is explicit. Likewise the generic
quotient theorem can be written while K2/K3 remain named premises. The two
Gen entries are independent and may be split between workers after another
packet freezes. Wach comparison and Selmer comparison can also be assigned
to separate available workers; they join only on the actual canonical datum.
Do not spend the coordinator's review time simultaneously promising a fifth
independent drafting stream. Prefer finishing one complete source interface
over starting several partially overlapping chapters.

Every author first records the objects that cannot be independently
rechosen. For analysis these include the representation, compact type,
coefficient slots, Cayley matrix, original Haar pair and character. Geometry
retains one moduli object, normalized family, both levels, coefficient field,
embeddings, Fits and eta agreements. Arithmetic retains one coefficient

D, NChoice C_D, complement family, HW, Fon, Cmp, X and exceptional set.

7. REVIEW, ACCEPTANCE AND INTEGRATION STOPS

At each bounded endpoint freeze the manuscript and announce its SHA before
independent review. Initial review rotation is G reviews A, A reviews R,
R reviews C, C reviews G; authors pause or work in a separate unfrozen
component. Rotate later assignments to avoid repeated assumptions passing
unchecked. Every reviewer checks the original definition before the proof,
then verifies quantifier order, all fields, constants and shared witnesses.

Acceptance requires a self-contained mathematical argument, exact source
filenames/ranges and hashes, root-relative proof paths, explicit inherited
dependencies, retained-premise contracts and a hash-bound independent
review. A source theorem's existence is not its readable proof. Any invoked
published theorem needs a primary-source statement and a checked fit to
this application. Compile TeX twice in draft mode when a chapter is frozen;
no Lean replay or new PDF is required. Count only accepted numbered claims,
never planned cards or statement-only wrappers.

Stop integration for an altered coefficient field, mismatched Cayley/Haar
data, independently selected frame, strengthened gap, omitted local ring,
lost ell=3 case, weakened quotient kernel, incompatible comparison functors,
or a threshold chosen after a later quantified object. Stop whole-obligation
closure whenever one original field is merely retained. Preserve useful
complete subtheorems and record the precise unresolved supplier. Freeze all
reviewed v0.51 mathematics; new work belongs in fresh components.

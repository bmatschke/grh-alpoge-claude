# The finite factors in an interior double theta lift

Fix an even $0\le j\le m-4$ and put $\sigma=(m-j-4)/2$. In the interior finite comparison, the split places over the level prime $T$ and the split twist places $Q_t$ have different roles. The following calculations apply to the original local models on a supplied matched word. They specify a choice of the scalar data and give the chart identity and the fixed-type scalar for the [positive-index row formula](PERIOD_POSITIVE_INDEX_DOUBLE_LIFTS.md#what-changes-at-a-twisted-tuple). ## The level-prime factor

Let $p\in T$, let $k_p$ have uniformizer $\pi_p$ and residue cardinality $q_p$, and put $d=\dim X_{j+1}$. Write $u_p$ for the idele supplied by the split chart from $\pi_p$, and $\mu_{t,j+1}$ for the first edge's target splitting character. Use the prescribed self-dual lattices, integral frames of the two glued cores, local additive character of conductor $O_p$, unit-volume additive Haar measure and the two registered operators representing $\phi_{\rm one}$ on the definite edges. Assume the tuple has the prescribed Weil splittings and is unramified on $T$, and use a column in the specified ramified subspace and row tests vacuum at $T$. Then the local coefficient can be chosen as
$$
 c_{T,t,j,p}=(q_p^d-1)q_p^{-d/2}\,\mu_{t,j+1}(u_p),\qquad
 c_{T,t,j}=\prod_{p\in T}c_{T,t,j,p}.
$$
This is the scalar multiplying the **corrected** two-column lift in (4). The ramified column is acted on by the prescribed finite Fourier operator followed by the residue-sign operation. The common two-variable correction and the two level-prime Heisenberg operators act on its test. For two tuples on the same charts and with this choice of normalization, the ratio is exactly $\prod_{p\in T}(\mu_{t,j+1}/\mu_{t',j+1})(u_p)$. Its absolute value is one. For a previously fixed word with separately chosen witnesses $r_{j,p},e_{j,p}$, its recorded scalar is the monomial in those witnesses. Use the explicit witnesses here when constructing the word, or verify agreement before replacing its recorded number. **Proof.** Put $\Lambda$ equal to the middle lattice. The source Fourier expansion contributes $q_p^{-d}$. The change of lattice has determinant valuation $-1$ in the uniformizer convention, so the split Weil character contributes $q_p^{d/2}\mu_{t,j+1}(u_p)$. This fixes the exponent of the idele character at $+1$. The intermediate labels with lattice $\Lambda$ are precisely the nonzero linear functionals on $\Lambda/\pi_p\Lambda$. Thus their number is $q_p^d-1$. At a first label $\ell$, the Fourier pairing of its nontrivial residue character with the undecorated dual-lattice indicator is zero. Among the decorated dual cosets, exactly the second label $\varepsilon_p\ell$ gives the vacuum pairing, where $\varepsilon_p\in\{1,-1\}$ is the sign of the prescribed split line Fourier transform. The other cosets pair to zero by character orthogonality. The lattice stabilizer carries any nonzero label to any other and the unramified characters are trivial on its determinants. Consequently all surviving terms have the same value after moving the sign to the ramified source operator. Multiplication by their number gives the displayed factor. This argument compares bilinear expressions before specializing the row and does not require either period to be nonzero. The choices of signs, positive constants and integer exponents precede the tuple. At distinct level places the finite translations commute and preserve the other local ramification conditions. Applying the single-place formula successively gives the displayed product with $r_{j,p}=(q_p^d-1)q_p^{-d/2}$ and $e_{j,p}=1$. The source also covers the case where the contraction of the changed vacua is identically zero. Both corrected lifts then vanish on this vacuum space and the same witnesses work. In the other case a common left inverse of the finite operator on the vacuum space supplies the correction. ## The twist places on each specified incoming chart

Use the word's split twist charts and compatible row and column splittings, with a bijection from each of the three global chart sets to the product of the specified local chart sets. Assume equality of the recorded row tests, column operators and traces with their local products, and the prescribed upper row type. Normalize ambient and subgroup measures at $Q_t$ so that the corresponding standard lattice compacts have mass one. For *each* incoming chart $\alpha$, continuous automorphic column $\phi_\alpha$, continuous automorphic upper row $f$ of the prescribed type, and the two vacua $\Psi_1,\Psi_2$ at $Q_t$, the ordered $Q_t$ integral of its weighted incoming trace equals
$$
 \left(\prod_{p\in Q_t}[{\rm idx}_{p,j+1}{\rm idx}_{p,j+2}]^{-1}\right)
 \sum_{\gamma,\beta}\mathcal P_{j+2,\sigma}\!\left(
   \mathcal D^{\operatorname{biComb}(d^Q_{j,\alpha\gamma},d^Q_{j+1,\gamma\beta};C)}\phi_\alpha,
   \operatorname{tr}(\varepsilon_{j+2,\beta})f\right).
$$
Here $C$ is the contraction of the two flat vacuum tests, and $\operatorname{biComb}$ applies the two recorded finite Heisenberg combinations in their respective variables. In the incoming integrand those same two chart vacua carry the two prescribed **row** test operations $e^Q_j,e^Q_{j+1}$, and the incoming trace is $\varepsilon_{j,\alpha}$. The integral runs first over the index-$\sigma$ Bessel quotient and then over the index-$\sigma+1$ Jacobi quotient at $Q_t$. [Single-chart equality, both sides and hypotheses](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DecoratedLatticeProofs/SeeSawIteratedIntegralLawAtTwistOfFactorisation.lean#L229-L260)

**Proof.** The two flat entries are evaluated at the local chart representatives. The source chart bijections cover exactly the contributing level cosets, and the two definite-edge operators represent those entries. The global lattice conditions agree with integrality in each local split frame. Apply the ordered local integral with these measures and sum over the representatives. Reindex the intermediate and outgoing sums using the chart bijections. The incoming weight times the intermediate and outgoing local weights is exactly the product of the two column coefficients and the outgoing trace coefficient in the display. The two local level volumes give the reciprocal indices. This also holds after the permitted rescaling by chart phases $s_j(\alpha)$, since along $(\alpha,\gamma,\beta)$ the two edge factors and the upper trace acquire
$$
 \frac{s_j(\alpha)}{s_{j+1}(\gamma)}\,
 \frac{s_{j+1}(\gamma)}{s_{j+2}(\beta)}\,s_{j+2}(\beta)=s_j(\alpha),
$$
which is the factor of the incoming trace. In the source's family constructed from its geometric word hypotheses, the unrescaled twist records satisfy the product identities by construction, the row type is the specified simultaneous local type, and the assembled word uses exactly these phase rescalings. Thus no additional twist normalization is required for members identified with those records. For an independently proposed selected member one must still identify its records and row readings, match the level-prime correction with the prescribed arithmetic input operator, and identify its fixed-type frames as below before using [equation (4)](PERIOD_POSITIVE_INDEX_DOUBLE_LIFTS.md#what-changes-at-a-twisted-tuple). ## The fixed-type scalar on the prescribed split frames

At each fixed-type place $p$ suppose the registered split frames identify the row and column lattices, and their registered Heisenberg combinations represent the original two row tests and two normalized column entries at $(1,1)$. These are the fixed local data of the word. Suppose also that its recorded global row and column operators act as the products of these local operators on theta vectors. Write $q_p$ for the residue cardinality and use $b_h,n_h$ from the [symmetric contraction](PERIOD_SPLIT_SYMMETRIC_CONTRACTION.md) for the chain there. For $j+2(\sigma+1)+2=m$, put
$$
 E_{p,j}=b_{j+1}+b_{j+2}
     +\binom{\sigma+2}{2}(n_j+n_{j+1})
     +\binom{\sigma+1}{2}(n_{j+1}+n_{j+2}).
$$
Then the two fixed-type row tests inside the contraction can be replaced by the two specified column operators outside it with multiplier
$$
 c_{G,j}=\prod_{p\ {\rm fixed\ type}}q_p^{-E_{p,j}}.
$$
This positive rational number is common to every tuple on these fixed frames and has empty-product value one. The formula gives a compatible choice when the word's scalar is recorded through an existential witness. [Row-test and column-entry identifications](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/LatticeFrames/HzUnit.lean#L60-L98)

**Proof.** In the split linear model the framed Bessel data force the local position parameter to be $2\sigma$. Apply the linked pair of flat transforms to the two original tests, then the local line pairing. The symmetric calculation identifies this contraction with $q_p^{-E_{p,j}}$ times the product of its two normalized identity entries. On the two integral vacuum tests the corresponding local contraction is the product of the two integral matrix indicators, with coefficient one. The local model of a two-variable Heisenberg combination carries the product of indicators to the product of the prescribed identity entries. The same scalar thus compares the two *adelic* test expressions for every pair of theta vacua at $p$ and every pair of edge splittings in the matched frames. This argument never divides by a period or an entry. At distinct places, the operations at the remaining places preserve the vacuum condition at $p$. Apply the comparison successively in the recorded product order and use linearity to take out each scalar. This proves the asserted value of $c_{G,j}$ on those inputs.

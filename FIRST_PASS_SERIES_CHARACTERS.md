# Preparing the character labels for the theta series

At the first intermediate level, the list supplies the form spaces on both sides, the coefficient embeddings, the finite Hecke and type actions, and the character grid on the fitted state. The preparation makes the tame character at each marked place a **single character over $L_0$** which can be read at every coefficient prime, including primes of the same residue characteristic as that marked place. It also puts the form spaces in the required row range and installs the prescribed universal character. [Preparation theorem][prep] · [Use with the fitted first list][call]

## The row range and the two sides

For each coefficient index $i$, side $b$, and integer row index $j\ge0$, take the original lattice and form space if $1\le j\le m$, and their zero subspaces otherwise. Restrict the Hecke operators and finite group actions. On valid rows the inclusion is an isomorphism and transports the lattice, projector range and eigenspace spanning assertions. On an invalid row both sides of the range identity vanish and every characteristic polynomial is $1$. The first auxiliary realization uses zero theta submodules and an empty theta-data set; the ensuing series construction supplies its theta data separately. The explicit witness sends every new coefficient index to **itself**, with the same residue characteristic $\ell_i$ and excluded set $S_0(i)$. The two sided embeddings $e_{i,+},e_{i,-}$ of $L_0$ belong to the same index. This explains why the original good-prime and exceptional-place comparisons apply at every index of the prepared state. [Restricted spaces][cut] · [Range, spanning and identity of indices][cut2] · [Auxiliary theta data][empty]

## One tame character for every coefficient prime

Fix $g=g_k$, one of the two marked rational primes, and a designated $v\mid g$ of $E$. The host is completely split at $g$, hence $E_v=\mathbf Q_g$. For $x\in\mathbf F_g^\times$ take the integer lift $1\le u<g$ and, in the fixed algebraic closure, choose $\pi^{g-1}=g$. In geometric reciprocity the local Artin element of $u$ has an inertia lift $\sigma$ satisfying $\overline{\sigma(\pi)/\pi}=x$. This sign can be checked with $\zeta_g-1$ as a uniformizer of the cyclotomic extension. Any inertia character of exponent dividing $g-1$ factors through this tame quotient, so the reading is independent of the representative. [Tame reciprocity calculation](LOCAL_TAME_CHARACTER.md) · [Comparison with the chosen inertia action][read]

For $1\le j\le m$ the complex value in the old reading at $\ell_i\ne g$ is $\mu_{0,j,v}(u)$. Indeed the first Galois character comes from $\mu_{0,j}\delta_{\ell_i}\eta^{-1}|\cdot|^{-r/2}$; the last three factors are trivial on the local units at $v$. The two sides use their respective coefficient embeddings. To descend simultaneously, choose a rational prime $p>g$, a coefficient prime over $p$, and a covering index $i_0$ at that prime. At $i_0$ the inertia bound gives $\mu_{0,j,v}(u)^{g-1}=1$. The starting field contains a primitive $g(g-1)$-th root of unity in the complex image of $L_0$, so this value has a unique preimage $\tau_{j,v}(x)\in L_0^\times$. Every old reading with $\ell_i\ne g$ equals $e_{i,b}(\tau_{j,v}(x))$. Multiplicativity follows by applying the injective $e_{i_0,+}$ and using multiplicativity there. The auxiliary $p$ tests the character and places no further restriction on the coefficient indices. The selector used for the state is the original $P_q$. It selects one member of each distinct conjugate pair and its selected places above a common rational prime have the same restriction to the distinguished imaginary quadratic field. [Field and selector on this state][choice] · [Single covering index and simultaneous descent][desc]

At $\ell_i=g$, set the new reading to $e_{i,b}\circ\tau_{j,v}$ as well. The old inertia-matching law was restricted to $v\nmid\ell_i$. To fit the new character on the definite level $J$, multiply its old character by the product over marked places of
$$
 \frac{\tau'_{i,j,b,v}(d_v(\kappa))}{\tau^{\rm old}_{i,j,b,v}(d_v(\kappa))},
 \qquad \kappa\in J,
$$
where $d_v$ is the reduced determinant of the bottom-right block below the first $r$ coordinates. On the parahoric this determinant is a multiplicative residue-field unit. The correction is trivial on a sufficiently deep principal congruence subgroup and is supported at the marked places. With this character take the corresponding definite automorphic forms of the given weight on $J$ for each admissible twist in range, and the zero auxiliary space for the other indices. The finite-dimensional space has a full lattice spanned by a basis. This realizes the revised level character and proves the common-value law `TauTrue` at **all** coefficient indices. [Change of level character][rechar] · [Admissible spaces][adm] · [Full lattice and common-value law][install]

The extension datum takes its universal character $\delta_U(Q,M)$ componentwise. At $P\in Q$ use the chosen anticyclotomic character into $\mathbf Z/\ell^M$ supported on $\{P,\bar P\}$, trivial on decomposition groups above $\ell$ and surjective on inertia at the pair, whenever the pair is in $Q$ and such a character exists. Otherwise take the trivial coordinate. The result has open kernel, is unramified outside $Q$, and respects restriction among conjugation-stable sets. The stronger surjectivity is asserted on admissible $Q$. [Coordinates and fallback][univ] · [Inertia on admissible sets][surj]

Finally carry the original grid datum $(C,q_0,w_0,c,\chi)$ to the prepared state using its transported frame. The conductor, the powers indexed by $s$, and $\lambda_0(\ell,j,a)=\mu_{0,j}\delta_\ell\chi_a^{-1}$ agree with their old definitions. At every index satisfying the nonzero-place condition, the same comparison embedding and the same labels witness both directions of the full list condition, including every row, character index, allowed $s$, and side. This transfer holds for every fully tied grid on the original state. [Grid definitions][lam] · [Full two-sided coverage][tie] · [Transport and subsequent application][call]

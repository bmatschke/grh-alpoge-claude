1. The direct input construction has exactly the needed scope
=============================================================
The literal Forms0 record contains 13 fields: atT, cls0, val0, gl, gl_K,
glev0, lv0, fMe0, fv0, up_natural_level_zero,
down_natural_level_zero, norm0 and bw0. It contains no period, terminal
row class, til_ties, CarT, HSym, lamSym or full FormsTiedSym field.

FC19 assigns each field from the independently reconstructed FC/CV/NB/SF
results. In particular valOfG requires the singleton chart, exact frame
and determinant identities, CV9's all-prime values, the metric comparison
and dilation; it has no full-form premise. The all-prime intersection
argument recovers the global lattice using the denominator ideal and
maximal ideals, without assuming freeness. The upOfG/dnOfG conversions
retain NB7/NB8's actual gamma, eta, kappa, exact lattice and functional
relations, and inverse character; their only endpoint change is the
singleton chart reindexing. Norm and Bw retain the same frame map.

The apparent FormsTiedSym argument of source exists_colLevel is used in
its proof only through the level-zero class data, gl and glev0. Its actual
reindexing and ColLevel construction therefore apply to the corresponding
fields of the directly constructed Forms0. The proof does not invoke the
unfinished full-form constructor. All five SameCar fields are the stated
Joint0 identities, including the SAME local groups, global group, class
matrices and stabilizer matrices. The same split and local frame are
retained, and the auxiliary group clause is the FC15 step-zero one.

FC16's coefficient-character argument uses the specified roots in the
coefficient field, not a new embedding. SF2 supplies StdTied for the actual
spaces. The companion lattice identity uses the same base lattice
O^(k-1) direct_sum pi_u O, the ideal (conjugate(pi_u)), and its actual
Hermitian dual. This matches the span(dualSet) expression in the source
exists_concrete_of_record under the already proved coordinate identities.
No index, unrecorded scalar, or arbitrary isomorphic lattice is substituted.

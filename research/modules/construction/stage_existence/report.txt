This component distinguishes a typed record's existence from the stronger
geometric identifications and quantitative assertions needed downstream.
It supplies useful actual data, and also identifies explicit auxiliary

or independent Lean verification is claimed.

SCALAR DESCENT AND DIVISIBILITY (SE1–SE6)

If one K-basis maps to a complex-independent family under a semilinear
map e, every semilinear map from the K-space extends complex-linearly on
the complex span of that family, and then to the ambient space. Dual
coordinate maps show that every K-independent family remains independent.
This proves actual scalar extension, rather than merely a dimension count.

For finitely many operators preserving e(V), descend their joint map N to
V. Choose a section of its image, extend that section to the product space,
and use p=1-rN. The identity 1=p+sum(r i_s N_s) complexifies. Therefore
the complex common kernel is spanned by the embedded K-kernel. This applies
to the finite norm operators because the local actions preserve the actual
embedded integral lattice, which spans V.

For U contained in V and basis-column matrix E, the Gram matrix
(X^{-1}E)^*(X^{-1}E) is positive definite. Its square root gives frames
whose norms agree exactly with the ambient restriction. The intersection
L cap U is finitely generated because O_K is Noetherian and spans U by
clearing a nonzero integral denominator for each vector. No global O_K
freeness is assumed. These facts give the literal ramEmb basis statement,
all fourteen DivStage fields, good lattices and exact nuIn image equal to
ramL. The source's auxiliary Bk and height values are explicitly recorded.

ACTUAL PERIOD SPACES AND AUXILIARY ROWS (SE7–SE8)

Evaluating an equivariant polynomial-valued function on finitely many class
representatives is injective, since representatives and full-group
equivariance recover every value. Hence the actual period spaces are
finite dimensional. The exact weighted positive class-sum metric is
Hermitian, nonnegative and definite, so the actual Laws supply Fits.

The row model keeps this actual Mram and its metric, and the actual base
and twisted period pairings. It is otherwise allowed to use coordinate
spaces and truncation, standard lattices, identity projections and zero
auxiliary scalars. Every group of fields is specified in SE8. The period
pairing is bilinear; the class metric is Hermitian. Conflating these would
give the wrong semilinearity and is expressly avoided.

ORDINARY LOCAL AND SPECTRAL STAGE (SE9–SE10)

The ordinary local algebra is F_v tensor_F E with its induced involution,
integral closure, vector topology, trace and standard p-adic character.
The two prescribed rational primes split completely, giving the product
local algebra and swapping involution. The optional split field is marked
exactly there; none elsewhere does not assert nonsplitting elsewhere.
Standard completion/CRT/local-degree facts are named classical background.

The spectral-stage model uses the exact HasLetters gap and weight data,
the actual positive class metric, and a conjugate-linear isometric
inclusion from EE into C^{Dall}. All other terms are written explicitly:
singleton term/pair types, positive unit scales, zero spectral terms and
the one-block point partition. This proves all nine SStage fields without
silently asserting any positive spectral contribution.

COMPACT-DUAL HAAR AND JET STAGE (SE11–SE12)

For J=diag(1_P,-1_Q), write Z=(Z-JZ*J)/2+i[-i(Z+JZ*J)/2]. This exhibits
the real Lie algebra as a real form of the full complex matrix algebra.
Rescaling a real Lebesgue measure by the square root of the product-volume
factor gives IsOmegaLeb exactly. The exponential and logarithm give local
analytic coordinates; a left-invariant smooth density is a Haar measure.
Its continuous positive coordinate density at zero yields the required
small-ball limit. The proof also treats the zero-dimensional point case.
Compact Haar is made probability, and noncompact Haar is rescaled to its
compact dual's finite positive density. All four CDHaar fields follow.

The prescribed-rank local GAtP model uses no variable places, a trivial
level and character, and d components of constants. Its integral module is
Z_l^d and its fractional module has rank d, with finite class sets. Lying
over supplies the coefficient prime for each jet. Empty situation types,
constant positive volumes and the constructed CDHaar give bare GJStage
existence. This does not identify constant volume with the source's actual
trueVol, nor produce nonempty jets or lift estimates without their inputs.

ADAPTATION, REMAINING N-STAGE AND PINS (SE13–SE16)

The central idempotent family supported at (X-1)^r is zero on every good
squarefree polynomial when r>=2. Products over a nonempty selected-prime
family and sums over good tuples are consequently zero. The source adapt
operation changes the exact RowsRest object, setting eOp/eMt/eta to zero
and shaLen to an extended-natural-to-natural scalar. In particular it does
not prove Sha finite: infinity.toNat is zero as well.

The precise remaining five-stage join is conditional on NStage for that
same adapted model. RowsRest, DivStage, SStage and GJStage are reconstructed.
The full Galois-stage field assembly and LocStage's Ainf/Bcris/BdR structures
and ties remain open. Ordinary completions are not these period rings.
The model with Mt=C and eMt=0 also proves that a positive projector-rank
bound requires additional premises.

There is an additional exact compatibility obstruction: this particular
source NStage model has Tw empty, whereas NChoice requires twI from every
coefficient place to Tw, including off-support places. There is a
coefficient prime above2, so that map cannot exist. The bare Later model
therefore cannot itself supply the NChoice-compatible arithmetic package.
This concerns that particular model, not all possible Later constructions.

Diagonal positive/negative coordinates give the required hyperbolic pairs,
orthogonality, signatures and norm-T top vector. Extension by zero sends
each matching standard frame vector to the matching standard frame vector.
For an existing PreX with pi_u conjugate(pi_u)=T_u, replacing c(u) by
pi_u0/pi_u proves the scalar pin by cancellation and its norm field by
multiplication. These are normalizations of an existing sized geometric
input; its prior existence, analytic windows and later compatible Ok remain.

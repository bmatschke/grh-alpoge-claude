1. Outcome and exact scope

The principal new result is an actual nonempty coefficient construction on
every finite prime of the fixed coefficient number field K = D.LL0. Its
TwR has finite Z_l coefficient DVRs, complete discrete coefficient DVRs with
algebraically closed residue fields, finite cyclotomic extensions, the
normalized delta and chi1 characters, and universal finite anticyclotomic
l-primary quotients. It satisfies every field of RingSpec. Its Tw index
set is the set of all nonzero prime ideals of O_K, rather than a type
already assumed to cover the relevant places. In particular it is infinite,
and the actual auxiliary delta at every index has inertia image of order 15.

This closes ExistsTwistRingsCharactersClassGroups.exists_ringSpec, including
its non-record prerequisites, and ExistsTwistIndexOfCoefficientFieldPlace.
exists_setting_of_place. The latter is exactly the coefficient part of
SideB.cover. A further human implication, NR18, gives a Reading inducing
any prescribed place of the entire original coefficient field L, not just
its restriction to K. It uses the constructed index and its actual base
embedding; it does not replace L or its fixed complex embedding.

A second chain proves that a continuous finite-dimensional representation
of a compact Hausdorff group over Qbar_l is defined over one finite local
field and is conjugate into its integer matrices. With the source's actual
GalEx, RowCusp, ChebSplit and SideA retained, this proves the complete
ExistsGaloisRepFamilyBounded.rho_rows supplier. Its finite-field bound is
chosen before the coefficient rings, simultaneously for the finite row
family and both sides. Combining this bound with the ring construction's
bound gives a coherent simultaneous family.

TwR is the coefficient/character/universal-quotient part of NTw. It has no
Sig, B, Theta or actual automorphic form realization. The constructed TwR
is new data parametrized by the original D, and does not assert that an
already installed arbitrary D.ntw equals this new family. Installing it
requires the source's explicit TwNew/setTw transport and the relevant form
and toric data. The host, NData algebraic closure and chosen places, rows,
K, L, and epsilon remain fixed in all the proved constructions. No mutation
of a previously installed NTw or of its dependent objects is inferred.

2. Retained inputs and their quantifiers

For the full ring construction the literal inputs are: the fixed D and its
CharFrame; GlobalArtinReciprocity with the source's exact quotient, inertia
and inverse-Frobenius conventions; ValLL0 for the specified embedding of K;
star-stability of K inside L; and integral eExp(j,g). The original Host and
NData include the stated type-prime splitting and the two distinct
auxiliary primes, with the original character-frame laws. Those are used
by the previously proved GA16 character constructor. NR does not assume
that an arbitrary number field already contains the frame values. HF16
constructs a finite common value field and proves ValLL0 when its actual
generator is contained in the given K; the containment remains necessary.

The published endpoint accepts a prescribed family J of embeddings at
each prime of K. NR1 constructs such a family from prime completions, so
existence of J is discharged. If J is already specified with its exact
center law, NR15 preserves that very family. The coefficient lower bound
c0(l,j) is then chosen before b0, b1 and Sx, for every rational prime l and
every embedding j:K->Qbar_l. It contains j(K), the lth roots of unity and
the finitely many normal closures needed by the host local bridges.

The exact order is:
  fixed D/frame/value and conjugation hypotheses;
  choose J if not already given;
  choose c0(l,j);
  for all b0 dominating c0, all b1 and all finite Sx;
  construct T and RingSpec, with j0=J, K0=b0(l,j0), Lw=b1(l,j0).

The bounded representation family has a separate c1(l,j), constructed
under GalEx, RowCusp, ChebSplit and SideA. It is chosen before b0,b1,Sx,T
and RingSpec. Taking the compositum c0*c1 as a common lower bound resolves
the two existential choices without a circular dependency. The bound is
pointwise in l and j; no single number-field extension uniform in all l
is asserted.

Classical inputs are clearly named: unique extension of the l-adic norm
to finite algebraic extensions, completeness of finite local fields,
finite-dimensional nonarchimedean norm equivalence, algebraic closedness
of C_l, Baire's theorem, the elementary fixed-field theorem and extension
of embeddings in a separable algebraic closure, and finiteness of ideal
class groups. Global Artin reciprocity and automorphic Galois existence
are separate retained inputs, not consequences of these elementary facts.

3. NR1–NR3: prescribed prime embeddings and genuine local bridges

For a nonzero prime q of O_K, the completion K_q is finite over Q_l, where
l is the residue characteristic. Restricting any Q_l-embedding K_q into
Qbar_l gives exactly a in q iff |J(a)|<1 on O_K. For a chosen valuation
ring A of the global algebraic closure, transitivity of conjugate
extensions of a valuation gives an embedding j inducing precisely A.
The inverse-limit compactness argument passes the finite normal-extension
conjugacies to a single global automorphism; no prime is discarded.

Every algebraically closed global image j(Omega) in Qbar_l is dense.
Approximate the coefficients of a monic local polynomial f with f(a)=0
by rational coefficients, producing g(a) arbitrarily small. All roots of
g belong to j(Omega). The product formula g(a)=product(a-b_i) gives a
root as close to a as required. This elementary density proof is used
again in the compact-representation argument.

Two embeddings pulling back the same valuation ring have the same
normalized norm: test l^{-m}x^n for all m in Z and n>0, which compares
both absolute values with the dense set l^{m/n}. One must extend their
isometry first to C_l. The target Qbar_l is incomplete. The extension
fixes Q_l by density of Q and preserves elements algebraic over Q_l,
so its restriction gives the required Q_l-automorphism of Qbar_l.

The local field Kw=Q_l(j(E)) is finite and equals the closure of j(E).
The decomposition group at the chosen A acts on this dense image and
extends uniquely to Gal(Qbar_l/Kw). Conversely a local automorphism
preserves the global algebraic elements and their valuation, hence
restricts to the decomposition group. Restriction is continuous on
finite stabilizers; compact-to-Hausdorff bijectivity gives continuity
of the inverse. This proves all group and topological clauses of
LocBridge. A finite normal closure of Kw supplies every local embedding
required by LL_split. For any larger coefficient field the eO map is
the identity, including both continuity and Z_l compatibility.

The finite coefficient integer-ring facts are also proved. If [K0:Q_l]=d,
the norm product gives |x|^d in l^Z. A least positive value supplies a
uniformizer, and the unit ball is a DVR. Norm equivalence bounds all
coordinates of that ball in a fixed Q_l-basis, so it is a Z_l-submodule
of a finite fractional lattice. Noetherianity makes it finite; torsion
freeness makes it free. It equals the integral closure, has fraction
field K0, finite residue field, and the correct adic complete topology.

4. NR4–NR7: the complete DVR and finite cyclotomic tower

The source's maximal unramified algebraic field is generated by roots
of unity of order prime to l. For m prime to l, Z_l[mu_m] is the finite
span of those roots and is compact and closed. With N=l^{phi(m)},
Frobenius modulo l gives y^N-y in l Z_l[mu_m]. If |y|<1, the sequence
l^{-1}(y-y^{N^s}) lies in that closed ring and tends to y/l. Thus the
ring contains y/l. Repeating this division until the norm becomes one
proves that every nonzero element has norm a power of l. Rational
expressions in finitely many roots, followed by denominator clearing,
give the same result for the entire unramified algebraic field U_l.

For F=L0 U_l with L0 finite, F/U_l has finite degree d. All conjugates
over U_l have the same normalized l-adic norm, so the field norm shows
that the value group of F lies in d^{-1}Z. Its least positive value
is attained by an element pi. The closed set consisting of norms at
most |pi| or at least one contains F and therefore its completion.
This preserves the gap below one. Repeated division by pi then proves
that the unit ball in the completion is a DVR, not merely a rank-one
nondiscrete valuation ring. Its maximal-ideal powers are exact norm
balls, giving the adic topology and completeness.

Residue algebraic closedness uses actual approximation. The residue
field of C_l is algebraically closed by lifting a monic polynomial
and reducing one of its integral roots. Approximate a norm-one element
by an algebraic element to distance <1; its residue lies in a finite
field and is represented by a prime-to-l root of unity. Such roots
already lie in U_l. Hence the constructed DVR has the full algebraic
residue field. LS8's residue-root lifting can be used for this exact
step. This does not identify the constructed DVR with O_{C_l}.

For adjoining a single algebraic z, the powers 1,z,...,z^{n-1} span
the finite-dimensional algebra over the completed base. The image
of the coefficient map is a complete, closed subfield containing
the dense uncompleted adjunction. A complement to the map's kernel
gives a bounded inverse onto that image. This is needed because the
degree of z can drop after completion: a claim of linear independence
of those powers over the completion would be unjustified.

For z a primitive l^k root, this coefficient bound gives one uniform
denominator l^N for every integral element. The larger integer ring
lies inside a finite fractional lattice over the base DVR and is
finite by Noetherianity. For an arbitrary element of its fraction
field, first multiply by a power of l to make it integral. The
result is exactly x*d=p(zeta), with d nonzero in the base DVR and
p a polynomial over it. This is Ok_gen. It is weaker than, and
does not rely on, an unproved assertion O_k=O[zeta].

5. NR8–NR11: universal finite anticyclotomic ring-class quotients

At a place of residue characteristic p different from l, a principal
unit u satisfies u^{p^a}->1. The binomial theorem increases the positive
valuation by at least one at every pth power, including p=2. Any
continuous character with finite l-primary target and open kernel
therefore kills u: its value has order dividing both an l-power and
a p-power. Complex infinite components are divisible and have no
nontrivial homomorphisms to a finite group.

The source permits ramification in Q but imposes unramifiedness at
every prime over l even when such a prime belongs to Q. Consequently
all the relevant idelic characters kill the same congruence subgroup
U_Q: unrestricted infinite components, principal units at Q, and
full units elsewhere. Finite-support approximation of unit ideles
and closedness of the character kernels justify passing from local
components to the full restricted product.

The quotient of all ideles by diagonal elements and full local units
is the ideal class group. The extra congruence quotient is bounded
by the finite product of residue-unit groups at Q. Thus the common
idele quotient is finite. Pullback through the supplied Artin map
shows that the intersection of all the permitted Galois-character
kernels has finite index. Only after proving finite index do we
deduce that finitely many distinct kernels occur: they are subgroups
of a single finite quotient. Finite intersections are again kernels
of finite l-primary anticyclotomic characters. This proves that the
intersection is open and its quotient is itself l-primary.

For Q' contained in Q, the kernel for Q is smaller, so the quotient
map goes from RCG_Q to RCG_Q'. These maps commute exactly with the
surjective Artin quotients. Universality for every character ramified
only in Q is asserted only when Q avoids l. The stronger restriction
at l is part of the actual construction and has not been weakened.
The construction does not prove that RCG_Q is nontrivial, nor that
it contains a character ramifying at every prime of Q or trivial
on the entire l-decomposition group. Those are different conditions.

6. NR12–NR15: field-by-field audit of the actual RingSpec

The finite lower bound c0 is the compositum of j(K), mu_l and the
finitely many local bridge normal closures, all over Q_l. For each
q, K0 and Lw are exactly b0 and b1 evaluated at its prescribed J.
The completed rings are the closed integer balls of K0 Lw U_l and
of the corresponding l^k-cyclotomic adjunctions. These are explicit
formulas, not a selected record assumed to satisfy desired fields.

The abstract complex field readings exist because Qbar_l and C_l
are algebraically closed of characteristic zero and cardinality
continuum. Over the countable number field K their transcendence
bases have that cardinality. Extend the prescribed K-isomorphism
through algebraic closures and matching transcendence bases.
These readings are not continuous and are not used as continuous
maps. The Qbar_l reading used for normalized characters and the
C_l reading used for iCq need agree only on the prescribed K.

The actual delta is chosen at p_delta except when l=p_delta, in
which case it is the original delta' at p_delta'. GA16 supplies
its exact inertia order 15, not just finite order. The normalized
row character is beta=mu0_j*delta_l*eta^{-1}*abs^{-r/2}; its value
at an unramified prime is mu0_j(P)delta_l(P)eta(P)^{-1}N(P)^{r/2}.
The inverse of this value is the arithmetic-Frobenius image. Thus
the NC.Read exponent is -r, while delta's exponent is zero.
For the false side, the complex reading is conjugated and the
K embedding is precomposed with star; the two conjugations cancel
on K. The full natural-number family uses the literal source
reindexing a(j)=min(j-1,m-1), with truncated subtraction. It agrees
with the j-1 row in the required range and gives all TwR laws even
outside that range. No out-of-range character has been left undefined.

7. NR16–NR18: the distinction between center, SidePl and Induces

Positive valuation on algebraic integers defines a nonzero prime
center. The residue characteristic is in this center. Equality
of centers alone first says something only about O_K; it is not
yet the full SidePl condition on K. NR16 proves that upgrade.
Because O_K localized at q is a DVR, every x or its inverse has
a fraction expression with denominator outside q. Those
denominators map to units in both valuation rings. If the numerator
is in q the inverse case has negative value and cannot be integral;
otherwise it is a unit. This proves exact equivalence on every x.

For a place w of L, select its restricted center q in K. Its
residue characteristic and l(q) must coincide by Bezout. The
result gives SidePl(w,q,true) for every w and also TwCover on
positive centers. It is not necessary to assume that an actual
row is nonzero at w for this coefficient assertion.

Reading.Induces is stronger and refers to every x in L. NR18
proves it as a new consequence, not by confusing it with SidePl.
Start with an embedding L->Qbar_l inducing w. Its restriction
and the fixed J_q give the same normalized norm on K. Their
isomorphism extends to the finite K_q completions, and then to
an automorphism of Qbar_l. Adjusting the L embedding by that
automorphism matches J_q exactly and leaves its place unchanged.
Choose a complex field isomorphism matching all of L. Its inverse
on epsilon(L) is now precisely the adjusted local embedding,
which proves Induces. The canonical O0 fraction embedding has
not changed. The same proof works for any supplied side with
the actual SidePl hypothesis.

8. NR19–NR21: compact descent and the integral lattice

Krasner's step is proved explicitly. If y is closer to x than
any distinct Q_l-conjugate of x, every automorphism fixing y
must fix x by the ultrametric inequality. Hence x lies in
Q_l(y). The dense global Qbar supplies such y. Finite subsets
of the countable global Qbar consequently index a countable
cofinal family of finite Q_l fields in Qbar_l.

For a continuous representation of a compact Hausdorff group,
the elements whose matrix and inverse-matrix entries lie in
one of these finite fields form a closed subgroup. The subgroups
cover the whole compact group. Baire gives one with interior,
so it is open and has finite index. Adjoining the finitely many
entries of coset representatives to its coefficient field then
captures all matrix entries for the entire representation.
This avoids the invalid inference that a compact subset of
Qbar_l automatically lies in one finite field without argument.

Once the field is finite, compactness bounds all matrix entries.
The O-span of rho(g)O^n for every g lies in one finite fractional
lattice and contains O^n. It is finite, torsion-free and free of
rank n over the DVR. It is stable because left multiplication
permutes its summands. A basis produces the exact conjugation
P^{-1}rho(g)P in GL_n(O), including integral inverses. No residual
comparison or prescribed original lattice is inferred from
this existence of a stable lattice.

9. NR22–NR24: actual conditional row representations and normalization

GalEx is retained with its precise universal content, including
the existing automorphic package, every l and complex reading,
and the shift (1-a)/2. For rank r the constructed scalar twist
is the avatar of eta^{-1}abs^{-t/2}. Its inverse Frobenius value
multiplies each Frobenius eigenvalue of the original GalEx
representation. Since nV=r+t, the shift becomes (1-nV)/2.
This proves the literal ExistsGaloisRepOfCuspidal endpoint
under GalEx; it does not prove GalEx from a rank-one character.

RowCusp gives a cuspidal parameter for an actual good occurring
row, its split local agreement, and a cofinite family of normalized
polynomials in the fixed K. The last clause makes changing the
complex reading safe: injectivity of the first reading shows
the l-adic polynomial is exactly the image of the K-polynomial,
so the second reading gives the same answer. Continuity of a
complex field reading is never used.

ChebSplit is used to compare the polynomial of the same chosen
representation with another parameter having the same split
local row. A second representation exists by GalEx. Its
characteristic-polynomial coefficients agree with those of
the first on a dense set of Frobenius lifts, so continuity
gives equality everywhere. The first representation therefore
also has the required Frobenius identity for the second parameter.
No arbitrary isomorphism is selected and no automorphic
parameter is manufactured from this density argument.

For each embedding j and each good occurring row, choose and
integralize one representation. Take the finite compositum
over the finite row-type set and both j and j composed with
star. This is c1(l,j). Rows not satisfying GoodOcc use the
trivial representation only as an unused total-function value;
the theorem does not assert an automorphic realization for them.

Every RingSpec whose b0 contains c1 admits the resulting
O0-valued family by its exact integer-ring identification.
For any other fraction embedding phi respecting Z_l, finite
separability gives phi=tau*kappa for a Q_l-automorphism tau.
Its norm is unchanged. The specified complex reading composed
with tau agrees with epsilon on the actual sided K embedding,
so the universal previous reading assertion applies. This
checks the full quantifier over phi and iota in rho_rows.

10. Remaining interfaces; no hidden promotion to Core

The full SideB producer second_step still requires RatDat and
RatIn, actual integral and Hecke-stable row lattices, MtFam,
MtInput and compatible level/tuple data, together with the
retained GalEx/RowCusp/ChebSplit. NR supplies its ring constructor,
coefficient coverage and bounded-rho primitive, not the entire
second_step proof. The coefficient bound for its remaining
weight constructor is independent and may be combined later;
that constructor still invokes the actual highest-weight and
irreducibility inputs exposed by the AR frontier.

MtReal is not merely a finite-dimensional module. It maps the
specified Mt(mu,j) injectively onto an entire actual adelic type
space with open level and character. MtAd additionally gives
standard row frames and the exact pointwise equality with the
fixed Y.IL.jMt, rational-group correspondence, weight-action
intertwining and finite-matrix identity. The existing
ExistsMotiveAdjointWithLevels theorem transports an already
supplied MtAd through exact reading identities; it is not an
initial existence theorem for this structure.

IsTripleWith further requires GoodOcc, the specified cuspidal
parameter with exact infinity/type/unramified labels, the
Galois representation for that parameter, the scalar equation
with the already installed rhoS, the hEig equations, Induces,
and the actual residual characteristic polynomials and
Teichmuller reduction. IsTripleFor adds both-side IsChars,
fixed chiT calibration and signature/eigenspace coverage.
NR18 supplies the precise Induces ingredient. NR22–NR24 give
conditional representations. They do not assert the remaining
simultaneous equations for pre-existing signatures or reductions.

NTw also needs a genuine TwF (signatures, B, Theta, Hecke data)
and TwL (toric characters and comparison values). The source's
TwR.ntw installs these over a bare local index type; subsequent
construction of nonempty local indices and LocsCover has its own
requirements. Empty-index or zero-form choices do not resolve
those requirements. The universal quotient constructed here
does not yet produce a nonempty-support IsPsi with the required
trivial action on the whole l-decomposition group.

The other v0.21 components are not silently used for these gaps.
The volume reconstruction supplies its stated Haar and mass
identities, not MtFam, MtAd, SideB or LocsCover. All four global
completion branches and the original RH/GRH status are unchanged
by the conditional arithmetic results in this component.

11. Reproduction and verification

Run build_provenance.py with --repo pointing to the immutable
source repository. It reads pinned Git blobs without changing
the checkout, checks a present checkout file against that blob,
locates each exact declaration, hashes its inclusive source
range and full file, hashes every NR paragraph and both proof
artifacts, checks the local dependency graph, and emits the
ledger, source manifest, source locators and next-target map.
Independent reviews are attached only when their recorded
paragraph hashes match the current proofs in their stated scope.

All new written mathematical files belong to nonempty_realization.
No previous component or Lean source was edited. The construction
is a human reconstruction from the pin, with explicit classical
inputs and conditional endpoints; no new machine-checked build
or unconditional proof of RH or GRH is asserted.

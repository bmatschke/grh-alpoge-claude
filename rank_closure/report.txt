This report records the exact dependency assembly of the local theorem.

HYPOTHESES
Take the literal Conclusion2 setting from
Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/ValuationLowestDegreeRename.lean:
an unramified quadratic setting at p with period n'>0, p>=13 prime,
r>=2, k=r+K, valid periodic frames F, and all supplied degrees, scales,
cardinalities, gaps and size inequalities. These inequalities are written
out in the CG, LA and RS reports. Require CLa,Ck>=1088. The coefficient
ring O is a DVR; the ordinary field of fractions and residue field are
those of the source setting. Its uniformizer w obeys w^2=p, so p lies
in the square of the maximal ideal and is nonzero. Completeness is part
of the setting. Thus the source UniformizedDVR hypotheses hold.

Write N=|TT|, q=p^n', La=CLa Ns^2 log a, and
B=(1-Nr^2k/Y) product_(t in TT) binom(deg_t+k-1,k-1).
Let gen be the actual source Gen family (fine segment conditions and
fine label bounds, with physical row degree d_t=deg_t-E'_t on TT).
Let NN be its O-span and NNbar its residue-field span. Let LamBar be
the coarse-generator residue span, using a'_t=d_t-Et_t.

RK1. THE COARSE RESIDUE COUNT
GP constructs, for every integer segment j, an actual function G_j(b)
satisfying all four SegOK clauses: nonnegativity; dependence only on late
degrees; comparison G_j(b)<=rank_O M_j(b) at admissible b; and the required
sum over every terminal-degree residue class. LA3 supplies the finite
rank condition where needed. RS4 proves that every family degree
b_(lambda,e,j) is supported on its segment, has the required old-frame
lower bounds and satisfies AdmLL.

The family sets LA and EE(lambda) are finite. Hence termwise monotonicity
of nonnegative products and sums gives

 sum_lambda sum_e product_j G_j(b_(lambda,e,j))
 <= sum_lambda sum_e product_j rank_O M_j(b_(lambda,e,j)).

RS8 proves q^(-rK)B is at most the first expression. LA6 constructs an
independent residue family whose cardinality is the second expression,
inside the actual LamBar. Thus

                q^(-rK)B <= dim_f LamBar.

This is RestCount 1088 1. If B<=0 the first inequality is automatic;
RS's proof never multiplies potentially negative lower factors.

RK2. TRANSFER TO THE ACTUAL GENERATOR SPAN
The exact determinant polynomial is Delta=Dl. SD3 proves its residue
Delta_bar is nonzero. CG8 proves that each coarse generator u admits an
individual actual generator g with reduction(Delta*u)=reduction(g).
Define the f-linear multiplication map T(h)=Delta_bar*h. The residue
polynomial ring is a domain, so T is injective. On the generators of
LamBar, its image lies in NNbar; linearity extends this inclusion to
the whole span. Therefore dim_f LamBar<=dim_f NNbar, proving

                q^(-rK)B <= dim_f NNbar.

Finite dimensionality is direct: all actual generators have fixed
physical row degree d, so their monomials lie in a finite set. The
coarse family similarly has row degree a'. This is the full RestRank
1088 1 statement, with an explicit map and finite-dimensional target.

RK3. PRIMITIVE ACTUAL GENERATORS, PLANE NORMALIZATION AND DEGREE
Apply SD1 to the actual Gen set and its finite common monomial support.
Choose actual polynomials whose residues form a basis of NNbar. A unit
coefficient minor defines a retraction of the full polynomial module
onto their span Sg. Consequently Sg is an ambient direct summand, lies
inside NN, has an O-basis of actual generators, and has rank exactly
dim_f NNbar. RK2 gives rank(Sg)>=q^(-rK)B.

For any finite field Fq of cardinality q, each linear map Fq^r->Fq^K
has an r-dimensional graph in Fq^(r+K). Projection onto the first r
coordinates recovers the map from its graph, so these q^(rK) graphs are
distinct. Thus nPl(Fq,k,r)>=q^(rK)>0. If B>=0, then
B/nPl<=q^(-rK)B<=rank(Sg). If B<0, then B/nPl<0<=rank(Sg).
This proves the exact extra normalized-rank clause in Conclusion2.

SD4 proves, for every P in the entire actual span NN and every monomial
in its support, the normal-degree bound
  normal_degree <=10N(La*a/(pk)+a*p^(-L1)+kQ).
The same holds in Sg by inclusion. It is the literal Cdeg=10 clause;
the sharper intermediate SD4 estimate is retained in its earlier proof.

Thus the source's second selection conclusion has the human witnesses
C0=1088, Crk=1, Cdeg=10, in the stated supplied-data setting. This also
reconstructs the mathematical content used by SecondPartClosed at these
witnesses, without assuming the whole AdmB conjunction as a black box.
Only its relevant integral basis, counting and graph-plane assertions
are used, and those are proved in the corresponding components.

BOUNDARY
This theorem is local and conditional on the explicit frame/parameter
data. It does not prove that those data can be chosen compatibly with
every other Type-II step, nor construct the separate r=1 family, nor
supply the spectral, theta, congruence or cohomological window estimates.
Those remain identified in the cumulative note. It is a human proof
assembled from checked source translations and shorter arguments; it
has not been formalized or independently replayed in Lean.

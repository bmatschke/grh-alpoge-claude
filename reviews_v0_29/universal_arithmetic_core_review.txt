3. KX3: henselization/completion transports the actual decomposition group and inertia. Local reciprocity gives Z-hat times the unit group; deep logarithms and the structure theorem isolate the finite ell-primary torsion P and the free Z_ell^d part, including ell=2. 4. KX4: the prescribed th character descends through the closed commutator kernel to the finite torsion group P; its reduction is one. Combining it with the Teichmuller lift gives an actual IsPsi. Quotients of any other lift by this base character kill the whole inverse image of P. Testing finite maximal-ideal quotients and then using separatedness gives the claimed unique continuous factor through a.

5. KX5: only the inertia coordinates enter the weight ring, so the number of variables is n times [F:Q], not n times the sum of d_v+1. Actual full-character extensions witness IsInert. Testing elements of inertia mapping to basis vectors proves uniqueness and independence of the chosen full extensions, including n=0.

The global and local finite-cohomology fields of BX8 apply to these exact finite modules; the CM subgroup is open. Thus the full PhiAll interface is supplied without automorphy.

Propositions 3.3 and 3.7 under condition (F) have no residual-irreducibility or ell>n restriction. Completed base change from W(k) to the given finite coefficient DVR preserves the exact residue field and universal maps. The degree-zero branch is proved separately.

8. KX8: the preliminary determinant/full-local-character coproduct has exactly the required universal property.

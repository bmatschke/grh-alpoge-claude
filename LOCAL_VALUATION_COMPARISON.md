# One cyclotomic field for all the local Galois groups

A global Galois representation restricts at a prime $u$ to the subgroup preserving a chosen extension of $u$ to the algebraic closure. The local theory describes this same action using the absolute Galois group of the completion $E_u$. The part called $F_3(w)$ in HLP32 makes these two descriptions agree over the coefficient ring used for the Hecke operators. For the host field in this proof, one can give the coefficient bound explicitly. [The four fields in HLP32](local_hecke_projection/main.tex#L981-L1004)

**Proposition.** Let $E$ be the host number field, $N>0$ its specified conductor, and $\Omega$ its chosen algebraic closure. For a coefficient place $w$ of residue characteristic $\ell$, put $m=\operatorname{lcm}(\ell,N)$ and fix the source's algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Then one can take

$$
F_3(w)=\mathbb Q_\ell(\mu_m)\subset\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell .
$$

For every finite coefficient field $L$ inside this closure containing $F_3(w)$, and every $u\mid\ell$ in $E$, there is a local comparison realizing the **prescribed** valuation ring $A_u\subset\Omega$. Its coefficient ring is $\mathcal O_L$, its map on integral coefficients is the usual inclusion into $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, and $L$ contains the images of every $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding of $E_u$. The same field works for every index $j$ of the Hecke family. [The formal statement for each place](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/UnitsLiftSpec.lean#L305-L310)

## From a global valuation to the local Galois group

For each $u\mid\ell$, choose an embedding $i_u:\Omega\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ inducing $A_u$. One way to see the choice is to embed $E$ through $E_u$, extend to an embedding of $\Omega$, and conjugate by an element of $G_E$ to get the specified prolongation. Transitivity on prolongations follows at each finite Galois level from transitivity on primes over $u$, and at the infinite level by compactness of $G_E$. Thus
$x\in A_u$ exactly when $|i_u(x)|_\ell\leq1$. The closure of $i_u(E)$ is the embedded completion $K_u=\mathbb Q_\ell i_u(E)$. Indeed $K_u$ is finite over $\mathbb Q_\ell$ and $\mathbb Q$ is dense in $\mathbb Q_\ell$. [Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Chapter 8, extending absolute values and Proposition 8.10](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf#page=137) · [The prescribed valuation in the pinned source](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/FiniteDimensionalAdjoinRange.lean#L36-L75)

Write $D_{A_u}$ for the stabilizer of $A_u$ in $G_E$. The decomposition-group theorem gives a topological isomorphism

$$
e_u:D_{A_u}\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell/K_u),
\qquad e_u(g)(i_u(x))=i_u(gx).
$$

At each finite Galois extension $M/E$ in $\Omega$, a member of the decomposition group extends uniquely to the completion $K_ui_u(M)$, and restriction inverts this identification. These local fields exhaust $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Indeed, by Milne's Corollary 7.62 any finite extension of $K_u$ inside that closure is generated over $\mathbb Q_\ell$ by a number field. Its algebraic generators lie in $i_u(\Omega)$, hence in $i_u(M)$ for some finite Galois $M/E$. Taking inverse limits gives the displayed isomorphism with the indicated action and Krull topologies. [Milne, Proposition 8.10](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf#page=141) · [Milne, Corollary 7.62](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf#page=134)

## Why adjoining roots of unity suffices

The host construction embeds $E$ into $\mathbb Q(\mu_N)$. Every $i_u|_E$ extends to this cyclotomic extension, so $K_u\subset\mathbb Q_\ell(\mu_N)\subset F_3(w)$. Since $F_3(w)$ is the splitting field of $X^m-1$, it also contains every $\mathbb Q_\ell$-conjugate of $K_u$. This supplies the splitting condition in the local comparison for every $u\mid\ell$ at once. The other coefficient condition, called `GoodCoef`, requires exactly that all $m$-th roots of unity belong to $L$. These include the $\ell$-power roots prescribed by $m$, even when $\ell$ divides $N$. [Cyclotomic image of the host](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/PrimeDiscriminants/CycImage.lean#L33-L64) · [Cyclotomic containment of the completion](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CyclotomicBounds/FiniteDimensionalRoots.lean#L32-L57) · [Definition of GoodCoef](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/RhoDetPrimeEqMuTildeMulChiPow.lean#L28-L31)

For any such $L$, take the identity on $\mathcal O_L$ as the comparison of coefficient rings. It preserves the given $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebra structure and topology. The map called $\mathrm{iq}$ is the identification $\operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O_L)\simeq L$ followed by inclusion in the fixed algebraic closure. Thus both routes take an integral scalar $z$ to the same element $z\in\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. This gives the simultaneous comparisons on the final field $L=K_x(j,w)$ of HLP32, with the prolongations fixed before adjoining any Hecke eigenvalues or twist coefficients. [Comparison and identity on the coefficient ring](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/FiniteDimensionalAdjoinRange.lean#L303-L325) · [The equality on integral scalars](https://github.com/CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean/blob/7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/UnitsLiftSpec.lean#L318-L321)

The pinned existence proof allows a finite field containing the normal closures of the finitely many $K_u$ and the cyclotomic field. Taking their compositum gives exactly $F_3(w)$ by the host containment above. This proves the $F_3$ assertion used in the common local specialization construction for every given host datum.

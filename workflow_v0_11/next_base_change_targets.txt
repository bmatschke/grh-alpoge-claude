PRECISION TO PRESERVE
---------------------
1. GroupCompatibilityFamily supplies a standard-subring field; it is stronger than the local coefficient-extension theorem, and its existence must not be inferred from BC.
2. HM12 is isomorphism invariance, BC7 is finite coefficient extension, and D is comparison of an actual global lattice with a local function lattice. They are three distinct mathematical statements.
3. No parameter ramification multiplier is needed for the mapped powers of omega and p. The image of omega may not generate the maximal ideal of the extended DVR.
4. The finite-place embeddings can be chosen independently for BC2. The simultaneous Frobenius/archimedean/place identifications required by IsLocalFamily are new compatibility work.
5. The next family theorem must retain the selected tops, degree pair, uniform thresholds, and the actual comparison map. Replacing a family by an unrelated recC record would not establish that join.

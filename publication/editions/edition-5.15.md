ETS15–20 give a special-value proof of the complete odd-minus estimate on every abelian CM host. The weighted sum over all odd primes and twists below $t$ is at most $(n+1)t^2\log(tN)$ for degree $n$ and conductor $N$. This includes primes dividing the field degree and proves the original assertion with no exceptional-prime term. The proof uses Wiles’s and Kolster’s higher class number formulas, ramified descent, and an explicit integral bound for the relevant product of Dirichlet values.

For $\mathbf Q(\mu_9)$ at twist two, the new proof computes the full odd-minus contribution explicitly. The strict group is cyclic of order $13$ at the prime $13$ and vanishes at every other odd prime.

The total strict sum on the chosen windows differs by explicit bounded errors from a product involving the complementary odd eigenspaces and the full dyadic orders. Its uniform size, the arithmetic model comparisons and the remaining analytic inputs are recorded in the audit.

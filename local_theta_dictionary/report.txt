Scope and premise
-----------------
This component accepts the user's premise that the pinned Alpoge-Claude
Lean development is fully checked and correct. It supplies human proofs of
eight exact implications on the original objects. It does not replay Lean
and does not claim to have produced the common geometric state, Bundle,
NdSpec, RatIn, or original theta comparison.

Concrete improvement
--------------------
The strongest new consumer closure is:

  actual NdSpec on (D,L,M,C)
      => LocsCoverTw(withLoc L), together with joint injectivity of
         the original prMt maps on MtL(muTw(w),j);

  NBuilt(hLL,Dprime)
      => LocsCoverTw(Dprime);

  exists x0, forall x>=x0, forall original i,
      exists NBuilt(hLL,Fm.D(x,i))
      => LinkEta(Fm).

LTD3 proves the finite algebra behind this implication. An arbitrary,
possibly infinite commuting family of split operators on a finite space
admits a finite simultaneous generalized eigenblock decomposition. Every
generator eigenvalue being in the fixed DVR makes each supported block's
character an O-valued algebra character. Grouping the blocks by residual
character kernels proves both the sum-of-dimensions identity and the zero
intersection of complementary blocks. This proof handles the zero space
and does not assume diagonalizability or finite generation of the Hecke
algebra over O.

LTD4 matches every premise to NdSpec: std supplies commutation and
integrality; B.split supplies splitting; iV_basis gives the dimension and
injectivity on the original rational space; mx_max/mx_inj/mx_all enumerate
the maximal ideals; Psi gives the actual local block; and Psi_pr gives the
original projection modulo its exact complementary block. The rank formula
is asserted only for 1<=j<=m and muAt(w)!=mu0, exactly as LocsCoverTw. For
coverage and joint injectivity, AutSpec is unnecessary. To assemble the
whole NormalizedLocalCompatibility record, its iotaQ and iotaQC equations
are needed for the final complex-embedding square.

This removes a second independent HeckeStd supplier at this concrete
consumer. It does not remove the obligation to construct NdSpec. The
generic source link_eta consumes only TSel's existing MtFam, cover and
HeckeTied and ignores Duty; it still calls its HeckeStd theorem. We do not
assert that an arbitrary TSel contains NBuilt. On the actual construction
route, the certificate can be retained from nTrueAtWeight, whose datum
equality and dependent character-choice pack equality are reflexive.

Other closed dictionary slices
------------------------------
LTD1 checks that sel, selS, selP, selDH, selC and selB are projections of one
literal RawSel Bundle. In particular selIOf is that same Bundle's B.Q and
selKOf is B.T.Q.c.F. WireSel and LinkAlt therefore retain the same data
without an additional compatibility choice. This is conditional on the
bundle; it does not close the BundleExB producer.

LTD2 unfolds LocAll.lay, fixZ, reEta and setN. Local indices and their local
objects are replaced; Z/Zgood and etaJ are prescribed as in the source.
The original row spaces, embeddings, form/theta operators, twist rings and
Artin maps, and normalized NChoice maps are preserved. Preservation cannot
upgrade the initialization theta fields to a substantive evaluation or
nonvanishing theorem.

LTD6 identifies the row operators on the actual transported geometric
state. The key source equalities are SL_heq, hL_eq, and rep_eq in
RowsAtStart.normalizedRowDatum. The final RatIn.span and hL_int assertions
are precisely those on the original S.WL(j), with its original coefficient
lattice and exceptional set. The exponent n in hL_int may depend on j,k,v.
The coefficient action is conjugate to u^k times the scalar-extended row
action; the same u is the specified cHk unit. Ordinary and generalized
eigenspaces consequently transport with that exact eigenvalue scaling.

A material distinction is made explicit: RatIn/SideB/NRow.hL acts on the
rank-r row space, while NdSpec.B.hL/hK acts on the ambient MtL space and
allows Hecke indices through nV. They are different families. NChoice's
basis/lattice fields and ChoiceSide.eMt_level do not alone provide an
intertwining square between them. The latter field asserts equality of
levels and their characters after the given extending embedding; it does
not assert a pointwise eMt equality.

LTD7 transports the actual existing GeoTie.ThetaTie and then reconstructs
the complete SerAt localization. The theta functions, generator sets,
universal coefficients and finite class sums have identical pointwise
readings. Every CellTie field is checked, and the same nonzero scalar is
kept through both norm and conditional rationality clauses. ExpRel.reads
supplies the final injective coefficient factorization for a replacement
NExpTied on the installed datum. Existing evaluation, rationality and
series content is retained as a premise, not inferred from a record name.

LTD8 proves field transport only through actual image containment in the
fixed embLL. HasNumberField is weaker than FieldOK. It also records the
additional denominator nonvanishing, bottom-triangle, integrability and
grid-identity inputs required by the FieldCond consumer. BottomIn/SumsIn
already provides field membership of normalized monomial integrals
I/Omega and I'/Omega', subject to integrability guards. The independent
hI hypothesis discharges those guards for the selected triangle, and
rationalSums multiplies through the already nonzero periods. Rationality
is an explicit input here, not a consequence of integrating a kernel whose
pointwise values happen to lie in the coefficient field.

Remaining priority
------------------
GLOBAL_LOCAL_THETA_BINDINGS is strictly narrowed, not globally closed.
The same-model installation, operator identity, and subsequent theta/
series transport are B implications now. Actual producer work remains at
one LC/RowsAtStart state: compatible NChoice/ChoiceSide and NdSpec, the
original rational spectral span and integral preservation, genuine theta
evaluation and EMtTheta, the exact base theta square used by the rank
consumer, and toric/jet/period values in the specified coefficient field.
These should be carried together into the Bundle constructor. The next
branch should attack one of those actual producers, not repeat local
coverage algebra after NdSpec is available.
